Your blessings for whom You will; in Your hand are the keys of خیرات and treasures, and You are the one who makes easy the difficult matters, and in Your hand are the دقائق of pearls from light, and the one who raises the spirit of the generous one to the innermost secrets of the people of breasts. And by Your care every closed matter is opened, and by Your command the secret of every locked and sealed thing is unveiled. I ask You, O Fattah of every good and repeller of every harm, that You make me standing with You, accepting from You, receiving the effusions of the scientific life and the eternal grants, and the good awaiting for the appearance of the faces of Your gentleness, constantly watching for the attainment of the perfection of Your فضل, continually looking toward finding the آثار of Your nobility, and open upon my heart and my sight the doors of unveiling and witnessing, and support me upon accepting the light of Your face when the خزائن of what is in Your mercy and Your forgiveness are spread forth, O Ancient of beneficence, O Compassionate, O Bestower, O Lord of the worlds. * And as for His تعالى name, al-‘Alim, it is a عظیم name in which are two letters from the letters of the Greatest Name, and it is an ancient name because God تعالى has never ceased to be Knowing. And in it is a great secret for whoever desires unveiling of knowledge from the world of the unseen and the witnessed, because God تعالى is subtle; He does not disclose His unseen to anyone except whom He is pleased with from a messenger. And the angel created from it is Lutfiya'il عليه السلام, and he is a chief over four commanders; under the hand of each commander are 150 rows, each row 150. And the one who remembers it, the angel descends upon him and fulfills his need, and that is by his felicity to his father and to his grandfather. And as for supplication by this noble name, say: O God, You are the Knower of what is in the breasts of the worlds, and You are the Knower of what is in the secrets of the humble, and You see what is in the hidden essences of the worlds, and You encompass what is in the movements of the thoughts of all creatures. I ask You by the hidden guarded things of Your mercy, and by the gleaming fragrances of Your compassion, and by the great majesties of Your immense favor, that You make my knowledge encompassing everything, its outward and its inward, its high and its great, its first and its last, its opening and its end, until I drown in the expansion of the secrets of Your oneness and the spread of the دقائق of Your bounty, and I seek means to You in my beginning and my end, and I do not show my hope to other than You, O Knower of the hidden things and the secrets, and O Gatherer of the scattered in the insights, provide me with clarity and openings and unveiling and sipping upon the name of what is hidden in the thoughts and the gazes, for You are the one encompassing the existents in knowledge and existence, and You are the ruler over the secrets in expansion and witnessing, Lord of the worlds, and may God bless our master Muhammad and his family and his companions and grant peace.

(Chapter twenty-third in the third pattern and what indicates the attributes and the supports)
* Know, may God grant me and you success in obedience to Him and understanding the secrets of His names, that among the beautiful names of God are al-‘Alim, al-Hakim, al-Basit, al-Karim, al-Wahhab, al-Tawwab, al-Nasir, al-Badi‘, ‘Allam al-Ghuyub. These names have differing properties and secrets, and the one who remembers this noble pattern, God تعالى gives him knowledge from لدن and secrets upon which no one in his time is informed except one who comes with the like of it, and God تعالى provides him with every good, improves his character, gives him eloquence in his speech, and supports him. As for His names al-Hakim, al-‘Alim, they are two noble names for whoever desires reaching wisdom and the sciences of secrets, so persist in remembering them in seclusion, bareheaded, sitting on the ground without barrier, facing the qiblah, for God inspires him with wisdom and conveys him to what he wants, and decrees for him a ruling that guides him or an angel according to his aspiration. And whoever brings down their numbers in a square of 4 by 4 in the exaltedness of Mercury and Jupiter connected to it, then a tablet of green jade and carries it on his head, God makes him speak with wisdom and his gaze does not pass over anything except that he memorizes it. * And as for His تعالى names, ‘Allam al-Ghuyub, they are two noble names; whoever remembers them, God removes forgetfulness from him and expands his provision.
