( Chapter ) I mention in it a great benefit, and that is that I entered some of the mosques to pray in it, and I found his brother among my brothers, among those with whom I used to gather with him at our shaykh Abu عبد الله, the shaykh Abd al-Haqq al-Sabti, and he recited to him. So when I saw him that day in the mosque I approached him to greet him, and I found him standing toward the qiblah while he was looking toward the sky once and toward his palm once, so I approached him and I heard him saying: O Allah, O Responder of supplications, and O Accomplisher of the needs of the askers, and O Hearer of voices, and O Forgiver of slips, and O Remover of stumbles, and O Sender down of blessings, and O One whose knowledge has encompassed everything, I ask You to send blessings and peace upon our master Muhammad and upon the family of our master Muhammad, and that You fulfill my need, and it is such and such, by the right of these names: ha ha hi hi hu hu yah yah ah, Subbuh, Quddus, Lord and Lord of the angels and the Spirit. I ask You, O Lord, by what is in this slip of paper of the names and what is in these supplications of blessings, that You fulfill my need. So he had not completed his supplication until his need was fulfilled. Then he turned and saw me, so he came near to me and greeted me, then apologized to me and said: I am supplicating Allah for the fulfillment of a need, and my Lord fulfilled it for me and favored me with it. So I said: O my brother, I have heard your supplication, but I see you looking into this slip of paper, what is in it? So he said to me: O man, I inform you of what happened, and that is that I served our shaykh, whom I and you used to take from and recite to for a long time. So he said to me: O this one, do you have a need? So I said to him: Yes. So he said to me: What is it? So I said: I want you to teach me a supplication by which I may supplicate Allah in my important matters, and it will be answered. So he said: Hearing and obedience; your right over me has become due for your serving us for a long time. Then he handed to me that slip of paper, and in it were written these noble names, and they are: sahha, sahhih, habba, habba, habba, with khallisahu.
