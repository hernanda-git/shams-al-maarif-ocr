English:
- 294 -

His love in the hearts of humans and jinn. And it is said that His name الودود is that to which reference is made in the story of the merchant and the thief, that he said in his supplication: O ودود, O Possessor of the glorious Throne, O Originator, O Restorer, O Doer of what He wills, etc.; so Allah aided him with an angel from among the angels upon a horse, in whose hand was a spear, and he struck the thief and killed him. * And as for His name الحنان, whoever remembers it, Allah casts His love into the hearts of the creatures. * And if it is written 140 times in a pure vessel and washed with the white of egg and used to anoint whoever has fallen into the fire, he is healed immediately, and its remembrance removes hot diseases. * And as for His name اللطيف, it is swift in response, penetrating in secret in the relief of distresses; whoever mentions it in hardship, it is not but that it vanishes. And whoever persists in remembering it, Allah makes for him what has been written upon him of difficulty a dream. And this is called the hidden لطف, because of its being hidden from the perceptions of the intellects. And the least of its remembrance is 160 times. And whoever is granted it 4 by 4 on clean paper and carries it, or in an agate ring and seals with it, it is spoken with in his states. * And as for His تعالى name الحفيظ, it is a عظیم name; whoever remembers it, Allah preserves him from every disliked thing. And if the one remembering imagines, at the time of remembrance, a city and a dwelling or other than that, Allah preserves him. * And whoever is granted its numbers and letters in a square in a silver ring and seals with it, nothing from jinn, humans, creeping things, and other than that harms him. * And as for His تعالى name الرقيب, it causes joy in the awe of hearts and their submission, and whoever remembers it is accompanied by modesty from his Master and by courtesy in secret and public, outward and inward. * And as for His تعالى name البر, it has a عظیم secret; whoever remembers it, blessings descend upon him and good things hasten to him from Allah. * And as for His تعالى name الشافي, it is a عظیم name for the swiftness of healing of ailments; whoever remembers it, Allah grants him well-being from every evil and affliction and cures him from every sickness, and ailments do not approach his body. * And whoever remembers it for a sick person 422 times after reciting al-Fātiḥah 7 times, saying: O Allah, heal; You are the Healer, there is no healing except Your healing, O Allah, a healing that leaves no sickness and no pain, then that sick person is healed. * And I ordered Mahmūd ibn Shāh with it when leprosy afflicted him and the physicians fled from him, and he did not remain after that except fifteen days, and he was healed as though nothing had been with him. * And whoever is granted its mentioned numbers in a square in a pure vessel and Zamzam water or rainwater, and a sick person is given to drink from it for 3 days on an empty stomach, Allah heals him. * And as for His تعالى name المحصي, it is a عظیم name in which is a letter from the letters of the الاسم الأعظم, and its king is Qaḥṭiyā’īl, and he is chief over 4 commanders, under the hand of each commander 148 rows, each row 148 thousand angels, and the one remembering it, the angel descends upon him, and its supplication: you say, You who have counted the breaths of the creatures, and You who have uprooted from Your saints the paths of attachments, and You who brought the people of knowledge to the greatest light, which is above the favor of the eyes and the sharp-witted, and You are the Preserver of all creatures whose deeds and terms and breaths are counted in all times, so that no deviant matter is absent and no striving striver is lost with You. I ask You, O Allah, O Possessor of the عظیم فضل, that You provide me with counting and preserving the realities of the names and reaching their secret. * And as for His تعالى name المبدئ, it is a عظیم name in which is a letter from the letters of the الاسم الأعظم, and its king is Kahā’īl, and he is chief over four commanders, under the hand of each commander 56 rows, each row 56, and the dāl, the angel descends upon him, and its supplication: you say, O Originator, You who manifested the secret of oneness in the hearts of the people of tawḥīd, and raised the banner of glory in the breasts of the people of detachment, and set up the banner of knowledge in the deserts of the intellects of the people of singling-out. I ask You, O Allah, by what You manifested in the heart of the Seal of the Prophets, and by what You established in the Seal of the Saints, and by what You spread in their essence of the subtleties of favors and blessings, that You return me to You.
