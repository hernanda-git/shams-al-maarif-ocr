(Chapter twenty-seven on the seventh pattern of the names of Allah تعالى and what there is
of hidden blessings from them)

And know that among the names of Allah تعالى are الحكيم الرؤوف الودود الغفور الحنان اللطيف الحفيظ الرقيب البر الشافي. These ten names from the names of Allah تعالى, from the support of their secret, are the harmony of discordant hearts and the turning of spirits and the secret of endearment and the casting of tenderness and mercy in remembrance and the relief of distress and the vanishing of hardships and protection from jinn and humans and the ملازمة of modesty and the دوام of health in religion and body and the continuous supplies of good upon the one who remembers. As for the two names تعالى الحكيم الرؤوف, they are two عظیم names in the acceptance of repentance and pardon for sin; whoever remembers them repeatedly, if sins have ruined him, he repents, and pardon for what he has committed, and his repentance is accepted and he is protected. And whoever is granted the name تعالى العفو in a square and carries it, his evil deeds among people are like good deeds. And as for the names تعالى الودود and الغفور, they are two noble names in the acceptance of repentance; if he repeats them, hearts become attached to him with a great attachment through love.
