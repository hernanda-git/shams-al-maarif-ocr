(Chapter twenty-five on the fifth pattern and what is in it of the selected secrets)
Know that among the beautiful names of Allah are: the Most High, the Great, the Majestic, the Great, the Light, the Radiant, Possessor of majesty and honor.
The rememberer of this pattern will not be, in his time, more repelling in rank with the kings and the sultans and the great people, and they hasten
to fulfill his needs, and everyone who sees him fears him and respects him, and he is never abased. As for His تعالى names, the Most High, the Great, whoever remembers them
remains honored, magnified, high in aspiration, granted by the most stubborn of people ease of life, his word heard, people love him,
his provision expands, and he attains his aims. And whoever is granted success in their numbers, letter by letter and number by number, in a white amulet in the exaltation of the moon
and carries it will see from Allah’s gentleness and the magnification of people what he had not known before. * And as for His تعالى name, الجميل, it is suitable
for the bride; it is written according to a form and carried, for one will not see anything more joyous than it nor more beautiful. And whoever takes it as a remembrance, Allah makes him beautiful among His creation,
so he becomes good in character and creation. * And as for His name, الكبير المتعال, whoever carries them, Allah clothes him with awe
and dignity, and his aspiration and his spirit rise, and his soul becomes pure. And whoever is granted success in their numbers in a square upon a ring in the exaltation of the sun
and seals with it, no one looks at him except that he loves him, and if enemies see him, Allah casts terror into their hearts. * And as for His name, الجليل, whoever remembers it
humans, jinn, beasts, and vermin fear him. * And as for His تعالى name, النور البهي, the light of its secret spreads in the heart of
its rememberer and appears upon his outward aspect. And whoever remembers His name, النور, in most of his times, Allah illuminates his heart with the light of his hearings
and deposits in him the hidden of His secrets. * And whoever is granted success in its numbers, which are 602, and hangs it upon one afflicted with ophthalmia or weakness in his eyes,
his sight is illuminated. * And as for His تعالى names, المعز ذو الجلال والإكرام, whoever remembers them, Allah clothes him with garments of might and awe
and majesty and dignity. And whoever remembers them and enters upon the sultan, Allah casts his awe into the hearts. And whoever is granted success in His تعالى name,
المعز, with its letters in a triangle of red rubies and seals with it, no imam over him is abased. And for every pattern
there is a spiritual exercise. * And as for His تعالى name, الحسيب, it is a great name for repelling enemies and the envious, and the king created from its number
is Maṭā’īl and his domain: 4 commanders under his hand; each commander [has] 80 ranks, each rank 80 of the angels standing in support of creation.
Whoever remembers it, the spirit descends upon him and his need is fulfilled. And his supplication: you say, O Ḥasīb, You who gather the scattered things for the manifestation of
tawḥīd, and You who separated all essences in the station of adjustment, and joined together the scattered things of the breasts for the cohesion of
the secrets.
