English:
And to Allah belong the Most Beautiful Names, so call upon Him by them.

In the name of Allah, the Merciful, the Compassionate.

A testimony, pre-eternity; and from the light of this testimony the chosen ones drew up knowledge, so understand that, and the arrangement which appeared in the two testimonies
connected with the noble angels and those possessed of knowledge. So this is the testimony of eternity, for in them is the secret of these two testimonies, the witness
of the two kingdoms, and what He deposited in it by the secret of connection with the unveilings, and for every form its reaching to all the wisdoms.
And I ask Allah, the Living, the Self-Subsisting, that He make it a pure, accepted alms before my whispered prayer, and that the spirit of its ease accompany me
in my turning about and my abode, and that He make clear for me and for you the path, and bestow upon us and upon you the lights of verification. Verily this lightning-flash,
the merciful, and the shining, illuminating suns, are the paths of the knowers, and the way of the veracious, and the broad road of the righteous,
for the presence of the holiness of the Lord of the worlds, the Lord of the lords, and the splitter of the causes, and the lifter of the veil, invented without example,
the transcendent above forms, the everlasting, who has not ceased to be described with the attributes of beauty, ever-existent in pre-eternity, the lifter
of the higher things by His determination and His wisdom, and the spreader of the lower things by His power and His will; there is no god except Him, the Great, the Most High,
the veiled by the veils of lights, hidden from all the secrets, concealed from the pavilions of the sights, and He perceives
the sights by His inwardness, by His Essence, in His pre-eternity, and He appeared by His attributes in His eternity, and He became manifest by His instruments in His sempiternity, and He manifested Himself
by His acts in His eternity. He is the First in pre-eternity, and the Last in eternity, and the Manifest in sempiternity. Exalted is He above
substances and accidents, and above bodies and parts, and above being disposed of by purposes. The directions and the regions do not contain Him,
and the succession of the cycles does not follow Him, and the passing of night and day does not annihilate Him. I praise Him,
glorified is He and exalted, and everything with Him is by measure, Knower of the unseen and the witnessed, the Great, the Most High. And I bear witness that there is no god except
Allah, alone, no partner has He, a testimony that accompanies the spirits with تثبيت in the barzakh-realms. And I bear witness for you: His creation has counted it,
the living and the dead, and He has seen the dead and the powers, the world with what has passed and what is coming, and the Reviver of the dead
after they had been bones. And I bear witness that Muhammad is His servant and His Messenger, the sun of the creed and the deliverer of the servants from shirk
and abasement.

Indonesia:
Dan bagi Allah nama-nama yang paling indah, maka berdoalah kepada-Nya dengan nama-nama itu.

Dengan nama Allah, Yang Maha Pemurah, lagi Maha Penyayang.

Satu kesaksian, pra-abadi; dan dari cahaya kesaksian ini para pilihan menimba ilmu, maka pahamilah hal itu, dan susunan yang tampak pada dua kesaksian
yang tersambung dengan para malaikat yang mulia dan orang-orang yang berilmu. Maka inilah kesaksian keabadian, karena pada mereka terdapat rahasia kedua kesaksian itu, saksi
dua kerajaan, dan apa yang diletakkan-Nya di dalamnya dengan rahasia hubungan kepada kasyaf-kasyaf, dan bagi setiap bentuk jalannya mencapai segala hikmah.
Dan aku memohon kepada Allah, Yang Hidup, Yang Berdiri Sendiri, agar Dia menjadikannya sedekah yang murni lagi diterima di hadapan bisikan doaku, dan agar ruh ketenangan-Nya menyertaiku
dalam perubahan keadaanku dan tempat tinggalku, dan agar Dia menerangkan bagiku dan bagimu jalan itu, serta menganugerahi kami dan kalian dengan cahaya-cahaya penyingkapan hakikat. Sesungguhnya kilatan ini,
yang penuh rahmat, dan matahari-matahari yang terbit lagi menyinari, adalah jalan-jalan para arif, dan manhaj para shiddiq, dan jalan luas para shalih,
bagi hadirat quds rabbul-‘alamin, Rabb segala rabb, dan pembelah segala sebab, dan pengangkat hijab yang diciptakan tanpa contoh,
Yang disucikan dari bentuk-bentuk, Yang kekal, yang tiada henti-hentinya disifati dengan sifat-sifat keindahan, senantiasa ada dalam pra-abadi, Yang mengangkat
hal-hal tinggi dengan takdir-Nya dan hikmah-Nya, dan Yang membentangkan hal-hal rendah dengan kekuasaan-Nya dan kehendak-Nya; tiada tuhan selain Dia, Yang Maha Besar, lagi Maha Tinggi,
