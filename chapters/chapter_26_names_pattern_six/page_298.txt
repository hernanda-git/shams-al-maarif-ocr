other, and when the servant remembers his Lord, then his remembrance is in compliance, not for the purpose of worldly [gain]. Some of the righteous predecessors have said: whoever remembers Allah تعالى for the purpose of a worldly thing or an otherworldly thing, that is his portion from remembrance; and whoever remembers Allah تعالى in devotion and compliance, Allah تعالى gives him what no eye has seen, nor ear heard, nor has it occurred to the heart of a human being, by His saying تعالى: “Whoever is occupied by My remembrance from asking Me, I give him better than what I give the askers” — and Allah singles out for His mercy whom He wills, and Allah is Possessor of the عظیم فضل. And as for His تعالى name الشهيد, it is a عظیم name; whoever persists in it is given testimony at the time of need. And the created king from its number is Nūr Yā’īl عليه السلام, and he is chief over four commanders, under the hand of each commander 219 rows, each row 219 thousand angels from the angels of the witnesses over all the creatures. And when this noble name is mentioned, the angel descends upon him thus as has preceded, and its supplication: you say, O شهيد, You who bore witness to Your own self with oneness, and You are the sign by which You made Your servants know singularity, and You are the One who enabled Your saints in the sciences of the clouds, and You are Knower [~] of the unseen and the witnessed, and You manifest the unseen of creation and the will. I ask You, O Allah, O Light of the light and witness to what is in the breasts, to make clear to me the realities of Your limit and to make plain to me the subtleties of Your glory, and make me a witness for You, returning to You, in Your sea and Your sea; indeed You are the strong, the enduring. *

And as for His تعالى name الحق, it is the sword of Allah on the earth; by it He cut the mountains of falsehood and established the proofs and the arguments — and Allah gives His kingdom to whom He wills, and Allah is All-Encompassing, All-Knowing — and its king is Ṣarfiyā’īl, and he is chief over four commanders, under the hand of each commander 108 rows, each row 108 thousand angels appointed to nullify falsehood. And whoever remembers it, the angel descends upon him and his need is fulfilled. And as for the supplication by it, you say: O Allah, O Truth, You who made the affairs true and illuminated the darknesses of the hearts and breasts, and You who brought forth the secret for manifestation and joy and gladness and intimacy and the delight of rejoicing, and You are the Truth speaking with every tongue. I ask You, O Allah, by Your Beloved and Your Intimate Friend and Your Confidant and Your Chosen One, that You provide me with fulfillment of Your right and compassion toward Your creation; indeed You are Allah, the Great Judge of شأن. *

And as for His تعالى name الوكيل, it is a عظیم name, and its king is Kahyā’īl, and he is chief over four commanders, under the hand of each commander 66 rows, each row 66 thousand angels appointed over everything, and they are the keepers of treasures. And whoever remembers it, the angel descends upon him, and its supplication: you say, O وكيل, You who took charge of the affairs of the creatures, and You who perfected the paths and the realities, and You who built the دقائق and the رقائق, You rose in sufficiency for the servants and hardened Yourself in the will of increase and power, and to You belong enabling and stability. I ask You, O Lord of lords and Causer of causes, that You provide me with increase in strength and perfection in power and light in might and firmness in the horn and a vision by which I attain clarification and a tongue by which I attain البيان; for You are the gatherer of the scattered affairs, and You are able to raise up those in the graves. *

And as for His تعالى name القوي, it is a عظیم name, and the spirit created from it is Wāṭā’īl عليه السلام, and he is chief over four commanders, under the hand of each commander 116 rows, each row 116 thousand angels, and the one who remembers it, the angel descends upon him and his need is fulfilled. And its supplication: you say, O قوي, You who strengthened the seekers of Your presence for ascent, and You who made the people of love know the paths of unveiling and disclosure, and You who illuminated the hearts of Your beloved ones with encompassing and containment. I ask You, O Allah, by Your عظیم authority and the strength of Your شأن and the efficacy of Your proof, that You provide me with strength from You and a power by which I may be able to cut through the deserts of other than You, and support me with Your all-encompassing gentleness so that I find none but You, Allah, O Allah, O Allah, O قوي. *

And as for His تعالى name المتين, it is a عظیم name in which is a letter from the الاسم الأعظم, and its king is Qaṣr Yā’īl, chief over four commanders, under the hand of each commander 000 rows.
