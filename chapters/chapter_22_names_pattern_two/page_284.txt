The prevailing and the might is for God, Hittah, قادر over what You will in the present and the end; there is no existent except You, and everything that You have brought forth of the created things is within under Your subjugation. I ask You by the دقائق of Your hidden gentleness and Your faithful beneficence that You make my soul, with kinds of cultivation, cultivated, and my spirit, with the secrets of knowledges, spread forth, and my heart, with the realities and دقائق of Your names and Your attributes, one, for You, witnessing. O God, I ask You for the لطائف of Your kindness and the successive beneficence of Your beneficence, that You perfect by them my soul in actions and perfect by them my tongue in sayings, and You are the one who makes lawful what You have made unlawful in the cycles, O Qahhar. * And as for His تعالى name, al-Wahhab, it is a عظیم name for whoever seeks the world and the nobility of the Hereafter, and by it Solomon عليه السلام was given, and he is a chief over four commanders; under the hand of each commander are 45 rows, each row 45 thousand angels, and they are from the worlds of Michael عليه السلام. And the one who remembers this name, the angel descends upon him and his need is fulfilled; and analogize upon it whatever you will, you will attain eternal felicity. (O Wahhab) And as for supplication by this noble name, you say: O Wahhab, You bestow the abundant and give the majestic, and You guide Your servants to the abode of felicity without doubt. I ask You by the secret of secrets deposited in the letters of the oath, and by the gifts of Your gentleness included in the oath, and by what You have spread of the لطائف of Your generosity in the determinations of the roots, that You make me returning to You with good intent, preserving right guidance, O You who is in ambush, calling the servants to the Return, O Wahhab. * And as for His تعالى name, al-Razzaq, it is a عظیم name, for God تعالى has never ceased to be Razzaq, and the angel created from its number, Bahwa'il عليه السلام, and his worlds on earth are entrusted with driving the provisions to all creatures. And they are the ones who nurture the crop and the plants. And whoever knows the name of this king and entrusts him with his cultivation or his garden, it bears fruit and ripens and brings forth contrary to the custom of the earth. And he is a chief over four commanders; under the hand of each commander are 308 rows, each row 308 thousand bearers of the green carpet, entrusted with rain and vegetation. And the one who remembers this name, the angel descends upon him and gives him sufficiency, and he becomes spending from the unseen. And know that provision with God is wider than creation, so understand the secret of the Truth in creation and reflect upon that; you will find what I say inscribed in existence. And God is the Giver, the Withholder. And as for supplication by this noble name, you say: O God, You are the Provider for everything that You have brought into existence from Your generosity, and You are the perfecter of what I describe of the life of Your witnessing, and You are the one who sends down their provision from the hidden things of Your knowledge by means of Your heaven and Your earth. I ask You by the hidden things of Your making that You make my existence a place of خیرات and a means of blessings from actions and attributes, and provide me with beneficial knowledge for the النفسية hearts, and a state gathering the human states, and a giving hand for the pleasing gifts, and make me one who takes from You upon the description of الجمع and التفصيل, conveying to Your servants nothing except perfection and being perfected, and reach me with the لطائف of tawhid and the special qualities of success and right guidance; indeed You are effective in what You will. * And as for His تعالى name, al-Fattah, it is a noble name whose knowledge none knows except God تعالى, and by it the inward aspects of matters are opened. And the angel created from its number is Lihya'il عليه السلام, and under his hand are four commanders; under the hand of each commander are 489 rows, each row 489 thousand angels from the noble angels, in their hands are the keys of blessings, and they have no occupation except opening the خیرات upon people. So glory be to the Bestower of gifts. And the one who remembers this name, its number multiplied by itself, with ablution and fasting and spiritual discipline, the angel descends upon him as the foremost, so strive in remembrance, for it is the greatest might. And God is the Giver, the Withholder; there is no god other than Him, nor any worshipped besides Him. And as for supplication by this noble name, you say: O Fattah, You are the one who opens the locks of breasts with the keys of eternal care, and You are the rich, the generous, and You are the generous Giver of Your blessings.
