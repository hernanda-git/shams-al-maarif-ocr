And whoever writes the noble basmalah in the middle of a circle eight times, and writes around it His تعالى saying — Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah and those with him to the end of the surah — and fumigates it with a pleasant fragrance at a fortunate time and carries it, then he becomes feared, عظيما, honored among people, and no one sees him except that he loves him and inclines to him by his nature, and all purposes succeed for him by permission of God تعالى. And whoever writes the entire basmalah, and the moon is in Pisces and the ascendant is fortunate, on gazelle paper and carries it on the head, the number falling upon it, then he will live happily and die a martyr, and he will not see in himself, his wealth, or his child what he dislikes. And know — may God grant me and you success in obedience to Him — that Bism is the concealed name, and God تعالى is the Greatest Name, and al-Rahman al-Rahim is an attribute by which He described Himself, so He is the Merciful of the world and the Merciful of the Hereafter. And “Praise be to God, Lord of the worlds” is opposite to “Bism Allah al-Rahman al-Rahim,” so “Bism Allah” is opposite to “Praise be to God,” “al-Rahman al-Rahim” opposite to “Lord of the worlds.” And know that every attribute of His in His saying “Master of the Day of Judgment” is the manifestation of lordship, for He is King and Owner. And upon you is the clarification of the saying and the lights, and for the request of the Day of Judgment, and by the attributive quality of ownership, so He becomes King of kings and manifested to souls by subjugation and overpowering; and by kingship, so He becomes King of kings and manifested to the hearts by ownership, according to His saying تعالى — with a Powerful Sovereign —. And thus all of it is in Bism Allah al-Rahman al-Rahim. So “Bism Allah,” which is the name, to convey all the comprehensive words to the end by the truth, and to raise the call with the tongue of diversion to God, ascent, and “la-hut” al-Rahman al-Rahim descended to the second beginning. Bism Allah al-Rahman al-Rahim is an ascent to the first beginning. In it is the secret of beginning, and in it are the ranks of tawhid, because Bism is opposite to “I bear witness,” and Allah is opposite to “that there is no god but He.” And in it are the ranks of the angels opposite to al-Rahman, and those endowed with knowledge opposite to al-Rahim.
