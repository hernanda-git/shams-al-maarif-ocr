Beginning
In the name of Allah, the Most Merciful, the Most Compassionate.

The cloud fled to the east, and the winds became still, and the seas became agitated, and the beasts listened to Allah by the permission of Allah, and the devils were pelted from heaven.

And the Lord of Might swore:
“My name is not named upon a sick person except that he is healed, nor upon anything except that it is blessed.”

And Ibn Masʿūd, may Allah Most High be pleased with him, said:
Whoever wants Allah to save him from the nineteen Zabāniyah, let him increase it.

And it is nineteen letters. Every letter is a salvation from each one of them.

And whoever makes much remembrance of it is granted awe among the upper and lower worlds.

And by it the kingdom of Solomon son of David, peace be upon them both, stood.

And whoever writes it one hundred times and carries it, he is granted awe in the hearts.

And it was narrated from ʿAbd Allāh ibn ʿUmar, may Allah Most High be pleased with him, that he said:

Whoever has a need with Allah Most High, let him fast Wednesday, Thursday, and Friday.

Then, when it is Friday, let him bathe, go to the mosque, and give something in charity.

Then, when he has prayed Friday prayer, let him say afterward:
“O Allah, I ask You by Your name, the Most Merciful, the Most Compassionate: Allah, there is no god except Him, the Living, the Self-Sustaining, until the end of the verse, before whom the faces have become humbled, the voices have become subdued, and the hearts have become fearful from His fear. I ask You to send blessings and peace upon our master Muhammad, and upon his family and his companions, and that You fulfill my need, thus and it is such-and-such,”

and he names it.

And he used to say:
“Do not teach it to your fools, lest some of them supplicate against others and it be answered for them at the time.”

And it was narrated from the Prophet, peace and blessings of Allah be upon him, that he said:
“There is nothing between ‘In the name of Allah, the Most Merciful, the Most Compassionate’ and the Greatest Name of Allah except as there is between the whiteness of the eye and its blackness.”

And the Prophet, peace and blessings of Allah be upon him, said:
“There is nothing between human beings and the devils except ‘In the name of Allah, the Most Merciful, the Most Compassionate.’”

So it is the concealed name which indicates that what comes after it is the Greatest Name, and it is Allah.

Because the Greatest Name is the Divine Name (al-jalālah), and it is the pole of the names, and to it they return.

And among the names it is like the proper name, because if you are asked, “From the Merciful?” you say, “Allah.”

And likewise the rest of the names are attributed to Allah and are known through the Divine Name and the loftiness of its elevation.

And it has an additional distinction over the names, which is that when you remove from it the letter alif, there remains لله.

And when you remove from it the first lām, there remains له.

And when you remove the second [lām] also, there remains هو.

Thus every letter of it stands by itself.

And this is not likewise with the other names, because when you remove a letter from them, their meaning becomes void.

And the letters of this Greatest Name have not been disrupted, so it has distinction over the names.

And it is evidence that it is the honored Essence, established in might and permanence.

And it has another distinction indicating the singular Essence of lordship, and indicating the unity of divinity.

For its beginning is the alif, and it is the first of the letters, and the first of the numbers, and the first of the units.

Thus it is singular in its attribute, one in its number.

It points to the oneness of its secret, before which the created things have submitted.

And its end is the letter hāʾ, and it points to the unity of divinity, and it is not found in the other names.

And it says with the tongue of its state:
“I am the First and the Last, and the Manifest and the Hidden.”

Then He followed it with the attribute of Mercifulness.

The Most High said:
“Say: Call upon Allah or call upon the Most Merciful. Whichever you call upon...”

So He gives you the choice between saying:
“O Allah”

or:
“O Most Merciful,”

for it combines the two merciful attributes.

And all of them are a noble name.

If you wish to seek mercy, you say:
“O Most Merciful,”

and it is the more particular, because Allah Most High is the most particular of the names and the greatest of them, by agreement.

And it is a Syriac name.

As for its interpretation, it is that He brings things out from non-existence into existence.

And it has other meanings.

It is necessary for the observer concerning them to restrain them from the fools, lest they arrive at committing reprehensible things and prohibitions.

Then he falls before Allah, like Bāʿūrā, when Allah Most High desired disobedience for him.

We seek refuge in Allah Most High from His anger.
“O Allah, do not make us among those who use His names for disobedience to Him.”

And this name has four letters:

alif, two lām’s, and hāʾ,

because the natures are four, and the directions are four:

east, west, north, and south.

And the angels of glorification are four:

Jibrīl, Mīkāʾīl, Isrāfīl, and ʿAzrāʾīl.

As for Jibrīl, he is the possessor of the message to the messengers, and the possessor of dominance and overpowering, and through him Allah Most High destroyed the unbelievers among the nations and the [al-qadhf].

And Isrāfīl is the possessor of the Trumpet and the blowing.

He has three blowings:

The blowing of terror.

Allah Most High said:
“And the blowing of the one who is in the heavens and the one who is on the earth...”

And the blowing of the swoon.

Allah Most High said:
“And the one who is in the heavens and the one who is on the earth was struck down...”

And the blowing of resurrection.

Allah Most High said:
“Then it was blown into it another time, and behold, they were standing, looking.”

Thus each blowing has matters particular to it.

And ʿAzrāʾīl, peace be upon him, is entrusted with taking the souls and their passing away.

And in it is the subduing of the tyrants and the cutting off of the end of the unbelievers and the arrogant.

And in it is the comfort of the believer and his arrival to his Lord, and his joy at what Allah has prepared for him from His generosity, His bounty, His pardon, and His forgiveness.

And Mīkāʾīl, peace be upon him, is entrusted with the provisions of the servants, and the sustaining of their breath, and the preservation of their existence.

There is not upon the earth a sesame seed except that there is an assistant from the angels entrusted with what belongs to its possessor.

And Jibrīl, peace be upon him, has assistants that cannot be counted.

And he has remembrances and deeds distinguishing them.

And these four have kings [and] days particular to them.

For Jibrīl, peace be upon him, is Monday, because it is cold and moist.

And for Isrāfīl, peace be upon him, is Thursday, and it is hot and moist.

And for ʿAzrāʾīl, peace be upon him, is Saturday, because it is cold and moist, and its nature is earth, death, and passing away.

And for Mīkāʾīl, peace be upon him, is Wednesday, and it is mixed from the four natures.

And they have four wafaqs particular to them:

the sevenfold for Jibrīl, peace be upon him;

the fourfold for Isrāfīl, peace be upon him;

the threefold for ʿAzrāʾīl, peace be upon him;

and the eightfold for Mīkāʾīl, peace be upon him.
