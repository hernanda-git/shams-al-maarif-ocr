Whoever wishes to see the outcome of his affair, let him fast that day purely for Allah, the Exalted, and break his fast with whatever bread is available. Let him recite Sūrat al-Mulk, and sleep in a state of ritual purity, upon his right side. Let him place [something] beneath his head, and let him not speak to anyone, and then sleep. For Allah, the Exalted, will show him the outcome of his affair, according to the measure of the oath that he intended.

This is suitable only for the people of purity of hearts and the people of spiritual disciplines.

Whoever writes it in a glass vessel and drinks it, Allah will make understanding and wisdom easy for him. Whoever hangs it upon himself, Allah, the Exalted, will make him speak with wisdom.

Whoever writes it together with There is no god but Allah; Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah, eighty times, and carries it upon his right upper arm, or wraps it in a garment and wears it, Allah, the Exalted, will grant him awe and [companionship / unclear].

And if you wish to take as brothers from among the believing jinn, who will fulfill your needs and assist you in your difficulties, begin by fasting on Wednesday until the fourth Saturday thereafter, after washing the garment and the body.

Recite Sūrat al-Ikhlāṣ one thousand times every day, Sūrat Yā-Sīn once, Sūrat al-Dukhān, and [the verses of] the prostration, and Tabāraka alladhī bi-yadihi al-mulk (Blessed is He in Whose Hand is the Kingdom) once.

Then, when it is the afternoon of Saturday, which is the tenth hour, withdraw from the people into an empty place, in a clean location.

Take seven pieces of camphor and write upon them as follows:
- Upon the first: His Most High saying: "And He is the One who gives life and causes death, and to Him belongs the alternation of the night and the day."
- Upon the second: His Most High saying: "When He decrees a matter, He only says to it, 'Be,' and it is."
- Upon the third: His Most High saying: "Allah holds them. Indeed, He is the All-Hearing, the All-Knowing."
- Upon the fourth: His Most High saying: "Who is it that can intercede with Him except by His permission?"
- Upon the fifth: "Then they will emerge from the graves, hastening toward their Lord."
- Upon the sixth: "And the Trumpet will be blown," until His saying: "Then they will be standing, looking."
- Upon the seventh: "The Day when they emerge from the graves, swiftly," [and] "Allah will suffice you against them, and He is the All-Hearing, the All-Knowing."
After that, pray four rakʿahs. Recite al-Fātiḥah and Yā-Sīn in the first, and al-Dukhān in the second, and in the third al-Fātiḥah, al-Sajdah, and Tabāraka al-Mulk.

At the end of every prostration, say:

"Glory be to Him who is not [possessed] of might, and who is [possessed] of glory. Glory be to Him who is adorned with majesty and honored through it. Glory be to Him who has encompassed everything through His knowledge. Glory be to Him to whom glorification alone is due. Glory be to Him who, when He desires a thing, says to it, 'Be,' and it is. Glory be to Him who, when He desires a thing, it is, and what He does not will does not come to be. Glory be to the Possessor of bounty, favor, and blessings. Glory be to the Possessor of knowledge and forbearance. Glory be to the Possessor of abundance and favor. Glory be to the Possessor of the Throne, the Tablet, the Pen, and the Light."

Then raise your head and say:

"O Allah, indeed I ask You by the foundations of might of Your Throne, and by the utmost extent of mercy in Your Book. And I ask You by Your Greatest, Most Great Name, and by Your Most Noble, Most Generous Face, and by Your complete words, to subject to me those among the righteous jinn who will assist me in whatever I desire from the needs of this world.

For indeed, seven persons from among their nobles and their dignitaries will appear to you, and they will greet you and consult you concerning your affair.

Before reciting the Names, attach seven slips to yourself on a thread, like a conical headpiece, and place it upon your head before beginning the prayer.

There should also be wax with you. Take the seven slips from among those which you have written, recite over them, and say: 'O Master of this [rite] and the possessor of this [rite]...'"

Then write his name upon the slip, and say: "O so-and-so, I ask you..." Take the thread and the wax, and seal it with them beneath the slip, like the written seal.

Then say: "One by one," until you reach the seventh. Then say: "I have divided among you in this slip these Names, the greatest Names."

Then complete your supplication and say: "If you obey me, then I have appointed you [to this task]." Then say: "May Allah bless you, and may Allah reward you."

Raise those slips and the enclosed slip in a clean place until you have fulfilled your need of food, drink, or any other requirement.

Then summon them, and they will come to you quickly, by the permission of Allah, the Exalted. And beware lest you be among those who are not strong [in this matter].
