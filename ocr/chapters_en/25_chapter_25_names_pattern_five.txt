Know that among the beautiful names of Allah are: the Most High, the Great, the Majestic, the Great, the Light, the Radiant, Possessor of majesty and honor.
The rememberer of this pattern will not be, in his time, more repelling in rank with the kings and the sultans and the great people, and they hasten

and dignity, and his aspiration and his spirit rise, and his soul becomes pure. And whoever is granted success in their numbers in a square upon a ring in the exaltation of the sun
and seals with it, no one looks at him except that he loves him, and if enemies see him, Allah casts terror into their hearts. * And as for His name, الجليل, whoever remembers it
humans, jinn, beasts, and vermin fear him. * And as for His تعالى name, النور البهي, the light of its secret spreads in the heart of
