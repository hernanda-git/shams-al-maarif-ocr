Uses of the letters and their retreats upon the sentences and the details (the thirty-ninth chapter) in the explanation of the Beautiful Names of God
as they came by way of elucidation and details (the fortieth chapter) in the single supplications by which one is supplicated in
all the nights and times

(The first chapter concerning the dotted letters and what is in them of secrets and hidden meanings)

I say, and with God is success and guidance, the aims of the seekers have been divided into two parts, worldly and otherworldly, and each
one of them is divided into sections according to the purposes. And people have spoken concerning the opposition of the times and the standing upon the stars
and the exercises and the acts of talismans before the setting down of this book and the discourse upon it. And this science is expansive; many
creatures have desired it, and upon it especially whoever found for that a great effect. So I desired to oppose that by a description that proceeds in the manner of the sense in the aspects of
the people of this science. And the early sages spoke concerning it, and many people agreed to that saying. So those, if they are many
in the world, harm in the Hereafter. And this which I mention to you, you benefit by it in the world and the Hereafter.
(Chapter concerning the mention of the dotted letters: are they the roots of speech and its foundation, and by them its building is raised)
(And know) that for numbers there are secrets just as for letters there are effects, and that the upper world extends to the lower world; so the world of the Throne extends to the world of
the Kursi, and the world of the Kursi extends to the sphere of Saturn, and the sphere of Saturn extends to the sphere of Jupiter, and the sphere of Jupiter extends to the sphere of Mars, and the sphere of Mars
extends to the sphere of the Sun, and the sphere of the Sun extends to the sphere of Venus, and the sphere of Venus extends to the sphere of Mercury, and the sphere of Mercury extends to the sphere of the Moon,
and the sphere of the Moon extends to the sphere of heat, and the sphere of heat extends to the sphere of moisture, and the sphere of moisture extends to the sphere of cold, and the sphere of
cold extends to the sphere of dryness, and the sphere of dryness extends to the sphere of air, and the sphere of air extends to the sphere of water, and the sphere of water extends to the sphere of
earth, and the sphere of earth extends to the sphere of Saturn. In the upper things is the letter jim, and the numbers falling upon it are three
in general; and as for in detail, then fifty-three, thus the mim with four and the ba with three. And it
also is with three letters, and for it among the lower things is the letter sad, and it is in number ninety, and those in the upper things in general are five,
and it is the letter ha. And for it among the fivefold accords and the sphere of Jupiter is six, and it is the letter waw. And for it among the sixfold accords
(And as for) the inflection of the sphere of Venus, for it is the letter zay, and for it among the sevenfold accords. And as for the inflection of the sphere of Mercury, for it is
among the number eight, and it is the letter ha, and for it among the eightfold accords. (And as for) the inflection of the sphere of the Moon, for it is among the number
nine, and it is the letter ta, and for it among the ninefold accords. And Saturn has the triangle famous among the scholars, so understand that.
(Chapter concerning the relation of the human essence)
So the Throne has the letter alif, and the Kursi has the letter ba, and Saturn has the letter jim, and thus to the Moon as has preceded.
(Chapter) And the letters are of kinds: among them is that by which one begins on the right, and they are the letters of the Arabs; and among them is that by which one begins
on the left, and they are the Roman, the Greek, and the Coptic. And every writing on the right is connected, and every writing on the left
is separate, so understand that. (And the letters) are twenty-eight, other than lam-alif, and it is the completion of the twenty-nine, and that is
the number of the lunar mansions. And when the mansions, from which fourteen appear above the earth, were such, these letters
among them are what is assimilated with the lam of the definition, and they are fourteen letters: t th d dh r z s sh s d t z l n

شَكْلُهُ الْعَدَدِيُّ وَحَمْلُهُ مَعَهُ أَمَّنَهُ اللَّهُ تَعَالَى مِنَ الْأَعْدَاءِ
الْمُضِرِّينَ مِنْ أَيِّ الْعَوَالِمِ كَانُوا وَهَذِهِ صُورَتُهُ
( وَأَمَّا الْحَرْفِيُّ ) خَاصِيَّتُهُ يَذْهَبُ النِّسْيَانَ وَيُحِدُّ الْفَهْمَ وَالْعَقْلَ
لِمَنِ اسْتَدَامَ شُرْبَهُ فِي مَاءِ الْمَطَرِ وَعَسَلِ نَحْلٍ وَيَنْفَعُ لِمَنْ يَشْتَكِي
صَدْرَهُ وَأَذَانَهُ، وَالْقَمَرُ فِي الْعَقْرَبِ وَالْمِرِّيخُ يَنْظُرُ إِلَيْهِ نَظَرَ عَدَاوَةٍ فِي لَوْحٍ مِنْ نُحَاسٍ وَيَحْمِيهِ مِنَ الدَّارِ فَهُوَ عَظِيمٌ
لِلْدَغِ الْعَقْرَبِ إِذَا سُقِيَ مَاؤُهُ بَعْدَ أَنْ يُغْمَسَ الْخَاتَمُ فِيهِ وَلَمَّا كَانَ هَذَا الشَّكْلُ الْمُرَبَّعُ بِمَجْمُوعِ الْأَلِفَاتِ الْأَرْبَعَةِ الَّتِي هِيَ
سِرُّ الْعَقْلِ وَسِرُّ الرُّوحِ وَسِرُّ النَّفْسِ وَسِرُّ الْقَلْبِ فَالْأَلِفُ فِي الْعَدَدِ وَاحِدٌ وَإِذَا ضُرِبَتِ الْأَرْبَعَةُ فِي نَفْسِهَا بَلَغَتْ سِتَّةَ
عَشَرَ وَهُوَ انْتِهَاءُ الْعَدَدِ التَّفْصِيلِيِّ لِأَنَّ الْعَرْشَ وَالْكُرْسِيَّ وَالسَّمَوَاتِ السَّبْعَ وَالْأَرَضِينَ السَّبْعَ الْجُمْلَةَ سِتَّةَ
عَشَرَ وَهَذَا الْعَدَدُ هُوَ انْتِهَاءُ هَذَا الشَّكْلِ الْمُرَبَّعِ الَّذِي هُوَ سِتَّةَ عَشَرَ يَتَأَلَّفُ السِّتَّةَ عَشَرَ شَفْعَةً الْأَرْبَعَةَ عَشَرَ
وَهِيَ السَّمَوَاتُ السَّبْعُ وَالْأَرَضُونَ السَّبْعُ وَفِيهِ شَفْعَةُ الْبُرُوجِ وَهِيَ اثْنَا عَشَرَ وَفِيهِ شَفْعَةُ الثَّمَانِيَةِ وَهُمْ حَمَلَةُ
الْعَرْشِ وَفِيهِ شَفْعَةُ السِّتَّةِ وَهِيَ الْحُدُودُ الثَّمَانِيَةُ وَهِيَ فَوْقَ وَتَحْتَ وَخَلْفَ وَأَمَامَ وَيَمِينٍ وَشِمَالٍ وَفِيهِ شَفْعَةُ
الْأَرْبَعَةِ وَهِيَ شَفْعَةُ النَّبِيِّينَ وَالصِّدِّيقِينَ وَالشُّهَدَاءِ وَالصَّالِحِينَ وَفِيهِ شَفْعَةُ الْآيَتَيْنِ وَهِيَ شَهَادَةُ أَنْ لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ
وَأَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا رَسُولُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ فَهَذِهِ سَبْعَةُ أَشْفَاعٍ وَفِيهِ مِنَ الْوَتَرِيَّةِ وَتَرُ الْخَمْسَةِ عَشَرَ وَهِيَ الْكُرْسِيُّ
وَالسَّمَوَاتُ وَالْأَرَضُونَ وَفِيهِ وَتَرُ الثَّلَاثَةَ عَشَرَ وَهِيَ الْقَلَمُ وَاللَّوْحُ وَالصُّورُ وَرُوحُ الْقُدُسِ وَالْكُرْسِيُّ وَالْعَرْشُ
وَالسَّمَوَاتُ السَّبْعُ وَفِيهِ وَتَرُ الْإِحْدَى عَشَرَ وَهِيَ مَا فِي عَالَمِ الْإِنْسَانِ مِنَ الْحَوَاسِّ الْخَمْسِ وَهِيَ السَّمْعُ وَالْبَصَرُ
وَالشَّمُّ وَالذَّوْقُ وَاللَّمْسُ وَالْجِهَاتُ السِّتُّ وَالْفَوْقُ وَالتَّحْتُ وَالْيَمِينُ وَالشِّمَالُ وَالْخَلْفُ وَالْأَمَامُ وَفِيهِ وَتَرِيَّةُ التِّسْعَةِ
وَهِيَ ذَوَاتُ الْإِنْسَانِ وَطَبَائِعُهُ الثَّمَانِيَةُ وَهِيَ الْحَرَارَةُ وَالْيُبُوسَةُ وَالْبُرُودَةُ وَالرُّطُوبَةُ فَالصَّفْرَاءُ حَارَّةٌ يَابِسَةٌ
وَالْهَوَاءُ حَارٌّ رَطْبٌ وَهُوَ طَبْعُ الدَّمِ وَالْبَلْغَمُ طَبْعُهُ بَارِدٌ رَطْبٌ وَالسَّوْدَاءُ بَارِدَةٌ يَابِسَةٌ فَهَذِهِ ثَمَانِيَةٌ مُنْفَصِلَةٌ
وَفِيهِ وَتَرُ السَّبْعَةِ وَهِيَ وَتَرُ الْأَفْلَاكِ السَّبْعَةِ فَلَكَ زُحَلَ وَالْمُشْتَرِي وَالْمِرِّيخِ وَالشَّمْسِ وَالزُّهْرَةِ وَعُطَارِدَ
وَالْقَمَرِ وَوَتَرُ الْأَيَّامِ السَّبْعَةِ وَالسَّمَوَاتِ وَالْأَرَضِينَ السَّبْعِ وَكُلُّ مَسْبَعٍ وَفِيهِ وَتَرُ الْأَشْفَاعِ وَهِيَ الصَّلَوَاتُ الْخَمْسُ
وَفِيهِ وَتَرُ الثَّلَاثَةِ وَهِيَ الدُّورُ الثَّلَاثَةُ دَارُ الدُّنْيَا وَدَارُ الْبَرْزَخِ وَدَارُ الْآخِرَةِ وَفِيهِ وَتَرُ الْوَاحِدِ وَهِيَ وَتَرُ الْعَقْلِ
فَاجْمَعْ فِي السِّتَّةَ عَشَرَ ثَمَانِيَةَ أَشْفَاعٍ وَثَمَانِيَةَ أَوْتَارٍ وَكُلُّ شَفْعٍ يَتَلَقَّى كَلَوْتٍ وَكَلَوْتٍ وَيَتَلَقَّى كُلُّ شَفْعٍ * مِثَالُ
ذَلِكَ وَاحِدٌ وَاحِدٌ اثْنَانِ وَثَلَاثَةٌ وَسِتَّةٌ وَهَكَذَا إِلَى آخِرِهِ وَكَذَلِكَ الْأَشْفَاعُ * وَأَمَّا الشَّكْلُ الْعَدَدِيُّ بِالْقَلَمِ
الطَّبِيعِيِّ وَهُوَ الْهِنْدِيُّ عَلَى مَا يَأْتِي بَيَانُهُ وَهَذَا الْقَلَمُ أَوْلَى بِهِ وَأَحَقُّ وَلَهُ فِي الْحُرُوفِ أَسْرَارٌ عَجِيبَةٌ وَذَلِكَ أَنْ
تَضَعَ شَكْلًا وَمَكَانَ الْأَعْدَادِ حُرُوفًا وَيَكُونُ بَعْدَ صِيَامٍ أُسْبُوعَيْنِ لِأَنَّ كُلَّ فِيهَا لَيْلًا إِلَّا الْجُزْءَ وَحْدَهُ
بِشَرْطِ الرِّيَاضَةِ وَدَوَامِ الطَّهَارَةِ وَذِكْرِ اللَّهِ تَعَالَى ثُمَّ اعْمَدْ إِلَى صَفِيحَةٍ مُرَبَّعَةٍ مِنْ قَصْدِيرٍ مُصَفَّى مَنْقُوشٍ
عَلَيْهَا هَذَا الشَّكْلُ الْحَرْفِيُّ وَأَنْتَ مُسْتَقْبِلُ الْقِبْلَةِ بَعْدَ الصَّلَاةِ رَكْعَتَيْنِ بِالْفَاتِحَةِ وَآيَةِ الْكُرْسِيِّ مَرَّةً وَالْإِخْلَاصِ
مِائَةَ مَرَّةٍ فِي سَاعَةِ الْمُشْتَرِي عِنْدَ طُلُوعِ الشَّمْسِ وَالْقَمَرُ مَحْفُوظٌ مِنَ الْمُشْتَرِي وَالشَّمْسِ وَالطَّالِعِ
الْجَوْزَاءِ وَبَخِّرْهُ بِالْمَصْطَكَى وَالصَّنْدَلِ الْأَبْيَضِ كُلَّ يَوْمٍ خَمِيسٍ فَلَابِسُ هَذَا الْخَاتَمِ يَسْهُلُ اللَّهُ لَهُ أُمُورَ الدُّنْيَا
وَالْآخِرَةِ وَالدِّيَانَةِ وَيُوَفِّقُهُ لِلْأَعْمَالِ الصَّالِحَةِ وَالطَّاعَاتِ وَتَيَسَّرَ لَهُ أَسْبَابُ الرِّزْقِ وَيُبَارِكُ اللَّهُ تَعَالَى فِيمَا بَيْنَ
يَدَيْهِ (وَمَنْ كَتَبَهُ) وَوَضَعَهُ فِي دُكَانِهِ أَوْ صُنْدُوقِهِ كَثُرَ مَالُهُ وَرِزْقُهُ وَيَكُونُ مَعَهُ كَلَامُهُ الْمَخْصُوصُ بِهِ طَلَبُ الرِّزْقِ

English:
— 9 —

Its numerical form, and carrying it with him, God Most High secured him from the harmful enemies, from whichever of the worlds they were, and this is its form.

(And as for the literal one), its property is: it takes away forgetfulness and sharpens understanding and intellect for whoever continues drinking it in rainwater and honey of bees, and it benefits for whoever complains of his chest and his ears. And the moon is in Scorpio, and Mars looks toward it with a look of enmity, in a tablet of copper, and it protects him from the house, so it is عظیم for the sting of Scorpio if its water is drunk after the ring is dipped in it. And since this square form is with the total of the four alifs, which are the secret of the intellect and the secret of the spirit and the secret of the soul and the secret of the heart, then the alif in the number is one, and if the four are multiplied by themselves they reach sixteen, and it is the end of the detailed number, because the Throne and the Footstool and the seven heavens and the seven earths, the total, [is] sixteen. And this number is the end of this square form, which is sixteen; the sixteen is composed of fourteen pairs, and they are the seven heavens and the seven earths, and in it is the pair of the signs, and they are twelve, and in it is the pair of the eighth, and they are the bearers of the Throne, and in it is the pair of six, and they are the eight limits, and they are above and below and behind and before and right and left, and in it is the pair of four, and they are the pair of the prophets and the truthful and the martyrs and the righteous, and in it is the pair of the two verses, and they are the testimony that there is no god except God and that Muhammad is the Messenger of God, peace be upon him. So these are seven pairs. And in it is from the oddness the odd of fifteen, and they are the Footstool and the heavens and the earths. And in it is the odd of thirteen, and they are the Pen and the Tablet and the Sur and the Holy Spirit and the Footstool and the Throne and the seven heavens. And in it is the odd of eleven, and they are what is in the world of man of the five senses, and they are hearing and sight and smell and taste and touch, and the six directions, and above and below and right and left and behind and before. And in it is the oddness of nine, and they are the entities of man and his eight natures, and they are heat and dryness and coldness and moisture. So the yellow bile is hot, dry, and the air is hot, moist, and it is the nature of blood, and phlegm, its nature is cold, moist, and black bile is cold, dry. So these are eight separate. And in it is the odd of seven, and it is the odd of the seven spheres: the sphere of Saturn, and Jupiter, and Mars, and the Sun, and Venus, and Mercury, and the Moon; and the odd of the seven days, and the seven heavens, and the seven earths, and every sevenfold. And in it is the odd of the pairs, and they are the five prayers. And in it is the odd of three, and they are the three abodes: the abode of the world, and the abode of the barzakh, and the abode of the Hereafter. And in it is the odd of one, and it is the odd of the intellect.

So gather in the sixteen eight pairs and eight odds, and every pair receives as an odd and as an odd, and every pair receives * Example of that: one, one, two, and three, and six, and thus to its end, and likewise the pairs. * And as for the numerical form with the natural pen, and it is the Indian, according to what the explanation of it will come, and this pen is more fitting for it and more deserving, and in letters there are wondrous secrets. And that is that you place a form and in the place of the numbers letters, and it is after a fast of two weeks, because every one of them is a night except the part alone, on the condition of discipline and the دوام of purity and the remembrance of God Most High. Then proceed to a square sheet of refined tin, engraved upon it this literal form, while you are facing the qiblah, after two rak‘ahs of prayer with al-Fatiha and Ayat al-Kursi once and al-Ikhlas one hundred times, in the hour of Jupiter at sunrise, and the moon is محفوظ from Jupiter and the sun and the rising sign of Gemini. And fumigate it with mastic and white sandalwood every Thursday. So the wearer of this ring, God makes easy for him the affairs of the world and the Hereafter and religion, and He grants him success for righteous deeds and acts of obedience, and the means of provision are made easy for him, and God Most High blesses what is between his hands. (And whoever writes it) and places it in his shop or his صندوق, his wealth and his provision become abundant, and there will be with him his speech, the one specified by it: the seeking of provision.
