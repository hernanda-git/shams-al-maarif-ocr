لأربع خلون من تموز وطلوع النثرة لسبع عشرة ليلة خلت منه، طلوع نجم الشعرى العبور
وطلوع الطرفة لأول ليلة من آب، وطلوع الجبهة لأربع عشرة ليلة خلت منه، وطلوع الزبرة لسبع
وعشرين منه، وطلوع الصرفة لثمان خلون من أيلول، وطلوع العواء لتسع عشرة ليلة خلت منه،
وطلوع السماك لليلتين بقيتا منه، وطلوع الإكليل لاثنتي عشرة ليلة خلت من تشرين الثاني، وطلوع
القلب لخمس وعشرين منه، وطلوع الشولة لثمان عشرة من كانون أول، وطلوع النعائم لإحدى
وعشرين منه، وطلوع البلدة لثلاث خلون من كانون الثاني، وطلوع الذابح لإحدى عشرة ليلة منه،
وطلوع سعد السعود لسبع وعشرين ليلة خلت منه، وطلوع سعد الأخبية لإحدى عشرة ليلة
خلت من شباط، وطلوع سعد بلع لست وعشرين منه، وطلوع الفرغ لليلتين خلتا من
آذار، وطلوع الفرغ المؤخر لأربع وعشرين خلت منه، وطلوع الرشا لأربع من نيسان، والله أعلم.
( فصل في أقسام هذه المنازل على الفصول الأربعة )
اعلم أن لفصل الربيع الشرطين والبطين والثريا والدبران والهقعة والهنعة والذراع، ولفصل
الصيف النثرة والطرفة والجبهة والزبرة والصرفة والعواء، ولفصل الخريف الغفر والزبانان
والإكليل والقلب والشولة والنعائم والبلدة، ولفصل الشتاء سعد السعود وسعد الذابح وسعد
الأخبية وبلع والفرغان والمؤخر والرشا، ولكل فصل سبع منازل.
( فصل في أسجاع العرب المتعلقة بالمنازل وما نقل عن الأوائل )
قال بعضهم: قرأت على شيخنا الكندي رحمه الله تعالى، قال: قرأت على أبي منصور
الخولاني، قال: بلغني عن أبي محمد المناوي أنه قال: تقول العرب: إذا طلع الشرطين استوى الرمان،
واخضرت الأغصان، وعمرت الأوطان، وتهادى الجيران، وبات الفقير بكل مكان. وإذا طلع البطين
انقضى الدين. وإذا طلعت الثريا ابغ لراعيك كساءً، وإذا طلعت له غداً ابغ له سقاءً. وإذا طلع
الدبران توقدت النيران ويبست الغدران. وإذا طلعت الهقعة رجعت الناس عن الفحمة. وإذا
طلعت الهنعة انقطعوا إلى المنعة. وإذا طلع الذراع حسرت الشمس الشعاع وتوقد الشراب بكل قاع.
وإذا طلعت النثرة ضعف العجل بكثرة ولم ينل في دره قطرة. وإذا طلعت الطرفة سهل أبو الضيف بخفة.
وإذا طلعت العواء فرت الحيات وطاب الهواء. وإذا طلع السماك كثر على المال الملاك. وإذا طلع الفرغان
والنسر فاضت الغدران وسال جسر. وإذا طلع الزبانان أخذ كل معيان. وإذا طلع الإكليل بطل المقاليل.
وإذا طلع القلب هان كل صعب. وإذا طلعت الشولة أجلت الشيخ البولة. وإذا طلعت النعائم حصل النعم
إلى كل قائم. وإذا طلعت البلدة فأكلك العصيدة وهو ما تخرج من الزبد والسمن من أسفل القدر.
وإذا طلع الذابح حمى عديل الناح. وإذا طلع الأخبية حب الناس لبس الأقبية. وإذا طلع بلع صارت
الأرض لمعاً. وإذا طلع الشولا [~] رر الكلا. وإذا طلع الفرغ المقدم فاخدم ولا تندم. وإذا طلع الفرغ
المؤخر فأسرع ولا تؤخر. وإذا طلع السمكة أمكنت الحركة وتعلقت الحسكة. وهذه أسجاع العرب
ذكرنا طرفاً منها على تمام الكلام على المنازل، والله أعلم.

And we have mentioned the doctrine of the ancients concerning the form of the spheres and what is connected to them. As for the doctrine of the legal scholars, and they are heavens with them, and there were narrated in the hadith reports from Ibn ‘Abbas, and it was said [from] al-‘Abbas ibn ‘Abd al-Muttalib: he said, We were with the Prophet, may God bless him and grant him peace, at al-Batha’, and a cloud passed by us, and the Prophet, may God bless him and grant him peace, said, Do you know what this is? We said, the cloud. He said, and al-muzn? We said, al-muzn. He said, and al-‘anan? We said, al-‘anan, and we were silent. Then the Prophet, may God bless him and grant him peace, said, Do you know how much is between the heaven and the earth? We said, God and His Messenger know best. He said, Between them is the journey of five hundred years, and the thickness of each heaven, that is, its thickness, is five hundred, and above the seventh heaven is a sea, between its top and its bottom as between the heaven and the earth, then above them is a world, and He has called them, like the son of the heaven and the earth, and God تعالى is above that, and nothing of the deeds of the children of Adam is hidden from Him, and the proof for it is His saying تعالى: God is He who created seven heavens and of the earth the like of them, so the distance of the whole becomes the journey of these children of Adam, and as for the angel, he traverses the whole in one hour, and likewise the devil reaches the earth before the angel in the heaven. And it was narrated that Abu Razin al-‘Uqayli, may God تعالى be pleased with him, was asked how much is between the heaven and the earth, and he said, An answered supplication. And it was said to him, how much is between the east and the west? He said, The journey of a day. And al-Tha‘labi mentioned from Ibn ‘Abbas: he said, The sun rises every year upon three hundred and sixty windows, and the uppermost window does not rise that day until the coming year. I say: and in the sun are benefits and indications, and among the indications is that it is one and its light shines upon all the horizons and all the world; likewise the Creator, glory and exaltation be to Him, is one and He manages the world. Then the second: that the benefits of the sun are distant, its reaching is near, and God تعالى is distant from creation in essence, near in response. The third: that its light is not withheld from anyone, and for that the provision of God is not withheld from any of creation. And the fourth: that its eclipse is a proof of the existence of the Resurrection and other than it of what indicates its darkness. The fifth: the cloud covers it, and for that al-ghashiyah covered the knowledge. And as for its benefits, they are very many. The first: that it is the lamp of the world. The Exalted said: And We made the sun a lamp. The second: that it is the ripening for their foods and ripening for their fruits, and without burden. The third: that it travels from the east to the west for their welfare. The fourth: that it does not stop in one place lest it harm creation. The fifth: that it is in winter in the lowest of the signs and in summer in their highest for the benefits of the world. The sixth: that it does not gather with the moon in its authority lest each one of them nullify the light of the other. If you say: it is in the fourth sphere, and the heavens did not veil it, yet the clouds veil it, I say: the heavens are subtle, transparent substances, and the cloud is dense because it rises from the earth, and God knows best.

(Chapter on the moon and its similitudes and what is in it)

It was narrated from Abu Hurayrah that he said: The Prophet, may God bless him and grant him peace, said, Do you see the full moon and there is no cloud beneath it? They said, Yes. He said, Then do you dispute concerning the sun, there is no cloud beneath it? They said, Yes. He, may God bless him and grant him peace, said, Then indeed you see it likewise. If you say, Why was the example not struck with the sun, since it is brighter and more complete in light, and the moon, its light is from it? I say: for two matters. The first: that the light of the sun overpowers the eyes, so one is not able to look at it. And the second: that it was broken for the sake of the Truth, so God تعالى mended it. And that was when Gabriel struck the light of the moon with his wing, its heart was broken because it was resembling the light of the sun, so God تعالى mended it with two things: one of them is that He made the eyes look at it in the world every month, and the second is that He commanded His Prophet Muhammad, may God bless him and grant him peace, to strike by it the example in the greatest things and the highest of them. If you say, God تعالى said: The eyes do not perceive Him, while He perceives the eyes, I say: we do not claim that the eyes perceive Him in the sense of encompassing Him, but rather the perceived is a ray

the southern forms, they are three hundred and twelve stars, among them Cetus, twenty-two stars, and its form is the form of an animal
bare and two legs and a tail like the tail of the whale; and among them is Orion, thirty-eight stars, and its form is a man running and his hand
a staff and in his middle a belt and a sword, and among his stars is al-jawzāʾ, a red star; and among them are twelve stars gathered
under the leg of Orion, resembling a rabbit whose face is toward the west and its rear toward the east; and among them is the Great Dog, eight
and twenty stars, and what is outside the form is eleven behind the stars of al-jawzāʾ, among them al-sunbulah from the stars of
al-shiʿrā al-ʿabūr and it is called al-abbān al-marzbān; and among them is al-shiʿrā al-ghumaysāʾ, and they are the stars that rise after
al-jawzāʾ; as for Cancer, the forms that are in al-jawzāʾ and al-shiʿrā al-ghumaysāʾ that are in the arm, and the Arabs claim that they are
the two sisters of Suhayl; and among them is the Southern Crown, and it is thirteen stars, and what is outside the form is six stars,
and its form is a great fish, their stars upon the south of the star al-dālī, its head toward the east and its tail toward the west,
and among them is the Milky Way upon the south of ḥazzāt al-ʿa [~], so this is the total of the southern and northern stars. I say:
and this which some of them mentioned is restricted to the famous stars, and as for the غير famous, they are very many,
so mention of them will come later, if God تعالى wills.
