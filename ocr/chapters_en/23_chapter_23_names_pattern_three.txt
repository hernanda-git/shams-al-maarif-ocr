For Your Essence I ask You, by the secret of Your Friend in the station of exaltation and by the light of Your self-subsistence over the abodes of moderation, that You spread over my heart and my spirit the pouring of provisions, and that You bring forth from my soul the آثار of disbelief and hypocrisy, O You in Whose hand is the covenant of the pact on the Day of Meeting. O Allah, make me expanded in every constricted thing and presented before You in the inward of every presented thing, and provide me, by Your عظیم فضل, with the universality from the secret of the outpouring, and from the open manifestation of constriction a grasp, and from the lights of expansion a quickening. Regard me with the آثار of Your mercy in the worlds, and attain the آثار of Your tenderness; surely You are ancient in beneficence, O Qabid. * As for His تعالى name al-Basit, it is عظیم. So when it is mentioned, constriction is strengthened against him; his thought and his kingdom become expanded. Its ملك is Bitya'il, and he is chief over four commanders; under the hand of each commander are 72 ranks, each rank 72 thousand angels for expansion and generosity. Whoever remembers it, the angel descends upon him and his need is fulfilled. As for his supplication, you say: O Allah, You are the One who expands the spirits in the bodies whose essences are, and You are the One who gathers in the heart and the heart of the commanders, my secret: I am for Allah, there is no god except I, on the Day of the calling. I ask You by Your جامع secret and Your shining light, by every heard thing and hearer, that You provide me with insight into the ranks of Your presence in existence, by the secrets which You inserted in the Praised Station, and expand my heart in the land of the greater wilayah, and spread my secret for attaining the realities of the آثار of the Beautiful Names, and make me expanded by spending, disposing in the خزائن of provisions, O You in Whose hand is الحكم absolutely, and with Whom are the creatures, O Basit. * As for His تعالى name al-Khafid, it is a name in which there is a secret for one whose aversion and kingdom are increased; its ملك is ʿAykā'īl, and he is chief over four commanders; under the hand of each commander are 1481 ranks, each rank 1481 thousand of the angels of might and awe, and they are from the worlds of Israfil. When the rememberer is averse, the angel descends upon him and fulfills his need. (O Khafid) As for supplication by it, you say: O Khafid, You are the One who lowered the ranks of the people of denial into the abysses, and You are the One who crushes them by Your subduing and Your manifold attributes, and You are the One who عزز عليهم when You brought them into being, at the division of good deeds and evil deeds. I ask You by the secret of secrets in the hearts of the righteous and the good, and by the light of the lights spread out in the regions, that You make me a guardian of myself and my secret in the station of servitude, humble before You at the ظهور of the manifestation with lordship and intimate speech, and provide me with an abundant share of the divine knowledges. Surely You are the Hearer of supplication, Able over whatever You will, Lord of the worlds. * As for His name al-Rafiʿ, in it are 3 letters of the Greatest Name, and in it are subtleties: Allah makes for one whose sight Allah unveils, and by it he sees the rest of the ailments for which there is no cure; and it is the medicine of Allah on earth. Its ملك is Marqiyā'īl, and he is chief over four commanders; under the hand of each commander are 351 ranks, each rank 351 thousand angels entrusted with دفع of afflictions. Whoever remembers it, the angel descends upon him and presents to him the affairs of this world and the Hereafter; if he chooses the world, He gives it to him and withholds the Hereafter from him. (O Rafiʿ) Its supplication: you say, O Allah, You are the Rafiʿ who raised the prophets and the saints by Your divine light, and You are the One who perfected the souls of the people of love and affection by the splendors of Your Face and Your Highest Presence, and You are the One who manifest the habit and detachment in the hearts of Your saints for encompassing the worlds of things, and You are the One who raised the degrees of gnosis and the rank of the people of gnosis and faith when the darknesses widened and the secret of unveiling appeared. I ask You by the secret of the Kaf and the Nun, and the secret of the secrets of exaltation, by the secret of the meanings of the Nun, by the hidden contents of the letters of lowering in the raising that necessitate a reward not cut off, and by the secrets of the circles of benefit with You, the revealer of the preserved judgment, that You raise my contemplations above the محسوسات and my will above the bliss of desires, and raise me to You in the most complete states, and the changing of evil deeds. I ask You, O Allah, that You make me humbled before You in this world, with the perfection of knowledge and worship, turning toward You in the Hereafter at the spreading of the lights of felicity.
