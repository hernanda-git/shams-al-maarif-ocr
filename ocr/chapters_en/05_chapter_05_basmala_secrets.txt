وَكَذَلِكَ الزُّهَرَةُ وَعُطَارِدُ وَالْمِرِّيخُ وَجِرْمُ الْمُشْتَرِي قَدْرُ الدُّنْيَا اثْنَيْنِ وَثَمَانِينَ مَرَّةً وَجِرْمُ زُحَلَ قَدْرُ الدُّنْيَا

{ Chapter on their bodies and what is connected to them }
Know that the body of the sun is the measure of the world one hundred and a half, and the body of the moon is the measure of the world thirty-nine times,
and likewise Venus and Mercury and Mars, and the body of Jupiter is the measure of the world eighty-two times, and the body of Saturn is the measure of the world
ninety-nine times, and some of our scholars said that the body of the sun is fifteen degrees in front of it and likewise behind it, and the body of
the moon is twelve degrees in front of it and likewise behind it, and the body of Mars is eight degrees behind it and likewise in front of it, and the body of Venus
seven degrees in front of it and likewise behind it, and the body of Mercury likewise, and God Most High knows.
{ Chapter that every star in the heaven is the measure of the world many times } And as for the fixed stars, and they are fifteen
stars, al-shiʿrā al-ʿabūr and al-simāk and al-nasr al-ṭāʾir and the heart of the lion and the like of them, every star among them is the measure of
the world sixty-four times.

{ Chapter on the traversing of the seven stars the spheres }
Know that the moon traverses the sphere in twenty-nine days and a third of a day, and Mercury traverses it in twenty-eight days,
and Venus traverses it in two hundred and twenty-four days and a quarter of a day, and the sun traverses it in three hundred and sixty-five
days and a quarter of a day, and Mars traverses it in six hundred and thirty, and Jupiter traverses it in eleven years, and Saturn traverses it
in twenty-nine years, and God knows.

{ Chapter on the stations of the signs }
Know that the station of the moon in every sign is two days and three nights, and the station of Mercury in every sign is fifteen days, and the station of
Venus in every sign is twenty-five days, and the station of Jupiter in every sign is a year, and the station of Saturn in every
sign is thirty months.

{ Chapter on the dignity of the star }
Know that the dignity of the moon is Taurus, and the dignity of Mercury is Virgo, and the dignity of Venus is Pisces, and the dignity of the sun is Aries,
and the dignity of Mars is Capricorn, and the dignity of Jupiter is Cancer, and Saturn is Libra, and the Pleiades it was said are the lamp of the world in
the heaven because they are the gathering of the stars like the knot, and it is in the heaven, and the Arabs call it the stars because there is not in the heaven a spot
more starry than it.

{ Chapter on what belongs to each star of the seven days } Sunday is for the sun, and Monday for the moon, and Tuesday for Mars,
and Wednesday for Mercury, and Thursday for Jupiter, and Friday for Venus, and Saturday for Saturn.
{ Chapter on the conjunction of the stars } And the meaning of conjunction is that the star be in a sign and the other in
its opposite, and of meeting is that they meet in one sign, so it affects by the command of God Most High every act; that in the Qurʾan, so understand that. If
Saturn approaches Jupiter, wars become general in the regions, and a king from the kings of the world and the earth dies; and if Mars approaches
Saturn, that is; and if Saturn approaches the sun, that is; and if Saturn conjoins Venus, it indicates the highness of prices
and drought; and if Saturn conjoins Mercury, it indicates the rectification of the state of the book; and if the moon conjoins, it indicates the ظهور of injustice
in judgments; and if Jupiter conjoins Mars, in the world are many hardships.

(Chapter on the natures of the planets) Know that the moon is cold, feminine, phlegmatic; in it is an accidental heat because its light
is from the light of the sun, and its dominion is laughter and adornment, and it is yellow bile. Mercury is masculine and feminine, and it is
sometimes fortunate and sometimes unfortunate, and the nature of heat is equal in it, and its dominion is speech and writing. Venus is female and she is fortunate,
cold, moist; she has phlegm, and her dominion is marriage and joy, and she has desire, and the arranging of the crown, and affectation, and the bringing together
of hearts and women and beauty and amusement and laughter. The sun is masculine, hot, dry; it has yellow bile, and it is
fortunate by aspect, unfortunate by opposition; its essence is gold, and its dominion is over the sciences, and it has the highest nobility and joy
and gladness and kingship. And Mars is feminine, hot, dry; it has yellow bile, and its essence is iron, and its taste is bitter, and its dominion is
over the head and the stomach and killing and women and others. Jupiter is masculine, moderate, spiritual, fortunate; it has blood
and its essence is tin, and its taste and its color are white, and its dominion is over the wind dwelling in the heart, and it has abundant gifts
and leadership. Saturn is masculine, cold, dry, dark; it has black bile, and its essence is lead, and its taste is bitter, and its color is
black, and its dominion is over deliberation, and it has heat and injustice and subjugation and coercion. I say: and a people claimed that these
zodiacal signs and spheres and moving planets act in the world by influence, and that they are the governors of the worlds, and they argued for that by His
saying, تعالى, “and the directors of an affair” and the like * and we do not, for it has been related that the Prophet, may God bless him and grant him peace, when he ascended to
the heaven informed us of the zodiacal signs and the stars and others, so whatever has come from him in this الباب is accepted, and whatever is forbidden from it is not to be regarded;
rather, the proofs and evidences have been established that the Creator, تعالى, invented them and brought them into being. As for His saying, تعالى, “and the directors
of an affair,” Ibn Abbas said that the angels, some of them are entrusted with provisions, and some of them are entrusted with rain, and some of them are entrusted
with the winds, as mentioned in the creation of the angels. And if they were directors, then by the permission of the Creator, جل وعلا, because He is the Powerful, the Knowing,
the Wise. So glory be to Him whose power is His power and whose wisdom is His wisdom; surely to Him belongs the خلق and the الأمر. Blessed is God, Lord of the worlds,
and God knows best what is right.

(The fifth chapter on the secrets of the basmalah and what there is of its hidden properties and blessings)
Know, may God grant me and you success in obedience to Him and understanding the secrets of His names, that whoever knows what God تعالى has deposited in Bism Allah al-Rahman
al-Rahim of secrets will not be burned by fire and it will not consume him, and whoever writes it and makes it successful will not be burned by fire. And all the scholars have agreed
that it is recommended to begin with Bism Allah al-Rahman al-Rahim in every matter of importance, following the Noble Book and what Abu Hurayrah, may God تعالى be pleased with him, narrated
from the Prophet, may God bless him and grant him peace, that he said: every matter of importance in which Bism Allah al-Rahman al-Rahim is not begun is cut off, and in a narration it is cut off, and in a narration it is mutilated; and its meaning is little blessing and there is no blessing in it.
And it is narrated that the books sent down from heaven to earth are one hundred and four: the صحف of Sheth sixty, and the صحف of Ibrahim

English:
Bism Allah al-Rahman al-Rahim, the clouds fled to the east, and the winds became still, and the seas raged, and they listened to it by the permission of
God, and the devils were pelted from the heaven, and the Lord of Might swore: My name is not named upon the sick of longings, nor upon a thing
in which there is no escape for it. And Ibn Mas‘ud, may God تعالى be pleased with him, said: whoever wishes that God save him from the nineteen adulteresses,
let him write it, and it is nineteen letters; every letter is salvation from every one of them. And whoever increases in its remembrance is provided with
awe among the upper world and the lower world, and by it the kingdom of Sulayman ibn Dawud, upon them both السلام, was established. And whoever writes it one hundred times and carries it
is provided with awe in the hearts. And it is narrated from Abdullah ibn Umar, may God تعالى be pleased with him, that he said: whoever has a need
to God تعالى, let him fast Wednesday and Thursday and Friday; then when it is Friday, let him bathe and go to the congregational mosque and give charity
with something; then when he prays the Friday prayer, let him say after it: O God, I ask You by Your name, al-Rahman al-Rahim, God, there is no god except Him, the Living, the Self-Subsisting, to
the end of the verse, “to whom the faces are humbled, and the voices are subdued, and the hearts are terrified from His fear”; I ask You to send blessings
and peace upon our master Muhammad and upon his family and his companions, and peace, and that You fulfill my need thus and it is thus and thus, so he names it. And he used to say:
do not let your fools know it, lest some of them invoke against some of them, and it be answered for them at the time. And it is narrated from the Prophet, may God bless him and grant him peace,
that he said: there is nothing between Bism Allah al-Rahman al-Rahim and between the Greatest Name of God except as between the whiteness of the eye and its blackness. And the
Prophet, may God bless him and grant him peace, said: there is nothing between the children of Adam and the devils except Bism Allah al-Rahman al-Rahim; so it is the concealed name
which يدلُّ upon that what after it is the Greatest Name, and it is Allah, because the Greatest Name is the Majesty, and it is the pole of the names, and to it
they return, and it is among the names like the proper name, because if you ask from al-Rahman, then you say Allah, and likewise the rest of the names are annexed to Him
and are known by the Majesty and the loftiness of His exaltedness. And there is for it an additional nobility over the names, and it is that if you remove from it the letter alif,
there remains for it “lillah,” and if you remove from it the first lam, there remains for it, and if you remove the second also, there remains “huwa”; so every letter
of it is قائم by itself, and it is not like that with other than it among the names, because if you remove a letter from them, its meaning is invalidated. And this name,
the Greatest, its letters have not been altered, for it is a nobility over the names and a proof that it is the honored essence, established in might and permanence. And for it
there is another nobility indicating the singular essence of lordship and indicating the unification of divinity, for its beginning is the alif, and it is the first of
the letters and the first of the numbers, and its end is the letter ha’, and it points to the unification of godhead, and it is not found in other than it among the
names, and it says by the basmalah of its state: I am the First and the Last and the Manifest and the Hidden. Then He followed it with the attribute of Rahmaniyyah. He تعالى said:
Say: call upon Allah or call upon al-Rahman; whichever you call upon, so He gave you the choice between saying O Allah or O Rahman, for He is the جامع of the two
Rahmaniyyah attributes and the noble speech. So if you wish the seeking of mercy, then say O Rahman, and it is the more specific, because God تعالى is the most specific
of the names and the greatest of them in agreement, so it is a رباني name. As for its explanation, it is that He brings things out from nonexistence into existence. And it has meanings
other than these, concerning which it is obligatory upon the one looking into them to withhold them from the fools, lest they reach to the doing of the reprehensible and the forbidden acts, and so it fall
with God like “he sold,” and when God تعالى intended by it disobedience— we seek refuge in God تعالى from His wrath—O God, do not make us among those who seek help
by Your names upon their disobedience. And this name has four letters: alif, lam, and ha’; for it are four natures, and the quarters
are four: east and west and north and south; and the angels of glorification are four: Jibril and Mikail and Israfil and Izra’il.
So Jibril is the possessor of the message to the sent ones, and the possessor of victory and subjugation, and by him God تعالى destroyed the disbelievers from the nations
. al-qidhah

دائرة بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم وفي الحديث الصحيح من جاء وفي صحيفته بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم ثلثمائة مرة
وكان موقنا بربوبيته أعتقه الله تعالى من النار وأدخله دار القرار وفي الإنجيل يا عيسى ليكن بسم الله
الرحمن الرحيم في افتتاح قراءتك وصلاتك فإن من جعلها في افتتاح قراءته وصلاته لم يره منكر ونكير
وإذا مات على ذلك هون الله عليه الموت وسكراته وضيق القبر وفسح له في قبره مد البصر وأخرج
من قبره أبيض الجسم ووجهه يتلألأ نورا وحاسبه الله حسابا يسيرا وثقل ميزانه وأعطي النور
التام على الصراط حتى يدخل الجنة وينادى عليه في عرصات القيامة بالسعادة والمغفرة قال عيسى
عليه السلام يارب لمن هذا وخاصيته قال لك ومن اتبعك وأخذ بأخذك وبالقولك يكون ذلك لمحمد
وأمته من بعدك فأخبر عيسى عليه السلام أصحابه فلما رفع عيسى عليه السلام الى السماء انقرضت
الحواريون وجاء آخرون ضلوا فغيروا واستبدلوا الذين دنيا فرفعت آية الإيمان من صدر
النصارى والرهبان وبقيت في صدور أهل الإنجيل حتى بعث الله نبينا محمدا صلى الله عليه وسلم
فكتبت في أوائل السور والدفاتر ورؤوس الرسائل وحلف رب العزة بعزته لا يسميه عبد مؤمن
على شيء إلا بورك له فيه وقال النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم من قرأ بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم وكان مؤمنا
حقا سبحت له الجبال واستغفرت له ولا يسمع تسبيحها وقال النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم إذا قال العبد بسم
الله الرحمن الرحيم قالت الجنة لبيك وسعديك اللهم إن عبدك فلانا قال بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم فتنقل
حسناتهم على سيئاته ثم يقول سبحان الله رب رجح حسنات أمة محمد صلى الله عليه وسلم فتقول
لهم ليناهم إنما كان هذا لأنه كان ابتداء كلامهم ثلاثة أسماء من أسماء الله تعالى العظام لو وضعت في
كفة ميزان ووضعت السموات والأرضون وما فيهن وما ناسبهن في الكفة الثانية لرجحت عليهم وهي
هذه بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم قال وقد جعلها أمنا من كل بلاء وداء وحرزا من الشيطان الرجيم وقد
أمنت هذه الأمة من الخسف والقذف والمسخ ببركتها فقدموها وها لذي الجلال والإكرام وهذه
صورته كما ترى فافهم ترشد

قال الحسن البصري رحمه
الله تعالى في معنى قوله: {وإذا
ذكرت ربك في القرآن
وحده ولوا على أدبارهم
نفورا} ومعنى ذلك: بسم الله
الرحمن الرحيم. وقال في
قوله: {وألزمهم كلمة التقوى
وكانوا أحق بها وأهلها} أنها
كانت: بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم،
وقيل: لا إله إلا الله. ومن كتب
بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم
وجوّدها تعظيماً لها كُتب
عند الله من المقربين. وروي
عن عكرمة أنه قال: كان الله تعالى ولا شيء معه، ثم خلق الله النور، ثم خلق من النور اللوح والقلم، ثم أمر الله
تعالى القلم أن يجري على اللوح بما هو كائن، فأول ما كتب القلم في اللوح: بسم الله الرحمن
الرحيم، فجعلها الله تعالى أماناً لخلقه من داوم على قراءتها، وهي قراءة أهل السموات السبع وأهل
سرادقات الجنة من الملائكة الكروبيين والصديقين والمسبحين. وأول ما نزل على آدم عليه السلام: بسم
الله الرحمن الرحيم، فقال: الآن علمت أن ذريتي لا تعذب بالنار ما داموا على قراءتها. ثم رفعت بعده إلى زمن
إبراهيم عليه السلام، ونزلت عليه وهو في المنجنيق فنجاه الله تعالى من النار. ثم رفعت من بعده إلى زمن سليمان،
فلما نزلت عليه قالت الملائكة: الآن قد تم ملك سليمان، وأمر الله تعالى أن ينادى في جميع الأسباط
والزهاد والعباد: ألا من أراد أن يسمع آية الإيمان فليأت إلى سليمان بن داود عليهما السلام في محراب أبيه.

سمعوها ازدادوا فرحاً وقالوا: نشهد أنك رسول الله حقاً يا ابن داود. ثم رفعت بعده إلى زمن موسى، فلما

Al-Hasan al-Basri, may God تعالى have mercy on him, said regarding the meaning of His saying: {And when you mention your Lord in the Qur’an alone, they turn upon their backs in aversion}: and the meaning of that is: Bism Allah al-Rahman al-Rahim. And he said regarding His saying: {And He bound them to the word of piety, and they were more deserving of it and its people} that it was: Bism Allah al-Rahman al-Rahim, and it was said: there is no god but Allah. And whoever writes Bism Allah al-Rahman al-Rahim and perfects it in magnification of it, he is written with God among the near ones. And it is related from Ikrimah that he said: God تعالى was and there was nothing with Him, then God created the light, then from the light He created the Tablet and the Pen, then God تعالى commanded the Pen to run upon the Tablet with what is to be. So the first thing the Pen wrote in the Tablet was: Bism Allah al-Rahman al-Rahim. Then God تعالى made it a security for His creation for whoever persists in reciting it, and it is the recitation of the people of the seven heavens and the people of the pavilions of Paradise, from the cherubic angels and the truthful and the glorifiers. And the first thing that descended upon Adam عليه السلام was: Bism Allah al-Rahman al-Rahim, so he said: Now I know that my offspring will not be punished by the Fire so long as they persist in reciting it. Then it was raised after him to the time of Abraham عليه السلام, and it descended upon him while he was in the catapult, so God تعالى saved him from the Fire. Then it was raised after him to the time of Solomon, and when it descended upon him the angels said: Now the kingdom of Solomon has been completed. And God تعالى commanded that it be proclaimed among all the tribes and the ascetics and the worshippers: Lo, whoever desires to hear the sign of faith, let him come to Solomon son of David عليهما السلام in the prayer niche of his father. He said: So they gathered to him, and Solomon stood and ascended the pulpit and recited to them the sign of faith, which is: Bism Allah al-Rahman al-Rahim. And when they heard it, they increased in joy and said: We bear witness that you are truly the Messenger of God, O son of David. Then it was raised after him to the time of Moses, and when it descended upon him he subdued with it Pharaoh and his hosts and Qarun and Haman and their hosts. Then it was raised after him to the time of Jesus عليه السلام, and God inspired him: O son of Mary, have you not known which sign has descended? He said: Yes, my Lord. He said: The sign of faith has been sent down upon you, which is: Bism Allah al-Rahman al-Rahim, so adhere to reciting it in your night and your day and your going and your coming and your sitting and your standing and your eating and all your states, for whoever comes on the Day of Resurrection and it is in his scroll, God will forgive him what has preceded of his sin. And it is related from some of the righteous that he came to one of the saints to visit him and seek his supplication and blessing, and he found the people gathered at his door awaiting his خروج, and Qaws Quzah had placed his foot and said: Bism Allah al-Rahman al-Rahim. So the shaykh who had come to visit him, and he was called al-Madihi, when he saw the act of the shaykh cried out and said: The men have preceded, and we are thus. Then the shaykh took to earnestness and striving.
