والعقد وما كان متوسطاً من الأعمال ويكره فيه تفصيل الجديد ولا تعمل فيه طلسم ولا تعالج فيه
الروحانيات والعزلة فيه محمودة ومن ولد فيه كان رديئاً مشؤوماً كذاباً نماماً فاجراً بخوره قشر رمان

بها وهو كوكب ناري سعيد غير مشوب ينحط فيه إلى العالم مباركاً سعيداً وتدعو إلى المودة
والحظ والسعادة وهي محمودة العاقبة في جميع الأحوال ويصلح فيه تدبير الصناعات المكرمة
ويبدأ فيه الحكم والمواعظ والعلوم الفقهية وعمل الطلسمات وابنِ بناءً واغرس الشجر والبس

فيه روحانية تعمل فيه للبغضاء والعداوة والقطيعة ولا تحمد فيه عواقب الأمور والأعمال وتحرك
فيه الملوك بالغضب والسخط ويتم فيه البيع والشراء ويصلح فيه الحفر والنبش والزراعة ويخرج
فيه الخبايا والدفائن وتكتم الأسرار ومن ولد فيه كان حسناً مباركاً حريصاً على الدنيا محتالاً بخوره

وعنزروت وفلفل والله أعلم ( القول على منزلة الفرغ المقدم وهذه صفته ) ٠ ٠ وله حرف
الضاد

English:
22

It is fortunate. Through it, by the permission of God, a mixed spiritual being descends into the world, doing evil and its opposite. It is suitable for [UNCLEAR: the source's phrase concerning al-maḥall and bindings] and for moderate works. Cutting new garments is disliked in it; do not make a talisman or treat spiritual entities in it. Seclusion in it is praiseworthy. Whoever is born in it will be base, ill-omened, a liar, a tale-bearer, and wicked. Its incense is pomegranate peel and mastic.

[Discourse on the mansion of al-Naʿāʾim, and this is its form]

Its form is six dots (• • • • • •), and its letter is rāʾ. When the Moon descends in it, it is a fiery, fortunate, unmixed planet. A blessed and fortunate influence descends into the world through it, and it calls toward affection, luck, and happiness. Its outcome is praiseworthy in every condition. It is suitable for managing noble crafts; beginning judgment, admonitions, and jurisprudential sciences; and making talismans. Build a structure, plant trees, and wear new clothes in it, for whoever wears them will remain in joy and happiness until they wear out. Whoever is born in it will be blessed, happy, and successful in all their movements. Its incense is male frankincense. God knows best.

[Discourse on the mansion of al-Baldah, and this is its form]

Its form is four dots (• • • •), and its letter is shīn. When the Moon descends in it, it is an inauspicious fiery planet. A spiritual being descends in it, working for enmity, hatred, separation, the honored stone, and the exalted jewel. Do not treat spiritual entities, plant anything, travel, associate with kings, marry, buy slaves, wear new clothes, or perform any work in it. Whoever is born in it will be unlucky and a trickster. Its incense is spikenard and aloeswood. God knows best.

[Discourse on the mansion of Saʿd al-Dhābiḥ, and this is its form]

Its form is two dots (• •), and its letter is thāʾ. When the Moon descends in it, it is an earthy, inauspicious, mixed planet. A spiritual being descends in it, working for hatred, enmity, and separation. The outcomes of matters and works are not praiseworthy in it. It moves kings to anger and wrath. Buying and selling are completed in it; digging, excavation, and agriculture are suitable in it; hidden things and buried treasures are brought out in it; and secrets are concealed. Whoever is born in it will be handsome, blessed, worldly-minded, and a trickster. Its incense is safflower. God knows best.

[Discourse on the mansion of Saʿd Bulaʿ, and this is its form]

Its form is two dots (• •), and its letter is tāʾ. When the Moon descends in it, it is a mixed planet. Through it, by the permission of God, a spiritual being descends into the world, doing evil and its opposite, the good and the bad. It is suitable for buying slave women and mamluks, buying beasts of burden, speaking with shaykhs, important agricultural tasks, splitting rivers, digging wells, and similar strenuous works, as well as fires and preparing foods. Whoever is born in it will be blessed and righteous. Its incense is chamomile. God knows best.

[Discourse on the mansion of Saʿd al-Suʿūd, and this is its form]

Its form is two dots (• •), and its letter is ḥāʾ. When the Moon descends in it, it is a planet mixed of earth and air. A spiritual being descends in it and erases the last of what preceded it; all works are suitable in it. Begin in it with works of love and affection and whatever resembles them for mending hearts. Treat spiritual entities in it, meet kings, chiefs, officeholders, and others, and do whatever you wish of acts of affection; your work will succeed. Whoever is born in it will love the righteous. Its incense is aloeswood and mastic. God knows best.

[Discourse on the mansion of Saʿd al-Akhbiyah, and this is its form]

Its form is three dots (• • •), and its letter is dhāl. When the Moon descends in it, it is an airy planet. A spiritual being descends into the world through it, working for separation, sedition, hatred, division, and wars. Works do not reach completion in it; if they do, they are not praiseworthy. Do not treat the sick or spiritual entities in it, make a talisman, or undertake alchemy or alsimya operations in it. Whoever is born in it will be wicked and unbelieving. Its incense is male frankincense, anzarut, and pepper. God knows best.

[Discourse on the mansion of al-Fargh al-Muqaddam, and this is its form]

Its form is two dots (• •), and its letter is ḍād.
