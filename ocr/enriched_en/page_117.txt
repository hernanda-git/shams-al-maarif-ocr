هذا الدعاء المبارك إما عقب كل صلاة ثم يسأل حاجته تقضى ومن كان مضطرا في حاجته وأراد
قضاءها عاجلا فليتوضأ وليأت إلى بعض المساجد أو عند توابيت الأولياء ثم يصلي ركعتين
وينوي فيها قضاء حاجته يقرأ في الركعة الأولى الفاتحة والإخلاص ٣ ثم يقول بسم الله الرحمن
الرحيم ٧٠ مرة وفي الركعة الثانية كذلك ويزيد المعوذتين فإذا سلم استغفر الله ٧٠ مرة
ويصلي على النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم ٧٠ ثم يقرأ هذا الدعاء ٧ مرات بنية صادقة ويجمع
حواسه في قراءته حتى تقضى حاجته خصوصا إن كان صاحب حالة صادقة مع الله وهو هذا الدعاء
المبارك تقول (بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم) رب أسألك باسمك الذي فتحت به عالم الأمر والخلق بالتجلي
المظهر لنسب التنزيل والمتعالي أمرا وجودا وباطنا معقولا ذلك لمن أردت بل معلوما لمن أشهدت
مجهولا لمن تشابه منه كثرة لا تقدح في وحدة ما أحكمت من محكمه يا عليم يا حليم يا فتاح
يا الله يارب وأسألك اللهم بسر الإضافة الرابطة بين حضرتي الوجوب والإمكان المقتضية لظهور
النعت الأعظم بالاسم المبهم لثبوت الألوهية عموما وخصوصا بدءا وعودا ممن وسعته عموم الرحمانية
التي لا تناهي استقرارا أو ثبوتا عن فيض خاص الرحيمية الرافع لشهود إثبات التقرب بالقرب
المجهول الماهية منك يا رحمن يا رحيم يا فتاح يا عليم أسألك التنوير والتيسير والمعونة والفوز
والحفظ والرعاية والستر والتكميل وطيب الرزق والبركة فيه والرجاء وحسن الظن بك واليأس عن
غيرك وأسألك بحق البسملة تكونا لأمرك وتكميلا بجودك وبركة منك تبارك اسمك وتعالى
جدك ولا إله غيرك بك آمنا ولك أسلمنا وعليك توكلنا حققنا اللهم بنورك وبنور اسمك رغنا عن
غيرك اذهولا فيك يا الله شهودا لك يا رحمن سلام قولا من رب رحيم اللهم إني أسألك بحق هذا الدعاء

English:
( 11 )

This blessed supplication is performed either after every prayer, and then the one who prays asks for his need, and it is fulfilled. And whoever is in distress concerning his need and desires its fulfillment speedily, let him perform ablution and go to one of the mosques, or to the tombs of the awliya, then let him pray two rak’ahs and intend within them the fulfillment of his need. He recites in the first rak’ah al-Fātiḥah and al-Ikhlas, 3, then he says: In the name of Allah, the Most Compassionate, the Most Merciful, 70 times; and in the second rak’ah likewise, and he adds the two Muʿawwidhatayn. When he has given the salutation, he seeks forgiveness of Allah 70 times and sends blessings upon the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, 70; then he recites this supplication 7 times with a sincere intention, and he gathers his senses into its recitation until his need is fulfilled, especially if he is the possessor of a truthful state with God. And it is this blessed supplication:

He says: (In the name of Allah, the Most Compassionate, the Most Merciful) My Lord, I ask You by Your name by which You opened the world of command and creation, through the disclosure that manifests the relation of the descent and of the Most High: as command, as existence, and inwardly as that which is intelligible. That is for whom You have willed; rather, known to whom You have made witness; unknown to whom there is likeness to it from it. Abundance does not diminish the unity of that which You have made sound of what You have decreed. O All-Knowing, O Forbearing, O Opener, O God, O Lord.

And I ask You, O God, by the secret of the relational attachment that binds the two presences of necessity and possibility, requiring the appearance of the greatest attribute by means of the ambiguous name, for the establishment of divinity generally and specifically, at the beginning and again upon the return, from Him whose all-encompassing is the generality of raḥmah, which has no end in stability or establishment, and from a special outpouring of His mercy that raises the witnessing of the affirmation of drawing near by a nearness whose quiddity is unknown from You.

O Most Compassionate, O Most Merciful, O Opener, O All-Knowing, I ask You for illumination, ease, help, success, protection, care, concealment, completion, goodly provision and blessing within it, hope and good opinion of You, and despair of other than You. And I ask You by the right of the basmala to be for Your command and for its completion through Your generosity and a blessing from You. Blessed is Your name and exalted is Your majesty; there is no god other than You. In You we have believed, to You we have submitted, and upon You we have relied. Make it true for us, O God, by Your light and by the light of Your name: our contentedness away from other than You, and our bewilderment in You. O God, our witnessing of You. To You, O Most Compassionate, a greeting, a word from a Merciful Lord. O God, I ask You by the right of this blessed supplication that You fulfill my need, and it is such-and-such, and that You name what I wish, so that therein there be good pleasure; and beware of asking for what is not permitted.

* And among the important supplications of the one who prays, the one with which he used to supplicate, is that whose recitation is not completed before his need is fulfilled: (In the name of Allah, the Most Compassionate, the Most Merciful) O God, I ask You by virtue of that You are God in the realities of pure verification, and I ask You by virtue of that You are God in every state of the states that are bounded and that are equitable, and by virtue of that You are God, sanctified by the properties of oneness and self-sufficiency, transcendent to the opposite, the peer, the contrary, and that which is outwardly manifest, and by virtue of that You are God, who has nothing like Him, and He is the All-Hearing, the All-Seeing. I ask You to send blessings and peace upon our master Muhammad, and upon the family of Muhammad, and upon everyone who loves Muhammad, and that You fulfill my needs in that by which there is good for me in this world and the Hereafter, surrounded by care and preserved from afflictions by the properties of the ends. O One who returns good things in repayment, O One who in truth is the possessor of taqwa and the possessor of pardon and good deeds. O God, this is the request of a servant, ʿAzru, who magnifies Your House by the manifestation of his request; for You are the Knower of the unseen and the Witnesser of the reality of the requests before their being undertaken, that which is requested. So complete it with a beautiful conclusion, O best of that which is requested. And may God bless our master Muhammad, beloved of the hearts, and his family and his companions, and grant peace.

* And among the supplications of the one who prays, for whatever he desires of deeds: (In the name of Allah, the Most Compassionate, the Most Merciful) O God, I ask Your forgiveness and I repent to You from every sin to which I drew near and then returned. I ask Your forgiveness from every deed by which I sought Your noble Face and then something other than You was mixed with it, and I ask Your forgiveness from every sin that I committed in the darkness of night and day. I submitted to God as a servant, submissive, humbled, subjected. I believed in God: O Forgiving, O Thankful. I was pleased with Your Prophet, Your Beloved, Your Chosen One, and the best of Your creation, Muhammad, peace and blessings be upon him, who came to all with the message, in rejoicing.
