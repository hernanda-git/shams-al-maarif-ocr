English:
— 312 —

The men, and its Arabic and Indian form are one. And it has names by which it is invoked — and they are the preceding names, call by them as preceded. ( The Letter 'Ayn ) [it is the letter of knowledge; among the men…] ( The Letter Ghayn ) [‘ ‘ ‘ ‘ ‘ ‘] It is a speaking, moist letter, the last rank of water; and among its names are 'al-Ghanī' (the Rich) and 'al-Ghaffār' derived from it; and it is the letter of happiness, joy, and delight. And whoever writes its Arabic form upon a tin tablet 17, and a man carries it — Allah provides for him from whence he expects not, and blesses him in what he lives by, and the hearts of creatures are softened toward him. And from the secret of this letter — it is not written but in Arabic. And some mentioned that among His names, exalted, is 'al-Ghayb' (the Unseen), and adduced His saying: { Those who believe in the unseen }; supporting this saying is that whoever places its numbers in a 10-by-10 square, and writes around it 19 Arabic Ghayns of equal dimensions divided upon the square, and in its corners from outside His names: 'Ghanī, Ghārr, Ghaffār, Ghafūr' — upon pure paper with ink, and fumes it with amber and qamarī aloeswood, and carries it, and mentions the name a thousand times while facing the qibla with gathered resolve — Allah teaches him from the hidden of His knowledge, and shows him the wonders of His creatures and the secrets of His names and words. And whoever breaks the numerical thus [Gh ‘ N] in a triangle upon a silver ring on Monday in the waning of the Moon, and seals with it — Allah withholds the tongues of creatures from him, and they do not speak of him but with good, and they do not move but in what has benefit and good. And it has names by which it is invoked for its works: "O Allah, indeed I ask You — O Rich, O Forgiving, O All-Forgiving — by what You lodged in the letter Ghayn of the secrets…". ( The Letter Fā' ) It is a silent, cold, dry letter, and is called the letter of suspension — for what is in it of setting matters in motion and stopping them, and casting strife among the rebellious faction; and its dryness exceeds its heat. And it has an Arabic and Indian form. And whoever writes it [F] on Tuesday upon an iron tablet, with the Moon in conjunction, and buries it in the gathering of the rebellious — they contend and some kill others. And whoever places this letter inside a house in an ill-omened hour — it is ruined. And whoever writes it 20 times upon a lead sheet, and depicts beneath it the form of a dragon or scorpion, and buries it in the middle of the house — no snake or scorpion enters it. And from the secret — that this sheet or the sword or any instrument is anointed with balm-oil and buried in the earth — it does not rust; and by it the sages used to anoint the talismans. And whoever mixes the name of him he wills with this letter, and places it in his goods or house — his means are suspended. And whoever writes 20 Fā's upon his house-door — it is stilled. And whoever places its numbers falling upon it in a 4-by-4 square upon a tablet of a sheep's shoulder-blade, and writes around it twenty Fā's with the name of him he wills to hinder from travel or other — it is so. And it has names and talismans by which it is invoked: "O Allah, indeed I ask You — O Opener, O Originator, O Splitter of the grain — that You subject to me Your noble angels. Indeed You are over all things Able."
