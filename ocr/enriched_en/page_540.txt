English:
— 534 —

"…for it is the best companion and the best tender friend and the best loyal beloved for the people of the path and the reality, and the best weapon for the striving and the best lances for the witnessing — until I did not speak from caprice but from the flame of a fire I kindled from the most auspicious valley of happiness, ignited at the valley of Ṭūr of light upon the branches of the tree of presence, when I traveled the valley of realization with the arrival of the companion of success — the iron limit and the new earnestness and the auspicious fortune and the firm resolve. Indeed in that is a reminder for who has a heart or lends the ear while he is witness. And some of the sages said: 'Who is not moved by the lute and its strings, and the spring and its flowers — his temperament is corrupted and he may need treatment.' [Poetry:] 'The sun of the forenoon — its rising — does not harm * that it is not seen by who has no sight.' So who understood its allusions and untied the talismans of its treasures attained the hidden knowledge and the guarded secret and the Greatest Name and the most glorious mention. So if you are called in the garden of the sundus meadow and the narcissus garden and the most noble tree and the alluded degree and the meaningful breath and the kingly withdrawals and the Paradise gardens and the holy pages and the luminous dictation and the Self-Sufficient secrets and the merciful supplications and the Sufi subtleties and the spiritual descents and the Qur'ānic knower[s] and the Throne indications and the divine allusions and the unveiling managements and the Sufi skills and the Davidic psalms and the Ladunnian sciences and the Mosaic managements and the Solomonic seals and the Lokmanian exhortations and the Meccan openings and the temporal breaths and the beauty realities and the foundational forms and the Atlantean circles and the most glorious benefits — then upon you is the unveiling of the veils from your insight, to peruse your tablet which is the firm Book of Allah and His straight secret and His ancient treasure. God Most High said: 'And in yourselves — do you not see?' So who does not know His book which is He, then he is not he. [Poetry:] 'And read the forms of temples that are lined * they inform you of the secret of the obscure address. So read my book — it suffices as your witness * guiding you from it by a knowledge you did not know.' And perhaps the veil is an unveiling, and the manifestation is a concealment * . And know that the false does not come to my book from before it nor from behind it, as He said: 'For it has successors from before it and from behind it, guarding it by the command of Allah' * . What you found in it — know that the matter in it is as you found it; and by Allah I swear I do not cast it to you but manifest, and I do not leave you in it pondering * . If you remember it and cast it, then a house of a Lord protecting it — and be clever for its reception. So who has intellect, Allah was his witness; and who has a self, the body was its witness. So O my regret over who was excessive in the day of his heedlessness, and from the company of the people of knowledge was parted — indeed his loss appeared at the profits of the two worlds, and his name was erased from the Tablet of the near ones. May Allah protect us and you from the abasement of the servant and the hate of rejection — indeed He is bountiful, generous, adorned, merciful, Compassionate, liberal, Bestower, Recompenser by the beneficence. * And Allah I ask that He inspire the understanding of what we aimed at and the unveiling of what we concealed — a brother, a friend, an agreeing, a true [companion]. And in this measure is sufficiency for who Allah granted success; and there is no power nor strength but by Allah the High, the Great; and may Allah bless our master Muhammad, the seal of the prophets and the Imam of the messengers, and his family and his companions altogether, and the followers until the Day of Religion — as the rememberers mentioned, and the heedless neglected to mention; and praise belongs to Allah, Lord of the worlds.
