باسم الخالق إلى الخط الأزلي والكتاب الدهري وعدد من يخلقه من النسب الروحانية والأطوار
البدائية وإن الإمداد الكلي هو مائتان وأربع وأربعون نوراً يمد للجن من اسمه الباري بتدبير تلك
العوالم وتدبير تلك الأنوار المنبعثة من هذا الاسم المخزون ولومت إضافة نورانية رحمانية إلى زمام أهل
السعادة وأهل الخذلان ثم بعد ذلك يستولي عليه اسمه تعالى المصور فيض على وجوده بنسبته المشرقة
وروحانيته المحرقة التي عددها ثلثمائة وسبعة وثلاثون يوماً وذلك لكل يوم ثمانية أجزاء وتسعة أعشار بلطف
تدبير إبداعه فلا تزال هذه الأطوار النورانية تدور عليها أفلاكها الدورية إلى أن يكمل دور
الأسماء الثلاثة وهي اسم الخالق والباري والمصور فهو لا يزال يمدهم اسمه تعالى القدير وذلك أن
يفيض عليه من أنوار المقادير واختلاف التدابير ثلثمائة نوع في خمسة وأربعين نوعاً والترجع
إلى الخواص فمن خاصته لقضاء الحوائج تقرأه عدد ٥١١٥ في مكان خال وعند تمام العدد
يسأل حاجته فإنها تقضى كائنة ما كانت وتحضر عوالمه بحسب استعداده ويقضون حاجته وخادمه
طماخيل وهو من عوالم ميكائيل عليه السلام ويسبح ويقول سبحان الخالق الباري المصور
مدة الخلوة والرياضة أربعين يوماً فإنه يكشف لك عن دقائق الأمور وأما ذكره فالبسملة اللهم
أنت خالق الموجودات الأصلية ومكونها وأنت الذي أظهرتها من العدم المخترع بقوة التدبير
بإرادتها تفضلت به مما سبق من علمك في القدم فأنت المخترع لأنواع الأشياء على ما تشاء من
إيجادها وإبرازها من ظلمة الغيب بأحسن الترتيب والتفاصيل أسألك يا مبدع الأشياء ومحيي
الأحياء أن تنزل في قلبي نوراً ذاتياً تجذب به شهودك وأن تسخر لي عبدك طماخيل
خادم هذا الاسم ليوقفني على أسرار الاختراع لأتحقق به ولعه في النعيم الأكبر وتحقيق
الكلمات الظهور من صفاتك العليا وأثلني ذلك يا الله يا خالق ما من عبد تلا هذا الذكر والاسم
الشريف عدده إلا كشف له عن أسرار المخلوقات

English:
— 444 —

"…by the name al-Khāliq to the eternal line and the perpetual book, and the number of those He creates from the spiritual lineages and the primitive phases; and the total outpouring is two hundred and forty-four lights, which He extends to the jinn from His name al-Bārī by the governance of those worlds and the governance of those lights emanating from this stored name. And if there is added a merciful, lordly lightness to the reins of the people of happiness and the people of deprivation, then after that His name, God Most High, al-Muṣawwir takes possession of him and pours upon his existence by His shining relation and His burning spirituality whose number is three hundred and thirty-seven days — and that is for each day eight parts and nine-tenths, by the gentleness of the governance of His origination. So these luminous phases keep revolving upon their cyclical spheres until the cycle of the three names is completed — and they are the name al-Khāliq, al-Bārī, and al-Muṣawwir — so He still extends to them His name, God Most High, al-Qadīr, and that He pours upon him from the lights of the measures and the difference of the governances — three hundred kinds in forty-five kinds. And returning to the properties: of its properties for the fulfillment of needs — you read it the number 5115 in an empty place, and at the completion of the number you ask your need, and it is fulfilled whatever it was, and his worlds attend according to his preparedness and fulfill his need; and its servant is ṬAMĀKHĪL, and he is of the worlds of Mīkhā'īl (upon him peace), and he glorifies and says 'Glory be to the Creator, the Evolver, the Fashioner.' The period of the retreat and discipline is forty days, and he unveils to you the subtleties of affairs. And as for his mention — the basmala: 'O Allah, You are the Creator of the original existents and their Fashioner, and You are the One who brought them forth from the invented non-existence by the force of governance; by His will He favored with it from what preceded of Your knowledge in the eternity — so You are the Inventor of the kinds of things as You will their bringing-forth and their extraction from the darkness of the unseen in the best arrangement and detail. I ask You, O Originator of things and Reviver of the living, that You send down in my heart a essential light by which You draw Your witnessing, and that You subordinate to me Your servant ṬAMĀKHĪL, servant of this name, to inform me of the secrets of invention, that I may realize by it his love in the greater bliss and the realization of the manifest words from Your highest attributes; and grant me that, O Allah, O Creator. There is no servant who recites this mention and the noble name its number but He unveils to him the secrets of the creatures.' (Section on His name, God Most High, al-Bārī) Know that al-Bārī is the Creator, for He is the One who brought the creation into existence from dust — and the witness is His saying exalted 'He who created you from dust,' and dust is the Arabs' name for the moist earth, and the moist earth is the dust, and the created ones are the creation — except that He has a wisdom and the appearance of an attribute in the difference of the kinds. For if the names were synonymous He would not say exalted 'To Allah belong the beautiful names, so call upon Him by them.' * And know that the name of union and origination — when the essences of the stored things come out from eternity to existence — and the name of creation comprises all the creatures. (Notice) Know that the Real, glory be to Him and exalted, when He brought the intellect into existence in the first knowledge, then brought the world into existence in the subtle air, then transferred them to the appearance of the atoms — so these three were an earthly origination from before the knowledge of composition and the appearance of the gradual and the composition; the power of the bodily phases by bodies constrained for them molds upon which the creation of bodies is imprinted — a party in Paradise and a party in the Blaze, and they are the people of the left; and the form is one and the movement is one and the stillness — we learned that the uppers are not the lowers. So whose soul was purified in a mold of light in the attribute of mercy came out tranquil; and whose soul rebelled in a mold of darkness came out commanding to evil; and whose soul rebelled in a mold of light and was pierced by darkness came out blaming — among them is he who obeys it, [the Allah] in the heart which is imprinted…"
