English:
— 63 —

The beginnings of the surahs are taken from the names of Allah (exalted is He), and Abu al-‘Aliyah said: there is not among them a letter except that it is the key of a name from the names of Allah. So the alif is from Allah, and the lam is from Latif, and the mim is from Malik, and the sad is from Sadiq, and the ra is from Rabb, and the kaf is from Karim, and the ta is from Tayyib, and the sin is from Sami‘, and the ha is from Hamid, and the qaf is from Qadir, and the nun is from Nur. And this is its description according to what Abu al-‘Aliyah, may Allah (exalted is He) have mercy on him, arranged it: Alif Lam Mim, Alif Lam Mim Sad, Alif Lam Ra, Kaf Ha Ya ‘Ayn Sad, Ta Sin, Ha Mim, ‘Ayn Sin Qaf, Qaf, Nun. And he made the middle letter a letter of indication, and it is the ha and the ba, and he advanced the letters of Alif Lam Mim Sad and Alif Lam Ra and Kaf Ha Ya ‘Ayn Sad and Ta Sin and the ha from Hamim and the qaf from Q and the Glorious Qur’an and the nun from Nun and the Pen. And Ibn ‘Abbas, may Allah (exalted is He) be pleased with them both, said concerning the meaning of Alif Lam Mim: its meaning is “I am Allah,” and Alif Lam Ra: “I am Allah, I know and I see,” so the alif conveys “I,” the lam conveys the name of Allah, the mim conveys every knowledge, and the ra conveys to the vision. And their arrangement is: Alif Lam Mim, Alif Lam Mim Sad, Alif Lam Ra, Kaf Ha Ya ‘Ayn Sad, Ta Ha, Ta Sin, Ta Sin Mim, Ya Sin, Ha Mim, Ha Mim ‘Ayn Sin Qaf, Qaf, Nun. And from the repeated ones fourteen fell away, among them Alif Lam Mim and the Hawamim, for their letters are established in these fourteen surahs, which are the aforementioned. And Abu al-‘Aliyah indicated to the saying of Ibn ‘Abbas, may Allah (exalted is He) be pleased with them both, that the openings of the surahs are the Greatest Name of Allah, and Allah knows best. (Benefit of the names) The number of the درجات of Paradise; from it knowledge separated and to it it returns, and from it the existents appeared, and the existents are a sign indicating the Beautiful Names. And the names have flowed in the conduct of the spirits and the bodies, and have taken the place of security from creation. There is no existent except that the names of Allah encompass it, eye and hearing. And the requirement of the name of Divinity is comprehensive of the meanings of the rest of the names, so the alif is a standing letter from which the letters arose and from which they arise, and it is their support. So among its counterparts are intellect, knowledge, the Throne, and the Tablet. And the three of the lam, and it is the connecting letter from the lower and the higher, and its counterpart is the Tablet, the Kursi, and the soul. And after the lam is the mim, and it is a letter indicating completion, and its counterpart is the body. So the alif is the first created thing, and the body is only for the created things, and the rest of the meanings of the letters are included in the alif. And the alif is the structure of gathering and totality, just as the letters are total in knowledge. So understand the meaning of completion and interpenetration; great meanings of spiritual secrets will appear to you, reaching with guidance in their sciences. So understand that. And know that the awliya’, may Allah (exalted is He) be pleased with them, spoke in the science of letters and names with shining rarities, and it was poured upon them from the source of اختصاص when certainty and sincerity were attained in their hearts. So they were distinguished in the science of names over others by three things: the first is that they understood the meanings of the ninety-nine names by support and inspiration, what others do not know by النظر and proof. The second is that they knew inward names beyond these ninety-nine. The third is that they were singled out for access to the Greatest Name. As for the prophets, عليهم الصلاة والسلام, they knew the ninety-nine by the light of revelation, what the awliya’ did not know. That is because they knew the sciences of the inward names from the science of the Name of Allah the Greatest. And every name of these names is not known as it is except by the one who is named by it and characterized by its meaning, and He is Allah alone, no partner has He. And beyond all these names which Allah (exalted is He) taught His prophets and His awliya’ is what Allah (exalted is He) has kept to Himself in the knowledge of the unseen with Him, and no sent prophet nor near angel was informed of it. And the first thing by which Allah singles out the servant, if He intends to take him as a wali, is that He teaches him the knowledge by which he becomes a knowing wali, and that He singles him out from the knowledge of the ninety-nine names, so that He opens for him from them sciences that are not opened for the scholar by the way of النظر, then He raises him to the knowledge of the inward names and the outward ones among them, just as the outward ones returned to Allah (exalted is He). And after his knowledge, He teaches him the inward things, which are separate letters, and they are the fourteen letters occurring in the Mighty Qur’an.
