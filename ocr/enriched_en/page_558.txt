{ Treatise: Fawātiḥ al-Raghā'ib fī Khaṣūṣiyyāt Awqāt al-Kawākib [The Openings of the Desires in the Specialities of the Times of the Planets] }
In the name of Allah, the Most Merciful, the Compassionate. {A verse} 'Praise belongs to Allah, the Director of the revolving sphere * and Who adorned the sky with the adornment of every wandering planet * in the risings of the flourishing zodiac, it shines upon the meadows of flowers * as signs for the reflectors in His dominion, and a diversion for those of insight * — truly in that is a lesson for those of vision * . I praise Him — glorified and exalted is He — with the praise of those of reflection and consideration; and I thank Him — glorious and sublime — with the thanks of those of insight and thought. And I testify that there is no god but Allah, the King, the Compeller * , the Originator of the heavens and the earth, the One, the Subduer * , the Governor of beings and the Turner of night and day * , the God of creation and command, and everything with Him by measure * . And I testify that our master Muḥammad is His servant and His messenger, the Chosen, the Selected * , the point of the circle of existence, the niche of the rays of lights * , and the manifestation of the theophany * , the pole of the axis of the sphere of secrets * — may Allah the Exalted bless and grant peace upon his lofty, precious station * , and upon his family, for whom the best from their Lord has preceded, and they are the pure masters * , and his companions, the stars of guidance for those of emulation, the leaders, the scholars * , and upon those who follow their guidance in succession of ages and epochs.' (Now then) — says the one hoping from his Lord, the Gentle, the Hidden, and the loyal favor * — 'Abd al-Qādir ibn 'Abd al-Qādir al-Ḥusaynī, known as al-Adhamī * , the servant of the noble chamber * , in the elevated Prophetic room * — may Allah be for him wherever he was * and enclose him in the knowings of generosity and goodness.
