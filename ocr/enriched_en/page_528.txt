"…7 times — 'illumine me with a light by which are blinded the eyes of the envious of jinn and men, until their powers contract from the contraction of the eye of the bat from the light of the sun, so they cannot face me — by a support from You. For You are the Light, and Your attribute is the light, and Your name is the light, and Your act is the light, and Your Throne is light, and Your Footstool is light, and Your Pen is light, and Your Tablet is light, and the angels of Your Presence are hearers of the light, and the flowing of Your remaining face is light, sunk in knowledge in its back is light, and every standing [thing] is by light, and every name of Your names is revived in the light — so make my hair and my flesh and my inner and my outer and every affair of You light upon light. You are the Most High, the Great, the Transcendent, and You are over everything Powerful.' And this supplication is of the breaths (nafahāt) — whoever exposes himself to it, there opens for him the door of the thoughts and the indications of the calls and the secrets of the divine wisdom * . And whoever calls by this supplication until the dawn and asks Allah any need, it is decreed; and they began it from the morning of that day to its like, and make your resolve upon it, imagining the speed of the answer, until your inner and your outer rejoice in the witnessing of the acts. And suits it of the names of God Most High 12 names, and they are for the preservation of the hearts and the removal of the calamities; and for the people of the knowledge it is a converse, and its effect appears in the hearts and imposes the might of the self, and in it is the expansion of the breasts and the allusions of the unveiling for who wants to look into his aim * . And whoever mentions them on his bed and mentions his need at the sleep, it was the strongest effect — so when he does that, there appears to him the form of what will be in his need itself, and what resembles it guides to that in everything he intends or what he seeks; and it relieves the griefs and hastens their removal and confirms his desire, and beautifies the inner of its rememberer and bearer, and selects for him the hearts, and he looks into the wonders of the secrets of the call and the return in everything; and its secret is the manifestation of the darkness of the heart and the ruling of the heart over all its worlds — and there gathered in it the special properties of all the names * . And they are: 'He is Allah, there is no god but He — al-Muḥīṭ, al-Kāmil, al-Wāḥid, al-Wāsi', al-Barr, al-Ṣādiq, al-Nūr, al-Badī', al-Mubdi', al-Mu'īd, al-Mughīth.' And suits it of the subtleties this subtle [thing] in which is the Greatest Name of Allah — when called by it He answers, and when asked by it He gives; and for the people of the unveilings by it is an acquaintance, and it is of the greatest and noblest of the invocations — and none perseveres in its mention but there is unveiled to him what he wills, and eased for him the object from the provision in the immediate and the deferred matters * . And if he mentions them, he sees of the matters of the world what is in it of secrets of the stillness, and there is subdued for him every year and the people of the imagery — and they are the perfect words, and they are ten names: al-Muḥīṭ, al-'Ālim, al-Rabb, al-Shahīd, al-Ḥasīb, al-Fa'āl, al-Khallāq, al-Khāliq, al-Bāri', al-Muṣawwir * . And it is mentioned of the witness, the friend of Allah the Shaykh 'Abd al-Qādir al-Jīlānī — and he mentions them at midnight, and how he witnessed their secrets and saw their effects until he rose in the air until he vanished from the sights, and he was amazed at the greatness of what he witnessed of the secrets; and his pure truthfulness aided him in that, and the strength of his certainty and the intensity of his resolve; and his state appeared, and he saw the Prophet (may Allah bless him and grant him peace) and Isrāfīl (peace be upon him) in the description he is in of his greatness — and that a pillar of the pillars of the Throne is upon his shoulder, and that his two feet have pierced the seven earths and the Tablet and the Trumpet whose breadth is five hundred years in his mouth; and he described Jibrīl (peace be upon him) when he appeared to the Prophet (may Allah bless him and grant him peace) in the form he was created upon, and he spread seven hundred wings, each wing blocking what is between the east and the west; and the Prophet (may Allah bless him and grant him peace) had asked Allah to show him that form — so when he saw him, he swooned and fell on his face despite the strength of his heart and the firmness of his side; so Jibrīl returned to the form and shape he used to appear to him in, and began wiping the dust from his face and running his hand over his chest and neck until he returned to his first state.
