يا فلان بن فلانة واقض حاجة فلان بن فلانة لا يتكلم أحد في حق فلان بن فلانة إلا بخير أو يصمت
هذا يوم لا ينطقون ولا يؤذن لهم فيعتذرون اعتبر يا فلان بن فلانة واقض له ما يطلب وما يريد بحق
الله المجيد المجيد وبحق طهبوب ٢ لهوب ٢ حياة كل شيء ما عصاك عبد إلا احترق ولا
جبار إلا ذل وهلك هيد هيد وهاها هو هواه بيه بيه رموه وهو القاهر فوق عباده له الملك الباذخ
والعز الشامخ أنت هو وهو أنت على كل شيء قدير وأسألك اللهم أن تسخر لي ملائكتك الكرام
الخدام لهذه الأسماء والمطيعين لهذه الأقسام يتوكلون ويمتثلون فيما أمرهم به المساعدة لفلان بن فلانة
ويقضون له ما يطلب وتجعلونه طوع يده ولا يخالفه في أمر من الأمور هيا الوحا العجل الساعة بارك
الله فيكم وعليكم ويكتب الوفق الآتي وتبخر الكاغد بعود هندي طيب وجاوي ومصطكى وقليل

English:
— 225 —

…from So-and-so son of So-and-so, by the right of these great verses and noble names, and by the right of Allah, the King, the All-Knowing: "Hear and obey, O So-and-so son of So-and-so, and fulfill the need of So-and-so son of So-and-so; let no one speak of So-and-so son of So-and-so but good or be silent. This is a day they do not speak, nor are they permitted, so they apologize. Consider, O So-and-so son of So-and-so, and fulfill for him what he seeks and wants, by the right of Allah, the Glorious, the Glorious, and by the right of Ṭahbūb 2, Lahūb 2 — the life of everything: no servant disobeyed You but was burned, and no tyrant but was humbled and perished. Hayd Hayd and Hāhā Hu and Wāh Bīh Bīh — they cast him; and He is the Subduer above His servants, to Him the lofty dominion and the exalted might; You are He and He is You, over every thing Powerful" — and I ask You, O Allah, to subject to me Your noble honored angels, the servants of these names and the obedient to these oaths: they rely and comply in what He commanded them of assisting So-and-so son of So-and-so, and fulfill for him what he seeks, and make him at his hand, and he does not oppose him in any matter. "Come, O Wahā, hasten, the hour — may Allah bless you and upon you" — and the following congruence is written, and the paper is fumigated with fragrant Indian aloeswood and Javanese and mastic and a little saffron and hair and khitmī seed and seven grains of the jinn's apple — which is dried coriander — and 7 grains of flax seed. And after it is fumigated, feed it to whom you will, then say: "I have folded the tongue of So-and-so and So-and-so as the folding of this writing" — then turn this paper over his head three times; and if you cannot, turn it over him however you wish, then place it in your turban, and enter upon whom you want. If it is for a specific person by name; and if you want it for all creation, fumigate it with the mentioned incense and place it in his turban, and he sees wonders upon wonders. And this is the description of the previously mentioned congruence: [the 5×5 "Lā ilāha / Kāf-Hā-Yā-'Ayn-Ṣād / Nāfit / al-Ilāha" magic square]. And of the properties of the Throne Verse for love and harmony between those who hate each other: you begin your work in an auspicious hour as we mentioned, then write the names of those who hate each other on a paper and gather it between your hands; then take forty pebbles of male frankincense the size of pepper, and 40 grains of the jinn's apple; then divide the incense in half, twenty of each, and fumigate with two grains of incense until reaching 40, while you recite the Throne Verse, and each time you recite 5 times you say: "Rely, O servants of this verse, in casting love between So-and-so son of So-and-so or So-and-so daughter of So-and-so, by the right of this verse upon you and its blessing with you, and by the right of Him who said to the heavens and the earth: 'Come willingly or unwillingly' — they said: 'We come willingly.'
