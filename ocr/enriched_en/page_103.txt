The heavens and the earth, O Possessor of Majesty and Honor, O Living One, O Self-Subsisting One, O All-Merciful, O Merciful, O One, O One on whom all depend, O Possessor of Majesty
and Honor, O Bestower, O Best of Inheritors, O Ever-Forgiving, O Near One, O All-Hearing, there is no god but You; glory be to You! I have indeed been
among the wrongdoers. O Most Merciful of the merciful, O Hearer of supplication, O our Lord, O our Lord, I ask You by Your Name Allāh, other than whom there is no god, Lord of
the Mighty Throne. Alif Lām Mīm; Kāf Hā Yā ʿAyn Ṣād; Ṭā Sīn Mīm; Ṭā Sīn; Ḥā Mīm ʿAyn Sīn Qāf. God suffices us, and what an excellent Trustee! I ask You by them, by all the verses,
by all the Names, and by the Great Name among them, O You who neither begot nor were begotten, and to whom no one is equal, to bless and grant peace to our master
Muḥammad and his companions; may God bless him and grant him peace. Ask for whatever need you wish: it will be fulfilled by the permission of God Most High. * Know—may God grant me and you success
in understanding His secrets—that, as for Shaykh Abū ʿAbd Allāh Muḥammad ibn Ismāʿīl al-Aḥmī, may God Most High have mercy on him, he is one of the great masters
of realization and the foremost gnostics, a possessor of manifest miracles, splendid spiritual states, and truthful breaths.
