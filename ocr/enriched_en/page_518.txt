"The intimate converse (munājāt) — whoever works by it and takes it as a mention, Allah the Exalted opens to him and subdues for him the knowledge and the excellence and its people, and he attains by it the unveiling, and his speech is refined, and he is correct in utterance and wisdom, and the one who mentions it sees in sleep what he asks about and wills — the clarification of the things he wants to do: whether they are good or evil. So when you want to unveil a secret from the secrets of the Real from the various unveiling sciences and their kinds, Allah eases that for you by the perseverance of the one mentioning it, and he carries it upon the face that we mentioned — of engraving or writing with the perseverance of the mention — for all the invocations are like the presence and the repetition until you mention with it the worlds of that mention, and it did not appear in one or two [times] — all is by perseverance; and if there must be an effect, the repetition is the principle relied upon. And from that is this noble group in the spreading of the glorious sciences, and it is the root of things. And whoever works by it and takes it as a mention, Allah the Exalted opens to him and blesses him and subdues for him the people of knowledge and excellence, and he attains the unveiling of its secrets. And it is six names: al-'Alīm, al-Ḥakīm, al-Khabīr, al-Mubīn, al-Hādī, 'Allām al-Ghuyūb. And it suits that magic which descends in it the command of our Lord to the lowest heaven, saying 'Is there a caller, so I answer him? Is there one seeking forgiveness, so I forgive him? Is there an asker, so I give him his ask?' — and the first third of the last [portion], the ninth hour, and it is attributed to the Moon. And this is a supplication specific to the rectitude of the hearts and the understanding of the sciences — whoever calls by it in the last third of the night and persists upon it until the dawn, after the prayer and the seeking of forgiveness and the mention of 'Allah is Greatest,' Allah the Exalted inspires him all the causes of good altogether. And whoever writes it and carries it, there appear upon him the attributes of beauty and the good state such as he did not know of himself before that. And whoever asks Allah the Exalted by it what befits the rectitude of his state and the rectitude of the spirits and the souls and the understanding of the sciences and what is eased for him of the nearness (walāyah) and the devotion to the mighty religion — he has that, and there appears upon him of the beautiful attributes what gladdens him; and Allah judges by the truth. And this is the supplication: * 'O my God, I ask You by Your hidden name by which You preferred the detail of the articulation in the existents, so You articulate everything in detail — You made manifest in its difference the word of justice, so the languages differed and the names appeared and the acts opposed and the kinds varied and the species were of species and the spheres were ordered, and all that is in a sphere — by You they glorify, and by the compulsion of Your justice they are made just. Carry upon the oppression of my body toward You a gentle contraction, and spread upon [me] the light of Your care a gentle spreading — for You are the absolute disposer, and I am the limited disposer — until I cast from You and my certainty with everything that is influenced by it among all the sciences, until there draws near to my heart the molds of the existents, their gazes humbled and their insights compelled to that compelling secret; and every existent before my witness is eased in its meaning, perfected in it by Your judgment that is not turned back, not repelled. Indeed You judge by the truth, and it is not judged against You — judge by the truth; You are the Truth, and Your names are the truth, and Your acts are the truth, and Your knowledge is the truth, and the relating of all to Your knowledge the truth, and there is naught but the truth — so the truth to the truth from the relation of what I understand until I know what I did not know. Indeed You are the Knower of the unseen — His saying is the truth, and to Him the dominion.
