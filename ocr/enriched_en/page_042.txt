أولى، قال تعالى: {وأن تعفوا أقرب للتقوى}، وقال تعالى: {فمن عفا وأصلح فأجره على الله}، ودخن للخير
بالدختة الطيبة وللشر بضدها، فانظر فإن كان القمر في برج هوائي علق في الهواء، وإن كان في برج ناري
في النار، وإن كان في برج مائي ففي الماء أو ادفنه في قرب الماء، وإن أردت إرساله فيكون في قصبة
فارس مشمسة وأقرأ عليه ما يأتي، وإن كان في تراب ادفنه في التراب تحت عتبة بابه أو بابك إن أردت
جلبه إليك، وإن كان عظماً أجابك، وهذا ما تقرؤه عليه تقول: بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم، اللهم إني أسألك
بأسمائك الحسنى كلها الحمد لله التي إذا وضعت على شيء ذل وخضع، وإذا طلبت به الحسنة أدركت،
وإذا صرفت بهن السيئات صرفت، وبكلماتك التامة التي لو أن ما في الأرض من شجرة أقلام والبحر

معصيتك وبفضلك عمن سواك يا أرحم الراحمين، وأسألك اللهم باسمك الذي لا إله إلا هو الجليل الرحمن
الرحيم اللطيف العظيم الرزاق الغفور المؤمن المهيمن المميت المجيب القريب السميع سريع

وتجود بالكرم والرحمة للناس به، ويشاهد من معاني اللطيف عجائب ويحصل له القبول ويحمد ظاهره
وباطنه لأن فيه اسم الله الأعظم الذي إذا دعي به أجاب وإذا سئل به أعطى، وهو من أعظم الأذكار

فتح الأسرار المكنونة، ولا يداوم على ذكرها أحد إلا ورأى من أمور العالم من الملكوت العلوي
وسخر له كل علم من الملائكة، وهي الكلمات التامات و [~] بدائع الأسرار، فمن ذكرها مع اسمه الكافي
وهو يتمنى شأناً له من حيث لا يدرى ولا يخطر بباله ولا يذكره أحد وهو في مرتبة واهية وهمته

من غلبت عليه شهوته إلا أزال الله عنه ذلك، وللرجع ذلك كابصده من فوائد بسم الله الرحمن

ما لا يحصى عدده إلا الله وازدادت الملائكة إيماناً وتحركت الأفلاك وذلت لعظمتها الأملاك وكانت

[UNCLEAR: the opening clause is incomplete; it says that when the matter was in its dignity or fortunate state and safe from malefic influences, it was more effective.] If you want something else—vengeance against enemies or [UNCLEAR: a damaged phrase concerning a tyrant]—let the Moon be in its disappearance and combustion, connected with Saturn and Mars. Do not do this for anyone who does not deserve it; pardon is better. The Most High said: “That you pardon is nearer to piety.” He also said: “Whoever pardons and makes reconciliation, his reward is with God.” Fumigate for good with pleasant incense, and for evil with its opposite.

Consider the following: if the Moon is in an airy sign, suspend it in the air; if it is in a fiery sign, place it in fire; and if it is in a watery sign, place it in water or bury it near water. If you want to send it, place it in a sunlit reed of Fāris [UNCLEAR: the source’s expression is obscure] and recite over it what follows. If it is in earth, bury it in the earth beneath his doorway or beneath your doorway if you want to bring him to you. If it is a bone, it will answer you.

This is what you recite over it: “In the name of God, the Most Compassionate, the Most Merciful. O God, I ask You by all Your Most Beautiful Names. [UNCLEAR: the source’s grammar then joins this invocation to a statement of praise.] Praise belongs to God, which, when placed upon something, makes it humble and submit; when good is sought by it, good is attained; and when evils are turned away by them, they are turned away. I ask You by Your complete words—if all the trees on earth were pens, and the sea, replenished by seven seas after it, were its ink, the words of God would not be exhausted. Indeed, God is Mighty and Wise.

“O Sufficient, O Protector, O Compassionate, O Gentle, O Provider, O Loving, O Self-Subsisting, O All-Knowing, O All-Encompassing, O Generous, O Bestower, O Expander, O Possessor of bounty, O Giver, O Enricher, O Most Compassionate, O Merciful, O Self-Sufficient, O Reliever, O Tender, O Bestower of favors, O Bountiful, O Good, O Avenger.

“O God, enrich me through Your majesty instead of what You have forbidden; through obedience to You instead of disobedience; and through Your bounty instead of anyone besides You, O Most Merciful of the merciful. I ask You, O God, by Your name besides which there is no deity: the Majestic, the Most Compassionate, the Merciful, the Gentle, the Magnificent, the Provider, the Forgiving, the Giver of security, the Guardian, the Causer of death, the Responsive, the Near, the All-Hearing, the Swift, the Generous, the Possessor of Majesty and Honor, the Possessor of bounty, the Bestower of favors.

“Know that whoever carries these names and remembers them will have his character ennobled and will show generosity and mercy toward people through them. He will witness wonders among the meanings of the Gentle, gain acceptance, and have his outward and inward praised, because the Greatest Name of God is within them. When He is called by it, He answers; when He is asked by it, He gives. It is among the greatest and noblest forms of remembrance.

“Whoever persists in remembering them will have wondrous secrets disclosed to him, the desired matter made easy for him, and what he seeks provided in immediate affairs. Whoever continues these names through half the night will witness wonders according to the measure of his aspiration and persistence, and the hidden secrets will be opened to him. No one persists in remembering them without seeing matters from the higher realm and having every knowledge from the angels placed at his service. They are the perfect words and [~] the marvels of secrets.

“Whoever remembers them together with the name al-Kāfī, while wishing for a matter whose source he does not know, which does not occur to his mind and which no one mentions to him, while he is in a weak rank and his aspiration seeks something higher than the easier [UNCLEAR: the source’s wording is obscure], God Most High will enable him to reach it without toil.

“Whoever continually remembers al-Kāfī and al-Jāmiʿ over something he has lost will find it, and what was lost will return to him. Its name is al-Tafwīḥ, for repelling painful things from great matters. al-Raʾūf is a remembrance for the frightened [UNCLEAR: the source’s phrase about the distressed person is damaged]; whoever is afflicted will find tranquility and his fear will be calmed.

“Whoever persists in remembering it until a state arising from it overcomes him, while his stomach is empty of food, may hold fire in his hand. [UNCLEAR: the source then says that it does not harm him and mentions breathing upon a vessel that boils; the final wording is corrupt.] Its boiling continues by the permission of God Most High. He adds to it al-Ḥalīm, al-Raʾūf, and al-Mannān: ‘O Lord, O Bestower of favors.’

“If these names are written during the Moon’s hour and he encounters someone whose harm he fears, God Most High will suffice him against that person’s evil when he sees him. Whoever is overcome by desire and persists in these three names will have that desire removed by God.
