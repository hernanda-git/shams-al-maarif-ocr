جبار فليكن القمر في المحاق والاحتراق متصلاً بزحل والمريخ، وإياك أن تفعله لغير مستحقه، والعفو
أولى، قال تعالى: {وأن تعفوا أقرب للتقوى}، وقال تعالى: {فمن عفا وأصلح فأجره على الله}، ودخن للخير
بالدختة الطيبة وللشر بضدها، فانظر فإن كان القمر في برج هوائي علق في الهواء، وإن كان في برج ناري
في النار، وإن كان في برج مائي ففي الماء أو ادفنه في قرب الماء، وإن أردت إرساله فيكون في قصبة
فارس مشمسة وأقرأ عليه ما يأتي، وإن كان في تراب ادفنه في التراب تحت عتبة بابه أو بابك إن أردت
جلبه إليك، وإن كان عظماً أجابك، وهذا ما تقرؤه عليه تقول: بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم، اللهم إني أسألك
بأسمائك الحسنى كلها الحمد لله التي إذا وضعت على شيء ذل وخضع، وإذا طلبت به الحسنة أدركت،
وإذا صرفت بهن السيئات صرفت، وبكلماتك التامة التي لو أن ما في الأرض من شجرة أقلام والبحر

معصيتك وبفضلك عمن سواك يا أرحم الراحمين، وأسألك اللهم باسمك الذي لا إله إلا هو الجليل الرحمن

If it was in his honor or his fortune safe from the inauspicious, it was more eloquent, and if you desire other than that, from vengeance upon enemies or the darkness of a tyrant, then let the moon be in the waning and the combustion connected with Saturn and Mars, and beware that you do it for other than one deserving it, and pardon is more fitting. He تعالى said: {And that you pardon is nearer to piety}, and He تعالى said: {So whoever pardons and makes reconciliation, his reward is upon Allah}. And fumigate for the good with the good fumigation and for evil with its opposite. So look: if the moon is in an airy sign, suspend it in the air; and if it is in a fiery sign, then in the fire; and if it is in a watery sign, then in the water, or bury it near water; and if you desire sending it, then it will be in a sunlit reed of Faris, and recite upon it what follows; and if it is in earth, bury it in the earth beneath the threshold of his door or your door if you desire to bring him to you; and if it is bone, it will answer you. And this is what you recite upon it: In the name of Allah, the Merciful, the Compassionate. O Allah, I ask You by all Your most beautiful names. Praise be to Allah, which, if placed upon a thing, it becomes humbled and submissive, and if the good is sought by it, it is attained, and if the evil deeds are turned away by them, they are turned away, and by Your perfect words, that if what is on the earth of trees were pens and the sea were replenishing it after it with seven seas, the words of Allah would not be exhausted. Surely Allah is Mighty, Wise. O Sufficient, O Patron, O Compassionate, O Gentle, O Provider, O Loving, O Self-Subsisting, O Knowing, O Vast, O Generous, O Bestower, O Expander, O Possessor of bounty, O Giver, O Enricher, O Merciful, O Compassionate, O Rich, O Reliever, O Tender, O Bestower, O Bountiful, O Good, O Avenger. O Allah, enrich me by Your majesty from Your unlawful, and by Your obedience from Your disobedience, and by Your bounty from other than You, O Most Merciful of the merciful. And I ask You, O Allah, by Your name by which there is no god but He: the Majestic, the Merciful, the Compassionate, the Gentle, the عظیم, the Provider, the Forgiving, the Believer, the Guardian, the Slayer, the Responsive, the Near, the Hearing, the Swift, the Generous, Possessor of Majesty and Honor, Possessor of bounty, the Bestower. And know that the bearer of these names and the one who remembers them, his character is ennobled and he becomes generous with generosity and mercy toward people through them, and he witnesses from the meanings of the Gentle wonders, and acceptance is obtained for him, and his outward and inward are praised, because in it is the Greatest Name of Allah, by which if He is called upon He answers, and if He is asked by it He gives, and it is among the greatest remembrances and the most noble of them. And whoever persists in its remembrance, wondrous secrets are unveiled to him and the sought is made easy for him and the desired is provided in immediate matters. And whoever continues these names at midnight witnesses wonders according to the measure of his aspiration and his persistence, the opening of the hidden secrets, and no one persists in their remembrance except that he sees from the affairs of the world from the higher dominion and every knowledge from the angels is made subservient to him, and they are the perfect words and [~] marvels of secrets. So whoever remembers them with his sufficient name while he desires a matter for himself from where he does not know and it does not occur to his mind and no one mentions it to him, and he is in a weak rank and his aspiration seeks higher than the easier, Allah تعالى makes reaching it without toil. And whoever keeps up the remembrance of the Sufficient and the Gatherer upon something lost to him, he finds it and what he lost returns to him. And its name is التفويح for repelling the painful from the great matters, and the Compassionate is a remembrance for the one who is frightened from wandering; no distressed one but finds tranquility and his terror is calmed. And he adds to it the Forbearing, the Compassionate, and the Bestower: O Lord, O Bestower. And if these names are written in an hour of the moon and one meets one whose evil he fears, Allah تعالى suffices him against his evil at the time of seeing him.
