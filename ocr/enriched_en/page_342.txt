Then we mentioned the Ẓā', then the Ṭā', then the Yā' — and all that is in the circle, and they are the nine letters which are the root of the emanation, and from them branch all the jafr sources as we clarified. Then we raised the three names which are "Eternal" and its letters, then gathered the other names for it which are "Compassionate, Merciful, Forgiving". And all the letters placed in the circle of the sphere are but the letters of the Compassionate — having gathered between the end and the beginning. * And know — that what is placed in it like Maḥmūd and Muḥammad and Ibrāhīm and Nūḥ; their detail will also come, and the detail of the symbol-letters. And also every name of these placed names in the circle of the sphere — if you saw the principles of its letters it was ten; and for that we placed the Yā' of the completion of the letters which have the symbol and the proof. And the principles are the letters of "Alif-Lām-Mīm", and beneath them the letters of the Greatest Name which is the Name of the Essence. So if you verify the signs you find it the result of the holy Greatest Name from the side of the science of letters. * And as for the letters of the encompassing Name also with the principles — they are the letters of the entered names, stopped outside and inside for the principles of the names, and all is linked part to part, for every name a placement by the way of the science of letters. * And know the way of the Compassionate and His letters — the first of that { Alif-Lām-Mīm. The Romans were overcome in the nearer land, and they, after their overcoming, will be overcome in a few years; the affair is Allah's, before and after } — and Allah knows best. * And we mentioned for Imam Ja'far al-Ṣādiq the wazīrs of the seven climes and what befalls them until the sky arises. And these seven divisions are also not natural divisions — but imaginary lines that the ancients placed from the kings who circled the earth: like Afrīdūn the Nabatean, and Tubba' the Himyarite, and Sulaymān ibn Dāwūd, and Iskandar the Greek, and Ardashīr ibn Bābak the Persian, and others — that they might know the bounds of the lands and the passages. And each clime of them is as a carpet spread — its length from east to west, and its width from south to north. * And know — that the earth with all upon it of mountains and seas also, in relation to the seven, is as the point in the circle. For the sphere is 190 planets — each planet of them the measure of the earth one hundred and ten times, and the greatest of them a planet like the earth one hundred and nineteen times. * And know — and may Allah grant me and you success to His obedience, and guide me and you to the way of His love — that the round of the sphere at the place of the equator is 360 degrees, and the degree 25 parasangs, then the parasang 3 miles, and the mile 1000 cubits, and the cubit 4 forearms, and the forearm 24 fingers, and the finger 6 barley-corns placed belly to back, and the barley-corn 6 hairs from the tail of the mule. Then the first clime is the clime of the heart-core — and it is the clime of Saturn, and its gates the elders. The second the clime of black-bile — and it is the clime of Jupiter, and its gates the scholars. The third the clime of the clear — and it is the clime of Mars, and its gates the commanders. The fourth the clime of love — and it is the clime of the Sun, and its gates the kings. The fifth the clime of Egypt — and it is the clime of Venus, and its gates the poets. The sixth the clime of the intellect — and it is the clime of Mercury, and its gates the sages and the scribes. The seventh the clime of the heart — and it is the clime of the Moon, and its gates the wazīrs. Then each clime of these climes has a gate — so the gate of the first clime is the secret of life, and it is the gate of Ibrāhīm, upon him peace. And the second gate is the secret of knowledge, and it is the gate of Hārūn, upon him peace. And the third the secret of power, and it is the gate of Mūsā, upon him peace. And the fifth the secret of mercy, and it is the gate of Yūsuf, upon him peace. And the sixth the secret of wisdom, and it is the gate of 'Īsā, upon him peace. And the seventh the secret of action, and it is the gate of Ādam, upon him peace.
