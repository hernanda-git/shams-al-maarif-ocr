The Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, said to Ṭalḥah ibn ʿUbayd Allāh when his she-camel stumbled and threw him down: “If you had said Bismillah al-Raḥmān al-Raḥīm, the angels would have lifted you while the people looked on.” So look at the blessing of this noble Name: the angels lift the one who utters it, the devils depart when it is mentioned, and poison is neutralized when it is mentioned. Your master has made its worth known to you, and the Lord of Might—how wondrous are its virtue and its secret! Do not make a movement or remain still except by God’s permission, and [UNCLEAR: the source’s final imperative is obscure]; all of this is contained within Bismillah al-Raḥmān al-Raḥīm.

Jesus used it to treat pains and ailments. Whoever writes the form of the bāʾ on Friday, fasts the preceding Thursday, and carries it on his right upper arm—God will open his breast, remove laziness from him, and make blessing appear upon him. He will see the lights of the angels, and their higher and lower forms will appear. A standing form, complete in shape and pleasant in scent, will appear and utter the bāʾ. He will see the light fixed, its radiance not changing. When the name of the bāʾ is mentioned, its light appears upon its essence. It is among the hidden names. [UNCLEAR: the source’s description of the appearing form and light is obscure.]

These letters, when they occur in a name containing the bāʾ, benefit every dry pain and every difficult pain, which becomes easier by permission of God Most High. They are al-Barr, al-Bārī, al-Bāqī, and al-Bāʿith; within them is the secret of guidance, and all of this is contained in Bismillah al-Raḥmān al-Raḥīm. The standing alif is the head of the bāʾ, and it is the extension within the essence of the bāʾ, as follows. The bāʾ appears in His Most High names al-Baṣīr, al-Badīʿ, and al-Bāṭin. Each name therefore has a particular meaning. al-Barr, for people of righteousness, helps with righteous deeds and honoring parents.

Whoever mentions it for any matter two hundred and thirty-three times, after combining it with whichever of its names he wishes, should proceed as follows: take, for example, the name ʿAmr and its letters; then take the first letter of the name al-Barr and place it at the beginning of the line. Next take the first letter of the name ʿAmr and place it after that, continuing to the end of the two names, as follows: alif, ʿayn, mīm, rāʾ, wāw, bāʾ, rāʾ, wāw. Then take it, break it apart, and expand it until the first name returns. Then make the last one and drop it: ʿayn, lām, mīm, bāʾ, wāw, rāʾ, hāʾ. Then make the first one last and drop the last; [UNCLEAR: the source says that four mixed lines remain but visibly lists only] alif, rāʾ, ʿayn; rāʾ, lām, bāʾ; mīm.

Write them on whatever you wish, place them in your pocket, and recite over them the following. You say: “[UNCLEAR: the invocation begins with ‘O Lord,’ ‘mīm,’ ‘Lord of piety,’ ‘Lord of lords,’ ‘my nurturer,’ and ‘the Gentle One’; the remaining letter sequences and wording are damaged.]” The continuation contains [UNCLEAR: damaged fragments about the flow of God’s gentleness and rejoicing in the sweetness of that sea, through which spirits know His secrets]. “Grant me a name from the names of Your power by which I may supplicate You, and [UNCLEAR: protect me] from the evil of what You have created in the earth, what emerges from it, what descends from heaven, and what ascends in it. As for His Most High name al-Bārī, it is for healing illnesses and swellings. al-Bāʿith has properties for both of them that will be given in their proper places, if God Most High wills.

As for the letter sīn: when God Most High created it from the world of His command, He sent down with it nine thousand eight hundred angels. It is the first letter among the letters of the Greatest Name. The Greatest Name has an outward and an inward: through its outward, the heavens were established, and through its inward, the higher realms of the Footstool and the Throne were established. Therefore the sīn occurs at the beginning of the heavens, and in that is the rank of the Footstool.
