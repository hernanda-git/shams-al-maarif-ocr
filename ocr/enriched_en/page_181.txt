He said: "And whoever fears falling into a matter he cannot bear — let him abound in its remembrance, for Allah, exalted, delivers him from it; and whoever recites the Throne Verse before leaving his house is not afflicted by anything until he returns; and that he give charity of something of his wealth to the poor, for it is a cause of his safety." And a group passed by a man standing in a lion's den, his horse grazing around him — they left him and said to him: "Do you not fear, while you are in this place and there are beasts of prey?" So he raised his head and said: "I am ashamed before Him to fear other than Him." And whoever realises this name — Allah preserves him in all his times and movements, as narrated from Abū al-Daqā'iq that he said: "Ten thousand dinars came to one of the righteous, and he said: 'My God, I am in need of them, and if I do not guard them well, hand them to You and return them to me at the time of my need for them' — and he gave them in charity to the poor and needy. So whenever he needed something, he asked Allah and He gave him what he asked, until He gave him multiples of them; and Allah is the Giver."
