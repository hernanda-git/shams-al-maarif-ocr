بِسْمِ اللهِ الرَّحْمَنِ الرَّحِيمِ اللَّهُمَّ أَنْتَ الْمَلِكُ الْحَمْدُ حَمِدْتَ نَفْسَكَ بِنَفْسِكَ فِي أَزَلِ قُدْسِكَ ثُمَّ أَعْلَمْتَ الْخَاصَّةَ مِنْ عِبَادِكَ يَحْمَدُونَكَ بِمَا أَوْلَيْتَهُمْ مِنْ لُطْفِ أُنْسِكَ وَأَظْهَرْتَ مِنَ الْإِنْعَامِ مَا أَوْجَدَ الْحَمْدَ وَالثَّنَاءَ مِنَ الْخَاصِّ وَالْعَامِّ عَلَى مَمَرِّ الشُّهُورِ وَالْأَعْوَامِ يَا هَيْبَةَ الْجَلَالِ وَيَا وَاهِبَ أُنْسِ الْجَمَالِ وَبِتَمَامِ أَوْصَافِ الْكَمَالِ أَنْ تَجْعَلَنِي عِنْدَكَ مَحْمُوداً مُتَهَجِّداً بِكَ مَسْرُوراً بِنُورِ الْعَمَلِ مَعَ أُولِي الْأَلْبَابِ مَرْفُوعاً عَنْ ظُلْمَةِ الْحِجَابِ مُشَاهِداً لِلْكَمَالِ وَالْجَمَالِ إِنَّكَ أَنْتَ اللهُ حَمِيدُ الْفِعَالِ مَنْ لَازَمَ عَلَيْهِ رَفَعَ اللهُ قَدْرَهُ وَسَهُلَتْ عَلَيْهِ الْأُمُورُ الصِّعَابُ.

( فَصْلٌ فِي اسْمِهِ تَعَالَى الْمُحْصِي )
اعْلَمْ أَنَّ الْمُحْصِيَ هُوَ الْعَالِمُ بِالشَّيْءِ جُمْلَةً وَتَفْصِيلاً وَتَقَدَّمَ مَعْنَى الْعَالِمِ فِي اسْمِهِ الْعَلِيمُ وَلَهُ خَلْوَةٌ وَفِيهِ حَرْفٌ مِنَ الِاسْمِ الْأَعْظَمِ وَمَنْ تَلَاهُ عَدَدَهُ نَزَلَ عَلَيْهِ الْمَلَكُ مُحْصَائِيلُ وَهُوَ يُسَبِّحُ وَيَقُولُ سُبْحَانَ الْعَالِمِ خَفِيَّاتِ الْأُمُورِ وَمُحْصِيهَا وَيَأْتِي لِلذَّاكِرِ فِي النَّوْمِ أَوِ الْيَقَظَةِ وَلَهُ مُرَبَّعٌ نَافِعٌ لِبَلَادَةِ الذِّهْنِ يُكْتَبُ وَيُسْقَى عَلَى الرِّيقِ ثَلَاثَةَ أَسَابِيعَ وَيُرْسَمُ فِي لَوْحٍ مِنْ فِضَّةٍ وَيَحْمِلُهُ قَلِيلُ الْفَهْمِ فَإِنَّ اللهَ يَشْرَحُ صَدْرَهُ وَيَفْهَمُ وَذِكْرُهُ الْبَسْمَلَةُ وَاللَّهُمَّ أَنْتَ الْمُحْصِي لِلْمَوْجُودَاتِ قَبْلَ وُجُودِهَا عَلَى الصُّوَرِ وَالْمِثَالِ وَأَنْتَ الْعَالِمُ بِمَثَاقِيلِ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالْعَرْشِ وَالْكُرْسِيِّ وَالْحُجُبِ الْعَوَالِي وَعَدَدِ النُّجُومِ وَأَوْزَانِ الْأَثْقَالِ الثِّقَالِ وَأَوْزَانِ الْأَرْضِ وَالْجِبَالِ وَقَطْرِ الْبِحَارِ وَالْأَمْطَارِ وَعَدَدِ جَمِيعِ الْحَيَوَانَاتِ وَأَوْرَاقِ الْأَشْجَارِ وَعَدَدِ الرَّمْلِ وَالْأَحْجَارِ وَعَدَدِ الْإِنْسِ وَالْجَانِّ وَعَدَدِ مَا يَصْدُرُ مِنْهُمْ مِنَ الْأَنْفَاسِ أَسْأَلُكَ بِعِلْمِكَ الْمُحْصِي لِجَمِيعِ الْمَعْلُومَاتِ مِمَّا عَلَّمْتَنَا فِي الْأَرْضِ وَالسَّمَاوَاتِ وَمَا لَمْ نَعْلَمْهُ مِنْ أَسْرَارِ الْمُغَيَّبَاتِ أَنْ تَسْتُرَ عَوْرَتِي وَتُؤَمِّنَ رَوْعَاتِي وَتَغْفِرَ سَيِّئَاتِي وَتُضَاعِفَ حَسَنَاتِي وَتَحْشُرَنِي مَعَ أَوْلِيَائِكَ وَأَنْبِيَائِكَ وَرُسُلِكَ وَتُزِلَّنِي دَرَجَاتِي وَأَسْأَلُكَ أَنْ تُطْلِعَنِي عَلَى حَقَائِقِ الْمَوْجُودَاتِ يَا اللهُ يَا مُحْصِيَ الْمَوْجُودَاتِ يَا رَبِّ مَنْ وَاظَبَ عَلَى هَذَا الذِّكْرِ فَتَحَ اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَأَطْلَعَهُ عَلَى حَقَائِقِ الْأَشْيَاءِ.

( فَصْلٌ فِي اسْمِهِ تَعَالَى الْمُبْدِئُ الْمُعِيدُ )
اعْلَمْ أَنَّ الْمُبْدِئَ هُوَ الَّذِي يُوجِدُ إِيجَادَ مَا لَمْ يَكُنْ مَسْبُوقاً بِمِثْلِهِ وَالْمُعِيدُ أَيِ الَّذِي يُعِيدُ مِنَ الْعَدَمِ إِلَى الْوُجُودِ وَاللهُ تَعَالَى يُبْدِئُ الْخَلَائِقَ ثُمَّ يُعِيدُهُمْ فَالْأَشْيَاءُ مِنْهُ بَدَتْ كُلُّهَا وَإِلَيْهِ تَعُودُ وَاسْمُهُ الْمُبْدِئُ مَنْ تَلَاهُ فِي رِيَاضَةٍ وَخَلْوَةٍ فَإِنَّ خَادِمَهُ كَهْيَائِيلُ يُطْلِعُهُ عَلَى حَقَائِقِ الْإِبْدَاعَاتِ وَهُوَ مُوَكَّلٌ بِيَدِهِ كُلُّ شَيْءٍ فَتَبْدَأُ فِي شَيْءٍ وَوَكِّلْهُ بِهِ فَإِنَّهُ يَكُونُ فِي غَايَةِ النَّجَاحِ.
( وَأَمَّا اسْمُهُ تَعَالَى الْمُعِيدُ ) خَادِمُهُ حَفْيَائِيلُ وَمَرْحَلَتُهُ كَمَا تَقَدَّمَ وَتِلَاوَتُهُ عَدَدُهُ فِي خَلْوَةٍ يُعْطِي الذَّاكِرَ قُوَّةَ حُضُورِهِ وَخَوَاصُّهُ إِذَا ضَاعَ لِأَحَدٍ شَيْءٌ وَذَكَرَهُ عَدَدَهُ رَدَّ اللهُ عَلَيْهِ مَا ضَاعَ وَهُوَ مِنْ أَذْكَارِ الصَّالِحِينَ وَإِذَا كُتِبَ عَلَى فِضَّةٍ وَحَمَلَهُ مَلِكٌ أَوْ أَمِيرٌ رَفَعَ اللهُ قَدْرَهُ وَنَفَذَتْ كَلِمَتُهُ فِي رَعِيَّتِهِ وَلِهَذَيْنِ الِاسْمَيْنِ مُرَبَّعٌ يُعْطِي صَاحِبَهُ قُوَّةً تَامَّةً فِي سَائِرِ أُمُورِهِ وَيَكُونُ لَهُ شَرَفٌ عِنْدَ مَنْ يَرَاهُ وَهَذِهِ صُورَتُهُ بِالصَّحِيفَةِ الْآتِيَةِ.

وَأَمَّا

English:
— 478 —

"(In the name of Allah the Merciful, the Compassionate) O Allah, You are the King, the Praised — You praised Your Self by Your Self in the eternity of Your holiness, then You made known to the specific of Your servants that they praise You by what You favored them of the gentleness of Your intimacy and manifested of the favors what brought forth the praise and the laudation from the specific and the general over the passage of the months and the years. O Awe of the majesty, and O Bestower of the intimacy of the beauty, and by the completion of the attributes of perfection, that You make me with You praised, vigilantly-praising You, rejoicing in the light of the deed, with the people of understanding, raised above the darkness of the veil, witnessing the perfection and the beauty. Indeed You are Allah, Praised in actions. Whoever perseveres upon it, Allah raised his estimation and eased for him the difficult matters. (Section on His name the Exalted, al-Muḥṣī) Know that al-Muḥṣī is the Knower of the thing in sum and detail; and the meaning of the Knower preceded in His name al-'Alīm. And it has a retreat, and in it is a letter of the Greatest Name; and whoever recites it its number, the angel MuḥṣĀ'ĪL descends upon him, and he glorifies and says 'Glory be to the Knower of the hidden matters of the affairs and their enumeration'; and he comes to the one remembering in sleep or wakefulness. And it has a beneficial square for dullness of the mind — written and given on the empty stomach for three weeks, and drawn on a tablet of silver and carried by the one of little understanding, so Allah expands his breast and he understands. And its mention is the basmala — and 'O Allah, You are the Enumerator of the existents before their existence in forms and likenesses, and You are the Knower of the weights of the heavens and the Throne and the Footstool and the high veils and the number of the stars and the weights of the heavy burthens and the weights of the earth and the mountains and the drops of the seas and the rains and the number of all the animals and the leaves of the trees and the number of the sand and the stones and the number of the humans and the jinn and the number of what issues from them of the breaths. I ask You by Your knowledge enumerating all the knowns, of what You taught us in the earth and the heavens and what we did not know of the secrets of the unseen, that You conceal my nakedness and secure my terrors and forgive my evils and multiply my good deeds and gather me with Your saints and Your prophets and Your messengers and elevate my degrees. And I ask You that You make me reach the realities of the existents, O Allah, O Enumerator of the existents, O Lord — whoever perseveres upon this mention, Allah opens for him and makes him reach the realities of the things.' (Section on His name the Exalted, al-Mubdi' al-Mu'īd) Know that al-Mubdi' is He who brings into existence what was not preceded by its like, and al-Mu'īd — i.e. He who returns from non-existence to existence. And Allah the Exalted originates the creatures then returns them, so the things all began from Him and to Him they return. And His name al-Mubdi' — whoever recites it in spiritual practice and retreat, his servant KAHĪ'ĪL makes him reach the realities of the innovations, and he is entrusted with everything in his hand, so you begin in a thing and entrust him with it, and it will be in the utmost success. (And as for His name the Exalted, al-Mu'īd) his servant is ḤAFYĀ'ĪL, and his stage is as preceded; and its recitation its number in retreat gives the one remembering the strength of his presence. And of its special properties: if something is lost to one and he mentions it its number, Allah returns to him what was lost; and it is of the invocations of the righteous. And if it is written on silver and carried by a king or a prince, Allah raises his estimation and his word is executed among his subjects. And for these two names there is a square that gives its possessor complete power in all his affairs, and he has honor before who sees him — and this is its figure in the following page.
