(And this is another invocation)
So invoke with it in important matters, and know its worth. It is this:

"In the name of God, the Merciful, the Compassionate. Praise be to God, Lord of the worlds": praise surpassing the praise of those who praise, Lord of the first and the last; praise that will bring me approval and protection with the Lord of the worlds. "The Merciful, the Compassionate": He who spread out the regions and chose Moses, the one God spoke to. God is the Reviver of bones when they are decayed. The Merciful, the Compassionate: these are two noble Names and a cure for every sick person. "Master of the Day of Judgment": He who has no rival in kingship, nor companion, nor minister, nor counselor; rather, He was before the existence of the world and all the worlds together. You are my encompassing guard and my protection from all devils, my help against the far and the near, and my face against the opposing kinds [UNCLEAR: the sense of "my face against the opposing kinds"]. "You alone we worship," with acknowledgment, shame over sins, and fervor [UNCLEAR: the sense of "fervor" here]. We repent to You of sins, and we bear witness that there is no god but God alone, with no partner, no rival, and no likeness to Him, Possessor of Majesty and Honor; and we bear witness that our master and our prophet Muhammad is His servant and His messenger, may God bless him and grant him peace. "And You alone we ask for help," with every need and every affair of this world and the Hereafter, O Guide of those who lead others astray. "Guide us to the straight path, the path of those upon whom You have bestowed favor," from among the prophets, the utterly truthful, the martyrs, and the righteous. "Not those upon whom wrath has fallen, nor those who go astray. Amen."
