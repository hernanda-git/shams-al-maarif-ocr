Shahat 2, Ashhat 2, al-Muqsiṭ, al-waḥā, yā Maymūn, wa-Shahdān, al-waḥā yā Shahdān, al-ʿajal (haste) [UNCLEAR: the formula's wording and segmentation are uncertain]. Undertake such-and-such. I adjure you by the might of God's might, by the light of God's face, by the Opening of the Book, and by what the Pen has recorded from God: you must answer and hasten to fulfill my need, which is such-and-such.’ ‘His command, when He intends something,’ etc.; Sūrat Yā Sīn: recite it 3 times.
The Prophet, peace be upon him, said: ‘Whoever recites al-Fātiḥah at bedtime, and with it recites al-Ikhlāṣ 3 times and the two surahs of refuge, is safe from everything except death.’ When al-Ḥasan and al-Ḥusayn fell ill, the Prophet, peace be upon him, was distressed, so God revealed to him: ‘Recite a surah containing no fāʾ, for fāʾ is from tafaʾul (taking a favorable omen). Recite it 40 times over a vessel containing water, and wash with it his hands, his feet, his head, his face, and the rest of his body, for God will heal him of every pain.’
Some scholars said: ‘Any sick person who writes al-Fātiḥah in a clean vessel, washes the writing off with water, and drinks it will be healed by God.’ Whoever suffers from much forgetfulness, writes it in a glass vessel, washes the writing off, and drinks from it will lose his forgetfulness. Whoever recites it frequently will have his heart and inner being purified of all promptings of the lower self and satanic suggestions. Whoever writes it in a glass bowl, washes the writing off with pure oil, and rubs the oil on the sciatic nerve and the site of back pain will have the pain leave him; it benefits wind, paralysis, and every dry or wet illness.
Whoever writes al-Fātiḥah in a gold vessel at the first [UNCLEAR: moment or hour] of Friday with musk, saffron, and camphor, washes the writing off with water, and puts it in a bottle, then wipes his face with some of it when he wishes to enter the presence of rulers, will gain acceptance. Whoever enters the presence of someone whose evil he fears and recites al-Fātiḥah will be safe from that person's evil. Ibn al-Shaʿbī complained of back pain, so [UNCLEAR: ‘say to him’; damaged narrative connection]: ‘You should use the foundation of the Qur'an, which is al-Fātiḥah.’ He kept to it, wrote it down, washed the writing off, and drank, and was healed. Ibn ʿAbbās said: ‘Everything has a foundation; the foundation of the Qur'an is al-Fātiḥah, and the foundation of al-Fātiḥah is the basmalah.’
Ibn al-Qayyim said: ‘The best treatment is with al-Fātiḥah. That is because I stayed in Mecca for a long time, during which an illness afflicted me, and I could find neither a physician nor medicine. So I said: “I shall treat myself with al-Fātiḥah.” I did so and saw that it had a tremendous effect. I would therefore recommend it to anyone complaining of severe pain, and he would recover.’
Whoever recites al-Fātiḥah 19 times when entering the presence of a tyrant will be safe from him. Whoever writes it as many times as the number of its letters on Friday and carries it will be protected from the assaults of jinn and humans. Whoever says every day three times, ‘In the name of God, the Merciful, the Compassionate; in the name of God, the best of names; in the name of God, Lord of the earth and the sky; in the name of God, with whose name nothing on earth or in the sky causes harm, and He is the All-Hearing, the All-Knowing,’ will not be harmed by anything.
Whoever writes the letters with which the surahs begin in a glass bowl, washes the writing off with rainwater, and gives it to a poisoned person to drink will find that the poison has no effect on him; this has been tried repeatedly. And [UNCLEAR: sallama; the wording before ‘the Prophet’ is damaged] the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him: whoever places his hand on his head and recites the saying of God Most High, ‘He is God, there is no god but He, Knower of the unseen and the witnessed,’ to the end of the surah will have healing.
