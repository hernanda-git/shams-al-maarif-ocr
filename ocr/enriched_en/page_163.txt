…a poor man from the best food, or give charity of seven dirhams to seven poor ones — for it suffices against the evil of whoever intends [harm]. * And let us close this chapter with mention of a strange [invocation] and a wondrous wird (litany), by which no servant converses with Allah but he is freed, no captive but he is released, no fearful one but he is secured, no poor one but he is enriched, no abased one but he is honoured — and in it is a marvellous meaning for subduing the tyrants and cutting off the root of the wrongdoers and corrupters. Whoever writes it and carries it, every rebellious tyrant and defiant Satan is humbled before him, and no one sees him but loves him. * And whoever abundant in its remembrance, Allah revives his heart with the light of gnosis and preserves him in his family, wealth, dependents, and self, and suffices him against the evil he fears; and no king remembers it but his dominion expands and his word takes effect; and in it is the Greatest Name of Allah; and whoever remembers it before a tyrant in his anger — he is calmed; and whoever asks Allah by it for a need — He gives him what he asked. So understand tawḥīd by this guarded secret, and seek help by it from many a dispositional invocation of this kind; and approaching it by 'Arafah or the Gate of the One-Who-Arrives, and mentioning it — the comprehensive name of the great lovers: "Living, Self-Sustaining, no Lord, Gate of the Beginnings." And were a person to disclose the secrets of this shining ruby and the radiant secret — from the side of its numeric secrets, its letter-effects, its luminous mothers, and its wafq arrangements — [he would find] it does not encompass ten [parts] of that. And it is fitting for kings, commanders, notables, the righteous, the excellent scholars, and the skilled sages to turn to it in the first [hour] of Friday, or Sunday, or the Day of 'Arafah, or the two 'Īds, or the Day of 'Āshūrā', or the middle night of Sha'bān, or the 27th night of Ramaḍān, or the first of every month, or the nights of Friday — that you may win the two goods of this world and the next and the greatest happiness. * And this is the blessed wird: (In the name of Allah, the Most Merciful, the Most Compassionate) "There is no power nor strength except by Allah the High, the Great.
