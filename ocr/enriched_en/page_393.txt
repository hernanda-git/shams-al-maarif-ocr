حارا على حار يصيران جسدا واحدا ثم تأخذ من الشمس ١ ومن القمر ٢ وتسبكها
ومن الزهرة ٣ ومن المريخ ٤ ويسكان ثم تلقى المسبوك الاول على الثاني كافعات أولا حارا على حار
يصيران جسدا واحدا ثم اجمع السبيكة التي من الجدول الاول على الذي من الجدول الثاني يصيرا

The description of the working: you take of Mars, free of its blackness, 1; and of Venus with the shadow cut, 2; then — you take of the weighed Moon, 3; and of the Sun, 4; and cast. Then cast the second casting upon the first — hot upon hot — and they become one body. Then you take of the Sun 1, and of the Moon 2, and cast them; and of Venus 3, and of Mars 4, and cast. Then you throw the first casting upon the second like the falling — first hot upon hot, they become one body. Then gather the ingot from the first table upon that from the second table, and they become one ingot. File it with a fine file, and amalgamate it with the like of pure quicksilver, and volatilize it from it — do that 3 times; then after that cast it a second time firmly, [weighing] 18 carats.

( The description of the descent of Mars for this method ) You take an ounce of fresh iron-filings, amalgamate it with the like of soft quicksilver with one part verdigris and lemon-water by grinding, so it swallows its due with half an ounce of 'uqāb and two dirhams of tankār; and grind it with the mixture and cast it a second time with vitriol and 'uqāb and natron — as a lining, a bed, and a cover; do that to it 3 [times]. ( The description of calcining vitriol ) It is heated and quenched in vinegar 3 [times] and calcines. ( The description of washing Mars ) You deserve it with the like of andarānī, and coat it with egg-white, and place it in the lunar [vessel], and lead upon it until it heats; you fry it to hardness and wash with water and salt with the pestle, doing to it until it is purified of its filth. ( The description of adorning the Moon ) You take cotton-seed and Yemeni alum and human hair; they are ground and twisted with a fresh egg and made into grains, and you anoint the Moon with them while it circles until its color and weight please you. ( The description of cutting the shadow of Venus ) Natron and alum and hūtīt-salt are taken, parts, ground separately and together; then you take a ripe eggplant, bruise it, and soak it in hot vinegar 3 times, and filter, and what was mentioned is placed, half, a day, and divided 8; and you take of the patched Venus, heat it, and quench it in fresh water once, and from it are taken the aforementioned weights closely — so conceal your affair. ( The description of the balance of hair ) Hair.
If you seek the specific of the balance * by the D take, and the K — they are the weights:
from chrysolite two hamzatayn yahram * and the Sun hā and the yadar dāl the second
and what is in them other than iron from 'afar * so proceed and suspend pure fires
there comes to you a pure sun to its Lord * so thank the One, gracious Lord
Its explanation: Mars red, descended, 8; Venus sick: Moon 4; Sun 8 — it makes a pure sun. ( The description of fermenting Mars ) You take an ounce of fresh iron-filings and grind with it a dirham of zinjifar and the like of green Cypriot vitriol and the like of red 'ilm, and anoint with egg-yolk, and bury a night doing that to it until the filings redden; then descend it with oil and natron, roast; and the tankār from crucible to crucible, another, there descends a red chick. ( The description of the garden of Venus ) You take an ounce and ... with them, and it is a circle of barūd and white glass powdered, and they are an ounce and a half.
