English:
— 188 —

…and the great ones triumph over their enemies; and it is written in the dignity of the Sun for judges and scholars, in the dignity of Jupiter for scribes and ministers, in the dignity of Mercury for the shaykh and the poor in the dignity of Saturn, gradated — so it is of the stored secrets and hidden properties; and its cutting points to the honoured stone in which is the Greatest Name of Allah; and whoever recites these noble names one hundred times and gestures to the destruction of a wrongdoer or tyrant — Allah, exalted, destroys him; and he places it in his house or place, and it is so; and it cannot be explained by explicit expression but is illumined by its understanding from his habit of understanding from Allah, exalted — so blessed is Allah, the best of creators. And these noble names — whoever engraves them on paper in the first [hour] of Friday while facing the qibla in purity and remembrance and places them at his head — Allah, exalted, provides him awe, glory, and dignity; and everyone who sees him loves him, magnifies him, and has his breast expanded. And Allah says the truth, and He guides the way; and this is its form as you see [the web of the great names: Allah, Aḥad, Wāḥid, Jawād, Wahhāb, Ḥayy, Mūjid, Dā'im, Walī, Mujīb, Wadūd, Awwal, Hādī — in a square placed within the square of Sūrat al-Ikhlāṣ].

( The Sixty-Eighth Chapter: On His name, exalted, { al-Ṣamad } ) This great name and noble secret — whoever abounds in its remembrance, his need diminishes forever; and it should be taken as a remembrance by the masters of the blessed spiritual exercises for what creation is in need of of food, drink, sleep, and other than it; and if a possessor of a true state persists in its remembrance, needs return to him; and al-Ṣamad fits those sick with hunger, so when he remembers it he does not feel pain.
