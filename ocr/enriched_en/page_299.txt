( The twenty-seventh chapter, on the seventh pattern of the Names of Allah (exalted) and what hidden blessings they have )
And know that among the Names of Allah (exalted) are: al-Hakīm (the Wise), al-Ra'ūf (the Compassionate), al-Wadūd (the Loving), al-Ghafūr (the Forgiving), al-Hannān (the Affectionate), al-Latīf (the Subtle), al-Hafīz (the Preserver), al-Raqīb (the Watchful), al-Barr (the Good), al-Shāfī (the Healer). These ten names of Allah (exalted)—from the support of their secret is the conciliation of discordant hearts, the inclination of spirits, the secret of endearment, and the casting of tenderness and mercy in the remembrance, the relief of anguish, the vanishing of distresses, the protection from jinn and men, the adherence of shyness, the continuance of health in religion and body, and the continuous arrival of good supports upon the rememberer ۿ. As for the two names (exalted) al-Hakīm and al-Ra'ūf, they are two great names in the acceptance of repentance and the pardon of sin; no one mentions them who was ruined by sins except he repents and [receives] pardon for what he earned, and his repentance is accepted and he is protected ۿ. Whoever aligns His (exalted) name al-'Afw (the Pardoning) in a square and carries it, his evil deeds are with people as if they were good deeds ۿ. As for the names (exalted) al-Wadūd and al-Ghafūr, they are two majestic names in the acceptance of repentance; if he repeats them, hearts gather to him with great love, and whoever perseveres in his remembrance morning and evening never sees an enemy for himself, and everyone who sees him loves him and his passion intensifies ۿ.
