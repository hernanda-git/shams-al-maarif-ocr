الشَّيخُ الصَّالِحُ ابْنُ عَبدِ اللهِ حُسَينِ بنِ مَنصُورٍ أَنَّهُ فَعَلَ ذَلِكَ فَأَتَاهُ الخَادِمُ بِعَشَرَةِ آلَافِ دِينَارٍ وَحَكَى

يَنطِق لَهُ بِكَلِمَةٍ وَلَمَّا فَتَحَ عَينَيهِ وَجَدَهُ بَينَ يَدَيهِ فَلَمَّا أَفَاقَ وَطَالَ الأَمرُ وَلَم يَنطِقِ انصَرَفَ الخَادِمُ
عَنهُ وَلَم يَحصُل مِنهُ ضَرَرٌ فَعَلَيكَ أَيُّهَا الطَّالِبُ بِثَبَاتِ الجَأشِ فَإِنَّ خَادِمَ هَذِهِ السُّورَةِ مِنَ الجِنِّ
المُؤمِنِينَ وَهُوَ لَم يَضُرَّ الطَّالِبَ وَالعَزِيمَةُ وَالدَّعْوَةُ هِيَ السُّورَةُ الشَّرِيفَةُ بِتَمَامِهَا وَكَذَا البَخُورُ ﵀ وَاعْلَم
أَيُّهَا الوَاصِلُ أَنَّهَا مِنَ الأَسرَارِ المُخَصَّصَةِ وَأَنَّهَا مِن كُتُبِ الأَنبِيَاءِ وَالأَولِيَاءِ وَأَسرَارِهِم نَفَعَنَا اللهُ بِهِم ﵀ وَهِيَ
هَذِهِ تَقُولُ ﴿ بِسمِ اللهِ الرَّحمَنِ الرَّحِيمِ قُل أُوحِيَ إِلَى ﴾ اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسأَلُكَ يَا مُنزِلَ الوَحيِ مِن فَوقِ سَبعِ
سَمَاوَاتٍ أَن تُيَسِّرَ لِي مَا أَنَا قَاصِدُهُ وَطَالِبُهُ وَتُسَخِّرَ لِي خُدَّامَ هَذِهِ السُّورَةِ المُبَارَكَةِ يُطِيعُونَنِي فِي جَمِيعِ
مَا أُرِيدُهُ إِنَّكَ عَلَى كُلِّ شَيءٍ قَدِيرٌ ﵀ اللَّهُمَّ يَا مَن إِلَيهِ يَهْرُبُ الهَارِبُونَ وَيَا مَن فِي عَفوِهِ يَطمَعُ
الطَّامِعُونَ ﴿ إِنَّهُ استَمَعَ نَفَرٌ مِنَ الجِنِّ ﴾ اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسأَلُكَ يَا مَن يَسمَعُ وَيَرَى وَيُارَى وَهُوَ بِالمَنظَرِ
الأَعلَى ﴿ فَقَالُوا إِنَّا سَمِعْنَا قُرآنًا عَجَبًا يَهْدِي إِلَى الرُّشْدِ فَآمَنَّا بِهِ وَلَن نُّشْرِكَ بِرَبِّنَا أَحَدًا ﴾ اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي
أَسأَلُكَ بِحَقِّ مَن آمَنَ بِكَ مِنَ المُؤمِنِينَ بِأَنبِيَائِهِم وَبِنَبِيِّكَ وَبِكَ وَبِالسَّائِلِينَ أَن تُسَخِّرَ لِي خَادِمَ هَذِهِ
السُّورَةِ يَكُونُ لِي عَونًا عَلَى مَا أُرِيدُهُ ﴿ وَأَنَّهُ تَعَالَى جَدُّ رَبِّنَا مَا اتَّخَذَ صَاحِبَةً وَلَا وَلَدًا ﴾ اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسأَلُكَ
يَا مَن لَم يَتَّخِذ صَاحِبَةً وَلَا وَلَدًا أَن تَنطِقَ لِي بِالحِكمَةِ وَلِسَانِي بِالمَعرِفَةِ وَأَن تَكُونَ عَونًا لِي وَمُعِينًا
وَأَن تُسَخِّرَ لِي قُلُوبَ خَلقِكَ أَجمَعِينَ ﴿ وَأَنَّهُ كَانَ رِجَالٌ مِنَ الإِنسِ يَعُوذُونَ بِرِجَالٍ مِنَ الجِنِّ فَزَادُوهُم
رَهَقًا وَأَنَّهُم ظَنُّوا كَمَا ظَنَنتُم أَن لَن يَبعَثَ اللهُ أَحَدًا ﴾ اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسأَلُكَ يَا رَافِعَ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَيَا خَالِقَ
المَخلُوقَاتِ وَيَا مُكَوِّنَ الأَكوَانِ وَيَا مُدَبِّرَ الزَّمَانِ وَيَا مُنزِلَ التَّورَاةِ وَالإِنجِيلِ وَالزَّبُورِ وَالفُرقَانِ
وَبِأَفضَلِ بَنِي آدَمَ عَلَى جَمِيعِ المَخلُوقَاتِ يَا حَيُّ يَا قَيُّومُ يَا مَن لِأَتَامَ يَا مَن سَخَّرَ الجِنَّ وَالإِنسَ لِسُلَيمَانَ
عَلَيهِ السَّلَامُ أَسأَلُكَ اللَّهُمَّ أَن تُسَخِّرَ لِي جَمِيعَ خَلقِكَ وَجَمِيعَ الأَشيَاءِ وَأَشهَرَ ذِكرِي فِي الخَيرِ يَا حَيُّ يَا قَيُّومُ
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسأَلُكَ بِالاسمِ العَظِيمِ المَخزُونِ المَكنُونِ بِالنُّورِ الكَرِيمِ أَن تُسَخِّرَ لِي رُوحَانِيَّةَ هَذِهِ السُّورَةِ
حَتَّى يُجِيبُونِي وَيَكُونُوا لِي عَونًا عَلَى مَا أُرِيدُ تَوَسَّلتُ بِكَ إِلَيكَ يَا مَن هُوَ فَعَّالٌ لِمَا يُرِيدُ أَقسَمتُ
عَلَيكُم أَيُّهَا الأَرْوَاحُ الرُّوحَانِيَّةُ العِظَامُ المُعَظَّمَةُ البَهِيمَةُ بِالاسمِ الَّذِي كَانَ مَكتُوبًا عَلَى قَلبِ آدَمَ عَلَيهِ
السَّلَامُ وَبِالاسمِ الَّذِي فَضَّلَكُمُ اللهُ بِهِ عَلَى كَثِيرٍ مِنَ الأَمْلَاكِ لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللهُ الرَّبُّ البَرُّ بِهِ أَجِيبُوا أَيَّتُهَا الأَرْوَاحُ
الرُّوحَانِيَّةُ الطَّاهِرَةُ الزَّكِيَّةُ المَلَكُوتِيَّةُ أَن تَكُونُوا عَونًا لِي عَلَى مَا أُرِيدُ حَتَّى لَا يَقدِرَ أَحَدٌ أَن يُخَالِفَ
أَمرِي مِنَ الخَلقِ أَجِيبُوا مَنِ استَعَانَ بِكُم يَا مَلَائِكَةَ رَبِّ العَالَمِينَ اللَّهُمَّ أَحسِن عَونِي وَكُن لِي مُعِينًا
فَإِنِّي عَبدُكَ وَابنُ عَبدِكَ وَاستَعَنتُ بِكَ فَأَغِنِي وَأَغِثْنِي وَانصُرنِي فَإِنَّهُ لَا مُعِينَ لِي إِلَّا أَنتَ وَلَا نَاصِرَ لِي
عَلَيهِم غَيرُكَ وَلَا أَسأَلُ أَحَدًا سِوَاكَ اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسأَلُكَ بِالآيَاتِ وَالذِّكرِ الحَكِيمِ أَن تُسَخِّرَ لِي رُوحَانِيَّةَ
وَخُدَّامَ هَذِهِ السُّورَةِ المُبَارَكَةِ أَنَّكَ عَلَى كُلِّ شَيءٍ قَدِيرٌ أَجِيبُوا يَا مَلَائِكَةَ رَبِّ العَالَمِينَ بِحَقِّ اسمِ اللهِ
الأَعظَمِ وَبِحَقِّ هَذِهِ الدَّعْوَةِ وَالذِّكرِ الحَكِيمِ أَقسَمتُ عَلَيكُم مَلَائِكَةَ رَبِّ العَالَمِينَ بِحَقِّ اسمِ اللهِ
طَاعَتِهِ فَإِنِّي أَستَعِينُ عَلَيكُمُ اللهَ الرَّحمَنِ الرَّحِيمِ وَبِالحَمدِ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ العَالَمِينَ بِحَقِّ الأُمَمِ
المَكتُوبِ عَلَى قَلبِ القَمَرِ وَالشَّمسِ وَبِحَقِّ الاسمِ العَظِيمِ الأَعظَمِ ﴿ يَا مَذهَب ﴾ بِحَقِّ رَبِّ العَالَمِينَ

English:
The righteous shaykh Ibn ʿAbd Allah Husayn ibn Mansur that he did that, so the servant came to him with ten thousand dinars, and he related that his disciple did it. Then when the servant of the surah was present before him, he feared, and his teeth chattered, and his tongue became dumb, so he did not utter to him a word. And when he opened his eyes he found him before him. Then when he recovered and the matter lasted and he did not speak, the servant withdrew from him, and no harm occurred from him. So upon you, O seeker, is steadfastness of heart, for the servant of this surah is from the believing jinn, and he did not harm the seeker. And the ʿazimah and the daʿwah are the noble surah in its entirety, and likewise the incense ﵀ And know, O arriver, that it is from the specialized secrets, and that it is from the books of the prophets and the saints and their secrets; may Allah benefit us by them ﵀ And it is this: it says: ﴿ In the name of Allah, the Merciful, the Compassionate. Say: It has been revealed to me ﴾ O Allah, I ask You, O Sender down of the revelation from above seven heavens, that You make easy for me what I intend and seek, and that You subjugate for me the servants of this blessed surah; they obey me in all that I desire. Indeed, You are powerful over everything ﵀ O Allah, O He to whom the fugitives flee, and O He in whose pardon the covetous covet ﴿ Indeed, a company of jinn listened ﴾ O Allah, I ask You, O He who hears and sees and is seen, and He is in the highest view ﴿ So they said: Indeed we heard a wondrous Qur’an, guiding to right conduct, so we believed in it, and we will never associate anyone with our Lord ﴾ O Allah, I ask You by the right of those who believed in You from among the believers, with their prophets and with Your Prophet and with You and with the petitioners, that You subjugate for me the servant of this surah, that he may be for me a help upon what I desire ﴿ And that He, exalted is the majesty of our Lord, has not taken a consort nor a child ﴾ O Allah, I ask You, O He who has not taken a consort nor a child, that You speak to me with wisdom and my tongue with knowledge, and that You be a help for me and an assistant, and that You subjugate for me the hearts of all Your creation ﴿ And that there were men from mankind who used to seek refuge with men from the jinn, so they increased them in burden, and that they thought, as you thought, that Allah would never raise up anyone ﴾ O Allah, I ask You, O Raiser of the heavens and O Creator of creatures and O Former of the universes and O Disposer of time and O Sender down of the Torah and the Gospel and the Psalms and the Criterion, and by the best of the children of Adam over all creatures, O Living, O Self-Subsisting, O He for whom there are orphans, O He who subjugated the jinn and mankind to Solomon, peace be upon him, I ask You, O Allah, that You subjugate for me all Your creation and all things, and make my remembrance famous in good, O Living, O Self-Subsisting. O Allah, I ask You by the great name, stored, concealed, by the noble light, that You subjugate for me the Ruhaniyyah of this surah, until they answer me and are for me a help upon what I desire. I sought mediation by You to You, O He who is effective in what He wills. I have sworn upon you, O mighty spiritual spirits, the عظيمة البهيمة, by the name that was written upon the heart of Adam, peace be upon him, and by the name by which Allah preferred you over many of the angels: there is no god but Allah, the Lord, the Righteous, by it answer. O pure, chaste, ملكوتية spiritual spirits, that you be a help for me upon what I desire, so that no one among creation is able to oppose my command. Answer, O angels of the Lord of the worlds. O Allah, make my help good and be for me an assistant, for I am Your servant and the son of Your servant, and I have sought help by You, so enrich me and succor me and grant me victory, for there is no helper for me except You, and no supporter for me over them other than You, and I ask no one besides You. O Allah, I ask You by the verses and the wise remembrance that You subjugate for me the Ruhaniyyah and the servants of this blessed surah, that You are powerful over everything. Answer, O angels of the Lord of the worlds, by the right of the greatest name and by the right of this supplication and the wise remembrance. I have sworn upon you, O angels of the Lord of the worlds, by the right of the name of Allah, its obedience, for I seek the help of Allah, the Merciful, the Compassionate, upon you, and by praise be to Allah, Lord of the worlds, by the right of the nations written upon the heart of the moon and the sun, and by the right of the great, greatest name: ﴿ O Madhhab ﴾ by the right of the Lord of the worlds.
