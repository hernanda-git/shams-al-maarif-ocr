( Chapter ) And it is said: the Seal of Solomon, upon him peace, which was in his hand, and in it his dominion, and in it the Greatest Name which was written on the heart of Adam. I say: and among the magnificent benefits is His saying, exalted: { "Board it — in the name of Allah is its course and its mooring; indeed my Lord is Forgiving, Merciful" } — this verse is for whoever has a ship at sea and wants its safety from the surges of the sea: let him engrave that on a plank of teak wood and nail it to the prow of the vessel — and in a copy, to the stern — for it is a protection and a guard by the permission of Allah. * And from al-Ḥasan, may Allah be pleased with him, who said: the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said: "A security for my nation from drowning when they board the ship is to say: 'In the name of Allah is its course' — the verse — 'and they did not estimate Allah with His true estimate' — the verse"; and he stands at the end and faces the prow, and gestures to the right and left, and says "Abū Bakr" and gestures, " 'Umar" and gestures, " 'Uthmān" and gestures to the prow: 'In the name of Allah, we named you — by you we are shielded, by our Ma'ṣaq we are protected' — 'and Allah is from behind them encompassing' — to the end of the sūra." And Ibn 'Abbās, may Allah be pleased with them both, said: whoever says when boarding a mount or a vessel: "In the name of Allah, the King" — "and they did not estimate Allah with His true estimate" to "they associate" — and said "Board it" the verse — then turned to his companions and said: "If the speaker of it drowns or perishes, his debt is upon me." And Ibn Shukr said: "I reached the shore of Damascus and found on the shore twenty-two ships loaded with food; I entered one of them and said the words and recited the verses, and the ships sailed with a fair wind, and the wave swelled, yet none reached the shore but the ship I was in." And from 'Abd Allāh ibn 'Umar, who said: "A security from drowning and perishing if one boards the sea is to say: 'In the name of Allah, the Merciful, the Compassionate' — 'and they did not estimate Allah with His true estimate' — the verse — and said 'Board it' the verse; so when you and those with you are settled on the ship, say: 'Praise be to Allah who saved us from the wrongdoing people; indeed Allah holds back the heavens and the earth lest they cease' — the verse — 'and Allah is from behind them encompassing' — the verse." And from Ibn 'Abbās, may Allah be pleased with them both, that he said: whoever says when boarding the sea: "In the name of Allah, the King; O Allah, to whom the heavens and earth are fearful, and the forms that go are humble, and the sea and the swellings are submissive" — preserve me
