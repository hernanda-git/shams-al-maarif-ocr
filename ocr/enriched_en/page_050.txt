The swift [one/name]: whoever mentions it for a number of days, and asks Allah Most High for something, He will grant it to him. And whoever has a need, let him draw it upon his palm and invoke through the Names, multiplied according to the days. Let him reach the number through which he invokes it, for Allah Most High answers his supplication. Its number, multiplied by the days, is four thousand, two hundred and seventy-seven times. It is for whoever desires to see the spirits, and through it are hidden secrets and manifest works. Therefore, strive and perform the work, and [unclear].

And as for His Most High Name, The All-Hearing (al-Samīʿ), whoever adds to it The All-Seeing (al-Baṣīr) and says: "O All-Hearing, O All-Seeing," and writes them at an auspicious time and carries them, whoever is afflicted with [unclear] will recover at that very time.

This is the ultimate [practice] of the possessors of secrets. For when they came to it, they found at its gate Ibrahim ibn Jarūj. He had been afflicted with [unclear], so he drew the wafq (magic numerical square) for him and carried it after mentioning the Name seven hundred times. He recovered, and what afflicted him departed by the permission of Allah Most High.

And whoever writes it in gold and carries it with him will hear the languages of the jinn, and will be able to command the spirits. Whoever perseveres in it will have revealed to him the secrets of creation, and what is in their chests, and the conditions of all the servants will become apparent to him, and he will witness the secrets.

And as for His Most High Name, The Peace (al-Salām), it is for seeking safety and seeking security. It is a remembrance of the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, on the Day of Resurrection, at the time when his community passes over the Bridge (al-Ṣirāṭ). He says: "In peace, [salvation]." And in a narration, he, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, says: "Islam" [the reading here is uncertain].
