في الأيام أربعة آلاف ومائتان وسبعة وسبعون مرة وهو لمن أراد رؤية الأرواح وسألهم فيجيبونه

[Continuation concerning] al-Sarīʿ: whoever mentions it—[UNCLEAR: the source’s phrase “O its preparer” is obscure]—and asks God Most High for something, He gives it to him. Whoever has a need should draw it on his palm and supplicate with the Names, multiplied by the days; let him complete the prescribed count when he invokes, for God Most High answers his supplication. Its number, multiplied by the days, is four thousand two hundred and seventy-seven repetitions. It is for whoever wishes to see spirits and ask them questions, and they answer him. It contains hidden secrets and manifest works. So strive and act, and you will attain. *

As for His Most High Name al-Samīʿ, whoever adds al-Baṣīr to it and says, “O All-Hearing, O All-Seeing,” writes them at an auspicious time, and carries them, a person who has fainted will recover at once. This is the utmost attainment of the people of secrets. When they came to it, they found Ibrāhīm ibn Jārūḥ with it, and he had fainted. A wafq was drawn for him, and he carried it after mentioning the Name seven hundred times; he recovered, and what he was suffering left him, by permission of God Most High. Whoever writes it in gold and carries it with him will hear the languages of the jinn and exercise authority over whatever he wishes among the spirits. Whoever persists in it will have the secrets of creation disclosed to him, will be informed about what lies in their hearts, will see the conditions of all servants, and will witness the secrets. *

As for His Most High Name al-Salām, it is for seeking safety and security. It is the invocation of the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him: “O Salām.”

The letter mīm is a realm among the realms of letters. Every letter whose end is like its beginning, such as wāw, mīm, and nūn, points to totality because of the union within it, and points to stillness because of the awe within it. It is among the letters of the Tablet. When God Most High created it, He created it as a concealed light, [UNCLEAR: the source’s phrase about it being whispered or suffused with light is obscure]. It is among the letters of the intellect because of its encompassing, and from it the sun draws in the fourth sphere. Through its secret God Most High established the kingdom and the dominion and manifested the world through the mīm. He aided it in its works through the secret of the mīm-light. It is the final rank in Bism, and within it is the secret of reaching full strength. God Most High said: “until, when he reached his full strength and reached forty years.”

The numerical occurrences of the mīm upon it are forty, and God Most High has entrusted it to ninety angels from the angels of the spirit. It is the secret that God Most High deposited in the name of His Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, at its beginning. That is the ease of the dominion; in its middle is the ease of the kingdom. Thus the world of the kingdom and the world of the dominion come together.

Whoever looks at the form of the mīm forty times every day while saying, “Say: O God, Owner of the kingdom,” up to His saying, Most High, “without reckoning,” God Most High will make the means of good and blessing easy for him, and he will not know from where his provision comes. [UNCLEAR: the source’s sentence about the form and the later discussion of it being an incoming on Wednesday is abrupt.] The form is the subject on which the discussion will follow, and it has an appointed occurrence on Wednesday.

Whoever draws its numerical secret after fasting forty days, maintaining ritual purity and the remembrance of God Most High, facing the qiblah in complete purity, when the Moon is in the auspicious mansions and during the hour of the Sun, will not have a blameworthy thought occur to him. God Most High will open for him the realities of faith and the sacred lights, and he will be safe from every harm. Whoever supplicates with it on Friday while fasting and constant in remembrance, whatever his need, it will be fulfilled by permission of God Most High. Whoever carries it while pursuing a livelihood will have his good increase and his provision made easy from where he does not expect. It also serves to bring hearts together, make them affectionate, and draw them toward the person who seeks this.

* [UNCLEAR: the source then adds that this is an addition for whoever contemplates it and brings its form and image together with the seven forms for the seven days, God willing.]

Know that whoever understands the secrets of the mīm, its encompassing and its conformity, and the worlds contained within it, will witness wonders of colors. Whoever wants God to make memorization easy for him should write this numerical secret on Thursday while pure, facing the qiblah, together with the name of the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, forty times. He should erase it, drink it with water and bee honey, and say: “O God, by the blessing of what I have drunk, make memorization and understanding easy for me.” Let him continue this for forty days; God will open for him an outward or inward understanding of its secret. [UNCLEAR: the source’s final explanation of the hidden power of the mīm in every world and the resulting opening is damaged.] As for its letter-form, it is among the secrets.
