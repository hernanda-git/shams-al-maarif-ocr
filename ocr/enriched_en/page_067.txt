English:
- 61 -

Compassionate, Merciful. And as for His name, God Most High, Mālik al-Mulk (Master of the Kingdom), whoever is frequent in His remembrance and seeks authority, God Most High will grant it to him. And as for His name, God Most High, Dhū al-Jalāl wa-l-Ikrām (Possessor of Majesty and Honor), whoever is frequent in His remembrance and asks God Most High for anything, He will grant it to him. And as for His name, God Most High, al-Muqsiṭ (the Equitable), whoever is frequent in His remembrance will be characterized by justice in all his affairs. And as for His name, God Most High, al-Jāmiʿ (the Gatherer), whoever is frequent in His remembrance, God will restore to him every lost item and return every runaway slave. And as for His name, God Most High, al-Ghanī (the Self-Sufficient), whoever is frequent in His remembrance, the avenues of wealth and provision will multiply for him. And as for His name, God Most High, al-Mughnī (the Enricher), whoever is frequent in His remembrance, God Most High will make him independent of all creation. And as for His name, God Most High, al-Māniʿ (the Preventer), whoever is frequent in His remembrance, God Most High will avert from him every harm; and whoever writes it upon the wall of a city or town around it one hundred and sixty-one times during the hour of Venus, God Most High will protect it from all calamities. The sages placed it upon the citadel of Mardin, so no one was able to conquer it and harm was repelled from it by the power of God Most High. And as for His name, God Most High, al-Ḍārr (the Creator of the Harmful), whoever is frequent in His remembrance and asks God Most High for the affliction of any enemy or oppressor, in the dawn of its deployment I see a wonder, by permission of God Most High. And as for His name, God Most High, al-Nāfiʿ (the Benefactor), whoever is frequent in His remembrance in a state of distress, God Most High will grant him well-being. Whoever persists in its remembrance with a sincere spiritual state until some of his spiritual realms align with him and a state from it prevails over him, he does not wipe his hand over a sick person except that God Most High cures him. Whoever places it in a silver ring upon an incised setting during the exaltation of the Moon and a sick person wears it, God Most High will cure him. Do you not see that it points to His name, God Most High, the Healer (Muʿāfī), and the names of its letters point to two majestic names that are the instruments of healing? And as for His name, God Most High, al-Nūr (the Light), whoever is frequent in His remembrance, God Most High will illuminate his heart; and if it is combined with al-Nāfiʿ, it becomes a cure for any intractable pain that defies healing; it is written and drunk as an infusion, and it has a venerable magic square (murabbaʿ), and this is its form. And as for His name, God Most High, al-Hādī (the Guide), whoever is frequent in His remembrance, light will increase in his heart and God Most High will guide his inner secrets to His gnosis (maʿrifah). Whoever faces a pressing difficulty among the manifest or hidden affairs of this world and the Hereafter, let him pray two rakʿahs reciting Āyat al-Kursī and al-Ikhlāṣ, and repeat the Name until his breath ceases, for it will guide him to what he seeks. And as for His name, God Most High, al-Badīʿ (the Incomparable Originator), whoever is frequent in His remembrance will continually originate insights in divine sciences and inspired ladunī secrets. And as for His name, God Most High, al-Wārith (the Supreme Inheritor), whoever is frequent in His remembrance, God Most High will grant him goodness and increase in all his movements. And as for His name, God Most High, al-Rashīd (the Infallible Guide), whoever is frequent in His remembrance, the outcomes of all his affairs will be praised. And as for His name, God Most High, al-Ṣabūr (the Patient), whoever is frequent in His remembrance, God Most High will grant him steadfastness amid hardships and crises. And God speaks the truth and He guides to the path; and may God's blessings and peace be upon our master Muḥammad, his family, and his companions.

(Chapter Nine: On the Properties of the Openings of the Qur'anic Surahs and the Decisive Verses)

Know—may God grant me and you success in obedience to Him—that regarding the dotted letters at the beginnings of the surahs, and the mysterious letters in their entirety, and what pertains to them concerning matters of affirmative belief, some scholars, may God Most High be pleased with them, said concerning His words, Most High: “Alif Lām Mīm Ṣād” (al-Muṣ): its meaning is “I am God.” Al-Ḥasan said: “Alif is pre-eternity (al-azal), lām is post-eternity (al-abad), and mīm and ṣād are the connection of whoever connects with Him and the separation of whoever separates from Him.” In reality, there is neither connection nor separation; these expressions proceed according to conventional usage, and whoever seeks the Truth guards it from literal phrasings.
