Two letters of the Greatest Name; and by it Allah will raise the creatures on the Day of Resurrection, and each is raised upon what he died upon, and in it is a strange secret in the raising of souls and bodies. Its angel is Bikhtiyā'īl, who is chief over 4 commanders, under the hand of each commander 573 rows, each row 573 thousand angels—and for this reason it is repeated "the angel descends upon him and two robes of honor are conferred upon him." As for the inward [effect], it draws the world like a magnet—if the beholder is [in] love, it effaces him or otherwise; and as for the outward, his spirit sees the noble places and the great visitations. And as for the supplication by this noble Name, you say: "O Raiser (al-Bā'ith), You are the One who sends the secret of Your life to the hearts and breasts; You are the One who brought into being the spirit of Your effusions, You do not darken affairs; You are the One who dyed the innards of the secrets of the people of unveiling with the Spirit, and sent Your messengers and prophets by manifesting the secret of destiny and unveiling Your trial. I ask You, O Allah, by the spreading of Your guardianship at the time of Your friends, and by the secret of Your permanence in the breasts of Your prophets, to make me attached to my deeds and actions, constant by Your power in my states, prevailing over my affair, attaining the utmost attainment in my remembrance; for I dedicate my praise and thanks, returning to You in my secret and my open, taking my knowledge and my work, and aid me by Your power in attaining perfection and reaching the degrees. Truly You are Allah, Compassionate to the servants, and Restorer of their bodies to the Abode of Return."
{ The twenty-sixth chapter, on the sixth expansion, in the secrets of the conditional accidents }
Know—may Allah make me and you succeed in His obedience and in understanding the secrets of His Names—that among the Most Beautiful Names of Allah are: al-Ghanī (the Rich), al-Shakūr (the Grateful), al-Mughnī (the Enricher), al-Razzāq (the Provider), al-Fattāh (the Opener), al-Kāfī (the Sufficient), al-Hasīb (the Reckoner), al-Wakīl (the Trustee), al-Mu'tī (the Giver), al-Mughīth (the Helper). These ten names—their secret's support is the blessing that breaks habit and the facilitation of provisions and sufficiency from everything; [may He] make me succeed in mending understanding and maintaining the abundant intellect and richness through Allah (exalted) from everyone, and reaching the station of reliance, which is the highest and most beautiful of stations. As for His (exalted) names al-Ghanī and al-Shakūr, their rememberer is given by Allah (exalted) richness in himself, and is inspired with diligence and gratitude in ease and hardship. Whoever persists in remembering them, and was by nature stingy, Allah (exalted) replaces it with intelligence and generosity. Whoever aligns His (exalted) name al-Mughnī [written] a number of times on a sheet of tin and places it in the water he drinks from, finds in himself richness and contentment he had not known. Whoever persists in remembering His name al-Shakūr, Allah (exalted) makes the beautiful appear upon him and conceals the ugly from him. Among the special properties of this name is the permanence of blessings upon its rememberer and the return of what strayed from them. As for His (exalted) names al-Mughnī, al-Razzāq, al-Fattāh, their rememberer has blessing descend upon him, and Allah (exalted) provides for him from whence he does not reckon, and provisions are made easy for him from every direction, and no one mentions them over food or drink except blessing and increase appear in it, and its breaking is not possible due to its clarity. Whoever takes them as a remembrance after his prayer will never be poor. Whoever aligns their numbers jointly 4-in-4 on yellow silk and places it in the money-chest and the purse of dirhams, the wealth grows by the permission of Allah. As for His names al-Hasīb and al-Wakīl, they are two great names; their rememberer is sufficed by Allah (exalted) against the evil of his enemies and He gathers what concerned him. If an oppressor overcomes him and he remembers them their [prescribed] number at the time of dawn, then says thereafter: "O Allah, I seek my reward from You and rely upon You in the matter of So-and-so the oppressor," he is taken at his time. As for His (exalted) names al-Mu'tī and al-Mughīth, they are two great names; their rememberer has the spring of provision gush for him, and the rivers of this life burst forth for him, so he lives happily and dies a martyr, and no one persists in remembering them who is in debt except Allah (exalted) fulfills it for him. This majestic pattern has a great effect in removing poverty, fulfilling debt, facilitating provisions, increasing food and drink, and bringing down blessing. In sum: the sufficiency of a child—"Allah is Greatest."
