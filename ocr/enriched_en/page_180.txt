English:
— 174 —

…and everyone he calls submits to him, and sees in his lifetime the shining loftiness and in his self the splendid elevation; and in it is a wondrous secret for the shaykhs and the grand ones and the seekers of sciences and lights; and when his name al-'Alīm is added to it, it is among the greatest invocations; and whoever sets them on a gold ring and fumigates it with aloeswood and carries it — everyone who sees him submits to him; and the kings used to take it from after al-Ṣaffāḥ to our time, Allah establishing their dominion. And it was said to al-Ma'mūn: "How are you, when the kings of Persia have come to you?" So he brought out his hand with a ring on which the two names are set, and said: "No one can overcome us as long as this is with us." And the suitable time for it is the dignity of the Moon. And this is its form:

And this name has the number 125: the twenty indicates manifestation in the high place, and the hundred indicates the companions in what appears from it of encompassment. He, exalted, said: { And Allah is, from behind them, Encompassing }; and since its manifestation in everything is its existence as a single existence, and its concealment upon its elevation is by the requirement of His wisdom — so the number pointed to its specialness; and Allah described with wisdom what He described with loftiness. He, exalted, said: { And indeed it is, in the Mother of the Book, with Us, exalted and wise }; and it is among the numbers of pair and odd excessive, whose parts 42 increase over it by its like — as if He says: "Alī (High) or A'lā (Most High) is the Wise"; and as for the names of its letters, they are 200 pointing to His name Mālik al-Mulk (Owner of the Dominion). ( The Thirty-Eighth Chapter: On His name { al-Kabīr } ) Whoever abounds in its remembrance — everything becomes small before him, and no one sees him but reveres him; and it is of the majestic invocations mentioned before kings and tyrants, so their souls are truly humbled before him. And it has the number 333, which is the pair of a deficient odd whose parts are 262 as a figure; and the names of its letters point to two majestic names: Baṣīr (Seeing), Wāḥid (One); and as for its square, it is on this description.

( The Thirty-Ninth Chapter: On His name, exalted, { al-Ḥafīẓ } ) Whoever abounds in its remembrance in his travel — Allah preserves him until his return from it; and whoever draws it on a sheet in the dignity of Jupiter — nothing is placed in but Allah preserves it; and whoever abounds in its remembrance is preserved from every disliked thing; and it is the square of answer for the fearful in travels — for its rememberer is secure in his homeland from fear and sees no disliked thing. And he timed it in places of awe and seizing, so he turned to its remembrance and I saw of the wonders of Allah's making what none can grasp; and whoever engraves it on the stone of a silver ring and makes its number a wafq, breaking its letters inside the ring, and carries it — he is in the midst of beasts and no harm reaches him; and he says after it "O Ḥafīẓ, preserve me" three times — the sought is attained. And this is its form, letter-wise and numeric:
