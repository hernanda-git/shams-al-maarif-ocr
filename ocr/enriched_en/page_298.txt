other than Him; and when the servant remembers his Lord, his remembrance should be compliance, not for a worldly aim—for some of the righteous predecessors said: "Whoever remembers Allah (exalted) for a worldly or otherworldly aim, that is his share from the remembrance; and whoever remembers Allah (exalted) in worship and compliance, Allah (exalted) gives him what no eye has seen, no ear has heard, and no human heart has conceived," by His saying (exalted): "Whoever is diverted by My remembrance from asking Me, I give him the best of what I give the askers." And Allah singles out with His mercy whom He wills, and Allah is Possessor of great bounty. As for His (exalted) name al-Shahīd (the Witness), it is a great name; whoever keeps to it is given witness-bearing at the time of need, and the angel created from its number is Nūr Ya'īl (upon him be peace), who is chief over four commanders, under the hand of each commander 219 rows, each row 219 thousand angels of the witnessing angels over all creatures. And when this noble name is mentioned, the angel descends upon him as mentioned, and his supplication: "O Witness (al-Shahīd), You are the One who testified for Yourself of Oneness, and You are the Knowledge by which You taught Your servants the Uniqueness, and You are the One who enabled Your friends in the sciences of the clouds, and You are the Knower of [~] the unseen and the visible, and You manifest the unseen of creation and the will. I ask You, O Allah, O Light of light and Witness to what is in the breasts, make clear to me the realities of Your boundary, and clarify to me the subtleties of Your glory, and make me a witness for You, returning to You in Your sea and Your sea—Truly You are the Strong, the Enduring." As for His (exalted) name al-Haqq (the Truth), it is the sword of Allah on earth, by which He cut the mountains of falsehood and established the proofs and arguments; { and Allah gives His dominion to whom He wills, and Allah is All-Embracing, Knowing }. His angel is Sarfiyā'īl, chief over four commanders, under the hand of each commander 108 rows, each row 108 thousand angels entrusted with nullifying falsehood. His rememberer—the angel descends upon him and fulfills his need. As for the supplication by it you say: "O Allah, O Truth (al-Haqq), You are the One who realized the affairs and illuminated the darkness of hearts and breasts; You are the One who manifested the secret for manifestation, and joy, and gladness, and intimacy, and the delight of bliss; and You are the Truth who speaks in every tongue. I ask You, O Allah, by Your Beloved, Your Friend, Your Confidant, and Your Elect, to grant me fidelity to Your right and compassion upon Your creation. Truly You are Allah, the Judge of great majesty." As for His (exalted) name al-Wakīl (the Trustee), it is a great name, and His angel is Kahyā'īl, chief over four commanders, under the hand of each commander 66 rows, each row 66 thousand angels of those entrusted over everything, and they are the guardians of the treasuries. His rememberer—the angel descends upon him, and his supplication: "O Trustee (al-Wakīl), You are the One who undertook the affairs of the creatures; You are the One who perfected the paths and the realities; You are the One who built the minute and the subtle; You stood with the sufficiency of the servants and were resolute in the will of increase and capability; to You is the enabling and the stability. I ask You, O Lord of lords and Cause of causes, to grant me increase in strength, perfection in power, light in glory, firmness in the horn, and a vision by which I attain clarification, and a tongue by which I attain eloquence. For You are the Gatherer of scattered affairs, and You are the Able to raise those in the graves." As for His (exalted) name al-Qawī (the Strong), it is a great name, and the spirit created from it is Waṭā'īl (upon him be peace), chief over four commanders, under the hand of each commander 116 rows, each row 116 thousand angels. The rememberer—the angel descends upon him and fulfills his need, and his supplication: "O Strong (al-Qawī), You are the One who strengthened the seekers of Your presence in ascension; You are the One who taught the people of love to traverse the paths of unveiling and manifestation; You are the One who illuminated the hearts of Your beloved with encompassing and containing. I ask You, O Allah, by Your great dominion, and the strong majesty of Your affair, and the penetrating proof, to grant me strength from You and power by which I prevail over cutting the wilds of other than You, and aid me by Your all-encompassing gentleness until I find none but You. Allah, O Allah, O Allah, O Strong."
