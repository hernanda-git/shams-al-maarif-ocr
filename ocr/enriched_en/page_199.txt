( The Eightieth Chapter: On His name, exalted, { al-Tawwāb } ) This name of noble standing, high, great, majestic in proof — whoever abounds in its remembrance, Allah, exalted, eases for him the return to his origin; so it is fitting for everyone not to be without its remembrance in his day and night. And in it is a beautiful secret for driving flies from the body. And it has the number 515, which is a deficient odd rectangular number whose parts point to our saying "He is the Wise One of repentance (mātiba) from wisdom"; and it also points to our saying "Subbūḥ (Glory)" — for the return to the origin is a return to pure transcendence where the lights of the glorifications shone, so the repentant swims in the sea of His light, and in it is his purity. He, exalted, said: { "Indeed, Allah loves the repentant." } And the names of its letters are 530, pointing to two majestic names: Rafī' (Lofty), Quddūs (Holy); and it has a square of majestic rank known to the people of illumination, and it is this:

( The Eighty-Second Chapter: On His name, exalted, { al-'Afw } ) This rising name and shining secret — whoever abounds in its remembrance, Allah endears to him noble character and freedom from being taken to account for sin; and whoever commits a sin and fears punishment from a ruler or other, and mentions the name its number — Allah, exalted, secures him from what he fears and is wary of; and it fits as a remembrance for one whose name is Yūsuf. And know that His names, exalted, al-Ghafūr, al-Ghāfir, and 'Afw are close names that fit to repel the painful from the great matters, especially from the affairs of this world and the next — so glory to Him who placed His secrets in His names. And the author of al-Muntakhab said: "The one who remembers this name is not struck by regret, nor panic, nor fear, nor tastes the vicissitudes of time." ( The Eighty-Third Chapter: On His name, exalted, { al-Ra'ūf } ) Whoever abounds in its remembrance — his heart is tender, his spirit refined, and his compassion for the creation of Allah increases; and when he meets a tyrant, his heart is tender and his spirit refined; and whoever persists in its remembrance until a state overcomes him from it — whoever sees him yearns for him and inclines to him with his heart.
