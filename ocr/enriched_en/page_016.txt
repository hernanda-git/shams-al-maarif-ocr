English:
— 10 —

And the attainment of blessing in what follows, if God Most High wills. (Whoever writes it) on Thursday on gazelle parchment at sunrise and carries it in the sewn seam of his clothing will, by God's help, be protected from thieves, every hardship, and everything that is feared and guarded against. This is the first of the subjects of numbers. I shall tell you about some of the secrets of numbers, what God has brought forth in them, their benefits and harms, and their transformations; about the secret of the separated letters in the Book of God Most High, which are the openings of the surahs—twenty-eight surahs that none but the elect of His creation can apprehend; and about what is contained in the names of God, which are treasuries of secrets and channels of destiny; and about knowledge of God's Greatest, Grandest Name and the divine secrets and attributes of lordship within it—things you will not find in a book or encounter in a register—from the pearls of knowledge and obscure secrets that I have gathered, so that whoever reads it and understands its meaning may benefit from it, if God Most High wills.

This is its form and description, as you see. It has a great supplication composed from its form and letters: alif, bāʾ, jīm, dāl, hāʾ, wāw, zāy, ḥāʾ, ṭāʾ, and yāʾ; yāʾ is added to them as the tenth. From them this noble supplication is arranged, as follows:

O God, I ask You by all Your Most Beautiful Names, those of them that I know and those that I do not know: O He, O One, O Singular, O Guide, O Lord, O Creator, O All-Seeing, O Originator, O Expander, O Everlasting, O Majestic, O Everlasting, O Inheritor, O Loving, O Living, O Wise, O Forbearing, O Manifest, O Manifestor, answer my supplication and fulfill my need, O Lord of the worlds.

(Since it has already been stated) that the letters are twenty-eight, corresponding to the twenty-eight lunar mansions, fourteen of them appear above the earth and fourteen beneath the earth. When one mansion rises, its counterpart—the fifteenth—rises, and so it continues forever. Therefore, the letters are fifteen dotted letters and fourteen undotted letters. The dotted letters are as follows: bāʾ, thāʾ, jīm, khāʾ, dhāl, zāy, shīn, ḍād, ẓāʾ, ghayn, fāʾ, qāf, nūn. The undotted letters are as follows: alif, ḥāʾ, dāl, rāʾ, sīn, ṣād, ṭāʾ, ʿayn, kāf, lām, mīm, hāʾ, wāw, lām-alif.

(And know)—may God grant us and you success—that the undotted letters are the mansions of good fortune, while the dotted letters are the mansions of misfortune. A letter with one dot is closer to good fortune; one with two dots is midway among misfortunes; and one with three dots is a greater misfortune, like shīn and tāʾ. Reflect on this.

(And know) that the mansions have different arrangements in the divine circle, with none resembling another. God created the moon round, and likewise the sun, for a hidden secret that cannot be explained, because disclosing the secret of lordship is unbelief. When the moon descends into the mansion of al-Naṭḥ, it has signs that indicate it, and a lengthy discussion will follow in part. But walls have ears, and concealment is best in this matter. Understand what I have indicated and reflect upon it; you will be guided.

(Chapter Two: On contraction and expansion and the arrangement of works in times and hours)

( Know )—may God grant me and you success in obedience to Him and understanding the secrets of His Names—that God Most High mentioned the sun and the moon in His Mighty Book, saying: “Each floats in an orbit.” This is because when the moon is in the mansion of al-Naṭḥ, it has the letter alif and is connected with the secret of alif. When it descends into that mansion, the spirituality of alif becomes manifest from it, and anger appears throughout the parts of the world, most of all among the nobles of the people of the world. Every creature finds coercion and anger within according to its own kind; [UNCLEAR: the source's final qualification of this condition]. Therefore, a person should remain still at that hour, occupy his limbs with worship of God Most High, increase supplication and remembrance, and maintain purity throughout that period.
