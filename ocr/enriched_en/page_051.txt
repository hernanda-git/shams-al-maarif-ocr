the hidden secrets. Whoever wishes to see the outcome of his affair should fast that day sincerely for God Most High, break his fast with whatever bread is available, recite Sūrat al-Mulk, sleep in a state of purity on his right side with it beneath his head, speak to no one, and sleep. God Most High will show him the outcome of his affair according to the portion he intended. This is suitable only for people whose hearts are pure and for people of spiritual discipline.

Whoever writes it in a glass bowl and drinks it will have understanding and wisdom made easy for him by God. Whoever hangs it upon himself will be made to speak with wisdom by God Most High. Whoever writes it together with “There is no deity except God; Muḥammad is the Messenger of God” eighty times and carries it on his right upper arm, or writes it on a garment and wears it, will be granted awe and compassion by God.

If you wish to take as brothers believing jinn who will fulfill your needs and seek your satisfaction, begin fasting on Wednesday and continue until the fourth day, Saturday, after washing the garment and the body. Recite Sūrat al-Ikhlāṣ one thousand times each day, and recite Sūrat Yā Sīn, Sūrat al-Dukhān, Tanzīl al-Sajdah, and “Blessed is He in whose hand is the kingdom” once.

When Saturday afternoon arrives, at the tenth hour, withdraw from people to a secluded place in a clean spot. Take seven barāʾāt [UNCLEAR: the term appears to mean written slips or sheets] made of paper. On the first write the saying of God Most High: “He is the One who gives life and causes death, and to Him belongs the alternation of night and day.” On the second write: “When He decrees a matter, He only says to it, ‘Be,’ and it is.” On the third write: “God will suffice you against them, and He is the All-Hearing, the All-Knowing.” On the fourth write: “Then, when He calls you with a single call from the earth, behold, you come forth.” On the fifth write: “Then, behold, they will hasten from the graves to their Lord.” On the sixth write: “And the Trumpet is blown,” up to His saying: “Then, behold, they are standing and looking.” On the seventh write: “On the day they come forth swiftly from the graves, God will suffice you against them, and He is the All-Hearing, the All-Knowing.”

Then pray four rakʿahs: al-Fātiḥah and Yā Sīn in the first; al-Dukhān in the second; and al-Fātiḥah, al-Sajdah, and Tabāraka al-Mulk in the third. [UNCLEAR: the source does not specify the recitation for the fourth rakʿah.] At the end of every prostration say:

“Glory be to Him who donned might and spoke by it. Glory be to Him who clothed Himself in praise and was ennobled by it. Glory be to Him who encompassed everything through His knowledge. Glory be to Him for whom glorification alone is fitting. Glory be to Him who, when He wills a thing, says to it, ‘Be,’ and it is. Glory be to Him who, when He wills a thing, it comes to be, and what He does not will does not come to be. Glory be to the Possessor of favor, bounty, and blessings. Glory be to the Possessor of knowledge and forbearance. Glory be to the Possessor of power and bounty. Glory be to the Possessor of the Throne, the Tablet, the Pen, and the Light.”

Then raise your head and say: “O God, I ask You by the seats of might of Your Throne, by the utmost limit of mercy in Your Book, by Your Great—Greatest Name, by Your Noble—Most Noble Face, and by Your perfect words, to subject for me a helper from among the righteous jinn who will assist me with whatever I seek of the needs of this world.” Seven people from among their nobles and great ones will appear to you; they will greet you and obey your command.

Before reading the names, hang seven barāʾāt upon yourself on a thread like a ṭarṭūr [UNCLEAR: a conical headpiece or turban], and place it on your head before beginning the prayer. Keep wax with you. Take the barāʾāt from the seven that you wrote, read them to the jinn, and say: “Honor the owner of this barāʾah, the owner of this sheet.” One of them will say, “I am its owner.” Ask him, “What is your name?” and he will say, “So-and-so.” Write his name on the sheet. Then say, “Your seal.” Take the thread and wax and seal the bottom of the sheet with them, as one seals a written document. Do the same with each of them until you reach the seventh.

Then say: “[UNCLEAR: the source’s oath reads approximately, ‘I adjure you by the names contained in this sheet: attend and answer my summons whenever I summon you.’]” Say: “[UNCLEAR: the following short command is damaged; it appears to ask for help and invoke God’s blessing upon and among them.]” Then lift the barāʾāt and the sealed sheet and place them in a pure location. Whenever you have a need for food, drink, knowledge of something, a treasure, a hidden object, or anything else, summon them and they will answer you quickly, by permission of God Most High. Beware of being weak.
