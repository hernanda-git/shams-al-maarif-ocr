التكونات في الأبد وظهور المعلومات في البصر القديم ولولا ما أطبق النظر على وجهه

ولما كان السابع من الكرسي المضاف إليه وسع كرسيه السموات والأرض وهو الذي عبر عنه

بأحكام السموات العلويات والعوالم الذاتيات واللطائف الروحانيات فهي باطن الكرسي المقدس

Then He poured upon the matching effusion a universal effusion, so it becomes a connecting cause for the plant of audition, by the quiver of the Beloved, in understanding, and the uncovering of the realities of wisdom. So when Allah willed the uncovering of His servants' unseen and their inferring by hearing His speech, then He poured from knowledge a ray-like effusion into the noble sight — so it behooved the apprehension of the beings in the eternal, and the witnessing of the formations in the everlasting, and the appearance of the known in the ancient sight. And were it not that the gaze was fastened upon His noble face in the last origination, on the known day, and the garment of apprehension was cast — so you apprehended Him by His apprehension, so by His apprehension you apprehended Him; and He is the apprehender and the apprehended, as the Exalted said: "Nothing is like Him, and He is the Hearing, the Seeing." Then He poured from sight an effusion connected to the ancient, high, eternal speech. So the benefit occurred in speech — and He is the speaker by His speech, and it is an attribute of His essence, not resembling the speech of the creatures. So in speech is the effusion of sight, and in sight the effusion of hearing, and in hearing the effusion of will, and in will the effusion of knowledge, and in knowledge the effusion of power, and in power the effusion of life, and in life the effusion of essence — and faith is the effusion of essence, and the intellect is the effusion of life, and the spirit is the effusion of power, and the soul is the effusion of knowledge, and the heart is the effusion of will, and man is the effusion of hearing, and composition is the effusion of sight, and form is the effusion of speech. And as the seventh is a single-string by power, and the first is a single-string by power, and the intellect connected the string — so it is first a single-string and last a single-string. And as the seventh is of the Throne, to which "His Throne encompasses the heavens and the earth" — and it is that which the people of realization and purity expressed from the particularities of certitude, and it is "your essence." So blessed be Allah, the best of creators. And as for the encompassing of the rulings of the upper heavens, and the essential worlds, and the spiritual subtleties — it is the inward of the holy Throne, and it is the reality of life, power, knowledge, and will. And as for the wider [part] of the Throne from the dominical, the omnipotent, and the barzakhian — it is from the reality of hearing, sight, and speech; the inward of the Throne is the principles of speech, and its end is the soul universally, and its outward is form and imaging, which the tongue of the Sufis expressed. And some of the great ones said: "Whoever wills to attain his aim and connection with the divine wisdom — let him abound in His remembrance: 'the Knowing', and it is said 'the Knower of the unseen', and it is said 'the Wise' with the vocative yā. So whoever remembers them and adheres to them — Allah will subject to him a philosopher from the connected ones, or al-Khaḍir (upon him peace), who teaches him this rank — the measure that plunges into the stone of Venus, in which are 440 worlds from the spiritual worlds; and He purifies it and confirms it to the smelting and the fever, even if smelted a thousand times, unless the knowledge and lead enter upon it." And for that it is done in every dissolved dye, until it is guarded and dissolves and coagulates — so then it is confirmed for deliverance. And Allah knows best. ( And this is a broth that dyes red ) When worked with the red stones, the red spirits, and the red souls — whoever manages it well works by it a wondrous operation; each one dyes by it hundreds, no doubt. And it is a gate sought help by: "There is no god but Allah alone, He has no partner; to Him the dominion and the praise, and He is over all things capable." And that you take from the head of good strong soap; and if you make it upon this broth it is best. You take from it a ratl or what you will, and add to it its half of bleaching natron salt, and table salt, and nitron, and Yemeni alum, and yellow orpiment, and vitriol, and talc if possible — after grinding each one alone, and the white of boiled egg, and a quarter of the first water of washed black hair; it is put in the mentioned water, and left a day and night in the sun or on a gentle fire until its water dissolves, flowing; and it is taken out of its time, then distilled. Then you take afterward from all of it and raise it by you.
