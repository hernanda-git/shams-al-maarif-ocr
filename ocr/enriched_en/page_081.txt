(And this is the spiritual discipline of al-Fātiḥah.) It is to retreat into a dark place where no one sees you except God, and fast for 3 days, the first being Sunday, on condition of abstaining in this discipline from anything that has a spirit. Break your fast with barley bread and oil, without eating your fill. Recite al-Fātiḥah after every prayer one hundred times, and supplicate with this, saying: My Lord, bring me into the sea of Your oneness and the vast ocean of Your unique singleness, until I emerge into the freshness of Your mercy, with flashes of the Qur'an on my face, from the traces of Your mercy, awe-inspiring through Your awe, strong through Your strength, mighty through Your might. Clothe me in the robes of honor and acceptance; make easy for me the easing of connection and union; crown me with the crown of karamah; and bring me into harmony with Your beloved ones. O Master of this world and the Hereafter, O You who took Ibrāhīm as a close friend, who spoke to Mūsā directly, and who greatly honored Muḥammad, peace and blessings be upon him: Peace, a word from a merciful Lord. O Master of the Day of Judgment, You alone we worship and You alone we ask for help. Guide us to the straight path, the path of those whom You have favored, not those who have incurred wrath, nor those who have gone astray.
After 3 days, a white cat will appear to you in the mihrab, puffing itself up until it fills the place where you are; then it will remain a heap. Then a man will come out from beneath it, saying: What is your need, and what do you seek? Do not ask for anything, and say to him: I want the Name and the seal. And brotherhood is a condition upon you [UNCLEAR: who imposes this condition is unspecified]. The recitation is 3 times after the Ṣubḥ prayer, and likewise at Ẓuhr and ʿAṣr, and ten after Maghrib. We supplicate with this supplication: (In the name of God, the Merciful, the Compassionate. Praise be to God, Lord of the worlds.)
