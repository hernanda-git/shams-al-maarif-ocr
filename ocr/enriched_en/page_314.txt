With him a hot illness, and did that for 3 consecutive days — Allah cured him. And whoever writes its specifically-assigned form upon tiger-skin, burns it and grinds it, and applies it as kohl — he sees the spirits without a veil. And whoever writes the Indian Ḥā' 64 around pustules — they depart. And whoever draws them in a glass bowl, and wipes them with fresh water, and drinks from it one who has inflammation in his heart or inward — his pain is stilled, and his spirit is refreshed. And whoever places its numbers in an 8-by-8 square, upon a tin tablet in the exaltation of Jupiter and the Moon safe from the malefics, and carries it with him — Allah gives him good, ample provision, and makes him beloved to His creatures; and if hung upon one who has headache from the yellow bile — he is cured. And it has an effect in calming disputes and wars; and if this octagon is hung upon the army's banner — it calms the opposing faction. And its supplication: "O Allah, indeed I ask You — O Living, O Wise, O Praised, O Tender, O Reckoner, O Preserver, O Truth, O Guardian — by what You lodged in the letter Ḥā' of the hidden secrets — that You subject to me the servants of this letter, who obey me in what is in Your good-pleasure. Indeed You are over all things Able." And it is silent, cold; and the oath-division of the letter Ḥā' and Hā' — one element, but differing in properties from one aspect without another; if they agree in nature, and the letter Ḥā' and every silent (letter) has no (division) but the basic number and what branches from it. And among its properties — if it is written in a china vessel that no blood touched, 600 times, and wiped with the juice of the bān-tree, and one who has palpitation drinks from it — it is calmed. And it has an Arabic and Indian form: the Arabic thus [Kh], and the Indian [600]. And among its properties — if one places a square, and makes the Ḥā' like a circle upon that square, and places in it the numbers of the letters, and the coward carries it upon his neck — his heart is strengthened and he becomes brave. And if hung upon a child — he is not frightened and does not cry, and it is a beneficial protective charm from jinn and men. And among its properties — if it is engraved upon a crystal stone, and mounted on a silver ring, and hung upon one whom labor-pains have overtaken — she gives birth at once. And whoever places its numerical compound numbers in a basin of red copper with a basil pen in rose-water and saffron, and the letter 600 is written around it, and it is wiped with rain-water, and given to drink to one who has a stitch-pain for 3 days — he is cured. And it has names by which it is invoked for its works: "O Allah, indeed I ask You — by the Creator-of-all, by the Creator, O Lowering, O Aware [Kh Kh Kh Kh], O Hidden, O Subtle — that You subject to me the servants of this letter in what You commanded them. Indeed You are over all things Able." And it is a speaking letter indicating sciences and wisdom, and it is attributed to Mercury, and its relation is natural agreeing with it in sense and meaning; and it is cold and moist — and all that is in Mercury of motions and other than them is in the letter Dāl. And this letter has properties — so whoever writes it four times upon a hot swelling or other — Allah removes it. And whoever writes its Indian form 67 upon a burn of fire — the pain of the burn does not harm him nor does it fester. And whoever places its numbers, which are 30, in a square, and writes in its corner the letter Dāl outside the array upon a tablet of the sea, and carries it for one who has pain of the intestines — it benefits him. And whoever writes them at dawn upon a silver tablet marked with quicksilver at the hour of Mercury and its exaltation, and looks at it every day 4 times, and each time he looks at it asks Allah for the secrets placed in the letter Dāl that He grant him wisdom and what he wills of sciences — he is given what he sought. And its supplication: "O Allah, indeed I ask You — by the Everlasting Might, O Possessor of Bounty — by what You lodged in the letter Dāl [D 44] of the hidden secrets and the stored lights — that You subject to me Your angels, the servants of this letter, in what You commanded them. Indeed You are over all things Able."
