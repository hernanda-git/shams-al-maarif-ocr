English:

It is a tremendous name containing a letter of the letters of the Greatest Name; and the angel created from it is 'Ayl', and he is chief over four commanders, under each commander 298 ranks, each rank 298 angels; and therefore the angel would descend upon him. And his supplication: 'O Patient, You are He who gave to everything its creation then guided it, and You are He who enlivened Your heart by the light of unity and oneness, then taught it the first of every manifest and the last of every concealing (veil). To You return the affairs and dominions after the passing-away of dominions, and You dispose the affairs to their end upon right guidance and rectitude — without guidance (other than Yours), and by sound preparation to attain the tower to the abode of return. (O You) whom impatience does not carry to reach the object before its time, and who do not await a matter whose time and place have come. I ask You by the might of Your dominion, and the glorification of Your Word, and by what is in the treasuries of Your stored protection, and the glories of Your Face, and the shade of Your Throne, and the pavilions of Your Holiness — that You make my supplication accepted, my call answered, my answer given, and make me a guide, rightly guided, and upright upon Your path, O Lord of the worlds.' ( Chapter Thirty-One — on the letters and their special properties ) Know — may Allah have mercy on you — that the secret of every nation is in its book, and the secret of the Book of Allah is in the letters. And the letters are of different forms; and when our Prophet Muhammad, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, appeared, He sent down upon him the Qur'an, and it was the secret of this nation, and abrogated the law of all laws. And the letters of this noble Book are Arabic; and when he was asked, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, about the lettered letters (ḥurūf al-mu'jam) — he said: "They are the letters: ā ب t ث j ح kh د dh ر z س sh ص ḍ ط ẓ ع gh ف q ك l م n هـ w لا ى — and they are Arabic," so he named them Arabic. And in them are the secrets of all the books and scriptures revealed, and an addition upon them. And as for the ajad — it is Syriac, sent down upon Adam, Idrīs, Nūḥ, Mūsā, and 'Īsā, may the blessings and peace of Allah be upon them all. And as for what the sages agreed upon of the ordering abjad hawaz ḥuṭī kalaman khay ṣād qarishaṭ thakhadh ḍaẓgh — it is the convention of the simiyā'ī sage. And as for the convention of our age's people of reversing the letters of the alphabet and making its last its first — it is an error; for they claim this is a subtle cipher, and in it is of woe what is not hidden — for it reverses the revealed letters and substitutes them, especially if the Names of Allah are written by them, whether the names are Arabic or non-Arabic. He, exalted, said: { And whoever substitutes the favor of Allah after it has come to him — indeed Allah is severe in punishment } — and were it not that this Arabic knowledge is a guarded secret, Allah would not have sent down the Qur'an by its words and letters. So look at His saying, exalted: { Alif-Lām-Mīm — that is the Book }, { Nūn — by the Pen } — and He swore by these letters that you may know their measure. And here I have opened for you a model for the meaning of the special properties of the letters, from which (knowledge) branches — so I said: ( A section on what we mentioned of the Arabic letters and their special properties, and the four khadam and the angels and the spirits ) ( The Letter Alif ) It is the first of the letters, and it is a luminous letter, and the first of number, and the first rank in the division of the letters upon the elements. And they named that and described it in volumes and various tables, and all of them are upon the truth in the method of him who takes from them. And they all agreed that the letter Alif is fiery, and it has a small and large extension (bast) — its small extension thus [alif], and the large thus [alif-lām]; for its numerical extension accords with its letter-extension, for it is 'alif' and the numerical (value) is one; and these two numbers are two extensions, which is not hidden to him who has the least reflection.
