English:
19

[Discourse on the mansions, their forms, and the rulings connected with them]

The discourse on the mansion of al-Sharṭayn: this is its form, hāʾ hāʾ, and its letter is alif. When the Moon descends in al-Sharṭayn, it is fiery and inauspicious. Perform in it works connected with worldly affairs, corruption, and bloodshed. The sages at this time would incline toward sleep and stillness. Some of them said that in sleep a person sees what frightens him and disturbs his disposition, and that not sleeping at this time is beneficial. If you desire to perform a work, perform works of evil in it for one who deserves them. Whoever is born in this mansion will be highly corrupt. Its incense is pepper and black seed. God knows best.

[Discourse on the mansion of al-Buṭayn]

Its form is hāʾ hāʾ, and its letter is bāʾ. When the Moon descends in al-Buṭayn, which is hot and moist, a righteous spiritual being descends through it into the world by the permission of God, Exalted is He. It is suitable for matters specific to men rather than women. Perform talismans in it; alchemy is sound in it, as is every craft of high rank. It is suitable for beginning the sciences, making rings, engraving, figures and numerals, incantations for illnesses, and treatment. Whoever is born in it will live happy, rightly guided, successful, and beloved by creatures. Its incense is aloeswood, saffron, and mastic. God knows best.

[Discourse on the mansion of al-Thurayyā, and this is its form]

Its form is hāʾ hāʾ hāʾ hāʾ, and its letter is jīm. When the Moon descends in al-Thurayyā, a spiritual being mixed of heat and cold is generated from it into the world by the permission of God, Exalted is He. Perform talismans in it and what is suitable for women; manage suitable medicines and cooling remedies. It is suitable for travelers, who may hope for increased profit. It is suitable for kings, marriage, and the purchase of slave women and male slaves. Everything managed in it is good, because it is the Moon's equilibrium rather than the Sun's, and everything made in it is praiseworthy.
