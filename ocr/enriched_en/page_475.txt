أَمَّا الذِّكْرُ الْقَائِمُ بِهِ فَالْبَسْمَلَةُ اللَّهُمَّ أَنْتَ الرَّقِيبُ الْمُرَاقِبُ لِأَعْيَانِ ثِقَةِ صِلَةِ

لَا يُخَالِفُهُ فِيمَا أَمَرَ بِهِ وَيَنْهَاهُ عَنْهُ وَبِمَا أَنْعَمَ اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَيَنْبَغِي لِلْعَارِفِ أَنْ يُشَاهِدَ

English:
— 469 —

"And it has a retreat that gives its possessor generosity, good, and the rectitude of the soul; so when the wayfarer recites it [its number], the servant MARQĀ'ĪL descends upon him and fulfills his need. * And as for the standing mention by it — the basmala: 'O Allah, You are al-Karīm, the Lavisher, the Giver of gifts, the Generous — by the bounty, by Your continuance over the creatures; so thank by the great praise for the little thanking, and overlook the great sin for the humble, imploring servant. I ask You, O Generous, by the extension of Your generous bounty, the manifestor of the giving, unto the non-existence, that You be generous to me by Your bounty from the giving of the giving and the existents — from the high subtletics and the high secrets — that the manifestor come to the holy presence, and that You extend to me the good things of the earthly bounties by the rained-down provisions from the likenesses of the vile, and make that for me a strength for the measure of my turning by the obedient deeds drawing near to You. O Allah, be generous to me by turning back the evils from me for the enemies, and by subduing the opposers from me, O Lord of the worlds.' Whoever persists in that — Allah preserves him from the evil of the jinn and the humans, and he is awed, generous, and the doors of good are opened for him. (Section on His name, God Most High, al-Raqīb) Know that the meaning of al-Raqīb is the one who watches the secret affairs of the secrets and the glances, ever-present in His witnessing of them, not delimited by time nor place; and none is that but God Most High. * And know that the Creator, when He created the creation in the heaven, made for them a watcher of the passing-away in the Oneness, then He transferred them to the abode of the Barzakh and made over them a watcher, then He transferred them to the admonition which is the natural disposition and made over them the watcher of the trust, then He transferred them to the Resurrection and made over them the watcher of the manifestation. He said exalted: 'And to Him returns the command, all of it.' And it has a retreat conditioned by the spiritual practice and the inward and outward purification, and the sitting in the darkness and the recitation of the important, and the worship of the day by the invocations and the night by the litanies and the recitation of the important with the supplication — so the servant attends and fulfills your need. And if it is written in a vessel and erased and given to drink to whom you will, he loves you with an intense love; and if it is written in a seal and hung upon a town of taste, Allah provides him understanding — and this is its figure. As for the standing mention by it — the basmala: 'O Allah, You are al-Raqīb, the Watcher over the essences of the trust of the connection of the extension in the existents and their details. O God of the servants, You are the one constantly accompanying by the continuous gaze — hark! — so do not be heedless a single glance of the glances, and You are the Preserver of their order in the most complete states in the analysis and the composition and the movements and the stillnesses. I ask You by the secret affairs of Your ancient unseen knowledge, over the order of Your will — the Knower by what Your pen caused to flow in the tablet of the detailing and the glorifying. I ask You that You illuminate my outward and my inward by a light from You, and that You inspire me to be characterized by the watching of my glances and my glances by what makes me for You a beloved, and by what You are pleased with from me, a beloved. O Allah, from You is the beauty of the observations by the continuance of the consonance and the beauty of the observation, from the sicknesses and the heart and the envy and from the evil of an envier when he envies. O Allah, O Raqīb.' (Section on His name, God Most High, al-Mujīb) Know that the meaning of al-Mujīb is the one who answers the askers and relieves the implorers and answers the distressed; and none is that but God Most High — He is ashamed to return His servant empty, so it behooves the servant to be an answerer to Him in all His commands, not opposing Him in what He commanded and forbade him; and by what Allah bestowed upon him, and it behooves the gnostic to witness… [page ends]"
