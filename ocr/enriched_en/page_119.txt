العلي العظيم

( فائدة مباركة لقضاء الحوائج )
من كانت له حاجة إلى الله وأراد قضاءها فليصل ركعتين من قبل صلاة الوتر يقرأ في كل ركعة الفاتحة
مرة والإخلاص ٣ فإذا فرغ من الصلاة فليجلس على قدميه أي يقوم على الأرض قليلاً ثم يقرأ ويقول
أستغفر الله العظيم الذي لا إله إلا هو الحي القيوم وأتوب إليه وأسأله التوبة والمغفرة لي ولوالدي ولجميع
المسلمين والمسلمات ألف مرة وعيناه مغمضتان فإذا فرغ يدعو الله بما أراد من أمر الدنيا والآخرة
يقضى له

( فائدة لجلب الرزق والقبول )
* اعلم أن هذه الآيات مباركة فاجعلها ورداً تجد بركتها * وإن حملتها معك يرزقك الله من حيث
لا تحتسب * وهي بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم وما رزقناهم ينفقون كلما دخل عليها زكريا المحراب
وجد عندها رزقاً قال يامريم أنى لك هذا قالت هو من عند الله إن الله يرزق من يشاء بغير حساب
وارزقنا وأنت خير الرازقين قل أغير الله أتخذ ولياً فاطر السموات والأرض وهو يطعم ولا يطعم
وأورثنا القوم الذين كانوا يستضعفون مشارق الأرض ومغاربها التي باركنا فيها وأيدكم
بنصره ورزقكم من الطيبات لعلكم تشكرون وأقيموا الصلاة فاجعل أفئدة من الناس تهوي
إليهم وارزقهم من الثمرات لعلهم يشكرون ولقد مكناكم في الأرض وجعلنا لكم فيها معايش
قليلاً ما تشكرون كلاً نمد هؤلاء وهؤلاء من عطاء ربك وما كان عطاء ربك محظوراً وإن من شيء
إلا عندنا خزائنه إنا مكنا له في الأرض وآتيناه من كل شيء سبباً فأتبع سبباً ورزق ربك خير وأبقى
ولهم رزقهم فيها بكرة وعشياً ولقد كتبنا في الزبور من بعد الذكر أن الأرض يرثها عبادي الصالحون
فخراج ربك خير وهو خير الرازقين ليجزيهم الله أحسن ما عملوا ويزيدهم من فضله والله يرزق
من يشاء بغير حساب قال أتمدونن بمال فما آتاني الله خير مما آتاكم أن يبدأ الخلق ثم يعيده
ومن يرزقكم من السماء والأرض أإله مع الله ونريد أن نمن على الذين استضعفوا في الأرض ونجعلهم
أئمة ونجعلهم الوارثين رب إني لما أنزلت إلي من خير فقير أولم نمكن لهم حرماً آمنا يجبى إليه ثمرات
كل شيء رزقاً من لدنا فابتغوا عند الله الرزق واعبدوه واشكروا له إليه ترجعون وكأين من دابة
لا تحمل رزقها الله يرزقها وإياكم وهو السميع العليم ألم تروا أن الله سخر لكم ما في السموات
وما في الأرض وأسبغ عليكم نعمه كلوا من رزق ربكم واشكروا له بلدة طيبة ورب غفور ما يفتح الله
للناس من رحمته فلا ممسك لها وما يمسك فلا مرسل له من بعده وهو العزيز الحكيم وما أنفقتم من شيء فهو
يخلفه وهو خير الرازقين وما كان الله ليعجزه من شيء في السموات ولا في الأرض إنه كان عليماً قديراً

English:

and, through Your bounty, from anyone other than You, O God, O Merciful One of this world and the Hereafter, O Lord of the worlds, I ask You to send blessings and peace
upon our master Muhammad and upon the family of our master Muhammad, and to open for me the gates of Your provision through the opening of Sūrat al-Wāqiʿah
and its secrets; to ease my provision for me as You have eased it for many of Your creatures, O God, O Lord of the worlds. * O God, send blessings upon our master
Muhammad and upon the family of our master Muhammad, a blessing of which You are worthy and of which he is worthy, O Lord of the worlds. There is no power and no strength except through God,
the Most High, the Great.

( A blessed benefit for the fulfillment of needs )
Whoever has a need of God and desires its fulfillment, let him pray two units of prayer before the witr prayer, reciting in each unit al-Fātiḥah
once and al-Ikhlāṣ 3 times. When he has finished the prayer, let him sit upon his feet, that is, stand on the ground a little [UNCLEAR: the gloss says stand, although the instruction says sit]; then let him recite and say:
"I ask forgiveness of God the Great, other than whom there is no god, the Living, the Self-Sustaining; I repent to Him and ask Him for repentance and forgiveness for me, for my parents, and for all
Muslim men and Muslim women" -- the entire formula one thousand times, with his eyes closed. When he has finished, let him ask God for whatever he desires concerning this world and the Hereafter;
it will be fulfilled for him.

( A benefit for attracting provision and acceptance )
* Know that these verses are blessed, so make them a regular recitation and you will find their blessing. * If you carry them with you, God will provide for you from where
you do not expect. * They are: In the name of God, the Merciful, the Compassionate. And what We have provided them, they spend. Whenever Zachariah entered the sanctuary to see Mary,
he found provision with her. Zachariah said, "O Mary, whence have you this?" Mary said, "It is from God. Indeed, God provides for whomever He wills without reckoning."
"And provide for us, for You are the best of providers." Say, "Shall I take a protector other than God, the Originator of the heavens and the earth, when He feeds and is not fed?"
And We caused the people who had been oppressed to inherit the eastern and western parts of the land which We had blessed. And He aided you
with His victory and provided you with good things, so that you might give thanks. And establish prayer. "So make the hearts of some people incline
toward them, and provide them with fruits, so that they might give thanks." And We have indeed empowered you in the land and made livelihoods for you therein;
little do you give thanks. To all -- these and these -- We extend your Lord's gift; your Lord's gift is not forbidden. And there is nothing
but that its treasuries are with Us. Indeed, We empowered him in the land and gave him a means to everything; then he followed a means. And your Lord's provision is better and more lasting.
And they have their provision therein, morning and evening. And We indeed wrote in the Zabūr, after the Reminder, that My righteous servants shall inherit the earth.
But your Lord's reward is better, and He is the best of providers; so that God may recompense them for the best of what they did and add to them from His bounty. And God provides for
whomever He wills without reckoning. He said [UNCLEAR: identity of the speaker], "Are you supplying me with wealth? What God has given me is better than what He has given you." That He begins creation, then repeats it [UNCLEAR: the link of this fragment to the adjoining passages],
and who provides for you from the sky and the earth? Is there a deity alongside God? And We wish to bestow favor upon those who were oppressed in the land and make them
leaders and make them the heirs. "My Lord, I am in need of whatever good You have sent down to me." Have We not established for them a secure sanctuary to which are brought the fruits
of everything, as provision from Us? So seek provision with God, worship Him, and give thanks to Him; to Him you will be returned. And how many a creature
does not carry its provision! God provides for it and for you, and He is the Hearing, the Knowing. Have you not seen that God has subjected to you whatever is in the heavens
and whatever is on the earth, and has lavished His blessings upon you? Eat of your Lord's provision and give thanks to Him: a good town and a forgiving Lord. Whatever God opens
of His mercy to people, none can withhold; and whatever He withholds, none can send after Him. He is the Mighty, the Wise. And whatever you spend of anything, He
replaces it, and He is the best of providers. Nothing in the heavens or on the earth can frustrate God; indeed, He is Knowing, Powerful.
