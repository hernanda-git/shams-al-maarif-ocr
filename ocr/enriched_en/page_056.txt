Ḥā Mīm ʿAyn Sīn Qāf; O Ever-Living One, O Sustainer, O Most Merciful.

Know that the letters placed in its corners are the letters of the triangle. They are the endpoints of the nine numbers, which are the initial letters of Abjad, as follows: alif, bāʾ, jīm, dāl, hāʾ, wāw, zāy, ḥāʾ, ṭāʾ, yāʾ. The tenth yāʾ in it is for calling; one says: "O Allah, O Raiser-Forth, O Majestic, O One, O Ever-Living One, O Preserver, O Manifest One." These are nine names [UNCLEAR: the source calls them nine although only seven invocations are visibly listed]; they have gathered the ingenuity of this great figure, and this is it:

Bismillah al-Raḥmān al-Raḥīm. O Allah, I ask You by Your being Allah in the realities of particularization; by Your being Allah, sanctified by the properties of oneness and self-sufficiency, beyond opposite, peer, contrary, and helper; and by Your being Allah, like whom there is nothing, and who is the All-Hearing, the All-Seeing. I ask You to send blessings and peace upon our master Muhammad and upon the family of our master Muhammad; and I ask You to fulfill my need and whatever contains the good of this world and the Hereafter, encompassed by care and preserved from afflictions through the properties of solicitous care. O One who repeatedly brings good things; O You who are in truth worthy of piety and worthy of forgiveness. This has preceded in the chapter before it, and this is the end of the greatest figure. Engrave and derive whatever you wish: you will find it and attain the request and every hope. For it is red sulphur and a pearl—ruby. Understanding its secret is not attained; he attained his affair [UNCLEAR: the subject and syntactic attachment of this clause are unresolved], by Allah's permission.
