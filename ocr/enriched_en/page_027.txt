[Discourse on the mansion of al-Ḥarthān, and this is its form]

Its form is two dots (• •), and its letter is kāf. When the Moon descends in it, it is hot and dry. It is suitable for treatments involving spiritual entities, making talismans, treating the sick and the chronically ill, buying and selling, entering upon kings and chiefs, travel and residence, noble works, and wearing new clothes. Whoever is born in it will be beloved among people, although there is some cunning and shrewdness in it. Its incense is pomegranate peel and [UNCLEAR: a further incense term]. God knows best.

[Discourse on the mansion of al-Ṣarfah, and this is its form]

Its form is six colon marks (: : : : : :), and its letter is lām. When the Moon descends in it, it is a watery planet that is continuously inauspicious. Whoever is born in it will be unlucky. Its incense is nadd perfume and saffron. God knows best.

[Discourse on the mansion of al-ʿAwāʾ, and this is its form]

Its form is five hāʾ marks (hāʾ hāʾ hāʾ hāʾ hāʾ), and its letter is mīm. When the Moon descends in it, it is an inauspicious planet, and a spiritual being descends through it into the world, by the permission of God, stirring desire and instilling in men love for women and the desire to associate with them. It is suitable for beginning the veneration of the sciences. Do not undertake the craft of the honored stone in it; do not fight enemies, quarrel, litigate, or enter upon kings in it. It is suitable for wearing new clothes and tailoring garments. Whoever is born in it will be fortunate, whether male or female. Its incense is male frankincense. God knows best.

[Discourse on the mansion of al-Simāk, and this is its form]

Its form is one dot (•), and its letter is nūn. When the Moon arrives in it, it is an earthy, dry planet. A spiritual being descends through it into the world, by the permission of God, causing enmity and corruption. It is suitable for works involving deadly poisons and everything that causes corruption. Beginning good works is disliked in it, and buying and selling are disliked in it. Whoever is born in it will be a liar and a tale-bearer, with an outcome that is not praiseworthy. Its incense is male frankincense and harmal seed. God knows best.

[Discourse on the mansion of al-Ghafr, and this is its form]

Its form is one dot (•), and its letter is ʿayn. When the Moon descends in it, a spiritual being descends from it, bringing love, affection, comfort, and benefit from kings. It is suitable for medicines and for substances that dissolve deadly poisons and repel their harm. It is suitable for managing the honored stone, treating spiritual entities, and making talismans. Whoever is born in it will be unlucky and marked by cunning and deceit. Its incense is male frankincense only.

[Discourse on the mansion of al-Zubānā, and this is its form]

Its form is two hāʾ marks (hāʾ hāʾ), and its letter is ʿayn. When the Moon descends in it, it is a fortunate planet. It is associated with a true iron strike, a dog bite, and a person about whom enemies have spoken evil; an illness may afflict his body, causing pain and making recovery arduous. Whoever is born in it will be fortunate in all his movements. Its incense is wormwood only. God knows best.

[Discourse on the mansion of al-Iklīl, and this is its form]

Its form is two hāʾ marks (hāʾ hāʾ), and its letter is fāʾ. When the Moon descends in it, it is a planet mixed of fortune and misfortune. A spiritual being descends in it, drawing tribulations and hatred and bringing about evil and its opposite. Do not travel in it, marry, buy slaves, or plant trees, for its outcome is not praiseworthy. Do not tailor clothes or quarrel in it, and do not settle needs in it. Whoever is born in it will be bad and ill-omened. Its incense is pepper, saffron, and aloeswood. God knows best.

[Discourse on the mansion of al-Qalb, and this is its form]

Its form is one hāʾ mark (hāʾ), and its letter is ṣād. When the Moon descends in it, it is a fortunate watery planet. A spiritual being descends from it and remedies what the preceding mansion damaged. It is suitable for buying weapons and instruments of war, buying beasts of burden and veterinary treatment, cutting trees and crops, plowing, bringing out buried treasure, treating animals, drinking mild medicines, phlebotomy, and cupping. Whoever is born in it will be unlucky, whether male or female, although there is some cunning in it. Its incense is the leaves of al-ihlīlaj. God knows best.
