قال ذو النون المصري رحمه الله تعالى: رأيت شاباً عند الكعبة يكثر الركوع والسجود، فدنوت منه وقلت: إنك تكثر الصلاة، فقال: انتظر الإذن من ربي في الانصراف، فدنوت منه فرأيت رقعة سقطت عليه مكتوب فيها: من العزيز الغفور إلى عبدي الصادق الشكور، انصرف مغفور لك ما تقدم من ذنبك وما تأخر. * وروي عن أنس بن مالك أن النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم مر برجل يصلي، فلما فرغ من التشهد جعل يقول: اللهم إني أسألك بأن لك الحمد، لا إله إلا أنت، يا حنان يا منان، يا بديع السموات والأرض، يا حي يا قيوم، فقال النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم: أتدرون ما دعا به؟ قلنا: الله ورسوله أعلم، قال: إنه دعا الله باسمه العظيم الأعظم الذي إذا دعي به أجاب، وإذا سئل به أعطى، عشر أسماء: الواحد الأحد الصمد الفعال لما يريد السميع البصير المقتدر القوي القائم، ليس شيء أفضل من العمل بها، فقال: إنها تنفع من معاناة الأثقال والأمور الصعاب. Dhū al-Nūn the Egyptian, may God Most High have mercy on him, said: "I saw a young man at the Kaaba who bowed and prostrated frequently. I drew near him and said: 'You pray a great deal.' He said: 'I await permission from my Lord to leave [UNCLEAR: the unvocalized verb can be read as either "I await" or "Wait"; its grammatical person is uncertain].' I drew near him and saw a slip of paper that had fallen upon him, on which was written: 'From the Mighty, the Forgiving, to My truthful, ever-grateful servant: depart; your earlier and later sins are forgiven you.'" * It is related from Anas ibn Mālik that the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, passed by a man who was praying. When the man had finished the tashahhud, he began to say: "O God, I ask You by the fact that praise belongs to You; there is no god but You. O Tenderly Compassionate One (Ḥannān), O Bestower of Favors (Mannān), O Originator (Badīʿ) of the heavens and the earth, O Living One (Ḥayy), O Self-Subsisting Sustainer (Qayyūm)." Then the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, said: "Do you know by what he made his supplication?" We said: "God and His Messenger know best." He said: "He has invoked God by His great and greatest Name, by which, when He is invoked, He answers, and when He is asked, He gives; ten Names: the One (al-Wāḥid), the Unique One (al-Aḥad), the One on Whom all depend (al-Ṣamad), the Doer of whatever He wills (al-Faʿʿāl limā yurīd), the All-Hearing (al-Samīʿ), the All-Seeing (al-Baṣīr), the All-Powerful (al-Muqtadir), the Strong (al-Qawī), the Subsisting One (al-Qāʾim) [the source declares ten Names but lists nine entries]. Nothing is better than acting by them." Then he said [UNCLEAR: the speaker of this further statement is not explicitly identified]: "They are beneficial in coping with burdens and difficult matters." (A benefit) This supplication is for the Names of which it has been said that each Name among them is the Greatest Name of God.
