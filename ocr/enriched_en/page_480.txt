اعلم أن الوكيل هو الذي توكل له الأمور كلها يدبرها كيف يشاء وهو على قسمين قسم يوكل
إليه بعض الأمور فذلك ناقص وقسم توكل إليه كل الأمور فذلك كامل وهو الله تعالى ومعنى الوكالة
الكفالة ومن نظر إلى إصلاح بطنه وتحقق قصده أرسل الله له نور الشبع وطمأنينة الاستغناء وذاك
خمسة أقسام الأول توكل بلزوم القلوب وذلك أن الله كتب في صحف القلوب بالإيمان ثم أيده بروح
منه ثم رتبه ثم أنزل السكينة لزيادة إيمان الأفعال للترتيب لأن الأول إيمان الفطرة وهيه منه أطمئن
بحقيقة مع حقائق الإيمان الواردة عليه في كل نفس من أنفاس وقته فإذا رأى ذلك علم أنه قد صح
له التوكل ولا يكون ذلك للقلب إلا بدوام الذكر والتزام الصدق ثم يليه على الإيمان أعني إيمان
الأعمال الذي وقعت المعرفة عليه من الأفعال لأن الله جعل عليه دلالة يعرف بها قال تعالى ولكن

English:
— 470 —

"…Know that God Most High brought forth the existents as He willed, and brought forth for every existent a name from His names, and spread over it that name that it accept the Oneness of the natural disposition and the bringing-forth by it; then He spread the meaning of His name over the existents. And he who is characterized by this name witnesses the made-things of Allah, and everything the Book spoke of is truth, and he witnesses every movement and every action and every deed — it is from the truth. And upon you is much litany and spiritual practice and sincerity and recitation of the name its number — so the angel descends upon you, RUMYĀ'ĪL, and fulfills your need; and he who is characterized by it distinguishes between the truth and the falsehood. And of its special properties: it benefits for the fulfillment of needs — whoever has its square and directs himself to a need, it is answered; and if he matches his number and recites it with the standing mention, he witnesses wonders of the making of God Most High; and if it is written on silver and carried by the possessor of cold phlegm, it benefits him; and if it is written and placed in a place of a just ruler and the truth, and he who increases much sees very strange things — and this is its figure.

[The square of al-Ḥaqq — columns ā ل ḥ ق: row 1 = 29 / 3 / 99 / 99; row 2 = 32 / 32 / 6 / 98; row 3 = 7 / 97 / 4 / 30]

And as for his mention, you say (In the name of Allah the Merciful, the Compassionate): 'O Allah, You are the absolute Truth existing in the reality of Your Essence, described by the realities of the highest attributes in Your sanctities. I ask You by the secret of the lights of Your names, the highest, that You realize for me every truth in existence and nullify for me every falsehood perished and absent. I ask You by the secret of Your existence by which You realized the realities of Your attributes, that You raise my heart by the truth of the Truth to the witnessing of the realities of Your Essence, so I be with You and with the existence of every existent eternally, constantly, O Ḥaqq, O Mubīn.' (Section on His name, God Most High, al-Wakīl) Know that al-Wakīl is He to whom all affairs are entrusted — He manages them as He wills; and He is of two divisions: a division to whom some affairs are entrusted — so that is deficient; and a division to whom all affairs are entrusted — so that is complete, and He is God Most High. And the meaning of the agency is the suretyship. And whoever looks to the rectitude of his inward and realizes his intention, Allah sends down upon him the light of satiety and the tranquility of self-sufficiency; and that is five divisions. The first: entrusting with the preserving of the hearts by the faith, then He aided it by a spirit from Him, then He ordered it, then He sent down the tranquility for the increase of the faith — the deeds for the ordering, for the first is the faith of the natural disposition, and it is the self-sufficing by the reality with the realities of the faith arriving upon it in every breath of its time; so when he saw that, he knew that the trust was established for him, and that is not for the heart but by the continuance of the mention and the adherence to sincerity; then he follows it by the faith — i.e. the faith of the deeds which the knowledge fell upon from the deeds, for Allah made upon it a sign by which it is known, as He said exalted 'He has made beloved to you the faith and adorned it in your hearts and made hateful to you disbelief and defiance and disobedience' — so this is a sign by which the existence of the faith is known, and it is by the meaning of the first natural disposition which is the knowledge of the gnostic from where the specificity of the Truth; and what was specific to the hand of His care from the bearer of the trust which was magnified; and from it the saying of the Prophet (peace be upon him): 'The Holy Spirit breathed into my conscience that no soul reaches its term until it completes its provision.' And he who is characterized by this name perseveres in the reverential piety and the cutting-off to God Most High; and the scholars said in the chapter of trust many things, and it is of the invocations of the saints and the realized masters. And it has a retreat and administers in everything the majestic administers — for its number is as its number, and its recitation after every prayer its number, and there accrue to him many knowings. And as for the standing mention by it, you say (In the name of Allah the Merciful, the Compassionate): 'O Allah, You are al-Wakīl, the Preserver of what You brought into existence in the details of the Jabarūt and in the details of the Dominion and the treasuries of the Kingdom, administering the world of the Throne and the Footstool and the secrets of the high worlds. I ask You that You witness me the completeness of the trust and witness me that in my states from the world of the Throne and the Footstool and the secrets of the high worlds to the worlds of the Divinity; and that You realize my trust in You and my reliance upon You, that I be by my trust in You screened by Your complete screening, watched over by Your highest names and Your attributes that are by Allah. O Wakīl.' [page ends — concluded on 481]
