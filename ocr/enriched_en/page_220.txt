عليهِ وسلمَ وأنصارِهِ اللهمَّ ادخلْ محبةَ يوسفَ في قلبِ زليخا و يا مَن أدخلَ محبةَ موسى في قلبِ
آسيةَ بنتِ مزاحمٍ ادخلْ محبةَ كذا وكذا في قلبِ كذا وكذا اللهمَّ يا مَن أدخلَ محبةَ محمدٍ صلى اللهُ
عليهِ وسلمَ في قلبِ خديجةَ بنتِ خويلدٍ وعائشةَ بنتِ أبي بكرٍ أدخلْ محبةَ كذا وكذا في قلبِ كذا وكذا
كما أدخلتَ الليلَ في النهارِ والنهارَ في الليلِ والذكرَ في الأنثى لو أنفقتَ ما في الأرضِ جميعاً ما ألفتَ بينَ
قلوبهم ولكنَّ اللهَ ألفَ بينهم أنهُ عزيزٌ حكيمٌ ولا حولَ ولا قوةَ إلا باللهِ العليِّ العظيمِ وإن شئتَ تكتبها
بعدَ طلوعِ الشمسِ من يومِ الجمعةِ.
(فصل) ومنها سُئلَ ذو النونِ المصريُّ عن أسماءِ أمِّ موسى فقالتِ الرواياتُ فيها كثيرةٌ والذي صحَّ

وتبخرْ في يومِ مسجدِكَ بكرةً وعشيةً باللبانِ الذكرِ والعودِ وبعدَ كلِّ صلاةٍ ٧ مراتٍ فإذا تمَّ ذلكَ
فأقرأهُ في نفسك ولا تدعو بهِ على قفلٍ ولا سلسلةٍ ولا على غلقٍ إلا وقد فتحَ لكَ أسرعَ من طرفةِ عينٍ
وهذا هو تقولُ بسمِ اللهِ الرحمنِ الرحيمِ ربِّ هل بايتَ رغماً للمؤمنةِ الصديقةِ أمِّ موسى عليهِ السلامُ

بنورهِ الآفاقُ وفتحتْ بهِ إذا قاسى افتحْ هذا القفلَ أو هذا العملَ وإن شئتَ قلتَ افتحْ قلبَ كذا وكذا

السماءَ بالمطرِ الغزيرِ افتحِ الأغلالَ والأغلالَ والقلوبَ إنكَ على كلِّ شيءٍ قديرٌ اللهمَّ اشبيهِ اشبيهِ اشبيهِ

English:
— 284 —

…"by His name the hearts are at rest": "O Allah, O So-and-so son of So-and-so, or So-and-so daughter of So-and-so — just as You set right between Muḥammad, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, and his helpers; O Allah, place the love of Yūsuf in the heart of Zulaykhā; and O You who placed the love of Mūsā in the heart of Āsiya daughter of Muzāḥim, place the love of such-and-such in the heart of such-and-such. O Allah, O You who placed the love of Muḥammad, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, in the heart of Khadīja daughter of Khuwaylid and 'Ā'isha daughter of Abī Bakr, place the love of such-and-such in the heart of such-and-such — just as You placed the night in the day and the day in the night and the male in the female — 'if you were to spend all that is in the earth, you could not reconcile their hearts, but Allah reconciled them; indeed He is Exalted in Might, Wise' — and there is no power nor strength except by Allah, the High, the Great." And if you wish, write it after sunrise on Friday.

( Chapter ) And among them, Dhū al-Nūn al-Miṣrī was asked about the names of the mother of Mūsā, and he said: "The narrations about it are many, and what is authentic with us is that you supplicate with this supplication and fast seven days without speaking to anyone, and give charity each day to seven poor, and fumigate in your mosque morning and evening with frankincense and aloes-wood, and after each prayer seven times; then when that is complete, recite it to yourself, and you do not supplicate by it over a lock nor chain nor bolt but it is opened for you faster than the blinking of an eye." And this is what is said: "In the name of Allah, the Merciful, the Compassionate; my Lord, did the faithful, truthful mother of Mūsā, upon him peace, pass the night in scorn — and Allah, the Exalted, the Wise, the Powerful, the Proud, the Guardian, the Great, the Merciful, the Compassionate — by whom the climes are opened and the horizons shone with His light, and by whom it is opened when one suffers — open this lock or this work"; and if you wish you said: "Open the heart of such-and-such with the love of such-and-such, Allah, the Exalted, the Proud, the Great, the Guardian, the Great." And it is said in another narration: the names of the mother of Mūsā loosen the bonds and locks and other than that, and they are: "Ṭasūma 2, Ḥayūma 2, Qayyūma 2, Dā'ima 2, Dayūma 2. O Allah, O You who opened the sky with abundant rain, open the shackles and the shackles and the hearts — indeed You are over all things powerful. O Allah, ishbīh ishbīh ishbīh wa-shabīh wa-dīdūḥ dīdah wa-ṭāḥūl 'ajīl lahu wa-makā'id wa-salām wa-mā yūḥā wa-maḥlūt dām aḥrāruhā junūduhā ḥābūrah yūh dah yadīḥā wa-ḥābīb jānūnah mardūduh — so efface ṭaffaf laffaf kahf sahf fa'īl yā baṭā wa-ṭiyā ṭiyā laturbih illā mutawakkal­tum wa-ajabtum wa-aṭ'amtum Allāh and His Messenger and His power and His dominion — open this lock — and if it is of iron, make it fly; and if it is of mifraq or copper or wood, break it — by the right of these names upon you. And if you wish you said: 'Open the heart of such-and-such with love and affection toward such-and-such.' "

( Chapter ) On the mention of the Seal of Solomon, upon him peace: know that whoever seals with it and preserves it — from disobedience he purifies the garment and body, silent of tongue, leaving sins, adhering to obedience, certain in Allah, exalted and majestic. And it is a seal of obedience which the Exalted touched from him; the Seal of Solomon, upon him peace, was upon four tablets, and above each tablet is written: on the right side { "Indeed we are to Allah" }, and toward the left side { "Indeed Allah, the Living, the Self-Subsistent" }, and on the third { "Allah the Exalted — no exalted one but Me, and exalted the span of My seal" }, and on the fourth is written the Throne Verse encompassing: "O Muḥammad, Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace."
