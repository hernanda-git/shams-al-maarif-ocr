English:
15

(Section) Since these mansions require the twelve signs of the zodiac for their wisdom to appear in them, the twelve letters are arranged in six divisions. They are the letters of “There is no god but God,” as follows: lām, alif, lām, alif, hāʾ, alif, lām, alif, lām, alif, lām, hāʾ. They are twelve letters, corresponding to the twelve signs of the zodiac, and each sign resides in them. Just as some of the signs are fixed and some mutable, some of these letters are fixed and some mutable. Affirmation and negation alternate from existence to nonexistence, from which it comes.

These circular letters are not the sphere of the Moon, because the Moon is closer to the earth than the others, while the letters are closer to us than the Moon: they are implanted in the nature of every human being. The letters were discussed before the mansions, so there is no need to repeat them. Everything increases as the Moon increases and decreases as it decreases; this is wisdom and knowledge of its rank. Do you not see how darkness and other things increase? [SECTION MARK]

Since there are seven planets, God placed in them the secret of guidance, as He says: “One who places a vicegerent on the earth,” and: “One who makes the angels messengers.” The powers of these seven planets are taken from the powers of the inner divisions, which are “There is no god but God.” Thus they draw from these exalted, holy celestial bodies.

These letters are hot, moist, cold, and dry. The hot letters are seven: alif, hāʾ, ṭāʾ, mīm, fāʾ, shīn, dhāl. The moist letters are seven: bāʾ, wāw, yāʾ, nūn, sīn, tāʾ, ḍād. The cold letters are likewise seven: jīm, zāy, thāʾ, ṣād, qāf, thāʾ, ẓāʾ. The dry letters are likewise: dāl, ḥāʾ, lām, rāʾ, khāʾ, ghayn. [UNCLEAR: the source calls this final list seven while displaying six letters.]

The explanation is that fire combines heat and dryness; air combines moisture and heat; water combines moisture and cold; and earth combines dryness and cold. These produce the four natures mentioned: yellow bile, blood, phlegm, and black bile. Yellow bile is the nature of fire and is dry; blood is the nature of air and is hot and moist; black bile is the nature of earth and is cold and dry; and phlegm is the nature of water and is cold and moist.

The effect of this has become evident. Some names repel fever when written; they are the cold and dry names, such as al-ʿAdl and al-Shadīd, which are entered into a heptagonal figure. Some names repel severe cold; [UNCLEAR: the source's final identification of these with burning yellow bile]. This is the form of its wafq, as you see. Understand, and you will be guided.

(Section on the fortunate and inauspicious times and their hours, and which of them accords with good and evil)

(Sunday). The first hour belongs to the Sun. Work in it for love, acceptance, and approaching kings and rulers; it is also suitable for wearing new clothes. The second hour belongs to Venus. It is a blameworthy hour; do not do anything at all in it. The third hour belongs to Mercury. Travel in it, and write in it for affection, love, speech, and whatever resembles them. The fourth hour belongs to the Moon. It has no benefit for anything; do not buy, and it is suitable for nothing. The fifth hour belongs to Saturn. Work in it for separation, hatred, enmity, and the like. The sixth hour belongs to Jupiter. Seek your needs from kings in it. The seventh hour belongs to Mars. Do not do anything in it. The eighth hour belongs to the Sun. Work in it for every need; it is suitable for all matters and is extremely fortunate. The ninth hour belongs to Venus. Write in it to attract people, incline hearts, and for whatever resembles that. The tenth hour belongs to Mercury.
