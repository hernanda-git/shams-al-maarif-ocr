English:
15

(Chapter) And when these mansions were in need of the twelve signs of the zodiac so that His wisdom might appear in them, the twelve letters were in six divisions, and they are the letters of “There is no god but Allah,” thus: ل ا ل ا ه ا ل ا ل ا ل اه, and they are twelve letters according to the number of the twelve signs of the zodiac; in them every sign establishes. And when the signs are among them fixed and among them mutable, likewise these letters are among them fixed and among them mutable; so affirmation and negation are mutable from existence to nonexistence, which is from it. And these circular letters are not among them the sphere of the moon, because the moon is nearer to the earth than other than it, and the letters are nearer to us than the moon because they are implanted in the nature of every human being. And the letters have been mentioned before the mansions, so it has sufficed from repeating them. And everything increases with the increase of the moon and decreases with its decrease, wisdom and knowledge of its rank. Do you not see how darkness increases and other than it? ۝ And when the seven planets were, Allah made in them the secret of guidance, according to His saying تعالى: making in the earth a vicegerent, and His saying تعالى: making the angels messengers. And the powers of these seven planets are taken from the powers of the inward divisions, and they are “There is no god but Allah”; so these are derived from these exalted sanctities. And these letters, the hot and the moist and the cold and the dry: the hot are seven letters, and they are ا هـ ط م ف ش ذ; and the moist are seven letters ب و ي ن س ت ض; and the cold are seven likewise ج ز ث ص ق ث ظ; and the dry likewise د ح ل ر خ غ. And the explanation of that is that fire is a gatherer of heat and dryness, and air is a gatherer of moisture and heat, and water is a gatherer of moisture and cold, and earth is a gatherer of dryness and cold. And the four natures mentioned have become, namely yellow bile and blood and phlegm and black bile. So yellow bile is the nature of fire, dry; and blood is the nature of air, hot, moist; and black bile is the nature of earth, cold, dry; and phlegm is the nature of water, cold, moist. And the effect of that has appeared by sight, and that is that some names repel fever by writing, and they are the cold and dry names, such as al-‘adl and al-shadid; one enters them in a Mabsu‘. And some names repel the cold of death, and they are the burning yellow bile. And this is the form of its square as you see; so understand, you will be guided.

for love and acceptance and entering upon kings and rulers, and it is suitable
in it for wearing new clothes. The second hour is for Venus, and it is an
blameworthy hour; do not do in it anything at all of all things. The hour
third is for Mercury; travel in it and write in it for affection and love
and speech and what resembles them. The fourth hour is for the moon; there is no benefit in it
in anything, and do not buy, and it is not suitable for anything. The fifth hour is for Saturn; work in it for separation and hatred and enmity and the like
. The sixth hour is for Jupiter; seek in it needs from kings. The seventh hour is for Mars; do not work in it anything
. The eighth hour is for the sun; work in it all needs, for it is suitable for all matters and it is very fortunate. The hour
ninth is for Venus; write in it for bringing people and inclining hearts and what resembles that. The tenth hour is for Mercury
