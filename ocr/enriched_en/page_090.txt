الأعداد في الحروف، فالأعداد علويات للروحانيات، والحروف للدوائر الجسمانيات والملكوتيات.
والأعداد سر الأقوال، والحروف سر الأفعال، فعالم العرش أعداد، وعالم الكرسي حروف، فنسبة
الحروف للأعداد كنسبة الكرسي للعرش، فبسر الأعداد فُهمت القدرة، وذلك أن الباري سبحانه
مدح نفسه بسر الأعداد في قوله تعالى: {وَكَفَىٰ بِنَا حَاسِبِينَ}، وجعل مدحه للحروف عائداً عليه في قوله
تعالى: {اقْرَأْ بِاسْمِ رَبِّكَ} الآية. ولما كان الكرسي الواقع متصلاً بذات الكرسي المحيط فأخر أحدها،
الأول والآخر هيئة الحروف من الأعداد، ولذلك أخر مرتبة الحروف، فبسر الأعداد فُهم سر
العقل الرباني، وبسر الحروف فُهم سر الروح الروحاني، فآخر مرتبة العقل أول مرتبة النفس
العلوية، وهي الفيض الأول أيضاً، كما أن الحروف مأخوذة من حرف الشيء وهو طرفه، وكان العدد
أوله وأوسطه، ولكل أول وسط وطرف، فبسر الحروف فُهم سر الكرسي الأعلى والكرسي الواسع
الأبهى، وذلك أن الذوات من العالم العلوية والسفلية مختلفة باختلاف ذواتها في الكرسي الأعلى
واختلاف نقلها وأطوارها في الكرسي الأبهى، فالكرسي الواسع أول مبادئ العرش من نسبة أول
انبعاثات الحقائق الملكوتيات، واستمداد آخر درجة من السفليات أول درجة من العلويات. ۞ واعلم
أن العرش الأبهى فيض النور الأول، والكرسي الواسع فيض النور الثاني، والكرسي الأعلى فيض
النور الثالث، فالفيض الأول أغنى هو الأول، والثالث هو أول الحروف وآخر مرتبة العدد،
وهو السر المعبر عنه بحقيقة البشر الذي فيه التنبيه بقوله تعالى: {إِنِّي خَالِقٌ بَشَرًا مِّن طِينٍ}، ثم لما كمل
القبضتين وجبت مخاطبته الحقيقة الإنسانية، فقال تعالى: {فَإِذَا سَوَّيْتُهُ وَنَفَخْتُ فِيهِ مِن
رُّوحِي فَقَعُوا لَهُ سَاجِدِينَ}، يعني القبضتين الآخريين والفيض الأول، فالعالم بأسره علويه وسفليه
بحقيقة هذه الثلاث إضافات، فمن العالم من محض فيضاً، ومنهم من حمل فيضتين، ومنهم من حمل ثلاثاً،

Numbers within letters; numbers are higher things belonging to spiritual beings, while letters belong to bodily and Malakūt circles.
Numbers are the secret of sayings, and letters are the secret of actions. The world of the Throne is numbers, and the world of the Seat is letters; the relation of letters to numbers is like the relation of the Seat to the Throne. Through the secret of numbers, power was understood. For the Creator—glorified be He—praised Himself through the secret of numbers in the saying of God Most High: {And enough are We as reckoners}, and made His praise of letters return to Him in the saying of God Most High: {Read in the Name of your Lord}—the verse.
Since the Situated Seat was connected to the essence of the Encompassing Seat, one of them was put later; the first and the last, the form of letters from numbers [UNCLEAR: the connection of these phrases and the agent of the postponement are unclear]. Therefore the rank of letters was put later. Through the secret of numbers, the secret of the divine intellect was understood, and through the secret of letters, the secret of the spiritual spirit was understood. The last rank of the intellect is the first rank of the higher soul, and it is also the first effusion. Letters are derived from the ḥarf of a thing, namely its edge, while number is its beginning and its middle; every beginning has a middle and an edge.
Through the secret of letters, the secret of the Higher Seat and the Vast, Most Splendid Seat was understood. For the beings of the higher and lower world differ according to the difference of their essences in the Higher Seat and the difference of their transitions and phases in the Most Splendid Seat. The Vast Seat is the first of the beginnings of the Throne, in relation to the first emanations of the Malakūt realities; the last degree of the lower things draws its support from the first degree of the higher things. * Know that the Most Splendid Throne is the effusion of the first light, the Vast Seat is the effusion of the second light, and the Higher Seat is the effusion of the third light.
The first effusion is richer; it is the first [UNCLEAR: the connection of 'richer' with 'it is the first' is unclear]. The third is the first of the letters and the last rank of number. This is the secret expressed as the reality of the human being, to which attention is drawn in the saying of God Most High: {Indeed, I am creating a human being from clay}. Then, when the two handfuls had been completed, addressing him, the human reality, became necessary [UNCLEAR: the grammatical relation of 'the human reality' to 'addressing him' is unclear]. God Most High then said: {So when I have formed him and breathed into him of My spirit, fall down prostrate before him}, meaning the last two handfuls and the first effusion.
The world in its entirety, its higher and its lower, is through the reality of these three attributions. Among the beings of the world, some are purely one effusion, some carry two effusions, and some carry three; this is the world, the all-containing pole. Therefore the bearer remains fixed in relation to their essences, on the basis of the poverty of its known Seats, not altering the realities of their own numbers, nor changing the essences of their bodies through what appears in the world of human reality and the secret of composition [UNCLEAR: the pronoun referents and the grammatical attachment of 'not altering' are uncertain].
It belongs to the realities of the Higher Seat; that is in the world of Mulk [UNCLEAR: the referent of 'it' and its connection to human reality or composition are unclear]. The human reality of the world becomes manifest to him: the secret of the Vast Seat, the knowledge of Jabarūt [UNCLEAR: the grammatical connection among 'human reality', 'secret', and 'knowledge' is unclear]. Through it, within the realities of the higher spirit, the secrets of the Most Splendid Seat bear witness. Therein he witnesses the realities of the genesis of the Hereafter, namely the reality of the lofty breath; his essence is thus completed and his reality becomes manifest [UNCLEAR: the referent of 'he' is not explicit here].
So whoever leaves the straight line for the oblique and enters into fortification; for when an oblique line is added to an oblique line and you extend them, they meet and become straight. When it is added to the straight line and you extend them to their balance, they meet; and turning it toward manifestation, growth, stability, and witnessing, with a finite count [UNCLEAR: the subject and object of 'turning it', and the continuation of the opening 'whoever' clause, are unclear].
