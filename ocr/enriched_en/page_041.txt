Isrāfīl is the bearer of the Trumpet and the blowing. He has three blasts: the blast of terror, of which the Most High said, “Then those in the heavens and those on the earth were terrified”; the blast of swooning, of which the Most High said, “Then those in the heavens and those on the earth swooned”; and the blast of resurrection, of which the Most High said, “Then it is blown into it again, and behold, they are standing and looking.” Each blast has matters peculiar to it.

ʿIzrāʾīl, peace be upon him, is entrusted with taking the souls and bringing about their extinction. Through him tyrants are subdued and the roots of the disbelievers and the arrogant are cut off. Through him the believer finds rest, reaches his Lord, and rejoices in what God has prepared for him of generosity, bounty, pardon, and forgiveness.

Mīkāʾīl, peace be upon him, is entrusted with the provisions and sustenance of God’s servants and with preserving their existence. There is no sesame seed on earth without an angelic helper assigned to it for its owner. Jibrīl, peace be upon him, has assistants too numerous to count, and he has invocations and works that distinguish them from one another.

These four are kings of days, with particular days assigned to them. Jibrīl, peace be upon him, has Monday because it is cold and moist. Isrāfīl, peace be upon him, has Thursday, which is hot and moist. ʿIzrāʾīl, peace be upon him, has Saturday because it is cold and moist; [UNCLEAR: the source then associates his domain with earth, death, and extinction]. Mīkāʾīl, peace be upon him, has Wednesday, which is composed of the four natures.

There are four awfāq associated with them: the sevenfold for Jibrīl, peace be upon him; the square for Isrāfīl, peace be upon him; the triangular for ʿIzrāʾīl, peace be upon him; and the eightfold for Mīkāʾīl, peace be upon him. These are the four awfāq, and this is their form, as you see.

Know—may God grant me and you success in obeying Him—that these awfāq have a tremendous influence on everything you desire. Whoever contemplates them finds them very accurate and can do with them whatever he wishes. Fear God, your Lord, in all your circumstances.

If you want to carry out an operation using one of the four awfāq, write its khātimah, or seal, after counting it and verifying its correctness, and add to it the name of the object sought; you will obtain what you want. The sevenfold is written on paper or pure white silver on Monday at sunrise, during the hour of the Moon.
