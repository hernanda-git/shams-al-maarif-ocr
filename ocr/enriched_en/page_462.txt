أزلية مضافة له بقوله تعالى (ولقد كتبنا في الزبور من بعد الذكر أن الأرض يرثها عبادي الصالحون)
ثم خلق عالم الملكوت ثم لما رتب هذه العوالم المتعددة للأمهات ودرجات الارتقاء وأظهر من أمره
العلي الذي قامت به الأكوان فاشتق ذلك الأمر عموما وخصوصا فكان على ١٢ مرتبة وأمر كان
بالإيجاد * الأول وهو يوم أخذ المواثيق على القبضتين وعلى الأرواح والعقول معا وذلك يوم أخذ
المواثيق في الفطر في حمل الأمانة * والثاني أمر قام به العرش للاستقلال لأهل السموات

والمنشآت وينفجر للتالي ينابيع الحكمة وشرط ذلك قطع العلائق الباطنية والتلذذ بمناجاته في
الأسحار وتلاوته عدده مضروبا في نفسه وخادمه شرائط من عوالم عزازيل * (تنبيه)
اعلم أن الله لما أراد أن يخلق آدم أمر جبريل أن يقبض من الأرض قبضة فنزل وأراد أن يقبض

English:
— 456 —

"…and He deposited it in the Preserved Tablet within it for the mention — not by a composite pen that does not understand approximation; and He completed them by an eternal will added to Him, by His saying exalted 'And indeed We wrote in the Psalms after the Reminder that the earth shall be inherited by My righteous servants.' Then He created the world of the Dominion; then when He ordered these numerous worlds for the mothers and the degrees of ascension, and manifested from His high command upon which the existences stood — so that command was derived generally and particularly, and was upon twelve ranks; and a command that was by the bringing-forth. * The first is the day of taking the covenants upon the two fists and upon the spirits and the intellects together — and that is the day of taking the covenants in the innate disposition in bearing the trust. * The second, a command stood by the Throne for the independence of the people of the heavens and the earth and the existences. * The third, a command stood by the Footstool and bore the forms of the stored existents. * The fourth, a command stood by the Command for its disposal to the manifestation by what Allah deposited in it of the secrets of the ordering for the existences. * The fifth, a command stood by the Spirit for the manifestation of the detail. * The sixth, a command stood by the Intellect for the people of the heavens and the existences. * The seventh, a command stood by the forms. * The eighth, a command stood by the heavens and the earth. * The ninth, a command stood by the banners after the bringing-forth. * The tenth, the command of the standing of the breath and the promised muster. * The eleventh, a command disposing between the people of Paradise and the Fire. * The twelfth, for the eternity, and it returns to what it began from. And this name [al-Qābiḍ] has a majestic retreat that gives its possessor the unveiling upon the roots of the two fists and the origination, and there gush for the follower springs of wisdom; and its condition is the cutting of the inward attachments and the delighting in His whispered prayer in the watches of the night, and the recitation of its number multiplied by itself; and its servant is SHARĀ'IṬ from the worlds of 'AZĀZĪL. * (Notice) Know that when Allah willed to create Adam, He commanded Jibrīl to take a handful from the earth; so he descended and willed to take, but she swore against him and refused, and ascended. So Allah commanded Isrāfīl to do that, and she swore against him; and likewise Mīkā'īl. So He commanded 'Azrā'īl by His name al-Qābiḍ; and when he descended to the earth she swore against him, but a compelling force said to her: 'Is not He whose soul is in His hand the One who sent me?' She said: 'Yes.' He said to her: 'Then I disobey you and he disobeys me' — then he took a handful from her and ascended to the sky. And he has a chant of glorification by His name al-Qābiḍ, so the earth shrank and diminished; and Allah said to him: 'You were the manifestor of the seizing, and you seize the spirits' — so he became trusted over the two fists. And if you recite the name and rebuke by it any king, he comes humble, abased, and under his hand four commanders under each commander — whatsoever Allah willed. * And whoever recites it over an oppressor and entrusts him to it, the worlds of this nation are seized from him and destroyed him. * And if it is written in a seal and you recite over it the name its number and write around it the king and the standing mention and carry it with you, it deafens against you the tongues of the creatures — and this is its figure. And its mention with the basmala: 'O Allah, You are the Seizer of the heavens and the Spreader of the earths and the all, by Your awe and Your greatness and Your power — You measured the things by the force of the will of the chosen. O Allah, O You who seize and spread the chosen and supplied the realized light with life in the earth and the heavens, the manifestor by the force of governance — truly the governance in the spreading of the movements and the seizing of the stillnesses and all the existents. I ask You that You seize my heart and my limbs from what distances me from the disobediences, and that my light of life and sincerity not be veiled from me, and seize from me the evil of every opponent and tyrant and the evil of every confused envier; and make my seizing and my perishing joyful, not seduced nor defrauded. O Allah, spread my provision and ease for me my affair, and what You measured in the eternity of eternities. O Allah, illumine my heart and spread, O Spreader, O Self-Subsistent, and bless.'
