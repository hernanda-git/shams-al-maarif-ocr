والشقاوات ﷺ وأما اسمه تعالى المؤخر فهو اسم عظيم ملكه جحائيل وهو رئيس على ٤ قواد تحت
يد كل قائد ٨٤٦ صفا كل صف ٨٤٦ والروح ينزل على الذاكر ودعاؤه يامؤخر أنت الذي
أخرت رحمتك لأهل الآخرة ومررت واحدة لوضع التراحم بين أهل الأرض والشهادة وأنت ذو القوة
والاقتدار وأنت الذي توجد الشيء كما تحب وتختار وتقدم من تقدم وتؤخر من تؤخر بواسطة
الأقدار أسألك اللهم بتقديم كل مقدم وتأخير كل مؤخر القدم في كل وأعوذ بك من شر الذي
أشكل فتحير واسألك اللهم بلطائف رحمتك أن تجعلني صحيحاً من الأسقام ثقة توالي الانعام وارزقني
الاحاطة الكبرى والنور الالهي والسر الاسنى إذا الجود والنعماء يارب العالمين ﷺ وأما اسمه تعالى
الاول فهو اسم عظيم والروح المخلوق منه دردائيل هو رئيس على ٤ قواد تحت يد كل قائد ٣٧
صفا كل صف ٣٧ والروح ينزل على الذاكر ودعاؤه ياأول أنت الذي ظهرت بك الاوائل
وأنت الذي سبق وجودك كل القبائل وأنت الذي أنزلت المواهب في الاواخر والاصائل وأنت
السابق الذي ما معك غيرك ولا انقضاء لجودك وبقائك وأنت القاهر فوق خلقك والقادر
عليهم بحقك والعلم المديد لأحوالهم والمتصرف في أفعالهم وأقوالهم لك العز والجبروت والبقاء
وبفضلك أعيان الملك والملكوت أسألك بسر أوليتك في الخلق أن ترزقني السابقة في الخيرات

English:
— 298 —

In good things, preceding to You upon the steed of gnosis and obediences, turning to You in the swiftest of times — O You in Whose hand are the keys of the unseen and the witnessed, and by Whose power the keys of the earth and the heavens and the people of happiness and misery. And as for His exalted Name the Delayer — it is a tremendous name; and its angel is 'Jaḥḥā'īl', and he is chief over four commanders, under each commander 846 ranks, each rank 846; and the spirit descends upon the invoker. And his supplication: 'O Delayer, You are He who deferred Your mercy for the people of the Hereafter, and allowed one (life) for the placing of mutual mercy between the people of the earth and the witnessed. And You are Possessor of power and ability, and You are He who brings the thing into being as You love and choose, and advance whom You advanced and delay whom You delayed by means of the decreed measures. I ask You, O Allah, by the advancing of every advanced one and the delaying of every delayed one — that You shelter me from the evil of that which was confused and strayed. And I ask You, O Allah, by the subtleties of Your mercy — that You make me sound from illnesses, confident in the succession of favors, and grant me the greatest encompassing and the divine light and the most sublime secret when the bounty and blessing (come), O Lord of the worlds.' And as for His exalted Name the First — it is a tremendous name; and the spirit created from it is 'Dardā'īl', and he is chief over four commanders, under each commander 37 ranks, each rank 37; and the spirit descends upon the invoker. And his supplication: 'O First, You are He by whom the first ones appeared, and You are He whose existence preceded every tribe, and You are He who sent down the gifts in the last and the first. And You are the Preceding, with whom there is none but You, and no end to Your bounty and subsistence; and You are the Subduer above Your creation, and the Able over them by Your right, and the vast knowledge of their states, and the Disposer in their deeds and sayings — to You belong might and dominion and subsistence, and by Your bounty the essences of dominion and the kingdom. I ask You by the secret of Your Firstness in creation — that You grant me precedence in good things, and the existence of the lasting righteous deeds.' And as for His exalted Name the Last — it is a tremendous name; and its angel is 'Daḥyā'īl', and he is chief over four commanders, under each commander 801 ranks, each rank 801; and the spirit descends upon the invoker. And his supplication: 'O Last, You are He who brought out the terms of every creature to its time, and You are He who deferred from the heart of every seeker, of what was possible, Your wrath and detestation, and executed by Your all-encompassing light at the end of his term and the fear of his time. I ask You by the subtleties of the unified gnosis in the secret of Your oneness, and the subtleties of the knowledge stored in Your Firstness — that You make me aware of the consequence of my affair, and grant me a bounty encompassing the subtleties of the realities of my secret and open (state), O Lord of the worlds.' And as for His exalted Name the Manifest — it is a tremendous name; and its angel is ''Ahhā'īl', and he is chief over four commanders, under each commander 1106 ranks, each rank 1106; and the spirit descends upon the invoker. And his supplication: 'O Manifest, You are He who manifested the outward and proclaimed the inward, and You are more manifest than them; You spread out the existent things, and gathered the hidden things, and collected the beings to conceal Your guarded secret. I ask You by the wondrousness of Your creation, and the flashes of Your tenderness and mercy — that You make me manifest in every affair, and make for me from Your decisive affair a matter, and support me by Your power, and bring forth for me from my difficulty ease. Indeed You are Compassionate, Merciful.'
