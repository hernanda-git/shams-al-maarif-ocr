English:
— 212 —

…al-Barr (the Kind), al-Jawād (the Generous), al-Muḥsin (the Doer of Good), al-Mun'im (the Bestower), al-Awwal (the First), al-Ākhir (the Last), al-Ẓāhir (the Manifest), al-Bāṭin (the Hidden), al-Quddūs (the Holy) — "He begets not nor was begotten, and there is none comparable to Him." * And know that these subtleties are swift in effect; it is fitting that each subtlety be engraved on a gold ring whose bezel is silver, or be in the bezel of a ring of one kind, so when you want the remembrance of one of them, seal with its ring and mention it — it hastens the effect for all you want; and it is by the content of a spiritual exercise that you gain what you want. * His saying, exalted: { "And if a whisper from Satan provoke you, seek refuge in Allah; indeed He is Hearing, Seeing" } — this verse is for whispering, fear, fright, the soul's talk, imagination and illusion; so whoever has something of that, let him write it with rose-water and saffron on Friday on seven leaves at sunrise, and swallow one leaf each day and drink a draught of water upon it — it departs from him. The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said: "Satan comes to one of you and says: 'Who created such-and-such' until he says: 'Who created your Lord?' So when he reaches that, let him seek refuge in Allah and stop." And in a narration: "People will keep asking until it is said: 'This created Allah — so who created Allah?' So whoever finds that, let him say: 'We believe in Allah and His Messenger.'" Al-Tirmidhī related from 'Ā'isha, may Allah be pleased with her, that the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said: "Whoever finds something of this whispering, let him say: 'We believe in Allah and His Messenger' three times, and it leaves him." Muslim related from 'Uthmān ibn Abī al-'Āṣ that he said: "I said: 'O Messenger of Allah, Satan has come between me and my prayer and recitation, confusing them for me.' He said: 'That is a satan called Khanza (Khanzab); so when you sense him, seek refuge in Allah, exalted, from him, and spit to your left three times' — and I did it and it left me." And he — Khanza(b) — is with a dotted khā', a quiescent nūn, a zāy, then a singly-dotted bā'; and the scholars differed on the pronunciation of the khā' — some opened it, some closed it, some rounded it. Abū Dāwūd related from Ibn Zamīl that he said: "I said to Ibn 'Abbās: 'What is this thing I find in myself?' — 'Is it...?' I said: 'By Allah, I will not speak it.' He said: 'A thing of doubt?' I laughed. He said: 'None escaped it until Allah sent down { "If you are in doubt of what We revealed to you" }' — then he said to me: 'When you find something in yourself, say: { "He is the First and the Last, the Manifest and the Hidden, and He is Knower of all things." }' " And some scholars said: it is recommended to say { "There is no god but Allah" } for one afflicted by whispering in ablution and prayer and the like, for Satan is the enemy of the disbelievers, and "there is no god but Allah" is the head of remembrance — therefore the masters chose the path of the wayfarers and the instruction of the novices with the saying "there is no god but Allah" as the sweet, and commanded them to persevere in it, and said: the most beneficial remedy to repel whispering is turning to the remembrance of Allah and abounding in it. The Shaykh Aḥmad al-Khwārazmī said: "I complained to Abū Sulaymān al-Dārānī of whispering, and he said: 'When you want it to be cut off from you at whatever time you sense it, rejoice — for when you rejoice at it, it is cut off from you, for the most hateful thing to Satan is the believer Muslim's joy; so when you are saddened by it, it increases you.' " The Shaykh Muḥyī al-Dīn said: "And this supports what some scholars said — that whispering afflicts him whose faith is complete, for the thief does not aim at a ruined house." From Abū al-Dardā', may Allah be pleased with him: whoever says every day seven times: { "But if they turn away, say: 'Allah is sufficient for me; there is no god but He; upon Him I rely, and He is the Lord of the Mighty Throne' " } — and in a narration: he does not die of worry, nor grief, nor harm by iron. And from al-Layth ibn Sa'd: a man's thigh was broken, and a visitor came to him and said: "Place your hand where you find your pain and say: 'Allah is sufficient for me; there is no god but He; upon Him I rely, and He is the Lord of the Mighty Throne' " — and his thigh was mended and he was cured. And of the property of this verse: whoever writes it and hangs it on himself, no care stands before it but his need is fulfilled.
