The mīm was added to it because it brings together the remembrances, supplications, and healing incantations. These are ordered under the Greatest Name, and it falls within its scope, like the prayer, which is the pillar of religion. This is because nothing but it suffices in the opening takbīr of consecration: the prayer is not valid without it, by the consensus and agreement of the scholars of the various regions and the righteous predecessors. Likewise with the call to prayer: with it the prayer is opened, and with it the prayer is concluded. God knows best.

(Section) This Name entails a name and that which is named. This Name is among the things whose knowledge God has reserved to Himself. I will give you an example through which what I swear to you may be understood [UNCLEAR: the connection of “what I swear to you” to the example]. A person may know the name of a medicine and understand its meaning, its powers, and its benefits; after this understanding, the person uses it. This is the level of understanding the utterance, establishing its meaning, and using it as that meaning requires. When a person understands the utterance and establishes its meaning, this is the reality. The manner of use then remains, and the person uses it. Undoubtedly, through this the fruit is obtained and the benefit is commended. This is the aspect to consider. The utterance has two states. One is that God Most High causes it to pass over a person's tongue without that person knowing that it is the Greatest Name of God. Is either of these two sufficient, or is neither sufficient, or is the second sufficient without the first? All of this requires consideration. It may come in a way through which awareness of the Greatest Name of God is obtained: it passes over the tongue without the person realizing that it is that Name. This is the purest of the degrees, and it is founded on expansiveness and following within the mercy of God Most High [UNCLEAR: the intended connection between “expansiveness,” “following,” and mercy]. What brings the servant perfection is understanding in truth. Whatever falls short of this still contains blessing and goodness, and differences arise according to the degrees of understanding. Its evidence is this: is someone whom God Most High has singled out—if this Name passes over that person's tongue—equal to someone whom God Most High does not single out and over whose tongue it does not pass? Rather, this shows you that its blessing is obtained however it occurs. Compare the subsequent degrees with this degree. Understanding may be through transmission, in that a person learns it and says, “This is the Greatest Name, great in goodness.” The same occurs in the Names al-Jabbār, al-Khalīl, al-Jawād, al-Majīd, al-Mājid, and al-Jāmiʿ, for they indicate great goodness. God Most High said: {In them are good, beautiful ones}. Among His Names, Most High, is al-Khayr, the Good. God Most High said: {And God is fully aware of what you do}. As for what indicates adornment and zahw (flourishing), God Most High said: {We have indeed adorned the lowest heaven with lamps}. God Most High also said: {The love of desires has been made attractive to people}. Zahw: zīd (“was increased”), the soundness of fruit [UNCLEAR: the connection between these words is unclear]; it is also said to be the adornment of trees with fruit. As for shīn, it indicates al-shahīd (the witness) and shahādah (testimony), because God Most High said: {God has borne witness that there is no god but Him, and the angels and those possessed of knowledge, upholding justice}. Mushāhadah (witnessing) is direct seeing. The martyrs are alive with their Lord, receiving provision. And shurb (drinking): God Most High said: {They drink from a cup whose mixture is camphor}. Then God Most High said: {In it, called Salsabīl}. And shifāʾ (healing): God Most High said: {We send down from the Qur'an that which is healing and mercy for the believers}. The Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, said: “The healing of my community is in three things: a verse from the Book of God Most High, or a lick of honey, or a cup from the hand of a cupper.” In another narration: “or an incision with a cupping instrument.” As for ẓāʾ, it indicates extended shade, and ẓuhūr (rising) is extended shade. God Most High said: {Upon it they rise}. God Most High also said: {Then they became uppermost}. It also indicates “stabbings for what is desired” [UNCLEAR: the intended sense of al-ṭuʿūn li-l-marghūb]. Among His Names, Most High, is al-Ẓāhir, the Manifest. As for fāʾ, it indicates fiṭrah (original nature), fākihah (fruit), and fuṭūr (fissures). God Most High said: {The fiṭrah of God upon which He fashioned people}.
