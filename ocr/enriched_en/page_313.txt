(The letter jīm) It is a luminous, triangular letter, the first of the degrees of heat and moisture, and it is closer to heat and dryness; for its moisture inclines to heat. This cause necessitated that its day be Tuesday and its planet Mars. * And Hippocrates the sage said: third from the rank of the letters and the first of the ranks of air, that is, bad predominates over its moisture; its form is triangular, and two weights united upon the point of moderation, and a chord in the flattening united upon its two ends. Those chords were not placed for the letter jīm for other than this form except what the ignorant innovated, and it is corrupt.

And it has properties: if its form is written on a crust of bread and around it is written the Exalted’s saying: وإذ قتلتم نفساً the verse, and the one accused of theft takes it, then if he is innocent he swallows it, and if he is a thief he is not able to swallow it. And whoever writes on the fingernails of his left hand, the index and the ring finger, on each nail the Indian letter jīm, and enters upon a tyrant or an arrogant one, he is humbled to him and has his need fulfilled, and no harm from him reaches him. * And whoever writes its letter-composition broken thus: ج ي م then writes around it the Exalted’s saying: سنريهم آياتنا في الآفاق ج ي م وفي أنفسهم ج ي م and the writing is on a tablet of athel wood and is hung on the trees whose bearing has ceased, they will bear fruit. * And whoever makes the numbers of its composition in a 3 by 3 square on a gemstone of crystal and draws around it 7 Indian directions and seals with it, everyone who sees him loves him.
