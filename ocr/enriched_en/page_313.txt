It is indicated by it in most languages, rather in most of them, and the tripled Tā' and the dotted Ẓā' did not appear. And this noble letter is luminous in form, its nature near to moderation, and it has wondrous properties in repelling deadly poisons — if its form is engraved in the following image in a vessel of pure silver, ten times around each form of the Indian Tā' once, and the poisoned and stung one is given pure water from it to vomit and is cured by Allah's leave. And whoever engraves this form upon a silver tablet and hangs it upon a child's head — the vermin do not approach him, nor does smallpox affect him or other (ills), and he is of little crying. And whoever arranges the number seven in a square within a circle, upon a silver ring, and engraves the Indian form around the circle 14 times, and seals with it — the vipers do not approach him, and if poisoned food is presented to him he knows it, and if the poisoned one places it in his mouth and swallows his saliva — he is cured of the poison at once. And whoever writes its numerical compound in seven upon camel-skin, burns it and grinds it, and applies it as kohl to one who has a white spot in his eye — it departs. And if this letter is mixed with the name of al-Aḥad — as a letter of the sought-one's name is written and a letter of the seeker's name is written, and it is hung toward the north wind, and what was authored of it is read upon it, and it is the letter Tā' seven times, and you say by it thus: "Būkulū, O servants of this letter — by such-and-such a charm, to this place"; and it is on Monday in the Moon's increase — the sought (result) is obtained. And as for its oath — none was found but its established name, and we mentioned it in the section of Tā', so one swears by it. ( The Letter Jīm ) It is a luminous, triangular letter, the first rank of heat and moisture, and it is nearer to heat and dryness — for its moisture inclines to heat; so this cause necessitated that it have Tuesday, and its planet Mars. And Buqrāṭ the sage said: "Third of the rank of letters, and first rank of air" — that is, it prevails over its moisture. Its form is a triangle balanced, two (sides) meeting at the point of moderation, and a chord in the plane meeting at its two ends; those chords — the letter Jīm was not placed in other than this form, except what the ignorant invented, and it is corrupt. And it has properties — so whoever writes its form upon a piece of bread, and writes around it His saying, exalted: { And when you killed a soul } (the verse), and the one accused of theft takes it — if he is innocent he swallows it, and if he is a thief he cannot swallow it. And whoever writes upon the nails of his left hand's index and middle, each nail the Indian Jīm, and enters upon a tyrant or arrogant one — he is humbled before him and fulfills his need, and no harm reaches him from him. And whoever writes its letter-compound broken: [J Y M], then writes around it His saying, exalted: { We will show them Our signs in the horizons [J Y M] and in themselves [J Y M] } — and the writing is upon a tablet of athl wood, and it is hung upon the trees whose fruit has ceased, they bear fruit. And whoever arranges the numbers of its compound in a 3-by-3 array, upon a crystal ring-stone, and seven Indian positions are drawn around it, and seals with it — every one who sees him loves him. And this letter has names by which it is invoked: "O Allah, indeed I ask You — O Majestic, O Majestic, O Beautiful, O Gatherer — by what You lodged in the letter Jīm of the hidden secrets and the stored lights — that You subject to me Your noble angels, the servants of this letter, by physicians, in what You commanded them of what is in Your good-pleasure. Indeed You are over all things Able." ( The Letter Ḥā' ) It is a silent, cold, watery letter, and its effect is wondrous in removing thirst and the yellow sore; and it has Thursday, its planet Jupiter, and its works are good in affections and reconciling hearts, and extinguishing the fire of anger.
