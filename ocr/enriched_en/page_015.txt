English:
— 9 —

Its numerical form, when carried with him, protects him by God's permission from harmful enemies, whatever worlds they may belong to. This is its form.

(As for the literal form,) its property is that it removes forgetfulness and sharpens understanding and intellect for whoever persists in drinking it in rainwater and bee honey. It benefits whoever complains of an ailment in the chest or ears. The moon should be in Scorpio, and Mars should regard it with an hostile aspect, on a copper tablet; it protects him from [UNCLEAR: al-dār], and it is highly effective against a scorpion's sting when its water is given to drink after the ring has been dipped into it. Since this square form is made from the sum of the four alifs, which are the secret of the intellect, the secret of the spirit, the secret of the soul, and the secret of the heart, the alif in number is one. When four is multiplied by itself, it becomes sixteen, which is the end of the detailed number, because the Throne, the Footstool, the seven heavens, and the seven earths together amount to sixteen. This number is the end of this square form, which is sixteen. The sixteen is composed of fourteen pairs: the pair of the heavens and earths, namely the seven heavens and the seven earths; the pair of the zodiacal signs, which are twelve; the pair of eight, namely the bearers of the Throne; the pair of six, namely the eight boundaries—above, below, behind, before, right, and left; the pair of four, namely the prophets, the truthful, the martyrs, and the righteous; and the pair of the two verses, namely the testimony that there is no god but God and that Muḥammad is the Messenger of God, peace and blessings be upon him. These are seven pairs.
There is also the odd number fifteen: the Footstool, the heavens, and the earths. There is the odd number thirteen: the Pen, the Tablet, the Trumpet, the Spirit of Holiness, the Footstool, the Throne, and the seven heavens. There is the odd number eleven: the five senses in the human world—hearing, sight, smell, taste, and touch—and the six directions—above, below, right, left, behind, and before. There is the odd number nine: the human entities and the eight natures—heat, dryness, cold, and moisture. Yellow bile is hot and dry; air is hot and moist, and this is the nature of blood; phlegm is cold and moist; and black bile is cold and dry. These are eight distinct elements. There is the odd number seven: the seven spheres—Saturn, Jupiter, Mars, the Sun, Venus, Mercury, and the Moon—the seven days, the seven heavens, the seven earths, and every set of seven. There is the odd number of the pairs, namely the five prayers. There is the odd number three, namely the three abodes: the abode of this world, the abode of the barzakh, and the abode of the Hereafter. There is the odd number one, namely the oddness of the intellect.

Gather within the sixteen eight pairs and eight odd numbers. [UNCLEAR: Each pair receives an odd and an odd, and each odd receives a pair.] An example is: one, one, two, three, and six, and so on to the end; and likewise with the pairs. As for the numerical form written with the natural pen, which is the Indian pen, the explanation of it will follow. This pen is more appropriate and more deserving for it, and it has wondrous secrets in the letters. The method is to place a form and put letters in the positions of the numbers. This is done after a fast of two weeks, with the condition of spiritual discipline, continual purity, and remembrance of God Most High. Then take a square sheet of refined tin, engraved with this literal form. Face the qiblah after praying two cycles, reciting al-Fātiḥah and Āyat al-Kursī once and al-Ikhlāṣ one hundred times, at the hour of Jupiter at sunrise. The moon must be protected from Jupiter, the Sun, and the ascendant of Gemini. Fumigate it with mastic and white sandalwood every Thursday. Whoever wears this ring will have God make easy for him the affairs of this world, the Hereafter, and religion. He will be granted success in righteous works and acts of obedience, the means of provision will be made easy for him, and God Most High will bless what lies before him. (Whoever writes it) and places it in his shop or his box will have abundant wealth and provision, and he should use the special formula associated with it for seeking provision.
