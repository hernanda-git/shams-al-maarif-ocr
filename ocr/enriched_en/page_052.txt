[Continuation concerning] a firm heart and resolve, a highly astute mind, a steady intellect, and a strong heart; you must be practiced in seclusion and spiritual disciplines. If you are not such a person, beware of summoning them, lest you harm yourself. Beware of witnessing them, for that exposes the heart’s veil. If you restrict yourself to the octagonal seal mentioned earlier, it is sufficient, God Most High willing.

Whoever writes the seal on gazelle parchment and hangs it on a person suffering bodily ailments, such as fevers, chills, and other conditions, will see them depart. This is because the secrets of numbers have an intellectual power: numbers point to the spiritual world, while letters point to the corporeal world. Within this, the spirituality of letters appears through the subtleties of corporeal things, and numbers through the subtleties of spiritual things.

Whoever understands the secret of the mīm will have revealed to him the secret of the ringing of the bell, which is the sent-down revelation. The Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, was asked: “How does revelation come to you, O Messenger of God?” He said: “Sometimes it comes like the ringing of a bell, and sometimes the angel appears to me, speaks to me, and I retain what he says.” The bell is the jingle-bell. Do you not see that when bells are gathered around the necks of horses and camels and they move as they walk, you hear the bell’s resonance from a distance? This is the description of revelation in the ringing of the bell. The Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, said that this was the hardest form of it for him.

[UNCLEAR: the source’s connective wording is damaged.] The comparison of the letter mīm to the bell is because of its roundness, its closure, and the severity of its matter. Do you not hear what he, peace be upon him, said in describing Isrāfīl, peace be upon him: the greatness of his creation, his strength, and his obedience; how he carried on his shoulder one of the legs of the Throne despite its greatness; the Tablet between his eyes despite its greatness and the size of its body; and the Trumpet, whose breadth is the distance of a five-hundred-year journey, which he has taken into his mouth? He has placed one foot forward and the other backward, and his feet pierce the seventh, lowest earth down to its limits.

The mīm was in the final rank because the Trumpet is for terror, swooning, and resurrection. Isrāfīl fixes his gaze upon the Throne, waiting to be commanded to blow the Trumpet. The blowing cannot emerge without the lips joining, and the mīm is produced by the lips joining; he cannot produce it without the lips joining. Thus the comparison was made to the ringing of the bell and the [UNCLEAR: source’s phrase “Mūsawī ringing”]. Ringing, by its nature, is a spiritual movement. The movement of the ringing and the mīm has two directions: an upper direction, which is the mīm, and a lower direction, which is the second mīm in the detailed text—thus: mīm.

Since the mīm has a secret in the higher spiritual realms and in the lower corporeal realms, numbers likewise have a relation to the higher realms, while their letters hold secrets in the lower realms. [UNCLEAR: the source’s next technical sentence calls this a letter outside the totality and describes moisture between two heats.] The heats are the first and second mīms, thus: mīm. From these two heats came its utterance and its agitation. Were it not for the moist yāʾ separating the two heats, it would make the two rugged mountains speak—[UNCLEAR: the technical image is obscure]. Know this and verify it.

Through the letter mīm is every lofty concealed Name; it is Bismillah, and God is the giver of success. The letter mīm is what is indicated in the name of the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him.

Whoever writes the letter mīm, its form and its wafq, recites over it what is to be recited, and [UNCLEAR: observes or places it according to the source’s damaged wording], then enters with it before kings, rulers, judges, and governors, will be accepted by them and his word will carry authority. Awe will be granted to him, so that even if he faced a lion, it would be humbled before him, fear him, and turn away fleeing. If he enters battle with it, he will overcome his enemy and his enemy will fall before him; no enemy approaches him with it without his gaining victory over that enemy. He will be beloved among people, honored and esteemed; everyone who sees him will love him and naturally incline toward him. It has many great and beneficial properties. Understand this. God speaks the truth and guides to the way. This is its form.
