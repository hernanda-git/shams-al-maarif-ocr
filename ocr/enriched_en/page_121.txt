Know that, as this text claims, this sufficient figure and complete drawing signify emirs, kings, and chiefs, and its bearer is granted what lies in its power [UNCLEAR: the referent of 'its' in 'its power' is uncertain] of dignity, awe, happiness, highness, elevation, and leadership. Through it blessings descend, infirmities are lifted, and needs are fulfilled. Within it are secrets for the people of beginnings and lights for the people of endings. It signifies religion, truthfulness, manners, success, strength, safekeeping, victory, prevailing, obedience, affection, love, preservation, sufficiency, security through it, safety, al-kalādah [UNCLEAR: the reading and meaning of this printed term are uncertain], and the granting of dominion over cities in the regions and lands; kingship, sultanate, vizierate, provision, abundance, emirship, expansion, joy, understanding, delight, increase in wealth, status, children, and family, a good life, a good condition, and the safeguarding of servants and children from harm and corruption; insight into the subtleties of the sciences and the finer details of understanding, the uttering of extraordinary things and wisdom, and speaking of realities and knowledge; for its nature is to increase wealth, status, family, and children, and to repel diseases, aches, and pains. The text further claims that whoever writes it on a lead plate, with the Moon in the ray [UNCLEAR: whose ray is meant is not specified], after reciting the ʿazīmah (conjuration) 1289 times, God makes the sight of every obstinate tyrant blind to that person, so that none of them sees him.
