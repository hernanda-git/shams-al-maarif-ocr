English:
— 282 —

"…and sovereignty — prostrating to Allah in the station of return, turning by the light of His judgment and renunciation, until no essence or attribute is attributed to other than You — that You are Doer of what You will, and You are over all things Powerful. (peace be upon him)" As for His exalted Name the Exalter (of honor) — in it are two letters of the letters of the Greatest Name, and in it are secrets for him who reflects on them and knows their meanings. And all these things are sought under the wing of the spirituality — so when you employ the spirituality, it unveils to you a hidden secret from the secrets of the Names. And know that the Names are beauty and the letters are inanimate, like a body without a soul — so when the spirituality of the Name is known, nothing of its meanings is hidden at that, by the leave of the Near, the Responding. And the angel created from its number is 'Ramṭā'īl', upon him peace, and he is chief over four commanders, under each commander 117 ranks, each rank 117 thousand angels. And the invoker — the angel descends upon him, and the invoker's awareness of his presence is not hidden — so when he inspires him with his right guidance, he has prospered in this world and the next. So understand (O Exalter). As for the supplication by this noble Name — you say: 'O Exalter, and I ask You by Your impregnable, momentous might, and Your great, powerful generosity, and Your wisdom over Your creatures, the great and the small — that You make me honored among creatures by being independent of them and poor to You. O Allah, make me honored at the door of the Real by steadfastness and witnessing — there is no being with You — and spread my honor in the hearts of the people of faith, that I attain from Your compassion at the appearance of the proof and the argument. Compassion, O He who, O Lord of the worlds. (peace be upon him)' As for His exalted Name the Abaser (of honor) — it descends from the Exalter, and between them is a veil; the first exalts and the second abases — and in it is a great relief for the imprisoned, the wronged, and the weak. So whoever perseveres in it and says over the head of every hundred: 'O Abaser, abase him who wronged me' — Allah, exalted, abases him by the measure of His omnipotence. And the angel created from its number is 'Aḥjāfīl', and he is an angel of great rank and awe, and he is chief over four commanders, under each commander 770 ranks, each rank 770 thousand of the great binding angels — and they are of the worlds of Isrāfīl, upon him peace, and they are appointed over the abasement of the tyrants and Pharaohs, and over whatever is an enabling against them. And the invoker — the angel descends upon him and fulfills his need, and he becomes rich without kin, and expanded without companions. So glory be to the King, the Bestower. As for the supplication by this noble Name — you say: 'O Allah, You are the Abaser of the tyrants of violent assault, the Seizer, the great, the subdual, the Transcendent over all opposers and rivals, the One exalted above consort and children — Your way is the subdual of enemies, and the fall of the tyrants is enabled by whom You will, and You are the best of plotters. I ask You by Your Name at which the forelocks submitted, and by which You sent down from the obstinate, and by which You cast terror into the hearts of enemies, and made wretched the people of wretchedness. I ask You that You extend to me a fineness from the subtle things of this Name — flowing in my whole and partial limbs, until I am able to do what I want against whom I want, so no wrongdoer reaches me with evil, and no tyrant prevails by oppression. And make my wrath for You, and in You coupled with Your bounty for Your Self — and efface the faces of my enemies, and harden their hearts, and strike between me and them a wall having a gate, its inward within it is mercy, and its outward from its side is punishment. Indeed You are severe in punishment. (peace be upon him)' As for His Name the All-Hearing — it is a name near to the invoker; its servant and angel is 'Quṭayṭīl', and he is chief over four commanders, under each commander 180 ranks, each rank 180. And the invoker — the angel descends upon him and fulfills his need — and his supplication says: 'O All-Hearing, You are He who hears the secret and the whispered conference, and You are He who knows the judgment and piety, and You are He who manifests in the hearts of Your friends the secret of manifestation, and You are He who knows what is more subtle and hidden — and You see by Your eye that does not sleep, and there is hidden from You not the creeping of the black ant upon the silent rock in the dark night…'
