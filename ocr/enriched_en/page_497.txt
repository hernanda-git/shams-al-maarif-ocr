Arabic:
— ٤٩١ —

به على شهواتي واقهر به أعداي وتبرز في باطني بروز ذاتك الباطن والظاهر ما يذهب به سيئاتي
وغفلاتي وتقدس بتقديس ذاتك ذاتي يا الله يا ظاهر يا باطن لا إله إلا أنت سبحانك إني كنت
من الظالمين ما من عبد واظب على هذا الذكر الأنور إلا أنار الله قلبه وأعطاه كل ما يتمناه وكشف له عما يريد
( فصل في اسمه تعالى الوالي المتعال )
بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم اعلم أن اسمه الوالي لم يرد في القرآن العظيم ومعناه مالك الأشياء والمستولى
عليها والمتصرف فيها بمشيئته وأمره ينفذ فيها حكمه ويجرى عليها أمره فإنه جل وعلا منفرد
بتدبيرها وهو المنفذ للتدبير بالتحقيق والقائم عليها بالإدامة والإبقاء وتقدم ذلك ( وأما اسمه تعالى
المتعال ) فقد ورد في القرآن العظيم في قوله تعالى الكبير المتعال وهو بمعنى العلو مع نوع من
المبالغة وقد تقدم معناه والله الموفق
( فصل في اسمه تعالى البر )
بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم اعلم أن معنى البر هو الحق والبر المطلق هو الذي منه كل مسرة وإحسان
والعبد إنما يكون براً بقدر ما يتعاطى من البر لاسيما لوالديه ومشايخه وروى أن موسى عليه السلام
لما كلمه ربه رأى رجلاً قائماً عند ساق العرش فتعجب من مكانه فقال يارب بلغ هذا العبد هذا
المحل فقال إنه لا يحسد أحداً من خلقي وكان باراً بوالديه فهذا هو البر عند الله وأما تفصيل بر الله تعالى
وألطافه بعبده المؤمن أن جعله من أهل اليمين وألهمه الإجابة في الدار على الرضا عنه فاشتق له ثم رزقه
الإجابة على ما سأل لنظره تريك في عالم الحسن وتراكمت عليه الشهوات ووطر القربات الطبيعات
عادة الحق تعالى على محل الإيمان منه وبره تعالى يبعث الرسل عليهم الصلاة والسلام والكتب المنزلة
عليهم وقد رزقهم الله تعالى القبول وهو أعظم . ثم ألهمه القيام بما عمل به عليه من شرائعه ثم وعده
القيام بالعمل ثم أخذه عن الشهوات أمانة وبره بروحه فيناله من البر والكرم . في دار الآخرة فهي
ممر برزخية ودار رسمه في حواصل طيور خضر من الجنة ترتع في رياض الجنة إلى يوم البعث ثم بره
بأن أحياه بعد موته البر الأعظم والرحمة الوافية ثم ثبته على الصراط المستقيم لئلا ينزل من هذا المطلع
في النار بعد أن حصل الإيمان بإبداء السلام عن يمينه والقرآن أمامه والسنة حامله ثم بره بأن سقاه
من حوض الحياة شربة لا يظمأ بعدها ثم بره بأن أدخله الجنة ومن عليه بالنظر إلى وجهه الكريم
ثم بره بأن جعله في هذا النعيم من الخالدين إلى أبد الآبدين ودهر الداهرين ثم بره بأن أخدم له
كلامه كما أخدم له في دار الدنيا الأكوان قال تعالى وسخر لكم ما في السموات وما في الأرض جميعاً
فهذه جملة تصرف الله تعالى بلطفه وبره في خلقه وعباده المؤمنين وروى عن الحسن بن علي رضي
الله تعالى عنهما أنه مكث مدة لا يأكل مع أمه فاطمة الزهراء رضي الله تعالى عنها فقالت له لماذا يا ولدي
فقال أخشى أن يقع بصرك على شيء من الإناء وأسبقك عليه ولا أشعر به فأكون عاقاً لك فقالت له
يا ولدي كل معي وأنت في حل من ذلك فامتثل أمرها واعلم أن من بره لك أن جعلك شاهداً على الأمم يوم
القيامة وستر قبائح فعلك عن الملائكة باستغفارك وينبغي للإنسان أن يبر جميع الخلق فيما يرد من
منه خصوصاً الفقراء والمساكين وأن تبر قلبك بالفكر والإخلاص ليكون ذلك سبباً للكشف عن

English:
— 491 —

"…by it over my desires, and subdue by it my enemies, and appear in my inward by the manifestation of Your Essence — the hidden and the manifest — what removes by it my evils and my heedlessness; and sanctify by the sanctification of Your Essence my essence, O Allah, O Ẓāhir, O Bāṭin, there is no god but You, glory be to You, indeed I was of the wrong-doers. There is no servant who perseveres upon this luminous mention but Allah illuminates his heart and gives him all he longs for, and unveils to him what he wills. (Section on His name, God Most High, al-Wālī al-Muta'āl) (In the name of Allah the Merciful, the Compassionate) Know that His name al-Wālī did not occur in the great Qur'ān, and its meaning is the master of things and the one dominating them and administering in them by His will, and His command executes in them and His affair flows over them — for He, exalted and lofty, is alone in managing them, and He is the executor of the management in realization, and the one standing over them by the perpetuation and the preservation. And that preceded. (And as for His name, God Most High, al-Muta'āl) it occurred in the great Qur'ān in His saying exalted 'the Great, God Most High' — and it is by the meaning of the elevation with a kind of exaggeration, and its meaning preceded. And Allah is the One granting success. (Section on His name, God Most High, al-Barr) (In the name of Allah the Merciful, the Compassionate) Know that the meaning of al-Barr is the truth, and the absolute goodness is that from which is every delight and beneficence; and the servant is only made goodly to the measure he engages of the goodness, especially toward his parents and his masters. And it is related that Mūsā (peace be upon him), when his Lord spoke to him, saw a man standing at the foot of the Throne, and wondered at his place, so he said 'O Lord, how did this servant reach this station?' He said: 'He does not envy any of My creation, and he was dutiful to his parents.' So that is al-Barr with Allah. And as for the detail of the goodness of God Most High and His subtleness toward His believing servant — that He made him of the people of the right and inspired him the answer in the abode upon contentment with him, so He derived for him, then provided him the answer for what he asked, for His sight shows you in the world of beauty, and the natural nearings accumulated upon him and the habits of closeness — the custom of the Truth God Most High over the place of his faith from Him, and His goodness God Most High sends the messengers (peace be upon them) and the Books revealed to them, and God Most High provided them the acceptance, and it is the greatest. Then He inspired him to establish what He commanded him of His laws; then He promised him the establishment of the deed; then He took from him the trusts of the desires and His goodness by His spirit, so he attains from the goodness and the generosity in the abode of the Hereafter — for it is a passage of the Barzakh and the abode of His drawing, in the craws of green birds from the Garden grazing in the meadows of the Garden until the Day of the Resurrection; then His goodness by that He gave him life after his death — the greatest goodness and the complete mercy; then He established him upon the straight path lest he descend from this outlook into the Fire after the attainment of faith, by His showing peace from his right and the Qur'ān before him and the Sunna his bearer; then His goodness by that He gave him drink from the basin of life — a draught after which he is not thirsty; then His goodness by that He entered him into the Garden and favored him with the looking upon His noble Face; then His goodness by that He made him in this bliss of the eternals to the eternity of eternities and the everlasting ever; then His goodness by that He made subservient to him His speech as He made subservient to him in the abode of this world the universes — He said exalted 'And He made subservient to you what is in the heavens and what is in the earth, all of it.' So this is the sum of the disposal of God Most High by His subtleness and His goodness in His creation and His believing servants. And it is related from al-Ḥasan ibn 'Alī (may Allah be pleased with them both) that he remained a period not eating with his mother Fāṭimah al-Zahrā' (may Allah be pleased with her), so she said to him 'Why, O my son?' He said: 'I fear that your glance falls upon something from the vessel and precedes me to it and I am not aware, so I would be undutiful to you.' She said to him: 'O my son, eat with me and you are absolved of that.' So he complied with her command.
