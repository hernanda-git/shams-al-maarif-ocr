رزقه وإذا أضيف إليه الوهاب وذو الطول كان من العجائب * واعلم أن
اسمه الكريم والوهاب وذا الطول أسماء جليلة فإن استدام ذكرهم من فقر عليه

وقال شمس العلماء أبو عبد الله شمس الدين يعقوب الكوفي رحمه الله:
ذاكر هذا الاسم يجد الزيادة في جميع أحواله ويوسع الله عليه نعمه ظاهرة
وباطنة وهو من أعظم الأسماء فليواظب عليه إلى أن يغلب عليه حاله وكذلك ذكر الملك كان اسمه
من نقشه وحمله وسع الله تعالى رزقه وخلقه وهو من الأسرار المخزونة ويصلح ذكر الملك كان اسمه
عبد الكريم وله من العدد ٤٨ وهو زوج فرد ناقص أجزاؤه ٨٤ تزيد على أصله وهو اسمه صفوح

( The Forty-Third Chapter: On His name, exalted, { al-Karīm } ) Whoever persists in its remembrance, Allah gives him his provision without toil or hardship, so Allah eases his provision; and when al-Wahhāb and Dhū al-Ṭawl are added to it, it is among the wonders * And know that His name al-Karīm, al-Wahhāb, and Dhū al-Ṭawl are majestic names — so if their remembrance is maintained, to whom provision is due, Allah eases for him from whence he is unaware; and whoever engraves it and carries it does not know how his needs were facilitated without difficulty or hardship. And this is its form:

And Shams al-'Ulamā' Abū 'Abd Allāh Shams al-Dīn Ya'qūb al-Kūfī, may Allah have mercy on him, said: "The rememberer of this name finds increase in all his states, and Allah widens for him His blessings, outward and inward; and it is among the greatest names, so let him be constant in it until his state overcomes him" — and likewise the remembrance of al-Malik: whoever engraved it and carried it, Allah, exalted, widened his provision and character; and it is of the treasured secrets, and it is fitting as a remembrance for one whose name is 'Abd al-Karīm. And it has the number 48, which is a pair of a deficient odd whose parts 84 increase over its original, and it is His name Ṣafūḥ (the Forgiving) for what generosity requires of pardon; and as for the names of its letters, they are 33 pointing to two majestic names: Rabb (Lord), Ma'ānī (Meanings). And know that the names al-Karīm, al-Wahhāb, Dhū al-Ṭawl, and al-Mun'im are great names that have a square of majestic rank carried for attracting provision, and one may combine its letter-square and its like numeric-square in a wafq; and this is their form as you see:

( The Forty-Fourth Chapter: On His name, exalted, { al-Raqīb } ) This is the Greatest Name and the most noble secret — whoever abounds in its remembrance is preserved in all his movements and stillnesses, all his states and dispositions; and it has a square of majestic rank placed in the dignity of the Moon, so its bearer is praised for preservation and protection, inward and outward * And know that His name, exalted, al-Raqīb — if recited every day four thousand times, or four hundred, or forty times, for forty days, with fasting, purity, discipline, and gathering of resolve until a state overcomes him and the angels of the name glorify [with him] — then after that, if he enters a place where there is wrongdoing, it is undone and annulled. And it has the number 612, which is a pair-odd excessive whose parts 528 point to two majestic names: Ḥayy (Living), Matīn (Firm). And this is its form:
