English:
— 11 —

And before the period, some distress of the souls occurs within it, so that a person does not know the cause of his constriction and becomes perplexed within himself. This is because alif is the first rank of the units in numbers and letters, with no ambiguity in it. Therefore disturbance occurs in the lower world; understand this. It can be used to distress and constrict whomever you wish to distress among the worldly people of tyranny and arrogance, since it is suited to causing his distress and constriction. The letter alif contains heat and dryness; it is the aspect of the red one, and the red one is hot and dry, its nature is fire—burning and restraining. If you invoke hot, dry names in accordance with its nature, this is done when the moon is in al-Naṭḥ, rising on the eastern horizon, with the moon in it; [UNCLEAR: source symbol and confirmation of the preceding claim].

Whoever writes the letter alif one hundred and eleven times on red copper, iron, or a shard of pottery [UNCLEAR: source verb], over the name of the person whom he wishes to distress, and buries it in that person's house after fumigating it with something of its kind, should make heat corresponding to the letter present in it and invoke the names one hundred and eleven times, which are the numbers assigned to it. Take the letters of the name of the person whom you wish to distress, expand them, and determine which of the four natures predominates in his name: heat, dryness, cold, or moisture. Take the hot and dry letters from his name and place them before you on a tablet. Add to them the letters of Mars and the Moon, make from them a name among the Names of God, and invoke it with the stated number. Concentrate your intention on his mouth and his subjugation; [UNCLEAR: source wording].

For example, Zayd and ʿAmr: arrange the letters like this—mīm, rāʾ, wāw; mīm, dāl, yāʾ, khāʾ, nūn, ṭāʾ, ḥāʾ, qāf, mīm, rāʾ—separated and expanded. These are fourteen letters according to the source, including fiery, airy, earthy, and watery letters; [UNCLEAR: the source's classification immediately following this list], namely wāw, yāʾ, and nūn. Among the moist letters there is one, namely qāf. The repeated hot letters are four: mīm, mīm, and ṭāʾ [UNCLEAR: the source's count/list]. The dry letters are ḥāʾ, ʿayn, dāl, dāl, and ḥāʾ. The hot letters number three and the moist letters one. Thus the fourteen letters are confined here, and heat and the heat of dryness predominate among them.

From the Names of God there emerges for us this ʿazīmah:

“O Samsāʾīl, by Him who created you, fashioned you, and made you a light in His sphere—[UNCLEAR: source wording]—I have empowered you over 652 and as a helper to me in what I desire of vengeance against so-and-so and so-and-so, and the loss of his senses. Mix the heat of Mars into the heat of his nature, stir up the heat of fire within him by crushing his joints, constrict his belly with it, bind his intellect with it, leave upon him the torment and fire of Mars, and stir up the fires, headache, and every other pain, by the right of Mars and the ill omen and fire within it, and by the right of your lofty, exalted station—dry, hot, and avenging against the tyrannical oppressors and transgressors. Send to him the spirit of this chosen one, the tyrannical, arrogant transgressor, and settle in his body the torment of ailments; subject his inward self to coercion, wrath, and vengeance. I have sworn you by the Powerful, the Encompassing, the Manifest, the Living, the Self-Subsisting, the Light, the Faithful, the Advancer, the Delayer, the Emitter of Lights, and the Giver of Secrets; by the right of fire and sparks and the Red Planet; and by the right of God, the One, the Subduer: respond obediently and swiftly to the Names of the Lord of the worlds.”

(The first mansion,) al-Sharṭayn, has the letter alif and a compliant wafq. Its planet is Mars and its servant is the red one. It is a letter of powerful effect: when you multiply it by itself, it manifests obedience, and it is the end of the units. (Know) that this noble letter has power over the disposition of all the other letters because it is like alif; understand this.

(Among its properties) for love: write “kābāynāh” at a fortunate hour. If you combine the name of the person for whom you intend the operation with the letters of the wafq, the result is more beautiful and stronger in effect. This ʿazīmah says: “I have sworn you, O Samsāʾīl, and your servants and helpers …”
