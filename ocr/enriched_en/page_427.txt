English:
— 421 —

"…God Most High — He is the First and the Last, the Manifest and the Hidden, and He is composed of two letters, and that is for a hidden secret: that God Most High made the hidden the locus of the heats — among them the heat of yearning for Allah, and among them the heat of greed; so Allah showed mercy to the hidden by the equating of these heats. And when the knower says 'Huwa Huwa,' the burning heats gathered and went out by the soul of the soul to the spirit of the air, so it returns to the spirit of the air and the coolness of the air — and it is the Alif in the manifest, its rejection in the hidden is the heat of the God; it is the secret of the extra Alif, except that He gathered between the hidden of the air and the manifest of the Alif in monotheism. Then the Wāw of 'Huwa' comes out from the lips with nasalization, and the soul finds its exit with heat; and the Wāw is last, and it is intermediate in the last of Allah, preceding the manifest of monotheism by His saying exalted 'He is Allah' — and that is His monotheism by His essence; and it precedes the monotheism of the existents by His monotheism in the knowns, by His saying exalted 'And He is with you wherever you are,' and the rulings of His will — it is the precedence of the First in the meaning of the hidden, by His saying exalted 'He is the First and the Last' — and He is the hidden of the manifest and the hidden of the inward; its measure is 'He is the First and the Last, the Manifest and the Hidden' — so He and the air are bearers of the subtle life; and the second soul returned to the breast — so the spirit of life and the subtle breathing of the air. So understand, and Allah is the Helper. (Section on the meaning of 'Huwa') Know that 'Huwa' is the form of the reality of certainty, inward and outward, uttered first; so when the soul enters, your inward utters it, and it is an expansion of the secret of the air — for the soul, the inward is contraction, the outward expansion; so the Hā' goes out with the soul of life, and the Wāw goes out with the burning of the heats, and the Wāw which is the passage of the heats is extinguished from the Hā' before it by the secret of life; and life connects by the secret of supply, and it is a circle until the ruling of the Hā' and the expansion is completed — so it meets with His saying exalted 'And to Him you return,' and you reflect and find the existents are for Allah. (Section) And know that the Name of Glory is the Greatest Name of Allah, and it has a retreat and disposal; and the description of standing by this name — first, the discipline, which is 66 days, and you mention the name after every prayer 66; then you aim for an apparent retreat and strive your soul from its desires and strip from yourself the ugly character and make your heart in the world of the Dominion, and you mention with your heart at the beginning of the retreat and say 'Allah' always with the heart until a state overcomes you and you are not aware of yourself, until your resolve rises and a door is opened for you, and you look from it upon the worlds of the earth and the silent Dominion, and you see the spirits of the prophets and the righteous servants of Allah, and the spiritual come to you in this retreat in sleep — and it is the first retreat, and you attain the rank of the rememberers, and among the specialities of Lordship is the heart by the realities of the Names of Allah. And since 'There is no god but Allah, Muḥammad is the Messenger of Allah' is 13 letters, and it is the fortress of Allah as He informed by His saying exalted 'There is no god but Allah is my fortress, so whoever enters it is safe from My punishment'; and some said 'There is no god but Allah' thus expanded: lā ilāha illā llāh — it is 12 letters, the number of the zodiacal signs, and by its blessing the orbits of the sphere and the planets and the moon turn, and every work done in it is quick of answer; and it is she who administers that, and it is the secret of the word; and this word is uttered by man without the breaths of the world — it is the movement decreed by the Creator for the spheres, and it is the circle of the perfection of existents and plants and minerals and animals, and it is the perfection of the four seasons and the complete months, 12; and since the hours are 12, and the standing of each letter of these by each month; and from the secret of these letters descends mercy and appears blessing and bursts forth wisdom and occurs guidance."
