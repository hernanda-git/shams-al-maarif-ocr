And as for the names of its letters, they point to two majestic names: al-Qābiḍ (the Withholder), al-Bāsit (the Expander), and their number is 1353; and as for its square, it is this.

( The Sixteenth Chapter: On His name, exalted, { al-Qahhār } ) This name — whoever calls by it upon a wrongdoer in seclusion takes him at once; and whoever engraves it in a square in the dignity of Mars and carries it — he does not argue with anyone but overcomes him and subdues him by proof; and it is fitting as a remembrance for one whose name is 'Abd al-Qahhār. And it has the number 311 as wording and 356 as figure; and as for the names of its letters, they are 396 pointing to two majestic names: al-Fāṭir (the Cleaver), al-Muqsiṭ (the Equitable). And this is the description of its square.

( The Seventeenth Chapter: On His name, exalted, { al-Wahhāb } ) This name — whoever persists in its remembrance sees how provisions are divided; and whoever abounds in its remembrance, Allah widens his provision; and whoever engraves it on paper in the dignity of Saturn and carries it subdues his self and restrains it from desires; and it is fitting as a remembrance for one whose name is 'Abd al-Wahhāb. And it has the number 14 as wording and 602 as figure; and as for the names of its letters, 499 point to two majestic names: al-Fāṭir, al-Muqsiṭ. And its rememberer asks for nothing but He gives it to him; and it is fitting as a remembrance for one whose name is Sulaymān. And it is of the odd and even secrets — he made it odd in the numerator and even in its figure; so from the figure 14 and from the wording 19; the first is an indication of al-Jawād (the Generous) for what is in it of secrets and effusion, so it matched the one and the first — a deficient odd-pair whose parts 9 point to a letter belonging to it which the meaning of the gift requires for the gifted; and its parts 142 point to His name, exalted: "O Salām" by invocation. And as for its square, it is this.

( The Eighteenth Chapter: On His name, exalted, { al-Razzāq } ) This name is among the invocations of Mīkā'īl, upon him peace — none remembers it but Allah eases for him his food, drink, and the apportioned provision; and whoever engraves it on a ring and wears it and abounds in its remembrance on the night of the middle of Sha'bān — Allah provides him the provision of his year; and it is fitting as a remembrance for one whose name is 'Abd al-Razzāq and one whose name is Yūsuf. And it has the number 31 and 28 as figure; so it is among the names gathering the secret of odd and even. And as for its verbal number, it is from the product of the first number in the first complete number, then from the product of the sum of one of them in the other; so its double is from A-J-H-M and L-Y-K in it is the standing of the Alif and the hunger of the Jīm and the interior and eye of the Hā' and the eye of the Zāy, and the Yā' is left and the Kāf becomes and the repetition of the Zāy — so in it is every wording and number, as if the seeker of provision must meet hardship in obtaining it; and it is a number whose utmost parts 311 point to His name al-Qahhār — so everyone who seeks provision from another is abased before him and enters under his subdual. ( A wisdom ) "Bind yourself, O Bā, to One and the doors open for you; submit to one Master and the necks submit to you."

And as for its numeric number, it is the pair-of-pair and the patron its quarters; and it is a deficient number whose parts 356 point to two names: al-Mūṣil (the Connector), Nūr (Light) — so it unites with the heart in its parts; and therefore people are ruined by the rush upon provision, and glorious is the end of the name — "from where do you eat?" He said: "By Allah, the treasuries of the heavens and the earth." And as for the names of its letters, they are 512 pointing to al-Muntaqim (the Avenger), al-Qarīb (the Near); and as for its square, this is the description — and whoever places His name, exalted, al-Kāfī in a fortunate rising in a square and abounds in looking at it while remembering the name and engraves it on a fitting body after writing the square on the engraving tool and remains remembering it — he is a help to him in all he wants of sufficing enemies and sufficing every important matter; and the Moon should be increasing in light in an auspicious sign, and if the rising is stronger, it is the truest of deeds.
