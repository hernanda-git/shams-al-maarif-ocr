English:
— 523 —

"…the angels had been given this form, so he said to him: 'O Muḥammad, if you had seen Isrāfīl with his seventy wings, each wing the measure of all the wings together' — and the Prophet (may Allah bless him and grant him peace) saw him in the most complete description on the night of the Ascension, and that he diminishes until he becomes the measure of a sparrow when he mentions the greatness of Allah, and grows until he fills all the existences by the power of Allah. And likewise my master 'Abd al-Qādir — when he mentions the names, his heart flies in their meanings and magnifies [~] for their greatness, and rises at times for their honor and the loftiness of their structures; and he in both aspects is ascending and rising — and by Allah is the success. (Section) On the management of the high [names] in the bodies and the stimulators * . Know — may Allah set right me and you — that the names have a management; and what is chosen for the soul in it, of the minerals and the stones, is gold mixed — a fifth of gold and four-fifths of silver; and of the stones, the crystal and the agate — for there appears to it a tremendous effect, on condition of the preservation and the perseverance of the purity and the magnifying of the sanctities of God Most High. And as for the seven stimulators, they have a glorification suited to them, and it is their mention by which they glorify Allah; and he who disposes by it is averse — let him engrave each planet in its stone and its mine, and Allah subdues the acts of that planet in the essence of the speaker * . And the outcome: if you want to engrave these talismans, take any name you will of the names, or two names, in the meaning you want and the need — so spread it and break it and place it in the numbers that complete the breaking, and that the first of it appears as its last, so the letters mingle and agree, and you find the secret of that never broken. And the description of the breaking and the spreading is as I describe to you — its example in al-Ḥayy al-Qayyūm is thus:

And the description of the breaking: the spreading is in the square, and drop the repeated, there remain six lines, so there gather in it the special properties of the letters and they enter some into some, and the special properties of the numbers in their inner [aspects] which Allah deposited in them — and it is its specific act from the noble mention indicating the life in everything. And that the numerical talismans have special properties and benefits upon which most of the scholars agreed — and it is the mingling of the benefit and the harmonious benefit of the letter and the name. So if you multiply 30 × 35, it was the name al-Ḥayy * in the utterance, though it is six in the writing, for the doubled letter is by two letters, and the doubled yā' in the two names * — if multiplied by 7, the result is 35. And this talisman is of the compounds, and it has a strong effect in what is wanted of the obtaining and the gathering of things.
