التوحيد وجاذبها بجذبات القرب والفتح المبين الذي أحسن كل شيء خلقه وبدأ خلق الإنسان من طين ثم جعل نسله من سلالة من ماء مهين «الرحمن الرحيم» الحكيم العلي العظيم الأزلي القديم السميع العليم الذي كتب آيات English:
— 71 —

Alṭafuka [UNCLEAR: damaged opening word], O Subtle One, be gentle, and Most Merciful of the merciful [UNCLEAR: connection of the opening words]. According to the text, the petitioner asks for whatever he wishes of the affairs of this world and the Hereafter, and they are fulfilled.

“And this is another invocation.” Set apart for yourself a pure, clean place in which to be alone, and perform your five prayers at their proper times. Then recite it after each prayer 18 times for a period of 14 days. When you have finished your prayer, invoke with this invocation:

“In the name of God, the Merciful, the Compassionate. Praise be to God, Lord of the worlds,” who illuminates the inner sight of the knowers with the lights of spiritual knowledge and certainty, draws all the realizers with the attractions of nearness and firm establishment, opens the locks of the hearts of those who profess divine oneness with the keys of divine oneness, and draws those hearts with the attractions of nearness and the manifest opening; who made excellent everything He created, began the creation of the human being from clay, and then made his progeny from an extract of despised fluid.

“The Merciful, the Compassionate,” the Wise, the Most High, the Magnificent, the Eternal, the Pre-eternal, the All-Hearing, the All-Knowing, who wrote the signs of divine oneness with the pens of power in the breasts of the people of instruction, and inscribed the lines of the people of guidance in the paths of the secret of the people of spiritual knowledge, for the people of sainthood [UNCLEAR: relationship between these paths, the secret, and its recipients]. Sufficient for you are the people of the Cave and the Inscription. God addressed Moses, the one with whom He spoke, with words of honor; God honored His noble Prophet by saying: “We have indeed given you seven of the oft-repeated and the magnificent Qur'an.”

“Master of the Day of Recompense,” who breaks the mighty tyrants and the rebellious, annihilates the despots and the aggressors, and subdues the heads of the Pharaohs, the people of heresy, and the unbelievers. That is God, your Lord; blessed, then, is God, Lord of the worlds. O You who adorned created beings with the garments of bringing-into-being, and sent forth the marvels of the unseen dominions, leading the mounts of steadfast generosity [UNCLEAR: the image of the dominions and the mounts]. O You who spread the clouds of the bonds of His pardon over all creation, every one [UNCLEAR: sense of the bonds of pardon]. O You who have no partner in Your dominion and no helper.

“You alone we worship,” acknowledging our inability to fulfill what is due in worship of You, “and You alone we ask for help,” in carrying out what You have commanded concerning the fulfillment of Your rights at every time and moment. O Possessor of magnificent bounty, O You who give life to bones when they have crumbled away.

“Guide us to the straight path”: the path of the people of the upright religion; the path of the people of uprightness and rectification; the path of those upon whom You have looked with the eye of Your care; the path of those who possess resolve and a sound heart; the path of the people of sincerity and submission; the path of those who have held fast to guidance and rejoiced in it. “The path of those upon whom You have bestowed favor,” among the prophets, the utterly truthful, the martyrs, and the righteous. Reinforce us with the angels of victory and firm establishment, and direct us in existing beings, things brought into being, and bringing-into-being.

“Not those upon whom wrath has fallen, nor those who go astray. Amen.” Do not make us go astray, nor make us lead others astray, nor make us be driven away from Your door. Gather us among the company of the God-fearing, by Your mercy, O Most Merciful of the merciful. May God bless our master Muhammad, his family, and his companions, and grant them peace.

“And this is its adjuration; say:”

O spiritual spirits, possessors of luminous essences, radiant with the spiritual body [UNCLEAR: al-matn may instead mean text] and the divine laws, abiding in the subtleties of the manipulation of letters and the fine points of their knowledge, brought into being, charged with subjugating hearts and spiritual spirits, the numbers and the knowledges of their stored-away secrets [UNCLEAR: syntax connecting the spirits, numbers, and knowledges]. Revive/be alive [UNCLEAR: intended reading of the command], O mighty spirits and noble angels, Jibril, Mikail, Israfil, and Rufail. Aṣbūʾt Āl Shadāy [UNCLEAR: opaque invocation]. Understand my intention, fulfill my need, and undertake my service, by the right of God: the Opener, the Provider, the Forbearing, the Bestower, the Most High, the Magnificent, the Deity, the Subtle, the Great. Kāf-hā-yā-ʿayn-ṣād; ʿayn-sīn-qāf. Answer, O Green King [UNCLEAR: king or angel]. May God grant blessing in you and upon you.

And if it is the last night of the 14 days, a large green bird comes to you and stands before you. Begin with the greeting of peace, for he is a great king [UNCLEAR: king or angel]. Direct him—then what you desire [UNCLEAR: connection of this phrase]—after he has taken from you a covenant and conditions, among them that you do not lie, do not eat unlawful food, and do not commit an indecency. If you remain upright, he will remain upright with you and appoint a servant to fulfill your needs. The incense is anything fragrant, such as Qumari aloeswood.
