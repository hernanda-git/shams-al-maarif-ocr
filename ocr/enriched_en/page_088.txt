السرور الحمد لله الذي أذهب عنا الحزن إن ربنا لغفور شكور ۞ واعلم أن جوهر العالم بأسره
سفلية وعلوية وأدناه وأقصاه كل ذرة أودعت فيه إنما هي من الحياة المودعة في أنموذج الماء فيه
سر الجعل وهو الماء وأما أجزاء الماء ففيه سر الحياة والماء بين الدائرتين برزخ فبسر الجعل وجد
الجبروت والملكوت وفي كل ظاهر سر الحياة وفي باطن سر الجعل فالحاء من الحياة بسر الحرارة
والجيم من الجعل بسر الجلالة فباطنه تجاه الحرارة وهو سر الحياة وظاهره بجيم الجلالة فقع
للملكوت وبالحرارة تجلت مرآة نور الحياة لذات العقل فانفلق نور الشعشعانة من صورة الوجود
جاءت الحرارة وجيم الجلالة أذهي سر الربوبية لأنه من شأن الربوبية والأنس ۞ تجلى الحق
الأعلى بالأنس لظهور الجيم ورؤية الوسائط والتوحيد أصل الحكمة باعتبار ذلك قوله تعالى قل
أرأيتم إن جعل الله عليكم الليل سرمداً إلى يوم القيامة فلو استولى جيم الجعل لظلمة التوحيد لعدم
التصرف بالحكمة ولو استولى حاء الحياة لوجود البسط لبطن التوحيد فذلك قوله تعالى قل أرأيتم
إن جعل الله الآية ثم جعلهم الله ميزان عدل وتنزل رحمة وفضل لظهور الحكمة في التعريف
وظهور التوحيد في الإيجاد ولذلك قال ومن رحمته جعل لكم الليل والنهار لتسكنوا فيه ولتبتغوا من
فضله ولعلكم تشكرون فهذه لطيفة سرمدية ۞ واعلم أن السرادقات العليا والأنوار المطهرة
تحجبت على أنوار العقل والسرادقات الرحمانية ظهور في أرواح المخترعات استوية والعقل
السرادقات العلية تحجبها الفكرة اللطيفة فباطن الملكوت الأزهر من نور أجزاء الملكوت الأبهر
فإذا أردت فهم ۞ ذلك فعليك بفهم هذه الإشارات فخذ أربعة من الطير الاسم المكنون والمخزون
والمحجوب والأعظم فصرهن إليك وآنسهن بسر الأنس وأسرج بهن في حضرة القدس فإذا
ملكت مقاليدكهن وشهدت مشاهد سرهن اجعل على كل جبل منهن جزءاً على جبل درجة الطير
الجزء الأعظم وعلى جبل الجبروت جزء الطير المطلوب المحجوب وعلى جبل الملكوت جزء المخزون
وعلى جبل الرفرف الأبهى جزء الطير المكنون ثم ادعهن سر أمتهن يأتينك سعياً وذلك
لمن تحقق باسم العزة والحكمة فإن فهمت سر النفسية الإلهامية والكشفية النورانية فخذ أربعة
من الطير فصرهن إليك الأول طير الحياة والثاني طير العلم والثالث طير القدرة والرابع طير الإرادة
حقق الحياة الحياة الإيمانية بالفناء عن الحس والعلم العلم الموصل إلى الله وجل والقدرة بسر
الاختراع والإرادة بسر التفكر في الإبداع وأجعل على جبل الدر بسر الحياة وعلى جبل
الفكر في الإبداع بسر العلم وعلى جبل التركيب بسر القدرة وعلى جبل الترتيب بسر الإرادة ثم
ادعهن بلسان الحكمة يأتينك سعياً وهذا يظهر إلى من تقرب إلى الله بكليته إلى أن يتصف بقوله
كنت سمعه وبصره ولسانه الحديث ۞ واعلم أن النار اشتكت لربها وقالت يارب قد أكل بعضي
بعضاً فأذن لها بنفسين نفس في الصيف فهذان نفسان مختلفان من نفس واحدة
وإنما سهل تفريق النفس لوجود الكثافة بل يخفى اللطافة الكريمة والقسمة العليا والإيجاب بالشروط

Joy. "Praise be to God, who has removed sorrow from us. Indeed, our Lord is Forgiving, Appreciative." * Know that the substance of the entire world, its lower and upper parts, its nearest and farthest reaches, every atom deposited in it, is only from the life deposited in the exemplar of water. In it is the secret of Making (jaʿl), and it is water. As for the parts of water, in it is the secret of life. Water is a barzakh, an intervening boundary, between the two circles. Through the secret of Making, Jabarūt and Malakūt came into existence. In every outward aspect is the secret of life, and in an inward aspect is the secret of Making. Thus ḥāʾ comes from life (ḥayāh), through the secret of heat (ḥarārah), and jīm from Making (jaʿl), through the secret of Majesty (jalālah). Its inward aspect faces heat, which is the secret of life, and its outward aspect is through the jīm of Majesty: faqaʿ [UNCLEAR: the reading and meaning of this word are uncertain] for Malakūt. Through heat, the mirror of the light of life manifested to the essence of the intellect; then the light of al-shaʿshaʿānah (radiance) split forth from the form of existence. Heat came, and the jīm of Majesty: adhhī [UNCLEAR: this word and its grammatical connection are uncertain], the secret of Lordship, because it belongs to the domain of Lordship and intimacy. * The Highest Truth manifested through intimacy, for the appearance of jīm and the seeing of intermediaries. Oneness is the root of wisdom in this regard. The saying of God Most High is: "Say: Have you considered, if God makes the night perpetual over you until the Day of Resurrection?" If the jīm of Making were to dominate: "the darkness of oneness" [UNCLEAR: the wording of this conditional result is defective], owing to the absence of action through wisdom. If the ḥāʾ of life were to dominate, owing to the presence of expansion, oneness would become hidden. Thus the saying of God Most High is: "Say: Have you considered, if God makes ..."—the verse. Then God made them a balance of justice and a descent of mercy and bounty, so that wisdom would appear in making known and oneness would appear in bringing into existence. Therefore God said: "Out of His mercy He made for you the night and the day, that you may rest therein and that you may seek of His bounty, and that you may give thanks." This, then, is an everlasting subtle mystery. * Know that the upper canopies and the purified lights have become veiled over the lights of the intellect. The merciful canopies are a manifestation in the spirits of invented things: istawiyya [UNCLEAR: the reading and meaning of this word are uncertain]. And the intellect—the lofty canopies are veiled by subtle thought [UNCLEAR: the syntactic connection of 'the intellect' to the canopies is uncertain]. The interior of radiant Malakūt is from the light of the parts of resplendent Malakūt. If you wish to understand * this, you must understand these indications. Take four of the birds: the Name al-Maknūn (the Hidden), al-Makhzūn (the Stored), al-Maḥjūb (the Veiled), and al-Aʿẓam (the Greatest). Tame them to yourself, make them familiar through the secret of intimacy, and saddle them in the Holy Presence. When you have gained possession of the keys [UNCLEAR: the possessive ending is malformed] and witnessed the witnessings of their secret, place a portion of them on each mountain: on the mountain of the degree of the bird, the portion of the Greatest; on the mountain of Jabarūt, the portion of the Requested, Veiled bird; on the mountain of Malakūt, the Stored portion; and on the mountain of the most splendid Rafraf, the portion of the Hidden bird. Then call them—the secret of amthunna [UNCLEAR: the reading and meaning of this word, and its connection to the command, are uncertain]—they will come running to you. This is for one who has realized the Name of Might and Wisdom. If you understand the secret of the inspirational quality of the soul and luminous unveiling, take four of the birds and tame them to yourself: the first is the bird of Life, the second the bird of Knowledge, the third the bird of Power, and the fourth the bird of Will. Realize life—the life of faith—through annihilation of sense; and knowledge—the knowledge that leads to God, "and Majestic" [UNCLEAR: the honorific is broken here]; and Power through the secret of invention; and Will through the secret of thought in origination. Make the placement on the mountain of al-durr (pearls), through the secret of Life; on the mountain of thought in origination, through the secret of Knowledge; on the mountain of composition, through the secret of Power; and on the mountain of arrangement, through the secret of Will. Then call them with the tongue of wisdom: they will come running to you. This appears to one who approaches God with his whole being, until he is characterized by God's saying: "I was his hearing, his sight, and his tongue"—the hadith. * Know that the fire complained to its Lord and said: "My Lord, part of me has consumed part of me." So God permitted it two breaths: a breath in summer. These, then, are two different breaths from one nafs [UNCLEAR: the unvocalized word can mean 'soul' or 'breath' here]. The separation of the breath was made easy only because of the presence of density; rather, the noble subtlety, the lofty partition, and the obligation under the full conditions are hidden. Understand this from the saying of God Most High, "There is none of you except one who approaches it": this is an expression for the worldly day.
