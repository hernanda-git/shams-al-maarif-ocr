Arabic:
— ٥١ —
الله تعالى في نصف الليل وآخره ويذكر العشرين اسما بحضور قلب بحيث لا يشتغل بشيء مما هو
بصدده ويقرؤهم ألفاً وسبعمائة وثلاثة وسبعين مرة أو مائة وثلاثين مرة ويسأل الله تعالى حاجته فإنها
تقضى بإذن الله تعالى خصوصا إن أراد تسهيل علم فإن الله تعالى يفتح عليه من اسمه العليم طرقا فيرى
عجبا ومنها ما يمكن النطق به ومنها ما لا يمكن التعلق به ومن ذلك أن الإنسان إذا كتب هذه الدائرة
وجعلها في متاعه في السفر والحضر فإنه يكون محروسا بإذن الله تعالى ومن كتبها وعلقها على عضده
الأيمن ومشى بها بين أعدائه نجاه الله تعالى منهم وخذلهم ومن دخل بها على من يخاف شره من الجبابرة ذل
له وخضع له وقلب الله تعالى قلبه وجبروته بين يديه وانقطعت نفسه لمراده وأعطاه الله تعالى مطالبه
وكفاه كل شر لما فيه من الأسرار العجيبة ومن كتبها بماء الورد ومسك وزعفران وشعر وكافور
طيب وسقاه لمن في جسمه علة جسمانية أو علة نفسانية يبرأ بإذن الله تعالى وتعطى حاملها قوة في جسمه
ونفسه وروحه وتعطيه الأسماء هيبة وجلالة بحيث يشاهد ذلك شهادة لا ريب فيها ومن ذكر الأسماء
بعد صلاة الصبح كل يوم سبعا وسبعين مرة وكانت من جملة أوراده فإنه يرى من لطف الله تعالى ما لا
يدخل تحت حصر بحيث لا تكاده همة تتعلق بأحد من الخلق ومن ظلمه أحد من الجبارين أو آذاه أحد
وأراد الانتقام منه فليذكر الأسماء في أول ساعة بكمالها ويدع على من ظلمه وآذاه فإن الله تعالى ينتقم
منه قبل الأسبوع وينصره عليه وهي هذه الأسماء يا الله يا سميع يا سريع يا بديع يا عدل يا معز
يا مذل ومن أراد الصلح بين المتغاغضين فليكتبهم ويمحهم ويسقهم لهما فإنهما يتحابان بإذن الله
تعالى أو محبة شخص فإنه يحبك حبا شديدا إذا شربها في يوم الأحد ساعة الشمس أو عطارد
ويبخر ببخور طيب وهو عود ومصطكى وعنبر وجاوي ومسك وند فإنه يكون ذلك إن شاء الله
تعالى ولها خواص كثيرة قد اختصرناها خوف الإطالة * وهذه العشرون اسما المشار إليها تقول يا الله
يا سميع يا عليم يا سريع يا نافع يا عدل يا علي يا عظيم يا متعال يا عزيز يا عفو يا باعث يا فعال لما يريد
يا رفيع يا معبود يا مانع يا بديع يا كافي يا رؤوف وهذه صفة الدائرة وهذا دعاؤها تقول
بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم اللهم إني أسألك اسمك الذي
فتحت به عالم الخلق والأمر بالتجلي للحق المظهر
لسبب التنزيل والمتعالي أمرا ووجودا بطونا معقولا
ذلك حسا لمن أيدت معلوما لمن أشهدت مجهولا لمن
شئت مما تشابه منه كثرة لا تقدح في وحدة
ما أحكمت من حكمة يا عليم يا حليم يا فتاح يا الله يا رب
وأسألك اللهم يا سميع يا عليم يا سريع يا واسع يا عدل
يا علي يا عظيم يا متعال يا عزيز يا عفو يا باعث يا شهيد
يا رفيع يا معبود يا مانع يا معيد يا نافع أسألك بسر

English:
— 51 —

[UNCLEAR: continuation from the preceding page.] Allah (exalted is He) in the middle of the night and its latter part, and he mentions the twenty names with a present heart, so that he is not occupied with anything other than what he is engaged in. He recites them 1,773 times or 130 times and asks Allah (exalted is He) for his need; it is fulfilled by Allah's permission, especially if he intends the facilitation of knowledge. Allah (exalted is He) opens for him, through His name al-ʿAlīm, paths, and he sees wonders, some of which can be uttered and some of which cannot be attached to [UNCLEAR: the source's phrase about what cannot be attached is opaque].

If a person writes this circle and places it among his belongings, in travel and residence, he is guarded by Allah's permission. Whoever writes it, hangs it on his right upper arm, and walks with it among his enemies, Allah (exalted is He) saves him from them and abandons them. Whoever enters with it before someone whose evil he fears, among the tyrants, that person is humbled before him and submits to him; Allah (exalted is He) turns that person's heart and might before him, and that person's self is cut off toward his will [UNCLEAR: the source compresses the relation of the person's heart, might, and self to the bearer's will]. Allah (exalted is He) gives him his requests and suffices him against every evil because of the wondrous secrets in it.

Whoever writes it with rose water, musk, saffron, hair, and fragrant camphor, and gives it to drink to someone who has a bodily ailment or a soul-related ailment, the text says that he is cured by Allah's permission. Its bearer is given strength in body, self, and spirit, and the names give him awe and majesty, so that he witnesses this with no doubt.

Whoever mentions the names after the dawn prayer every day, seventy-seven times, as part of his litanies, sees from Allah's gentleness what cannot be encompassed by enumeration, so that hardly does an aspiration attach itself to anyone among created beings. If one of the tyrants wrongs him or someone harms him and he wants revenge, let him mention the names in full in the first hour and invoke evil against the one who wronged and harmed him; the text claims that Allah (exalted is He) will take revenge on him before a week and grant him victory over him. These are the names:

yā Allāh; yā Samīʿ; yā Sarīʿ; yā Badīʿ; yā ʿAdl; yā Muʿizz; yā Mudhill.

Whoever wants reconciliation between those who hate one another [UNCLEAR: the source's transmitted term is unusual; the context suggests mutually hostile people] should write them, erase them, and give them to both of them to drink; the text claims that they will love one another, by Allah's permission. Or [for] a person's love [UNCLEAR: the source's phrase is elliptical]: he will love you with intense love if he drinks it on Sunday at the hour of the Sun or Mercury and it is fumigated with fragrant incense—oud, mastic, amber, jāwī, musk, and nadd—and that will occur, if Allah wills. It has many properties, which we have abridged for fear of length.

These are the twenty names referred to; say: yā Allāh; yā Samīʿ; yā ʿAlīm; yā Sarīʿ; yā Nāfiʿ; yā ʿAdl; yā ʿAlī; yā ʿAẓīm; yā Mutaʿāl; yā ʿAzīz; yā ʿAfū; yā Bāʿith; yā Faʿʿāl limā yurīd; yā Rafīʿ; yā Maʿbūd; yā Māniʿ; yā Badīʿ; yā Kāfī; yā Raʾūf. [UNCLEAR: the source calls this twenty names, but the transmitted sequence has nineteen vocative units; no missing name is supplied.]

This is the description of the circle, and this is its prayer; say:

Bismillah al-Raḥmān al-Raḥīm. O Allah, I ask You by Your name by which You opened the world of creation and command through manifestation to the manifest Truth [UNCLEAR: the attachment of the manifest Truth and the following phrase is compressed]; for the cause of the sending-down, and the Transcendent in command and existence, inwardly intelligible; that, sensibly, for one whom You supported, known for one whom You made witness, unknown for one whom You willed, among what resembles it, abundance that does not impugn unity; by what wisdom You made firm. O Knowing, O Forbearing, O Opener, O Allah, O Lord. And I ask You, O Allah: O Hearer, O Knowing, O Swift, O Vast, O Just; O High, O Magnificent, O Most Exalted, O Mighty, O Pardoner, O Resurrector, O Witness; O Elevated, O Worshipped, O Preventer, O Restorer, O Benefactor. I ask You by the secret of al-iḍāfah, the bond relating the Presence of Existence. I ask You by what You spread in the malakūt of Your jabarūt and by what You made clear in jabarūt [UNCLEAR: the source page ends here before the object or continuation].
