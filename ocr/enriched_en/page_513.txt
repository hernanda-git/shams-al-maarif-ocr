Arabic:
— ٥٠٧ —

حفيائيل عليه السلام يأتي للذاكر في النوم واليقظة ويكشف له عن أسرار المخلوقات ومن أكثر ذكره
من ملك أو متولٍ عُزل عن منصبه رده الله إليه وله مربع عظيم الشأن ينفع لحفظ الأمتعة إذا كُتب عليها
وهذه صورته
وأما الذكر القائم به فتقول بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم اللهم أنت بديع السموات
والأرض ومبدع جميع المخلوقات علويها وسفليها خالقها الموجود بلا مثال
واخترعتها بلا معين ولا شريك ولا دليل وعماد أسألك اللهم بقوتك على
اختراع أنواعها واصطناعها وتأليف ذواتها وبيان أوصافها وتصور صورها
وما أوجدت في كنفها أن تكشف عن قلبي ظلمات الكثائف وتبدع في فؤادي أنوار المعارف
وتودع في مرآي من أنوارك المقدسة أصناف اللطائف إنك أنت الله بديع الصنع ما من عبد لازم
على هذا الذكر إلا فتح الله تعالى عين قلبه ووفقه إلى معرفة الإبداع
( فصل في اسمه تعالى الباقي )
بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم اعلم أن الباقي هو الذي لا ينقطع وجوده أبداً وهو واجب الوجود لذاته
ولكنه إذا أضيف إلى الذهن أتم للاستقبال فيسمى باقياً وإذا أضيف إلى الماضي يسمى باقياً وقدماً
والباقي هو الذي لا ينتهي تقدير وجوده في الماضي وعبر عنه أوله وأنه أزلي والواجب الوجود
بذاته يتضمن جميع ذاك وإنما هذه بحسب إضافته إلى الماضي والمستقبل لأنها عبارات
عن الزمان ولا يدخل إلا في التغيير والحركة لأن الحركة بذاتها تنقسم إلى ماض ومستقبل والمتغير يدخل
في الزمان أما بواسطة التغير فمن أخل التغير في زمان وليس فيه ماض ومستقبل فلا
يتصل فيه القدم على القائل بالماضي والمستقبل وهما أمور مستوجبة في وقت لابد فيه وحدث
شيئاً بشيء حتى ينقسم إلى ماضٍ وانقضى وإلى مستقبل وهو ما يتوقع تجدده ولا انقضاء ولا
زمان فكيف وهو الحق تعالى قبل الزمان لم يتغير من ذاته شيء قبل خلق الزمان ولم يكن للزمان
عليه جريان ويبقى بعد الزمان على ما هو عليه وهذا أبعد من قال أن البقاء صفة زائدة عن ذات
الباقي أبعد من قال البقاء وصف زائد على ذات القديم وأهلك برهان على فساد ما ألهمه بالتزام
اللحظ في إبقاء البقاء وبقاء الصفات وليس للسلك في هذا الأمر تخلق بل يعلم أنه فانٍ في نفسه وأن
يتلوه في خلوته عند نجوم الأرواح هو اسمه الثابت ولهذا الاسم الشريف خلوة جملة وخادمه
عطيايئل عليه السلام ينزل على الذاكر ويعطيه ما يريد فيصير إذا وضع يده على مريض يبرأ لوقته
وهو من أذكار الأبدال وله مربع جليل القدر فمن كتبه وحمله ووافق اسمه يكون اسماً أعظم في
حقه يفعل به ما شاء الله الموفق وهذه صورته كما ترى
وأما الذكر القائم به فتقول بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم اللهم أنت الباقي فلا انتهاء
لوجودك وأنت الصمد القيوم الأزلي وأنت الحي الباقي في الأزل بعد زوال
الأسباب والعلل اللهم إني أسألك بحياتك التي لا تموت أبداً وببقائك الذي
لا ينقضي ولا يفنى وبملكك المحيط بكل شيء وبقدرتك على حياة كل

English:
— 507 —

"…His servant ḤAFYĀ'ĪL (peace be upon him) comes to the one remembering in sleep and wakefulness and unveils to him the secrets of the creatures; and whoever increases his mention — if a king or a deputy is removed from his post, Allah restores him to it; and it has a square of great standing that benefits for the preservation of the belongings if written upon them. And this is its figure. And as for the standing mention by it, you say (In the name of Allah the Merciful, the Compassionate): 'O Allah, You are the Originator of the heavens and the earth, and the Producer of all the creatures, their upper and their lower, their Creator existing without like, and You originated them without helper, partner, or guide, and [without] pillar. I ask You, O Allah, by Your power over the originating of their kinds and the fashioning of their essences and the exposition of their attributes and the conception of their forms, and what You brought into being in Your charge — that You unveil from my heart the darknesses of the dense [veils], and originate in my heart the lights of the knowledges, and deposit in my mirror of Your holy lights the kinds of the subtleties. Indeed You are Allah, the Wonderful of making. There is no servant who perseveres upon this mention but Allah the Exalted opens the eye of his heart and grants him success to the knowledge of the origination.' (Section on His name the Exalted, al-Bāqī) (In the name of Allah the Merciful, the Compassionate) Know that al-Bāqī is He whose existence never ceases — He is the Necessary-Existent by His Essence. But when attributed to the mind it is more complete for the future, so He is named 'Remaining'; and when attributed to the past He is named 'Remaining' and 'Ancient'. And al-Bāqī is He whose estimation of existence does not end in the past, and He is expressed as His First and that He is Eternal; and the Necessary-Existent by His Essence encompasses all that — and all of that is by His attribution to the past and the future, for they are expressions about time, and nothing enters but in change and movement, for movement by its essence divides into past and future, and the changing enters into time by means of the change — so what is free of change in time, there is not in it past and future, so the ancient does not connect in it by the speaker of the past and the future, and they are matters necessitated in a time in which there must occur something by something until it divides into what passed and elapsed, and into what is expected to be renewed and not elapse — and no time, so how [with] the Truth the Exalted before time, nothing of His Essence changed before the creation of time, and time had no running over Him, and He remains after time as He is — and this is further from [the one] who said that the remaining is an attribute additional to the Essence of the Remaining, [further] from [the one] who said the remaining is an attribute additional to the Essence of the Ancient, and destroys the proof of the corruption of what he was inspired with the obligation of the instant in keeping the remaining and the remaining of the attributes; and there is no wayfaring in this matter of characterization, but he knows that he is perishing in himself, and that he recites it in his retreat at the rising of the spirits — it is His established name. And for this noble name there is a retreat in sum, and its servant 'AṬYĀ'ĪL (peace be upon him) descends upon the one remembering and gives him what he wills, so that when he places his hand on a sick one he is healed on the spot; and it is of the invocations of the substitutes (abdāl), and it has a square of great measure — so whoever writes it and carries it and accords his name, it is a Greatest Name in his regard, and he does by it what he wills. And Allah is the granter of success; and this is its figure as you see. And as for the standing mention by it, you say (In the name of Allah the Merciful, the Compassionate): 'O Allah, You are al-Bāqī — there is no end to Your existence, and You are the Eternal, the Self-Subsistent, the Eternal; and You are the Living, the Remaining in the Eternity after the passing of the causes and the reasons. O Allah, I ask You by Your life that never dies, and Your remaining that never fails and never perishes, and Your dominion encompassing everything, and Your power over the life of every…' [continues on 514]
