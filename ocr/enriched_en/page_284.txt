English:
— 278 —

"…As for His exalted Name the Bestower — it is a tremendous name for him who seeks the world and the honor of the next; and by it Sulaymān, upon him peace, was given. And its servant is 'Salāmān', upon him peace, and he is chief over four commanders, under each commander 45 ranks, each rank 45 thousand angels — and they are of the worlds of Mikā'īl, upon him peace. And the one who invokes this Name — the angel descends upon him and fulfills his need — and divide upon it what you want; you are made happy with eternal happiness. As for the supplication by this noble Name — you say: 'O Bestower, You give the abundant, and grant the majestic, and guide Your servants to the abode of happiness without doubt. I ask You by the secret of secrets deposited in the letters of the oath, and by the gifts of Your gentleness folded in the oath, and by what You spread of the subtleties of Your generosity in the resolves of the roots — that You make me returning to You with good intention, preserving right guidance — O He who is on the watch, calling the servants to the return, O Bestower.' As for His exalted Name the Provider — it is a tremendous name; for Allah, exalted, has never ceased to be Provider, and the angel created from its number is 'Bahā'īl', upon him peace, and his worlds are in the earth, appointed over bringing provisions to all creatures — and they are those who nurture the crop and the plant. And he who knows the name of this angel and entrusts him with his agriculture or his garden — it bears fruit and ripens and sprouts contrary to the custom of the earth. And he is chief over four commanders, under each commander 308 ranks, each rank 308 thousand bearing the green carpet, appointed over rain and vegetation. And the one who invokes this Name — the angel descends upon him, and gives him sufficiency, and he comes to spend from the unseen — and know that provision with Allah is vaster than creation; so understand the secret of the Real in creation, and reflect on that — you find what I say inscribed in existence. And Allah is the Giver, the Withholder. As for the supplication by this noble Name — you say: 'O Allah, You are the Provider of all that You brought into being from Your bounty, and You are the Completer of what I describe of the life of Your witnessing, and You are the One who sends down their provision from the obscurities of Your knowledge by means of Your heaven and earth. I ask You by the treasuries of Your making — that You make my existence the place of good things, and the means of blessings from deeds and attributes; grant me knowledge beneficial to the souls' hearts, and a state gathering the human conditions, and a giving hand for the pleasing gifts. And make me a taker from You in the description of collection and detail, a conveyer to Your servants — only perfection and completion is seen. And reach me by the subtleties of unification, and the special qualities of success and right guidance — indeed You are Doer of what You will.' As for His exalted Name the Opener — it is a noble name, none knows its knowledge but Allah, exalted; and by it the inwards of affairs are opened. And the angel created from its number is 'Laḥyā'īl', upon him peace, and under his hand are four commanders, under each commander 489 ranks, each rank 489 thousand of the noble angels — in their hands are the keys of blessings, and they have no occupation but opening good things upon people. So glory be to the Giver of gifts. And the one who invokes this Name — its number multiplied into itself upon ablution, fasting, and spiritual exercise — the angel descends upon him as the most ancient; so strive in the invocation — for it is the greatest honor. And Allah is the Giver, the Withholder, there is no god other than Him and no object of worship but Him. As for the supplication by this noble Name — you say: 'O Opener, You are He who opens the locks of breasts by the keys of eternal care, and You are the Rich, the Generous, and You are the generous Giver of Your favor to whom You will — in Your hand are the keys of good things and treasures, and You are the Facilitator of difficult matters, and in Your hand are the fine things of pearl from light, and the Arouser of the spirit of the generous to the consciences of the secrets of the people of breasts. And every locked matter is opened by Your care, and the secret of every locked and fixed thing is unveiled by Your command. I ask You, O Opener of all good, and Repeller of all harm — that You make me standing with You, receiving from You, grasping the outpourings of scientific life and perpetual inclination, and beautiful anticipation of the appearance of the faces of Your gentleness, constantly expecting the attainment of the perfection of Your bounty, ever longing for the existence of the traces of Your generosity. And open upon my heart and sight the doors of unveiling and witnessing, and support me in accepting the light of Your face when the treasuries of what is in Your mercy and forgiveness are spread — O Eternal in excellence, O Compassionate, O Bestower, O Lord of the worlds.'
