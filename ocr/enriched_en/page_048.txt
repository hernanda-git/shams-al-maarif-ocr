[UNCLEAR: the source’s wording about the bāʾ conveying good is awkward.] It leads to good, and it is a cold letter; for that reason, faith was always opened with it. The letter bāʾ is among the letters that remain until the Day of Resurrection, and it is an essential letter. This is because al-watr is among the secrets from the aspect of the Essence: it is an indication of the Reality, and it is from Him and to Him. *

Know that the first of the scriptures given to Abraham, peace be upon him, [UNCLEAR: the source’s wording says that this was reported in the first revelation to the Messenger of God] is connected with His saying, Most High: “Recite in the name of your Lord who created; He created man from a clinging substance.” [UNCLEAR: the phrase following this verse concerning “the easiest of the bāʾ” is damaged.] He created from it the angels, and they are “one and eight” [UNCLEAR: the source’s count is preserved as written], glorifying God Most High and sanctifying Him.

Among the properties of Bismillah al-Raḥmān al-Raḥīm is that the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, said: “Whoever says three times in the morning: ‘Bismillah al-Aʿẓam, by whose name nothing in the earth or in the heaven can cause harm, and He is the All-Hearing, the All-Knowing,’ no sudden affliction will strike him.” In one narration: “No paralysis will strike him.” *

It is narrated from Khālid ibn al-Walīd, may God be pleased with him, that he drank deadly poison [UNCLEAR: the source’s circumstance ‘when he was sent’ is abrupt]. He said: “If you are truthful and claim that poison does not harm with these words, then drink it.” He took the poison in the presence of the Companions and others, said “Bismillah al-Raḥmān al-Raḥīm,” drank it, and stood up unharmed. In one narration he said: “Bismillah, by whose name nothing in the earth or in the heaven can cause harm, and He is the All-Hearing, the All-Knowing.” Then he drank, and nothing harmed him; [UNCLEAR: the damaged source appears to say that he merely perspired] by the power of God. Look, my brother, at this noble Name and how it prevents the harm of poison.

By this noble Name the ship of Noah, peace be upon him, sailed; through it Abraham was saved from the Fire, and it was made coolness and peace for him. Through it comes your safety when you enter your house and when you leave it, according to the saying of the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him: “Say, when you enter your house and when you leave: ‘In the name of God we set out; in the name of God we went forth; and upon Him we rely.’” [UNCLEAR: the exact wording of this formula is damaged.]

You should also say it when closing a door, for Satan does not enter a house whose door was closed after it, and it is this: Bismillah al-Raḥmān al-Raḥīm; he does not approach it. Among its properties is that when you enter your bed, you should say: “Bismillah al-Raḥmān al-Raḥīm, and upon the way of the Messenger of God, peace and blessings be upon him”; nothing will befall you.

The Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, said: “There is no ablution for one who does not mention the name of God, and when he says it, [UNCLEAR: the source’s short continuation is abrupt].” Whoever eats with a leper and says, “Trusting in God and entrusting myself to Him,” will not be harmed by any of that. ʿUmar ibn al-Khaṭṭāb, may God be pleased with him, did this with ʿUtbah al-Sūmī, who was leprous. He brought food while ʿUtbah was present, invited him, ate with him, and said: “Bismillah, trusting in God and entrusting myself to Him.”

By this noble Name healing is sought from the evil eye. Strike your hand upon the eye and say: “Bismillah, trusting and relying upon Him. O God, remove its heat and its persistent affliction.” [UNCLEAR: the term rendered “persistent affliction” is obscure.]
