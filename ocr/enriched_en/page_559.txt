-- 553 --

"…of the risings for the times * , and what they have in the desires of works of the specialities * — so I reflected upon seeking the subject carried upon this foundation * , and that it be the third of the two luminaries * , for the rising of the two full moons * — so I hastened to compose those benefits * and to versify these pearls * , and I named it (Fawātiḥ al-Raghā'ib * fī Khaṣūṣiyyāt Awqāt al-Kawākib [The Openings of the Desires in the Specialities of the Times of the Planets]) — may God Most High make it encompassed by acceptance * , and connected to the reaching of the asked and the hoped * ; and Allah suffices me and is enough * , and peace upon His servants whom He chose.

(The Planet: the Moon) Its exaltation is in the third degree of the sign of Taurus — by it the efficacy of the work is strengthened; its depression in the sign of Scorpio; and its weal in the sign of Capricorn. Its rising is in the form of a man of beautiful face riding upon a wild ass [a type of beast]; its ascendant in the forms of the faces of the zodiac: in the first face, white — it accords at its beginning for attracting the chiefs, and at its end for their affection; in the second face, red — it accords at its beginning for binding, and at its end for dissolution; in the third face, dusty — it accords at its beginning for trust, and at its end for repelling the beasts. The first hour of Monday and from the night of Friday is fit for works of love; and nothing is worked in it but that. The eighth hour of it — what is worked in it for reconciliation between the discordant. The fifth hour of it from Tuesday and from the night of Saturday — it is blameworthy, nothing is worked in it. The twelfth hour of it is blameworthy and accords for hatred and separation and evils and stoning. The second hour of Wednesday and from the night of Sunday is blameworthy — nothing is ever worked in it; the ninth hour of it [is] for that. The sixth hour of Thursday and from the night of Monday is fit for everything desired, and suits travel by land and sea. The third and fourth hours of Friday and from the night of Tuesday — they are two malefic [hours], worked in for separation, hatred, and removal. The seventh hour of Saturday and from the night of Wednesday accords for nosebleed and for bleeding for illness. The fourth hour of Sunday and from the night of Thursday is not fit for work; and selling and buying are disliked in it. The eleventh hour of it accords for works of talismans. Its ink is what is white like ceruse; its incense is white aloes, camphor, frankincense, white costus, and likewise white sandalwood, aloe, and dragon's-blood, and saffron — in partnership with the Sun in that. Its alighting in the lunar mansions [manāzil] is connected to them by the requiring of their fortunes and inauspiciousnesses and its connection to them — questions in which are intents in works and means: (Manzil al-Sharṭayn) fiery, inauspicious — it accords for the hernia and what relates to it, and is fit for travel and the use of medicines, and the detailing of needs. (Manzil al-Buṭayn) hot, moist — its fortune weak — it accords for extracting hidden treasures and for works of agitation, and prolongs in it the horizon and prison. (Manzil al-Thurayyā) fortunate — accords for works of love and for the work of alchemy — I mean the alchemy of medicine — and for the corruption of cattle and for sea travel. (Manzil al-Dabarān) earthy, dry — and if the moon is full in it, goodnesses multiply in the markets — it accords for absolute corruption except what attaches to the charm. (Manzil al-Haq'ah) dry, mixed — accords for absolute reform except what relates to partnership, and is specific to the commencement of sciences, and marriage and travel are praised in it. (Manzil al-Han'ah) fiery, fortunate, mixed with inauspiciousness — accords also for reform when the medicine is drunk, and is fit for travel and marriage and the cutting of cloth. (Manzil al-Dhirā') profitable, fortunate, mixed with inauspiciousness — accords for absolute corruption except what relates to the slave; accords for trade and the fulfillment of needs and the binding of wild beasts and the corruption of crafts.
