English:
— 2 —
"[Publisher's colophon continued:] and the completion [of printing] came to pass in the beginning of the month of Rajab, the singular forbidden [month], of the year 1345 of the Hijra — may the blessings and peace be upon him and his family and his companions — as long as the nights and the days endure. Amen.
