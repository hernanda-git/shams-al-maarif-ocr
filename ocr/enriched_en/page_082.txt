(Benefit) For any illness. The Prophet, peace be upon him, said: Whoever has an illness, let him recite over pure water al-Fātiḥah and Āyat al-Kursī 7 times, and the two Sūrahs of Refuge 71 times, and drink it on an empty stomach for 3 days; God will restore him to health from every affliction. Those who know God said: The Mother of the Book contains a thousand outward properties and a thousand inward properties. If it is written in a vessel, washed off, and given as a drink for any illness, God removes it. The Prophet, peace be upon him, said: If you lay your side upon the bed and recite al-Fātiḥah and "Say: He is God, One," you have become safe from every matter. The Prophet, peace be upon him, said: Whoever recites the end of al-Ḥashr, places his hand beneath his head, and says, "O God, I ask You for healing from every disease," is healed.
