The subtlety [UNCLEAR: opening fragment]: understanding the secret of the subtle soul and its proportion in relation to dense things. Understand this fundamental secret through knowledge of the self and the inner nature of the causes of the spiritual austerities that lead to it in its entirety. After that, there will open to you, from the divine effusion and opening, that which takes you out of the circle of Turkish limitation (al-turkī) [UNCLEAR: the intended sense of “Turkish”] into the circle of formal unboundedness. This covering will be unveiled, space will widen for you, and you will ascend to the Lote Tree of the Utmost Boundary and be bewildered in the spacious heart of the Garden of Abode. What I have brought forth of these divine secrets in Sufi expression is in pursuit of God's good pleasure, and I offer them as a gift before my private conversation. Then you witness their suns, of life, and the symbol concerning the eye of his insight [UNCLEAR: syntax and possessive referents]; his harm and the sinking in the world of his thought disappear [UNCLEAR: whose harm and thought are meant]. I hope that he will be my companion in the Highest Assembly and sit with me in the most splendid garden [UNCLEAR: the hoped-for companion is not identified], because of the saying of God Most High: “And cooperate in virtue and God-fear”; and as the Prophet, peace be upon him, said: “A person's faith is not complete until he loves for his brother what he loves for himself.”

(A subtlety) Know that the abode of the Barzakh needs the realities of the things of the Malakūt. It is only reflected in the things of the Barzakh, so it is with the spirit, and the phantoms appear [UNCLEAR: what is reflected and how the clauses connect], as mentioned in the saying of the Prophet, peace be upon him: “Whoever institutes a good practice has its reward and the reward of whoever acts upon it until the Day of Resurrection.” I have a covenant and a pledge binding those who are characterized by purity and clothed in the mantle of faithfulness: whenever they peruse the curtain of this upright canon and contemplate the secret of this straight path, they are to turn their eyes away from the grossness of the wording and look with the eye of perfection at what is hidden.
