English:
— 299 —

And their secrets are a reward unwithheld — the hidden made manifest of the kinds of beings, the Kāf and the Nūn. And as for His exalted Name the Inward — its angel is 'Baṭāyīl', and he is chief over four commanders, under each commander 62 ranks, each rank 62; and the spirit descends upon the invoker. And his supplication: 'O Inward, You are He who concealed the degree of prophethood, and manifested from between it the secret of unveilings and the realities of the descents in the hearts of the people of retreats, by the hidden things of consciences and the secrets of insights. I ask You — that You grant me full insight and general unveiling upon the inwardness of His guarded affair, and undertake me by Your complete power; that I may bring forth from the defect of defects a guarded secret, and make me honored with You and with him who turns to You, reaching their hearts and secrets — a reward unwithheld.' ( Chapter Twenty-Nine — on the ninth pattern and what is in it of the hidden dispositions ) Know — may Allah grant you success — that among the Names of Allah, exalted, are: the Bestower of Blessing, the Favorer, the Good-Doer, the Generous, the Exalter, the Expander, the Forgiver, the Answerer, the All-Hearing. These names — from the support of Their secret: the immersion of the invoker in the blessings of his Master, and his joy in them by His abundant bounty, and the continuance of His good treatment in this world and the next, and the generosity of soul, and good character, and the elevation of resolve, and the expansion of his provision and knowledge, and the concealment of his faults, and the answering of his supplication, and the hastening of the fulfillment of his needs, and the increase of his intellect, and the strength of his faith, and the soundness of his understanding, and the preservation of blessings, and the return of what strayed of them, and the inspiration of gratitude for them. As for His exalted Names the Bestower of Blessing, the Favorer — two tremendous (names); none asks Allah, exalted, by their invoker in anything of blessing and bounty but He gives him beyond what he asked. As for His exalted Names the Good-Doer, the Generous — their invoker, Allah extends him from His bounty and good treatment of what has no end, from the abundance of good things and the connectedness of secrets. And whoever arranges them broken on pure paper and carries it with him — his character becomes beautiful, his nature is softened, and his soul is generous; and their invocation benefits him who has in his soul stinginess and miserliness — for his soul is purified, and Allah grants him noble character. As for His exalted Names the Exalter, the Expander — two tremendous (names), and they are among the invocations of the angels of the Throne; their invoker, Allah extends him by His extension, and increases him in expansion in knowledge, body, and wealth, and raises his rank and mention. And whoever arranges them in a square upon a gold ring and seals with it — he remains joyful and glad. As for His exalted Name the Answerer, the All-Hearing — two tremendous names; their invoker does not supplicate Allah in anything but He answers him at the time. And whoever writes in his left hand 'the Answerer' and in his right 'the All-Hearing', and raises them to the sky, and supplicates Allah for what he wills — he is answered; and this secret is the secret of secrets. As for His exalted Name the Oft-Returning (Acceptor of Repentance) — it is a tremendous name; its servant is 'Mīkā'īl', and he is chief over four commanders, under each commander 409 ranks, each rank 409; and the spirit descends upon the invoker. And his supplication: 'O Oft-Returning, You are the Oft-Returning to him who repents, and the Bringer-Near to him who turns (to You). And You are He who scattered the light of Your nobility upon the hearts of the seekers, and You are He who gave life to the spirits of the people of spirit and return until they returned to You by their secret thoughts, and repented to You by their hearts, and inclined to You by their outward (selves). From You is fear and assistance, and to You inclined the near and the far. I ask You, O Allah, by the light of repentance, and the radiance of return, and the perfection of tenderness and mercy — that You cause the return to You to ascend secretly and openly, and the standing before You in judgment and command, and that You preserve me by Your nobility so that I am not crushed. Indeed the place of division is (both) silent and open; and restore me by a glance from You that I may attain the secret of Your saying: Allah will make after difficulty ease.'
