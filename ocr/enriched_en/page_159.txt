English:
— 153 —

…"There is none on the face of the earth more pious than this." Then I looked at him, and behold, he was standing on ground of white silver, and he said to me: "O you, backbiting is unlawful" — and he vanished from my sight. These are they whose secrets Allah guarded, whose invocations He purified, and whose vision He illuminated.

( Section ) Al-tasbīḥ is a verbal-noun formation from sabḥ, and sabḥ is coming and going. He, exalted, said: "Indeed for you by day is a long sabḥ" — i.e., coming and going. And some said: al-sabḥ — his innermost reality swims in the purity of the descriptions of his thought in the field of the wonders of the Dominion of the subtleties of the Jabarūt; and the wayfarer swims by his remembrance in the seas of the heart, the murīd swims by his heart in the seas of thought, the lover swims by his spirit in the seas of longing, the knower (ʿārif) swims by his secret in the seas of the Unseen, and the Truthful One (al-Ṣiddīq) swims by his secret in the seas of the holy lights, impervious, of the meanings of the names of the attributes — together with the fixity of the feet of establishment amid the variation of states. So understand the realization of the utmost witnessing of every wayfarer from the presences of the names — it is only the name that he arranged; and his witnessing of it is not complete unless he encompasses that witnessing and the incapacity and the Presence in that witnessing, such that it is an implicit expression of it and the comprehension of it is incapacity. Hence the widest of creation in witnessing, upon him peace, by his saying: "Glory be to You, I cannot enumerate Your praise — You are as You praised Yourself"; and he used to say: "O Allah, increase me in bewilderment in You"; and he used to say: "Among the Most Beautiful Names of Allah there is no name of the Essence — the name 'Allah' — for the name of the Essence is an expression for what is posited for the reality without any consideration whatsoever, such that the word indicates it."

( Section ) And know that the names of actions (asmā' al-af'āl) are of two kinds: a kind that came in the Law with mention of His act without His name, such as "the wrath of Allah," "the anger of Allah," "the curse of Allah," "the bounty of Allah"; and a kind that came in the Law with its mention, such as "Allah creates what He wills" and "Allah is the Creator of all things" — end [of quote]. And know that the realities of the names of Allah are of two kinds: one has no manifest form indicating it — and to it is the indication by his saying, upon him peace: "O Allah, I ask You by every name that is Yours — by which You named Yourself, or sent down in Your Book, or taught to one of Your creation, or kept exclusively in the hidden Unseen with You." * And the second kind has a manifest form, verbal or numerical, with us — it is the name that indicates to us; and that is of two divisions: one hidden (muḍmar), the other manifest (muẓhir). So understand.

( Section ) And know that the aspects of every person are referred to a universal or particular of the divine names — so understand that and you triumph. For, glorified be He, He has men who are the men of the Unseen and the names — and they are ninety-nine men, and one comprehensive man called al-Ghawth al-Fard al-Jāmi' al-Qutb (the Succour, the Unique, the Comprehensive, the Pole) — and none of the ninety-nine knows him, even though all draw support from him absolutely. And whenever a name among the names of Allah, exalted, matches a name of the Essence in letter-count and number and its breaking (kasr) and its correspondence (wafq) agrees, it becomes a greatest name in his right — by which is done what is done by the Greatest Name. * And I heard some of those who know Allah say: "Indeed for every supplicant who calls Allah there is a name that, in relation to him, is the greatest of names" — as "Most Merciful of the merciful" is for Ayyūb, and "the Bestower" for Sulaymān, and "that You — glory be to You — indeed I was of the wrongdoers" for Yūnus; and this is by the state of the one calling, not by correspondence to what is asked and sought by the supplication. And this saying is close to this meaning, and it is the saying of the majority of the Sufis. * And the Shaykh who knows Allah, exalted, Muḥammad al-Khwārazmī, in the sanctuary of Mecca — may Allah sanctify his secret — in the year 670 [AH] said: "Whoever knows Allah, exalted, by His name al-Witr (the Odd) in his state and discourse has known the Greatest Name specific to him." * And know that Allah, exalted, from His hidden gentleness, manifested names of differing constructions so that each name of them indicates a kind of His acts and His way — so every wayfarer finds an easy path.
