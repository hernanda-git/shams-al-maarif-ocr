English:
— 223 —

…so know its measure and call by it in grave matters and at the fulfilling of needs. And it is recounted of some righteous ones that he was in a ship and the black wind rose against him — from which few escape — so he drew the Throne Verse on parchment and hung it in the path of the wind, and spread his palms to Allah and said: "O Allah, I ask You by Your great name: 'Allah, there is no god but He, the Living, the Self-Subsistent' — etc.; and I ask You, O Allah, by its blessing, to deliver us from what has befallen us, and You are the Knower of the unseen, the Remover of cares; and I ask You, O Allah, by the rank of Your most noble Prophet Muhammad, may Allah bless him and grant him peace" — and not had he finished his supplication but Allah relieved them, and the fair wind came to them, and they traveled in safety and security. And of the properties of this verse: whoever wants Allah to cure him of every sickness in his body of all diseases, writes in a glass bowl with musk and saffron and rose-water the Throne Verse three times, and writes with it: "And if We had sent down this Qur'an upon a mountain" — to the end of the sūra — and His saying, exalted: "And if there were a Qur'an by which the mountains were moved or the earth were cloven or the dead were spoken to — but to Allah belongs the affair altogether" — then when he finishes the writing he recites over it the Throne Verse seven times and fumigates it with incenses and drinks it for three days morning and evening, and Allah cures him of what ails him. And whoever writes it and hangs it on himself, it is more effective. And this verse is written for ophthalmia and eye-pain: it is written and recited over him, the Throne Verse three times, and His saying, exalted: "Allah is the Light of the heavens and the earth"; and there is written in a red ophthalmia eye in the white: "Allah is sufficient for me, the Eternal; O my helper in distresses, Allah is sufficient for me, the Eternal; He begets not nor was begotten, and there is none comparable to Him — I adjure you, O ophthalmia, afflicted, clinging to the veins of the head and the skins: for I adjure you by Yūsuf son of Ya'qūb and by his torn shirt, and by the right of the Torah of Moses, the Gospel of 'Īsā, the Psalms of David, and by the right of the great Qur'an and by Muhammad, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, the lamp of existence and the lamp of the worshipped Lord — depart, O ophthalmia, from the bearer of my writing, by the right of 'there is no god but Allah, Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah,' and by a thousand thousand there is no power nor strength except by Allah, the High, the Great" — and may Allah bless our master Muhammad and his family and companions and grant peace. And of the properties of this noble verse, it is written for the crying of infants and hung on them: al-Fātiḥa is written letter by letter separated and the Throne Verse likewise three times: "And Allah is victorious over His affair"; "But if they turn away, say: Allah is sufficient for me; there is no god but He; upon Him I rely, and He is the Lord of the Mighty Throne"; "Ṣ Ṣ Ṣ Ṣ Ṣ Ṣ Ṣ Ṣ Ṣ Ṣ — be silent, O newborn, and be quiet by the sanctity of the worshipped Lord; 'and the voices are humbled to the Merciful, so you hear naught but a whisper'; 'and the faces submit to the Living, the Self-Subsistent, and disappointed is he who carried wrong'; 'faces on that day radiant, smiling, rejoicing'; 'then of this discourse do you wonder, and you laugh and do not weep, while you are heedless — so prostrate to Allah and worship' — be silent, O newborn, by the right of the worshipped King and the loving Lord; Kāf-Hā-Yā-'Ayn-Ṣād, Ḥā-Mīm-'Ayn-Sīn-Qāf; 'and Allah is from behind them encompassing; nay, it is a glorious Qur'an in a preserved tablet, preserved, preserved' — strengthen whoever wears my writing by Allah, exalted, from the evil of what He created, and I have fortified him by 'there is no god but Allah, Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah,' and by the right of 'In the name of Allah, the Merciful, the Compassionate': 'Say: I seek refuge in the Lord of the dawn from the evil of what He created, and from the evil of darkness when it settles, and from the evil of the blowers in knots, and from the evil of an envier when he envies'; and I fortified him by Allah, the Living, the Self-Subsistent, who does not die; and remove, O Allah, harm and evil from the bearer of my writing; I fortified him by Allah, and there is no god but Allah, Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah, and by the right of 'In the name of Allah, the Merciful, the Compassionate' by a thousand thousand there is no power nor strength except by Allah, the High, the Great."
