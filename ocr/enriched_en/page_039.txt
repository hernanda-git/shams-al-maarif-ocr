(Chapter on the natures of the planets)

Know that the Moon is cold and feminine. [UNCLEAR: the source’s wording associates it with phlegm and an accidental heat because its light comes from the Sun.] Its influence is laughter and adornment, and it has yellow bile. Mercury is masculine and feminine; it is sometimes fortunate and sometimes unfortunate, and its nature combines heat in balance. Its influence is speech and writing.

Venus is feminine and fortunate, cold and moist. It has phlegm, and its influence is marriage and joy. It is associated with desire, arranging the crown, affectation, bringing hearts together, women, beauty, amusement, and laughter.

The Sun is masculine, hot, and dry. It has yellow bile. It is fortunate by aspect and unfortunate by opposition. Its substance is gold, and its influence is over the sciences. It has the highest dignity, joy, delight, and kingship.

Mars is feminine, hot, and dry. It has yellow bile; its substance is iron and its taste is bitter. Its influence is over the head, the stomach, killing, women, and other things.

Jupiter is masculine, moderate, spiritual, and fortunate. It has blood; its substance is tin; its taste and color are white. Its influence is over the wind residing in the heart, and it brings abundant gifts and leadership.

Saturn is masculine, cold, dry, and dark. It has black bile; its substance is lead; its taste is bitter and its color black. Its influence is over study and deliberation, and it is associated with heat, injustice, subjugation, and coercion.

I say: Some people claimed that these zodiac signs, spheres, and moving planets exert influence in the world and govern the worlds. They used as evidence His saying, exalted is He: “By those who direct an affair,” and similar passages. [UNCLEAR: the author’s transition here is damaged.] It has been transmitted that when the Prophet, may God bless him and grant him peace, ascended to heaven, he informed us about the zodiac signs, the stars, and other matters. Whatever has come from him in this subject is accepted, while whatever is prohibited is not to be followed. Rather, proofs and evidence establish that the Creator, exalted is He, invented and brought them into being.

As for His saying, exalted is He, “By those who direct an affair,” Ibn ʿAbbās said that some angels are entrusted with provisions, some with rain, and some with the winds, as is mentioned in the creation of the angels. If they are directors, then they direct only by the permission of the Creator, majestic and exalted is He, because He is the Powerful, the Knowing, and the Wise. Glory be to Him: His is the power and His is the wisdom. To Him belong creation and command. Blessed is God, Lord of the worlds. God knows best what is correct.

(Chapter five: the secrets of the basmalah and its hidden properties and blessings)

Know—may God grant me and you success in obeying Him and understanding the secrets of His names—that whoever knows what God, exalted is He, has deposited in the basmalah (“In the name of God, the Most Compassionate, the Most Merciful”) of secrets will not be burned by fire, nor will fire consume him. Whoever writes it and [UNCLEAR: performs the source’s unclear accompanying action] will not be burned by fire.

All scholars have agreed that it is recommended to begin every important matter with the basmalah, following the Noble Book and because of what Abū Hurayrah, may God be pleased with him, narrated from the Prophet, may God bless him and grant him peace. He said: “Every important matter that is not begun with the basmalah is severed.” In one narration: “It is cut off”; and in another: “It is amputated.” Its meaning is that it has little blessing and is not blessed.

It is narrated that the books sent down from heaven to earth number one hundred and four: the scrolls of Seth, sixty; the scrolls of Abraham, thirty; the scrolls of Moses before the Torah, ten; the Torah; the Gospel; the Psalms; and the Furqān. The meanings of all the books are gathered in the Furqān; the meanings of the Furqān are in al-Fātiḥah; the meanings of al-Fātiḥah are gathered in the basmalah; and the meanings of the basmalah are gathered in its letter bāʾ. [UNCLEAR: the source then gives the cryptic meaning that everything brought into being will be what it will be.]

It is narrated that when the basmalah was revealed, the Throne trembled at its descent, and the keepers said: “No one who recites it will enter the Fire.” It consists of nineteen letters, corresponding to the number of angels entrusted with the Fire. May God protect us from it.
