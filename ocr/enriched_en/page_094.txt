Some of them performed it against a tyrannical king, and he perished.

And if you want to prevent ships from voyaging, so that if they voyage they sink, write the seal in a wooden reed, with ink of sārib of the bathhouse [UNCLEAR: the substance meant by sārib] and seawater from where the vessel is. Take some of the water into your mouth and spray it onto the vessel: it will stop and not proceed. When al-Maʾmūn wanted recreation on the Tigris, he would write the seal and hang it in a high place: “encircles white silk” [UNCLEAR: the construction and subject of “encircles” are damaged]. Then waves would surge from every side until they were on the brink of drowning. They would realize that this was the caliph's doing and appeal to him for help; he would then bind it off from them [UNCLEAR: what he binds, and the sense of binding it off from them, are not specified clearly].

And if you want to expel the intruding affliction from the body, stamp it on the afflicted person's forehead and recite the adjuration over him: he will regain consciousness.

*

And if you want the prisoner's release, draw the seal on a little earth from the graveyards. Then the prisoner is to pass it in through his collar and out through his sleeve, and do the reverse of that after reciting the adjuration: the prisoner will be freed.

*

And if you want to bring a person, draw the seal on his trace if possible, and his name on clean paper. Fumigate it with the nails of the jinn after writing the sought person's name with it, and hang it in the wind: he will come quickly.

*

And know that the workings of this Name are valid if you persist in ascetic discipline, fasting, and renouncing the world for them, and do that: you will attain what you want.

*

Imam ʿAlī, may God honor his face, said that the Qur'an has an outward aspect and an inward aspect. Therefore this Name has an outward aspect and an inward aspect: its outward aspect is what is seen, and its inward aspect is the adjuration. Whoever places this seal on a pot while it is boiling, its boiling will cease.

*

And if you want to bring an absent person, write it on a sheet of paper, and around it “By the sky and the night-comer” in separate letters. Hang it on the side of the sun at a favorable hour, with the moon in an air sign, and recite the adjuration 21 times: the person will come.

And if you want to harm someone, and for sorrows, worries, and thoughts to come to him, take a bottle in the name of whomever you wish and his mother's name. Draw the seal on it after depicting the sought person on it, and put in the bottle a little water, sulfur, pepper, and oil. Place it on a fire between two stones: worries, thoughts, and ailments will come to the person subjected to the working.

And if you want love and affection, draw the seal in a glass bowl with musk, saffron, and rosewater, along with the name of the sought person and his mother. Wash it off with water and give him some of it to drink: he will be unable to bear parting from you. If it is not possible to give him a drink, sprinkle some of it on his clothes: the desired result will be obtained.
