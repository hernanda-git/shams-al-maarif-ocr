معلوم بل ذلك يتلاشى في الحضرة العظيمة ويضمحل في حساب اللاهوت منور النور وكاشف الصور
يشاهد أهل التعظيم حقائق النور وهذا العرش سر باني وبرق روحاني يكسى ظاهره حلة الربوبية
وباطنه أنوار الرحموتية فيوسين ومرسيرين وكون كونين وتنزيل أمرين وتقليب أصبعين باطنه
عدم منه الكيف وظاهره يقطع عنه الابن شموس الطلاب إليها انتهاء أولها وعلوم الدقائق عند
ورود الموجودات منه أمهاؤها وشهود الأحوال اللاهوتية وعنه صدور البوارق الكثيفة ومنه
انبعاثات الشعاعات البرزخية حاوي القطبين والميزان أعني حقيقة ميكائيل المكملة الأولية وفيه الثمانية
المختصة بالجنان لثمانية ودائرة النعيم وجنة الخلد وجنة البقاء وجنة الكرامة وجنة التجلي وجنة
النظر وجنة السماع وجنة الحمام ومن عصم من الزيغ فاتبع الأمهات الراسخات ولم يقع في المشابهات
الفانيات المتعلقات بأقصى الدرجات السفليات ومنعه بإشراقه النفسية والآنية العطرية وقصر
الرمز عن رق كنزها صرفاً غير ممتزجة بالأكوان فقد تعلق بالعرش العظيم وسار على الصراط
المستقيم وحينئذ يأتي الله بقطب سليم من العالمين وانفطر في سلوك الطالبين وانتقل من فضل الطالبين
إلى حقيقة المطلوبين قال تعالى أو من كان ميتاً فأحييناه وجعلنا له نوراً يمشي به في الناس وقال
تعالى ذلك فضل الله يؤتيه من يشاء الله ذو الفضل العظيم (والعرش السادس) عرش التدبير وهو
عبارة عن تحقيق العرش الرباني وهو ذو المعارف اللطيفة والتلويحات الشريفة بطور الأطوار ومقام
الأبرار وفيه إجابة الدعوات وتجليات الخلوات وتشكيل النورانيات هو حقيقة إخلاص الأنفس
فيه سر ستره العلي وحكمه اللدني منه أسرار التدبير وفيه يتصل التقدير روح النفوس المعنوية
والأرواح المتصافية المائية من الصحف المنزلة الأول حقائق التجليات العلويات صحف ٦ معلومة
٣ من نسبة العقول و ٣ من نسبة الأرواح وأما الثلاثة الروحانية التي نزلت على الأرواح
فهي العلميات والعمليات والرسوم العليات المنقوشة المكنونة التي أشرقت منها شموس الأرواح
فصحيفة فيها قوة الفيض وحمل القبول فيه يقع تصرف المعاني المعلومات بالتلويحات
الخارجة من كشف العبارات ويعبر من هذه الحقيقة على حقائق النزول الأدنى الأول محيط
النتائج ومظهر العجائب وترقى للذاكرين على كثائف الركون إلى عالم المركبات والثاني فيه
تصرف قوة الحروف الروحانيات وتشكيل التجليات وتقييد العلميات وصحف العمليات وزيادة
قوة قلبه تصدر عنها القوة الفكرية تأثيراً حسناً في ذوات الأجرام وهو المعبر عنه بلسان التصوف
الهمة المؤثرة فافهم ذلك والثالث هو تيار الاستنباطات العلوية في الترتيبات السفلية وموافقة المثل
بالمثل وبه حكمة أو تدبر أو موضع وهو استواء الكمال في التشرف حتى يكون له حركة الباطن
وهي حركة الظاهر وهذا هو الاعتدال وهو أول طور من أطوار المقربين بحضرة رب العالمين أنوار
البرزخين وأقطاب الدائرتين ومصابيح الظاعنين فهذه حقيقة ما تقيدت به أنوار القلوب والأرواح

— 316 —

It is known — rather that vanishes in the Great Presence and dissolves in the reckoning of divinity; the Light of lights, the unveiler of forms — the people of magnification witness the realities of light. And this Throne is a building secret and a spiritual lightning — its outward is clothed in the robe of lordship, and its inward in the lights of mercy; "Wāw-Sīn" and "Mīm-Sīn", and "Kun Kun" (Be! Be!), and the descent of two commands, and the turning of two fingers — its inward has no "how" from Him, and its outward cuts off from it the son of the suns of seeking. To it ends the first of them, and the sciences of subtleties at the coming of the existences from their mothers, and the witnessing of the divine states; and from it issue the dense lightnings, and from it the emanations of the isthmus rays — containing the two poles and the balance; I mean the reality of Mīkā'īl, the primal perfecting (angel). And in it are the eight specific to the Gardens — eight: the circle of bliss, the Garden of Eternity, the Garden of Abiding, the Garden of Honor, the Garden of Manifestation, the Garden of Vision, the Garden of Hearing, the Garden of Doves. And whoever is preserved from error, and follows the fixed mothers, and does not fall into the perishing resemblances attached to the lowest degrees — and is prevented by his spiritual and fragrant-essence shining, and restricts the symbol to the pure parchment-treasure, unmixed with the worlds — he has attached to the Great Throne and walked the straight path. Then Allah brings a sound pole (qutb) from the two worlds, and splits (the veil) in the conduct of the seekers, and transfers from the grace of the seekers to the reality of the sought. He, exalted, said: { Or one who was dead and We gave him life and made for him a light to walk with among men }; and He said: { That is the grace of Allah, He gives it to whom He wills; Allah is Possessor of great grace }. ( And the sixth Throne ) the Throne of Administration (al-Tadbīr) — it is the realization of the lordly Throne, the one of subtle knowledges and noble allusions, by the stage of stages and the station of the righteous; in it the answering of supplications, the manifestations of seclusions, and the forming of the luminous ones — and it is the reality of the sincerity of souls. In it is the secret of His higher veiling and His direct ruling; from it the secrets of administration, and in it the estimation connects — the spirit of the spiritual souls, and the purged watery spirits, from the first revealed sheets: the realities of the upper manifestations — six known sheets: three from the relation of the intellects, and three from the relation of the spirits. And as for the three spiritual (sheets) that descended upon the spirits — they are the sciences, the works, and the high engraved stored forms from which shone the suns of the spirits. A sheet containing the power of outpouring and the bearing of acceptance — in it occurs the disposal of the known meanings by the allusions going out from the unveiling of expressions. And one passes from this reality to the realities of the lower descent: the first encompassing the results and manifesting the wonders — ascending for the rememberers over the denseness of reliance upon the world of compounds. And the second — in it the disposal of the power of the letters, the spiritual ones, and the forming of manifestations, and the binding of the sciences, and the sheets of works, and the increase of the heart's power — from it goes out the intellectual power with a good effect upon the essences of bodies; and it is what is expressed in the tongue of Sufism as "the effective resolve" (al-himmah al-mu'aththirah) — so understand that. And the third — it is the current of the higher derivations in the lower arrangements, and the agreement of the like with the like; and by it is wisdom, or administration, or a place — it is the equilibrium of perfection in honoring, until he has the inward movement which is the outward movement. And this is the moderation — and it is the first stage of the stages of those brought near, in the presence of the Lord of the worlds: the lights of the two barzakhs, and the poles of the two circles, and the lamps of the departed. So this is the reality of what the lights of hearts and spirits were bound by of the absolute inward causes. He, exalted, informed of what We drew, and announced the reality of what We manifested: { Indeed this is in the former sheets — the sheets of Ibrāhīm and Mūsā }. So whoever turned from the dispersion of the outward to the gathering of the inward, and fulfilled the sensible forms in perfection, and the denseness in summary, and the lordly in states — the Truth, glory be to Him, informed us by His saying: { And Ibrāhīm who fulfilled }; so he is in reality a fire, rather its light settled in the subtle barzakh — the Lord of light connected to the upper movement; its melodies are for annihilation, for deaths — by His moving the instrument of subsistence by the difference of kinds; and it is of the greatest witnessed signs and brilliant miracles. He, exalted, said: { And of His signs is the difference of your tongues and colors }. So from the reality of this world — the Throne, the lordly — spoke to it the secret of the minerals, plants, and animals, from the root of its placement upon the expressed reality and the unity lodged in it. So these are the ends of perfection in heart-manifestation. And as for the relation of the spiritual heart-sheets to the reality of their unveiling — that his humanity connects to secrets and brilliant judgments; and the expressions spoke, and the model of the indication was fine. So if there is added to them a subtlety of radiance — a light bolt from it to the pure Truth which is not conceived in meaning nor its end attained. So these are the equipment of the Throne. ( And the seventh Throne ) the Throne of Descent — the Prophet (peace be upon him) indicated this in his saying: "Our Lord descends to the lowest heaven at the last of every night by its measure" — and it is the House of Glory which revolves around the Much-Frequented House (al-bayt al-ma'mūr). For to it is the relation of the Thrones; so these Thrones are the reality of veiling, and to them ends the world of veiling and the hidden reality. So whoever understood the secrets of the veils understood the secret of the answer in supplication. And the seven veils: the veil of dominion, the veil of enfolding, the veil of the circles — which is the spiritual movement, the veil of the first unseen — which is longing, the veil of the middle jabarūt — which is the barzakh, the veil of the soul — which is the imaginary line and disposal, the veil of the heart — which is the secret of the first and second mixtures, and the veil of the intellect — which is the connection of the intercession in the odd (numbers) and the letters in the numbers. So all these veils are barriers between the Maker and the made, between the Truth and the reality, between the subtleties and the densenesses, between the sciences and the knowledges. So whoever lifts these veils witnessed the reality of that abode and the spiritual subtleties — so he supplicates with what he willed and witnesses the reality of increase. And to this specific Throne ends the human breaths, the lordly powers, the prophetic manifestations, and the apostolic supplications — from it the witnessing of miracles, and the appearance of the graces (karāmāt) extraordinary to habits. And from it one penetrates into the seas of the ends from the shores of the beginnings. "So become, if you are a swimmer; and roam, if you are a wanderer." So these are the pearls of the indications that appeared in the shells of expressions, and the realities of the upper that descended in the quarters of the lower. So buy them with a small price, and expend the reality of your saving as a dowry for its bride — before drinking the cup of regret from the jars of our Lord. { So return us to Him sound, other than what we were doing } — may Allah protect us and you from the betrayal of rejection. Indeed He answers the call of the distressed when he calls Him. And as for the reality of these preceding Thrones — they are the vessel of numerical stability. He, exalted, informed of that: { For every news is a resting-place; and soon you will know }. So whoever unveiled his sight and was blind to insight was addressed by the pure intellect for use — so the meaning of the barzakhian transfer: whoever had that present, and understood in the premises the reverse of the future, and witnessed in it the awe of the hopes and the realities of the deeds — so these are the Thrones, exalted be He, and Allah is the Director.
