God Most High said, "Do you see any fissure?" God Most High said, "They and their spouses are enjoying themselves in shades." God Most High said, "And fruit of whatever they choose." These, then, are more important than those. Thāʾ, zāy, and ḥāʾ are cold letters, and their nature is the nature of water. The moon's nature is that of extended shade and the Garden of Eternity. Both ḥāʾ and thāʾ are cold, through the attribution of the nature of earth and the nature of water. Ḍād is moist, and fāʾ is hot and dry, with the nature of fire. Among the radiant heavenly bodies, the moon and the sun belong to it. They have come together in seven Names: the first is al-Thābit (the Constant), who establishes the servants; al-Jabbār (the Compeller); al-Khabīr (the All-Aware); al-Walī (the Guardian); al-Ẓāhir (the Manifest); al-Fard (the Single One); and al-Shahīd (the Witness). Thāʾ has not appeared in any of the Names of God Most High except His Names al-Wārith (the Inheritor) and al-Bāʿith (the Raiser), at the last rank of the world. As for the meaning, it is "whenever it comes together in the Name of God Most High, al-Bāʿith" [UNCLEAR: the clause is incomplete]; it points to the meaning in the Name of God Most High, al-Wārith. No letters of the alphabet are marked with three dots except thāʾ and shīn, because shīn encompasses what is other than it, and the secret of thāʾ is beneath it. It does not have alif's special property in the world of lower bodies. It is a cold, dry letter, like the earth and the pegs—I mean the mountains. The letter fāʾ is dry; the letter of heat acts within it, and it is the fifth degree of heat. Its form is taken into account in the letter alif, for His Name al-Fāṭir (the Originator), al-Fāliq (the Cleaver). Shīn is cold, and its secret is the secret of shīn and its activity. No other letter of the alphabet has three marks and three forms. Shīn brings together the very rank of units and tens, and shīn was placed in His testimony. Three testimonies branched from it: the testimony of the angels, the testimony of those who possess knowledge, and the testimony of those other than the possessors of knowledge. For that reason, "created the rank of knowledge between the rendering of the highest affirmation of divine unity, from the Truth, the building, and the affirmation of divine unity that appeared during the testimony to God, for me" [UNCLEAR: the clause is corrupt; its subject and the connections between its phrases are uncertain]. All the affirmation of divine unity came together in the Throne—I mean the lights of the affirmation of divine unity.
