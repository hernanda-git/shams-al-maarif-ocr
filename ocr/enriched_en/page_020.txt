English:
- 14 -
From it is a Ruhaniyyah not specified for the doing of good; so act in it with what is suitable from the affairs of the world, the righteous, you will prosper. (The eighteenth mansion) Mansion of al-Qalb, and it has the letter ṣād. If the moon descends in it, from it descends a Ruhaniyyah that aids in the acts of good; so act in it with what is suitable from the righteous deeds. (The nineteenth mansion) Mansion of al-Shawlah, and it has the letter qāf. If the moon descends in it, from it descends a mixed Ruhaniyyah; so let nothing of the affairs of the world move in it. (The twentieth mansion) Mansion of al-Naʿāʾim, and it has the letter rāʾ. If the moon descends in it, from it descends a mixed, pure Ruhaniyyah that purifies the hearts and makes the النفس rejoice, good for everything that is attempted in it from the affairs of the world and the Hereafter. (The twenty-first mansion) Mansion of al-Baldah, and it has the letter shīn. If the moon descends in it, from it descends a mixed Ruhaniyyah; it is not suitable for anything of the affairs of the world, nor benefit for movement in it, nor harm. (The twenty-second mansion) Mansion of Saʿd al-Dhābiḥ, and it has the letter tāʾ. If the moon descends in it, from it descends a mixed Ruhaniyyah; it is not suitable for anything of the affairs of the world, nor benefit for movement in it, nor harm. (The twenty-third mansion) Mansion of Saʿd Bulaʿ, and it has the letter thāʾ. If the moon descends in it, from it descends a Ruhaniyyah of moderate nature; perform in it all works of good deeds. (The twenty-fourth mansion) Mansion of Saʿd al-Suʿūd, and it has the letter ḥāʾ. If the moon descends in it, from it descends a happy Ruhaniyyah for movement, of moderate nature, aiding all the acts of good; so do in it whatever you want from works of good. (The twenty-fifth mansion) Mansion of Saʿd al-Akhbiyah, and it has the letter dhāl. If the moon descends in it, from it descends a happy Ruhaniyyah aiding all the acts of good; so do by it whatever you desired from works of good. (The twenty-sixth mansion) Mansion of al-Farʿ al-Muqaddam, and it has the letter ḍād. If the moon settles in it, from it descends a happy Ruhaniyyah aiding all the acts of good; so do in it whatever you want. (The twenty-seventh mansion) Mansion of al-Farʿ al-Muʾakhkhar, and it has the letter ẓāʾ. If the moon descends in it, from it descends a mixed Ruhaniyyah; in it is followed trial and causes. (The twenty-eighth mansion) Mansion of al-Rashā, and it has the letter ghayn. If the moon descends in it, from it descends a good, praiseworthy, pleasant Ruhaniyyah aiding in the seeking of the sciences, and supplication in it is answered to response, and the righteous deeds in it are complete. So look, my brother, at what God تعالى established by the letters of benefits. And since from them the speech of God تعالى is composed, and by them the names of God تعالى are disposed, and by them one understands His address, the meaning that is in their inward is the Ruhaniyyah descending from the mansions. And just as the عظیم Qurʾān فيه verses of mercy and verses of punishment, the verses of mercy were the angels of good fortune in respect of the one shown mercy by them, and the verses of punishment the angels of punishment are felt by the one punished by them, and verses necessitating promise and threat; those are what are expressed by the mixed Ruhaniyyah. And that is only in respect of the human being, and there is no deficiency in respect of the angels, and they are pure good. And it does not negate that the human being is pure good: he is the name قائم by him, and pure evil, and he is the disbeliever, and mixed good, and he is the sinful believer, those concerning whom God تعالى said: “And others who acknowledged their sins, they mixed a righteous deed and another evil one; perhaps God will turn to them.” So this is the mainstay of the secrets in the letters: the cycles turned around the point upon the manifestation of composition, to the cycles of the zodiac signs, to the world, every mansion and every Ruhaniyyah and every letter gathered in the point in forty days, then likewise to the end of the mansions. So the last of the letters are letters of Ruhaniyyah that gather the fortunes and the misfortunes. Were it not for this lettered differentiation and the celestial cycle, man would not have known the causes of happiness from wretchedness, from the misfortunes, and the causes of mixture from lagging behind; and all that is branched from the children of Adam.
