English:
— 14 —

From it descends a spiritual force not appointed to perform good; perform in it what suits the righteous affairs of this world, and you will prosper.

(The eighteenth mansion,) the mansion of al-Qalb, has the letter ṣād. When the moon descends into it, a spiritual force descends from it that assists good actions; perform in it whatever righteous works are suitable.

(The nineteenth mansion,) the mansion of al-Shawlah, has the letter qāf. When the moon descends into it, a mixed spiritual force descends from it; no worldly activity should be undertaken in it.

(The twentieth mansion,) the mansion of al-Naʿāʾim, has the letter rāʾ. When the moon descends into it, a pure, mixed spiritual force descends from it that purifies hearts and gladdens the soul. It is good for everything attempted in it concerning the affairs of this world and the Hereafter.

(The twenty-first mansion,) the mansion of al-Baldah, has the letter shīn. When the moon descends into it, a mixed spiritual force descends from it. It is suitable for nothing in the affairs of this world; activity in it brings neither benefit nor harm.

(The twenty-second mansion,) the mansion of Saʿd al-Dhābiḥ, has the letter tāʾ. When the moon descends into it, a mixed spiritual force descends from it. It is suitable for nothing in the affairs of this world; activity in it brings neither benefit nor harm.

(The twenty-third mansion,) the mansion of Saʿd Bulaʿ, has the letter thāʾ. When the moon descends into it, a spiritual force of moderate nature descends from it. Perform all works of goodness in it.

(The twenty-fourth mansion,) the mansion of Saʿd al-Suʿūd, has the letter ḥāʾ. When the moon descends into it, a fortunate spiritual force, favorable for activity and of moderate nature, descends from it and assists every good action. Do whatever good work you wish in it.

(The twenty-fifth mansion,) the mansion of Saʿd al-Akhbiyah, has the letter dhāl. When the moon descends into it, a fortunate spiritual force descends from it, assisting every good action. Do whatever good work you wish through it.

(The twenty-sixth mansion,) the mansion of al-Farʿ al-Muqaddam, has the letter ḍād. When the moon settles into it, a fortunate spiritual force descends from it, assisting every good action. Do whatever you wish in it.

(The twenty-seventh mansion,) the mansion of al-Farʿ al-Muʾakhkhar, has the letter ẓāʾ. When the moon descends into it, a mixed spiritual force descends from it; follow in it the endeavor and the causes.

(The twenty-eighth mansion,) the mansion of al-Rashā, has the letter ghayn. When the moon descends into it, a good, praiseworthy, wholesome spiritual force descends from it, assisting the pursuit of knowledge. Supplication in it is answered, and righteous works performed in it are complete.

So look, my brother, at the benefits that God Most High has established through the letters. Since the speech of God Most High is composed from them, His Names are ordered through them, and through them His address is understood, the meaning within them is the spiritual force descending from the mansions.

Just as the Mighty Qurʾān contains verses of mercy and verses of punishment, the verses of mercy are fortunate angels in relation to the one shown mercy through them, and the verses of punishment are angels of punishment felt by the one punished through them. There are also verses requiring promise and threat; these are what are called the mixed spiritual force. This applies only to the human being, not to the angels, for there is no deficiency in the angels and they are pure good. Nor does this contradict the human being's being pure good—[UNCLEAR: the source's syntax here]—while pure evil is the disbeliever and mixed good is the sinful believer, concerning whom God Most High said: “And others have acknowledged their sins; they mixed a righteous deed with another that was evil; perhaps God will turn to them in repentance.”

This is the foundation of the secrets in the letters. The cycles revolve around the point, displaying composition, then through the cycles of the zodiac and into the world. Every mansion, every spiritual force, and every letter gathers at the point for forty days, and then likewise through the remaining mansions. The final letters are spiritual letters that gather good fortune and misfortune. If not for this differentiation of the letters and the celestial cycle, human beings would not know the causes of happiness and misery, the causes of misfortune, or the causes of mixture and falling behind. All of this branches from the children of Adam.
