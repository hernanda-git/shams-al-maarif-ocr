through wisdom, his sight through insight, his witnessing through reality, and his hearing through unveiling.

As for the details, God, out of His hidden gentleness and His subtle wisdom, established what He brought into
the ordering of the world within a single day, arranging it over 12 hours, one hour corresponding to each
month, in which “the river passed” [UNCLEAR: marra al-nahr, “the river passed,” is an uncertain reading in

Since daytime was 12 hours and the ordering was completed through it, if the servants' daytime were to
continue without interruption, the very blessing would turn into torment, for upholding it befits only the
Self-Subsisting. Human knowledge is composed of motion and rest, and its passing away and the unveiling of its
phases are inevitable. So God made night for it: the existence of its secret and its return to the world of
the reality of the secret of transition and resurrection, the ascent of spirits, the rising of intellects, and
the stillness of human beings under the wisdom of darkness. He made night 12 hours.
Since the circle of “Muhammad is the Messenger of God,” peace be upon him, was 12 months and 12 letters, there
is a letter for every hour. For “There is no deity except God” does not complete the affirmation of divine
oneness except together with “Muhammad is the Messenger of God.” Likewise, the circle of daytime is not
complete except through the circle of night. Thus wisdom in night and day has been completed by the mingling
of mercy, according to the saying of God Most High: “Out of His mercy He made for you night and day, so that
