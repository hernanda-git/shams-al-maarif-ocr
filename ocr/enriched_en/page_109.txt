with lamps." He, Most High, said: "The love of desires has been made attractive to people"—the verse. [U+06DE] As for blossoms, they indicate the soundness of fruit. It is said: "The trees were adorned
with fruits." [U+06DE] As for shīn, it indicates al-Shahīd, the Witness, and testimony. He, Most High, said: "God bears witness that there is no god but He."
