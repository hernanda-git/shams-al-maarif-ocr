English:
— 29 —

We have already mentioned the doctrine of the ancients concerning the forms of the celestial spheres and what relates to them. As for the doctrine of the scholars of sacred law, these are heavens according to them. Reports have been narrated in the hadith from Ibn ʿAbbās—some say from al-ʿAbbās ibn ʿAbd al-Muṭṭalib—who said: We were with the Prophet, may God bless him and grant him peace, at al-Baṭḥāʾ when a cloud passed over us. The Prophet, may God bless him and grant him peace, said: “Do you know what this is?” We said: “The cloud.” He said: “And al-muzn?” We said: “Al-muzn.” He said: “And al-ʿanān?” We said: “Al-ʿanān,” and we fell silent. The Prophet, may God bless him and grant him peace, then said: “Do you know how far it is between the heaven and the earth?” We said: “God and His Messenger know best.” He said: “Between them is a journey of five hundred years, and the thickness of each heaven—that is, its thickness—is five hundred [UNCLEAR: the unit is not repeated in the source]. Above the seventh heaven is a sea whose upper and lower bounds are as far apart as the heaven and the earth. Then above them is a world. [UNCLEAR: the next source phrase is garbled and mentions ‘the son of the heaven and the earth.’] God, exalted is He, is above that, and nothing of the deeds of the children of Adam is hidden from Him.” The proof of this is His saying, exalted is He: “God is the One who created seven heavens, and of the earth the like of them.” Thus the distance of all of them is [UNCLEAR: described in relation to] the journey of the children of Adam. As for the angel, he traverses all of it in a single hour; likewise, the devil reaches the earth before the angel reaches the heaven.

It was narrated that Abū Razīn al-ʿUqaylī, may God be pleased with him, was asked: “How far is it between the heaven and the earth?” He said: “An answered supplication.” He was asked: “How far is it between the east and the west?” He said: “A day’s journey.” Al-Thaʿlabī related from Ibn ʿAbbās: He said: “The Sun rises each year through three hundred and sixty apertures, and the highest aperture does not rise on that day again until the following year.”

I say: The Sun has benefits and indications. Among the indications is that it is one, and its light illuminates all horizons and the whole world. Likewise, the Creator, glorified and exalted is He, is one, and He governs the world. Second, the Sun’s benefits are distant while its arrival is near; and God, exalted is He, is distant from creation in essence but near in answering. Third, its light is withheld from no one; therefore God’s provision is withheld from none of creation. Fourth, its eclipse is evidence of the existence of the Resurrection, along with other things that indicate its darkness. Fifth, the cloud covers it; [UNCLEAR: the source then says that al-Ghāshiyah covered knowledge].

As for its benefits, they are very numerous. First, it is the lamp of the world. The Exalted said: “And We made the Sun a lamp.” Second, it ripens their food and brings their fruits to maturity without cost. Third, it travels from east to west for their welfare. Fourth, it does not remain in one place, lest it harm creation. Fifth, in winter it is in the lower part of the constellations and in summer in their upper part, for the benefit of the world. Sixth, it does not combine with the Moon in its dominion, lest each of them nullify the light of the other.

If you say: It is in the fourth sphere, and the heavens do not veil it, while the cloud veils it. I say: The heavens are subtle, transparent substances, whereas the cloud is dense because it rises from the earth. God knows best.

(Chapter on the Moon, its similitudes, and what is in it)

It was narrated from Abū Hurayrah that he said: The Prophet, may God bless him and grant him peace, said: “Do you see the full Moon when there is no cloud before it?” They said: “Yes.” He said: “Then do you dispute concerning the Sun when there is no cloud before it?” They said: “Yes.” He, may God bless him and grant him peace, said: “Then you will see Him in the same way.”

If you say: Why was the example not made with the Sun, since it is brighter and more complete in light, while the Moon’s light comes from it? I say: For two reasons. First, the Sun’s light overpowers the eyes, so one cannot look at it. Second, it was broken for the sake of the Truth, so God, exalted is He, mended it. This happened when Gabriel struck the Moon’s light with his wing. Its heart broke because it had been comparable to the light of the Sun, so God, exalted is He, mended it in two ways. One is that He made eyes look at it in this world every month. The second is that He commanded His Prophet Muḥammad, may God bless him and grant him peace, to use it as an example for the greatest and highest things.

If you say: God, exalted is He, said, “The eyes do not perceive Him, while He perceives the eyes.” I say: We do not claim that the eyes perceive Him in the sense of encompassing Him; rather, what is perceived is a ray.
