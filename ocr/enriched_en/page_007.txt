English:
Part One
of the book
Shams al-Maʿārif
al-Kubrā
and Laṭāʾif al-ʿAwārif
by Shaykh Aḥmad ibn ʿAlī al-Būnī; may Allah sanctify his spirit; he died in the year 622 AH.
It is stated in Kashf al-Ẓunūn: The purpose of this book is to make known through it the nobility of the Names of Allah, exalted is He, and what has been deposited
in their sea of various wisdom-jewels; and how to work with the Names and invocations, and what follows them,
of the letters of the sūrahs and verses, so that through them one may reach the Divine Presence without toil and reach through them
the desires of this world without hardship. End quote.
❖ And following it ❖
The Epistle of the Balance of Justice on the Purposes of the Rules of Sand ❖ the Epistle of the Openings of Desires on the Particular Properties of
the Planets' Times ❖ the Epistle of the Flowers of the Meadows on the Signs of the Zodiac ❖ the Epistle of the Subtleties of Indication
on the Special Properties of the Wandering Planets ❖ authored by the eminent scholar, Sayyid ʿAbd al-Qādir al-Ḥusaynī al-Adhamī
❖ May Allah benefit the Muslims through his knowledge. Amen.
❖ Notice ❖
Since the book Shams al-Maʿārif, owing to the people's great need for it, has been printed many times (in Egypt and India), and the Egyptian printing presses took care to correct it,
yet it was not free from errors and distortions because correct copies were unavailable.
This time it has been printed after collation with copies from “Egypt and India” and another copy in the handwriting of Hajj Mirza Husayn,
with careful correction by a committee of eminent Egyptian scholars; it was corrected again by the great scholar Shaykh
ʿAbd al-Raḥmān al-Jazīrī. Thus, praise be to Allah, this edition has become one of the finest copies in both printing and correction.
