…"You are the best Preserver; You are the most Merciful of the merciful"; { "and they did not estimate Allah with His true estimate when they associated" }. And may Allah bless our master Muḥammad and his family and companions, and grant peace abundantly, and upon all the prophets and messengers and the near angels. And he said { "Board it" } the verse, then Ibn 'Abbās turned to his companions and said: "If the speaker of it is known or perishes, his debt is upon me" — and Allah knows best.

[ Chapter Eighteen: On the Properties of the Throne Verse and the Blessings it Has [

Know — may Allah make me and you succeed in His obedience — that the names are indicative meanings, and among the greatest names in the Book of Allah is the exalted Throne Verse, for His saying, exalted: { "His Throne encompassed the heavens and the earth" }, and in His saying, exalted: { "To Him belongs what is in the heavens and what is in the earth" } is dominion. * And know that when this verse descended upon the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, with it descended seventy thousand angels in veneration of it and exaltation of its rank — so know the measure of what reached you, for it is the saving verse, the preventing, the benefiting, the guarding; and it is the mistress of the Qur'an, related in sound hadiths: whoever recites it at the beginning of the day, there comes to him the master, the obeyed king, to understand the Throne Verse; and it is counted as one hundred seventy letters, fifty words, and seven sections. And whoever recites it the number of its letters, 170 times, and wants something from the sultan, his intercession is accepted. And whoever recites it in distress in the depths of night by lamplight facing the qibla finds in his heart a state he had not known and humility — so let him supplicate Allah and he is answered from the good of this world and the next. And whoever recites it mid-night 225 times is safe from his enemy and Allah destroys him. And whoever recites it 313 times, Allah suffices him in what concerns him of his religion and his world, and opens for him the door of good things. And of its properties: if you write it letter by letter separated in a glass bowl with saffron and rose-water and musk, and drink it for the sake of its words, and you are fasting, breaking fast only on it, Allah makes him eloquent in kinds of wisdom, and the beginning of the work is in the month of Nisan; and if he adds of its water it is better. And when he wants to break fast upon the verse as we mentioned, he recites the verse seven times, then says: "O Allah, I ask You by the right of this noble verse that You grant me the immediate knowledge of Allah." And when you want a knowledge from the sciences, remember by the right of your work — I indicated this to some brothers and they used it, and before completing the count Allah opened upon him various sciences and he attained what he sought. * And of its properties: when you wear a new garment and say at wearing it: "O Allah, just as You clothed me in a new garment, give me life happy and make for me a long life" — the serving angels of this verse ask forgiveness for the wearer of the garment until he wears it out. And if he adds Sūrat al-Qadr, it is better. * And its properties: when you visit a sick person, ask him about the state of his illness; if it is from a headache in his head, write it for him letter by letter separated and hang it on him from the side of the pain; and if the sick says his pain is from his interior or from all his body, draw its famous congruence in a glass bowl, and the writing is with musk and saffron and rose-water, then write the noble verse letter by letter separated, and write with it the verses of healing, which are His saying, exalted: "and He heals the breasts of a believing people" * "and a healing for what is in the breasts and a guidance and mercy for the believers" * "and in it is a healing for mankind" * "and We sent down of the Qur'an that which is a healing and a mercy for the believers." * And when he fell ill
