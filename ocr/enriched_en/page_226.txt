English:

…the Eternal, the Ancient — and I have girded the sword of the Commander of the Faithful, upon him peace, and donned the cloak of 'Ā'isha, Mother of the Believers, and entered into the treasuries of "In the name of Allah, the Merciful, the Compassionate" — their locks "Praise be to Allah, Lord of the worlds." And whoever recites the Throne Verse once and "Say: He is Allah, One" three times, and blows to his right and left — and whoever wants brevity, let him recite what we mentioned then blow into his palms and wipe his face and all his body with them, and he is safe from what he fears until he returns home. And whoever recites it in the morning is safe until evening, and whoever recites it in the evening is safe until morning. And if it is recited over the head of one terror-struck eleven times, he recovers at once; and if the apparition stays in the body, it is burned. And if it is recited after the prayers, it effaces the sins the one praying has. And of its properties: when it is recited upon entering upon a tyrant or a unjust ruler, and he says in completing it: "O Allah, O Living, O Self-Subsistent, Originator of the heavens and the earth, O Possessor of Majesty and Generosity — I ask You by the right of this noble verse and what is in it of the great secrets that You bridle his mouth from me and silence his tongue so he speaks naught but good or is silent" — your good is before you and your evil under your feet — and he enters upon him, and Allah bridles his mouth from him, and no harm reaches him from him. And of its properties: when you fear someone and harm has come to you from him, pray the sunset two units, and recite the Throne Verse while prostrating three times; then when you reach His saying, exalted: "and their guardianship does not burden Him, and He is the High, the Great," repeat it three times seven, and say during your recitation: "O Allah, separate between me and So-and-so son of So-and-so as You separated between the heaven and the earth, and bridle his mouth from me as You bridled the beasts from Daniel, upon him peace, by the right of these noble names" — and you are spared his evil and his mouth is bridled from you so he speaks of you naught but good. And of its properties: when you are in a group and want to be spared their evil and harm, recite the verse three times and blow into your palms three, and wipe your hand over your face saying: "O Allah, suffice me against the evil of these people, O Sufficer; and grant me well-being from their harm, O Granter of well-being" — and Allah makes you secure from them and no harm reaches you from them. And of the properties of this verse: if you recite it by night you are safe until morning, and if you recite it by morning you are safe until evening. And it is recounted that a man dwelled in an abandoned house; when night came upon him, lo, an opening and a rumbling, and the man looked and behold, a rebel (mārid) making for him; and when he saw it he feared, and was inspired to recite the Throne Verse, and as I said each word it said it with me until His saying, exalted: "and their guardianship does not burden Him, and He is the High, the Great" — so I stopped it, and repeated it over it many times, and it vanished from me and I saw it not the rest of the night; and when morning came I looked and behold, in the corner of the house, ashes — and I marveled at that; then I told my story to a brother of mine who was righteous, and he said to me: "This was an 'ifrīt that wanted to harm you, and this verse rent it because you were inspired to recite it" — so when I heard that I took it as a daily portion and remembrance night and day, and I saw of its blessing something great. And of its properties: it is written for the companions (qarīn) and the followers (tawābi'), for whoever wears it is safe; and if added to it is His saying, exalted: "And Allah is from behind them encompassing; nay, it is a glorious Qur'an in a preserved tablet; so Allah is the best Preserver, and He is the most Merciful of the merciful" — "for him are successive guardians before and behind him who preserve him by the command of Allah" — for that is the estimation of God Most High, the Knowing — and with it are written Sincerity (al-Ikhlāṣ) and the two protectors (al-Mu'awwiḏatān), and it is a great veil for the companions and followers. And of the properties of this verse: if placed among goods, they are preserved and their owner is safe from thieves and others. And whoever draws this verse in an eightfold numerical congruence, placed in the hour of the sun, moistened, among commercial loads — it sells and turns over.
