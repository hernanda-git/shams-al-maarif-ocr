all the ranks, for it is the name of the Essence described by the holy attributes; all the names return to it, and whoever becomes aware of its meaning becomes aware of the meanings of the inward names, which are the individual letters. So understand these indications and do not stop at the expressions, that you may be among the certain. And the first of the inward and outward names, all of them, is a benefit, because God Most High made the ranks of the secret to be in Adam, peace and blessings be upon him, and did not establish it in the angels, so the letters flowed on the tongue of Adam, peace and blessings be upon him, in the varieties of movements and the kinds of languages. And God Most High made the forms of all the letters in the heart, and they are spiritual, and they are what appear in the speech of a human being and in the numerical writing: letters in the chest, and letters in the tongue, and letters in the two hands. And that is the meaning of His, God Most High, saying: "Sād. And by the Qur'ān of admonition. Qāf. And by the glorious Qur'ān. Nūn. And by the Pen and what they inscribe." And the letters point to the signs of the Book, a reminder for those of understanding. And every letter has three stations according to the three movements: the ḍamma, and the fatha, and the kasra; and the letters of prolongation and of softness among them are after the likeness of the elements, and each one of the three is bodily, and spiritual, and psychic, so they are nine. And the numbers are nine, and the spheres are nine, and the natures and the senses are nine, so the correspondence appeared. So search for the secrets of the number and the letters; you will find abundant knowledge in the gatherings and in the separations, in accordance with the Rahmani and the Rahimi of "In the name of God, the Merciful, the Compassionate." From the "In the name of God" the cosmos is nourished, and it eats and drinks. So reflect on the secret of the Qur'ān; you will find it in the multiplication of six by seven, and the sūrahs of the Qur'ān are likewise, and the six appears in the form of the perfect number according to the number of the days in which God created the heavens and the earth and what is between them. So these three are detailed into nineteen: the seven heavens with the Throne and the Kursi, and the earth is ten, and the letters are ten, which are the openings of the sūrahs, arranged in five ranks, apart from the dual, and the triple, and the quadruple; and as for their total, it is twenty-eight.

(A chapter on the letters, and they are of two kinds)
the two-dotted and the three-dotted. The three-dotted are shīn and thā', so shīn indicates the gathering that is apart, and thā' indicates the gathering that is joined. The two-dotted are tā', yā', and qāf, so tā' -- its appearance is in His kingdom, and yā' -- its appearance is in His power, and qāf -- its appearance is in His limit. And everything is from it a manifestation of the al-Qāʾim and the al-Qādir, and everything is made manifest, encompassing like the light of the sun and the essences. And yā' distinguishes between the two ascriptions, and every born thing that stands, or every gathering by which a people are borne, like al-shayʾ, for everything is that by which a people is. And shīn's meaning is "bring us with the secret of the letters," and its three faces are as in the kāhu of shīn, and of sha'n, and of thaghar, and of ghashm, and the like. And nūn's meaning is a manifest appearance, like the nūn of al-Ḥasan and of nūr, light, and of waraʿ, piety, and the axis of falsehood by which its inner secrets are made manifest. And as for al-Mazn, by which a hidden secret appears, so that the position of nūn may be preserved in a word that comprises it -- the Essence, its exterior and its interior, and what lies between them. And therefore a garden was confined in its form, like the kāhu in the name, named for its namesake; and journey is built upon the character of men, and the like. And al-Ḥasan, may God Most High be pleased with him, said: in the Qur'ān is the knowledge of everything, and the knowledge of the Qur'ān is in the letters that are in the openings of the sūrahs. And know that the letter is in the lām-alif, and the lām-alif is in the alif, and the knowledge of the alif is in the point, and the knowledge of the point is in the primordial knowledge in pre-eternity and the will, and the knowledge of the will is in the unseenness of the air, and the knowledge of the air is in the clothing of the mass of a thing -- and He is the All-Hearing, the All-Seeing. And it was said concerning the secret of shīn that it is a name among the names of God Most High, like the rest of the letters of the alphabet that are present in the openings of the sūrahs; and they are the fourteen luminous letters, not repeated, and these they are: (alif, ḥā, rā, ṭā, kāf, lām, mīm, nūn, sīn, 'ayn, fā, ṣād, hā, yā). And it is narrated from Ibn 'Abbās, may God Most High be pleased with them both, that he used to say: "the openings."
