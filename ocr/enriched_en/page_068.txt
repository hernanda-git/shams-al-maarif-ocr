A useful point: when God Most High placed the ranks of the secret in Adam, peace be upon him, and it was not established in the angels, the letters flowed on the tongue of Adam, peace be upon him, with various vocalizations and different languages. God Most High placed the forms of all the letters in the heart; they are spiritual, and they are what appear in human speech and in reckoning script: letters in the breast, letters on the tongue, and letters in the two hands. That is the meaning of His saying, Most High: "Ṣād. By the Qur'ān that bears the Reminder. Qāf. By the glorious Qur'ān. Nūn. By the Pen and what they write." The letters indicate the verses of the Book, as a reminder for people of understanding. Every letter has three stations according to the three vocalizations: ḍamm, fatḥ, and jarr. The letters of prolongation and softness among them resemble the elements. Each of the three is bodily, spiritual, and psychic, so they are nine. The numbers are nine, the celestial spheres are nine, and the natures and senses are nine; thus the correspondence becomes apparent. So investigate the secrets of number and letters, and you will find abundant knowledge in combinations and separations, in accordance with the quality of the Merciful (raḥmāniyyah) and the quality of the Compassionate (raḥīmiyyah) in "In the name of God, the Merciful, the Compassionate." From "In the name of God" the cosmos receives nourishment, "and by eating and by drinking" [UNCLEAR: the connection of this phrase is garbled]. So reflect on the secret of the Qur'ān: you will find it in the multiplication of six by seven; the surahs of the Qur'ān are likewise. And the six, as forms within the perfect number [UNCLEAR: the wording is incomplete], corresponds to the number of days in which God created the heavens, the earth, and what is between them. These three are detailed into nineteen: the seven heavens with the Throne and the Footstool, and the earth is ten. The letters are ten, namely those at the openings of the surahs, arranged in five ranks, apart from the twofold, threefold, and fourfold; their total, however, is twenty-eight.

(Chapter on the letters, which are of two kinds)
Those with two dots and those with three dots. Those with three dots are shīn and thāʾ: shīn indicates a dispersed gathering, and thāʾ indicates a united gathering. Those with two dots are tāʾ, yāʾ, and qāf: tāʾ is His manifestation in His dominion, yāʾ is His manifestation in His power, and qāf is His manifestation in His bounty. Everything from Him is a manifestation of al-Qāʾim (the One Who Stands) and al-Qādir (the Powerful). Everything is manifest and encompassing, like the light of the sun and the essences. Yāʾ distinguishes between the two relations.
