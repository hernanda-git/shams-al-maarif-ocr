And it is said that His name al-Wadūd is the one referred to in the story of the merchant and the thief—that he said in his supplication: "O Loving (al-Wudūd), O Possessor of the Glorious Throne, O Originator, O Restorer, O Doer of what He wills," etc.—so Allah succored him with an angel from the angels upon a horse, in his hand a spear, and he struck the thief and killed him. * As for His name al-Hannān (the Affectionate), his rememberer—Allah casts his love into the hearts of creatures; and if it is written 140 times in a pure vessel and wiped with egg-white, and one anointed with it who fell into the fire, he is healed instantly, and its remembrance removes hot illnesses. * As for His name al-Latīf (the Subtle), He is swift of answer, penetrating of secret in relieving distresses; no one is mentioned in difficulty except it vanishes, and whoever perseveres in His remembrance, Allah makes for him what was written upon him of difficulty a sleep—and this is called the hidden gentleness for its concealment from the perceptions of intellects; and the least of its remembrance is 160 times. Whoever aligns it 4-in-4 on pure paper and carries it, or in an agate ring and seals with it, is spoken-by-it in his states. * As for His (exalted) name al-Hafīz (the Preserver), it is a great name; his rememberer—Allah preserves him from every disliked thing; and if the rememberer imagines during the remembrance a city or a dwelling or other than that, Allah preserves it. Whoever aligns its numbers and letters in a square in a silver ring and seals with it, nothing of jinn, men, vermin, or other harms him. * As for His (exalted) name al-Raqīb (the Watchful), He gladdens in the anxiety of hearts and their submission, and his rememberer is accompanied by shyness before his Lord, and courtesy in secret and open, outward and inward. * As for His (exalted) name al-Barr (the Good), it has a great secret; his rememberer—blessings descend upon him, and good things hasten to him from Allah. * As for His (exalted) name al-Shāfī (the Healer), it is a great name for the speed of healing of ailments; his rememberer—Allah cures him of every evil and affliction, and heals them of every sickness, and ailments do not approach his body. Whoever mentions it at a sick person 422 times after reading al-Fātiḥah 7 times, saying: "O Allah, heal—You are the Healer, there is no healing but Your healing, O Allah, a healing that leaves no sickness nor pain," that sick person is healed; and I commanded Mahmūd ibn Shāh by it when leprosy afflicted him and the physicians recoiled from him, and he remained after it but fifteen days and was healed as if nothing had been with him. Whoever aligns its mentioned numbers in a square in a pure vessel with Zamzam water or rain water, and gives it to a sick person to drink 3 days on an empty stomach, Allah heals him. * As for His (exalted) name al-Muhsī (the Reckoner), it is a great name, in it is a letter of the letters of the Greatest Name, and His angel is Qahtiyā'īl, chief over 4 commanders, under the hand of each commander 148 rows, each row 148 thousand angels; and the rememberer—the angel descends upon him, and his supplication: "You are the One who counted the breaths of the creatures; You are the One who uprooted from Your friends the paths of attachments; You are the One who delivered the people of gnosis to the greatest light which is above the favor of the skilled eyes; and You are the Preserver of all creatures whose deeds and lifeterms and breaths are counted in all times, so that no deviant matter is absent and no striver's striving is lost with You. I ask You, O Allah, O Possessor of great bounty, to grant me reckoning and the preservation of the realities of the names and reaching their secret." * As for His (exalted) name al-Mubdi' (the Originator), it is a great name, in it is a letter of the letters of the Greatest Name, and His angel is Kahā'īl, chief over four commanders, under the hand of each commander 56 rows, each row 56; and the rememberer—the angel descends upon him, and his supplication: "O Originator (al-Mubdi'), You are the One who manifested the secret of oneness in the hearts of the people of unification, and raised the banner of glory in the breasts of the people of detachment, and erected the standard of gnosis in the wilds of the intellects of the people of isolation.
