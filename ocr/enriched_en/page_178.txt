وهذه صفة مربعه
۞ واعلم أن هذا الاسم له خواص جليلة في تفريج الكروب في أوقات الشدائد
ولا يضاف إليه غيره يظهر من آثاره العجب ولا يذكره من تولاه بشيء في نفسه أو بدنه
لا زال في أثنائه الله كرر ولا يذكره أحد في نفسه أمر عظيم إلا ومثل لذلك الأمر
في خلوته وأقبل عليه الذاكر وهو يلاحظ تلك الكيفية أو شاهدها كيف تتجلى وتضمحل فلا يقوم
من مقامه وبقي شيء يرهبه وفي ذلك أسرار بديعة والله الموفق
( الفصل الثاني والثلاثون في اسمه تعالى خبير )
هذا الاسم يصلح ذكراً لمن أراد الاطلاع على أمر خفي في نومه أو يقظته ومن وضعه في مربع في
شرف عطارد ووضعه تحت رأسه اطلع على أمور خفية ومن ذكره ٧ أيام في خلوة ورياضة تأتيه
الروحانية بكل خبر يريده من أخبار السنة أو من أخبار الملوك وعدده ٨٣٨ وهو زوج فرد زائد
أجزاؤه ٢٦٨ تشير إلى اسمين جليلين وهما خالق وواسع فإنه لا يخبر بالأشياء على حقيقتها إلا من
وسعها علماً وأبدأها خلقاً أيعلم من خلق وهو اللطيف الخبير وأما أسماء حروفه فهي ٢٠٩ تشير إلى
اسمين جليلين وهما آخر وواحد وهذه صفة مربعه

( الفصل الثالث والثلاثون في اسمه تعالى حليم )
هذا الاسم من ذكره عند جبار وقت غضبه سكن ومن وضعه في شرف القمر في مربع وأمسكه
عنده حسنت أخلاقه وطابت نفسه ورغبت فيه الناس وأمن من الاضطرار والاضطراب عند
نزول الشدائد وهو من الأسماء الجليلة لا يعرف قدره إلا العارفون وله من العدد ٨٨ وهو زوج الزوج
والفرد زائد أجزاؤه ٩٣ تشير إلى اسمه تعالى أمان وهذا الاسم من أخص أسماء سيدنا محمد عليه
السلام فلذلك كان دعاؤه اللهم اغفر لقومي فإنهم لا يعلمون ولذلك كان اسمه عدده رقاً يشير لهذا
الاسم وأما أسماء حروفه ١٨٣ فتشير إلى اسمه ماجد باعتبار وإلى اسمه متى باعتبار وهذا مربعه

English:
— 172 —

…and this is the description of its square: [§] And know that this name has majestic properties in relieving distress at times of hardship; nothing is added to it but wonder appears from its effects, and none who is devoted to it mentions it for anything in his self or body but Allah is in its midst, repeating; and none mentions it for a great matter in himself but it is represented for that matter in his seclusion, and the rememberer turns to it while observing that mode or witnessing how it manifests and dissolves — so he does not rise from his station, and something remains that terrifies him; and in that are wondrous secrets, and Allah is the granter of success.

( The Thirty-Second Chapter: On His name, exalted, { al-Khabīr } ) This name is fitting as a remembrance for one who wants unveiling of a hidden matter in his sleep or wakefulness; and whoever places it in a square in the dignity of Mercury and puts it under his head is unveiled to hidden matters; and whoever mentions it 7 days in seclusion and discipline — the spiritual entities bring him every report he wants, of the reports of the year or of the reports of kings. And its number is 838, which is a pair-odd excessive, whose parts 268 point to two majestic names: Khāliq (Creator), Wāsi' (Vast); for none reports things in their reality but one who has vast knowledge of them and originated them in creation — "Does He not know, He who created? And He is the Subtle, the Aware." And as for the names of its letters, they are 209 pointing to two majestic names: Ākhir (Last), Wāḥid (One). And this is the description of its square.

( The Thirty-Third Chapter: On His name, exalted, { al-Ḥalīm } ) This name — whoever mentions it before a tyrant at the time of his anger, he is calmed; and whoever places it in the dignity of the Moon in a square and keeps it with him, his character is beautified, his self is pleased, people desire him, and he is secure from constraint and turmoil when hardships descend. And it is of the majestic names whose rank none knows but the knowers; and it has the number 88, which is the pair-of-pair and the odd excessive, whose parts 93 point to His name, exalted, Amān (Security). And this name is among the most special names of our master Muḥammad, may Allah bless him and grant him peace — therefore his supplication was { "O Allah, forgive my people, for they do not know" }; and therefore his name's number as a figure points to this name. And as for the names of its letters, 183 point to His name Mājid (Glorious) in one consideration and to His name Mattā (Giver) in another. And this is its square.

( The Thirty-Fourth Chapter: On His name, exalted, { al-'Aẓīm } ) This is the red sulphur and the greatest magnet — whoever persists in its remembrance, Allah gives him lasting might, and he is magnified in the eyes of people, and his faults are hidden from them; so if its possessor is of true state and complete orientation, he witnesses the command of Allah, exalted, for the worlds, and witnesses the secrets in every seclusion; and whoever persists in its remembrance, Allah gives him lasting might and everyone who sees him loves him and he is magnified in the eyes of people. And it has the number 1020, which is the pair-of-pair excessive for what greatness requires of the nine; its parts 191 increase over the original — and 'A-Gh: the Wāw is for loftiness, i.e. the comprehensive of the preferment of existence; the 'Ayn is an indication of concealment — glory be to Him who is hidden by the intensity of His manifestation; and the Ghayn is the name of the Rich and the Light of lights; and for the Yā' to descend upon the manifestation of the Ghayn and the consideration of the numbers of the rest of this name and the exits of its parts as it goes beyond the limit of restriction — but there has been from the likeness what suffices for one who lends an ear while witnessing. And as for the names of its letters, they are 331 pointing to two majestic names: Ghālib (Prevailing), Mānih (Granting). And as for its square, it is on this description.
