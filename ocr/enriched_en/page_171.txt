English:
— 165 —

…the tyrants and he is effective in word among them; and it has the number 731, which is the number of the pair-of-pair and the odd of the deficient numbers, and its parts 499 point to two majestic names: Ḥakam (Judge), Khāliq (Creator) — (and this is the description of its square).

( The Twelfth Chapter: On His name, exalted, { al-Khāliq } ) This name is fitting for the one in difficulty and the masters of wise crafts; so whoever engraves it on a ring when the rising [sign] is one of the fiery triangles, and seals with it, and the husband of his wife [i.e., he and his wife] — she conceives. And it has the number 731, which is a primal number pointing to the letter Dhā', and therefore creation is bound in abasement to the Creator; and as for the names of its letters, 94 point to two majestic names: Awwal (First), Ākhir (Last). And as for its square, it is on this description:

( The Thirteenth Chapter: On His name, exalted, { al-Bāri' } ) Its property is aid in heavy works, so it is fitting as a remembrance for the blacksmith and the goldsmith and the like; so whoever persists in its remembrance, there is unveiled to him the world of imagination; and if he is a physician, his cure succeeds in bodies, and Allah heals every patient he treats. And it has the number 213, which is a deficient rectangular odd number whose parts 78 point to His name al-Dayyān (the Requiter); and it is from the product of Jīm in Mīm — the Jīm for beginning, the Lām for connection, the Mīm for completion; and it may be placed in a numeric triangle surrounded by a square. And this is its form:

( The Fourteenth Chapter: On His name, exalted, { al-Muṣawwir } ) Whoever abounds in its remembrance — Allah eases for him what he wants of crafts needing mixing and shaping; and whoever engraves it on a glass or clay ring-square, no work of his is spoiled; and if the one of true state and firm footing abounds in its remembrance, the intelligible meanings descend upon him in sensible forms; and none understands what we indicated but the one of unveiling and insight. And whoever abounds in its remembrance, Allah eases for him what he wants to make of manual crafts, like those who depict permitted images and make pottery and glass and the like. And it has the number 343 as wording, which is a deficient pair-of-pair whose parts 366 point to two majestic names: Karīm (Generous), Muṣliḥ (Reformer); and 336 points to His name, exalted, al-Qāhir (the Subduer) — this in the way of the masters of secrets. And as for the names of its letters, they are 399 pointing to two majestic names: Māni' (Withholder), Mukram (Honoured). And this is its square:
