English:
— 127 —

…from you, by abandoning all that cuts you off from His attribute, and make your heart His house, your existence Makkah, your witness the Sanctuary, and make perpetual your circumambulation around the House — a secret circumambulation for His glorification, like the existence of the House, and a secret by which, living, you behold the Ever-Living, the Self-Sustaining; for He is intense in existence, the fashioning of attributes, and the secret of states. And these indications point to the affirmation of the uniqueness (fardāniyyah) of the God of the attainable, from the principles and the ends.

( Section ) And know that among the special properties of the Qur'ān is His saying, exalted: { Allah bears witness that there is no god but He, and the angels } up to His saying { the Wise }. And in this verse are three meanings: the first — what Allah began with Himself, which is the affirmation of the Necessary Existent in the epochs, and He illumined the attributes of the existence of His accompanying (ma'iyyah) from Him — an attribute of His greatness and His majesty and His attributes that prevent the accompaniment of other than Him. The second — the consideration of what Allah willed by His angels, for their attestation of His existence; that is the testimony of His existence and the knowledge of His care, in which ranks are impossible because of the angels' sanctification from the veil of the soul and the darkness of forms. The third — what Allah established for His servants and described them as possessing knowledge, standing with justice and attesting Him. And Ibn 'Abbās, may Allah be pleased with him, said: "The import of the discourse is: Allah bore witness by Himself, even if none other than Him bore witness to Him, that there is no god but He, and the angels bear witness to Him thereby." And His saying { and those of knowledge } means the prophets and the believers bear witness to Him thereby, standing with justice — i.e., equity, for they are the people of equity, and because the meaning of equity is placing a thing in its place, and that is only by Allah, and there is no god but He, the Mighty, the Wise — the Mighty who confers favour upon whoever does not believe in Him; the Wise, by what they bore witness: that there is no god but He, and that they are not worshipped except Him, and that the religion with Allah is Islam.

( Section ) And know that the reality of the testimony of tawḥīd is what the Truth bore witness to for Himself, for He is the witness of His essence, and He called to witness whom He called of His creation before creating him, by inspiration, as an alert to them that He knows what will be of His testifying to Himself — a testimony of truth and knowledge — so that no testimony is accepted except from the truthful monotheists who will come and know Him and unify Him and bear witness to His deity and lordship, by His saying, exalted: { Allah bears witness } the verse. So that is a testimony of compulsion (iḍṭirār) for what they witness of the majesty of His dominion and His manifest traces, for they were created upon that. Then He, exalted, said { and those of knowledge } — i.e., the scholars who are the masters of the hidden realities in the realities of tawḥīd, who point to the establishment and express the meanings of the states; who are singled out from all by the Unique, and unify the One, the Self-Sufficient, and know the meanings of the names of the Truth and the realities of His attributes, and witness the Unseen; and they are Allah's proof upon the lands, and to them is the recourse of the servants — they pitched their camps in the Presence, and their ranks rose in manifold truthfulness before a powerful King. And Ibn 'Abbās, may Allah be pleased with him, said: "Allah bore this testimony a thousand years before He created the creation; in a report, twelve thousand years — each year 360 days, each day a thousand years of what you count." And transmitted from the Brethren of Purity (Ikhwān al-Ṣafā) is the avoidance of plunging into the seas of demonstration, for it occasions division; rather, upon them is plunging into the seas of understanding when they hear the saying of Allah { Allah bears witness that there is no god but He } — for it is a place of prostration, for the richness of existence by the existence that is among the secrets; so He is He, the First in the first and the Last in the last. Then after that, plunging into the seas of the secrets of { god but He }, for they are of tasting. And know that the great Qur'ān is of three divisions: a division indicating knowledge of the essence of Allah, His attributes, His tawḥīd, and His sanctification; a division indicating the legal matters; and a division indicating knowledge of the affairs of the Hereafter. And it is not hidden that the knowledge of the indications of the verses of the essence of Allah, exalted, and His attributes, by the description of existence, oneness, and sanctification, equals a third of the Qur'ān that indicates…
