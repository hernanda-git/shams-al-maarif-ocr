English:
— 31 —

[UNCLEAR: the page begins with “the water,” continuing the preceding instruction.] Any sick person who drinks it will be healed by God Most High. If a woman having difficulty giving birth drinks from it, she will deliver immediately.

If it is written on a sheet of paper thirty-five times and hung in a house, no Satan or jinn will enter it and blessing will increase there. If that sheet is hung in a shop, its customers, profit, and merchandise will increase, and it will blind the eyes of onlookers.

If it is written on a sheet one hundred and seven times on the first day of Muḥarram and a person carries it, no harm will befall him or the members of his household for the duration of his life.

If it is written one hundred and ten times for a woman whose children do not survive, or for a barren woman who has not yet conceived, after she has been purified from menstruation for three days, and she carries the sheet and her husband has intercourse with her, she will conceive by the permission of God Most High. She should not put down the sheet after sixty days; she will bear a righteous child and will experience no pain or hardship in bearing it, by the permission of God Most High.

If it is written sixty-one times and carried by a woman whose children do not survive, her children will live. This has been tested and confirmed, and God is powerful over all things.

If it is written on a sheet one hundred and one times and buried in a crop, that crop will become fertile and be protected from all pests. If it is written seventy times and placed with the dead person in his shroud, he will be safe from the terror of Munkar and Nakīr, and it will be a light for him until the Day of Resurrection.

If it is written on a lead tablet and placed in a fisherman’s net, his catch will increase. If it is written once on a card and placed beneath the bezel of a ring, and the ring is placed in [UNCLEAR: a liquid the source calls “menstrual milk”] and a person bitten by poison drinks it, [UNCLEAR: the source’s following wording is damaged]; the poison will leave him by the permission of God Most High.

If its letters are written separately and carried, it will have great virtue. The bāʾ is God’s splendor; the sīn is His radiance; the mīm is His glory and dominion; the alif is His pre-eternity; the lām is His gentleness; the hāʾ is His guidance; the alif is His command; the lām is [UNCLEAR: associated in the source with “the alām of dominion”]; the rāʾ is His mercy; the ḥāʾ is His wisdom; the mīm is His kingdom; and the nūn is His favor.

[UNCLEAR: the source then gives a damaged instruction to write a form of the basmalah beginning with the letter bāʾ twenty-one times and place it among one’s wealth, increasing its blessing.] For protection, write this number and add to it the following verse, whose letters are given as: “Peace upon Noah among the worlds.” Erase it and give it to the poisoned person to drink; he will be cured immediately by the permission of God Most High.

Whoever writes it on a sheet of paper and looks at the letter mīm forty times every day while saying, “O God, Owner of the dominion,” up to the words “in Your hand is the good,” will not know from where good comes to him, and God will bless what is before him.

Whoever writes al-Raḥmān al-Raḥīm fifty times on a sheet, recites the basmalah over it one hundred and fifty times, carries it, and enters before a ruler or tyrant will be safe from his evil and no harm will befall him.

If he recites it one thousand times every day with sincere intention and a humble heart, after fasting and spiritual discipline, for fourteen days—or, according to another report, one thousand times after every prayer for the stated period—he will see the spiritual angels, speak with them, and they will speak with him, and he will direct them to do whatever he wishes.

Whoever writes it broken and cut into separate letters, as follows—mīm, ḥāʾ, alif, lām, rāʾ, ḥāʾ, mīm, nūn—also writes the name of the person he wants and that person’s mother’s name, then turns toward him; [UNCLEAR: the source says that his need will be fulfilled in what he seeks.]

Whoever writes al-Raḥmān split as “al-Raḥ / man” seven times, writes with it the name of the slave who flees, buries it in the house, and weighs it down with a stone, while saying: “O God, I ask You by the right of Bismillah al-Raḥmān al-Raḥīm and by the right of Your name al-Raḥmān to prevent this slave from absconding, O Lord of the worlds,” he will never flee or leave the house in which he is.

Whoever writes al-Raḥmān on a knife [UNCLEAR: the source’s phrase about its making and purpose is damaged], recites over it three hundred and thirty-one times, slaughters a rooster with it, and separates its head from its body—when the source’s unclear condition concerning the head occurs, take the head and bury it beneath the threshold of the door of the desired house. All insects will leave it, as will the jinn.

If the rooster’s head is fried in good oil and the person suffering pain is anointed with it, it will benefit him greatly. If a bleeding woman carries it, it will benefit her greatly.

Whoever writes al-Raḥīm on a banner one hundred and ninety times, carries it, and enters a battle, no weapon will affect him and no illness will befall him. [UNCLEAR: the page ends with the incomplete fragment “and whoever…”].
