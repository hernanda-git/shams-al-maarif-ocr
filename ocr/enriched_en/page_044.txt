English:
— 31 —

## Literal English translation

And if it is written in a glass vessel **forty times**, and it is erased with Zamzam water or fresh well water, and any sick person drinks from that water, God, Most High, will cure him.

And if a woman experiencing difficulty in childbirth drinks from it, she will give birth immediately.

And if it is written on a sheet of paper **thirty-five times** and hung in the house, no Satan or jinn will enter it, and blessing will increase therein.

And if that paper is hung in a shop, its customers will increase, its profit will increase, its merchandise will increase, and God will blind the eyes of those who look upon it.

And if it is written on the first day of **Muḥarram** on a sheet of paper **one hundred and thirty times**, and a person carries it, no misfortune will befall him or his household for the duration of his life.

And if it is written **110 times** for a woman whose children do not live, and for a barren woman who has not conceived, after her purification from menstruation for three days, and she carries the paper and her husband has intercourse with her, she will conceive by the permission of God, Most High.

And she should not put down the paper until after **sixty days**. Then she will bear a righteous child and will experience neither pain nor hardship in carrying him, by the permission of God, Most High.

And if it is written **sixty-one times** and carried by one whose children do not survive, they will live. This has been tried and proven, and God is powerful over everything.

And if it is written on a sheet of paper **one hundred and one times** and buried in the crop, that crop will become fertile and will be protected from all afflictions.

And if it is written **seventy times** and placed with the dead person in his shroud, he will be safe from the terror of **Munkar and Nakīr**, and it will be a light for him until the Day of Resurrection.

And if it is written on a tablet of **lead** and placed in the fisherman's net, his catch will increase.

And if it is written **once** on a card and placed beneath the stone of a ring, and that ring is placed in **buttermilk**, and a person who has been stung drinks it and vomits, the poison will come out by the permission of God, Most High.

And if its letters are written separately and carried, it has a great virtue.

For the **bāʾ** is the splendor of God;

the **sīn** is His radiance;

the **mīm** is His glory and His sovereignty;

the **alif** is His pre-eternity;

the **lām** is His gentleness;

the **hāʾ** is His guidance;

the **alif** is His command;

the **lām** is the Mother of Sovereignty;

the **rāʾ** is His mercy;

the **ḥāʾ** is His wisdom;

the **mīm** is His kingdom;

and the **nūn** is His blessing.

And so forth.

And if one writes from the Basmala **“bā”**, in this manner, **b**, twenty-one times, and places it in wealth, blessing will increase in it and it will grow.

And if he writes this number and adds to it this verse, with its letters written separately, in this manner:

**س ل ا م ع ل ى ن و ح ف ي ا ل ع ا ل م ي ن**

**“Peace be upon Noah among the worlds,”**

and erases it and gives it to the one who has been stung to drink, he will be cured immediately by the permission of God, Most High.

And whoever writes it on a sheet of paper and looks at the letter **mīm** every day **forty times**, while saying:

**“O God, Master of Sovereignty,”**

up to His saying:

**“In Your hand is the good,”**

he will not know from where the good comes to him, and God will bless what is before him.

And whoever writes **al-Raḥmān al-Raḥīm** fifty times on a sheet of paper, recites the Basmala over it **one hundred and fifty times**, carries it, and enters before a sultan or tyrant, he will be safe from his evil and no harm will befall him.

And if he recites it every day **one thousand times**, with a sincere intention and a humble heart, after fasting, spiritual discipline (*riyāḍah*), and purification, for **fourteen days**,

and according to another narration, after every prayer, the aforementioned number one thousand times,

then he will see the **spiritual angels**, speak with them, and they will speak with him, and he will direct them in whatever he wishes.

And whoever writes it in broken, separated form, thus:

**م ي ح ا ل ر**

and also writes the name of the person desired and the name of his mother, then turns toward him, his need concerning whatever he requests will be fulfilled.

And whoever writes **al-Raḥmān** in this manner:

**ا ل ر ح م ن**

seven times, and writes with it the name of the servant who is fleeing, and buries it in the house and weighs it down with a stone, and says:

**“O God, I ask You by the right of Bismillāh al-Raḥmān al-Raḥīm, and by the right of Your name, al-Raḥmān, to prevent this servant from fleeing, O Lord of the worlds,”**

then he will never flee and will not leave the house in which he is.

And whoever writes **al-Raḥmān** upon a knife made of steel, sharpening its blade with it, and recites over it **three hundred and thirty-one times**, and slaughters a rooster with it and separates its head from its body,

then, when it walks without a head, take its head afterward and bury it beneath the threshold of the door of whomever you wish.

Then all the insects will come out of it, and likewise the jinn.

And if the rooster's head is fried in good oil and the person suffering from pains is anointed with it, it will benefit him greatly.

And if a bleeding woman carries it, it will benefit her greatly.

And whoever writes **al-Raḥīm** on a banner **one hundred and ninety times**, carries it, and enters a battle of war, no weapon will affect him and no illness will befall him.
