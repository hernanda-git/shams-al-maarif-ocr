English:
— 125 —

The earthy spirits obedient by the right of what I have sworn upon you, and it is a mighty oath, were you but to know ( "And that if they had remained straight on the way, We would have given them abundant water to test them; and whoever turns away from the remembrance of his Lord, He will lead him to a steep punishment" ). Answer, and let none of you lag behind, by the right of what I have sworn upon you ( "And that the places of worship are for Allah, so do not call upon anyone along with Allah" ). And when 'Abd Allah stood calling upon Him, they were about to become a compact mass over him. Say: "I only call upon my Lord and I do not associate with Him anyone." Say: "Indeed I do not possess for you harm or guidance." Say: "Indeed I will not find for me from Allah any refuge, nor will I find besides Him any resort, except a conveyance from Allah and His messages; and whoever disobeys Allah and His Messenger, then indeed for him is the fire of Hell, abiding therein forever." Until when they see what they are promised, they will know who is weaker in helper and fewer in number. Say: "I do not know whether what you are promised is near, or He assigns for it a term." ( "The Knower of the unseen—no one discloses His unseen except him whom He is pleased with of a messenger, for indeed He sends before him and behind him a watcher" ).

O Allah, I ask You by the bā' of Your permanence, and the bā' of Your subsistence, and the qāf of Your power, and the tā' of Your blessing, and the thā' of the establishment of Your dominion, and the vastness of Your Chair—O You whom the conjectures do not reach in His dominion; is there one who seeks refuge but everything of His creation seeks refuge in Him and is not judged against in His dominion? O Allah, indeed I possess for myself neither benefit nor harm except by Your permission. O Allah, I ask You by the right of the promise which You promised Your prophets and guided thereby Your friends. O Allah, O Majestic (×3), O Great (×3), O Holy (×3), O Allah (×3), O Yours is the dominion of the heavens and the earth, O You who know and none other than You is known. O Allah, I ask You by Your rank, and the 'ayn of Your knowledge, and the ghayn of Your forgiveness, and the fā' of Your bounty, and the kāf of Your greatness, and the lām of Your speech, and the yā' of Your certainty, and the alif of Your divinity, and the ḍād of Your radiance. O Allah, I ask You by the zāy of Your adornment, and the shīn of Your healing—O Living, O Self-Subsisting. ( "Except him whom He is pleased with of a messenger, for indeed He sends before him and behind him a watcher, that he may know that they have conveyed the messages of their Lord, and He has encompassed what is with them, and enumerated everything in number" ). O Allah, I ask You by the right of "the places of worship are for Allah," and by the right of Your righteous servants, and by the right of those who bow and prostrate, and by the right of those who supplicate—for You are Allah the Generous—and by the right of him who called upon You, that You subject to me my aim and be for me a helper. O Allah, I ask You by the right of him who associated nothing with Him, that You witness for me, facilitate for me, aid me, and prepare for me in my affair right guidance. O Allah, O You whose speech this is—I ask You by Your Mighty Speech, and by the sūrah "Say: It is revealed to me," and by the Wise Promise. O Allah, O You who enumerated everything in number, and caused the sea to flow in streams, and the creatures perish while He remains forever—O You whom the describers do not describe, nor is He described by standing or sitting, that You subject to me the servants of this sūrah and the names, that they serve me and obey me; indeed You are over everything powerful. O Allah, by the servants of this spiritual invocation: O you company of noble spirits entrusted with the spheres, whom He created from His light and lodged beneath His Throne—answer, hearing and acting as I will. I have sworn upon you by this invocation and the names and the sūrah, by the right of Arqūsh (×2), Kalluhūsh (×2), Baṭaṭūsh (×2), Kaṭṭūsh (×2), Hūsh (×2), Qānūsh (×2). I have sworn upon you, O Rūqiyā'īl, the angel entrusted with the sphere of the sun, by the right of Allah—there is no god but He; everything perishes except His face; His is the judgement and to Him you return. I have sworn upon you, O Rūqiyā'īl, by the presence of the school (al-madhhab)—answer, O madhhab, by the right of the King who prevails over His affair. O Rūqiyā'īl, and by the right of the moon, by the right of the Subduer over His servants—and He is the Subtle, the Aware. Answer, O Jibrā'īl, O Jibrā'īl, answer the White, by the right of the King who prevails over his affair—Jibrā'īl—and by the right of Sām, that you answered and hastened.
