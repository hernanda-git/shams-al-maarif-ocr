( A chapter on the mention of the balances and their manner )
Know that the balance in this science — its knowledge is a great matter. So if you wish an operation and break its letters, gather the letters,
and look at the letters of the ranks, for they are upon the ranks; and that every letter of the rank-letters resists six
letters of the degree-letters; and every twelve letters of the minute-letters equals a letter of the
rank-letters; and that every eighteen letters of the second-letters [equals] one letter of the four-letters
[category]. And measure by that. And this is the description of the balances by the coming sheet.
