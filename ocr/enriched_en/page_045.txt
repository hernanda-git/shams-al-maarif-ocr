English:
— 39 —

And whoever writes it on a sheet of paper **twenty-one times** and hangs it upon a person suffering from headache, it will benefit him.

And whoever writes it upon **seven almonds** with a needle of yellow copper, on Friday during the **hour of Venus**, and recites the name its prescribed number upon the almonds, and feeds them to whomever he wishes, he will love him intensely.

And whoever writes, in this form:

**ا م ي ح ر ل**

on a new mirror, on Monday at sunrise, and the person suffering from **al-lūqah** looks at it repeatedly, God, Most High, will cure him.

And whoever writes, in this form:

**ا ل ر ح ي م**

on a silver ring weighing **two dirhams**, and carries it with him, God, Most High, will grant him awe and obedience.

And whoever wishes to **subdue every tyrant**, let him write the **wafaq of Bismillāh al-Raḥmān al-Raḥīm** on a piece of lead and place the name of the person he wishes in the *wafaq*.

And let him fumigate it with **ḥalṭīt** and red garlic and bury it near a fire that is continually fueled.

And beware that the fire touches the lead, for the working will be destroyed, and you will be held accountable for it before God, Most High.

And this is its form, as you see.

| Bism | Allah | al-Raḥmān | al-Raḥīm | Fulan |
| Allah | al-Raḥmān | al-Raḥīm | Fulan | Bism |
| al-Raḥmān | al-Raḥīm | Fulan | Bism | Allah |
| Fulan | Bism | Allah | al-Raḥmān | al-Raḥīm |

## Literal English translation

And this is what you recite over him. You say:

**“In the name of God, the Most Merciful, the Most Compassionate. In the name of God, the Living, the Self-Sustaining, before whom the faces are humbled, to whom the voices are subdued, and before whose awe the hearts tremble. I ask You to bless and grant peace to our master Muhammad, and upon his family and his companions, and to fulfill my need concerning so-and-so. O God, You know that if he is going to turn away from what he is in, then guide him and grant him success. And if You know that he will not turn back, then bring down upon him Your affliction, Your displeasure, and Your wrath, and destroy him, O Subduer, O Overpowering One, O Powerful One, O All-Powerful One, O God.”**

[Recite the final invocation] **seven times**, and supplicate with it **seven hundred times**.

For the oppressor will either turn away from his oppression or perish quickly.

So fear God, Most High.

And whoever writes the noble **Basmala** in the center of a circle **eight times**, and writes around it the saying of God, Most High:

**“Muhammad is the Messenger of God, and those who are with him…”**

to the end of the sūrah, and fumigates it with a pleasant fragrance at a favorable time, and carries it, he will become awe-inspiring, honored, and respected among people.

No one will see him except that he will love him and incline toward him naturally, and all his purposes will succeed by the permission of God, Most High.

And whoever writes the entire Basmala, while the **Moon is in Pisces** and the **ascendant is auspicious**, upon **gazelle parchment**, and carries it upon his head for the number written upon it, he will live happily and die a martyr, and he will not see in himself, his wealth, or his child anything that he dislikes.

And know, may God grant both you and me success in obedience to Him, that **“Bism”** is the concealed name, and God, Most High, is the Greatest Name.

And **al-Raḥmān al-Raḥīm** is an attribute of Himself. Thus, He is **Merciful in this world and Compassionate in the Hereafter**.

And **“Praise belongs to God, Lord of the worlds”** is corresponding to **“Bismillāh al-Raḥmān al-Raḥīm.”**

Thus:

**“Bismillāh”** corresponds to **“al-ḥamdu lillāh,”**

**“al-Raḥmān al-Raḥīm”** corresponds to **“Rabb al-ʿālamīn.”**

And know that all His attributes in His saying:

**“Master of the Day of Judgment”**

are the manifestation of Lordship.

Thus He is **King and Owner**.

And you should concern yourself with the manifestation of speech and lights, and with the request on the Day of Judgment, and with the attribute of ownership.

Thus He becomes **King of kings**, manifesting to souls through domination and overcoming.

And through sovereignty He becomes King of kings, manifesting to those brought near through granting ownership, according to His saying, Most High:

**“With a Powerful King.”**

And all of this is contained in:

**Bismillāh al-Raḥmān al-Raḥīm.**

Thus **Bismillāh**, which is in the Name, is so that all the comprehensive expressions of speech may reach the King through truth.

And you raise the supplication with the yearning tongue toward God: an ascent and not a descent.

**Al-Raḥmān al-Raḥīm** descends to the second Principle.

**Bismillāh al-Raḥmān al-Raḥīm** ascends to the first Principle.

Within it is the secret of beginning, and within it are the degrees of **tawḥīd**, because:

**“Bism”** corresponds to **“I bear witness,”**

and **“Allāh”** corresponds to:

**“There is no deity except Him.”**

And within it are the degrees of the angels corresponding to **al-Raḥmān**, and those possessing knowledge corresponding to **al-Raḥīm**.

Likewise is the relation of the fourfold world, which is His saying, Most High:

**“Those are with those upon whom God has bestowed favor: the prophets, the truthful, the martyrs, and the righteous.”**

from the **raḥīmiyyah** to the **raḥmāniyyah**.

Therefore the degree in ascent reaches **Bismillāh al-Raḥmān al-Raḥīm**.
