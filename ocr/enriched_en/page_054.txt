في كنه ذات الله تعالى: أهو معلوم للبشر أم لا؟ وقال به غير معلوم، قال: لأن الشيء يُعرف بالعيان إذا حضر، وبالمثال إذا غاب، والله تعالى ليس كمثله شيء ولا يُرى بالعيان، قال تعالى: {لا تدركه الأبصار وهو يدرك الأبصار وهو اللطيف الخبير}. وقال بعض المحققين: لما ثبت قِدَمه تعالى بلا ابتداء، وبقاؤه بلا انقضاء، ووحدانيته لا عن عدد، وصفاته خارجة عن صفات الخلق، وجب أن لا يبلغ كنه صفته الواصفون؛ إذ لو كان كذلك لظهر لها حد ومثال، وهو يؤدي إلى الذهاب والفناء، وهو محال في حق الله تعالى. وقال المحاسبي رحمه الله تعالى: لما نزل جبريل على النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم بالاسم الأعظم في ورقة من ورق الجنة مطبوعة بخاتم مسك، مكتوب فيها: "اللهم إني أسألك باسمك المخزون المكنون الطاهر المطهر القدوس الحي القيوم الرحمن الرحيم ذي الجلال والإكرام". قال أنس: قالت امرأة: علمنيه يا رسول الله، فقال صلى الله عليه وسلم: "لا تعلمه النساء والصبيان". وقد سأل بعض الأحبار بعض الأئمة أن يجمع له ألفاظاً يدعو بها في مهماته، فكتب له هذا الدعاء المبارك، وهو هذا: "اللهم إني أسألك باسمك أنك أنت الله المقدس في حقائق محض التخصيص، وبأنك أنت الله كل حال من الجد والتعديل، وبأنك أنت الله المقدس بخصائص الأحدية والصمدية على الضد والند والنقيض والنظير، وبأنك أنت الله الذي ليس كمثله شيء وهو السميع البصير، أن تصلي على سيدنا محمد، وأن تقضي حوائجي كلها قضاءً يكون فيه خير الدنيا والآخرة، محفوظاً بالرعاية من الآفات، ملحوظاً بخصائص العنايات، معوداً بالخيرات، يا من هو في الحقيقة أهل التقوى وأهل المغفرة وأهل الحسنات. اللهم إنها مسألة خادم لعز ربوبيتك، بإظهار مسألة أنك علام الغيوب، وشاهد حقائق المطالب قبل مباشرتها للقلوب، فتممها بجميل الخاتم بخير المطلوب، وصلى الله على سيدنا محمد حبيب القلوب". هذا الدعاء فيه اسم الله الأعظم، كما ورد في "بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم" أن ما بينها وبين اسم الله الأعظم إلا كبياض سواد العين وبياضها، والله يقول الحق وهو يهدي السبيل، وصلى الله على سيدنا محمد وآله وصحبه وسلم.

(الفصل السادس في الخلوة وما يختص به أرباب الاعتكافات الموصلات للعلويات)
اعلم وفقني الله وإياك لطاعته، أن هذا الفصل العظيم الشأن يتوصل به إلى اسم العزيز الرحمن. وقد اعتكف بعض الصالحين من أئمة الدين ببيت الخطابة بجامع حلب، وكان مظلماً كالقبر وليس له منفذ للضوء إلا من الباب، فإذا غلق الباب صار كالقبر، فكان يصلي مع الجماعة ويخرج بظهره وقت قيام الصلاة، فإذا سلم الإمام دخل على حاله مستقبل القبلة ولا ينظر إلى أحد، وكان أكثر دعائه وتضرعه وسؤاله الله تعالى في سائر أوقاته أن يعلمه الاسم الأعظم. فبينما هو كذلك ذات ليلة جالساً مجداً في الاجتهاد والابتهال إلى الله تعالى بالذكر، وإذا بلوح من نور قد تصور بين عينيه فيه أشكال مصورة، فأعرض عنه لئلا يشتغل بالنظر إليه عن إقباله إلى الله تعالى، فذكره به في وجهه وقيل له: خذ ما تنتفع به. فعند ذلك فتح عينه وأقبل على اللوح يتأمله، وإذا هو أربعة أسطر: سطر أعلاه، وسطر أسفله، وواحد على اليمين، وواحد على اليسار، وفي الوسط دائرة، وداخل الدائرة أخرى، وما بين الدائرتين مقدار الفتحة، وفي وسط الدائرة الصغيرة خط يقطعها نصفين، وفي النصف الأعلى ملتقى الخطين خطان آخران إلى الخط القاطع شكلاً مثلثاً مكتوباً، وفي وسطه من قطب الدائرة: {كلا بل هو الله} وحيم في أمة.

English:
— 48 —

On the essence of the Self of God Most High: is it known to human beings or not? It was answered that it is not known. The reason given is that a thing is known by direct observation when it is present, and by comparison when it is absent; but God Most High is not like anything, and is not seen by direct observation. God Most High said: {Vision does not apprehend Him, while He apprehends the visions, and He is the Subtle, the Aware}. Some of the investigators said: since His pre-eternity is established without beginning, His permanence without end, His oneness not from any number, and His attributes exterior to the attributes of creation, it is necessary that His essence cannot be reached by those who describe His attributes; for if it were so, a limit and a likeness would appear to it, and that leads to departure and annihilation, and that is impossible in respect of God Most High.

Al-Muhāṣibī, may God Most High have mercy on him, said: when Jibrīl descended upon the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, with the Greatest Name, in a sheet from the leaves of Paradise, sealed with a signet of musk, upon which was written: "O Allah, I ask You by Your Name, the stored, the concealed, the pure, the purified, the holy, the Ever-Living, the Sustainer of all, the Merciful, the Compassionate, Possessor of Majesty and Honor." Anas said: a woman said, "Teach it to me, O Messenger of God." The Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, said: "It is not to be taught to women and boys." Some of the scholars asked some of the imams to compile for them words by which they would supplicate in their important affairs, so he wrote for them this blessed supplication, and it is this: "O Allah, I ask You by Your Name, that You are Allah, the sanctified in the realities of pure specialization; and that You are Allah, every state of gravity and measure; and that You are Allah, sanctified by the properties of oneness and self-sufficiency against the opposite, the match, the contrary, and the like; and that You are Allah, there is nothing like unto Him, and He is the All-Hearing, the All-Seeing — that You send blessings upon our master Muhammad, and that You decree all my needs a decree in which there is the good of this world and the Hereafter, guarded by care from afflictions, marked by the properties of favors, restored to good things. O You who in truth are the God-fearers, and the people of forgiveness, and the people of good deeds. O Allah, this is the request of a servant for the might of Your lordship, by the manifestation of the request that You are the Knower of the unseen and the Witness of the realities of requests before they reach the hearts; so complete it with a beautiful ending and the best of what is sought, and may Allah send blessings upon our master Muhammad, beloved of the hearts." In this supplication is the Greatest Name, as it has come in "Bismillah al-Raḥmān al-Raḥīm" that what is between it and the Greatest Name is nothing but the whiteness of the blackness of the eye and its whiteness. And Allah says the truth, and He guides the way, and may Allah send blessings upon our master Muhammad and his family and his companions and grant them peace.

(The sixth chapter on seclusion and what belongs to those who perform the retreats that lead to the higher things)
Know — and may God grant me and you success in His obedience — that this chapter, great in importance, is the means by which the Name of the Mighty, the Merciful is attained. Some of the righteous among the imams of the religion performed a retreat in the house of the sermon in the congregational mosque of Aleppo, and it was dark like a grave, and it had no opening for light except from the door, so when the door was shut it became like a grave. He used to pray with the congregation and go out with his back at the time of the standing of the prayer, then when the imam gave the salām he would enter in the state he was in, facing the qiblah, and would not look at anyone. And most of his supplication and entreaty and his asking of God Most High in all his times was that He teach him the Greatest Name.

So while he was thus, one night, sitting, diligently striving in effort and supplication to God Most High with remembrance, behold a tablet of light had appeared between his eyes, in which were depicted forms. So he turned away from it, lest he be occupied by looking at it and distracted from turning toward God Most High. It was mentioned to him in his face, and it was said to him: "Take what you will benefit from." At that, he opened his eye and turned to the tablet to contemplate it, and behold it was four lines: a line above, and a line below, and one on the right, and one on the left. In the middle was a circle, and inside the circle another, and between the two circles was the measure of the opening. In the middle of the small circle was a line cutting it in half, and in the upper half, at the meeting of the two lines, two other lines ran to the cutting line in a triangular shape written there, and in its middle, from the pole of the circle: {Nay, but it is Allah} and [UNCLEAR: the source reads Ḥā Yā Mīm, the three letters] in a nation.
