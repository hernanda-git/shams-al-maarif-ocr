English:
— 279 —

"…in Your hand are the fine things of pearl from light — and the Arouser of the spirit of the generous to the consciences of the secrets of the people of breasts. And every locked matter is opened by Your care, and the secret of every locked and fixed thing is unveiled by Your command. I ask You, O Opener of all good, and Repeller of all harm — that You make me standing with You, receiving from You, grasping the outpourings of scientific life and perpetual inclination, beautiful in anticipation of the appearance of the faces of Your gentleness, ever expecting the attainment of the perfection of Your bounty, ever longing for the existence of the traces of Your generosity. And open upon my heart and sight the doors of unveiling and witnessing, and support me in accepting the light of Your face when the treasuries of what is in Your mercy and forgiveness are spread — O Eternal in excellence, O Compassionate, O Bestower, O Lord of the worlds.' As for His exalted Name the All-Knowing — it is a tremendous name, containing two letters of the letters of the Greatest Name; and it is an ancient name, for Allah, exalted, has never ceased to be Knowing, and in it is a great secret for him who wants unveiling for knowledge from the world of the unseen and the witnessed — for Allah, exalted, is Subtle, and reveals His unseen to none but him He is pleased with of a messenger. And the angel created from it is 'Laṭfiyā'īl', upon him peace, and he is chief over four commanders, under each commander 150 ranks, each rank 150. And the invoker — the angel descends upon him and fulfills his need — and that is by his fortune for his father and his forefathers. As for the supplication by this noble Name — you say: 'O Allah, You are the Knower of what is in the breasts of the worlds, and You are the Knower of what is in the consciences of the humble, and You see what is in the hidden things of the essences of the worlds, and You are the Encompasser of what is in the movements of the thoughts of all creatures. I ask You by the treasuries of the guarded things of Your mercy, and the flashes of the scents of Your compassion, and the majesties of Your great blessing — that You make my knowledge encompassing of everything: its outward and inward, its lofty and majestic, its first and last, its opening and end — until I drown in the spreading of the secrets of Your oneness, and the diffusion of the subtleties of Your bounty. And I seek means to You in my beginning and my end, and show my hope to none other than You — O Knower of hidden things and consciences, and O Gatherer of the scattered in insights. Grant me clarity, openings, unveiling, and the sipping upon the name of what may be in thoughts and sights — for You are the Encompasser of beings in knowledge and existence, and You are the Ruler over the consciences in spreading and witnessing — O Lord of the worlds. And may Allah bless our master Muḥammad and his family and companions and grant peace.' ( Chapter Twenty-Three — on the third pattern, and what indicates the attributes and emanations ) Know — may Allah make me and you successful to His obedience, and to understanding the secrets of His Names — that among the beautiful Names of Allah are: the All-Knowing, the Wise, the Expander, the Generous, the Bestower, the Ever-Returning, the Helper, the Originator, the Knower of the unseen. These Names differ in properties and secrets; and the invoker of this majestic pattern — Allah, exalted, gives him a knowledge from near Him, and secrets no one in his time is privy to save him who comes with its like — and Allah, exalted, grants him every good, improves his character, gives him eloquence in his speech, and helps him. As for His Names the Wise, the All-Knowing — they are two majestic names for him who wants to attain wisdom and the sciences of secrets; so let him persist in invoking them in seclusion, head uncovered, sitting on the earth with no barrier, facing the qibla — for Allah inspires him with wisdom, and conveys him to what he wants, and decrees for him a judgment that guides him, or an angel according to his aspiration. And whoever engraves their numbers in a square of 4 by 4 in the dignity of Mercury and Jupiter connected to it — a tablet of green jasper, and carries it on his head — Allah makes him speak with wisdom, and his gaze passes over nothing but he remembers it.
