English:
- 77 -

all my body, my hair, and my skin from the evil of jinn, humankind, and spiritual and lower beings, by ṭawūs, dabbūs, dasūs, and by the Great, Greatest Name, and by the impregnable veil against all rebellious devils and all the armies of Iblis, bi-luṭf 3, salṭaʿ, Asmāṭūn, mahlash, kūhūsh, ʿalyā, qashū. Descend, all of you spiritual spirits, and you, O Sarfiyail, and screen so-and-so and so-and-so from the spirits, fear, and terror afflicting him, and from the evil of those who come by night and day, and from the evil of every rebellious, obstinate devil. And by the right of ṭalḥ, aṭwār, yaj, ʿaẓmiyā, kamhaṣ, kufīt, ʿamsaq, ḥamīt; by the right of faqaj, makhmat: His word is the truth, and His is the dominion on the day when the Trumpet is blown; Knower of the unseen and the witnessed, and He is the Wise, the All-Aware. And by the right of Ahiya, Sharahya, Adonay, Asbaut, Al Shaday, Aylohim: indeed it is a mighty oath, if you knew. God will suffice you against them, and He is the All-Hearing, the All-Knowing. Respond, O servants of these names, and take charge of such-and-such and such-and-such.

(And this is the adjuration of the four angels, peace be upon them.)
