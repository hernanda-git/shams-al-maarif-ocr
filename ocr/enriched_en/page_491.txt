( فصل في اسميه تعالى القادر المقتدر )
بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم اعلم أن معناها ذو القدرة لكن المقتدر أكثر مبالغة والقدرة عبارة
عن المعنى الذي به الشيء والمقدر بتقدير الارادة والعز وافقا فقهما القادر هو الذي إن
شاء فعل وإن شاء لم يفعل وليس من شرطه أن لا يشاء لا محالة فإن الله تعالى قادر على قيام القيامة
إلا أنه لو شاء أقامها وإن كان لا يقيمها لأنه لم يشأها فإنه لم يشأها لما جرى في سابق علمه
من تقدير أجلها ووقتها وذلك لا يقدح في القدرة المطلقة هو مخترع لكل موجود اختراعاً انفرد
به ومستغن عن معاونة غيره في ذلك وهو الله تعالى وأما العبد فله قدرة في الجملة لكن ليست
مثل تلك القدرة بل يخترع المقدورات بواسطة قدرته وجميع أسباب الوجود المذكورة هذا المختصر
لا يحتمل تفصيلها وقد ذكرت ذلك في كتابنا علم الهدى وقبس الاهتداء في شرح أسماء الله
الحسنى والمتقرب الى الله تعالى بهذا الاسم يشهد أن جميع الاشياء موجودة بقدرة الله تعالى

English:
— 485 —

"…and it is the falling of the point of the beginning, then the fourth the point of the end; and the point is from the knowledge of the secret of the sphere and the circle of the heart, then the intellect, then the spirit, and the circle of the body — all these are in the circles, so understand the secret of the knowledge and the will and the primary point, the resting-place of the soul, the circle — and it is the place of the truthful, for they are the reality of the numerical nearness after the reach of the knowledge to them from the world of the secret of the command; so it is the first subject in the atom 11 from before the states and the prophethood — the first subject in the pole of the command; then the point of the end, which is the secret of the will, that the degree of the truthful be completed in their stations. And the talk preceded on that in its place. * And know that the scholar-practitioners know these ranks, and I did not mention that but that it be a wayfaring for the guided and an incitement for the negligent, that they know the excellence of knowledge and its reality. And this is a sign by which the causes of thought and the management in it are completed, upon the rulings of the flow of their measures — and that is by the secret of the knowledge from the world of the power to the world of the will, until the secret of that is unveiled to you. And Allah is the One granting success. And this is its figure. And know that His two names al-Wāḥid al-Aḥad have no creation, and their special properties were mentioned with His name al-Ṣamad in our book 'Qabas al-Ihtidā' in the explanation of the Beautiful Names of Allah, so seek it and you will find it there, if Allah wills.

(Section on His two names God Most High, al-Qādir al-Muqtadir) (In the name of Allah the Merciful, the Compassionate) Know that their meaning is 'the Possessor of power', but al-Muqtadir is more of an exaggeration; and the power is an expression for the meaning by which a thing is, and the measured is by the measuring of the will, and the might agreed with them, so understand them: al-Qādir is He who, if He wills, acts, and if He wills, does not act; and it is not of His condition that He necessarily wills, for God Most High is capable of establishing the Resurrection, except that if He willed He would establish it, even if He does not establish it because He did not will it — for He did not will it for what flowed in His prior knowledge of the measuring of its term and its time; and that does not detract from the absolute power — He is the Inventor of every existent by an invention He alone undertook, and is independent of the aid of other than Him, and He is God Most High. And as for the servant, he has power in sum, but it is not like that power; rather he invents the measured-things by the mediation of his power and all the causes of existence mentioned — this abridgment does not bear their detail; and I mentioned that in our book 'ʿIlm al-Hudā' and 'Qabas al-Ihtidā' in the explanation of the Beautiful Names of Allah. And he who draws near to God Most High by this name witnesses that all things exist by the power of God Most High, and Allah measured them and created the thing at the doing of the thing — as the fire does not burn by itself, but Allah creates the burning at its existence, and it is not hidden from the wayfarer. (And of the special properties) of these two names: for the repelling of ailments and pains — they are written in two squares and honey is placed over them and erased with water and the sick drinks it, so he is healed by the permission of God Most High; and if these two names are written for the tying of the tongues and the humility of the heart on silver and carried by a person, he attains that. * And know that he who is characterized by these two names becomes of the single servants of Allah; and each name of them has a magnificent retreat with the condition of the spiritual practice and recitation of the number of his name — al-Qādir's servant is Jibrā'īl, who descends upon the one remembering in sleep or wakefulness, and he is from the worlds of 'Azrā'īl (peace be upon him), and he commands him with the mention of the spirituality; and if he appears to an enemy or a wrong-doer, he is victorious — the majesty is subdued and perishes, so know that and realize it. * And as for His name, God Most High, al-Muqtadir — it is a great name, and it has a magnificent retreat that gives its possessor… [page ends]
