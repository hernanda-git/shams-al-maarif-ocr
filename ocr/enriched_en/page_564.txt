Treatise: Zahr al-Murūj fī Dalā'il al-Burūj [The Blossom of the Meadows in the Indications of the Zodiacal Signs]
* In the name of Allah, the Most Merciful, the Compassionate *
Praise belongs to Allah — glorious and sublime — the Raiser of the high heavens, who placed in the sky the zodiacal signs and adorned them for the beholders, and placed the stars in the darknesses of land and sea as guidance for the travelers. I praise Him — glorified and God Most High — and thank Him with abundant thanks; and I testify that there is no god but He, the Creator of the heavens and the Governor of the affairs of beings in expansion and contraction; and I testify that our master Muḥammad is His servant and His messenger, the possessor of the loftiest station, who drew near and descended until he was two bows' length or nearer — may God Most High, by the majesty of His greatness, bless and grant peace upon his noble self and his exalted station, and upon his family, the masters, the illustrious, and his companions, the leaders, the guides, and upon those who follow their guidance among mankind as long as a star has risen in the sky of the zodiac and traveled * . (Now then) — says the one hoping from his Lord, the manifest grace, 'Abd al-Qādir al-Ḥusaynī al-Adhamī, son of 'Abd al-Qādir, the servant of the function of the noble chamber in the fragrant, elevated Muḥammadan room — may Allah be for him by His pure guardianship and the watching of His care and His protection, amen, O Allah, amen. This is a concise treatise of the pearls of gems, precious in the jewels of the twelve zodiacal signs, from each of which is drawn a judgment — I named it (Zahr al-Murūj fī Dalā'il al-Burūj). I gathered the scattered gems of its benefits and versified the scattered benefits thereof, as completion of my treatise (Lata'if al-Ishārah) on the special properties of the wandering planets; and I ask God Most High — He is the pearls of it — that He benefit by it the specific and the general. Upon Him I rely and to Him I turn; and He — glorified is He — is over every soul for what it earned, a watcher; and the best of hopes and the answerer and the most generous bestower and the recompenser for the best of the best; and to Him returns the whole affair; and Allah suffices me and is enough; and peace upon His servants whom He chose.

* The First Sign — Aries [al-Ḥaml ] *
Masculine, diurnal, cardinal, eastern, ascending, crooked, long, having a voice, barren, shining, fiery, hot, moist, sanguine, fortunate, mixed, praiseworthy; the peg of the east [watad al-mashriq] — the strongest and best of the places of the sphere; and the planet that alights in it becomes the strongest of the planets, especially if that alighting is in its own house or in its exaltation or in its triangle or in its term or in its face. Its planet Mars — in its nineteenth degree is the exaltation of the Sun; in it is the depression of Saturn; and in it Venus aspects it from the seventh sign by opposition, and the third and eleventh by sextile, and the fourth and tenth by square, and the fifth and ninth by trine; and the aspect of the third and fourth and fifth from before, and the aspect of the eleventh and tenth and ninth from behind — and it is stronger than the aspect of the Imam [the ascendant], and the strongest of these aspects is the opposition, so it indicates…
