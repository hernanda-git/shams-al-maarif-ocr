Arabic:
- ٥٢٩ -

مَن لا يسأله لا تمنع من سألك، إلهي كيف يُستحيا من السؤال مع الخطايا والزلات؟ أم كيف يُستغنى عن السؤال مع الفقر والفاقات؟ أم كيف يجوز لعبد آبق عن باب مولاه أن يقف على الباب طالباً جزيل عطاياه؟ وإنما ينبغي له أن يطلب المغفرة والتعلق بأذيال المعذرة، لكنك ملك كريم وجواد رحيم، دللت بجودك عليك، فأطلقت الألسن بالسؤال لديك، وأكرمت الوفود أن تخلو إليك. يا حبيب القلوب، أين أحبابك ومن وصل إلى بساط قربك واشتهى؟ من ذا الذي عاملك فلم يربح؟ ومن ذا الذي التجأ إليك أراد ولدى طلبت للراحة هلا طلبت منك واستفادت؟ وعزائم سعت إلى صرخاتك ما الذي ردها فعادت؟ وهل نقصت أموراً استقرضتها؟ لا وحقك بل زادت، قد سبق اختيارك فبطلت الحيل، وجرت الأقدار فلم يغيرها العمل، وتقدمت محبتك لأقوام قبل خلقهم في الأزل، وغضبت على قوم فلم ينفع عاملهم بما عمل. اللهم لا قوة على طاعتك إلا بإعانتك، ولا حول عن معصيتك إلا بمشيتك، ولا ملجأ منك إلا إليك، ولا خير يُرتجى إلا من يديك. يا من بيده إصلاح القلوب، أصلح قلوبنا بما تصاغر في جنب عفوه الذنوب، اغفر ذنوبنا، قد أتيناك طائعين فلا تردنا خائبين، واجعلنا بفضلك من أهل اليمين. إلهي لولا أنك بالفضل تجود ما كان عبدك إلى الذنوب يعود، ولولا محبتك للغفران ما أمهلت من يبارزك بالعصيان، وأسبلت سترك على أهل الطغيان، وقابلت إساءتنا منك بالإحسان. إلهي ما أمرتنا بالاستغفار إلا وأنت تريد المغفرة، ولولا كرمك ما ألهمت المعذرة، أنت المبدي بالنوال قبل السؤال، أدعوك بلسان أملي لما كل عملي، إن أطعتك رجوت إحسانك، وإن عصيتك رجوت طالباً غفرانك. اللهم إنا نسألك برحمتك التي ابتدأت بها الطائعين حتى قاموا بطاعتهم أن تمن بها على العاصين بعد معصيتهم، فإنك أنت المحسن الكريم ذو الفضل العظيم. اللهم يا من أمهل ولا أهمل، وستر حتى كأنه غفر، أنت الغني وأنا الفقير إليك، وأنت العزيز وأنا الحقير لديك. اللهم انظر إلينا نظر الرضا، واختمنا من ديوان أهل الجفاء، وأثبتنا في ديوان أهل الصفا، وارزقنا حسن الوفا. اللهم إنا نسألك بحق أسمائك الحسنى عليك، وفضلها وبركتها لديك، وبجاه من اخترته من خلقك واصطفيته لنفسك، وقرنت اسمه باسمك، وأوصلته إلى حضرة قدسك، وأودعته أسرار علمك، وجعلته خاتم أنبيائك ورسلك، وهو عبدك وحبيبك وصفيّك ونجيّك وخليلك سيدنا محمد صلى الله عليه وسلم، أسألك بجاهه عندك وبحرمته لديك أن توفقنا بتوفيقك إلى فهم علمك وطريقتك. اللهم إنك قَبِلت الوفاء من السحرة حين ذكرك مرة واحدة وسجدوا لك سجدة واحدة، ونحن لم نزل مقرين بربوبيتك، معترفين بوحدانيتك، ما سجدنا قط إلا بين يديك، ولا رفعنا حوائجنا إلا إليك. اللهم اجعلنا بكرمك، وارحمنا برحمتك، وداركنا بلطفك، وعاملنا بحلمك، ووفقنا لخدمتك، واغفر لنا ولوالدينا ولجميع المسلمين بجاه سيدنا محمد صلى الله عليه وسلم وآله وأصحابه وأتباعه وشيعته، مصابيح القلوب ومفاتيح الغيوب، أصحاب اللطائف وأرباب المعارف، ما أشرقت شموس الأرواح من جنادس الأشباح. شعر

( ٢٤ - شمس المعارف - رابع )

English:
- 529 -

"…[continuation, bridging the split word] …who does not ask Him, do not withhold from him who asks You. My God, how is one ashamed of asking amid offenses and slips? Or how can one dispense with asking amid poverty and need? Or how is it permissible for a runaway servant from his Master's door to stand at the door seeking His abundant gifts? Rather he should seek forgiveness and cling to the skirts of apology — but You are a noble generous king; by Your bounty You magnified [it], so You loosened the tongues in asking of You, and honored the delegations that they be alone with You. O beloved of the hearts, where are Your beloveds, and who reached the carpet of Your nearness and longed? Who is there who dealt with You and did not profit? And who is there who took refuge with You and willed, and at rest sought — did he not seek from You and benefit? And resolves that raced to Your cries — what turned them back so they returned? And did You diminish matters borrowed? No, by Your right — rather they increased; Your choice preceded so the stratagems failed, and the destinies flowed so the deed did not change them, and Your love advanced for peoples before their creation in pre-eternity, and You were angry at peoples so their work did not benefit them by what they did. O Allah, there is no power for Your obedience but by Your aid, and no turning from Your disobedience but by Your will, and no refuge from You but to You, and no good hoped but from Your hands. O He in whose hand is the reform of the hearts, reform our hearts by what diminishes before His forgiveness the sins; forgive our sins — we came to You obedient, so do not return us disappointed, and make us by Your bounty of the people of the right. My God, were it not that You bestow by the bounty, Your servant would not return to the sins; and were it not for Your love of forgiveness, You would not reprieve who confronts You with disobedience, and You would not let down Your veil over the people of rebellion, and would not meet our evil from You with good. My God, You did not command us to seek forgiveness but while You want the forgiveness; and were it not for Your generosity You would not inspire the apology. You are the one who bestows before the asking — I call You by the tongue of my hope for what is all my work: if I obeyed You I hoped Your good, and if I disobeyed You I hoped, seeking Your forgiveness. O Allah, we ask You by Your mercy with which You began the obedient until they stood by their obedience, that You graciously confer it upon the disobedient after their disobedience — for You are the good-doer, the generous, the possessor of the great bounty. O Allah, O He who reprieves and does not neglect, and veils until as if forgiven — You are the rich and I am the poor to You, and You are the mighty and I am the lowly before You. O Allah, look at us with the look of contentment, and register us out of the register of the people of harshness, and establish us in the register of the people of purity, and grant us the good fidelity. O Allah, we ask You by the right of Your beautiful names upon You, and their excellence and their blessing with You, and by the rank of whom You chose from Your creation and selected for Yourself, and coupled his name with Your name, and conveyed him to the presence of Your holiness, and deposited in him the secrets of Your knowledge, and made him the seal of Your prophets and Your messengers — and he is Your servant and Your beloved and Your chosen and Your confidant and Your friend, our master Muhammad (may Allah bless him and grant him peace) — I ask You by his rank with You and his sanctity with You, that You grant us by Your granting success to understand Your knowledge and Your way. O Allah, indeed You accepted the fidelity from the sorcerers when they mentioned You once and prostrated to You one prostration, and we have not ceased affirming Your lordship, acknowledging Your oneness, never prostrating but before You, nor raising our needs but to You. O Allah, make us by Your nobility, and have mercy on us by Your mercy, and turn to us by Your gentleness, and deal with us by Your forbearance, and grant us success to Your service, and forgive us and our parents and all the Muslims by the rank of our master Muhammad (may Allah bless him and grant him peace) and his family and his companions and his followers and his partisans — the lamps of the hearts and the keys of the unseen, the people of the subtleties and the masters of the knowledges, by whom shone the suns of the spirits from the mud of the phantoms.
