"And make them, O servants of these Names, in a sea of darkness, so that they see not — deaf, dumb, blind — so that they perceive not." Then the servant is silent and does not speak, for he is hidden from them. Then you say: "O God, I ask You by the hidden Subtlety — hide me by Your hidden Subtlety, for whoever You have hidden by the hiddenness of Your Subtlety has indeed become hidden, for he is hidden. Then go wherever you wish, for if you speak you appear to everyone, and the hidden secret departs and the Name goes unheard." "Insā, O Shamkhiyādh — no need of ours goes unfulfilled." Its meaning in Arabic: I am He who gives everything, and all that is in the heavens and the earth. These Names the spirits Wījā and Asākhā, abiding forever, obey. "And O Maṭiyyā Sulayṭā Lamūthā" — its meaning in Arabic: He who has aided servants and has shown mercy to them when they fall into distress. So whoever writes them on a mirror and places it under his head, and asks his spirits to inform him of whatever he wishes — of a theft, or a missing thing, or anything else — sees that. "Sīmḥū Lūrathā, Īh and Yāh" — its meaning: I am He who by My Oneness stands singular upon everything; I am the Everlasting of all everlasting things, the Age of all ages, and the most Merciful of the merciful. Whoever recites these Names — God grants his need and makes his affairs easy.

* And whoever adds to it the first Name and engraves it upon a ring, he has great acceptance with everyone, and whoever turns toward it with it, it answers him.

* Now as for the dispensation of the Names — fast three days while you are pure. So if you desire the destruction of one of the wrongdoers, write them on citron-leaf paper and bury it at the side of the house, with the name of whomever you wish and of his mother, and ask for whatever you wish of illnesses and maladies, for that will come to pass. And the writing is on Monday at mid-morning; the incense is mīʿah and sandalwood; and mention whatever you wish of illness or sickness, or bleeding, or headache, or swelling. And whatever you wish, do it, and do not leave it more than seven days, for he is destroyed, and you are the one called to account for it.

* And whoever writes them on a leaf of silver at the hour of Venus and carries it — whatever need he turns to is granted.

* And whoever writes them on gazelle parchment and binds them under the wing of an eagle, and orders the servants to carry it to whatever place they will — that is, if you desire acceptance and standing, write them in a clean vessel and erase them with the oil-water, and anoint with it the cleanness of your flask. Then if you turn to a need, take from what has been mentioned and anoint your face, and go to your need; it is granted, and everyone who sees you loves you with a strong love.

* And whoever writes them on the skin of a fox at an auspicious hour and carries it, and walks among the enemies — no one sees him as long as he is silent.

* And if you desire to see the jinn and hear their discourse and that they obey you, so that they come to you and inform you of whatever you ask — write the Names on the skin of a he-goat, then burn it on a shaqfa and apply it as collyrium to your eye, for so you see the jinn and hear their discourse.

* So if you wish to ask about a thing, speak with the Names from the first of them to the last of them, and say: "By the right of these Names I adjure you, and do not fail to obey my command," for then you see their learned ones before you, so ask them for whatever you wish. And if you have a need, sit in a clean place and recite them after every prayer seven times, for a period of three days. Then the spirits are present, each one of them preceded by a great company of the jinn. So bow in prostration in gratitude to God, and ask for whatever you wish, and say "O Helper, help me!" three times; then raise your head and say: "My suffice is God; there is no god but He; upon Him I rely, and He is the Lord of the Great Throne."

* And these are the bare Names, gathered together. Say: "Answer, O Kasfiyāʾīl, and O Ruqyāʾīl, and O Marqiyāʾīl, and O Madiyāʾīl, and O Mīkāʾīl, and O Manāʾīl, and O Rūyāʾīl, and O Dahriyāʾīl, and O Maṭriyāʾīl, and O Lūmarqabāʾīl, and O Alyāthīl, and O Ṭūṭāʾīl, and O Haʿqiyāʾīl, and O Qūṭiyāʾīl, and O ʿAsqariyāʾīl, and O Daḥḥāʾīl, and O Qalyā ʾīl, and O Darahāʾīl, and O Marqadāʾīl, and O Daqiyāʾīl, and O Marqiyāʾīl, and O Jibriyāʾīl, and O Samsamā ʾīl, and O Saʿyā ʾīl." And these Names are the most effusive, and they are the Shamkhūniyya Names, and they are called the Names of the Retreat and the Shamkhūniyya. Say: "Ishmakhīthā Maythā Shakhūnyā, and O Madhūkhā, and O Sayyid Khūnā, O Shamrāsiyā, and O Marmūṭīf, Nār Satū, O Jalṭaf, and O Sīṭaʿ al-Nūr — so cut with these two; they are opened by the gentleness of my eye. And O Mas Ḥikīfal, O Everlasting."
