…once and the Throne Verse ten times, and raises his head and hands to the sky and supplicates and says: "O Allah, I ask You — Allah by Allah, O Living, O Self-Subsistent, O You whom slumber seizes not nor sleep; and I ask You, O Allah, by the sanctity of the Throne Verse with You, to do for me such-and-such, and to undertake all my concerns and aims and what I ask of You" — and name your need, for it is a right upon Allah, exalted, to fulfill it; then send blessings upon the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, at the beginning of your start and at its end, and your work succeeds, if Allah, exalted, wills, and Allah is the One who grants success. * And of the properties of the noble Throne Verse: whoever recites it by day, Allah, exalted, preserves him that day; and whoever recites it by night, Allah, exalted, preserves him in his night. * And of the properties of the Throne Verse: whoever recites it after every prayer, Allah forgives his sins and expiates his misdeeds until the next prayer. * And of the properties of the Throne Verse: whoever recites it at his sleeping, it is a safeguard for him from the accursed satan. * And of the properties of the Throne Verse: whoever recites it at his anger and spits to his left, his satan withdraws and his anger departs by the permission of Allah, exalted. And let us mention this blessed supplication of the Throne Verse with confidence — and I have abbreviated its exposition and discussion lest it fall into the hand of one who does not know it and he calls by it and is answered; and it is a great supplication, noble in rank, tremendous in proof, high in standing — you say: "Allah, there is no god but He, alone in permanence and continuance; the entities of creatures are not truly established with His entity, nor their attributes with His attributes, nor their names with His names, nor their acts with His acts, and none besides Him is one of beauty in reality but for His majesty, and there is no majesty but for His majesty. And He is ever in His perfection, the Self-Subsistent, enduring upon His Throne by the continuance of His dominion, and all creatures are led to the perfection of knowledge of Him, and they know that He is One in His dominion, Single in the everlastingness of His eternal glory — despite the difference of their intellects and religions, all return to the reality of knowledge of Him, and know that He is the Creator, the Provider, the Giver of life, the Bringer of death, and the affair altogether returns to Him. And as for the knowers and the verifiers, they are bewildered in the reality of knowledge of Him — He who illuminated their hearts and sights by access to the realities of knowledge of His subjects — they are bewildered in the seas of His love and by what He conferred on them, and plunged into the waves of proofs, the seas of the surging of His power, so they confessed incapacity to attain knowledge of Him, and drowned in the seas of His dominion, and knew and verified that there is no god but He. And it indicates that He is Living, Self-Subsistent, so He gave life to their hearts and illuminated their sights and cores, and they beheld in existence none but Him, and no Lord but Him, so they affirmed that 'slumber seizes Him not nor sleep' — that is, no languor seizes Him from creation of the made things, nor sleep from attaining the known; 'His command, when He intends a thing, is but to say to it "Be," and it is' — so glory be to Him in whose hand is the dominion of all things, and to Him you shall return. All existing things declare Him far above indwelling, likeness, union, beginning, end, connection, and separation; 'nothing is like Him' — before things and the return of creatures and their submission to Him, and He in His dominion is the First and the Last, One, Single, alone in Himself in the unseen, above conjectures and understandings — 'His is what is in the heavens and what is in the earth', and all beings are witnesses for Him, and the made things know that He is the God of the earths and the heavens. 'Who is it that intercedes with Him except by His permission' — the people of the heavens and the earth glorify Him, and there is not a thing but glorifies Him with His praise, but you do not understand their glorification.
