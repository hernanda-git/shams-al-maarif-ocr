And by the right of the King, the Prevailing, over whom is your affair, Radqāyāʾīl, come, you and your helpers and your tribes and all your clans and whoever was under your rule. Answer and be a help upon what I desire, by the right of what I recited to you of the name of Allah, the عظیم. O Allah, be for me a help and an assistant. I have sworn upon you by Ismāyāʾīl, by the right of the صاحب of this highest structure.
