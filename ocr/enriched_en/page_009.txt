And the one by whose call the sphere of tawhid revolved ❊ and the suns of his wisdom shone forth ❊ and the stars of misguidance set at his sight ❊ and the dawn of tawhid broke forth through his blessedness ❊ may the best of prayers be upon him and upon his family ❊ and may Allah be pleased with the verifying, truthful Companions ❊ may Allah be pleased with them all together ❊ a prayer that raises them to the highest ranks and loftiest degrees ❊ (Now then) ❊ truth has banners, reality has an order, souls have familiarity with divine knowledge, the means is sought, and the ability to carry out its forms is bestowed, and felicity is coupled with the suns of perfection, and eternal experience by the use of the rites of the Shari‘ah is bestowed, and among the highest degrees in ‘Illiyyin is the degree of those who act, highest of all being the station of the guiding, verifying ones, and there is no rank for a scholar in Allah's religion who brings no benefit, just as a soul that brings no benefit has no truly living reality, and the farthest from felicity is the one who treats the rulings of the religious community lightly and violates the conditions upheld by the verifying people of the qiblah (And I), when I saw the speech of the eminent ones, those whose word has become lofty and whose wisdom has spread in the horizons and whose blessing has encompassed the creatures, they had written about working with the Names and Attributes, and about the secrets of letters, remembrances, and supplications, and someone bound to me by affection asked me to clarify what they had written and the treasure they had stored, so I answered him, while acknowledging my inability to comprehend the insights of the earlier forebears and the guiding, verifying imams, and I hoped that Allah, through this acknowledgment and undertaking, would grant me subtle assistance from the spirits of their spirits, so that my words might accord with verification and be articulated in the language of affirmation (so I say) ❊ in Allah is the sought help and upon Him is the reliance: the intended purpose of the chapters of this book is knowledge of the nobility of the Names of Allah (exalted is He), and what Allah (exalted is He) has deposited in their sea: various wisdom-jewels and divine subtleties, and how to work with the Names and supplications and what follows them of the letters of the surahs and the verses, and I arranged this book in chapters so that each chapter would point to the precise sciences I selected and gathered through which one reaches the Divine Presence without toil or hardship, and through which one reaches the desires of this world and what is sought within it, and I named this selected, unparalleled book of lofty knowledge (Shams al-Ma‘arif wa Lata’if al-‘Awarif) because it contains subtleties of spiritual operation and knowledge of effects. Whoever comes into possession of this book of mine must not show it to those unfit for it or disclose it in an improper place, for if he does so, Allah (exalted is He) will deprive him of its benefits, and its advantages and blessing will be withheld from him, and do not touch it except while you are pure, and do not read it except while engaged in remembrance, so that you may obtain from it what you want, and do not use it except for what pleases Allah (exalted is He), for it is the book of the saints, the righteous, the obedient, the seekers, the workers, and those who aspire, so guard it carefully and omit neither little nor much of it, and let your certainty be true and your faith in its realities firm. Actions are only by intentions, and only for every man is what he intended, and if an intention arises for you in an act among the acts, believe in it and affirm it, in accordance with the saying of the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him): “None of you should supplicate unless he is certain of the answer.” Set your action firmly and correctly, in accordance with the saying of the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him): “When one of you asks, let him be resolute in his asking,” trusting in the answer. Set your action firmly and correctly, in accordance with the saying of the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him): “One of you will be answered so long as he does not hasten, saying: ‘I supplicated, but I was not answered.’” ❊ Beware of considering the answer slow; remain continually purified and await it ❊ And this upright law and straight path comprises forty chapters; every chapter comprises meanings and indications and hidden and manifest symbols, so reflect on it with your intellect and contemplate it deeply ❊ And these chapters begin with (the first chapter), concerning letters
