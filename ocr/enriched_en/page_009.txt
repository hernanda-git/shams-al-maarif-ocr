English:

And the one who, by his call, made the sphere of tawhid revolve ❊ and the suns of his wisdom shone forth ❊ and the stars of misguidance sank away by his sight ❊ and the dawn of tawhid broke forth by his felicity ❊ may the best of prayers be upon him and upon his family ❊ and may Allah be pleased with the verifying, truthful Companions ❊ may Allah be pleased with them all together ❊ a prayer that reaches them the highest ranks and the loftiest degrees ❊ (Now then) ❊ then the truth has banners and for the reality there is a system and for the spirits, by the divine knowledges, an acquaintance, and the means is sought and the ability to its divisions is bestowed, and felicity is coupled with the suns of perfection, and eternal experience by the use of the rites of the Shari‘ah is bestowed, and the highest degrees in ‘Illiyyin are the degree of the doers, and the highest of them is the station of the guiding, verifying ones, and there is no station for a scholar in the religion of Allah who does not benefit, just as there is no existence of life for the reality of a soul that does not benefit, and the farthest of people from felicity is he who makes light of the rulings of the millah and breaches the conditions of the verifying ones from among the people of the qiblah (And I), when I saw the speech of the eminent ones, those whose word has become lofty and whose wisdom has spread in the horizons and whose blessing has encompassed the creatures, they had authored in the conjugation of names and attributes and the secrets of letters and remembrances and supplications, and there had inclined to me from one attached to me his affection in clarifying what they had authored and the treasure of what they had stored up, so I answered him, with acknowledgment of incapacity to understand the perceptions of the past forefathers and the guiding verifying imams, and I hoped from Allah, by the exertion of acknowledgment and acquisition, that He would extend to me from the spirits of their spirits with subtle aids, so that the utterance would be in accordance with verification and detailed with the tongue of confirmation (so I say) ❊ in Allah is the sought help and upon Him is the reliance: the intended purpose of the chapters of this book is knowledge of the nobility of the names of Allah تعالى and what Allah تعالى has deposited in their sea of the kinds of الحكميات jewels and divine لطائف, and how to act with the names of the supplications and what followed them of the letters of the surahs and the verses, and I made this book chapters so that every chapter would indicate what I selected and counted of subtle sciences by which one reaches the divine presence without fatigue and without perceiving hardship, and what by them one reaches the desires of the world and what is desired in it, and I named this selected, peerless, lofty-knowledge book (Shams al-Ma‘arif wa Lata’if al-‘Awarif) because of what is within it of subtle acts of disposition and the knowledges of influences. So it is forbidden upon whoever this book of mine falls into his hand that he display it to other than its people or divulge it in other than its place, for whenever he does that Allah تعالى will deprive him of its benefits and its profits and its blessing will be withheld from him, and do not touch it except while you are pure, and do not read it except if you are remembering, so that you may obtain from it what you want, and do not direct it except in what Allah تعالى is pleased with, for it is the book of the awliya’, and the righteous, and the obedient, and the seekers, and the doers, the desirous ones, so be stingy with it and do not leave from it a little nor a lot, and let your certainty be truthful and your faith in its realities confident. Actions are only by intentions, and only for every man is what he intended, and if an intention arises for you in an act among the acts, then believe in it and affirm, according to His saying عليه الصلاة والسلام: let none of you supplicate except while he is certain of the response, and decide upon your act with correctness, according to His saying عليه الصلاة والسلام: if one of you asks, then let him resolve the request, for it is not disliked, and the certainty of response, and decide upon your act with correctness, according to His saying صلى الله عليه وسلم: one of you is answered if he does not hasten, saying, “I supplicated, but I was not answered.” ❊ And beware that you do not deem the response slow, and do not cease being purified always and awaiting it ❊ And this upright law and straight path comprises forty chapters; every chapter comprises meanings and indications and hidden and manifest symbols, so ponder it with your intellect and contemplate it with your thought ❊ And these chapters (the first chapter) are in the letters
