Arabic:
— ٥٠٠ —

حقائق الأسماء وهو اسم أعظم وتلاوته عدد بسائطه وتلاوة المذكور القائم به فإن الملك الموكل بخدمة
هذا الاسم يهبط ومعه سبعون ألفاً من العوالم ويخلع عليه خلعة الكمال ويخاطبه وتقضى حوائجه
وخادمه بطيئيل عليه السلام يأتي للذاكر بحسب اجتهاده ومن خواصه للضالة والآبق يكتب
ويوضع في المكان ويتلوه عدده ويقول اللهم يا جامع الناس ليوم لا ريب فيه اجمع علي كذا وكذا
فإنه يحصل له ذلك وإذا أردت الجمع بين اثنين في خير مثل غضب على عبده أو رجل على
زوجته فاكتب له الاسم بحسب ما يليق ولا يخفاك بقية التصريف وهذه صورته
وأما الذكر القائم به فتقول بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم اللهم أنت جامع الموجودات
بعضها على بعض وجميع حالاتها في الإبرام والغضب منعت الأشياء عن
مقاصدها بالأمر القاهر وأوصلت بعضها لبعض بالرحمة والحظ أسألك
اللهم بمرادك من منع الأشياء أن تقطع عني كل قاطع يقطع عنك ويحجبني
منك الله يا جامع أسألك أن تجمع علي إدراكاتي وذاتي بالسلامة القدسية وتتجلى على روحي دوام
حفظك وفني لخدمتك وحضوري بين يديك إنك أنت الجامع لكل خير لا إله إلا أنت يا من عبد
لازم على هذا الذكر الأجمع الله له بين خيري الدنيا والآخرة
( فصل في اسمه تعالى الغني المغني )
بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم اعلم أن الغني هو الذي لا يحتاج إلى غيره في شيء لا في ذاته ولا في صفاته
ولا افتقار له لغيره بل يكون منزهاً عن العلائق فمن تعلقت ذاته أو صفاته بأمر خارج يتوقف عليه
وجوده وكماله فهو فقير محتاج إلى الكشف ولا يتصور ذلك إلا لله تعالى والله تعالى هو
الغني المطلق وبغناه يصير من شاء غنياً والغني عن الناس يحتاج إلى المغني فهذا يكون غنياً أي
مستغن عن غير الله تعالى بأن يمده بما يحتاج إليه بأن يقطع عنه أصل الحاجة والمغني الحقيقي هو
الذي لا حاجة له إلى أحد من الخلق أصلاً والذي يحتاج ومعه ما يحتاج إليه فهو غني مجازاً وهو غاية
ما يدخل في الإمكان في حق غير الله تعالى فأما إن فقد الحاجة فلا ولكن إذا لم تبق الحاجة إلى الله
تعالى لأنه هو المسمى بالغني تعالى والله هو الغني الحميد والفقير ما سوى الله تعالى وهو الغني عن
العالمين وقد قال صلى الله عليه وسلم ليس الغنى عن كثرة العرض إنما الغنى غنى النفس ألا ترى
أن التاجر عنده من المال ما يكفيه طول عمره وما عنده في نفسه شيء من الغنى بل هو من الفقر إلى
غاية الحاجة ويطلب الزيادة في ماله ولو كان فيه هلاك نفسه وأعلى درجة الغنى الاكتفاء بالموجود
فلا غنى إلا غنى النفس ولا غنى إلا من أعطاه الله تعالى غنى النفس وقد يكون الإنسان فقيراً جداً
وعند الناس متجملاً غنياً قال تعالى يحسبهم الجاهل أغنياء من التعفف وأما الإنسان الحيواني
الذي لا معرفة له به فهو فقير إلى العالم ويدعى بينهم فقيراً والمتقرب إلى الله تعالى بهذين الاسمين
يكون خافي الفكرة غني النفس صاحب هيبة ووقار ولهما خلوة جليلة القدر وإن خيرت إن شئت
تلوت كل اسم على حدة وإن شئت تلوت الاسمين فإن الملك القائم بهما يهبط وخدم اسم الغني

English:
— 500 —

"…the realities of the Names — and it is a Greatest Name, and its recitation [is] the number of its letters, and the recitation of the standing mention — for the angel charged with the service of this name descends with him seventy thousand of the worlds, and casts upon him the robe of perfection, and addresses him, and his needs are fulfilled; and its servant Baṭiyā'īl (peace be upon him) comes to the one remembering according to his striving. And of its special properties for the lost and the runaway: it is written and placed in the place and its number is recited, and he says 'O Allah, O Gatherer of the people for a Day of no doubt, gather for me such-and-such' — and he attains that. And if you want to bring together two [people] in good, like [when] a master is angry at his servant or a man at his wife, write for him the name as is fitting, and the rest of the management is not hidden from you. And this is its figure. And as for the standing mention by it, you say (In the name of Allah the Merciful, the Compassionate): 'O Allah, You are the Gatherer of the existents, some upon others, and all their states in the confirming and the wrath — You prevented the things from their goals by the overpowering command, and connected some to some by the mercy and the lot. I ask You, O Allah, by Your will in preventing the things, that You cut from me every cutter who cuts me off from You and veils me from You. O Allah, O Gatherer, I ask You that You gather for me my perceptions and my essence with the holy soundness, and manifest upon my spirit the permanence of Your preservation, and my devotion to Your service and my presence before You. Indeed You are the Gatherer of every good; there is no god but You, O He whom the servant who persists in this mention — Allah gathers for him between the good of this world and the Hereafter.' (Section on His name the Exalted, al-Ghanī al-Mughnī) (In the name of Allah the Merciful, the Compassionate) Know that al-Ghanī is He who needs not other-than-Him in anything — not in His Essence nor in His attributes, and He has no need of other-than-Him, but is transcendent above attachments. So whoever's essence or attributes are attached to an external matter on which his existence and perfection depend — he is poor, in need of unveiling, and that is not conceivable except for Allah the Exalted; and Allah the Exalted is the absolute Rich, and by His richness He makes rich whom He wills. And the rich one independent of the people needs the Enricher, so this one becomes rich — that is, independent of other-than-Allah the Exalted by His supplying what he needs and cutting from him the root of need. And the true Enricher is He who has need of none of the creatures at all; and he who needs while having with him what he needs — he is rich by metaphor, and that is the utmost of what enters possibility with respect to other-than-Allah the Exalted. So as for [one] devoid of need — there is none, but if the need toward Allah the Exalted does not remain, then He is the One named al-Ghanī the Exalted; and Allah is the Rich, the Praiseworthy, and the poor is other-than-Allah the Exalted, and He is independent of the worlds. And the Prophet (may Allah bless him and grant him peace) said: 'Wealth is not by abundance of possessions; wealth is the wealth of the soul. Do you not see that the merchant has of wealth what suffices him the length of his life, yet in himself has nothing of wealth — nay, he is in the utmost need and seeks increase in his wealth even if it be his destruction; and the highest degree of wealth is contentment with what exists. So there is no wealth but the wealth of the soul, and no wealth but from Him who gave him the wealth of the soul.' And a person may be very poor yet appear rich among the people — He said exalted 'The ignorant deems them rich because of their abstinence.' And as for the animal human who has no knowledge of Him — he is poor toward the world and is called among them 'poor'. And he who draws near to Allah the Exalted by these two names is of hidden thought, rich in soul, possessing awe and dignity; and they have a magnificent retreat of great measure. And if you choose, you may recite each name separately, or you may recite the two names — for the angel standing by them descends, and the servant of the name al-Ghanī is 'Aṭā'īl (peace be upon him).
