English:

"…and by the right of Your beautiful names: O Allah, O Swift, O Responder, O Extender, O Loving 3, O Possessor of the glorious Throne, O Originator, O Restorer, O Doer of what He wills — I ask You by the light of Your face which filled the pillars of Your Throne, and by Your power by which You decreed over all Your creation, and by Your mercy which embraced all things — there is no god but You, O Helper of those who seek help, help me, and fulfill my need in this hour, O Most Merciful of the merciful. O Allah, cast my love into the heart of the servant of this sūra — { he was deeply in love with her} — { they love them with the love of Allah, and those who believe are stronger in love for Allah} — { He loves them and they love Him} — { all, and it is not fed, and prostrate and draw near}. ( Saturday litany ) You say: { that he may warn him who is alive} to its end, and you say { not of those who earned anger nor of those astray} Amen — 'O Manifest, O Mighty, O King, O Granter of security, O Overseer, O Able, O Great — answer, O Kisfāyīl, and you O Maimūn — by the right of { not of those who earned anger nor of those astray} Amen, and by the right of { the Subduer above His servants, the Great, the Transcendent}, and by the right of { the King whose command prevails, Ṣaṭa'}, and by the right of { Lahmaṭaṭīl} — { so the truth came down, and what they were doing failed} — answer, O servant of this sūra.' And you say: { glory to the Reliever of every debtor}, { glory to the Sincere for every prisoner}, { glory to Him who ran water in the seas and springs}, { glory to the Opener for every grieved}, { glory to Him who made His treasuries between Kāf and Nūn}, { glory to Him who, when He wills a thing, says to it 'Be, and it is'} to its end. 'O Allah, dispose for me the servant of this sūra — as You disposed the sea for Moses, and the fire for Abraham, and iron for David, and the jinn and men and devils and wind for Solomon, and the sun and moon and stars and all things for Muhammad, may Allah bless him and grant him peace — and upon them all. O Allah, dispose for me Kisfāyīl and Maimūn — by the right of Your supremely great Name, and by the right of Your beautiful names: O Allah, O Swift, O Responder, O Extender, O Loving 3, O Possessor of the glorious Throne, O Originator, O Restorer, O Doer of what He wills. I ask You by the light of Your face which filled the pillars of Your Throne, and by Your power by which You decreed over all Your creation, and by Your mercy which embraced all things — there is no god but You, O Helper of those who seek help, help me 3 — O Allah, O Most Merciful of the merciful. O Allah, dispose for me the servant of this sūra — { he was deeply in love with her} — { they love them with the love of Allah, and those who believe are stronger in love for Allah} — { He loves them and they love Him} — { all, and it is not fed, and prostrate and draw near}. O Allah, answer my supplication by the right of sūrat Yā-Sīn — for You are powerful over all things, and no power nor strength except by Allah, the High, the Great.' The seven litanies are complete — so know the worth of what has come to you. And Allah speaks the truth, and He guides the way.
