The continuing supplication reads: and that You make them "toward the darkness, granted respite" [UNCLEAR: the reading of al-ẓulmah and its connection to being granted respite], tried in abasement, and passing out of blessing; they are upright neither in secret nor
openly, and gain neither honor nor reward, and can obtain neither victory nor patience. Send upon them punishment from above them and from
beneath their feet; clothe them in factions, let some of them taste the violence of others, and make them firewood for them [UNCLEAR: whether the two pronouns refer to the same people]; burn their hearts away from
uprightness, give them abundant water to drink, make what they possess upon the earth barren ground, and send down upon their gardens a calamity from
the sky, so that they become slippery ground, or their water sinks away, so that you, the one addressed, will never be able to seek it [UNCLEAR: the identity of this singular addressee]. Do not set their condition right, and make them
among those whose deeds are the greatest loss; do not raise a head for them, make them among the fearful, and do not supply them "bi-mā" [UNCLEAR: whether mā means what or an unfinished reference to water]; make them among the treacherous,
unable to eat or drink, finding no rest, "earth nor back" [UNCLEAR: how earth and back relate to resting]. Place a barrier before them and behind them
a barrier, a rampart on their right and a rampart on their left, rock upon their head and rugged ground beneath their feet, so that "lā yalid lahum"
"mashyan" [UNCLEAR: lā yalid lahum mashyan has an uncertain actor and meaning; mashyan denotes walking]; and "lā tara lahum 'aynan" [UNCLEAR: the addressed seeing, an eye, and their relation to them are uncertain]; and "lā yaḥill lahum khayran" (no good for them [UNCLEAR: the actor and sense of yaḥill]). Put shackles around their necks, and drag them with chains and fetters on
their feet and legs, in humiliation, with shackles around their necks and enemies at their heels; and "akhtim in the dwellings" [UNCLEAR: whether this is an order to seal or a first-person statement; what is sealed is unstated], so that
they do not prosper. Reverse their speech so that they are not guided, turn their spirits upside down so that they do not bear witness, and plunge their souls into despair so that
they have no power. Seize their hearts so that they do not understand, deafen their ears so that they do not hear, and efface their eyes
so that they do not see. Seal their mouths so that they do not speak, and transform them in their place so that they can neither go forward
nor return to their families. Indeed, You are the Compeller, the Supremely Great, the Controller, the Helper, the Strong, the Victor, the All-Subduing, the Humiliator,
the Avenger, the Destroyer, the Severe, the Forsaker, the Delayer, the Withholder, the Abaser, the Bringer of Harm, the Crusher, the Possessor of Majesty and Honor,
the Protector, the Magnificent, the Trustee, the Majestic, the All-Encompassing, the Possessor of Power, the Firm, the Possessor of Severe Might, the Possessor of the Glorious Throne,
the Doer of whatever He wills. "God has sealed their hearts and their hearing; over their eyes is a covering, and theirs is a tremendous punishment. God mocks
them and lets them persist in their transgression, wandering blindly. Deaf, mute, blind: they do not return. Or like a rainstorm from the sky containing darkness,
thunder, and lightning. God is powerful. Abasement is laid upon them wherever they are found, except through a bond from God and a bond from people. They have incurred wrath
from God, and destitution is laid upon them." Those who disbelieved said to their messengers, "We shall surely expel you from our land, unless you return to
our religion." Their Lord revealed to them, "We shall surely destroy the wrongdoers, and We shall surely settle you in the land after them. That is for whoever fears My station and fears
My threat." They sought victory, and every obstinate tyrant failed. "We certainly help Our messengers and those who believe, in the life of this world and on the day when
the witnesses stand: the day when the wrongdoers' excuses will not benefit them; theirs is the curse, and theirs the evil abode. We destroyed the most violent of them, and the example of
the ancients has passed. That is because God is the Protector of those who believe, while the disbelievers have no protector. Until when they rejoiced in what they had been given, We seized them
suddenly, and there they were, in despair. The last remnant of the wrongdoing people was cut off. Praise be to God, Lord of the worlds. God has decreed: I and My messengers shall surely prevail.
God is Strong, Mighty. He is the One who expelled the disbelievers among the People of the Book from their homes to the believers. A seal has been set
upon their hearts, so they do not understand. When you see them, their bodies impress you, and if they speak, you listen to their words. How they are turned away! Have you not
seen how your Lord dealt with 'Ād, Iram of the pillars, the like of which was not created in the lands, and Thamūd, who hewed the rock in the valley,
and Pharaoh of the stakes? Have you not seen how your Lord dealt with the People of the Elephant?" Etc., to the end of the surah. The author claims that no
