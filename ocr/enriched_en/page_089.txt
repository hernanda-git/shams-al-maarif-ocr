* One of the People of Realities related, saying: "I boarded a vessel in which the number of the collection fundamental to it was 131 planks. This is a condition in the Ark of Salvation. I was cast into the sea, running with the wind of safety for the duration of the days of the four seasons, derived from the days of God Most High. I reached the seashore and found precious jewels, brilliant rubies, great stores, red sulfur, colored mines, and the spring of life flowing perpetually. I bathed in its water and drank a draught from it after which there is no annihilation. Then I boarded my vessel and returned toward my homeland. * Know that the movements are four: the movement of unveiling, which is the first, and the movement of veiling, which is the second. The first movement of unveiling is the movement of atoms; it is the essential movement, and it is the movement of the intellect. The movement of the first journey is the movement of the soul, and it is voluntary. The movement of the second journey is the movement of atoms, and it is the movement of longing. The first unveiling belongs to the first day, the day God created the spirits in the worlds of the covenant. The second day is the first journey, the day the intellect addressed him in the world of Dust (haba). These are the beginnings of the first things. The third day is the day of the second unveiling, the day the covenant was taken. The fourth day is the second journey, the day of endlessness, except that its end is the day of unveiling. Thus the first unveiling is a first Throne, and it is the first journey, [UNCLEAR: karami; term unclear] of beginninglessness; then the second unveiling is the second Throne of endlessness, [UNCLEAR: karami; term unclear] of duration. All these phases and cycles are the reality of Mercifulness and the truth of Compassionateness. The reality of Mercifulness is the secret of [UNCLEAR: al-marjahah; the precise meaning is uncertain]—I mean the breath ascribed to the manifest Presence of Lordship, and in its relation to the subtle things employed through dense things.

As for the secret of the veils—that is, emergence—our Prophet, peace be upon him, drew attention to it in his saying, reporting from his Lord: "Indeed, seventy veils of light and darkness: if He unveiled them, the splendors of His Face would burn whatever of His creation His gaze reaches." These veils are from your relation, not from His relation. They are not impossible on two grounds; those do not veil bodies, and the Real Most High is not a body. The third: the veiled must have a direction, whereas God Most High has no direction. Thus the veils of darkness are the veils of the fathers from the lights, and the veils of light are veils from the principles of the Essence—that is, its reality. Were it not for that, they would vanish.
