English:
- 55 -

By permission of God Most High: whoever carries it, his good multiplies, and
provision is made easy for him from a quarter he does not reckon on; and in it,
for companionship and for love and affection, there is what surpasses all
measure for whoever reflects upon that. And its figure comes among the figures
for the seven days, if God Most High wills.

*

Know that whoever is granted an opening into a secret of the secrets of the
mim, and into its compassing and its enfolding, and into what it contains of
worlds, witnesses the wonders of the existences. Whoever wants memorisation to
be made easy for himself, let him write this numerical secret on a Thursday,
while he is in a state of purity, facing the qiblah, and with him the name of
the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, forty times; and let him fumigate
it and drink it down with water and the honey of bees, and let him say: O God,
by the blessing of what I have drunk, make memorisation and understanding light
for me. And let him persist in that for forty days; God Most High opens his
outward and his inward. This is for the one who understands, in that he
witnesses the strength of what is in his belly of every world in the secret by
which the mim stands. With this resolve, the opening comes to be.

*

Whoever writes it and carries it on his right upper arm and walks among his
enemies, God Most High saves him from them and humiliates them. And whoever
enters with it upon one whose evil he fears, or upon a stubborn tyrant, is
humbled before him and submits; and God Most High seals his heart and his
arrogance between his hands, his soul is cut off from his own wish, and God Most
High grants him what he seeks and suffices him against his evil, because of the
wondrous secrets within it.

*

Whoever writes it, fumigates it, and hangs it facing the sun, in the place
where the sun rises upon it and draws near, never leaving it for the whole
length of its day with its rays, will have great acceptance among all
creatures. And this is its figure:

(The Eighth Chapter on the four harmonies and what is peculiar to it of the
circular chapters)

Know - may God grant success to me and to you for obeying Him and for
comprehending the secrets of His names - that upon this chapter the whole of
this book turns, and within it are mighty secrets. So if you desire to work
with these blessed names and these august harmonies, and with the names of the
angels who govern the time, and the names of the winds and the stars, know that
the year is twelve months and is divided into four parts, each part of them
being three months, and the seasons are four: the season of summer, and the
season of winter, and the season of spring, and the season of autumn; and each
season is three months. And the first taquqah is for the season of spring, and
it is the twenty-fourth of June. (The third taquqah is for the season of
autumn), and it is the twenty-fourth of September. (The fourth taquqah is for
the season of winter), and it is from the twenty-fourth of December.

(A chapter on the names of the kings who govern the time) So the lord of the
east, his name is Dānā’īl, and the lord of the west, his name is Dardā’īlā’īl, and
the lord of the north, his name is Ismā’īl, and the lord of the south, his name
is Ḥazqī’īlā’īl. So the lord of the east is for the season of summer, and the lord
of the west is for the season of winter, and the lord of the north is for the
season of spring, and the lord of the south is for the season of autumn.

(A chapter on the distribution of the helpers over the four quarters) So the
helpers of the lord of the east are Wajhā’īlā’īl and Ḥamrā’īlā’īl
