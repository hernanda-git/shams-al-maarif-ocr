العاقبة من ولد فيه عاش سعيدا وبغض الشر وكان محمود العاقبة ومن ولد فيه عاش سعيدا وبغض
الفجور ويحب الصالحين وبخوره بزر كتان وحبة سوداء والله أعلم (القول على منزلة الدبران وهذه
صفته) ٠ ٠ ٠ وله حرف الدال إذا نزل القمر بالدبران وهو أرضي ينحط فيه إلى العالم بإذن
الله تعالى روحانية تفصل العداوة والبغضاء والفساد في الأرض فاحذر فيه السعي في طلب الحوائج
والابتداء بالأعمال ولا تعمل فيه طلسا ولا تدبر فيه صنعة فكل الأعمال فيه رديئة
ولا تصلح إلا لدفن الموتى ودفن المال وكتم الأسرار وحفر الآبار وشق الأنهار ولا تصلح لما سوى
ذلك ومن ولد فيه كان مذموما مخذولا وبخوره قشر رمان حلو ولبان ذكر (القول على منزلة الهقعة)
وهذه صفتها ٠ ٠ ٠ ولها حرف الهاء إذا نزل القمر بها وهو تمزج من سعد ونحس بها فاعمل
فيه ترياقات السموم واختلاطها خاصة ولا تدبر فيه صنعة الشمس ولا القمر ولا تغرس فيه غرسا
ولا تلبس جديدا ولا تتزوج فيه فإنه غير محمود العاقبة بخوره عود ولبان وجاوي ومصطكى
والله أعلم (القول على منزلة الهقعة ٠ ٠ ٠ وهذه صفتها) ٠ ٠ ٠ وله حرف الواو إذا نزل القمر بالهقعة
وهو كوكب سعيد فاعمل فيه للعطف والمحبة والمودة وخر فيه بالرائحة العطرة وادخل فيه على الملوك
والأكابر واسع في حوائجهم وما تريد ومعاشرة الإخوان وابتدئ فيه بالأعمال التي تريدها وتزوج
فيه واشرب فيه الدواء واشتر فيه الجواري والخيل واغرس الشجر وابن فيه البناء كيل وزن وسافر
فيه وبع واشتر فكل ذلك محمود جدا ومن ولد فيه عاش سعيدا ومات شهيدا بخوره قطرب
وبزر شيح والله أعلم (القول على منزلة الذراع وهذه صفته) ٠ ٠ ٠ وله حرف الزاي إذا نزل القمر
بالذراع وهو رياحي سعد لين ينحط فيه إلى العالم بإذن الله تعالى روحانية صالحة يصلح فيه الابتداء
بالعلوم والأعمال الصالحة والاجتماع بالعلماء والعباد وعمل الطلسمات وأبواب النارنجيات والدخول
على الملوك والاتصال بأشراف الناس والإخوان ومن ولد فيه كان سعيدا رشيدا موفقا بخوره حب
كرفس وبزر كتان (القول على منزلة النثرة وهذه صفتها) ٠ ٠ ٠ ٠ ٠ ٠ ٠ : ولها حرف
الحاء إذا نزل القمر بها وهو بارد ممتزج سعد ينحط فيه إلى العالم بإذن الله تعالى روحانية تفعل فيه العداوة
والبغضاء والقطيعة وما أشبه ذلك وتصلح لعمل الطلسمات والدعاء على الأعداء والطغاة والبغاة والشحناء
ويتحرك فيه روحانية أسرع فيها بأعمال آلات الحرب والإشارة فيها لأنها رديئة تصلح لأعمال

وهذه صفتها) ٠ ٠ ٠ ولها حرف الظاء إذا نزل القمر بها وهو كوكب نحس مستمر ينحط فيه إلى
العالم روحانية تدل على مثل ما تقدم فلا تعمل فيه طلسا ولا تدبر فيه صنعة ولا تدخل على الملوك
ولا تبتدئ فيه بالمودة ولا تعمل فيه حكمة ولا تمل قسما والانفراد خير من الخلطة وهو رد لجميع

يبتدئ فيها بأعمال المودة والأعمال القريبة والرضى وتصلح فيه النقلة من مكان إلى مكان ويكره فيه

English:

As for the outcome: whoever is born in it will live happily, hate evil, and have a praiseworthy outcome. Whoever is born in it will live happily, hate debauchery, and love the righteous. Its incense is flaxseed and black seed. God knows best.

[Discourse on the mansion of al-Dabarān, and this is its form]

Its form is three dots (• • •), and its letter is dāl. When the Moon descends in al-Dabarān, which is earthy, a spiritual being descends into the world through it, by the permission of God, and separates enmity, hatred, and corruption in the earth. Beware of seeking needs or beginning works in it. Do not make a talisman in it or devise a craft, for all works in it are bad. It is suitable only for burying the dead, burying money, concealing secrets, digging wells, and cutting channels; it is not suitable for anything else. Whoever is born in it will be blameworthy and forsaken. Its incense is sweet pomegranate peel and frankincense.

[Discourse on the mansion of al-Haqʿah]

This is its form: three dots (• • •), and its letter is hāʾ. When the Moon descends in it, it is a mixture of fortunate and unfortunate influences. Work in it especially on antidotes to poisons and their mixtures. Do not devise a craft of the Sun or the Moon in it, plant nothing in it, wear nothing new, and do not marry in it, for its outcome is not praiseworthy. Its incense is aloeswood, frankincense, benzoin, and mastic. God knows best.

[Discourse on the mansion of al-Haqʿah, and this is its form]

This is its form: three dots (• • •), and its letter is wāw. When the Moon descends in al-Haqʿah, it is a fortunate star. Work in it for affection, love, and amity; go out with a fragrant scent; enter upon kings and notables; pursue their needs and whatever you want; associate with brothers; and begin the works you desire. Marry in it, drink medicine in it, buy slave women and horses in it, plant trees, build, measure, weigh, travel, sell, and buy. All of that is highly praiseworthy. Whoever is born in it will live happily and die a martyr. Its incense is qutrub and wormwood seed. God knows best.

[Discourse on the mansion of al-Dhirāʿ, and this is its form]

This is its form: three dots (• • •), and its letter is zāy. When the Moon descends in al-Dhirāʿ, it is airy, fortunate, and gentle. A righteous spiritual being descends into the world through it, by the permission of God, Exalted is He. It is suitable for beginning the sciences and righteous works, meeting scholars and worshippers, making talismans, the arts of naranjiyyāt, entering upon kings, and forming connections with noble people and brothers. Whoever is born in it will be happy, rightly guided, and successful. Its incense is celery seed and flaxseed.

[Discourse on the mansion of al-Nathrah, and this is its form]

This is its form: seven dots (• • • • • • •), and its letter is ḥāʾ. When the Moon descends in it, it is cold, mixed, and fortunate. A spiritual being descends into the world through it, by the permission of God, and produces enmity, hatred, separation, and what resembles that. It is suitable for making talismans and supplicating against enemies, tyrants, rebels, and rancor. A spiritual being also moves in it; hasten there with works involving weapons of war and signaling, because it is bad and suitable for works of evil, as we have mentioned. Whoever is born in it will be unlucky. Its incense is costus and pomegranate peel. God knows best.

[Discourse on the mansion of al-Ṭarfah, and this is its form]

This is its form: three dots (• • •), and its letter is ẓāʾ. When the Moon descends in it, it is a continuously unfortunate star. A spiritual being descends into the world through it and indicates what resembles the foregoing. Do not make a talisman in it, devise a craft, enter upon kings, begin with affection, or work on wisdom. [UNCLEAR: the source's instruction concerning a qasam.] Solitude is better than association, and it rejects all works. Whoever is born in it will be unlucky. Its incense is nadd perfume and saffron. God knows best.

[Discourse on the mansion of al-Jabhah, and this is its form]

This is its form: seven dots (• • • • • • •), and its letter is bāʾ. When the Moon descends in it, it is cold and inauspicious, though closer to rectitude. Begin in it with works of affection, works of closeness, and approval. Moving from one place to another is suitable in it. Shaping iron and wearing it are disliked in it. Whoever is born in it will be skilled, happy, and successful, but there will be some cunning and deceit in him. Its incense is seed.
