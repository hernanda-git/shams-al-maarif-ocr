English:
17

The sixth hour belongs to the Moon; it is suitable for travel on land and sea and for every good work you wish. The seventh hour belongs to Jupiter; it is suitable for judicial matters and for meeting people of the pen. The eighth hour belongs to Jupiter; it is suitable for the Sun and for every good work. The ninth hour belongs to Mars; it is suitable for meeting princes, sultans, and rulers. The tenth hour belongs to the Sun; seek your needs from princes and officeholders in it. The eleventh hour belongs to Venus; write in it for acceptance and love. The twelfth hour belongs to Mercury; nothing at all is suitable in it, for it is blameworthy.

(Friday). The first hour belongs to Venus; work in it for incitement, and for the betrothal and marriage of women. The second hour belongs to Mercury; perform all talismans in it and whatever you wish. The third hour belongs to the Moon; do not do anything in it at all, for it is blameworthy and bad. The fourth hour belongs to Saturn; it is suitable for deepening eyes and wells and what resembles that. The fifth hour belongs to Jupiter; write in it for the acceptance of women, notables, and others. The sixth hour belongs to the Sun; write in it for meeting sultans and fulfilling needs. The seventh hour belongs to Venus; work in it for incitement, and for the betrothal and marriage of women. The eighth hour belongs to Mercury; perform all other works in it, for they succeed and are completed. The ninth hour belongs to the Moon; work in it for separation and moving, for it is quick in response. The tenth hour belongs to Saturn. The eleventh hour belongs to Jupiter. The twelfth hour belongs to Mars; travel in it and do whatever you wish in it.

(Saturday). The first hour belongs to Saturn; work in it for whatever acceptance and love you wish. The second hour belongs to Jupiter; write in it for reconciliation between people. The third hour belongs to Mars; work in it for hatred and works of evil. The fourth hour belongs to the Sun; enter upon kings in it and fulfill your needs from them. The fifth hour belongs to Venus. The sixth hour belongs to Mercury; write in it for hunting. (And know) that [UNCLEAR: the source's phrase beginning “whoever knows from it therein” is incomplete]. Here I have explained to you what people discuss in this science, so that working with it from these aspects may be easier for you, and I have set down for you a table from which you can know the fiery, earthy, airy, and watery signs. If the Moon is in the fiery signs, then do for it what accords with it from the works of fire, and likewise with the remaining signs. Know what has come to you; this is its form. If a seeker of a need comes to you on any day, write his name, his mother's name, and the name of his object in separate letters, and arrange the third according to its element. If it is in a fiery, earthy, airy, or watery sign, perform for him a work that accords with it; otherwise, delay the work until the Moon enters the sign that is sought. If that time accords, its owner will be fortunate. Know that, and God knows best.

(And this is a great rule for knowing the Moon's sign.) Add what has elapsed from the Arabic month and add five to it; then give each sign the notation hāʾ hāʾ, beginning from the Sun's sign. Wherever the count is exhausted, that is the Moon's sign, the sign from which it derives. God knows best.
