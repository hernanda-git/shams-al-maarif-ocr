English:
— 481 —

"…the attributes and the descriptions — I ask You by the permanence of Your life and the eternality of the existence of Your Essence and the everlastingness of Your attributes, that You make me travel the paths of the special ones of the servants and the truthful of the saints, and that You make me with the eminent, the chosen; and revive for me, O Living, before every living one, and O Sustainer, the One standing by the management of the existents from the worlds and the creatures from every world. I ask You that You provide me what You apportioned for me in Your knowledge, without hardship and the movement of the moving ones and the stillness of the still ones, and You made everything in a rank of the oppositions and the equivalences from every silent and speaking. I ask You by the secret of the Sustenance in the existents and the power of the bringing-forth in the hidden knowns and the encompassing of the efficacy of the power in the Dominion and the Kingdom, that You establish me by Your obedience in everything that turns from me the darkness of the materialists, and unveil to me the secret of the Sustenance, and raise me to the heart-connections. O Allah, O Living, O Sustainer.

[Section on His name, God Most High, al-Qayyūm] Know that al-Qayyūm is an exaggeration of al-Qiyām and al-Qā'im, and al-Qayyūm is He by whom every existent stands, so the things are not conceived except by His eternal existence; and al-Qayyūm — for His subsistence is by His Essence, and the subsistence of everything is by His worlds. And know that the manifestation of this name — His divine manifestation — does not appear but in the Hereafter, for His outward is a circle that appeared in existence, and He established the worlds of the Dominion of the heavens and the earth upon the world of the Dominion by His Sustenance and the management of the states by His Sustenance — and that is a specificity; and He established the intellects and established the world of the Dominion and established the natural disposition and took the covenant and established the bodies and the spirits and the Garden and the Fire. And the likeness of that is what Allah established of the Essence of the station and the witnessed station and the witnessing — they stood by the gathering, and the gathering stood by the days and the days by the hours and the hours by the degrees and the degrees by the minutes and they are the seconds, and so on. And al-Qayyūm is of the subtletics of the worlds in the Essence of the soul of the soul, so the law stood by it; so the clinging stood, the drop stood, the drop stood the clinging, the bones by the muscles and the muscles by the ligaments and the ligaments by the membranes and the membranes by the web and the web by the veins and the veins by the flesh and the flesh by the blood and the flesh by His Sustenance — and it is an inventive attribute; and by the food the body stood, and the body for the water and the water by the mercy and the mercy is an attribute, for He is the Generous; and the gathered of that standing is the human — so the human stands by his worlds, and therefore the deeds stood by the knowledge and the knowledge stood by the search and the highest search stood by the realization of the circles of the world over its states and the rulings of its acts by the circles of his station — the secret of His Sustenance, so the name al-Qayyūm appears in the abode of the Hereafter over the secret that He deposited in the Footstool of the secret of the Sustenance, bearing the heavens and the earth and who is in them by the secret of the Sustenance that Allah deposited in it. And know that the knowledge of the names of Allah the Great is of the noblest of the sciences, and it is like the hidden pearl; and the difference of the scholars in the meaning of the Greatest Name is upon three views: the first — that to the Greatest Name belongs every name that is answered at the constraint; the second — that the most important of what Allah mentioned in it are sayings, so among them who said it is al-Jalālah and it is the most correct, and among them who said it is Dhū al-Jalāl wa-al-Ikrām, and among them who said it is al-Laṭīf, and among them who said it is Salām, a saying of a Merciful Lord, and among them who said it is al-Ḥannān al-Mannān Dhū al-Jalāl wa-al-Ikrām, and among them who said it is the first of iron [al-ḥadīd], and among them who said it is the last of the gathering [al-ḥashr], and among them who said it is al-Wadūd, and among them who said it is in Sūrat al-Ḥajj in His saying exalted 'And those who emigrated in the path of Allah then were killed or died — He will surely provide for them a good provision', and among them who said it is the first of the luminous letters of the chapters, and among them who said it is al-Māni', and among them who said it is the word of al-Jalālah when you repeat it, and among them who said His name is al-'Alīm, and among them who said it is al-'Alī al-'Aẓīm, and among them who said it is the testimony that there is no god but Allah; and reports multiplied by authentic narrations, and the hadith narrated from him (peace be upon him): 'Al-ẒABĀYĀ, Dhū al-Jalāl wa-al-Ikrām' — and it is a decisive proof. And this name was mentioned in the Syriac language by authentic reports.
