عذاب تصوير لانقلاب وسلب قوى الأجساد الحقيقي فتعكس الإرادات من نظر الحقيقة إلى
باطن الحكمة المغذية فيلقى إليه ذوات الصور الباطنية فمهما أحبه شيء منها طمحت إرادته إليها لينقلب
منها آنياً فيتحد فيه العذاب فيضاعف ما رزت له من ظاهر الصور حتى توفي منه تلك الصور حقيقتها
فيفصل منه حينئذ رجع إلى القوة المغذية فيشهد صورة أخرى فتضاعف له من الحسن فيها أضعافاً
فتطمح إرادته لها فينعكس عليه فيجد من تضاعف الآلام والعذاب وأنواع المخازي ما يتمنى عدم
رؤيتها فيبقى منها ما شاء الله من حكم كشافتها ولطافتها حتى توافي جميع تلك الصور الكرسي جميعاً
وذلك في سر قوله تعالى: {كُلَّمَا نَضِجَتْ جُلُودُهُم بَدَّلْنَاهُمْ جُلُودًا غَيْرَهَا لِيَذُوقُوا الْعَذَابَ} والجلود مائدة
عليهم بحسب القوة المغذية والتبديل لذوات الصور عائد على التنزيل الرباني لينقل تلك الصور
فهي ذات حسن من حيث وضعها في الكرسي وإنما تضاعف أعداد عذابها بتضاعف هيئات
حسنها والعذاب عائد عليه الانقلاب الرباني في الحسن باق في ملكات المعذبة قال تعالى: {فَضُرِبَ
بَيْنَهُم بِسُورٍ لَّهُ بَابٌ بَاطِنُهُ فِيهِ الرَّحْمَةُ وَظَاهِرُهُ مِن قِبَلِهِ الْعَذَابُ} وإذا أردت أن تشهد حقيقة الحسن
المطلق الواصل كيف انقلب إلى عين القبح المبعد وهذا هو ليس الانقلاب في ذاته ولا في صفاته ولا
في أفعاله وإنما هي مرآة الوجود الأول لمن يوجد الإحسان جميلاً وعلم العدل سر القبضة اليسرى
فكل رحمة فضل وكل نقمة عدل ولترجع للمانع عليه تحقيقاً وأشرنا بلطائف الفكر إليه لحظ
الانعكاس من حيث الأوصاف لا من حيث الذوات من نور العلي الأعلى إخباراً عن السر المطلق
الحق المبين النور الهادي للصراط المستقيم السر الأعلى والقسطاس الأسنى كتابه العزيز حيث قال:
{قُلْ هُوَ لِلَّذِينَ آمَنُوا هُدًى وَشِفَاءٌ} إلى قوله: {بَعِيدٍ} مفهوم ذلك قل هو للذين آمنوا هدى في دار الملك وهو
عليهم عمى في دار البرزخ أولئك ينادون من الصور التشكيكات المعذبات من مكان بعيد لوقر
آذانهم فإذا وفي بجميع الصور تعاقبت ذواتها باختلاف أجناسها وتجديد عذابها سلبت عنه قوة
النظرية المصورة واضمحلت عليه الصور الثانية من العذاب وهو عذاب الفيض الثالث
وهو نسبة الكرسي الأبهى وهو عذاب أول مراتب الأعداد المتصلة بأول أطراف الحروف وهو
عذاب الروح المعبر عنه بالطول النفساني وذلك أنه تقدم الكلام الرباني والنظر الأبهى واختلاع
حلة التزكية وهو أشد العذاب فيقدم الكلام بسبق النطق وتقدم النظر ويقدم
التزكية بانعدام فهم إدراكات مطلقة ونيران محرقة إن عطش من شدة الوهج وأما الحروف
السفلية فتقطع أمعاءهم وهم دينية ويطمعون إن جاعوا ويلبسون إن عروا من ظلمة وخشية
إلى أن يمتثل أمره العلي وحكمه الوفي في نسبة من قال منهم من عدم وفيهم في العوالم الثلاثة ولهم
عذاب أليم حينئذ يرتفع الحجاب من عين البصيرة الإنسانية فيظهر له أسرار الحروف الملكية
وحقيقة الأعداد الملكوتية وعين الحياة أعني الحوض المكرم المخصوص بالنبي عليه السلام هذا
إن أردنا في العالم البرزخي وإلا فالحكم يتبعه والعذاب يقطعه والتعداد يقبضه والحصر يشخصه
إلى يوم البعثة الجمعية * وأما ما ذكرناه من مر اللطائف القائمة بالكتائف فإنهم سر حقيقة
ذلك تعلمها وحقاً من عرف نفسه فقد عرف ربه ولا شيء أشرف من معرفة الله فافهم سر هذا

Torment of forming, for inversion and the stripping away of the actual powers of bodies: the wills are turned back from the vision of truth to the interior of the nourishing wisdom, and the essences of the inward forms are cast toward him. Whenever something among them loves him, his will aspires toward it, so that he may be inverted from it instantly; thus torment is united within him. Then what [UNCLEAR: the words mā razat do not yield a secure meaning] for him from the outward forms is multiplied, until those forms fully render their reality from him. It is then separated from him [UNCLEAR: what is separated is not specified]; at that point he returns to the nourishing faculty and witnesses another form. Its beauty is multiplied for him many times over, and his will aspires toward it; then it is inverted upon him. He experiences multiplied pains and torment and kinds of humiliation such that he wishes he had not seen it. Of these there remains whatever God wills, under the rule of their [UNCLEAR: kashāfatihā, perhaps a damaged word for density] and their subtlety, until all those forms reach the Seat, all together. This is in the secret of the saying of God Most High: {Whenever their skins are burnt through, We replace them with other skins, so that they may taste the torment}. The skins are a table spread upon them, according to the nourishing faculty. The replacement of the essences of the forms refers back to the lordly descent, to transfer those forms. They possess beauty by virtue of their placement in the Seat; the numbers of their torments are multiplied only through the multiplication of the configurations of their beauty. Torment returns upon him; the lordly inversion in beauty remains in the faculties of the tormented [UNCLEAR: the connection between these clauses is uncertain]. God Most High said: {Then a wall was erected between them, with a gate; within it is mercy, and outside it, on its side, is torment}. If you wish to witness the reality of the absolute beauty that unites, how it became inverted into the very ugliness that distances: this is not an inversion in His essence, nor in His attributes, nor in His actions. Rather, it is the mirror of the first existence for whoever finds beneficence beautiful, and knowledge of justice is the secret of the left Handful. Every mercy is bounty, and every punishment is justice. And let there be a return to the Preventer, upon him, in realization [UNCLEAR: the reference and connection of “upon him” are uncertain]. Through the subtleties of thought we have pointed toward it: the observation of inversion in respect of descriptions, not in respect of essences, from the light of the High, the Most High, as a report concerning the Absolute Secret, the Clear Truth, the Light guiding to the straight path, the Highest Secret and the Most Exalted Balance, His Mighty Book, where He said: {Say: It is guidance and healing for those who believe}, up to His saying: {far}. The meaning of this is: say, it is guidance for those who believe in the abode of dominion, and it is blindness for them in the abode of Barzakh. Those are called from the doubting, tormenting forms, from a far place, because of the heaviness in their ears. When all the forms have been fully undergone, their essences succeeding one another with their different kinds and the renewal of their torment, the form-producing faculty of vision is stripped from him, and the second forms of torment disappear for him. This is the torment of the third effusion, and it is the relation of the Most Splendid Seat. It is the torment of the first ranks of the numbers connected with the first extremities of the letters. It is the torment of the spirit, expressed as the length of the soul. That is because the lordly speech, the most splendid vision, and the stripping off of the robe of purification have preceded; this is the most severe torment. Speech is brought forward through the precedence of utterance and the advancement of vision, and purification is brought forward through the absence of understanding of absolute perceptions, and burning fires, if he is thirsty from the intensity of the blaze [UNCLEAR: the relation of the fires and this condition to the preceding clauses is broken]. As for the lower letters, they cut their intestines, and they are [UNCLEAR: dīniyyah, literally “religious”; its meaning and referent here are uncertain]. They hope if they are hungry, and they are clothed if they are naked, from darkness and fear [UNCLEAR: the phrase “from darkness and fear” has an uncertain connection to the clothing or nakedness], until His High command and His complete judgment are obeyed, in the relation of one who spoke among them, from absence, and within them in the three worlds [UNCLEAR: the connection between “one who spoke among them,” “from absence,” and “within them” is broken]; and theirs is a painful torment. Then the veil is lifted from the eye of human insight, and there appear to him the secrets of the royal letters, the reality of the numbers of Malakūt, and the spring of life: I mean the honored Pool reserved for the Prophet, peace be upon him. This is if we intend the world of Barzakh; otherwise judgment follows him, torment cuts him off, enumeration seizes him, and confinement individualizes him until the day of the collective Resurrection. * As for what we have mentioned of the [UNCLEAR: marr, possibly “passage,” but its meaning here is uncertain] of the subtle realities subsisting in the [UNCLEAR: katāʾif, as written with t; the intended word is uncertain], they are the secret of the reality of that; learn it.
