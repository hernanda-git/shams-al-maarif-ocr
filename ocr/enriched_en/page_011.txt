English:
5

Uses of the letters and their retreats upon the sentences and the details (the thirty-ninth chapter) in the explanation of the Beautiful Names of God
as they came by way of elucidation and details (the fortieth chapter) in the single supplications by which one is supplicated in
all the nights and times

(The first chapter concerning the dotted letters and what is in them of secrets and hidden meanings)

I say, and with God is success and guidance, the aims of the seekers have been divided into two parts, worldly and otherworldly, and each
one of them is divided into sections according to the purposes. And people have spoken concerning the opposition of the times and the standing upon the stars
and the exercises and the acts of talismans before the setting down of this book and the discourse upon it. And this science is expansive; many
creatures have desired it, and upon it especially whoever found for that a great effect. So I desired to oppose that by a description that proceeds in the manner of the sense in the aspects of
the people of this science. And the early sages spoke concerning it, and many people agreed to that saying. So those, if they are many
in the world, harm in the Hereafter. And this which I mention to you, you benefit by it in the world and the Hereafter.
(Chapter concerning the mention of the dotted letters: are they the roots of speech and its foundation, and by them its building is raised)
(And know) that for numbers there are secrets just as for letters there are effects, and that the upper world extends to the lower world; so the world of the Throne extends to the world of
the Kursi, and the world of the Kursi extends to the sphere of Saturn, and the sphere of Saturn extends to the sphere of Jupiter, and the sphere of Jupiter extends to the sphere of Mars, and the sphere of Mars
extends to the sphere of the Sun, and the sphere of the Sun extends to the sphere of Venus, and the sphere of Venus extends to the sphere of Mercury, and the sphere of Mercury extends to the sphere of the Moon,
and the sphere of the Moon extends to the sphere of heat, and the sphere of heat extends to the sphere of moisture, and the sphere of moisture extends to the sphere of cold, and the sphere of
cold extends to the sphere of dryness, and the sphere of dryness extends to the sphere of air, and the sphere of air extends to the sphere of water, and the sphere of water extends to the sphere of
earth, and the sphere of earth extends to the sphere of Saturn. In the upper things is the letter jim, and the numbers falling upon it are three
in general; and as for in detail, then fifty-three, thus the mim with four and the ba with three. And it
also is with three letters, and for it among the lower things is the letter sad, and it is in number ninety, and those in the upper things in general are five,
and it is the letter ha. And for it among the fivefold accords and the sphere of Jupiter is six, and it is the letter waw. And for it among the sixfold accords
(And as for) the inflection of the sphere of Venus, for it is the letter zay, and for it among the sevenfold accords. And as for the inflection of the sphere of Mercury, for it is
among the number eight, and it is the letter ha, and for it among the eightfold accords. (And as for) the inflection of the sphere of the Moon, for it is among the number
nine, and it is the letter ta, and for it among the ninefold accords. And Saturn has the triangle famous among the scholars, so understand that.
(Chapter concerning the relation of the human essence)
So the Throne has the letter alif, and the Kursi has the letter ba, and Saturn has the letter jim, and thus to the Moon as has preceded.
(Chapter) And the letters are of kinds: among them is that by which one begins on the right, and they are the letters of the Arabs; and among them is that by which one begins
on the left, and they are the Roman, the Greek, and the Coptic. And every writing on the right is connected, and every writing on the left
is separate, so understand that. (And the letters) are twenty-eight, other than lam-alif, and it is the completion of the twenty-nine, and that is
the number of the lunar mansions. And when the mansions, from which fourteen appear above the earth, were such, these letters
among them are what is assimilated with the lam of the definition, and they are fourteen letters: t th d dh r z s sh s d t z l n
