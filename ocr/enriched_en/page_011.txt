English:
5

Applications of the letters and their retreats in general and in detail (the thirty-ninth chapter) in the explanation of the Beautiful Names of God
as set out by way of explanation and detail (the fortieth chapter) in the individual supplications recited throughout
all the nights and times

(The first chapter concerning the dotted letters and what is in them of secrets and hidden meanings)

I say, and with God is success and guidance, the aims of the seekers have been divided into two parts, worldly and otherworldly, and each
one of them is divided into sections according to the purposes. And people have spoken concerning the opposition or correspondence of times and the observation of the stars
and the exercises and the acts of talismans before the setting down of this book and the discourse upon it. And this science is expansive; many people
have desired it, especially those who found it to have a great effect. So I wanted to address this with an account that operates like a form of perception in the practices of
those versed in this science. And the early sages spoke concerning it, and many people agreed to that saying. If such practices multiply
in this world, they harm in the Hereafter. What I mention to you will benefit you in this world and the Hereafter.
(Chapter concerning the mention of the dotted letters: are they the roots of speech and its foundation, and by them its building is raised)
(And know) that for numbers there are secrets just as for letters there are effects, and that the upper world supplies the lower world; so the world of the Throne supplies the world of
the Kursi, and the world of the Kursi supplies the sphere of Saturn, and the sphere of Saturn supplies the sphere of Jupiter, and the sphere of Jupiter supplies the sphere of Mars, and the sphere of Mars
supplies the sphere of the Sun, and the sphere of the Sun supplies the sphere of Venus, and the sphere of Venus supplies the sphere of Mercury, and the sphere of Mercury supplies the sphere of the Moon,
and the sphere of the Moon supplies the sphere of heat, and the sphere of heat supplies the sphere of moisture, and the sphere of moisture supplies the sphere of cold, and the sphere of
cold supplies the sphere of dryness, and the sphere of dryness supplies the sphere of air, and the sphere of air supplies the sphere of water, and the sphere of water supplies the sphere of
earth, and the sphere of earth supplies the sphere of Saturn. In the upper realm, the letter is jīm, and its number is three in general;
in detail it is fifty-three: mīm is four and bāʾ is three. It also consists of three letters; in the lower realm it is the letter ṣād, whose number is ninety, while its general number
in the upper realm is five, represented by hāʾ. Its fivefold wafq is associated with the sphere of Jupiter; its number is six, represented by wāw, and it has a sixfold wafq
The operation of the sphere of Venus uses the letter zāy and a sevenfold wafq. The operation of the sphere of Mercury has number eight,
represented by ḥāʾ, and an eightfold wafq. The operation of the sphere of the Moon has number nine, represented by ṭāʾ,
and a ninefold wafq. Saturn has the triangle well known among scholars; understand this.
(Chapter concerning the correspondence of the human essence)
So the Throne has the letter alif, and the Kursi has the letter ba, and Saturn has the letter jim, and so on to the Moon, as stated above.
(Chapter) And the letters are of kinds: some begin on the right—the Arabic letters—and some begin
on the left—the Roman, Greek, and Coptic letters. And every writing on the right is connected, and every writing on the left
is separate, so understand that. The letters number twenty-eight, excluding lām-alif; together they complete twenty-nine, the number of the lunar mansions.
Since fourteen of the mansions appear above the earth, these letters
include fourteen that assimilate with the lām of the definite article: tāʾ, thāʾ, dāl, dhāl, rāʾ, zāy, sīn, shīn, ṣād, ḍād, ṭāʾ, ẓāʾ, lām, nūn
