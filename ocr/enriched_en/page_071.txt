the Inward, beneath whom there is no one. Since the combination consisted of fourteen letters, the seven heavens and the seven earths, and all the dominion and spiritual kingdom within and between them, were sustained by the secret of God—exalted be His mention—in every particle of the world and in what is smaller than them, through something of the secrets of the Name of God Most High. Through that secret, testimony was borne to His oneness. God Most High said: {Do you know of anyone bearing His name?} God Most High also said: {Say, ‘God,’ then leave them playing in their vain discourse.} The shaykh, imam, scholar and erudite master Fakhr al-Dīn al-Khwārazmī—may God sanctify his spirit—said in the sanctuary of Mecca in the year 670: Whoever knows God Most High through His Name in their state and speech has known the Greatest Name particular to them. Thus He was the Most Merciful of the merciful to Ayyūb, peace be upon him, when Ayyūb said: {My Lord, affliction has touched me, and You are the Most Merciful of the merciful.} Likewise, He was the Bestower to Sulaymān, peace be upon him, when Sulaymān said: {My Lord, forgive me and bestow upon me a kingdom that will belong to no one after me. You are indeed the Bestower.} Likewise, He was the Best of Inheritors to Zakariyyā, peace be upon him: God gave him Yaḥyā, gave Sulaymān a mighty kingdom, and cured Ayyūb of his affliction. Whoever knows the Name corresponding to their need and asks God Most High by it, God answers them and brings them to their desired goal. Some shaykhs, when their student came to them wishing to undertake the spiritual path, would seat the student before them and recite the ninety-nine Names, looking at the student's face as they mentioned the Names, until the Name suited to the student became clear to the shaykh. The shaykh would then command the student to persevere in that Name until a door opened from it for the student; for his unique name is in it, and through it influence operates upon everyone other than him. This is his purpose. Knowledge of the Greatest Name of God is among the noblest sciences. The Greatest Name is the hidden pearl, protected from those who are not its people.
