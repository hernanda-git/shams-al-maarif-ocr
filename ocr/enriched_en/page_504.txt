( فصل في اسمه تعالى المقسط )
هو الذي ينصف المظلوم من الظالم ويدكدك الظلم في نحره أو يرضي مظلومًا من ظالم وذلك في غاية العدل
والإنصاف ولا يقدر على ذلك إلا الله تعالى ومنه ما يروى عن النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم إذ هو جالس فضحك
حتى بدت نواجذه فقال عمر بن الخطاب رضي الله تعالى عنه بأبي وأمي ما أضحكك يا رسول الله فقال صلى
الله عليه وسلم رجلان من أمتي وقفا بين يدي البارئ فقال أحدهما يا رب خذ مظلمتي من هذا فقال الله

English:
— 498 —

[The great square of MĀLIK AL-MULK — the Greatest Name (al-Ism al-A'ẓam) — a 25-by-25 magic square, reproduced verbatim in the Arabic layer above. The header row is the letters l ك dh و l ج repeated across 25 columns; each following row shifts the sequence by one place to the right, with the remaining cells filled by l, forming the spiral of the Name. This is the figure referenced at the end of the preceding page (— 497 —).]

(Section on His name, God Most High, al-Muqsiṭ) He is He who gives justice to the wronged from the wrong-doer and crushes the wrong in his throat, or satisfies a wronged one from a wrong-doer — and that is in the utmost justice and equity, and none is capable of that but God Most High. And of it is what is related from the Prophet (may Allah bless him and grant him peace): that he was sitting and laughed until his molars showed, so 'Umar ibn al-Khaṭṭāb (may Allah be pleased with him) said 'May my father and mother be ransomed for you — what made you laugh, O Messenger of Allah?' So he (may Allah bless him and grant him peace) said: 'Two men of my nation stood before the Originator…' [page ends]
