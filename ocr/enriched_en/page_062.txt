(A chapter) And you have gathered for God the supplications and the names, and whatever you need of Him, so the binding is honey; and do not question the covenant.
Indeed I have brought you a pure egg. So when you are in the season of spring and you desire the lord of the ruin, then call al-Ṭāqūfa, saying: In the name
of God, the Merciful, the Compassionate — I swear by you, Yā Ṭayā’īl, and your helpers: my spirit in His hand, and Ṭāḥūl, and the winds, and whatever was made
lawful, and Maysūr, and whatever Waṭish is, and upon the sun and the moon, and what surrounds — the name of God — and by His mighty name, Lord of the Hereafter and the first,
who has no end and no terminus, what is in the heavens and what is in the earth and what is between them and what is beneath the soil. God is great, is the subduer of enemies, ever
bestowing favours, merciful to the merciful, powerful and not vanquished, subduing and not subdued, equitable on the Day of Gathering and of Resurrection. There is no god but He,
the Knowing, the Wise, the Merciful, the Compassionate. He is God, there is no god but He, the King, the Holy — to the last of them. O God, I ask You,
O Lord of the worlds, by Your perfect Name — O Everliving, O Self-Subsisting — I bear witness that everything other than God is null. I have believed in You; there is no god but You; there is no lord
besides You, by Your Great Name which You have preferred over all Your names, that You subject to me the lord of the supplication and the lord of
al-Ṭāqūfa and the four quarters, to be my helpers in the granting of my need, by Your permission. O God, it is You who grants and none is
granted against You. Answer, O assemblies of spirits, and grant my need by the right of Him who has might and majesty, and by the right of the Truth, who
does not die, than whom nothing is like, the Self-Subsisting whose name is not forgotten, whose light is not extinguished, whose Throne does not cease and whose Seat does not move,
who sent down the Scripture to His servant. I ask You, O God, 3, there is no god but He, Master of the world and the Hereafter, I ask You to grant
my need and to subject to me the spiritual servants of these Names. Surely You are able over all things.

And when you are in the season of autumn,
then call the lord of al-Ṭāqūfa, saying: In the name of God, the Merciful, the Compassionate — I swear by you, Yā Dānā’īl, and by your helpers: Yā Ḥayā’īl
and Ḥaramā’īl, and Sam’a’īl, and by the winds: al-Qadḥ, Taf‘ahūn, Mardūd, and ‘Ārūd, and by the sun and the moon: Mā Ḥūd
and Sādūn. In the name of God, the Merciful, the Compassionate; there is no might nor power except with God Most High, the Mighty. O God, I ask You by the fact that You are living,
not dying; prevailing and not vanquished; creating and not created; seeing and not doubted; hearing and not deaf; subduing and not subdued; or Ṣamad, not fed,
and Self-Subsisting, not sleeping; faithful and not defaulting; just and arbitrating, not wronging; independent and not in need; a treasure, not exhausted; forgiving and not hastening; known
and not disowned; single and not made two; bestowing and not refusing; swift, not distracted and not straying; everlasting and not wearing out; responsive and not wearying; enduring and not perishing,
and single, with nothing like You; and mighty, not to be outstripped. O God, I ask You, O Everliving, O Hearing, there is no god but You, O Lord of the worlds, I ask You
by Your might, that You grant my need and that You subject to me all the spiritual beings, by God the Great and by Your hidden Name and by
the right of Your majesty and the light of Your face, that this is easy for You. I swear by you, by God the Great and by His hoarded Name, that you will be helpers
to me in the granting of my need, doing nothing but what I command you; and by the right of the Great Names of God Most High: hasten, hurry,
at once, this very hour. May God bless you.
