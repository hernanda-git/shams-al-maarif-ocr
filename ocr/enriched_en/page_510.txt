English:

"…Your subjugated power of the pens by the ruler of Your encompassing, ancient knowledge, of the manifest and the hidden command from the intended, and the decree and the benefit and the harm — that You give me the benefit of everything and ease for me the causes of the obediences by what connects me by them to the unions. O Revealer of the distresses and the griefs, O Possessor of the favor and the beneficence and the graces, O Allah, O Benefiter.' (Section on His name, God Most High, al-Nūr) (In the name of Allah the Merciful, the Compassionate) Know that the manifest light which appeared to you in the appearance — for the manifest in itself is the manifester of other-than-it, is named 'light'. And when it faced existence with non-existence, it was not ambiguous for the appearance of existence — for the darknesses of darknesses are from non-existence, and in existence is a light overflowing in the essence of existence from the light of its Essence and the names of its attributes and the reality of its acts — so He is the light of the heavens and the earth, more effaced than His light in the heavens and its lowest. And know that the light is of two kinds: sensible and meaning. The sensed is the light of the eyesight, and Allah deposited in it the lesson, as He deposited for the possessors of insight in the eyes of their hearts the passing of the management and the lesson — so it appeared upon the sense of the eyesight, and that is the passing of the empowerment of the flowing light upon them, and He is the one whose realities of the world of the subtle do not stand except by a known [light] from any aspect, on any kind — whether an intellectual or legal wayfaring; and the reality of the appearance of the wisdom and the witnessing of the servitude is as the purification of the lordship. And His light divides into eight divisions: the light of the heart, the light of faith, the light of the soul, the light of the spirit, the light of the intellect, the light of the secret, the light of the breast, and the light of the unveiling — so these are eight lights; and for each of these lights is a passing of other-than-thing, for they are all Throne-realms, and from them the eight who bear the Throne in His saying exalted 'And eight bear the Throne of your Lord above them that day' — and they are the bearers of the Throne of the Raḥmān. So the light of the heart is drawn from the light of faith, as if the faith is from the light of the attributes — so who the faith-light overflowed upon before the legal obligations and the witnessing commands, and from it His saying and the helpers and from the perceivers because of the generosity of God Most High in His noble Book by His saying exalted 'Indeed in that are signs for the perceivers.' And when the eyes of their hearts turn by the faith-light, Allah unveils to them the knowledge of the angel in sum and detail; then they perceive the world of their composition and what God Most High deposited in their states — so every particle in it, according to the difference of its kinds, they see as a light in the eye, and it is the reality standing by a light from the lights of God Most High, and it is the light of its standing by the light that connects to the light. So this is approached by it the movement of the part and the nearness from the earth; nay, they see by His name al-Nūr the burning of the walls as the burning of the light of the sun — and this is seen in his heart and body as light, and the boiling of the soul from the light of the spirit. So who sets aright his soul upon the composition by the obedience and the purification from the darknesses of the natures and the turbidities of the habits, until her light faces the light of the spirit from God Most High by the absorption of the witnessing in the Garden — and this is what Allah unveils to him by His Essence and His spirit by a light from the lights of the realities of the knowledge of the Jabarūt, which is its subtlety from the world of the dominion and the Dominion; in it suffices the secrets of the spirits and the souls that prevail to the divinity freely, and he witnesses the subtleties of the disposal of God Most High in the existents by the states of the noble angels according to the difference of their kinds in the ascension of the pure word, which is the generosity of God Most High from His saying of the rememberers 'light by the rising and the settling,' and the light of the intellect from the boiling of the secret of the straightness upon the knowledge of Him and the Knower of Him and the Faller from other-than-Him, until he looks by the face of the secret and witnesses the Dominion-realms, and hastens by the rising of the sciences and the lower and the partial and the universal by the one word — a degree below a degree, and a reality below a reality; so he saw it in the state from where, and in the detail from where… [page ends]
