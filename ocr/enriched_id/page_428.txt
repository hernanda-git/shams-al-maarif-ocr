إلا بها، وتمامها محمد رسول الله ١٢ حرفا تمام دائرة النهار، وقد كملت الحكمة بتمام الرحمة، فمن قال
لا إله إلا الله محمد رسول الله باشتراط ما ذكرناه فقد أخلص في التوحيد، وهي أفضل ما قاله النبيون

ذوات العرش كان من شأنهم فيه بالصورة المكتوبة بالنور الأبيض والأخضر، وهما السطران

إلى مقام الارتقاء حصل له ذلك، وروي عنه عليه السلام أنه قال: من قال لا إله إلا الله غفر له، وعنه أيضا
من كان آخر كلامه من الدنيا لا إله إلا الله، ومن كان له حاجة مهمة يلزم الخلوة ويجمع قلبه ثم

English:
— 222 —

"…and the growth is magnified and the good deeds multiplied — this in sum; and in detail: Allah placed, of His hidden gentleness, what He deposited in the governance of the world by the day, and prepared and arranged it upon 12 hours — the secret of the day, and from it the secret of the night; then He perfected by His gentle ruling, and made 3 hours the secret of summer, 3 of autumn, 3 of winter, and 3 by the secret of spring — and this time governs it. And these letters resting upon monotheism, which are the result of the God of gods and the Subsistence — 'there is no god but the Subsisting' — and the human world is composed of movement and stillness, and there must be their necessitation and the unveiling of their outward; so He made for it the night for the existence of its secret and its return to the real world by the secret of the act and the sending and the elevation of the spirits and the ascent of the intellects and the repose of humanity under the dominion of darkness; so He made the governance of the night 12 hours, for each letter an hour — and when he says 'There is no god but Allah,' the monotheism is not completed except by them; and its completion 'Muḥammad is the Messenger of Allah' is 12 letters, the completion of the circle of the day; and the wisdom was perfected by the completion of mercy. So whoever says 'There is no god but Allah, Muḥammad is the Messenger of Allah' with the condition we mentioned has been sincere in monotheism, and it is the best of what the prophets said, as in the noble hadith. And know that the twenty-four letters in their correspondence are 24 worlds, for each world gathered in the Alif; and the image of the letters preceded, and that this word was the reality of the upper and lower knowledge, and its relation in the essences of the Throne — it was their wont in it by the image written with white and green light, and they are the two lines expressed by 'There is no god but Allah, Muḥammad is the Messenger of Allah'; and these two lines written with light faced the Throne and are in it. And it is related that when the servant says 'There is no god but Allah,' there comes out of his mouth a column of light ascending under the Throne and glorifying until the Day of Resurrection — and this is a witness of infinitude in the dominion, and ascension in the Dominion, and rising in the Tremendousness, nothing of the realities closes and stands before it. He said exalted: 'To Him ascends the good word, and the righteous deed raises it.' And it is related that whoever says 'There is no god but Allah' a thousand times each day in complete purity, Allah eases for him the causes of provision at its source; and likewise whoever says it at his sleep a thousand times, his soul passes the night under the Throne; and whoever says it at the forenoon of noon with the sunrise, weakens the satan of his soul; and whoever says it at the sight of the crescent is safe from ailments and pains; and whoever says it with gathered resolve and sends it to a wrongdoer or tyrant — he perishes; and whoever says it the mentioned number upon entering a city is safe from its trial; and whoever says it with the intent of learning, to the station of elevation, attains it. And it is related from him (upon him peace) that he said: 'Whoever says "There is no god but Allah" is forgiven.' And from him also: 'Whoever's last word from the world is "There is no god but Allah"…' And whoever has an important need, let him bind himself to retreat and gather his heart, then say 'There is no god but Allah, alone, with no partner,' and ask what he will of the needs — it is fulfilled. And some said: 'Whoever mentions this number has bought his soul from Allah.' And some of the verifiers said the meaning of 'Is the reward of good aught but good?' is 'There is no god but Allah'; and that the intellect, when thanked, did not travel in the remembrances better than 'There is no god but Allah'; and that the nearness is the knowledge of 'There is no god but Allah.' And 'Uthmān ibn 'Affān (may Allah be pleased with him) said: 'While I was sitting with the Prophet (upon him peace), he gazed toward him, and behold Gabriel (upon him peace) came to him and said: "O Muḥammad, indeed Allah the Exalted commands you with justice and excellence and the testimony that there is no god but Allah" — and when I heard him say it, faith was planted in my heart; and this is the justice.' And he asked him about sincerity, and he said: 'The standing in servitude.' And He said exalted: 'O you who believe, fear Allah and be with the truthful' — i.e. the people of 'There is no god but Allah.' And it is related that all that Allah created of creation and taught them of all knowledge is 'There is no god but Allah,' and the knowledge of the firsts and the lasts is folded in the saying 'There is no god but Allah'; and it is related that all that Allah created of creation and taught them…"

Indonesia:
— ٢٢٢ —

"…pertumbuhan membesar kebaikan berganda — ini ringkasnya; terperinci: Allah letak dari lemah-lembut tersembunyi apa yang ditaruh dalam urus dunia siang, siapkan atur atas 12 jam — rahsia siang, daripadanya rahsia malam; lalu sempurnakan dengan hukum lembut, jadikan 3 jam rahsia panas, 3 gugur, 3 sejuk, 3 rahsia bunga — masa ini urusnya. Huruf-huruf bersandar tauhid, hasil Tuhan tuhan dan Qayyum — 'tiada tuhan melainkan Qayyum' — alam manusia dari gerak diam, mesti ada keperluan dan zahirnya; maka dijadikan malam untuk rahsia dan kembali ke alam hakiki dengan rahsia perbuatan kerasulan naik roh naik akal rehat manusia di bawah kerajaan gelap; tadbir malam 12 jam tiap huruf sejam — bila kata 'Laa ilaha illallah' tauhid tak sempurna melainkan dengannya; lengkapnya 'Muhammad rasulullah' 12 huruf lengkap bulatan siang; hikmah sempurna dengan rahmat. Sesiapa kata 'Laa ilaha illallah Muhammad rasulullah' dengan syarat disebut ikhlas tauhid, ia terbaik kata nabi seperti hadis. 24 huruf berpadan 24 alam, tiap alam kumpul di Alif; imej huruf telah lalu, kalimah ini hakikat ilmu atas bawah, nisbahnya di zat 'Arasy — mereka tulis dengan cahaya putih hijau, dua baris 'Laa ilaha illallah Muhammad rasulullah'; dua baris bercahaya menghadap 'Arasy di dalamnya. Diriwayat bila hamba kata 'Laa ilaha illallah' keluar tiang cahaya naik bawah 'Arasy bertasbih hingga kiamat — saksi tiada berhingga dalam domini, naik Malakut, Tremendousness, tiada hakikat tutup. Firman: 'Kepada-Nya naik kata baik, amal soleh angkatnya.' Barangsiapa kata seribu kali siang dengan suci, Allah mudahkan rezeki; kata waktu tidur seribu kali roh di bawah 'Arasy; kata waktu zohor terbit matahari lemahkan syaitan nafsu; kata nampak anak bulan selamat dari sakit; kata kumpul azam hantar pada zalim/tyran — binasa; kata bilangan masuk bandar selamat fitnah; kata niat belajar naik darjat — dapat. Sabda: 'Sesiapa kata Laa ilaha illallah diampun.' Dan: 'Siapa akhir katanya Laa ilaha illallah…' Sesiapa ada hajat penting, berkhalwat kumpul hati, kata 'Laa ilaha illallah esa tiada sekutu,' minta — tertunai. Ada kata: 'Sesiapa zikir bilangan ini beli jiwanya dari Allah.' Ahli tahqiq kata makna 'Bukankah balasan baik melainkan baik?' ialah 'Laa ilaha illallah'; akal bila disyukuri tak berjalan zikir lebih baik; kedekatan ilmu 'Laa ilaha illallah.' 'Uthman kata: 'Ketika duduk dengan Nabi, dia pandang, Jibril datang kata "Wahai Muhammad, Allah suruh keadilan, ihsan, syahadah tiada tuhan melainkan Allah" — bila aku dengar iman tertanam; ini keadilan.' Tanya ikhlas, kata 'Berdiri dalam hamba.' Firman: 'Wahai beriman, bertakwa dan jadi dengan yang benar' — ahli 'Laa ilaha illallah.' Diriwayat semua makhluk dan ilmu 'Laa ilaha illallah,' ilmu awal akhir lipat dalam 'Laa ilaha illallah'; diriwayat semua makhluk…"
