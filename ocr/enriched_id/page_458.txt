English:
— 452 —

"…from it [the name] is that he be patient until there are opened to him the locks of the difficulties of the godheads and the subtle high beings of the Dominion; and that Allah ease upon his understanding what is hard for creatures from the intimate sciences and the inwardnesses of the messengership and the secrets of the writing. * And know that this name is of the noblest names, and for whoever is adorned by it is the reckoning of his soul and the knowing of how the secret of sincerity is by it; so then Allah opens to him the secrets of the unseens. And the meaning of al-Fattāḥ is in His name al-Wahhāb; and the drawing near to this name [is] the use of the spiritual exercise and the retreat and hunger according to capacity, and recitation by night and day — Allah opens to him in an hour. And of the special properties of this name: if it is written on Friday and carried and the name recited, he witnesses strange things, and its servant TAMAKHĀ'ĪL comes to the one remembering and fulfills his need — and this is its figure. And its mention with the basmala: 'O Allah, You are al-Fattāḥ over the servants by what You will of the locks of the paths, the one who brings through by the secret of Your name al-Fattāḥ, the helper from severe perils, the judge between the servants by the subtleties of wisdom in the high world and the dominion; You rule by what You will and choose among Your creation. I ask You by Your secret flowing in the radiations of the world of the Dominion, sent down in the hidden things of His secret, until You reach the Deity returning in His ascension in the affairs of the world of the Omnipotence; and that You open in my heart, by witnessing, these secrets and realize them by the realities of the lights; and make me worthy of connection by the secret of the life of Your Essence, the favored by the majestic secrets of Your attributes. O Allah, support me by Your mighty, preventing help against every opponent, envier, and rival. O Allah, subordinate to me Your servant TAMAKHĀ'ĪL, servant of this name — indeed You are over all things powerful.' (Section on His name the Exalted, al-'Alīm) Know that the Knower is the one who knows by His making and His perfection, that He encompasses everything, outward and inward, first and last; and this is the attribute of the Creator — exalted be His power. And the knowledge of Allah is not countable, and the knowledge of the creatures — they know it by what He measured for them, that they may find by it the nearnesses to Allah the Exalted. And indeed Allah made the Dominion of the lights and made what He bound by His noble names, and they are established in the Dominion, and every name opposite the other, and they are as the innate disposition. Then He created the Omnipotence and the Dominion and created the angels from the lights of the Throne, for the Throne was created by the names of the Essence by the secret of secrets; and He created the angels of the letters by the lights of the Footstool, for it stood by the names of the deeds. The angels of the Dominion stood charged with disposal, and the angels of the Omnipotence stood charged with governance, and the angels of the Dominion [of lights] stood charged with the governance of the temperaments. And when Allah willed the manifestation of the difference of these worlds by the kinds of their sciences, that His knowledge appear in His wisdom, and His wisdom in His power, and His power in His will, from His ordering — He created Adam, father of mankind, and placed his meanings in the world of his dominion and his body, and made for every name of the names a limb from the limbs. And He taught Adam all that was and that shall be of the names of the existences — exalted — and created what you do not know. Then He created Eve, his wife, from a limb of his limbs, and poured upon him the divine lights and supplied his spirit with the lights of the highs, and made him a vicegerent on earth, and there appeared upon him the names of the attributes and the names of the deeds, and they are the completion of his creation. He said exalted: 'We have indeed created man in the best stature,' then made the Dominion of the intellect and mounted it in him, and he stands by it, and supplied him with these limbs."

Indonesia:
— ٤٥٢ —

"…daripadanya [nama] ialah bersabar hingga dibuka baginya kunci kesulitan ketuhanan dan halus makhluk tinggi Malakut; dan Allah mudahkan fahaman atas apa yang sukar bagi makhluk dari ilmu laduni batin risalah rahsia penulisan. * Ketahui nama ini antara nama mulia, dan sesiapa yang berakhlak dengannya berhitung diri dan mengetahui rahsia ikhlas; maka Allah buka rahsia ghaib. Makna al-Fattāḥ pada nama al-Wahhāb; mendekat nama ini dengan riyadhah khalwat lapar sesuai kapasitas, baca malam siang — Allah buka sejam. Sifat: tulis Jumaat bawa baca, nampak keanehan, khadam TAMAKHĀ'ĪL datang tunaikan hajat — inilah rupanya. Zikir basmalah: 'Ya Allah Engkau al-Fattāḥ atas hamba dengan kehendak kunci jalan, yang meloloskan rahsia nama al-Fattāḥ penolong dari bahaya, hakim antara hamba dengan hikmah alam tinggi domini; Engkau hukum pilih ciptaan. Kuminta rahsia-Mu mengalir cahaya Malakut turun tersembunyi hingga sampai kepada Tuhan kembali naik alam Jabarut; buka hatiku dengan syuhud rahsia ini hakikat cahaya; jadikan aku layak sambung rahsia hidup Zat-Mu yang dimuliakan sifat agung. Ya Allah tolong dengan pertolongan mulia menolak lawan pendengki pesaing. Ya Allah takluk hamba TAMAKHĀ'ĪL khadam nama ini — Engkau Maha Kuasa.' (Fasal al-'Alīm) Ketahui Yang Mengetahui yang tahu dengan ciptaan kesempurnaan meliputi segala zahir batin awal akhir; itulah sifat Pencipta. Ilmu Allah tak terhitung, ilmu makhluk mereka tahu dengan kadar diberi untuk hampir pada Allah. Allah jadikan Malakut cahaya ikat nama mulia tegak di Malakut, tiap nama berpasangan seperti fitrah. Cipta Jabarut Domini malaikat dari cahaya 'Arasy (cipta dengan nama Zat rahsia rahsia) dan malaikat huruf dari cahaya Kursi (nama perbuatan). Malaikat Domini urus pentadbiran, Jabarut urus tadbir, Malakut urus campuran. Bila Allah mahu zahir bezakan alam dengan ilmu, ilmu dalam hikmah kuasa kehendak — cipta Adam bapa insan, letak makna dalam alam jasad, tiap nama satu anggota. Ajarkan Adam semua nama wujud, cipta apa tak diketahui. Cipta Hawa dari anggotanya, limpah cahaya ilahi, siram roh cahaya tinggi, jadikan khalifah bumi, zahir nama sifat perbuatan lengkap ciptaan. Firman 'Kami cipta insan sebaik-baik bentuk,' lalu Malakut akal dipasang tegak siram anggota."
