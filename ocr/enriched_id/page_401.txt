( The Ninth Treatise ) You go to the open and make around you the circle; you sit 5 117 ṭ ' 1 ṭ 9 in it, and throw between your hands some of the ash around the circle from outside, and speak 1999 1001 177 with the words, and write the names on a burāwah and hang it on a pomegranate-stick — and all 1911 1114 ṭ 2 of the wild beasts and creeping things of the open attend you and do not harm you; take of them what you will and leave of them what you will, and dismiss them — you pull the burāwah from the stick. And this is its writing as you see. And the words upon it you say: "Āh ayyuh ayyuh abadan al-waḥā, al-'ajal al-sā'a — may Allah bless you." ( The Tenth Treatise ) You take a knife and write upon it the names of the ash, then take a reed and give it to whom you will to slaughter it with that knife; and when he takes it, speak the words and command him to draw the knife upon it and throw upon the reed some of the ash, and throw it from his hand — and it flies toward the sky like a rooster that crows and cackles and disturbs everyone who sees it. And this is what you write upon the knife on the first face: "this 1111119996111 k 5 wa-fiyāthā hādhā, 99 ṭ ' lā 100111 awj" — and the words upon it you say after throwing the mentioned ash: "Ishār shawshār shārāis asā ashālīsh, ajib wa'jal bikadhā wakadhā — may Allah bless you, and upon you peace." ( The Eleventh Treatise ) You take a copper cup and engrave upon it these names and fill it with water and speak upon it with this speech — and it becomes obedient to what he commanded, 7 ' ṭ. And thus you do with the tablet aḥ l mās, red skin — and this writing is upon the dirhams and dinars (the first face): "92 k wah" (and the second face): "aḥ 111111113 ḥ ṭ 1119111 ḥ" — and the words upon it you say: "Ṣāṣiyā lahr as, ajib wa'jal, and sprinkle upon them the ash — and all we mentioned comes to be; so admonish and verify, and be guided to the clearest path." And in the beginning of the work of Sīmiyā there must be long discipline and fasting and seclusion, and you attain what you will — and Allah is the One who grants success. And may Allah bless our master Muhammad and his family and companions and grant peace.

( Maqalah 8 ) Tulis nama ini pada burāwah gantung pada cawan dengan abu, katanya, campak ke sungai — segala ikan berkumpul; ambil mana jua. Mata tengah baris kedua berhadapan mata baris pertama begini. Katanya: "Jamī'an lillahiyyāh, ajib wa'jal sekian — semoga Allah memberkati, 711 ṭ ṭ ' 11 ṭ — sejahtera atasmu." ( Maqalah 9 ) Pergi padang buat bulatan sekeliling; duduk 5 117 ṭ ' 1 ṭ 9, campak abu luar bulatan, ucap 1999 1001 177, tulis nama burāwah gantung pada dahan delima — segala 1911 1114 ṭ 2 binatang liar datang padamu tak mudarat; ambil tinggal, suruh balik — cabut burāwah dari dahan.
