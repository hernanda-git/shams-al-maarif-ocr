أخرى أذيق بميزاب واجعل القرعة في قدر نحاس ملآن ماء وأوقد تحتها ناراً لينة حتى يصعد

* اعلم أن اسم الحجر عندهم هو مفرد على حسب اختلافهم فمنهم من قال إنه الشعر وهم الأكثرون
وقال آخرون البيضة وقال آخرون الرصاص وآخرون الدم إلى غير ذلك ثم أقول في حال التدبير
لم يكن مختلف فيه قولان ولا أكثر لأن تدبيرهم واحد يوصل البقية الشاملة ومنهم من بسط القول
ومنهم من عمه ومنهم من رمزه ومنهم من خلط في كلامه ونحن نبين إشارة القوم وتضم كل واحد
إلى صاحبه حتى يقع الفهم إلى كل ذي لب وقالوا إن حجرهم فرد كما أن الله فرد أحد ويدخله
التكسر من الفهم ولما أرادوا تطهيره قسموه أجزاء وها أنا أذكرها فكثرت الأجزاء ثم
استكمل جزءاً منها بأشياء كثيرة واتسعت الأجزاء حينئذ نظروه جزءاً منه أولاً فقالوا ذلك ثم
ذكروا أن الجزء ما لم يبيض رقيق على وجهه غبرة كأنها دهنة فسموه ماء المطر وماء الكلب
لأن الحكماء سموا ما سال من حجرهم بحراً ونهراً وعيناً وماء السحاب ومطراً ولبناً ودهناً وخلاً
وبولاً والكل سيال في العالم وكل مركب ثم شدوا النار فقطر ماء أبيض ثقيل براقاً لآلئ يخطف
الأبصار إذا وضع في الماء خيل لك أنه يشق الزجاج لقوة نوره وإن حرك يلمع كلمعان ماء البحر
والظلام فسموا هذا بالزيبق العربي وهو بارد رطب ثم شدوا النار فقطر هذا غليظاً إلى السواد
وهو الزيبق الشرقي حار يابس والصبغ في الطبيعة النارية ولا ينحل إلا بماء الزيبق العربي فإذا
انحلت صارت روحانية صافية صياغة لغيرها وفي الأرض التي لها سريان شرب للتبييض وشرب
للتجمير والأرض والهواء والنار وضده الثلاثة ينحل في الماء الزيبق حتى يصير إبخاراً فرفوراً بإشعاعه
يخطف الأبصار ويدور دوران القدر إذا زالت منه رطوبة الزيبق بالنار اللطيفة وهي الحكمة
التي تراد منه أن يصير ماء واحداً لا يقدر يفصل بعضه من بعض قال مارية إذا رأيت في كتاب
تسميع أو تصيية أو تصدية أو هضم أو تحليل أو تصعيد أو تقطير فإنما هو شيء واحد وقد يقع
في الماء الخالد المقيم فالصانع الزيبق وهو النفس فالنفس تصبغ الروح والروح لا يصبغ

English:

And you have finished all the dye from the soul, so place it in a glass cup with an open mouth, and deposit it in another cucurbit fed by a spout; and place the cucurbit in a copper pot full of water, and kindle beneath it a gentle fire until the dye rises from the water and the dye remains at the bottom of the cup like fire when its flame has gone — so then the perfuming occurs. Then you take of the earth a part, and of the water of life a part, and of the natron a part; and place it in a bottle, and fit upon them another bottle like the silvering, and tighten the joint between them; and place it in a hot sun until the earth dries and drinks all the water. Then open the bottle and add to it the like of the weight of the earth that was put of it; then dry it in the sun until it drinks, then grind it with the water and dry it until it is dry. If you are in the summer season, treat it with the sun; and if you are in the winter season, treat it with the gentle fire like the heat of the sun, until it dries a second time. So you have reached the utmost of the elixir. So grind it and raise it in a bottle, and close its head from dust, and cast a part upon 20 parts — and by Allah is success, and be of the thankful.

( Another chapter from it, on clarification and detail )
* Know that the name of the stone among them is singular according to their difference — so among them are those who said it is the hair, and they are the majority; and others said the egg; and others said the lead; and others the blood, and other than that. Then I say: in the state of management there was not a difference of two sayings nor more, for their management is one, reaching the comprehensive remainder. And among them are those who expanded the saying, and those who were brief, and those who symbolized, and those who mixed in their speech. And we clarify the indication of the people and gather each to his companion, until understanding reaches every possessor of intellect. And they said: their stone is one, as Allah is one, the Unique; and it enters the breaking from understanding. And when they willed to purify it, they divided it into parts — and here I mention them, so the parts multiplied; then they completed a part of them with many things, and the parts widened; then they regarded it first as a part of it, so they said that. Then they mentioned that the part, as long as it is not whitened, a fine dust is upon its face as if it were oil — so they named it rain-water and dog-water, because the sages named what flowed from their stone a sea and a river and a spring and cloud-water and rain and milk and oil and vinegar and urine — and all is fluid in the world and every composite. Then they tightened the fire and there dropped a white heavy water, glistening like pearls, stealing the sight — when placed in water it seems to you it splits the glass for the strength of its light; and if moved it shines like the shining of sea-water and darkness. So they named this "the Arabic quicksilver," and it is cold and moist. Then they tightened the fire and there dropped this, thick to blackness — and it is the eastern quicksilver, hot and dry; and the dye is in the fiery nature, and it does not dissolve except by the water of the Arabic quicksilver. So when it dissolved it became a pure spirituality, a casting for other than it. And in the earth that has flow is a drinking for whitening and a drinking for fumigation; and the earth and air and fire, and its opposite the three, dissolve in the water of quicksilver until it becomes a vapor, so it sparkles by its radiation, stealing the sight, and revolves the revolution of the pot when its quicksilver-moisture is removed by the subtle fire — and it is the wisdom desired from it, that it become one water not able to separate some of it from some. Mārīyah said: "When you see in a book 'sounding' or 'making red' or 'making yellow' or 'digestion' or 'analysis' or 'sublimation' or 'distillation' — it is only one thing." And it may occur in the eternal abiding water; so the maker is quicksilver, and it is the soul. So the soul dyes the spirit, and the spirit does not dye the body, and it is far from dyeing until it is seen as oil — for the ascending spirits, when they return to their earthly bodies —

Indonesia:
— ۳۸۲ —

Dan engkau tuntaskan semua pewarna dari jiwa, maka taruh di cangkir kaca bermulut terbuka, dan simpan di cucurbit lain berparit; taruh cucurbit di panci tembaga penuh air, api lembut hingga pewarna naik dari air dan tersisa di dasar cangkir seperti api padam apinya — maka terjadi pengasapan. Lalu ambil tanah bagian, air kehidupan bagian, natron bagian; taruh di botol, pasang botol lain seperti perak, ikat sambungan; taruh di matahari panas hingga tanah kering dan minum semua air. Buka botol, tambah seberat tanah yang dipakai; keringkan di matahari hingga minum, lalu giling dengan air dan keringkan hingga kering. Bila musim panas, terapi matahari; bila musim dingin, api lembut seperti panas matahari, hingga kering kedua kali. Maka capai puncak eliksir. Giling dan angkat di botol, tutup dari debu, tuang bagian pada 20 bagian — dan dengan Allah taufik, jadilah orang bersyukur.

( Bab lain darinya, tentang penjelasan dan rincian )
* Ketahuilah nama batu di kalangan mereka tunggal menurut bedanya — ada yang bilang rambut (mayoritas), ada yang telur, ada yang timah hitam, ada yang darah, dan lainnya. Lalu kuberi: saat tata tak beda dua pendapat pun, sebab tata mereka satu, mencapai sisa menyeluruh. Ada yang luas, ada yang singkat, ada yang simbol, ada yang campur. Kami jelaskan isyarat orang dan himpun tiap pada pasangannya hingga faham tiap berakal. Mereka berkata: batu mereka satu, seperti Allah Esa; dan masuk pemecahan dari pemahaman. Saat mau sucikan, mereka bagi jadi bagian — dan kusebut, maka bagian banyak; lalu lengkapi sebagian dengan banyak hal, bagian melebar; lalu mereka pandang sebagai bagian darinya, maka mereka berkata itu. Lalu disebut bagian yang belum putih, debu halus di wajahnya seperti minyak — mereka namakan air hujan dan air anjing, sebab cerdik menamai apa mengalir dari batu mereka laut, sungai, mata air, awan, hujan, susu, minyak, cuka, urine — semua cair di dunia dan tiap campuran. Lalu perkuat api, keluar air putih berat berkilau seperti mutiara mencuri pandang — bila di air seolah membelah kaca demi kuat cahaya; bila digerak berkilau seperti air laut dan gelap. Mereka namakan "raksa Arab", dingin lembap. Lalu perkuat api, keluar yang kental kehitam — raksa timur, panas kering; pewarna di alam api, hanya larut dengan air raksa Arab. Bila larut jadi spiritual murni. Di tanah beralir ada minum pemutih dan minum pembakaran; tanah, udara, api, dan lawan tiga, larut di air raksa hingga uap, berkilau, memutar seperti periuk saat kelembapan raksa hilang api halus — itu hikmah dikehendaki, jadi satu air tak terpisah. Mārīyah berkata: "Bila kauli 'bunyi' atau 'merah' atau 'kuning' atau 'cerna' atau 'analisis' atau 'sublimasi' atau 'destilasi' — itu satu hal." Dan terjadi di air kekal; pembuat adalah raksa, ia jiwa. Jiwa mewarnai roh, roh tak mewarnai tubuh, jauh diwarnai hingga nampak minyak — sebab roh naik bila kembali ke tubuh dunianya —
