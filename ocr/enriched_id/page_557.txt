English:
-- 551 --

[Poetic verses for each of the sixteen geomantic houses, on the omen indicated by its figure:] {The fifth house — the figure of al-Jawdah} 'It has come in what you hope for — al-Jawdah; and it is upon you a sunna [practice] of the eye; its first is Jawdāt, then al-Hā', its last better than its first.' {The sixth house — the figure of 'Aqlah} 'It has come in what you hope for — 'Aqlah; and she, when unveiled with the like of her name — so be patient and do not hasten, and be one taking refuge in patience until she is finished in her drawing.' {The seventh house — the figure of al-Inkīs} 'To you the omen — be patient for what you are resolved upon; in it I see evils and shrewdness; for there has come a figure in the exposition inauspicious — the opposite, for they named the figure al-Inkīs.' {The eighth house — the figure of al-Ḥumrah} 'O questioner about descending [affairs] that concern and much fear — be patient upon what has been cast, and entrust [matters] to Allah the Aware.' {The ninth house — the figure of al-Bayāḍ} 'You shall attain the attainment of every goal, after the subjugation of the opposers and the enviers; al-Bayāḍ has come informing of this, and upon Allah in affairs is my reliance.' {The tenth house — the figure of Naṣrah Khārijah} 'There has come to you in what is hoped — a victory, an outward one, good from the moon; so receive good tidings of glory and the reaching of the desire, and fortune and prosperity and victory.' {The eleventh house — the figure of Naṣrah Dākhilah} 'A victory from Allah and an opening near; your mistaken enemy and you the right one — so Naṣrah Dākhilah is of our God; for my Lord Allah is near, answering.' {The twelfth house — the figure of 'Atabah Khārijah} "'Atabah Khārijah — its blame is by all the people of the art who passed; so be patient upon what has been cast a while, and entrust [to] Allah — and excellent is the trustee.' {The thirteenth house — the figure of 'Atabah Dākhilah} "'Atabah Dākhilah — with delight, and fortune removes care and relief; so rise, hasten to what you hope, for your fortune is [from] rectitude, it resulted." {The fourteenth house — the figure of Ṭarīq} 'There has come to you Ṭarīq to every good; facilitated in it is every difficult affair; for in it is rectitude and in it success, and the gentleness of Allah, the Gentle, the Aware.' {The fifteenth house — the figure of al-Ijtimā'} 'Union in all you hope, O beloved, with the mine of friends and companions…'

Indonesia:

[Sajak bagi setiap enam belas rumah geomancy, tentang pertanda angkanya:] {Rumah kelima — angka al-Jawdah} 'Ia datang dalam apa kau harapkan — al-Jawdah; dan ia atasmu sunnah [amalan] mata; pertamanya Jawdāt, lalu al-Hā', akhirnya lebih baik dari pertamanya.' {Rumah keenam — angka 'Aqlah} 'Ia datang dalam apa kau harapkan — 'Aqlah; dan ia, bila disingkap serupa namanya — maka bersabar dan jangan tergesa, dan jadilah yang berlindung pada kesabaran hingga ia selesai dalam lukisannya.' {Rumah ketujuh — angka al-Inkīs} 'Bagi engkau alamat — bersabarlah pada apa kau tekad; padanya kulihat kejahatan dan kelicikan; kerana telah datang angka dalam terangan sial — sebaliknya, kerana mereka namakan angka al-Inkīs.' {Rumah kelapan — angka al-Ḥumrah} 'Wahai penanya tentang hal turun yang membingungkan dan ketakutan banyak — bersabarlah atas apa dicampak, dan serahkan [urusan] pada Allah Yang Maha Mengetahui.' {Rumah kesembilan — angka al-Bayāḍ} 'Engkau akan capai segala matlamat, setelah penundukan penentang dan pendengki; al-Bayāḍ telah datang memberitahu ini, dan pada Allah dalam urusan aku berserah.' {Rumah kesepuluh — angka Naṣrah Khārijah} 'Telah datang kepadamu dalam yang diharap — kemenangan, luar, baik dari bulan; maka terimalah khabar gembira kemuliaan dan capaian keinginan, serta tuah dan kemakmuran dan kemenangan.' {Rumah kesebelas — angka Naṣrah Dākhilah} 'Kemenangan dari Allah dan pembukaan dekat; musuhmu yang silap dan engkau yang benar — maka Naṣrah Dākhilah dari Tuhan kita; kerana Tuhanku Allah dekat, memperkenankan.' {Rumah kedua belas — angka 'Atabah Khārijah} "'Atabah Khārijah — kecelaannya oleh semua ahli seni yang lalu; maka bersabarlah atas apa dicampak seketika, dan serahkan [pada] Allah — dan baiklah yang diamanahkan." {Rumah ketiga belas — angka 'Atabah Dākhilah} "'Atabah Dākhilah — dengan kegembiraan, dan tuah membuang duka dan kelegaan; maka bangun, segeralah ke apa diharap, kerana tuahmu [dari] kebenaran, terhasil." {Rumah keempat belas — angka Ṭarīq} 'Telah datang kepadamu Ṭarīq ke setiap kebaikan; dimudahkan padanya tiap urusan sukar; kerana padanya kebenaran dan padanya kejayaan, serta kelembutan Allah Yang Lembut Lagi Mengetahui.' {Rumah kelima belas — angka al-Ijtimā'} 'Penyatuan dalam semua yang kau harap, wahai kekasih, dengan timbunan kawan dan sahabat…'
