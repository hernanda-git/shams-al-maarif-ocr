Know that the stone about which the ancients spoke much has an existing influence, not [mere] effect — that is, the heavier management appears in it. And many of the philosophers pointed to it. And it is a triangular stone, and in it are three colors; and these colors are the following soul, the connecting spirit, and the restraining body. And this stone is distinguished by their detail from it by what we mentioned — these colors which the people named "bodies," and they only meant the colors and what resembles them, all that they witnessed. And the ancients agreed that their stone and their management is detail and composition, dissolution and coagulation, cutting and returning, death and life — and each word of that, one of them is opposite the other, gathering all the work. And if you reflect on one word, it contains half the work — as their saying "detail and composition," rather calcination and purification and whitening and sublimation. So all this [is] half the knowledge of the stone. And the detail is only the separation of subtle and dense, and deficiency and gathering, for the distinction of each of them from its companion, until the dense remains dry with no subtlety in it at all, and the subtle [is] a spirit with no density in it at all. And composition is the gathering between subtle [and] subtle in a bound gathering; and the bound gathering is the assimilating of subtle and dense until they are in one form, and they complete in the natural color, so that neither increases over the other anything * And know that every one of the living bodies — fire alone calcined it, so its spirit is not mingled with its body. And were its spirit mingled with its body, it would not calcine, nor would its moisture depart from it — for it is that which confronts the fire, lest its form be corrupted. And there is not in the bodies what prevents the fire — this prevention confronting the fire — save gold and silver. And as for other than them of the bodies, their subtle departs from their dense at the fire. So when they calcined the bodies, as the people claimed, and returned to them of moisture like what departed from them, they became two calcinations. And only was there need to return the moisture to the calcination because nature gathered it at first upon non-equilibrium and non-accord — then the body would have been an elixir. But at the moment of its existence it was not found thus, so there was need to diminish it and compose it for its kind of moisture; then the ash returns it to it in equilibrium. And it is not thus except by fire, for the body of heat is that which gathered the parts of the body some to some, and also separates between the different bodies. And from here arose the assembling of the similar and the separating of the different. And for that their chiefs said: "Whoever does not know this fire and its secret knows neither the science of cooking nor the manner of kindling, nor knows anything of this craft." So if its harm upon him is more than its benefit, this one has uncovered much of the craft. And it behooves whoever wills to seek the stone of the people to make the fire sometimes — for it is of its nature to gather all the similar and separate the different. So the seeker seeks from the kind of the jewels of fire something with which he melts the bodies and it remains and wires them and breaks them. So if he finds it, let him know it is the stone; and if he does not find it, he turns from it to other than it — for fire bears the beings and returns them to what they were composed from, of necessity, either by speed or by slowness * So know that, for every composite which fire cannot corrupt its essence nor nullify its melting from it — it is truly alive like the essential bodies. So every calcined [thing] returned to what it was composed from * And know that everything whose moisture departed remains with its body rigid — it distinguishes between its dense and its subtle. So this is half their management, and they name it death — for they stipulated by death, and they should not be as the dead, for it became in the limit of dead dust, not benefited by. So they pointed to the living dead of appearance, and that the observer be alive. And only did they call to that because they needed the limit of moisture upon this calcination to the limit of dead dust, not benefited and not accepting its moisture.

Indonesia:

Ketahuilah bahwa batu yang banyak dibicarakan purba memiliki pengaruh ada, bukan [sekadar] efek — yakni, tata yang lebih berat muncul padanya. Dan banyak filsuf menunjuknya. Dan ia batu segitiga, di dalamnya tiga warna; dan warna ini adalah jiwa pengikut, roh penyambung, dan tubuh pengikat. Dan batu ini dibedakan dengan rinciannya darinya sebagaimana kami sebut — warna yang orang namakan "tubuh", dan mereka hanya maksud warna dan serupanya, semua yang mereka saksikan. Dan purba sepakat bahwa batu dan tata mereka adalah rincian dan komposisi, larut dan gumpal, potong dan kembali, mati dan hidup — dan tiap kata, satu berlawanan lain, menghimpun seluruh kerja. Dan bila engkau renung satu kata, ia memuat separuh kerja — seperti ucapan "rincian dan komposisi", bahkan kalsinasi dan pemurnian dan pemutihan dan sublimasi. Maka semua ini [adalah] separuh ilmu batu. Dan rincian hanyalah pemisahan halus dan padat, serta kekurangan dan pengumpulan, untuk membedakan tiap dari pasangannya, hingga padat tetap kering tanpa halus sedikit pun, dan halus [adalah] roh tanpa padat sedikit pun. Dan komposisi adalah pengumpulan antara halus [dan] halus dalam kumpulan terikat; dan kumpulan terikat adalah penyerupaan halus dan padat hingga mereka satu bentuk, dan lengkap dalam warna alami, sehingga tak ada yang lebih dari lain. * Ketahuilah tiap tubuh hidup — api sendiri mengkalsinasinya, maka rohnya tak bercampur tubuhnya. Dan andai rohnya bercampur tubuhnya, takkan terkalsin, pun kelembapannya takkan pergi — sebab ia yang menghadapi api agar bentuknya tak rusak. Dan tiada pada tubuh yang cegah api — pencegahan hadapi api — kecuali emas dan perak. Adapun lainnya, halus mereka pergi dari padat di api. Maka saat mereka kalsinasi tubuh, sebagaimana diklaim, dan kembalikan kelembapan seperti yang pergi, mereka jadi dua kalsinasi. Dan perlu mengembalikan kelembapan pada kalsinasi karena alam mengumpulkannya mula dengan tak seimbang; maka tubuh seharusnya eliksir. Tapi saat ada, tak ditemukan demikian, maka perlu dikurangi dan disusun untuk jenis kelembapannya; lalu abu kembalikan secara seimbang. Dan demikian hanya dengan api, sebab tubuh panas mengumpulkan bagian tubuh dan memisah yang beda. Dan dari sini timbul pengumpulan serupa dan pemisahan beda. Maka tokoh mereka berkata: "Barangsiapa tak tahu api ini dan rahasianya, tak tahu ilmu masak pun cara nyala, pun tak tahu kerajinan ini." Maka bila mudarat lebih dari manfaat, ia telah membongkar banyak kerajinan. Dan layak bagi yang mau cari batu orang agar menjadikan api kadang — sebab sifatnya kumpulkan serupa dan pisahkan beda. Maka pencari cari dari jenis permata api sesuatu yang melebur tubuh dan tetap serta mengawatinya dan memecahnya. Maka bila temukan, ketahuilah itu batu; dan bila tak, berpaling pada lain — sebab api menanggung makhluk dan kembalikan ke asal tersusun, cepat atau lambat * Maka ketahuilah, tiap campuran yang api tak rusak esensinya pun tak gugurkan leburnya — ia hidup sejati seperti tubuh esensial. Maka tiap terkalsin kembali ke asal tersusun * Dan ketahuilah segalanya yang kelembapannya pergi tetap kaku — membedakan padat dan halusnya. Maka ini separuh tata mereka, dan mereka namakan mati — sebab mereka syaratkan mati, namun jangan jadi seperti mati, sebab jadi debu mati tak bermanfaat. Maka mereka tunjuk pada yang mati namun hidup tampak, dan pengamat hendaknya hidup. Dan hanya mereka seru itu karena butuh batas kelembapan pada kalsinasi ini ke batas debu mati tak guna dan tak terima kelembapannya.
