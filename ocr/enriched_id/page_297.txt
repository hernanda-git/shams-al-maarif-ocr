Two letters of the Greatest Name; and by it Allah will raise the creatures on the Day of Resurrection, and each is raised upon what he died upon, and in it is a strange secret in the raising of souls and bodies. Its angel is Bikhtiyā'īl, who is chief over 4 commanders, under the hand of each commander 573 rows, each row 573 thousand angels—and for this reason it is repeated "the angel descends upon him and two robes of honor are conferred upon him." These ten names—their secret's support is the blessing that breaks habit and the facilitation of provisions and sufficiency from everything; [may He] make me succeed in mending understanding and maintaining the abundant intellect and richness through Allah (exalted) from everyone, and reaching the station of reliance, which is the highest and most beautiful of stations. As for His (exalted) names al-Ghanī and al-Shakūr, their rememberer is given by Allah (exalted) richness in himself, and is inspired with diligence and gratitude in ease and hardship. Whoever persists in remembering them, and was by nature stingy, Allah (exalted) replaces it with intelligence and generosity. Whoever aligns His (exalted) name al-Mughnī [written] a number of times on a sheet of tin and places it in the water he drinks from, finds in himself richness and contentment he had not known. Whoever persists in remembering His name al-Shakūr, Allah (exalted) makes the beautiful appear upon him and conceals the ugly from him. Among the special properties of this name is the permanence of blessings upon its rememberer and the return of what strayed from them. As for His (exalted) names al-Mughnī, al-Razzāq, al-Fattāh, their rememberer has blessing descend upon him, and Allah (exalted) provides for him from whence he does not reckon, and provisions are made easy for him from every direction, and no one mentions them over food or drink except blessing and increase appear in it, and its breaking is not possible due to its clarity. Whoever takes them as a remembrance after his prayer will never be poor. Whoever aligns their numbers jointly 4-in-4 on yellow silk and places it in the money-chest and the purse of dirhams, the wealth grows by the permission of Allah. As for His names al-Hasīb and al-Wakīl, they are two great names; their rememberer is sufficed by Allah (exalted) against the evil of his enemies and He gathers what concerned him. If an oppressor overcomes him and he remembers them their [prescribed] number at the time of dawn, then says thereafter: "O Allah, I seek my reward from You and rely upon You in the matter of So-and-so the oppressor," he is taken at his time.
