"…on the Day of Resurrection as a requital; then call him and he comes to you running. * And know that Allah is Mighty, Wise. So when Ibrāhīm looked with the light of the innate disposition, he found the whole world compounded of these phases and established by these names, and the truth of certainty appeared to him; so Allah showed him after that the wonders of the Dominion, as His saying exalted 'And thus We show Ibrāhīm the Dominion of the heavens and the earth.' And those human and innate forms are the realities of witnessing and the secrets of existence. So by the perfection of the knowledges of the names of the Essence was his station — he preserves the stations as the sun for the planets, they draw from him for the people; and in the station of the meanings of the names of the attributes he was as the full moon among the planets, they draw from him. * And know that the planets shine from some of them upon others, and do not shine from him, even if he cuts the world of the names comprising the deeds — as if the planets, of them is what is greater, to be followed, and of them is what is small, not to be followed; and their difference in their stations is as the difference of the bright planets from the bewildered. Therefore the creatures are gathered to Allah: of them is he who passes over the path like the snatching lightning, and of them he who passes like the strongest of men. Do you not see his (upon him peace) saying: 'The first troop of my nation enters Paradise, their faces like the sun and the moon on the night of the full moon in illumination,' then those who are near them 'like the faintest of the planets in the sky' — so the light of their faces is in their stations in their faith and their deeds? * And know that the manifestation of the forms, lasting in the two abodes, is established in the two origination; and therefore the innate disposition had deposited in it the realities of the names in sum and detail. Do you not see that Paradise manifests His name al-Khāliq, for Paradise has no limit to its bliss? Do you not see that in Paradise is a market in which are only the forms of the places — so whoever wills imprints upon them. And since the divine innate disposition was imprinted in the molds of the names, the permanence of subsistence belongs to annihilation. * And know that the origination of the world stands upon four, named the barzakhiyyāt, and they are four: so the first is the origination of the eternal (azal), and it is the inward of the haze; and the second is the origination of the everlasting (abad), and it is the ether; and the first existent in it is the Muḥammadan reality; and the origination of the perpetual (sarmadiyya), and it is the inward of the thought. So the origination connected to the haze — its reliance is by His saying exalted 'Does not man remember that We created him before, when he was nothing?' And he is the small world expressed by the human; and every world of the worlds was created for his sake, and he is the result of the known Real, and she is the spirit of the world, moving; and the gathering of his origination in this world and the next is for every man of the two parties equally in the state, except in knowledge, for each party knows the deficiency of its state — so the indication is only for the believers, and the disbeliever with happiness and misery and bliss and hell. * And know that the origination of the everlasting is the reality of the ether, and it is written in His saying exalted 'He was not anything mentioned'; then the third origination, the origination of the perpetual, and it is a reality in the atoms, in His saying exalted 'Am I not your Lord? They said: Yes'; then the fourth origination, His saying in the answer, and it is what is expressed as the origination of the everlasting in the reality of the innate disposition, in His saying exalted 'And We settle in the wombs what We will,' and His saying exalted 'He it is who fashions you in the wombs as He wills.' (Notice) Know that the known things are four: the Real the Exalted, and He is described by the absolute existence, for He the Exalted is not known by nothing and for a cause, but He exists, His existence not known by essence — but there is known what is the cause of the beginning from the attributes of the meanings and the attributes of perfection. And as for the knowledge of the reality of the Essence, it is forbidden — not known by proof nor by rational demonstration, for He, glory be to Him, resembles nothing so that He would be without it; rather He is 'nothing is like Him.' And the prohibition came in reflecting on that. And the second known is a prohibition in the universal reality which is for the Real the Exalted, and the world is not described by existence nor by non-existence nor by origination — so it is originated; so the known is not known whether it precedes them and their origination to come is known — this reality; and this is not found but by the description of its realization, for it does not accept division, and what is in it is all, and it is not reached to…"

"…hari kiamat sebagai balasan; kemudian panggil dia datang berlari. * Ketahui Allah Maha Perkasa Bijaksana. Bila Ibrahim lihat dengan cahaya fitrah, dapati alam semua tersusun fasa ini tegak nama ini, zahir kebenaran yakin; Allah tunjuk keajaiban Malakut firman 'Demikian Kami tunjuk Ibrahim alam Malakut langit bumi.' Bentuk manusia fitrah hakikat syuhud rahsia wujud. Dengan sempurna ilmu nama Zat maqamnya — pelihara maqam seperti matahari planet, mereka ambil untuk manusia; maqam makna nama sifat seperti bulan purnama di planet ambil daripadanya. * Planet bersinar sebahagian atas lain, tak dari dia walau potong alam nama perbuatan — seperti planet ada besar diikuti kecil tak diikuti; bezanya maqam seperti planet cerah dari keliru. Maka makhluk dihimpun pada Allah: ada lalu sirat seperti kilat, ada seperti lelaki kuat. Nampak sabda 'Troop pertama umatku masuk syurga wajah seperti matahari bulan malam purnama,' yang dekat 'seperti planet malap di langit' — cahaya wajah di maqam iman amal. * Zahir bentuk kekal dua alam tegak dua kejadian; fitrah ada hakikat nama ringkas terperinci. Syurga zahir nama al-Khāliq tiada had nikmat; pasar syurga bentuk tempat sesiapa mahu cap. Fitrah ilahi dicap acuan nama, kekal kepada fana. * Kejadian alam atas 4 barzakhiyyāt: pertama azal batin kabus; kedua abad eter; pertama wujud realiti Muhammadiah; sarmadiyya batin fikiran. Kejadian kabus sandar firman 'Manusia tak ingat Kami cipta sebelum tiada' — alam kecil manusia, setiap alam dicipta untuknya, hasil Real diketahui roh alam bergerak; kumpul kejadian dunia akhirat sama untuk dua puak kecuali ilmu, setiap tahu kurang keadaan — isyarat hanya mukmin, kafir dengan bahagia celaka nikmat neraka. * Kejadian abadi hakikat eter firman 'Dia tiada disebut'; ketiga sarmadiyya hakikat atom firman 'Bukankah Aku Tuhanmu? Ya'; keempat jawapan 'kejadian abadi fitrah' firman 'Kami tetapkan rahim apa dikehendaki' 'Dia bentuk kamu rahim kehendak.' (Tunjuk) Yang diketahui 4: Real Maha Tinggi sifat wujud mutlak, tak diketahui tiada sebab, wujud tapi wujud-Nya tak diketahui zat — diketahui sebab permulaan sifat makna kesempurnaan. Ilmu hakikat Zat dilarang tak dengan bukti akal, Dia tak serupa apa jadi tiada; 'tiada sama dengan-Nya.' Larangan fikir. Kedua diketahui larangan realiti universal untuk Real, alam tak sifat wujud tiada kejadian — ia dijadikan; tak diketahui didahului, kejadian akan datang diketahui; ini tak didapati melainkan sifat realizasi, tak terbahagi, semua padanya tak sampai…"
