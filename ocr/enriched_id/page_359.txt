( A chapter on the letter qāf ) Its count is 100, and its expansion qāf; its letters multiplied in their like reached 264.
We dropped the Alif-s, and BQ-Rā'īl remained — and it is outward in the higher [realms]. Then we returned to the root of the number and multiplied it in its
original letters; 200 came out; we pronounced it — and it was the interior of the interrogation. And as for its outward in the firsts: Afriyā'īl.
So know that.
( A chapter on the letter rā' ) Its count is 200, and its expansion rā', and its breaking 26; multiplied in its like it came out
236; we dropped the Alif-s, and the pronunciation was the king's halt — Waqqāfā'īl — and this was outward in the higher [realms]. And if we look
at the interior of the breaking, the number was 200; we multiplied it twice, and pronounced Iṭbānā'īl — and this was the outgoing
from the interior of the higher [realms], and its outward to al-Rā'īl; and this was the third interrogation — so know that.
( A chapter on the letter shīn ) * Its count is 300, and its expansion sh y n, and its breaking sh y ā n w n — its total
41 letters; multiplied in its like it reached 404; we dropped the Alif-s, and pronounced Siyāyīl — and this is outward in the interrogation in
the higher [realms]. And as for its interior, we multiplied the root of the number in the outgoing letters; 900 came out; we pronounced Ṭanāyīl;
then we returned to the first, and it was D-Yā'īl — so know that.
( A chapter on the letter tā' ) Its count is 400, and its expansion t ā, and the expansion of the letters 26; multiplied in itself it reached
621; we dropped the Alif-s, and pronounced Walqā'īl — and this is outward in the interrogation in the higher [realms]. And as for its interior, we multiplied
the root of the number in the outgoing letters; 800 came out; we pronounced it Raṭāyīl; then we returned to the first, which is Lorā'īl —
so know that.
( A chapter on the letter thā' ) Its count is 500, and its expansion th ā, and its expansion th ā ā l f — its expansion 316 letters;
multiplied in its like, it reached 316; we dropped the Alif-s, and from the cube 316 came out — and it was Wasqā'īl — and this was
outward in the higher [realms]. * And as for the interior of the higher [realms], we took the number and multiplied it in the expansion; 100 came out; we pronounced it
Ra'qyā'īl; we took the original number and pronounced it — Wa-T-Yā'īl — so know that.
( A chapter on the letter ḥā' ) Its count is 600; multiplied in its like, the interrogation 216 came out — and this was outward
in the higher [realms]. * And as for its interior, we took the original number and multiplied it in the first expansion; 100 came out; we pronounced Dayr-Yā'īl —
so know that.
( A chapter on the letter dhāl ) Its count is 700, and its expansion 66, and its breaking dh ā l ā l f l ā m; its letters multiplied in

44; we pronounced it Taf'iyāyīl — so know that.

so their count was 40 letters; we multiplied them in their like, and it was 425; we dropped the Alif-s, and the outgoing was Ṣanqā'īl.
Then we took the original number and multiplied it in the root of the letters, and the number was 24; we pronounced them Thaqfāyīl; then we took
the original number and pronounced it, and it was Iṭr-Yā'īl — so know that.

( Bab tentang huruf qāf ) Bilangannya 100, dan kembangannya qāf; hurufnya dikali pada seumpamanya mencapai 264.
Kami gugurkan Alif, dan BQ-Rā'īl tersisa — dan ia lahir di alam tinggi. Lalu kami kembali ke akar bilangan dan kalikan pada
huruf aslinya; 200 keluar; kami lafalkan — dan ia batin interogasi. Adapun yang lahir pada yang-pertama: Afriyā'īl.
Maka ketahuilah itu.
( Bab tentang huruf rā' ) Bilangannya 200, dan kembangannya rā', serta pemecahannya 26; dikali pada seumpamanya keluar
236; kami gugurkan Alif, dan lafalnya adalah hentian raja — Waqqāfā'īl — dan ini lahir di alam tinggi. Dan jika kami lihat
batin pemecahan, bilangannya 200; kami kalikan dua kali, dan lafalkan Iṭbānā'īl — dan ini yang keluar
dari batin alam tinggi, dan lahirnya ke al-Rā'īl; dan ini interogasi ketiga — maka ketahuilah itu.
( Bab tentang huruf shīn ) * Bilangannya 300, dan kembangannya sh y n, serta pemecahannya sh y ā n w n — jumlahnya
41 huruf; dikali seumpamanya mencapai 404; kami gugurkan Alif, dan lafalkan Siyāyīl — dan ini lahir interogasi
alam tinggi. Adapun batinnya, kami kalikan akar bilangan pada huruf keluar; 900 keluar; kami lafalkan Ṭanāyīl;
lalu kami kembali ke pertama, dan ia D-Yā'īl — maka ketahuilah itu.
( Bab tentang huruf tā' ) Bilangannya 400, dan kembangannya t ā, serta kembangan huruf 26; dikali pada dirinya mencapai
621; kami gugurkan Alif, dan lafalkan Walqā'īl — dan ini lahir interogasi alam tinggi. Adapun batinnya, kami kalikan
akar bilangan pada huruf keluar; 800 keluar; kami lafalkan Raṭāyīl; lalu kembali ke pertama, yaitu Lorā'īl —
maka ketahuilah itu.
