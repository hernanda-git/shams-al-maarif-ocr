And you sprinkle upon Venus while it circles a little, and you lead it — there descends the ounce, 4 dirhams.
( Chapter ) On the mention of the drugs, and they are 3 kinds: earthy, vegetative, and animal. So the earthy [is] 6 kinds: spirits, bodies, stones, vitriols, salts, and boraxes. So the spirits [are] 4: quicksilver, natron, sulphur, and orpiment. And the bodies [are] 7: gold, silver, copper, iron, lead, tin, and the specific [ones] of the stones 16: marqashīthā, magnesia, al-rawḍ, lazward, rahanj, dīzawarj, shādhank, shakk, kohl, ṭalaq, ḥibs, al-zāj, al-zājāt, al-qalqand, al-qalqaṭār, al-sūrī. And the boraxes [are] 6: the borax of ḥibr, the borax of the goldsmiths, al-tisqār, the red borax, the borax of rawand, and the western borax — 4 for the salts [are] 11: the good salt, al-murr, ṭarzad, al-andarānī, al-nifṭī, al-hindī, al-'aynī, and the salt of ashes — in them are natural [and] artificial [kinds], known; their colors, and the best of them the spirits of quicksilver that is fine and white — when you press it with burning, nothing remains in it like kohl. ( Natron ) Two kinds: one is mineral, a cutting of ṭarzad of free salt, made from Samarkand; and of it is a yellow — necessarily a defect in the work — and it is the natron of 'udhrah. So when you circulate it and melt it, the beloveds melt by it. Orpiment [is] 6: red, nonexistent; yellow, solid like sandarūs, ran solid; and another pure, wondrous, wide of yellowness; and white, ivory; and white mixed with dust, not suitable; and black mixed with gravel. ( Marqashīthā ) 4: white silvery, red coppery, black iron-y, yellow golden. ( Magnesia ) Earthy black colors; and of it are ones with eyes that have a glint; and of it are hard iron-y pieces — and it is the male; and of it is bare brittle — the female, in which are eyes that sparkle, and it is the best. ( Al-rawḍ ) Two kinds: Isṭakhrī and iron-water. ( Tutiyā ) Many colors: of it green cutting, yellow ceramic, maḥmūdī, green Kirmānī, qushūrī, white of rind, zurqā, and Indian. ( Rahanj ) It is a green stone, and in it are veins in which seals and beads are engraved; and of it is new and old and Egyptian and Kirmānī and Khurasānī, and the old Kirmānī is best. And lazward is one kind, and it is a stone in which you find redness and glittering eyes *; and fīrūzaj is one kind, and it is a stone in which is redness and delicate eyes, green golden. ( Shādhank ) Golden stones and gold-red, and it colors — for it is of the jewels of copper. ( Shakk ) Two kinds: white and yellow, brought from the silver mines — white. ( And kohl ) Two kinds: solid glassy, and the lover of Iṣfahānī. ( Ṭalaq ) Kinds: Yemeni, sea, and mountain; and it flakes when crushed and has a glint; and its best is the Yemeni, then the sea-white mixed with the red mineral. ( Glass, kinds ) Taken from sand, and the least is its best — the white Syrian that rivals crystal in the clarity of glasses; of it is yellow solid, and of it yellow with golden eyes that the cobblers and goldsmiths use; and of it yellow pieces resembling pieces of Ajamī indigo in the yellowness not in the color, and it is the best. ( Alum ) Kinds: of it white Yemeni with lines, and the ṭarzadī; and of it the white Syrian mixed with clay, and a stone that is tinged with redness; and of it the Egyptian dense yellow, and it is soft; and the Kirmānī soft, and it is the softest of them for the fixed precipitate. ( Al-qalqand ) A white vitriol; and al-qalqadūs is a green vitriol. ( Al-qalqaṭār ) A yellow vitriol. ( Al-sūrī ) A red vitriol; and these four are rare of existence, and the rarest of them is the Syrian, and it enters the chapter of redness, and it is borne from the mines of qabaraṣ, and their origin is vitriols and admixture, and the flood washes them and the digging cleanses them, so the sun descends upon them and coagulates them. And the sages may make [a substitute] when it fails them —

( Bab ) Tentang sebutan ubat-ubatan, 3 jenis: tanah, tumbuhan, haiwan. Tanah [ada] 6: roh, tubuh, batu, vitriol, garam, boraks. Roh 4: raksa, natron, belerang, orpimen. Tubuh 7: emas, perak, tembaga, besi, plumbum, timah, dan batu khusus 16: marqashitha, magnesia, rawd, lazward, rahanj, dizawarj, shadhank, shakk, kohl, talaq, hibs, zaj, zajat, qalqand, qalqatar, suri. Boraks 6: boraks hibr, boraks tukang emas, tiskar, boraks merah, boraks rawand, boraks barat — garam 11: garam baik, murr, tarzad, andarani, nifti, hindi, 'ayni, garam abu — ada semula jadi dan buatan, dikenali; warnanya, terbaik roh raksa halus putih — bila dipicit dengan bakar tiada tinggal seperti kohl. ( Natron ) Dua: satu mineral, potongan tarzad garam bebas dari Samarkand; ada kuning — pasti cacat kerja — natron 'udhrah; bila edar dan lebur, kekasih lebur olehnya. Orpimen 6: merah tiada, kuning pejal macam sandarus, lagi suatu tulen hairan luas kuning; putih gading; putih bercampur debu tak elok; hitam bercampur kerikil. ( Marqashitha ) 4: putih perak, merah tembaga, hitam besi, kuning emas. ( Magnesia ) Hitam tanah; ada bermata berkilat; ada keras besi — jantan; ada telanjang rapuh — betina, mata berkelip, terbaik. ( Rawd ) Dua: Istakhri, air besi. ( Tutiya ) Banyak warna: hijau potong, kuning seramik, mahmudi, hijau Kirmans, qushuri, putih kulit, zurqa, India. ( Rahanj ) Batu hijau bercanal ukir mohor dan manik; ada baharu, lama, Mesir, Kirmans, Khurasan, lama Kirmans terbaik. Lazward satu, batu berjalur merah dan mata berkilau *; firuzaj satu, batu merah bermata halus hijau emas. ( Shadhank ) Batu emas dan merah-emas, berwarna — dari permata tembaga. ( Shakk ) Dua: putih dan kuning dari lombong perak — putih. ( Kohl ) Dua: pejal kaca, kekasih Ispahani. ( Talaq ) Yemen, laut, gunung; berkeping bila ditumbuk, berkilat; terbaik Yemeni, lalu laut-putih bercampur mineral merah. ( Kaca ) Dari pasir, sedikit terbaik — putih Syria menanding hablur; ada kuning pejal, kuning bermata emas dipakai tukang kasut dan emas; ada kuning macam indigo Ajami pada kuning bukan warna, terbaik. ( Alum ) Putih Yemen berjalur, tarzadi; Syria putih bercampur tanah dan batu kemerahan; Mesir pekat kuning lembut; Kirmans lembut, paling lembut untuk mendakan tetap. ( Qalqand ) Vitriol putih; qalqadus vitriol hijau. ( Qalqatar ) Vitriol kuning. ( Suri ) Vitriol merah; keempat jarang, Suri paling jarang, masuk bab merah, dari lombong qabarash, asal vitriol dan campuran, banjir basuh, gali bersihkan, matahari koagulasikan.
