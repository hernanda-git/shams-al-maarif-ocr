English:
— 456 —

"…and He deposited it in the Preserved Tablet within it for the mention — not by a composite pen that does not understand approximation; and He completed them by an eternal will added to Him, by His saying exalted 'And indeed We wrote in the Psalms after the Reminder that the earth shall be inherited by My righteous servants.' Then He created the world of the Dominion; then when He ordered these numerous worlds for the mothers and the degrees of ascension, and manifested from His high command upon which the existences stood — so that command was derived generally and particularly, and was upon twelve ranks; and a command that was by the bringing-forth. * The first is the day of taking the covenants upon the two fists and upon the spirits and the intellects together — and that is the day of taking the covenants in the innate disposition in bearing the trust. * The second, a command stood by the Throne for the independence of the people of the heavens and the earth and the existences. * The third, a command stood by the Footstool and bore the forms of the stored existents. * The fourth, a command stood by the Command for its disposal to the manifestation by what Allah deposited in it of the secrets of the ordering for the existences. * The fifth, a command stood by the Spirit for the manifestation of the detail. * The sixth, a command stood by the Intellect for the people of the heavens and the existences. * The seventh, a command stood by the forms. * The eighth, a command stood by the heavens and the earth. * The ninth, a command stood by the banners after the bringing-forth. * The tenth, the command of the standing of the breath and the promised muster. * The eleventh, a command disposing between the people of Paradise and the Fire. * The twelfth, for the eternity, and it returns to what it began from. And this name [al-Qābiḍ] has a majestic retreat that gives its possessor the unveiling upon the roots of the two fists and the origination, and there gush for the follower springs of wisdom; and its condition is the cutting of the inward attachments and the delighting in His whispered prayer in the watches of the night, and the recitation of its number multiplied by itself; and its servant is SHARĀ'IṬ from the worlds of 'AZĀZĪL. * (Notice) Know that when Allah willed to create Adam, He commanded Jibrīl to take a handful from the earth; so he descended and willed to take, but she swore against him and refused, and ascended. So Allah commanded Isrāfīl to do that, and she swore against him; and likewise Mīkā'īl. So He commanded 'Azrā'īl by His name al-Qābiḍ; and when he descended to the earth she swore against him, but a compelling force said to her: 'Is not He whose soul is in His hand the One who sent me?' She said: 'Yes.' He said to her: 'Then I disobey you and he disobeys me' — then he took a handful from her and ascended to the sky. And he has a chant of glorification by His name al-Qābiḍ, so the earth shrank and diminished; and Allah said to him: 'You were the manifestor of the seizing, and you seize the spirits' — so he became trusted over the two fists. And if you recite the name and rebuke by it any king, he comes humble, abased, and under his hand four commanders under each commander — whatsoever Allah willed. * And whoever recites it over an oppressor and entrusts him to it, the worlds of this nation are seized from him and destroyed him. * And if it is written in a seal and you recite over it the name its number and write around it the king and the standing mention and carry it with you, it deafens against you the tongues of the creatures — and this is its figure. And its mention with the basmala: 'O Allah, You are the Seizer of the heavens and the Spreader of the earths and the all, by Your awe and Your greatness and Your power — You measured the things by the force of the will of the chosen. O Allah, O You who seize and spread the chosen and supplied the realized light with life in the earth and the heavens, the manifestor by the force of governance — truly the governance in the spreading of the movements and the seizing of the stillnesses and all the existents. I ask You that You seize my heart and my limbs from what distances me from the disobediences, and that my light of life and sincerity not be veiled from me, and seize from me the evil of every opponent and tyrant and the evil of every confused envier; and make my seizing and my perishing joyful, not seduced nor defrauded. O Allah, spread my provision and ease for me my affair, and what You measured in the eternity of eternities. O Allah, illumine my heart and spread, O Spreader, O Self-Subsistent, and bless.' "…disimpan dalam Loh Mahfuz untuk zikir — bukan pena tersusun tak faham hampiran; disempurnakan kehendak azali pada-Nya firman 'Kami tulis dalam Zabur sesudah Peringatan bumi diwarisi hamba soleh.' Cipta alam Malakut; bila susun alam ibu darjat naik zahir perintah tinggi berdiri wujud — perintah umum khusus 12 darjat; perintah pembawaan. * Pertama hari ambil perjanjian dua tapak tangan roh akal bersama — hari ambil perjanjian fitrah pikul amanah. * Kedua perintah 'Arasy kemerdekaan ahli langit bumi wujud. * Ketiga Kursi pikul rupa makhluk tersimpan. * Keempat perintah urus zahir rahsia susunan. * Kelima Roh zahir terperinci. * Keenam Akal ahli langit wujud. * Ketujuh rupa. * Kelapan langit bumi. * Kesembilan panji sesudah kejadian. * Kesepuluh berdirinya tiupan muster dijanji. * Kesebelas urus syurga neraka. * Keduabelas kekal kembali asal. Nama ini khalwat agung buka akar dua tapak kejadian, memancur hikmah; syarat potong ikatan batin nikmat munajat malam, baca darab diri; khadam SHARĀ'IṬ alam 'AZĀZĪL. * (Tunjuk) Bila Allah cipta Adam suruh Jibril cubit tanah; turun mahu ambil, bumi bersumpah enggan naik. Israfiel Mika'il sama. 'Azra'il dengan nama al-Qābiḍ turun, bumi sumpah, kuasa paksa kata 'Bukan Dia yang jiwaku di tangan-Nya yang utusku?' 'Ya.' 'Aku derhaka kamu dia derhaka' — ambil segenggam naik. Ada selawat nama al-Qābiḍ bumi mengecil; Allah 'Engkau zahirkan cabut, cabut roh' jadi amanah dua tapak. Bila baca tegur raja datang hina di bawah 4 komander. * Baca atas zalim takluk khadam alam umat ini binasa. * Tulis cincin baca bilangan keliling raja zikir bawa — tuli lidah makhluk, inilah rupanya. Ya Allah yang cabut lapang terpilih siram cahaya hidup bumi langit, zahir kuasa tadbir — tadbir melapang gerak mencabut diam wujud. Kuminta cabut hati anggota dari maksiat, cahaya hidup ikhlas tak bertudung, cabut kejahatan lawan tiran pendengki keliru; jadikan cabut binasaku gembira tak terpedaya tertipu.
