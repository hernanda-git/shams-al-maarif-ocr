…the Eternal, the Ancient — and I have girded the sword of the Commander of the Faithful, upon him peace, and donned the cloak of 'Ā'isha, Mother of the Believers, and entered into the treasuries of "In the name of Allah, the Merciful, the Compassionate" — their locks "Praise be to Allah, Lord of the worlds." And whoever recites the Throne Verse once and "Say: He is Allah, One" three times, and blows to his right and left — and whoever wants brevity, let him recite what we mentioned then blow into his palms and wipe his face and all his body with them, and he is safe from what he fears until he returns home. And whoever recites it in the morning is safe until evening, and whoever recites it in the evening is safe until morning. And if it is recited over the head of one terror-struck eleven times, he recovers at once; and if the apparition stays in the body, it is burned. And if it is recited after the prayers, it effaces the sins the one praying has. And of its properties: when it is recited upon entering upon a tyrant or a unjust ruler, and he says in completing it: "O Allah, O Living, O Self-Subsistent, Originator of the heavens and the earth, O Possessor of Majesty and Generosity — I ask You by the right of this noble verse and what is in it of the great secrets that You bridle his mouth from me and silence his tongue so he speaks naught but good or is silent" — your good is before you and your evil under your feet — and he enters upon him, and Allah bridles his mouth from him, and no harm reaches him from him. And of its properties: when you fear someone and harm has come to you from him, pray the sunset two units, and recite the Throne Verse while prostrating three times; then when you reach His saying, exalted: "and their guardianship does not burden Him, and He is the High, the Great," repeat it three times seven, and say during your recitation: "O Allah, separate between me and So-and-so son of So-and-so as You separated between the heaven and the earth, and bridle his mouth from me as You bridled the beasts from Daniel, upon him peace, by the right of these noble names" — and you are spared his evil and his mouth is bridled from you so he speaks of you naught but good. And of its properties: when you are in a group and want to be spared their evil and harm, recite the verse three times and blow into your palms three, and wipe your hand over your face saying: "O Allah, suffice me against the evil of these people, O Sufficer; and grant me well-being from their harm, O Granter of well-being" — and Allah makes you secure from them and no harm reaches you from them. And of the properties of this verse: if you recite it by night you are safe until morning, and if you recite it by morning you are safe until evening. And it is recounted that a man dwelled in an abandoned house; when night came upon him, lo, an opening and a rumbling, and the man looked and behold, a rebel (mārid) making for him; and when he saw it he feared, and was inspired to recite the Throne Verse, and as I said each word it said it with me until His saying, exalted: "and their guardianship does not burden Him, and He is the High, the Great" — so I stopped it, and repeated it over it many times, and it vanished from me and I saw it not the rest of the night; and when morning came I looked and behold, in the corner of the house, ashes — and I marveled at that; then I told my story to a brother of mine who was righteous, and he said to me: "This was an 'ifrīt that wanted to harm you, and this verse rent it because you were inspired to recite it" — so when I heard that I took it as a daily portion and remembrance night and day, and I saw of its blessing something great. And of its properties: it is written for the companions (qarīn) and the followers (tawābi'), for whoever wears it is safe; and if added to it is His saying, exalted: "And Allah is from behind them encompassing; nay, it is a glorious Qur'an in a preserved tablet; so Allah is the best Preserver, and He is the most Merciful of the merciful" — "for him are successive guardians before and behind him who preserve him by the command of Allah" — for that is the estimation of the Exalted, the Knowing — and with it are written Sincerity (al-Ikhlāṣ) and the two protectors (al-Mu'awwiḏatān), and it is a great veil for the companions and followers. And of the properties of this verse: if placed among goods, they are preserved and their owner is safe from thieves and others. …Yang Abadi lagi Qadim, dan aku memanggul pedang Amīr al-Mu'minīn 'alaihissalām, serta memakai jubah 'Ā'isyah Ummul Mu'minīn, dan masuk ke dalam perbendaharaan "Dengan nama Allah Yang Maha Pemurah lagi Maha Penyayang" — gemboknya "Segala puji bagi Allah Tuhan semesta alam." Dan barangsiapa membaca Āyat al-Kursī sekali dan "Katakanlah: Dialah Allah Yang Esa" tiga kali, serta meniup ke kanan dan kirinya — dan barangsiapa menghendaki singkat, hendaklah ia membaca apa yang kami sebut lalu meniup ke telapak tangannya dan mengusap wajah serta seluruh tubuhnya, maka ia aman dari apa yang ditakutinya hingga kembali ke rumahnya. Dan barangsiapa membacanya pagi aman hingga petang, dan barangsiapa membacanya petang aman hingga pagi. Dan apabila dibacakan di atas kepala yang ketakutan 11 kali, ia sadar seketika; dan apabila penampakan tetap di tubuh, ia terbakar. Dan apabila dibaca setelah shalat, ia menghapus dosa yang ada pada yang shalat. Dan dari sifatnya: apabila dibaca saat masuk menemui tiran atau penguasa zalim, dan ia membaca dalam menyelesaikannya: "Ya Allah, wahai Yang Hidup, wahai Yang Berdiri Sendiri, Pencipta langit dan bumi, wahai Pemilik Keagungan dan Kemuliaan — aku memohon kepada-Mu demi hak ayat mulia ini dan rahasia agung di dalamnya, agar Engkau kendalikan mulutnya dariku, dan bisukan lidahnya sehingga tiada berucap kecuali kebaikan atau diam" — kebaikanmu di hadapanmu dan kejahatanmu di bawah kakimu — dan ia masuk kepadanya, maka Allah mengendalikan mulutnya darinya, dan tiada mudarat menimpanya. Dan dari sifatnya: apabila engkau takut seseorang dan mudarat telah menimpamu darinya, shalatlah maghrib dua rakaat, dan bacalah Āyat al-Kursī saat sujud tiga kali; maka apabila engkau sampai pada firman-Nya Ta'ala { "dan memelihara keduanya tiada memberatkan-Nya; dan Dia Mahatinggi lagi Mahaagung" } ucapkan tiga kali tujuh, serta membaca saat bacaanmu: "Ya Allah, pisahkanlah antara aku dan Si Fulan bin Fulanah sebagaimana Engkau pisahkan antara langit dan bumi, dan kendalikan mulutnya dariku sebagaimana Engkau kendalikan binatang buas dari Dāniyāl 'alaihissalām demi hak nama-nama mulia ini" — maka engkau terhindar dari kejahatannya dan mulutnya dikendalikan darimu sehingga tiada berbicara tentangmu kecuali kebaikan. Dan dari sifatnya: apabila engkau dalam kelompok dan ingin terhindar dari kejahatan serta ganguan mereka, bacalah ayat tiga kali dan tiup ke telapak tangan tiga, serta usap tangan ke wajah sambil membaca: "Ya Allah, cukupilah aku dari kejahatan kaum ini, wahai Yang Mencukupi; dan sejahterakanlah aku dari ganguan mereka, wahai Yang Memberi Kesejahteraan" — maka Allah mengamankan engkau dari mereka dan tiada mudarat menimpamu. Dan dari sifat ayat ini: apabila engkau membacanya malam, aman hingga pagi; dan apabila membacanya pagi, aman hingga petang. Dan diceritakan bahwa seorang lelaki menetap di rumah terbengkalai; ketika malam menimpanya, tiba-tiba ada pembukaan dan gemuruh, dan lelaki itu melihat seekor mārid (setan durhaka) mendatanginya; dan ketika ia melihatnya ia takut, lalu diilhami membaca Āyat al-Kursī, dan setiap aku ucapkan kata ia mengucapkannya bersamaku hingga firman-Nya Ta'ala { "dan memelihara keduanya tiada memberatkan-Nya; dan Dia Mahatinggi lagi Mahaagung" } — maka aku hentikan, dan mengulanginya berkali-kali padanya, lalu lenyap daripku dan tiada kulihat sisa malam; dan saat pagi aku lihat di sudut rumah ada abu — aku heran; lalu kuceritakan pada saudaraku yang shalih, dan ia berkata: "Ini 'ifrīt yang hendak menyakitimu, dan ayat ini mengoyakkannya karena engkau diilhami membacanya" — maka ketika kudengar itu, aku jadikan ia wirid dan dzikir siang-malam, dan kulihat berkahnya yang agung. Dan dari sifatnya: ia ditulis untuk qarīn (pendamping) dan tawābi' (pengikut), karena barangsiapa menggantungnya aman; dan apabila ditambah firman-Nya Ta'ala: "Dan Allah dari belakang mereka meliputi; bahkan ia al-Qur'an yang mulia dalam loh terpelihara; maka Allah sebaik-baik Pemelihara dan Dia Maha Penyayang di antara para penyayang" — "baginya penjaga di depan dan belakang yang memeliharanya dengan perintah Allah" — itu adalah ketentuan Yang Mahatinggi lagi Maha Mengetahui — dan bersamanya ditulis al-Ikhlāṣ dan dua mu'awwiḏāt, dan ia tabir agung bagi qarīn dan tawābi'. Dan dari sifat ayat ini: apabila diletakkan pada barang, ia terpelihara dan pemiliknya aman dari pencuri dan lainnya. Dan barangsiapa menggambar ayat ini dalam keselarasan delapan-lipat bernomor, diletakkan di jam matahari, dibasahi, pada muatan dagang — ia laku dan berputar.
