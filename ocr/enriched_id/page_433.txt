الدائرة من ١٠ أركان ٥ ظاهرة وتقدمت وخمسة باطنة اجتمع فيها أسرار الذات والقدرة

لظهور الاختصاص الأزلي على الأبدي فقولك بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم أولاً مطلق غير مقيد وإنما ذكر

الباء مع الميم وحسبه وجسد عالم الغيب والشهادة ومن الباء مع السين ويكون عالم الملكوت العلوي

والجلالة سر العقل والروح والرحمن سر القلب والرحيم سر الحاء فإذا قلت الحمد لله فهو في عالم التركيب
وإذا قلت رب كان ظاهر الرحمن من بسم الله وهو ظاهر القاب لأنه محل كتابة ربوبيته وسر الرحمة
وهو الإيمان وإذا قلنا العالمين كان ظاهر الرحيم لأن الموجودات كلها ظهرت في الطور الترتيبي بنور

قلوبنا على ما ألهمنا من سماع حمده ولذلك جاءت البسملة وكان فيها اسم الله الأعظم وما نزلت اهتزت

English:
— 427 —

"…from His name al-Qādir, and the name is derived from samu (loftiness), and it is the exaltation, and it is derived from His name the Exalted al-'Alī; and the Mīm from the cosmic loci, and the locus is the all-encompassing which is derived from His name the Exalted al-Muḥīṭ — so [these] preceded by the effects of power, by spreading the place with the lights of the Exalted, and are divided by His name al-'Alī so that His name al-Muḥīṭ appears; and these three names were unfolded in the secret of the basmala so that the place is established to the Greatest Name, which is Allah. And since power is the attribute of the One Powerful, the Alif was an indication of the Essence; and since the Bā' was an indication of power, it faced the Alif, and the Bā' was of the secret of the Alif; and since it was of the secret of the Sīn, the Sīn was of the secret of the names; and since the Hā' is the container of the secrets of monotheism and the Mīm the container of the secrets of the worlds, the Hā' faced the Mīm — the secret of every knowledge appeared, every knowledge. So if you contemplate the basmala, the circle connected from 10 pillars — 5 manifest that preceded, and five inmost in which gathered the secrets of the Essence, power, encompassing, and exaltation — then it unfolded for the appearance of the kunyah and the witnessing of mercy, and reached the four names, the fifth which is al-Raḥmān, and there is nothing upon that eternal-everlasting world. I said: since mercy is a witnessing, the fifth connected the sixth for the appearance of the eternal particularization upon the everlasting — so your saying 'In the name of Allah, the Merciful, the Compassionate' is at first absolute, unbound; and only the first principle was mentioned because He the Exalted — His mercy preceded; so the basmala is the noblest of foundations and the greatest of names, and from it power issued from the Bā' with the Mīm and its like, and embodied the world of the unseen and the witnessed; and from the Bā' with the Sīn is the upper Dominion-world; and with the Bā' the stages were formed; and from the Rā' and Hā' mercy appeared; and from the Bā' and Nūn appeared the ruling of the two contractions. And when the eternal secret inspired you the secret of care and favor, you said 'Praise be to Allah' for what preceded for you in the knowledge of composition — and that is that the Truth, glory be to Him and exalted, praised Himself; and therefore entered the Alif and Lām, and the praise is from His name al-Ḥamīd, and the secret of 'In the name of Allah' — so it used to say 'In the name of Allah,' and this is an eternal beginning; and from it is the first — so when you say 'Allah,' then He praised Himself by Himself; so the name has the secret of the intellect, and the Glory-name the secret of intellect and spirit, and al-Raḥmān the secret of the heart, and al-Raḥīm the secret of the Hā'. So when you say 'Praise be to Allah,' it is in the world of composition; and when you say 'Lord,' it was the manifest of al-Raḥmān from 'In the name of Allah,' and it is the manifest of the receiver, for it is the place of the writing of His lordship and the secret of mercy, which is faith; and when we say 'the worlds,' it was the manifest of al-Raḥīm, for all existents appeared in the arranging stage by the light of the mercifulness and the subtlety of the stages — and therefore the bodies which are the world of man, gathered from the secrets of Allah, were praised; so he is alone in eternal praising. Then mercy appeared to you in the knowledge of the everlasting as it appeared in the knowledge of the eternal, so you said 'al-Raḥmān, who established our hearts upon what He inspired us of hearing His praise' — and therefore came the basmala, and in it was the Greatest Name of Allah; and never did it descend but the heavens trembled and the earths quaked and the angels increased in extolling and generations fell on their faces. And it is written on the brow of Isrāfīl and on the brow of Adam and on the wing of Gabriel and on the palm of 'Izrā'īl and the staff of Moses and on the tongue of Jesus and the seal of them (upon them peace); and it is the separation between the Qur'ān and at the end of a verse of every chapter; and its blessing overlooks the tremendous Qur'ān. And let us mention some of its properties, seeking blessing — of its properties: if the sick one recites it its number 7 days, Allah cures him; and if it is recited in the face of a wrongdoer 50 times, Allah suffices him against his evil; and if it is recited this number for the fulfillment of needs, they are fulfilled; and if he recites it at sleep 50 times, Allah secures him from the evil of what harms him; and if it is recited over a sick one a hundred times 3 days, he is cured; and if it is recited 40 times in the ear of an epileptic, he recovers; and if it is recited for the afflicted or the sufferer of winds 3 days every day 1000 times, Allah cures him — and likewise for the red wind; and if he recites it…"

Indonesia:
— 427 —

"…dari nama-Nya al-Qādir, nama terbit dari samu (tinggi), ia keagungan, terbit dari nama-Nya al-'Alī; Mīm dari tempat kosmik, tempat itu meliputi terbit dari nama-Nya al-Muḥīṭ — maka didahului kesan kuasa, sebar tempat dengan cahaya Yang Tinggi, bahagi dengan nama al-'Alī supaya nama al-Muḥīṭ zahir; tiga nama ini terkembang dalam rahsia basmalah supaya tempat teguh ke Ism A'zam iaitu Allah. Kuasa sifat Yang Maha Kuasa, Alif tanda Zat; Bā' tanda kuasa hadap Alif, Bā' dari rahsia Alif; dari rahsia Sīn, Sīn rahsia nama; Hā' bekas rahsia tauhid dan Mīm bekas rahsia alam, Hā' hadap Mīm — rahsia tiap ilmu zahir. Fikir basmalah, bulatan sambung 10 tiang — 5 zahir didahului, 5 batin kumpul rahsia Zat kuasa meliputi tinggi — lalu terkembang zahir kunyah saksi rahmat, sampai 4 nama kelima al-Raḥmān, tiada pada alam kekal abadi. Kata: rahmat saksi, kelima sambung keenam zahir kekhususan abadi atas abadi — 'Bismillah al-Raḥmān al-Raḥīm' mutlak tak terikat; prinsip pertama disebut kerana rahmat-Nya didahului; basmalah asas mulia nama agung, kuasa keluar dari Bā' Mīm jasad alam ghaib nyata; Bā' Sīn alam Malakut atas; Bā' jadi peringkat; Rā' Hā' rahmat; Bā' Nūn hukum dua kecut. Rahsia abadi ilham rahsia jagaan kurnia, kata 'Alhamdulillah' — Hakikat puji diri-Nya, masuk Alif Lām, hamd dari nama al-Ḥamīd, rahsia 'Bismillah' — kata 'Bismillah' permulaan abadi; bila kata 'Allah' Dia puji diri-Nya; nama rahsia akal, Jalalah rahsia akal roh, al-Raḥmān rahsia hati, al-Raḥīm rahsia Hā'. Kata 'Alhamdulillah' alam susunan; kata 'Rabb' zahir al-Raḥmān dari 'Bismillah,' zahir penerima tempat tulisan ketuhanan rahsia rahmat ia iman; kata 'al-'ālamīn' zahir al-Raḥīm, semua makhluk zahir peringkat tersusun cahaya rahmat lemah lembut — badan alam manusia kumpul rahsia Allah dipuji; esa dalam pujian abadi. Rahmat zahir ilmu abadi seperti ilmu azali, kata 'al-Raḥmān yang teguhkan hati kami dengan kurnia dengar pujian-Nya' — basmalah turun, Ism A'zam; bila turun langit bumi gegar malaikat tambah tasbih generasi sujud. Tertulis di dahi Isrāfīl Adam sayap Jibril telapak 'Izrā'īl tongkat Musa lidah Isa cap mereka; pemisah antara Quran akhir ayat tiap surah; berkatnya mengatasi Quran agung. Sebahagian sifatnya untuk berkat: sakit baca bilangannya 7 hari Allah sembuh; baca depan zalim 50 kali Allah cukup dari kejahatannya; bilangan ini tunaikan hajat; baca waktu tidur 50 Allah selamat dari kejahatan; baca atas pesakit 100 kali 3 hari sembuh; 40 kali telinga epilepsi sedar; atas tertimpa/angin 3 hari 1000 kali Allah sembuh — begitu angin merah; bila baca…"
