English:
— 489 —

"…the worlds, and in yourself an objection, and that you bring it out of your outward and inward; and let your mention in this station be Sūrat al-Ikhlāṣ, or gather the four names in your mention — you say 'He is the First and the Last, the Manifest and the Hidden.' And upon you is the purity of the fancies and leaving what does not concern you, and upon you is the major ablution every Friday or every day, and the recitation of these names, for they are the roots of the people and by them the wayfarer is opened. So enter the retreat and take the name its number after every prayer, and you will see how what is unveiled to you of the Truth — how He is manifest in His acts and the opposites of its states, and He is One in His Essence, not multiple. So when you witness that with certainty, it turns upon you from His light a purity of the intimate converse, and you know the reality from your inward, until He is to you nearer than the jugular vein. So when you are informed of that from yourself, be firm until He unveils that to you; and when you do that, the servant of His name al-Awwal descends upon you, and his name is ṬAHṬĀ'ĪL, and there comes to you the servant of His name al-Ākhir, and he is ARDHĀ'ĪL, and he casts upon the wayfarer the cloak of acceptance in the higher [worlds], and there is unveiled to him the worlds of the Barzakh. And for this name [there is] a quick contagion beneficial for repelling the enemy and acceptance among the higher worlds; and if it is written on silver and carried — he attains that, or on a child who has not spoken — by the permission of Allah the Exalted; and if it is written in a vessel and around it the standing mention and erased with pure water and drunk by it three days, Allah opens for him a knowledge he did not gather, and his intellect is split open, and he attains the retention and the proof and the acceptance and the abundant fortune. And whoever knows the interpenetration disposes in the names; and if you mix these two names with a name of whom you will at a favorable time and a human carries it, he witnesses from his object great love and his need is fulfilled and what he wills. And this is its figure as you see:

And as for the standing mention by these two noble names, you say (In the name of Allah the Merciful, the Compassionate): 'O Allah, You are al-Awwal, the Ancient — there is no end to Your existence; You are the Eternal, the Causer of causes and the Reasoner of reasons, the Bringer-forth of the universes, and the Delayer of each of them to a known term. I ask You, O He upon whom everything is poor in its existence for its bringing-forth and its establishing, and every living is constrained in its life to His spirit, and the existence of a thing returned by the return to Him after its perishing — I ask You that You make me live by Your life, O First, O Last, O Manifest, O Hidden, O Lord of the worlds.' (Section on His two names the Exalted, al-Ẓāhir al-Bāṭin) (In the name of Allah the Merciful, the Compassionate) * Know that al-Ẓāhir and al-Bāṭin from the names of Allah the Exalted are two attributes of the related — for al-Ẓāhir is manifest from an aspect and is not from one aspect manifest and hidden, but is manifest from an aspect by the relation; and Allah is hidden if you reflect on that from the relation of the perception of the senses, so He is hidden from that, and if you seek Him by transmission by the ways of demonstration, you find Him manifest. And many of the people spoke on that, and we do not want length but to what the realized ones pointed: so al-Ẓāhir is a report of His power, and al-Bāṭin is an informing of His wisdom. * And know that Allah the Exalted enslaved you by al-Ẓāhir… [page ends]

Indonesia:
— 489 —

"…alam, dan pada dirimu bantahan, serta engkau keluarkannya dari zahir dan batinmu; dan jadikan dzikirmu dalam maqam ini Surah al-Ikhlāṣ, atau himpunkan empat nama dalam dzikirmu — engkau ucapkan 'Dia Yang Awal dan Yang Akhir, Yang Zahir dan Yang Batin.' Dan atasmu kejernihan khayalan dan meninggalkan apa yang tidak mengganggum, serta mandi besar setiap Jumaat atau setiap hari, dan bacaan nama ini, kerana ia akar kaum dan dengannya musafir dibuka. Maka masuklah khalwat dan ambil nama bilangannya selepas tiap solat, dan engkau akan lihat bagaimana yang dibuka kepadamu dari al-Ḥaqq — bagaimana Ia nampak dalam perbuatan-Nya dan lawan keadaannya, dan Ia Esa dalam Zat-Nya, tidak berbilang. Maka bila engkau saksikan itu dengan yakin, ia berbalik padamu dari cahaya-Nya kejernihan munajat, dan engkau kenal hakikat dari batinmu, hingga Ia kepadamu lebih dekat dari urat leher. Maka bila engkau diberitahu itu dari dirimu, tetapkanlah hingga Ia buka itu kepadamu; dan bila engkau lakukan itu, khadam nama-Nya al-Awwal turun atasmu, dan namanya ṬAHṬĀ'ĪL, serta datang kepadamu khadam nama-Nya al-Ākhir, dan ia ARDHĀ'ĪL, dan ia lemparkan atas musafir jubah penerimaan di alam tinggi, serta dibuka kepadanya alam Barzakh. Dan bagi nama ini [ada] jangkitan cepat yang bermanfaat menolak musuh dan penerimaan di alam tinggi; dan bila ditulis di perak serta dipakai — ia mencapainya, atau pada anak yang belum berkata — dengan izin Allah Yang Maha Tinggi; dan bila ditulis dalam bekas serta di kelilingnya dzikir berdiri dan dihapus dengan air suci serta diminum dengannya 3 hari, Allah bukakan padanya ilmu yang tidak ia kumpulkan, dan fikirannya terbuka, serta ia capai hafalan dan hujah serta penerimaan dan untung melimpah. Dan barangsiapa tahu tindihan mengurus dalam nama; dan bila engkau campur dua nama ini dengan nama siapa dikehendaki pada waktu sesuai serta manusia memakainya, ia menyaksikan dari tujuannya cinta besar dan hajatnya dipenuhi serta apa yang dikehendaki. Dan ini gambarnya sebagaimana engkau lihat:

Adapun dzikir berdiri dengan dua nama mulia ini, engkau ucapkan (Bismillāh ar-Raḥmān ar-Raḥīm): 'Ya Allah, Engkau al-Awwal yang Azali — tiada akhir bagi wujud-Mu; Engkau Yang Kekal, Punca segala sebab dan Alasan segala alasan, Yang Mengadakan alam semesta, dan Yang Mengakhirkan masing-masing ke ajal yang diketahui. Aku memohon, wahai Yang kepada-Nya segala sesuatu faqir dalam wujudnya bagi pengadaan dan penetapannya, dan tiap yang hidup tersempit dalam hidupnya kepada roh-Nya, serta wujud perkara kembali dengan kembali kepadanya setelah binasanya — aku memohon agar Engkau hidupkan aku dengan hidup-Mu, wahai Yang Awal, wahai Yang Akhir, wahai Yang Zahir, wahai Yang Batin, wahai Tuhan semesta alam.' (Fasal dua nama-Nya Yang Maha Tinggi, al-Ẓāhir al-Bāṭin) (Bismillāh ar-Raḥmān ar-Raḥīm) * Ketahuilah bahwa al-Ẓāhir dan al-Bāṭin dari nama Allah Yang Maha Tinggi ialah dua sifat yang dinisbahkan — kerana al-Ẓāhir nampak dari satu aspek dan tidak dari satu aspek zahir dan batin, tetapi nampak dari aspek dengan nisbah; dan Allah tersembunyi bila engkau renungkan itu dari nisbah penerimaan pancaindera, maka Ia tersembunyi dari itu, dan bila engkau cari-Nya dengan naql melalui jalan demonstrasi, engkau dapati-Nya nampak. Dan ramai kaum berbicara tentang itu, dan kami tidak mahu panjang melainkan pada apa yang diisyaratkan para muhaqqiq: maka al-Ẓāhir ialah khabar kuasa-Nya, dan al-Bāṭin ialah pemberitahuan hikmah-Nya. * Dan ketahuilah bahwa Allah Yang Maha Tinggi memperhambakan engkau dengan al-Ẓāhir… [halaman berakhir]
