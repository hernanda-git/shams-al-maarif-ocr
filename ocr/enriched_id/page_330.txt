And detailed "Mīm Lām Qāf Kāf Dāl Wāw Sīn" — the number of the detailed letters 22 letters, and expanded as preceded; so its number the expanded letters 93 letters. The number was sound summarized 360, detailed 111, expanded 8873. The sum 9743 — and there is added to that the number of the rank and what comes out of it and what is attributed to it — the sum becomes 33347. Make it a septenary square — and this is its figure. ( The fourth rank — DMaT 444 ) for Wednesday, its planet Mercury; summarized "Dāl Mīm Tā" eight — or a letter and their number 526, and their expansion "one thirty four twenty-four forty four-hundred" — the sum 444, became summarized 556. And detailed 3965, and the sum of that 1935. Jumlah 9743 — dan ditambahkan padanya bilangan tingkat dan apa yang keluar darinya dan yang dinisbatkan padanya — jumlah menjadi 33347. Jadikan petak tujuh — dan ini rupanya.
