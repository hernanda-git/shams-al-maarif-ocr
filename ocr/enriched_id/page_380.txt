the paths ۞ And know that Allah exalted Idrīs, and the first knowledge He taught him after Adam was the knowledge of the stars; and from it He derived the knowledge of the Divine Craft by revelation from Allah. So when [he lived] in the time of the flood, and that it would destroy what is upon the face of the earth, they engraved it upon the obelisks in the land of Egypt, and it was disseminated — so it was preserved from the flood ۞ And know that when Allah spoke to Mūsā, Mūsā complained to Him of poverty, so He taught him the knowledge of the Divine Craft; and he set free by it the Torah, and relieved by it the Children of Israel. So Mūsā said: "At your service, O Lord," and fell prostrate in thanks to Allah, and said: "My God, glory be to You — how great is Your affair and how mighty Your dominion!" So He taught him of His wisdom. And Mūsā (upon him peace) said: "My Lord, make it a mercy and provision for the Children of Israel, and increase me in certainty, for all good is in Your hand alone, You have no partner." And it is mentioned that Mūsā (upon him peace) found it in the land of Shu'ayb — it stands from six stones; so Qārūn alone observed it, and knew it, and grew rich and filled treasuries. And there overtook him wonder and pride in himself, and the abundance of his wealth and the breadth of his state. Said the Exalted: "And We gave him of the treasures, the keys of which would burden a company of men of strength"; and His saying: "He said: I was given it only by knowledge with me." Then Mūsā asked of him the zakāt of his wealth; so he reckoned it and found it very much, and refused to pay it. So Mūsā called down upon him, and Allah caused him and his dwelling to sink into the earth. And Ibrāhīm (upon him peace) made it, and Dāwūd and Sulaymān, and all the prophets — because they were poor, so Allah enriched them by it, for Allah gives it only to whom He chose, so that their sustenance in the world be lawful, and their states be purified. And He made it a mercy for them, and a regret for the disbelievers — like Qārūn, Fir'awn, Hāmān, Shaddād son of 'Ād, and Nimrūd son of Kan'ān, and others ۞ So let us return to what we are upon, and say ۞ Know — may Allah guide me and you — that Saturn is of the higher planets, and its body is lead; next to it Jupiter, and its body is tin; next to it Mars, and its body is iron; the Sun, its body is copper; Mercury, mercury; the Moon, and its body is silver — upon the order of the spheres as preceded ۞ And know that the apparent light is as the ray, and the ray is the inward of the light. So to every ray [belongs] light, and to every light a ray. And the ray is a reality He pointed to, and the pointed-to is the reality of light and spirit and the world's coming-to-me, just as the ray is for the essence of the spirit. And that the animal — the ray was poured upon it first, then the light second; then He poured upon the subtlety of the ray and upon its density the light. So for that, the lower world altogether is between ray and light — the ray being the secret of life, and the secret of growth, and the secret of nourishment, the corporealities. So the ray is from the inward of the plant, and the light from the outward of the plant. So the outward [has] no animal from the side of the ray, and from the side of the light [has] only the single animal by the reality of the world; and the plant-knowledge is by the reality of the Tablet ۞ And as the Tablet was earth for the pen, the plant was earth for the animal; and as the Tablet was earth for writing with the pen, the plant was in need of the body of the animal ۞ And know that the plant — its ray was balanced upon its light. But that in which its divisions were balanced and its nature equalized — it is that from which arose the nourishment suitable for bodies, and it is that which begets sound excellent blood, which is receptive to the management of the judgment and the kinds of upper obediences. And the devil has no access in this blood, nor do the causes of occurrence approach it; and the year does not produce after it a medicine issuing from it in the continuity of times — and it is of the people of the five upper [ones] and the hereafter. And as for [that] whose light shone over its ray — it is that from which arise desire and nourishments; and from it is the filling of the natures, for lack of the transparent power of the gathering of the moistures of light, because light is nearer to moisture and density, for its movement is downward. And it is that from which the thoughts are suspended.

jalan ۞ Dan ketahuilah Allah tinggikan Idrīs, dan ilmu pertama yang diajarkan kepadanya setelah Adam adalah ilmu bintang; dan darinya diturunkan ilmu Kerajinan Ilahi dengan wahyu dari Allah. Maka saat [ia hidup] di zaman banjir, dan bahwa ia akan binasakan apa di permukaan bumi, mereka ukir pada obelisk di tanah Mesir, dan tersiar — maka terpelihara dari banjir ۞ Dan ketahuilah bahwa saat Allah berbicara pada Mūsā, Mūsā mengadu kemiskinan, maka Ia ajarkan ilmu Kerajinan Ilahi; dan dengan itu ia merdekakan Taurat serta ringankan Bani Israil. Maka Mūsā berkata: "Siap, ya Tuhan," dan sujud bersyukur pada Allah, serta ucap: "Tuhanku, Maha Suci Engkau — betapa agung urusan-Mu dan perkasa kekuasaan-Mu!" Maka Ia ajarkan kebijaksanaan-Nya. Dan Mūsā (salam) berkata: "Tuhanku, jadikan ini rahmat dan rezeki bagi Bani Israil, dan tambahkan kepastian padaku, sebab segala kebaikan di tangan-Mu sendiri, tiada sekutu bagi-Mu." Dan disebut bahwa Mūsā (salam) menemukannya di tanah Shu'ayb — berdiri dari enam batu; maka Qārūn sendiri mengamatinya, dan mengetahuinya, lalu kaya dan penuhi perbendaharaan. Maka hinggap padanya keheranan dan kesombongan diri, serta banyak harta dan luasnya keadaan. Firman Yang Maha Tinggi: "Dan Kami beri dia perbendaharaan yang kuncinya memberatkan satu rombongan yang kuat"; dan firman-Nya: "Ia berkata: Aku diberi itu hanya dengan ilmu padaku." Lalu Mūsā minta zakat hartanya; maka ia hitung dan dapati sangat banyak, lalu enggan menunaikannya. Maka Mūsā melaknatnya, dan Allah benamkan ia serta rumahnya ke bumi. Dan Ibrāhīm (salam) membuatnya, dan Dāwūd serta Sulaymān, dan semua nabi — sebab mereka miskin, maka Allah kayakan dengan itu, karena Allah memberinya hanya pada yang dipilih-Nya, agar rezeki mereka di dunia halal dan keadaan mereka suci. Dan Ia jadikan rahmat bagi mereka, serta penyesalan bagi kafir — seperti Qārūn, Fir'awn, Hāmān, Shaddād putra 'Ād, dan Nimrūd putra Kan'ān, serta lainnya ۞ Maka kembalilah ke apa yang kita perbuat, dan katakan ۞ Ketahuilah — semoga Allah tunjuk aku dan kamu — bahwa Saturnus dari planet atas, dan tubuhnya timah hitam; setelahnya Yupiter, tubuhnya timah; setelahnya Mars, tubuhnya besi; Matahari, tubuhnya tembaga; Mercury, raksa; Bulan, tubuhnya perak — atas urutan cakrawala sebagaimana lalu ۞ Dan ketahuilah cahaya lahiriah seperti sinar, dan sinar adalah batin cahaya. Maka tiap sinar [punya] cahaya, dan tiap cahaya sinar. Dan sinar adalah realitas yang Dia tunjuk, dan yang ditunjuk adalah realitas cahaya, roh, dan datangnya dunia kepadaku, sebagaimana sinar untuk esensi roh. Dan hewan — sinar dicurahkan padanya pertama, lalu cahaya kedua; lalu Ia curah pada halus sinar dan pada padatnya cahaya. Maka karenanya, dunia bawah seluruhnya antara sinar dan cahaya — sinar adalah rahasia kehidupan, rahasia tumbuh, dan rahasia pemeliharaan, kejasadan. Maka sinar dari batin tumbuhan, dan cahaya dari lahir tumbuhan. Maka lahir [tak punya] hewan dari sisi sinar, dan dari sisi cahaya [hanya punya] hewan tunggal oleh realitas dunia; dan pengetahuan tumbuhan adalah dengan realitas Loh ۞ Dan sebagaimana Loh adalah bumi bagi pena, tumbuhan adalah bumi bagi hewan; dan sebagaimana Loh adalah bumi untuk tulisan pena, tumbuhan membutuhkan tubuh hewan ۞ Dan ketahuilah tumbuhan — sinarnya seimbang atas cahayanya. Namun yang di dalamnya bagian seimbang dan sifatnya setara — ia yang darinya muncul pemeliharaan cocok bagi tubuh, dan ia yang melahirkan darah sehat unggul, yang menerima tata hukum dan jenis ketaatan atas. Dan setan tak punya akses dalam darah ini, pun sebab kejadian mendekatinya; dan tahun tak menghasilkan setelahnya obat darinya dalam kelanjutan waktu — dan ia dari ahli lima atas dan akhirat. Adapun [yang] cahayanya bersinar atas sinarnya — ia yang darinya muncul nafsu dan pemeliharaan; dan darinya pemenuhan sifat, sebab tiadanya kekuatan tembus pandang kumpulan kelembapan cahaya, karena cahaya lebih dekat ke lembap dan padat, sebab geraknya ke bawah. Dan ia yang darinya pikiran tertunda.
