English:
— 434 —

"…he has no right to it by that — and that is His saying exalted: 'And they love to be praised for what they did not do.' And as for the sanctification of the body, it is upon three kinds: the first, the sanctification of nourishment by seeking the lawful, and that by the mixing of reliance and gentleness with the manifest of entrusting; the second, the purity of the body by humility until [the gross] goes and its meaning remains and its dense is made subtle and its subtle appears — and that by the mixing of mention, retreat, and silence; the third, the persistence of the litanies and adherence to purity night and day and the use of night-vigil and service. And this is the station of the repenters and the first principles of the beginners and the first beginning of the god-fearing. So when your attributes are sanctified, the Spirit of Holiness meets you from the world of understandings with what is in your capacity to praise Him and speak by the ruling of the people of establishment from the secret of the secret of the wonders of the Dominion; and it appears upon the one of state and he sees what is in the worlds of the Footstool of the spirits, and he becomes of the people of unveilings. And we attained not this station except by our ceasing from the soully desires. * The Footstool — and it was the result of speaking with wisdom and the unveiling of the properties of the names. * And of its properties: whoever recites it its number while upon complete discipline attains awe and acceptance and nearness to Allah by this name — its recitation after every prayer its number in retreat and discipline; and he says after that 'Subbūḥ Quddūs, Lord of the angels and the spirit,' and persists in recitation and silence in the sanctification of the names, as came from the Prophet (upon him peace) that he said: 'Guard your mouths, for they are the channels of the Qur'ān' — pointing to their purity. And if it is written on a white paper with musk and saffron and a man carries it and multiplies its recitation — he is awe-inspiring and accepted. And if it is written in a silver ring and carried by one who commits sins and he persists in its recitation — Allah sanctifies him from desires. * And know that this name has contained a letter of the letters of the Greatest Name in its right, and its recitation multiplied by itself attains the sought — and this is its figure: And as for the mention standing by it, we say: the basmala — 'My God, sanctify me from the doubts of others and expand my breast by the light of lights, and unveil from me the world of dominion and Dominion; regard me by the Most Holy secret, the interpretation of the innermost, and unveil from my heart the veil of heedlessness and bring me near to You a nearness — O Subbūḥ, O Quddūs, and extend to me a subtlety from the subtleties of Your name al-Quddūs that I may sanctify by it my existence by the sanctification of the perfect, good righteous ones of the prophets and the righteous; and subordinate to me the servant of this name for adornment with realization and establishment, O Master of the Day of Recompense; answer, O master LAQĪTĪL and your helpers, by the right of Your name al-Quddūs.' (Section on His name the Exalted, al-Salām) * Know that the meaning of al-Salām is the One safe in His Essence from the marks of the contingent, and in His attributes from the attributes of the creatures — and that is not but for Allah; so therefore safety is not but to the Secure and to Him, as he (upon him peace) said: 'O Allah, You are the Peace and from You is peace and to You returns peace — blessed and exalted are You, O Possessor of majesty and honor.' * And know that no safety issues but from His name the Exalted al-Salām, and there is found in the right of the believer the peace, and it is upon an Islam of the elite and an Islam of the common. So the Islam of the common is His saying exalted: 'And to Him submitted whoever is in the heavens and the earth, willingly or unwillingly'; and the Islam of the elite is His saying exalted: 'So whomsoever Allah wills to guide, He expands his breast to Islam' — and that is that Allah made Islam added to Him, for He is the generality…"

Indonesia:
— ٤٣٤ —

"…dia tiada hak dengan itu — firman 'Mereka suka dipuji atas apa tak mereka buat.' Penyucian jasad 3: pertama suci makanan cari halal campur tawakal lemah lembut zahir tawakal; kedua suci badan dengan khusyuk hingga kasar pergi makna kekal halus zahir — campur zikir khalwat diam; ketiga istiqamah wirid suci malam siang jaga malam khidmat. Ini maqam taubat permulaan pemula permulaan wara'. Bila sifatmu disucikan, Roh Kudus jumpa dari alam faham dengan apa dayamu puji bercakap hukum ahli tamkin rahsia rahsia keajaiban Malakut; zahir pada ahli hal dia nampak alam Kursi roh, jadi ahli kasyaf. Kita capai maqam ini hanya dengan tinggal nafsu. * Kursi — hasil bercakap hikmah buka sifat nama. * Sifat: sesiapa baca bilangannya dengan riyadhah sempurna capai kekaguman penerimaan dekat pada Allah — baca selepas solat bilangannya dalam khalwat riyadhah; kata 'Subbūḥ Quddūs Tuhan malaikat roh,' istiqamah baca diam penyucian nama, sabda Nabi 'Jagalah mulutmu saluran Quran' — tunjuk suci. Tulis kertas putih kesturi za'faran bawa perbanyak baca — dikagumi diterima. Tulis cincin perak bawa ahli maksiat istiqamah — Allah suci dari nafsu. * Nama ini ada huruf Ism A'zam, baca darab diri capai diminta — inilah rupanya: Zikir berdiri — basmalah: 'Tuhanku sucikan aku dari syak selain, lapangkan dada cahaya cahaya, buka alam domini Malakut; pandang aku rahsia Aqdas tafsir batin, buka tabir lalai, dekatkan ya Subbūḥ ya Quddūs limpahkan halus nama-Mu sucikan wujudku dengan penyucian soleh nabi salih; takluk khadam nama ini untuk hakikat tamkin ya Tuhan hari pembalasan; jawab wahai tuan LAQĪTĪL penolong dengan nama al-Quddūs.' (Fasal nama al-Salām) * Ketahui makna al-Salām Yang selamat zat dari tanda baharu, sifat dari sifat makhluk — tiada melainkan Allah; maka keselamatan hanya pada Yang Selamat kepada-Nya, sabda 'Ya Allah Engkau Sejahtera dari-Mu sejahtera kepada-Mu kembali sejahtera, Maha Suci Tinggi wahai Pemilik kemuliaan.' * Tiada keselamatan keluar melainkan nama al-Salām, ada pada mukmin Islam khusus dan umum. Islam umum firman 'Kepada-Nya tunduk semua di langit bumi rela paksa'; Islam khusus firman 'Sesiapa Allah mahu hidup luaskan dada untuk Islam' — Allah jadikan Islam sandaran-Nya kerana Dia keumuman…"
