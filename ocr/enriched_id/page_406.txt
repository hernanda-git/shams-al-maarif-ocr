الأسماء التي أولها الباء وحملها من تعسر عليه رزقه يسر الله عليه وإذا كتب هذا الحرف ودعا كل
اسم أوله باء وسقى للمريض الذي مرضه من اليبوسة فإن الله يعافيه ويشفيه وإذا كتب حرف
الباء ١٦ والبسملة ١٩ فهو لكل ما تريد وتكتب معها قوله تعالى بديع السموات والأرض
إلخ الآية وإذا كتب وربط مع اسم من تريد والقمر في البطن وكتب الأسماء الحسنى والإضمار
وحمله فإن الله يعطفه عليه وكذلك من تعسرت عليه الأسباب تيسرت وإذا أردت المحبة والقبول
فاستقبل الهلال أول ليلة إذا ظهر في الغرب واكتب الحرف ١٩ مع إضمار ١٦ وأنت تقول
أجب يا خادم حرف الباء بحق بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم ثم استلم القمر وامسح به وجهك ثم مسح
الكتابة بلسانك تفعل ذلك كل ليلة إلى تمام البدر ١٤ ليلة فإن الأرواح تعطف عليك وكل حاجة
لك تقضى وإذا كتبت الحرف في كفك وتلوت الدعوة والإضمار واستقبلت القمر

"the knowledge, by the knowledge of the origin of matters, and by the secret of Your succoring by the Alif, and by Your penetrating command — by Kulīliyā, Milīliyā, Ṭaliyā, Haniyā, Riyā, Thiyā, Haythā — and by the Alif of command, and by the right of Ahiyā Sharāḥiyā, Adonai, Aṣbā'ūt, Āl Shaddai, and the great command — I rebuke the greatest president Hamṭahīl, Ayl Hamṭahīl, that you trust in such-and-such — the hastening, al-waḥā." [§] And know that whoever recites this invocation and persists in it, He casts his love into hearts. And if you will vengeance upon one of the helpers and kings, you write the figure of the Alif on an egg and recite the invocation and place the egg in the fire — and the helper attends and fulfills your need. And this is the description of the implicit concentration — you say: "Answer, O great King, the master Ṭahṭā'īl, the greatest president, and command by the right of Hīh 2, Yahūn 2, Shakmahīl 2, Saḥlū — answer and descend and manifest to me in a good form — al-waḥā, the hastening." And know that in the spirituality of the Alif you need no incense; but in other than it, incense with coriander and sandarac, hung in the air; and you write for what you willed and cast in the fire like the agitation, and the spirits and the seeker dispose as they will, then you say "Answer, O Alif, and do such-and-such." [The letter Bā'] — it is cold and dry, and it is of the remaining letters; it is the inward of the Alif and the secret of existence, and its disposal stands until the Day of Resurrection; and by it they know the realities of the worlds and deduce by it His Oneness. And the Bā' has a signification in all the worlds, high and low; and Allah honored the letter Bā' and made it the beginning of the basmala and the first page of Adam and of the heavens. [§] And know that when Allah sent down the Qur'an upon the Prophet — upon him peace — Gabriel said to him "Read, O Muhammad, in the name of your Lord" — and the Bā' was implicit for the Essence and the attributes; it implicitly concealed the Essence — the secret of the manifestation in its like you knew — and it implicitly concealed the attributes by the secret of the deeds. And when Allah created the Bā' He created with it 24 kings, under the hand of each king what Allah willed of angels glorifying Allah; and for that it was a key to the treasuries of the scriptures, and in it is the secret of expansion, and it is of the forms of the Alif. [§] And know that if you write the Bā' its original count and write with it the names beginning with Bā' and carry it, for whom provision is straitened Allah eases for him. And if you write this letter and call every name beginning with Bā' and give to drink to the patient whose illness is from dryness, Allah restores him and heals him. And if you write the letter Bā' 16 and the basmala 19, it is for all you will, and you write with it His saying "Originator of the heavens and the earth" etc. the verse. And if you write and bind with the name of whom you will while the Moon is in the Belly, and write the Beautiful Names and the implicit concentration and carry it, Allah turns him toward you; and likewise for whom the causes were straitened, they were eased. "ilmu, dengan ilmu asal urusan, rahsia pertolonganMu Alif, perintahMu tembus — Kulīliyā, Milīliyā, Ṭaliyā, Haniyā, Riyā, Thiyā, Haythā — demi Alif perintah, hak Ahiyā Sharāḥiyā, Adonai, Aṣbā'ūt, Āl Shaddai, perintah agung — kutegur presiden tertinggi Hamṭahīl, Ayl Hamṭahīl, agar kamu tawakal sekian — segera al-waḥā." [§] Sesiapa baca doa ini istiqamah, dicampak cintanya ke hati. Bila mahu balas dari pembantu raja, tulis rupa Alif pada telur bacakan doa letak telur api — pembantu hadir tunaikan hajat. Inilah sifat idmar: "Sahut wahai Raja agung penghulu Ṭahṭā'īl presiden tertinggi, perintah demi Hīh 2, Yahūn 2, Shakmahīl 2, Saḥlū — sahut turun zahir rupa baik — al-waḥā segera." Dalam rohani Alif tak perlu ubat; selainnya ubat ketumbar sandarac gantung udara; tulis apa dikehendaki campak api macam kegairahan, roh pencari urus lalu "Sahut wahai Alif buat sekian." [Huruf Bā'] sejuk kering, dari huruf kekal; batin Alif rahsia wujud, tasarufnya kebangkitan; dengannya tahu hakikat alam dan kesatuannya. Bā' bermakna semua alam tinggi rendah; Allah muliakan Bā' jadikan permulaan basmalah, helaian pertama Adam, langit. [§] Bila Allah turunkan Qur'an pada Nabi, Jibril kata "Bacalah dengan nama Tuhanmu" — Bā' tersirat untuk zat sifat; zat tersirat rahsia tajalli, sifat tersirat rahsia perbuatan. Allah cipta Bā' bersamanya 24 raja, tiap raja malaikat bertasbih; maka kunci khazanah kitab, rahsia bashth, dari bentuk Alif. [§] Tulis Bā' bilangan asalnya dengan nama bermula Bā' bawa — siapa rezeki sempit Allah mudahkan. Tulis huruf seru nama bermula Bā' beri minum pesakit kering — Allah sihatkan. Tulis Bā' 16 dan basmalah 19 untuk semua dikehendaki, tulis "Badi' al-samawat" dll ayat. Tulis ikat nama dikehendaki bulan di Batn, tulis nama indah idmar bawa — Allah condongkan; sebab sempit jadi mudah. Bila mahu kasih penerimaan, hadap anak bulan malam pertama di barat, tulis huruf 19 dengan idmar 16 sambil "Sahut wahai khadam Bā' demi Bismillah" — terima bulan sapu muka, sapu tulisan dengan lidah tiap malam hingga purnama 14 malam — roh condong hajat tertunaikan. Tulis di tapak bacakan doa idmar hadap bulan "Hidup wahai roh huruf tunaikan hajat campur rohku antara alam" — jadi. Tulis dalam bekas glazur dengan idmar basmalah "Badi' al-samawat" nama bermula Bā' letak minyak Yāsīn sapu muka — penerimaan sekalian makhluk. Tulis rupa Bā' Jumaat dengan basmalah nama bermula Bā' idmar bawa di lengan — Allah buka dada buang malas lemah-lembut. Bila mahu seseorang, berkat khadamnya, patahkan ikat bersamanya —
