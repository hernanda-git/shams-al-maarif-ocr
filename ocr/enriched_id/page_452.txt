"…the lower world by what is better than it, for Your higher command, and gathered between the opposites for the appearance of the clearest, manifest secret; and they interlaced by the interlacing of the spirits and the denseness of the specters, until the pen of governance ran with what You willed of corruption and rectitude. I ask You, O Bringer-forth of the existents from the non-existents, and Governor of the spheres by the subtleties of the movements, that You cut me off from everything cutting me off from You. O Allah, O You who saved from the events of time, save me from error and forgetfulness and laziness and deprivation, and from the evil of the satan and from every preoccupier that preoccupies me from You. O Allah, O Bārī, I ask You that You subordinate to me Your servant TAMALSĪYĀTHĪL, that he be a helper for me in my affair, by the right of Your name al-Bārī. There is no servant who recited this mention on Tuesday and was imprisoned but he came out of his prison, or [had] care or grief but Allah relieved him; and whoever took it as a mention, Allah provided him love and awe; and if he multiplies the recitation, all the worlds come to him and speak to him the hidden things of affairs. (Section on His name the Exalted, al-Muṣawwir) Know that al-Muṣawwir is the Fashioner of the thing and the Distinguisher of it from other than it. And creation is the bringing-forth and the forming and the shaping — the complete particularization upon the voluntary kind. He said exalted 'And We indeed created you' — intending the appearance of power over the first bringing-in, and it is the world of the sewing; then He said 'We fashioned you' with the connection of the enveloping, for between the first day, the day of bringing-forth, and the day of extraction is what none knows its measure but Allah. He said exalted 'O man, what deluded you about your Lord the Generous, who created you' — intending the bringing-forth of power; 'then proportioned you' — intending the inward, and it is the place of the proportioning and the alteration in the second and third day for the third phase, in His saying 'In whatever form He willed He assembled you.' And from it passed the fashioned ones; and the spirits are the forms of the Real, and the form is the form of the spirit, and it came not but by the breath of Allah, and His blowing is the secret of life. * And know that the forms divide into two — outward and inward: so the outward is what the shape appeared from, and the inward is what is apprehended of its inward by the eye of insight. * And know that the names are the spheres of existence, and the inward form is an expression of the innate disposition, rich between the names and the deeds; so the realities of the names and the deeds appeared His encompassing of existence, and they are constant witnessing, revealing the principle — first absolute over the finite end; so they are the secret of the spirit and the divine breath. And Allah created all the existents by His names and deeds in distinction, and invented them in sum and detail, and deposited that in the marital innate disposition to the first day in the eternity; therefore they were obliged to Him and stood firm for His knowledge and longed for His meeting and the turning to His commands. And whoever is unveiled to him the secrets of the Dominion witnessed that, as Ibrāhīm (upon him peace) saw, where he found difficult the realities of composition by His saying exalted 'And when Ibrāhīm said: My Lord, show me how You give life to the dead — He said: Have you not believed? He said: Yes, but that my heart be at peace.' And it contained three meanings: the preservation of the body, the passing of life — and it is the spirit; the second, the appearance of the rulings of mercy to the Abode of the Hereafter from the passing of the blowing in the forms and the gathering; and the third, and it is the greatest phase — my brother — the revival of the dead in the bodily and the meaning-world. "…alam bawah dengan yang lebih baik untuk perintah tinggi, kumpul lawan zahir rahsia nyata; bersilang roh dan kepadatan bayangan hingga pena tadbir jalan dengan kerosakan kebaikan. Kuminta wahai Pengada wujud dari tiada, Urus sfera hal gerakan, potong aku dari setiap yang memotong dari-Mu. Ya Allah yang selamatkan dari masa, selamatkan dari silap lupa malas terbiar, dari jahat syaitan setiap sibuk dari-Mu. Ya Allah ya Bārī takluk hamba TAMALSĪYĀTHĪL penolong aku dengan nama al-Bārī. Tiada hamba baca Selasa dipenjara melainkan keluar, atau cemas duka Allah lepaskan; jadikan zikir Allah beri kasih kekaguman; perbanyak baca semua alam datang bercakap rahsia urusan. (Fasal al-Muṣawwir) Ketahui al-Muṣawwir yang membentuk membeza dari selain. Ciptaan kejadian rupa bentuk — khas pada jenis ikhtiyari. Firman 'Kami cipta kamu' zahir kuasa bawa masuk alam jahit; 'Kami bentuk' sambung selubung, antara hari pertama kejadian dan hari zahir apa tak diketahui melainkan Allah. Firman 'Wahai manusia apa tipu Tuhanmul yang cipta' kuasa; 'lalu proportionkan' batin tempat susunan ubah hari 2-3 fasa ketiga 'dalam rupa dikehendaki Dia susun kamu.' Dari situ bentuk lalu; roh rupa Yang Haq, rupa rupa roh, dari nafas Allah tiupan rahsia hidup. * Rupa dua: zahir batin; zahir yang nampak, batin dengan mata basirah. * Nama sfera wujud, rupa batin ungkap fitrah kaya antara nama perbuatan; hakikat nama perbuatan zahir limpupan wujud syuhud kekal buka prinsip pertama mutlak akhir terhingga; rahsia roh nafas ilahi. Allah cipta semua wujud nama perbuatan bezakan, cipta ringkas terperinci letak fitrah berpasangan hari pertama azali; wajib padanya teguh ilmu rindu jumpa perintah. Sesiapa dibuka rahsia Malakut nampak seperti Ibrahim bila sukar susunan firman 'Ya Tuhanku tunjuk bagaimana hidupkan mati — Belum beriman? Ya tapi tenang hati.'
