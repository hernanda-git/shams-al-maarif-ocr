English:
— 464 —

"…anything was, and carries it, a child is benefited by it from every evil; and if one of the travelers persists in it, then recites the standing mention — the servant comes to him and informs him of the noble stone, and his name is JALHĪYĀ'ĪL, and he benefits from the inward and the manifest diseases. And as for his mention — the basmala: 'O Allah, You are al-Ḥalīm, who witnesses the disobedience of the disobedient and the corruption of the eye of the perverts, and does not hasten with the punishment and the anger upon what You see of the ugly attributes; You give respite to the disobedient to the coming and accept the repentance of the corrupter and the wrongdoer in what he committed and erred; and there remained not after the respite the taking and the vengeance and the chastisement by the penalty and the seizing by the forelocks and the feet. I ask You by the secret of Your equality upon Your Throne and what Your will enclosed of the decreed judgment in Your ancient knowledge, that You make enduring Your look upon forbearance and ease Your glance by the blessing and the mercy, and soften my heart from Your forbearance — by what moves from me the satans, so my soul be reassured to You by the lordly path; and that You subordinate to me the servant of this name, JALHĪYĀ'ĪL (upon him peace), O Lord of the worlds.' (Section on His name the Exalted, al-'Aẓīm) * Know that His name al-'Aẓīm is of the posited [things] of the names of the bodies, and in it is what indicates the sensible bodies — so the earth is not encompassed by it, as the sight does not encompass [it], as the sky and what is in it and what is greater than it; and the intellects and the Dominion and the Throne and the Footstool are imagined by the insights, and of it is what is not conceived that the intellect encompass its essence, and the Footstool is not encompassed by it — and He is al-'Aẓīm, the absolute, who passed beyond the limit of the intellects, and He is Allah the Exalted. * And it has a retreat and a discipline; so if the traveler recites it, let him add to it His name al-'Alī — and these two names, they are a great secret; so if the traveler wills to enter the retreat, he wears pure garments and recites the name after every prayer its number until the servant attends, and his name is QUṬYĀ'ĪL, and he fulfills his need; and if he writes to the king or the sultan, then the troops are sent after him; and if he writes in a seal of silver or gold and around it the name of the king and persists in its recitation — Allah raises his rank and attains his aims. And as for his mention — the basmala: 'O Allah, You are al-'Aẓīm, the Most Great, not as the greatness of the earthly bodies nor as the greatness of the heavenly spirits — for one of these two has a finite measure and posited relations and simple bodily bounded compounding things; but Your greatness, O God of the worlds, O Lord of the firsts and the lasts — it is the greatness of majesty and splendor and glory and the sultanate of Your divine force and the comprehensiveness of the lordly power and the highness of the greatness of the matter of the subdual of the oneness. I ask You, O You who are thus, that You make my heart watchful to Your greatness, that the submission endure for me between Your hands in Your awe. O Allah, You are al-Ghafūr, al-Ḥalīm, al-Shakūr — clothe my essence by Your greatness, that every tyrant rebel submit to me, and He subdue his evil from me and repel his guile. O Allah, O 'Aẓīm — whoever conversed with his Lord by it, his enemy's evil was sufficed from him. And if the traveler recites it in his retreat, Allah secures him from the evil of the jinn.' (Section on His name the Exalted, al-Ghafūr) Know that the meaning of al-Ghafūr preceded in His name al-Ghaffār, and it is beneficial for whoever wills to repel the anger of the kings; so if he recites it by any name of a king or a ruler and entrusts the standing king — ḤARFĀ'ĪL — and writes the accordance on an auspicious rising and writes the name of al-'Alawī, and enters upon him — Allah raises his rank. And likewise for the reconciliation between the mutually-hostile — he writes and carries, and his mention preceded in His name al-Ghaffār. (Section…"

Zikir basmalah: 'Ya Allah Engkau al-Ḥalīm yang saksikan maksiat fasad mata sesat, tiada segera hukum marah atas sifat buruk; beri masa pendosa bertaubat, terima taubat perosak zalim atas salah silap; sesudah masa tiada balas dendam azab pencabut. Kuminta rahsia kesetaraan 'Arasy kehendak hukum azali, pandangan-Mu sabar redha belas kasih lembut hati dari syaitan, jiwa tenang suluk rabbani; takluk khadam JALHĪYĀ'ĪL ya Tuhan alam.' (Fasal al-'Aẓīm) * Nama al-'Aẓīm dari letak nama jasad, menunjuk jasad deria — bumi tiada diliputi penglihatan seperti langit besarnya; akal Malakut 'Arasy Kursi bayangkan, ada yang tak terjangkau esensi, Kursi tiada diliputi — Dia al-'Aẓīm mutlak lampaui akal, Allah. * Ada khalwat riyadhah; bila ahli suluk baca tambah nama al-'Alī — dua nama rahsia agung; masuk khalwat pakai pakaian suci baca selepas solat bilangan hingga khadam QUṬYĀ'ĪL tunaikan; tulis raja sultan tentera dikirim; tulis cincin perak emas keliling nama raja istiqamah — Allah angkat darjat capai matlamat.
