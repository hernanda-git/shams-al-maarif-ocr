English:
— 239 —

…and many stations they take, and the doors of heaven open by what is in them — "victory from Allah and an imminent opening," and "the heaven was opened and became doors," "when the victory of Allah and the opening come, and you see the people entering the religion of Allah in multitudes, then glorify your Lord with praise and ask His forgiveness — indeed He is ever Accepting of repentance" — so write them and hang them on your right upper arm, and I did that, and Allah opened and eased for me from whence I did not reckon. * His saying, exalted: "So you will remember what I say to you, and I entrust my affair to Allah — indeed Allah is Seeing of the servants" — this noble verse: whoever engraves it on a seal and seals with it is enveloped by it in all his states; and if he enters with it upon a wrongdoer and recites it before him, my affair is sufficed by the might of Allah, exalted, and His power, and this is its form. Some scholars, may Allah be pleased with him, said: whoever wants to reach the greatest wealth and the utmost glory, let him write His saying, exalted: "Say: O Allah, Owner of dominion…" (to "without reckoning") on gold, silver, or paper on an auspicious day in the first hour of Thursday, and let him who wants the greatest secret and treasure fast, or set apart a day eating no animal nor what issues from an animal, breaking fast on the lawful. And let him recite Sūrat al-Ḍuḥā a thousand times daily at sunrise, then say "O Allah, ease for me the ease You eased for many servants, and enrich me by Your bounty from besides You"; likewise after sunset the same number, and place the drawn figure in a pure pouch on the first day with forty dirhams — so when he spends, none is quicker than the number spent but it remains fixed and unchanged, particular to the masters of states; so understand — the door of wealth is opened. * Know — and may Allah guide us — that kingship and dominion [Say: O Allah, Owner of dominion… to "without reckoning"], and ministry andcommand [Make for me a minister from my family, Hārūn my brother, strengthen my back by him, and make me with him his brother Hārūn a minister], and love and obedience [I cast upon you love from Me, and that you be formed before My eye], [if you spent all that is in the earth you would not have united their hearts, but Allah united them — He is Mighty, Wise], and He is intense in love of good [they love them — Allah wrote those who believe love Allah more strongly], and victory and overcoming [victory is only from Allah, and Allah will help you with an honored help], [enter upon them the gate, and when you enter it you are victorious, and upon Allah rely if you are believers] — so understand that, for it is not possible to state more explicitly, and measure by it what you understood of the verses you had not understood. * And know that whoever perseveres in reciting al-Ḍuḥā for forty days, each day after finishing his invocation and recitation saying "O Allah, O Enricher, enrich me by Your majesty from Your forbidden with a wealth I fear not poverty from; guide me in my error and teach me, for I am ignorant" — Allah, exalted, sends to him one who teaches him wisdom in his sleep or his waking. * His saying, exalted: "And Muhammad is not but a messenger; messengers have passed away before him. If he dies or is killed, will you turn back on your heels? And whoever turns back on his heels will never harm Allah at all, and Allah will reward the thankful" — if it is drawn in this form and at its directions: Muḥammad, Jibrīl, Mīkā'īl, Isrāfīl, 'Azrā'īl, upon them peace, and a person carries it with him — he is safe from the evil of jinn and men, and the evil of the visitant of night and day, and is guarded by it. And this is its form: [a magic square for Sūrat Muḥammad, the five names written at its sides].

Indonesia:
— 239 —

…dan banyak maqām mereka ambil, maka pintu langit terbuka dengan apa yang ada pada mereka — "pertolongan dari Allah dan kemenangan yang dekat," "langit dibuka serta menjadi pintu-pintu," "apabila datang pertolongan Allah dan kemenangan, dan engkau lihat manusia masuk agama Allah berbondong-bondong, maka bertasbihlah dengan memuji Rabbmu dan mohon ampun — sesungguhnya Dia Maha Penerima taubat" — maka tulislah dan gantung di lengan kananmu, aku melakukannya lalu Allah membuka dan memudahkan dari tempat tiada kuduga. * Firman-Nya Ta'ala: "Maka kelak kamu ingat apa yang kukatakan, dan aku serahkan urusanku pada Allah — sesungguhnya Allah Maha Melihat hamba" — ayat mulia ini: barangsiapa mengukirnya pada cincin dan memeteraikan, terbungkus olehnya dalam segala keadaan; dan apabila masuk padanya pada orang zalim serta membacanya di hadapannya, cukuplah urusanku dengan kekuasaan Allah Ta'ala dan quwwa-Nya, dan ini rupanya. Sebagian ulama raḍiyallāhu 'anhu berkata: barangsiapa ingin kekayaan terbesar dan keagungan tertinggi, hendaklah menulis firman-Nya Ta'ala "Katakanlah: Ya Allah Pemilik kerajaan…" (hingga "tanpa hisab") pada emas, perak, atau kertas di hari baik jam pertama Kamis, dan hendaklah yang ingin rahasia agung serta khazanah berpuasa, atau menetapkan hari tiada makan hewan nora apa yang keluar darinya, berbuka dengan halal. Dan hendaklah membaca Sūrat al-Ḍuḥā seribu kali tiap hari saat terbit matahari, lalu "Ya Allah, mudahkanlah kemudahan yang Engkau mudahkan pada banyak hamba, dan cukupkan aku dengan anugerah-Mu dari selain-Mu"; demikian setelah maghrib bilangan itu, serta meletakkan gambar dalam kantong suci hari pertama bersama empat puluh dirham — maka apabila membelanjakan, tiada yang lebih cepat dari bilangan yang dibelanjakan melainkan ia tetap dan tiada berubah, khusus ahli ahwal; pahamilah — pintu kekayaan terbuka. * Ketahuilah — semoga Allah menunjukkan — bahwa kerajaan dan kekuasaan [Katakanlah: Ya Allah Pemilik kerajaan… hingga "tanpa hisab"], dan kementerian serta kepemimpinan [Jadikanlah untukku menteri dari keluargaku Hārūn saudaraku, teguhkan punggungku dengannya, dan jadikanlah aku bersamanya saudaranya Hārūn menteri], dan cinta serta ketaatan [Aku lemparkan padamu cinta dari-Ku, dan engkau dibentuk di depan mata-Ku], [jika engkau nafkahkan semua yang di bumi tiada menyatukan hati mereka, tapi Allah menyatukan mereka — Dia Maha Perkasa lagi Maha Bijaksana], dan Dia sangat cinta kebaikan [mereka mencintai mereka — Allah tulis orang beriman mencintai Allah lebih kuat], serta kemenangan dan penguasaan [kemenangan hanyalah dari Allah, dan Allah menolongmu dengan pertolongan mulia], [masuklah pada mereka melalui pintu, maka apabila kamu masuk niscaya menang, dan pada Allah bertawakallah jika beriman] — maka pahamilah, karena tiada mungkin dinyatakan lebih terang, dan ukurlah dengannya apa yang engkau pahami dari ayat yang belum kau pahami. * Dan ketahuilah bahwa barangsiapa bertekun membaca al-Ḍuḥā empat puluh hari, tiap hari setelah selesai dzikir dan bacaannya membaca "Ya Allah, wahai Pemberi Kaya, cukupkanlah aku dengan keagungan-Mu dari yang haram-Mu dengan kekayaan yang tiada kutakuti fakir darinya; tunjukilah aku dalam kesesatanku, dan ajarkan aku karena aku bodoh" — Allah Ta'ala mengutus padanya yang mengajarinya hikmah dalam tidur atau jaganya. * Firman-Nya Ta'ala: "Dan Muḥammad tiada lain kecuali rasul; sungguh telah berlalu rasul sebelumnya. Apabila ia mati atau dibunuh, apakah kamu berbalik pada tumitmu? Dan siapa berbalik pada tumitnya tiada akan mendatangkan mudarat pada Allah sedikit pun, dan Allah akan membalas orang bersyukur" — apabila digambar dalam rupa ini dan pada arah-arahnya: Muḥammad, Jibrīl, Mīkā'īl, Isrāfīl, 'Azrā'īl 'alaihim al-salām, dan seseorang membawanya — ia aman dari kejahatan jin dan manusia, dan kejahatan yang datang malam dan siang, serta terpelihara olehnya. Dan ini rupanya: [kuadrat ajaib Sūrat Muḥammad, lima nama ditulis di sisinya].
