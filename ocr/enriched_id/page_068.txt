So Allah (exalted is He) made the forms of all the letters in the heart, and they are spiritual, and they are what appear in human pronunciation and in numerical writing, with letters in the chest and letters in the tongue and letters in the hands, and that is the meaning of His (exalted is He) saying: ṣ وَالْقُرْآنِ ذِي الذِّكْرِ q وَالْقُرْآنِ الْمَجِيدِ n وَالْقَلَمِ وَمَا يَسْطُرُونَ and the letters indicate the signs of the Book, a reminder for possessors of intellects. And every letter has three stations according to the three movements: damm, fatha, and jarr, and the letters of prolongation and softness among them are upon the likeness of the elements, and each one of the three is bodily and spiritual and psychic, so they are nine, and the numbers are nine, and the spheres are nine, and the natures and the senses are nine, so the correspondence became manifest. So search for the secrets of number and letters; you will find abundant knowledges in the gatherings and separations in accordance with the Rahmaniyyah and the Rahimiyyah from Bismillah al-Rahman al-Rahim. From Bismillah the cosmos is nourished, and by eating and by drinking. So reflect on the secret of the Qur'an; you will find it from the multiplication of six by seven, and the suras of the Qur'an likewise, and the six forms in the complete number according to the number of the days in which Allah created the heavens and the earth and what is between them. So these three are detailed into nineteen: the seven heavens with the Throne and the Kursi, and the earth ten, and the letters ten, which are the beginnings of the suras, arranged upon five ranks, without dual and triple and quadruple. And as for their total, it is twenty-eight.

(Section on the Letters, and they are of two divisions)
Dotted by two and dotted by three. So the dotted by three are shiin and tha', and shiin indicates the separated gathering and tha' indicates the gathered gathering, and the dotted by two are ta', ya', and qaf. So ta'—its manifestation is in His kingdom, and ya'—its manifestation is in His power, and qaf—its manifestation is in His limit. And everything from it is a manifestation of the قائم and the قادر, and everything is made clear, encompassing like the light of the sun and the essences. And ya' distinguishes between the two ascriptions, and every born one stood, or every gathering by which a people are borne, like الشيء, for every thing by which a people are. And shiin, its meaning is "bring us with the secret of the letters and its three aspects," as in shiin and sha'n and thighar and thaghm and the like. And nun, its meaning is a manifest manifestation, like the nun of al-Hasan and the light of the sun and wara'. And the axis of the lie by which its inner secrets manifest. And as for the mazn by which a hidden secret manifests, so that the position of nun may be preserved in a word that comprises it, the Essence, its outward and inward, and what is between them; and therefore a garden was confined in its form, as in the name, the named, from its naming, and travel is built upon the character of men and the like. And al-Hasan, may Allah (exalted is He) be pleased with him, said: in the Qur'an is the knowledge of everything, and the knowledge of the Qur'an is in the letters that are in the beginnings of the suras. And know that the letters are in lam-alif, and lam-alif is in the alif, and the knowledge of alif is in the point, and the knowledge of the point is in the original knowledge in pre-eternity and the will, and the knowledge of the will is in the unseen of air, and the knowledge of air is in the wearing of the mass of a thing, and He is the Hearing, the Seeing. And it was said regarding the secret of shiin that it is a name among the names of Allah (exalted is He), like the rest of the letters of the alphabet that are in the beginnings of the suras, and they are the fourteen luminous letters, not repeated, and they are these ( ā ح r ط k ل m ن s ع f ص هـ ى ). And it is narrated from Ibn 'Abbas, may Allah (exalted is He) be pleased with them both, that he used to say: beginnings.
