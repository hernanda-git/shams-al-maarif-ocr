English:
— 545 —

[Correspondence table: the sixteen geomantic houses mapped, column by column, to their associated figures and categories — Bayt al-Māl (the treasury-position), al-Af'āl (the actions), al-A'māl (the works), al-Nafaq (the passage), al-Faqr (the poverty), Tasīn al-Dawī (the settling of the course) — arranged as a sixteen-row table of the houses (Jamā'ah, Qabḍ Dākhil, Qabḍ Khārij, Naṣrah Khārijah, Ijtimā', Naṣrah Dākhilah, 'Aqlah, Naṣrah Khārijah, Inkīs, Naqī al-Khad, 'Atabah Khārijah, Bayāḍ, 'Atabah Dākhilah, Jawdah, al-Ḥayān, Ḥumrah) each linked to its figure-rows.]

(The Fifteenth Intent: on the conditions of the practice of geomancy) It should be [practiced] from the time of the rising of the sun until its equinox [noon], and if it passes the zenith; and that he strike from the first of the day until its midpoint; and that he observe in it the auspicious days of the month and avoid the inauspicious days. [A poetic couplet on fortune and ill-fortune:] 'Your lover guards your desire — will you return nights contrary to hope? So let the fortune linger in it, for it came; and its Lexicon — the ill-fortune in it has occurred.' And that its practitioner be cheerful, of expanded breast, free of distractions; and it is disliked on a cloudy day and at the time of wind and rain and at the time of the departure of the beasts [to pasture]. And the soundest geomancy is what is struck at night; and that he not look except for one who comes to him desiring or ailing or grieved, not one obstinately playing — for the need only comes out according to the concern of the querent with it, since the works are not established except by resolve and a firm conviction of the answer, for the souls have a complete effect and a strong action when directed to their sought.

[Closing reference: (25 — Shams al-Ma'ārif — Fourth [Part])]

Indonesia:
— 545 —

[Jadual korespondensi: enam belas rumah geomancy dipetakan, lajur demi lajur, ke angka dan kategori berkaitan — Bayt al-Māl (kedudukan perbendaharaan), al-Af'āl (tindakan), al-A'māl (amalan), al-Nafaq (lintasan), al-Faqr (kefaqiran), Tasīn al-Dawī (penetapan perjalanan) — disusun sebagai jadual enam belas baris rumah (Jamā'ah, Qabḍ Dākhil, Qabḍ Khārij, Naṣrah Khārijah, Ijtimā', Naṣrah Dākhilah, 'Aqlah, Naṣrah Khārijah, Inkīs, Naqī al-Khad, 'Atabah Khārijah, Bayāḍ, 'Atabah Dākhilah, Jawdah, al-Ḥayān, Ḥumrah) setiap dihubung ke baris angkanya.]

(Maqasad Kelima Belas: tentang syarat amalan geomancy) Ia hendaklah [diamal] dari terbit matahari hingga ekuinoks [tengah hari], dan jika melepasi zenit; dan hendaklah ia memukul dari awal hari hingga pertengahannya; dan hendaklah ia memperhatikan hari baik bulan serta menjauhi hari sial. [Rangkap puitis tentang tuah dan sial:] 'Kekasihmu menjaga keinginanmu — adakah engkau kembalikan malam bertentangan harapan? Maka biarkan tuah melengah padanya, kerana ia datang; dan Leksikonnya — sial padanya telah terjadi.' Dan pengamalnya hendaklah ceria, dada terbuka, bebas gangguan; dan dibenci pada hari berawan serta waktu angin dan hujan dan waktu binatang ternak pergi [meragut]. Dan geomancy paling sah ialah yang dipukul malam; dan jangan ia memandang melainkan bagi yang datang kepadanya berkeinginan atau sakit atau berduka, bukan yang degil bermain — kerana hajat hanya keluar menurut keprihatinan penanya, kerana amalan tidak tegak melainkan dengan azam dan keyakinan teguh akan jawapan, kerana jiwa mempunyai kesan sempurna dan tindakan kuat bila ditujukan ke yang dicari.

[Rujukan penutup: (25 — Shams al-Ma'ārif — Keempat [Bahagian])]
