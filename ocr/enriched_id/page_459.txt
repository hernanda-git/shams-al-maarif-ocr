وأما عالم الأيد العرش الرحماني يتضح رزق المقادر باتصال التدابير فيكثر الطرق إلى السبيل
الأقوم ويدرك الأرواح الصافية حقائق الآلام والنعماء * واعلم أن مجموع الإرادات العلويات
مجموع الآيات الكتابية والكلمة الإلهية لربادة أي آية الملك القدمي وحقيقة السوالا على وقد
أودع الله في هذه الآية شهود الإرادات باختلاف أدوارها وتعاقب حركات الفلك الطوالع
النمائية ومع ذلك يقارن شعاعاً بحركة الفلك الوجودي القائم بها هذا العارف الإنسان وما يقابل
منها في كل طالع ودقيقة من درج الفلك وإن الباري جل جلاله جعل هذا الإنسان قائماً
بالكمالات الإلهية والنسب النورانية وجعل يمينه تحت الصراط المستقيم وشماله تحت الجحيم لأن
هذه الكمالات المركبة في هذه الخليقة وهذه العلوم التي أودعها الله في سماه شمس معارفه ثم فاضت
عليه العلويات لأن كل ذرة في الوجود تشتمل على رقيقة من الرقائق وعلى عالم من العلويات

المظهر وجعل هذه الأسماء قائمة على هذه الصورة الإنسانية في الشخص إذا كان عارفاً بأصول

English:
— 453 —

"…the limbs, and he was a vicegerent who had been provided before [the rest of] the creatures in the world of His power — so this is the creation of the world by the human world. And as for the world of the supreme, the Throne, the merciful — there is made clear the provision of the Powerful by the connection of the governances, multiplying the paths to the straightest way, and the pure spirits perceive the realities of the pains and the blessings. * And know that the sum of the high wills is the sum of the bookish signs and the divine word for the turning — i.e. the sign of the ancient dominion and the reality of the asking; and Allah deposited in this sign the witnessing of the wills by the difference of their cycles and the succession of the movements of the rising sphere of growth. And with that there accompanies a ray with the movement of the existential sphere standing by them — this human knower; and what opposes them in every rising and minute of the degrees of the sphere. And the Creator — exalted be His glory — made this human standing by the divine perfections and the luminous relations, and made his right under the straight path and his left under hell, for these perfections are compounded in this creature, and these sciences which Allah deposited in His heaven — the sun of His knowledges — then the highs poured upon him, for every atom in existence comprises a subtlety from the subtleties and a world from the highs. And the names are ninety names; every name has a station, a name from the named. For that, by his adornment and his preparedness in that manifestation, and He made these names standing upon this human form in the person if he is a knower of the roots of things and knows what is in them by that straight path — he is of the companions of the right; and if he travels the path of the accursed, he is of the people of the left and of the worshipped. (Notice) Know that Allah the Exalted created seven heavens and seven earths, and created the vicegerents for the manifestation of the seven satans, the seven wandering stars, the seven — and likewise the near angels and the spheres and the comprehensive attributes and the divine names and the essential names, and created Paradise upon seven. * And know that the poles are seven, and by them the seven lowers are circled, and upon them is the drawing of the lights of the highs, each pouring upon the other Throne — and al-Ghawth, for he extends from the suspended Throne in His outpouring; therefore the drawing of the seven from him is by the mediation of the four, and the seven poles extend the seventy and the four the head of the forty, and the gathering from the relation of the drunkenness, and every world returns the other. This is the figure of the human and what he has of attributes and names — what is under his two feet, right and left. He (upon him peace) said: 'Paradise is beneath the feet of the mothers.' And this is its figure in the coming sheet."

Indonesia:
— ٤٥٣ —

"…anggota, dan dia khalifah yang diberi rezeki sebelum makhluk dalam alam kuasa-Nya — inilah ciptaan alam oleh alam manusia. Alam 'Arasy rahmat tinggi — nyata rezeki Maha Kuasa dengan sambungan tadbir, melipatgandakan jalan lurus, roh suci capai hakikat sakit nikmat. * Kumpulan kehendak tinggi ialah kumpulan ayat kitab kalimah ilahi untuk taubat — ayat domini azali hakikat permintaan; Allah letak dalam ayat ini syuhud kehendak bezanya siklus gerak sfera naik pertumbuhan. Bersamanya sinar gerak sfera wujud — insan arif ini; dan yang menentang tiap naik minit darjat sfera. Pencipta jadikan insan berdiri kesempurnaan ilahi hubungan cahaya, kanan di bawah sirat lurus kiri di neraka, kerana kesempurnaan tersusun dalam makhluk, ilmu disimpan di langit-Nya matahari ilmu, lalu cahaya tinggi limpah, tiap atom ada halus cahaya alam tinggi. Nama sembilan puluh; tiap nama maqam nama dari yang dinamakan. Dengan perhiasan persediaan, nama tegak atas rupa insan bila arif asas tahu isi sirat lurus — ahli kanan; kalau sesat jalan syaitan ahli kiri yang disembah. (Tunjuk) Allah cipta 7 langit 7 bumi, khalifah zahirkan 7 syaitan 7 bintang gerak 7 — juga malaikat hampir sfera sifat nama ilahi nama zat, syurga atas 7. * Kutub 7, dengan mereka 7 bawah berputar, limpah cahaya tinggi tiap atas 'Arasy lain — al-Ghawth dari 'Arasy tergantung; 7 tarik dari 4, 7 kutub panjangkan 70, 4 kepala 40, kumpul dari mabuk, tiap alam kembali. Inilah rupa insan sifat nama di bawah kaki kanan kiri. Sabda 'Syurga di bawah tapak ibu.' Inilah rupanya helaian datang."
