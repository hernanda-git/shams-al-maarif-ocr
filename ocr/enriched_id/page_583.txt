( فِهْرِسْتٌ )

English:
( The poor one in need of Him — exalted is He — Ibrahim ibn Hasan al-Anbani, servant of knowledge and head of the correction committee, at the press of the venerable Shaykh Mustafa al-Babi al-Halabi and his sons, in Protected Egypt )
Praise belongs to Him who singled out the choicest of His sincere servants with the noblest of secrets, and subjected to the children of Adam the structures of the letters and the meanings of the names from those of insight and vision; and He, sublime, favored them with abundant growth, and made them witness the treasured constituted [things] of the names, and disclosed to them the hidden of the unseen, and granted them the secrets of the spheres and the mansions of the stars and the times of the inauspiciousnesses and the fortunes. And blessings and peace upon the Master of the gnostics and his family, the guides of all creation, and his companions and the followers. (Now then) — since the great book named Shams al-Ma'arif has attained from the secrets what has a weighty measure, and its author — may Allah have mercy on him — did not precede [anyone] with its like, and no author rivals him in this field in his excellence, the owners of the mentioned press undertook to print it and spread it among its students, that they might win the great reward of it — and that by the mentioned press, whose center is in Shari' al-Tablita, in Saray No. 12, adjacent to the Azhar Riad. And completion came to pass in the beginning of the month of Rajab, the singular forbidden [month], of the year 1345 of the Hijra of the perfection of the blessing upon him — may the blessings and peace be upon him and his family and his companions — as long as the nights and the days endure. Amen.

Indonesia:
( Yang miskin memerlukan-Nya — Ibrahim ibn Hasan al-Anbani, khadam ilmu dan ketua jawatankuasa pembetulan, di percetakan Shaykh Mustafa al-Babi al-Halabi dan anak-anaknya, Mesir yang Terpelihara )
Segala puji bagi-Nya yang pilih hamba ikhlas dengan rahsia mulia, tundukkan struktur huruf dan makna nama bagi yang berpandangan; dikurniakan pertumbuhan, disaksikan khazanah nama, dibuka ghaib, diberi rahsia sfera manzil bintang masa sial tuah. Selawat atas Penghulu ahli gnosis keluarga penaung dan sahabat pengikut. (Adapun) — buku besar Shams al-Ma'arif capai rahsia berat timbangan, pengarangnya tiada tandingan, tuan percetakan ambil cetak sebar kepada pelajar dapat pahala besar — percetakan di Shari' al-Tablita, Saray No. 12, bersebelahan Riad Azhar. Selesai awal bulan Rajab 1345 Hijrah (Maret 1927M). Amin.
