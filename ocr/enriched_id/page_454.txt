— 448 —

"…its knowledge with excellence in conception, and by no proof nor demonstration. From this reality the world was found by the mediation of the Real the Exalted — so the Real brought us into existence from an ancient existence. And the third known is the whole world, and the dominions and the spheres and what they contain of worlds, and the air and the earth and what is in them — and it is the greater world. And the fourth known is the indication of the creature, as He said exalted: 'And He subjected to you whatever is in the heavens and whatever is in the earth, all from Him.' And it came in the reports that Allah created at each pillar of the Throne worlds and creatures the measure of this world; He said exalted 'And none knows the hosts of your Lord but He.' And we mentioned these indications for the path of the believers in the unveiling of the science of conception. So whoever is completed in him the realities of His names and ascends to a thought in the world of the Dominion, he travels by the name of the attributes and ascends after it to the Lote-tree of the Boundary; so when that is completed for him, there appear to him the lights of the religion, and he rises upon the unveiling from it by the length of the names and their union, name after name, to His remaining name — and by it He named His Essence and described Himself by subsistence, even though the Real the Exalted made the tailbone connected to the Day of Eternity; and he (upon him peace) pointed to Abū Bakr and mentioned the report, and then he is the truthful (ṣiddīq), where he said: 'If the veil were lifted, my certainty would not increase.' And he who draws near by this name attains by it the unveiling of the wills, and the recitation in retreat is the number of its letters, and he recites it each time until he reaches the mentioned number — there comes to him the servant ṢAQĀ'ĪL, and he is chief over four commanders under each commander three hundred and fifty-six rows of angels, and they fulfill his need. And of its properties: if it is written on Monday and carried by the woman who miscarries — she does not miscarry; and around it is written the name of the king and his mention; and if the number of a person agrees, it is a Greatest Name in his right, and he acts by it as is done by the Greatest Name. And his mention: 'O Allah, You are the Fashioner of forms and charged with the subtleties of the wonderful forms. {And this is its figure.}' 'O Fashioner, by choice, the imagining of the invented model with imaginations and its compositions — I seek; O Originator of its model and Fashioner of the upper forms with their shapes and realities, of the beautiful and the ugly and the majestic — and all is from Your act; You are the Originator of the spirits, the Inventor of the bodies. I ask You by the secret of Your outpouring in the upper and lower worlds that You remove from me the pains and the illnesses. O Allah, You are the Bestower, the Favorer, You favored the creatures with the blessing of bringing-forth; I ask You by the secret of this subtle secret that You extend to me a subtlety from Your subtleties that unveils to me by it the realities of the imaginal specters — O Creator, O Evolver, O Fashioner — in the evening and the morning, and extend to me the worlds of this name. Answer, O KHAFTĪYĀ'ĪL, and fulfill my need.' Whoever recited this mention — Allah raised him and provided him unveiling and attained the high ranks. {Section on His name the Exalted, al-Wahhāb} * Know that al-Wahhāb is the giver of the gift free of motives; so when the gifts and connections multiply, the one possessing them is called 'Wahhāb', and it is not conceived but from Allah the Exalted — and He is Wahhāb without recompense. Indonesia:

"…ilmunya baik saat konsep tanpa bukti demonstrasi. Dari hakikat ini alam dijumpai dengan perantara Real Maha Tinggi — Dia cipta kita dari wujud azali. Yang diketahui ketiga seluruh alam domini sfera apa di dalamnya udara bumi — alam besar. Keempat isyarat makhluk firman 'Dia taklukan untukmu apa di langit bumi dari-Nya.' Riwayat Allah cipta tiap tiang 'Arasy alam makhluk kadar dunia; firman 'Tiada tahu tentera Tuhan melainkan Dia.' Kami sebut isyarat jalan mukmin buka ilmu konsep. Sesiapa sempurna hakikat nama naik fikir alam Malakut, lalu nama sifat naik ke Sidratul Muntaha; bila sempurna zahir cahaya agama naik kasyaf dengan panjang nama penyatuan nama demi nama pada nama kekal — Dia namakan Zat sifat kekal, walau tulang ekor disambung hari azali; sabda pada Abu Bakr 'Kalau dibuka tabir tak bertambah yakin,' dia as-ṣiddīq. Dekat dengan nama ini buka kehendak, baca khalwat bilangan huruf sampai bilangan — datang khadam ṢAQĀ'ĪL ketua 4 komander tiap 356 baris malaikat tunaikan. Sifat: tulis Isnin bawa wanita gugur — tak gugur; keliling nama raja zikir; kalau bilangan sepakat Ism A'zam.
