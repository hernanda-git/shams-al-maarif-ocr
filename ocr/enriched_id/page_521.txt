So the orderer and the manager is taken from the description of his self and his will, subdued by the brilliance of what appeared of Your gentleness — O Most Gentle of the gentle, O Most Merciful of the merciful.' And Allah is more knowing of His unseen, and most decisive. (Section) And this is a sum of the names of Allah, and they are for awe and the jabarūt, and they are a part of the tremendous names, and by them are acted all the creatures — especially the separation of the assembled gathering and the gathering of the scattered. And whoever calls by it, Allah raises from him the evil of every painful [thing]; and whoever repels by it, Allah the Exalted destroys him; and it is suitable to be mentioned before a tyrant and the greatness of the creatures and the tyrants of the kings. And if he dislikes them, he is not ceased to be honored before the tyrants, and sees of the awe and the gravity what is not counted. And they are these names: al-'Azīz, al-Qahhār, al-Muqtadir, al-Qawī, al-Quwwah, al-Matīn, al-Qawī, al-Jabbār, al-Mutakabbir, al-Shadīd, al-Qāhir, al-Qahhār, al-Qā'im. And al-Qā'im and al-Qayyūm may be two acts, and may be two openers — so if they are two acts, their meaning is the management, from the saying of the Arabs 'The affair stood, so he is Qā'im and Qayyūm' when he manages it by his standing over it. And if they are two essences, their meaning is the Self-Subsistent needing none other — so they are of the attributes of the Essence. And the difference between al-Qā'im and al-Qayyūm is that al-Qā'im is He who stands over the other by His care for them and His preservation of them, by the evidence of His saying exalted 'Is he then who stands over every soul by what it earned?' and His saying exalted 'standing with justice' — i.e. standing over His creation. And al-Qayyūm is He who subsists by His self and everything needs Him, as the need of the creatures for the Creator. So this is the difference between al-Qā'im and al-Qayyūm. And its measure is fu'ūl, derived from it; and al-Qā'im's measure is fā'il from qāma yaqūmu, for Allah the Exalted is Self-Subsistent by His self, and there was not in existence other than Him, so it is necessary that other than Him be subsistent by His power and He is needed in His bringing into being and in His permanence. So when the essential attributes are established for Him — of knowledge, will, power, hearing, sight — he knew that He is the Manager of the creation. Dan bila ia benci mereka, ia tidak berhenti dimuliakan di hadapan tiran, serta nampak dari kegentaran dan berat apa yang tidak terhitung.
