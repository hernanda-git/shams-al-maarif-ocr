English:
— ٥٦٥ —
"[Capricorn continued:] in it, and strength in everything, and the swift turning of the affair to other than it, and the necessary weakness and darkness — by its being a feminine, cardinal, nocturnal sign.
( The Eleventh Sign — Aquarius [al-Dalw] )
Masculine, diurnal, fixed, western, ascending, crooked, long, in half a voice, silent, shining, airy, hot and moist, sanguine, fortunate, praiseworthy, watery — the peg of the sky [watad al-sama']; its planet Saturn — and in it the woe of the Sun. One looks to it in its faces: Jupiter, Mars, and the Sun. Its ascendant: in the first face, in the form of a headless man, in his hand a peacock — accords for talismans of poverty and need and toil and fatigue; in the second face, in the form of a dead dignitary — accords for the affairs of glory and honor; in the third, like the first, and before him an old woman — accords for talismans of lust and fatigue. It has, of letters: the kaf and the ta' for the students; and of the relation of man, from the orifices the flowing, and from the organs the belly and the two shins; it has the mouth-illnesses; and of tastes, the sweet and the sour; and of colors, what is light; and of metals, like glass; and of places, the seas and every terrible hidden thing. It is specific to the affairs of hopes and expectation and happiness and ascension and friendship and friends and the helpers of sultans and princes and their needs and their helpers and their works; and it indicates the deserts and the moons and what is elevated of the earth, and the winds and everything that is in the sky, and the states of people and their affairs, and the governor who is employed over the work after the first governor, and what may be of affairs, and steadfastness in affairs and good help in questions, and the necessary strength and illumination in them — it being fixed and diurnal.
( The Twelfth Sign — Pisces [al-Hut] )
Feminine, nocturnal, embodied, southern, ascending, crooked, long, in half a voice, silent, dark, watery, cold, moist, inauspicious, blameworthy, falling, vile in place, pure evil, an abode, strong — the empty planets in it and its benefit is the place of Saturn's joy in sorrows and annihilation and the type for God. Its planet Jupiter — in its seventh degree, and Jupiter from it is the exaltation of Venus; and in it the depression of Mercury and its woe; and in the eighth degree the depression of the ember of water; and in the fifteenth degree the depression of the ember of earth; and in the nineteenth degree the mixing of the two seasons winter and spring. One looks to it in its faces: Saturn, Jupiter, and Mars. Its ascendant: in the first face, in the form of a man with a human body and finger — accords for talismans of weakness and illness and fatigue and weariness; in the second face, in the form of a transformed man, in his hand a red [thing] — accords for drinking and elevation of ambition and attainment of what is great; in the third face, in the form of a wicked man, before him a woman upon whom is a veil — accords for copulation and rain and rest. It has, of letters: the lam and the ha' combined; and of the relation of man, from the orifices the two breasts; from the inner organs the liver; from the outer the ability and likewise the two shins and two feet (sharing with Aquarius) and the right hand and the left ear and the growing power (sharing); and it has the stupefying illnesses; and of tastes, every astringent and insipid; and of colors, every different color; and of metals, every shining stone like pearl and silver; and of places, the shores; and of plants, every moderate [one]. It is specific to the affairs of despair and severance and slipping and falling and bad travel and prison and confinement and commotion and sorrow and illnesses and affliction and care and grief and gloom and toil and enemies and opposites and harm and obstinacy; and it indicates the groups of people, and the beasts of burden, and the water and everything in it, and the places in which is moisture, and every work that is from moisture, and the contracts and the crown of Aries, and...

Indonesia:
— ٥٦٥ —
"[Capricorn disambung:] padanya, dan kuat dalam segala, dan pantas berubah urusan ke lain, dan keperluan lemah dan gelap — kerana ia tanda betina kardinal malam.
( Tanda Kesebelas — Aquarius [al-Dalw] )
Jantina lelaki, siang, tetap, barat, naik, bengkok, panjang, separuh suara, senyap, bersinar, berangin, panas lembap, sanguin, bertuah, terpuji, berair — pasak langit [watad al-sama']; planetnya Zuhal — dan padanya kecelakaan Matahari. Dipandang padanya dalam wajah: Musytari, Marikh, dan Matahari. Ascendeninya: wajah pertama, lelaki buntu kepala, di tangan merak — sesuai talisman kemiskinan dan hajat serta penat lelah; wajah kedua, bentuk bangsawan mati — sesuai urusan kemuliaan dan maruah; wajah ketiga, seperti pertama, di hadapan wanita tua — sesuai talisman nafsu dan letih. Ia ada huruf: kaf dan ta' bagi pelajar; dan hubungan manusia, liang mengalir, dan organ perut serta dua betis; penyakit mulut; rasa manis masam; warna cerah; logam seperti kaca; tempat laut dan setiap tersembunyi menakut. Ia khusus urusan harapan dan jangkaan serta kebahagiaan dan naik dan persahabatan dan kawan serta penolong sultan putera dan hajat mereka; dan menunjuk gurun dan bulan serta tanah tinggi, angin dan segala di langit, keadaan manusia, gabenor selepas gabenor pertama, keteguhan serta bantuan baik — kerana ia tetap siang.
( Tanda Kedua Belas — Pisces [al-Hut] )
Betina, malam, berjasad, selatan, naik, bengkok, panjang, separuh suara, senyap, gelap, berair, sejuk, lembap, sial, tercela, jatuh, buruk tempat, jahat tulen, tempat, kuat — planet kosong padanya dan faedahnya tempat kegembiraan Zuhal dalam duka dan binasa serta jenis bagi Tuhan. Planetnya Musytari — di darjah ketujuhnya, dan Musytari daripadanya ketinggian Zuhrah; dan padanya kerendahan Utarid serta kecelakaannya; dan darjah kelapan kerendahan bara air; dan darjah kelima belas kerendahan bara tanah; dan darjah kesembilan belas campuran musim sejuk dan bunga. Dipandang padanya dalam wajah: Zuhal, Musytari, dan Marikh. Ascendeninya: wajah pertama, lelaki berbadan manusia dan jari — sesuai talisman lemah dan sakit serta letih; wajah kedua, lelaki berubah, di tangan merah — sesuai minum dan tinggi cita serta capai yang besar; wajah ketiga, lelaki jahat, di hadapan wanita bertelek — sesuai persetubuhan dan hujan serta rehat.
