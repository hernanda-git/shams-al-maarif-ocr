In the first; then consider — is it even or odd? So work like the first of the names; so there come out to you from that the names of the division by which you divide upon the three assistants, and dispose them in what you intend. Its example — the number of the letters of the names and the number of the letters of fire running upon the number of nature; and the letters of fire: as "kaqd minnā" — so the number is: [H Ṭ M F Sh Dh] its middle the number of [A A Ḥ D-Kh M S H T S ' H A R B ' W N Th M A N W L W Th L Th M ĀY W-S B' M ĀY] — and the numbers of the manifest nature are understood by whoever seeks them. And the example of the air-letters [B W Y N Ṣ T Ḍ] — their expansion in number [A-F N Y Th B ' H Sh R-H], their sum 37, and their numbers are manifest. And the water-letters [J Z K S Q Ẓ] — their expansion in number, their sum 39 letters, and their numbers known. And the earthy [D Ḥ L ' D Kh Kh] — their expansion in number, and their numbers known to whoever seeks them. And as for knowing the letters of the seven days — its division, when divided by seven, divides upon the planets. So if you will a work of the works at any time — take its letters and the numbers of the planets, I mean the number of its letters, and expand the whole as preceded. And take the number of the letters of the hours in which you began the work, and expand the whole as preceded, and add to what you have of the name-letters; then use the whole. And as for the number of the day-letters — the letters of (Sunday) their expansion in number [H K A Ḥ D N A Ḥ D] then [A N Ā A R-B' H] sum 23, expand and numbers 551 — and it is known. The letters of (Monday) their expansion [A Ḥ D Th L A Th W R A Ḥ D Kh M ĀY H Kh M S WN 'Sh R H Kh S WN] sum 343, expand. (Tuesday) 35 letters. (Wednesday) its expansion 21 letters, numbers known. (Thursday) its expansion 36, numbers known. (Friday) likewise; (Saturday) its expansion 36 letters. So these are the letters of the seven days for the seven planets — first Saturn, and he is in the seventh sphere; his expansion [S B ' Th 5 M A N Ā H Th L Th W N] sum 16 letters. Jupiter — his expansion and number 41 letters. Mars — his expansion and number 31 letters. Sun — her expansion 67 letters. Venus — 67 letters, numbers known. Mercury — his expansion 23. Moon — her expansion 25, numbers known. ( The hour-letters ) the first their expansion in number reaches 5 letters; the second 33; the third 27; the fourth 32; the fifth 33; the sixth 29; the seventh 32; the eighth 24; the ninth 24; the tenth 51; the eleventh 25; the twelfth its expansion 27 letters, numbers known. ( The day-letters ) expand in number 27. ( The night-letters ) 20, numbers known. And as for the night-hours — like the day-hours. So if you will a work by night, and have subtracted the night-letters and the numbers of its letters 3861 — this enters into all night-works like what enters by day. And I will clarify to you the manner of disposing the letters in all creatures of Allah — and the speaking and silent animal — from good or evil, or attraction or repulsion, or illness or health. ( If you will that ) — know the name of the one worked for, and look at the number of its letters and their means, and know the four balances, and dispose in attraction, repulsion, health, and illness. Its example — if the name of the sought is 'Muḥammad' — expand its letters 23, and the numbers of the letters expand in number [forty then ĀY H forty four H], and their numbers 500. Then know that these are four balances: the balance of attraction — that you compute the number of his letters and their numbers as I taught you; you found that the balance of repulsion and attraction and the balance of health and sickness — at the time of work you add to them by adjusting them as preceded at the beginning of the book from the hours and elements and other. So if that is complete — you have known the stored secret. And know — that the purer your religion, the greater the obedience of the creatures to you.

Pada yang pertama; lalu pertimbangkan — genap atau ganjil? Maka amalkan seperti yang pertama dari nama; maka keluar padamu dari itu nama pembagian yang engkau bagi pada tiga pembantu, dan atur mereka pada apa yang engkau inginkan. Contohnya — bilangan huruf nama dan bilangan huruf api berjalan pada bilangan sifat alam; dan huruf api: seperti "kaqd minnā" — maka bilangannya: [H Ṭ M F Sh Dh] pertengahannya bilangan [A A Ḥ D-Kh M S H T S ' H A R B ' W N Th M A N W L W Th L Th M ĀY W-S B' M ĀY] — dan bilangan sifat alam yang nyata dipahami oleh yang mencarinya. Dan contoh huruf udara [B W Y N Ṣ T Ḍ] — pengembangannya dalam bilangan [A-F N Y Th B ' H Sh R-H], jumlah 37, dan bilangannya nyata. Dan huruf air [J Z K S Q Ẓ] — pengembangannya dalam bilangan, jumlah 39 huruf, dan bilangannya diketahui. Dan yang tanah [D Ḥ L ' D Kh Kh] — pengembangannya dalam bilangan, dan bilangannya diketahui oleh yang mencarinya. Dan adapun mengetahui huruf tujuh hari — pembagiannya apabila dibagi tujuh membagi pada planet. Maka apabila engkau hendak amalan pada waktu mana pun — ambil hurufnya dan bilangan planet, yaitu bilangan hurufnya, dan kembangkan seluruhnya sebagaimana lalu. Dan ambil bilangan huruf jam di mana engkau mulai amalan, dan kembangkan seluruhnya sebagaimana lalu, serta tambahkan pada huruf nama yang ada padamu; lalu gunakan seluruhnya. Dan adapun bilangan huruf hari — huruf (Ahad/Minggu) pengembangannya dalam bilangan [H K A Ḥ D N A Ḥ D] lalu [A N Ā A R-B' H] jumlah 23, kembangkan dan bilangannya 551 — dan itu diketahui. Huruf (Senin) pengembangannya [A Ḥ D Th L A Th W R A Ḥ D Kh M ĀY H Kh M S WN 'Sh R H Kh S WN] jumlah 343, kembangkan. (Selasa) 35 huruf. (Rabu) pengembangannya 21 huruf, bilangan diketahui. (Kamis) pengembangannya 36, bilangan diketahui. (Jumat) demikian; (Sabtu) pengembangannya 36 huruf. Maka ini huruf tujuh hari untuk tujuh planet — pertama Saturnus, dan ia di sfera ketujuh; pengembangannya [S B ' Th 5 M A N Ā H Th L Th W N] jumlah 16 huruf. Yupiter — pengembangan dan bilangannya 41 huruf. Marikh — pengembangan dan bilangannya 31 huruf. Matahari — pengembangannya 67 huruf. Venus — 67 huruf, bilangan diketahui. Merkurius — pengembangannya 23. Bulan — pengembangannya 25, bilangan diketahui. ( Huruf jam ) yang pertama pengembangannya dalam bilangan mencapai 5 huruf; kedua 33; ketiga 27; keempat 32; kelima 33; keenam 29; ketujuh 32; kedelapan 24; kesembilan 24; kesepuluh 51; kesebelas 25; kedua belas pengembangannya 27 huruf, bilangan diketahui. Maka apabila engkau hendak amalan malam, dan telah mengurangi huruf malam dan bilangan hurufnya 3861 — ini masuk ke semua amalan malam seperti yang masuk di siang. Dan akan kujelaskan kepadamu cara mengatur huruf di semua makhluk Allah — dan hewan berbicara dan bisu — dari baik atau buruk, atau tarik atau tolak, atau sakit atau sehat. ( Apabila engkau hendak itu ) — ketahuilah nama yang dikerjakan untuknya, dan lihat bilangan hurufnya dan tengahnya, serta ketahui empat neraca, dan atur pada tarik, tolak, sehat, dan sakit. Contohnya — apabila nama yang dicari "Muḥammad" — kembangkan hurufnya 23, dan bilangan huruf dikembangkan dalam bilangan [empat puluh lalu ĀY H empat puluh empat H], dan bilangannya 500. Lalu ketahuilah bahwa ini empat neraca: neraca tarik — bahwa engkau hitung bilangan hurufnya dan bilangannya sebagaimana kujelaskan; engkau dapati neraca tolak dan tarik serta neraca sehat dan sakit — pada saat amalan engkau tambahkan padanya dengan menyesuaikannya sebagaimana lalu di awal kitab dari jam dan unsur dan lainnya. Maka apabila itu lengkap — engkau telah mengetahui rahasia tersimpan. Dan ketahuilah — bahwa semakin suci agamamu, semakin patuh makhluk padamu."
