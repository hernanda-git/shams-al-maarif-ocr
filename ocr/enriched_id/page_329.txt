English:
— 323 —

( Jalash 333 ) its day is Tuesday, its planet Mars, its wafq (magic-square type) septenary, and among the Names of Allah are al-Malik al-Quddūs (the King, the Holy) ( Damat 444 ).
Its day Wednesday, its planet Mercury, its wafq quaternary; its Names are al-Kabīr al-Muta'āl (the Great, the Most High) ( Hanath 500 ). Its day Thursday, its wafq
octonary, and its Names are al-Shadīd dhū al-quwwah (the Strong, Possessor of Power) ( Wasakh 666 ). Its day Friday, its planet Venus, its wafq quinary; its Names are al-Fattāh al-Razzāq (the Opener, the Provider).
( Za'ad 777 ) its day Saturday, its planet Saturn, its wafq septenary; its Names are al-Qawī al-Qādir (the Strong, the Able) ( Hafad 888 ). Its day Sunday,
its planet the Sun, its wafq senary; its Names are al-Qawī al-Qahhār (the Strong, the Subduer) — on condition that you be pure of garment and body, in spiritual retreat.
And the system of the discipline: the discipline of each rank is 7 days, beginning from the day attributed to it, and he is fasting and standing [in prayer],
and the star of the wafq every night, and he fumigates with the incense of its planet morning and evening, and recites the two Names the number of the wafq while you fumigate,
in the daytime 4 times — after the dawn prayer, at the time of the meridian after the afternoon prayer, and in the depth of the night — and repeat the work 7 days.*
And know that the Names, planets, hours, minerals, incenses, and patience in the discipline, fasting, and prayer
are a condition woven from the act, just as its foundation is the formulation of concealment; and whoever falls short of their conditions, his work is nullified — and likewise the secret of
the letters and the wafqs: if anything of their conditions is lacking, they are corrupted. And I must make this clear to you so you need no
further explanation, and I will explain to you the ranks, the Names, their movements, their unknowns, their expanded forms, and their detailed forms briefly. So I say:
( The first rank ) Iya' (to bring about) thus: alif 831, yā' 11, qāf 181, ghayn 1060 — expanded as the number "one three," and
n l f ' sh r h kh m s, and from its total 1111 is joined to the count of the eight letters, the whole becomes 12978, and there come out
the Names up to al-Qayyūm, summarized 174, and detailed "ḥayā qāf yā wāw mīm" expanded as "a y t ḥ d ' sh r h a arba' wan" (eleven four and ten), and
the count of the letters is 16 letters, so the count of the two noble Names became 174, and the count of its detail 315, and their expansion 4699; the sum
of all the Names altogether is 5188, the sum of what came out from the rank and the Names is 8166, and its wafq is senary for the day Sunday, and this is its description.
( The second rank — Bakr ) 222, and the count of the detailed letters is 401,
and their numbers 30, and the totals 3, and the counts of its letters 222;
and the sum of all is 296, to be added to this and the twenty-eight letters,
and the ninety-nine 5995, the whole becomes 8955, and there come out the Names
al-Raḥmān al-Raḥīm (the Compassionate, the Merciful) 556; the detail of that: rā 201, ḥā 9, mīm
90, nūn 106; and its expansion is the number 12: "māt y n ḥ d th m ān" (dead, O, pain, new, from),
and n ' sh r h arba' wan, and n s t kh khams, and n māt y n aḥad (one), thamān "y h dārab 'an" (eight), and summarized 565,
and detailed 27, and expanded 7341; the sum of the Names becomes 8614, and the sum that comes out of the rank and comes out
of the Names is 1175 — decrease from the number 1 so it enters into the three, for it does not bear the fraction; and this is its form.
( The third rank ) Jalash, detailed and summarized: jīm lām shin, and expanded thus:
"three māy h n ' s r h arba' wan" (three), and three, and
"māy h ' s r wan" (fifty) detailed and summarized 484333, and expanded 5793;
and to that is added the count of the twenty-eight letters, which brings out for you the Names al-Malik al-Quddūs, summarized 13615.

Indonesia:
— 323 —

( Jalash 333 ) harinya Selasa, planetnya Mars, wafq-nya (jenis persegi ajaib) tujuh, dan di antara Nama-nama Allah ialah al-Malik al-Quddūs (Raja, Yang Mahasuci) ( Damat 444 ).
Harinya Rabu, planetnya Merkurius, wafq-nya empat; namanya al-Kabīr al-Muta'āl (Yang Agung, Yang Mahatinggi) ( Hanath 500 ). Harinya Kamis, wafq-nya
delapan, dan namanya al-Shadīd dhū al-quwwah (Yang Kuat, Pemilik Kekuatan) ( Wasakh 666 ). Harinya Jumat, planetnya Venus, wafq-nya lima; namanya al-Fattāh al-Razzāq (Yang Membuka, Yang Memberi Rezeki).
( Za'ad 777 ) harinya Sabtu, planetnya Saturnus, wafq-nya tujuh; namanya al-Qawī al-Qādir (Yang Kuat, Yang Mahakuasa) ( Hafad 888 ). Harinya Ahad,
planetnya Matahari, wafq-nya enam; namanya al-Qawī al-Qahhār (Yang Kuat, Yang Memaksa) — dengan syarat pakaian dan tubuh suci, dalam uzlah.
Dan tata disiplin: disiplin tiap martabat 7 hari, mulai dari hari yang disandarkan padanya, dan ia berpuasa serta berdiri [shalat],
dan bintang wafq setiap malam, serta dibakar dengan kemenyan planetnya pagi dan petang, dan membaca dua Nama sebanyak bilangan wafq sambil membakar,
di siang hari 4 kali — setelah shalat subuh, waktu tergelincirnya matahari setelah ashar, dan di tengah malam — serta ulangi amalan 7 hari.*
Dan ketahuilah bahwa Nama, planet, jam, mineral, kemenyan, dan kesabaran dalam disiplin, puasa, serta shalat
adalah syarat yang terjalin dari amalan, sebagaimana pokoknya adalah rumusan penyembunyian; barangsiapa kurang dari syarat-syaratnya, batal amalnya — dan demikian pula rahasia
huruf dan wafq: jika sesuatu dari syaratnya kurang, rusaklah ia. Dan aku harus menjelaskan ini kepadamu agar engkau tak perlu
penjelasan lebih, dan aku jelaskan kepadamu martabat, Nama, gerakannya, yang tak diketahui, bentuk terkembangnya, dan bentuk rincinya secara singkat. Maka aku katakan:
( Martabat pertama ) Iya' (mewujudkan) demikian: alif 831, ya 11, qaf 181, ghain 1060 — terkembang sebagai bilangan "satu tiga," dan
n l f ' sh r h kh m s, dan dari totalnya 1111 digabung ke hitungan delapan huruf, seluruhnya menjadi 12978, lalu keluar
Nama-nama hingga al-Qayyūm, ringkas 174, dan rinci "ḥayā qāf yā wāw mīm" terkembang sebagai "a y t ḥ d ' sh r h a arba' wan" (sebelas empat dan sepuluh), dan
hitungan huruf adalah 16 huruf, maka hitungan dua Nama mulia menjadi 174, dan rinciannya 315, dan kembangannya 4699; jumlah
seluruh Nama semua 5188, jumlah yang keluar dari martabat dan Nama 8166, dan wafq-nya enam untuk hari Ahad, dan ini gambarannya.
( Martabat kedua — Bakr ) 222, dan hitungan huruf rincian 401,
dan bilangan mereka 30, dan total 3, dan hitungan hurufnya 222;
dan jumlah seluruh 296, ditambah kepada ini dan dua puluh delapan huruf,
dan sembilan puluh sembilan 5995, seluruhnya menjadi 8955, lalu keluar Nama-nama
al-Raḥmān al-Raḥīm (Yang Maha Pengasih lagi Maha Penyayang) 556; rinciannya: rā 201, ḥā 9, mīm
90, nūn 106; dan kembangannya bilangan 12: "māt y n ḥ d th m ān" (mati, wahai, sakit, baru, dari),
dan n ' sh r h arba' wan, dan n s t kh khams, dan n māt y n aḥad (satu), thamān "y h dārab 'an" (delapan), dan ringkas 565,
dan rinci 27, dan terkembang 7341; jumlah Nama menjadi 8614, dan jumlah yang keluar dari martabat dan keluar
dari Nama 1175 — kurangi dari bilangan 1 agar masuk ke dalam tiga, sebab ia tak menampung pecahan; dan ini bentuknya.
