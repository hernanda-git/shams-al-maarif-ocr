— 323 —

( JLSh — 333 ) its day Tuesday, its planet Mars, its square the septenary, and of the names of Allah "King, Holy". ( DMaT — 444 ) its day Wednesday, its planet Mercury, its square the quaternary, its names "Great, Exalted". ( HaNaTh — 555 ) its day Thursday, its square the octagonal, its names "Severe, Possessor of Power". ( WaSaKh — 666 ) its day Friday, its planet Venus, its square the quintuple, its names "Opener, Provider". ( Za'Da — 777 ) its day Saturday, its planet Saturn, its square the septenary, its names "Strong, Capable". ( ḤFaḌ — 888 ) its day Sunday, its planet the Sun, its square the heptagonal, its names "Strong, Subduer" — on condition that you be pure of garment and body, secluded. And the system of the discipline — and the discipline of each rank is 7 days, beginning from the day attributed to it; and he is fasting standing, and the square rises every night, and he fumigates with the incense of his planet morning and evening, and recites the two names the number of the square while you fumigate. In the day 4 times: after the dawn prayer, at the zenith, after the afternoon prayer, and in the depth of night; and repeat the work 7 days. And know — that the names and planets and hours and metals and incenses and patience in the discipline and fasting and prayer — is a condition that is ordered of the act, just as its root is the formula of concealment. And whoever fell short of their conditions — his work is void; and likewise the secret of the letters and squares — if something of their conditions is lacking they are corrupted. And necessarily I clarify this to you — it needs no further explanation. And I clarify to you the ranks and the names and their movements and unknowns and expansions and details briefly — so I say: ( The first rank — AyQ' ) thus: Alif 831, Yā' 11, Qāf 181, 'Ayn 1060 — expanded in number "one three and N L F ' Sh R H Kh M S"; and from its sum 1111 it joins to the number of the eight letters — the whole becomes 12978. There come out the names up to "Self-Subsisting" — summarized 174, and detailed "Ḥayā Qāf Yā Wāw Mīm" expanded "A Y T Ḥ D ' Sh R H A R-B' W N" — the number of letters 16 letters. So the number of the two noble names became 174, and the number of its detail 315, and their expansion 4699. The sum of all from all the names 5188; the sum of what came out of the rank and the names 8166. And its square is hexagonal for Sunday — and this is its description. ( The second rank — BKRa ) 222, and the number of the detailed letters 401, and their numbers 30, the sums 3, the numbers of its letters 222. The sum of all 296 — is added to this and the twenty-eight letters 5995 — the whole becomes 8955. There come out the names "Compassionate, Merciful" 556; its detail: "Rā 201 Ḥā 9 Mīm 90 Nūn 106" — and its expansion in number "12 M Ā T Y N Ḥ D Th M A N W N ' Sh R H A R-B' W N S T Kh M S W N M Ā T Y N A Ḥ D Th M ĀN Y H D-AR-B '". Summarized 565, detailed 27, expanded 7341 — so the sum of the names 8614. The sum of what came out of the rank and the names is sound 1175 — less from the number 1 so it enters into the three; for it does not bear the fraction. And this is its figure. ( The third rank — JLSh ) detailed and summarized "Jīm Lām Shīn", and expanded thus: "three M ĀY H N ' S R H A R-B' W N Th-L-Āth W N M ĀY H ' S R W N Kh-M-S-ŪN" — detailed and summarized 484333, and expanded 5793. And there is added to that the number of the twenty-eight letters — it brings out to you the names "King, Holy" summarized 13615. ( ḤFaḌ — 888 ) harinya Ahad, planetnya Matahari, petaknya tujuh, namanya "Maha Kuat lagi Maha Mengalahkan" — dengan syarat engkau suci pakaian dan tubuh, mengasingkan diri. Dan susunan riyadhah — dan riyadhah tiap tingkat 7 hari, mulai dari hari yang dinisbatkan padanya; dan ia puasa berdiri, serta petak muncul tiap malam, dan ia berdupa dengan dupa planetnya pagi dan petang, serta membaca dua nama bilangan petak sambil berdupa. Di siang 4 kali: setelah shalat subuh, saat tergelincir matahari, setelah ashar, dan di tengah malam; serta ulangi amalan 7 hari. Dan ketahuilah — bahwa nama, planet, jam, logam, dupa, dan kesabaran dalam riyadhah serta puasa dan shalat — adalah syarat yang teratur dari perbuatan, sebagaimana akarnya adalah rumus penyembunyian. Dan barangsiapa kurang dari syarat mereka — batal amalannya; dan demikian rahasia huruf dan petak — apabila kurang sesuatu dari syaratnya, rusaklah. Dan pasti kujelaskan ini kepadamu — tiada perlu penjelasan lagi.
