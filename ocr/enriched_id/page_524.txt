English:
— ٥١٨ —

"…he attains it; and whoever wants from Allah a need he cannot reach, let him draw it in his palms and raise them to the sky and call by the name multiplied in the days of the week — each day the name is multiplied in it, so he reaches and asks by its number, for the answer is attained for him after the mentioned number. And the description of the supplication by it: his mention of it with sincere intention and sound resolve — you say: 'O Allah, I ask You by Your name the Swift, the Near, the Answerer, by which You ran the openings of Your mercy and the seals of Your will and the speed of Your answer — O Swift to who intends Him, O Returning to who asks Him, O Reviver to who calls Him, hasten the fulfillment of my need and the attainment of my will. O Hearer, O Near, O Answerer, O Swift.' The number multiplied in the days — its sum 611 — is struck in red agate and written 4277; and His name al-Qarīb — from breaking it with His name al-Muhaymin and drawing it in a ring of red agate and writing around it the circle 'the Originator of the heavens and the earth' to His saying 'the Subtle, the Aware,' and carrying it after prayer and recitation for him — Allah gives him what he wishes of the matters of this world and the next, and opens to him by the knowledge he did not know, and he attains the object from all the creation, until the spiritual spirits come to him morning and evening in the state of the answer with the mention. And as for His names the Exalted al-Raḥmān al-Raḥīm — a noble mention benefiting the distressed and a security for the fearful; and if one engraves them in a ring on Friday at the end of the day, he does not see what he dislikes as long as they are with him; and whoever increases their mention is treated with gentleness in all his states. And as for His names the Exalted al-Laṭīf al-Wāsi' al-Mashhūd — a magnificent pattern, and it is a mention for the masters of the orientations in the retreats; and if he tastes a part of the love and is characterized by something of it, to that his states reach — especially His name the Exalted al-Laṭīf, for it has swiftness in the relief of the griefs and the hardships, and none is joined to it but it, for there appears from it the effects of the wondrous; and none mentions it but finds in his self a tremendous matter that awed him and all but overcame him, but it is depicted for him at that time while he observes that condition, and he sees how it dissolves, so he does not rise from his station and there remains a thing concerning him. And as for His names the Exalted al-Ra'ūf al-Ḥalīm al-Ḥannān al-Mannān — they are tremendous names; none mentions them who feared a thing but found tranquility from it and his fright was stilled. And some of the masters of insight mentioned that who perseveres upon this mention until there overcomes him from it a state, upon an empty stomach from food and he holds the fire — it does not harm him, and if he breathes upon a measure that boils, its boiling ceases; and none writes them and meets who fears his evil but Allah the Exalted extinguishes his anger at seeing him; and none increases their mention from whom the passion overcame but Allah removes it from him. (Section) And as for His names the Exalted al-'Afw, al-Ghafūr, al-Ghaffār — a noble mention suitable for repelling the painful from the tremendous matters; so glorified is He who deposited His secrets in the names. And as for His names the Exalted al-Ra'ūf al-Ḥalīm al-Ḥannān al-Mannān al-Karīm — from breaking them and placing them in a triangle such that its three angles come out equal sides, there appears to you the proof of that; and it is a mention for the people of the secrets, and its breaking is thus: ال ال ال رم ك ون رف اى لم — and it is in gold on Friday in the first hour, and is drawn in a circle 'He who created the heavens and the earth in truth' to His saying 'the Aware'; and suits it this subtle [thing], and it is for the seeking of the worldly and otherworldly desires — and it is nine names, a security for the fearful and intimacy for the lonely: al-Raḥmān, al-Raḥīm, al-Ra'ūf, al-'Afw, al-Mannān, al-Karīm, Dhū al-Ṭawl, Dhū al-Jalāl wa al-Ikrām — he attains what he wills. And as for His name the Exalted al-Sarī' — whoever writes it and keeps it with him, the answer hastens to him whatever he asks in the important matters; and whoever wants the unveiling of anything and increases its mention, there is unveiled to him what he wills, for its special property is the unveiling of the matters of the unseen from the witnesses of the good — and it is…

Indonesia:
— ٥١٨ —

"…ia mencapainya; dan barangsiapa mahu dari Allah hajat yang tidak dapat dicapai, hendaklah ia gambar dalam kedua tapak tangannya dan mengangkatnya ke langit serta berdoa dengan nama yang didarab pada hari minggu — tiap hari nama didarab padanya, maka ia sampai dan memohon dengan bilangannya, kerana jawapan dicapai baginya selepas bilangan disebut. Dan sifat doa dengannya: dzikirnya dengan niat ikhlas dan azam benar — engkau ucapkan: 'Ya Allah, aku memohon dengan nama-Mu Yang Cepat, Yang Dekat, Yang Menjawab, yang dengannya Engkau jalankan pembukaan rahmat-Mu dan meterai kehendak-Mu serta segera jawapan-Mu — wahai Cepat bagi yang menuju-Nya, wahai Kembali bagi yang memohon-Nya, wahai Menghidupkan bagi yang menyeru-Nya, segerakanlah penyelesaian hajatku dan pencapaian kehendakku. Wahai Yang Mendengar, wahai Yang Dekat, wahai Yang Menjawab, wahai Yang Cepat.' Bilangan didarab pada hari — jumlahnya 611 — dipukul pada akik merah dan ditulis 4277; dan nama-Nya al-Qarīb — dari memecahkannya dengan nama-Nya al-Muhaymin dan menggambar dalam cincin akik merah serta menulis kelilingnya bulatan 'Yang Mengadakan langit dan bumi dengan hak' hingga firman-Nya 'Yang Halus, Yang Mengetahui,' dan memakainya selepas solat dan bacaan — Allah memberinya apa dikehendaki dari urusan dunia dan akhirat, serta membukakan padanya ilmu yang tidak diketahui, dan ia capai tujuan dari seluruh makhluk, hingga roh rohani datang kepadanya pagi dan petang dalam keadaan jawapan dengan dzikir. Dan adapun nama-Nya Yang Maha Tinggi al-Raḥmān al-Raḥīm — dzikir mulia berfaedah bagi yang susah dan keamanan bagi yang takut; dan bila seseorang mengukirnya dalam cincin pada Jumaat hujung hari, ia tidak nampak apa yang dibencinya selama bersamanya; dan barangsiapa memperbanyak dzikirnya diperlakukan lemah-lembut dalam semua keadaannya. Dan adapun nama-Nya Yang Maha Tinggi al-Laṭīf al-Wāsi' al-Mashhūd — corak agung, dan ia dzikir bagi tuan orientasi dalam khalwat; dan bila ia kecap sebahagian cinta dan bersifat dengannya, sampai kepadanya keadaannya — khususnya nama-Nya Yang Maha Tinggi al-Laṭīf, kerana ia segera dalam melegakan duka dan kesusahan, dan tiada disertai selainnya, kerana nampak daripadanya kesan ajaib; dan tiada yang menyebutnya melainkan dapati dalam dirinya perkara dahsyat yang menggerunkannya dan hampir menguasainya, tetapi digambarkan padanya pada waktu itu sambil ia perhatikan keadaan itu, dan ia nampak bagaimana ia larut, maka ia tidak bangun dari maqamnya dan tinggal perkara yang menggusarkannya. Dan adapun nama-Nya Yang Maha Tinggi al-Ra'ūf al-Ḥalīm al-Ḥannān al-Mannān — mereka nama agung; tiada yang menyebutnya yang takut sesuatu melainkan dapati ketenangan daripadanya dan ketakutannya reda. Dan sebahagian ahli pandangan menyebut bahwa yang bertekun dzikir ini hingga menguasai dia daripadanya suatu keadaan, atas perut kosong dari makanan dan ia memegang api — tidak memudaratkannya, dan bila ia bernafas pada sukat yang mendidih, didihnya berhenti; dan tiada yang menulisnya dan bertemu yang takut kejahatannya melainkan Allah Yang Maha Tinggi memadam kemarahannya bila melihatnya; dan tiada yang memperbanyak dzikirnya dari yang dikuasai nafsu melainkan Allah mencabutnya daripadanya. (Fasal) Dan adapun nama-Nya Yang Maha Tinggi al-'Afw, al-Ghafūr, al-Ghaffār — dzikir mulia sesuai menolak yang menyakitkan dari perkara dahsyat; maka Maha Suci Yang menaruh rahsia-Nya dalam nama. Dan adapun nama-Nya Yang Maha Tinggi al-Ra'ūf al-Ḥalīm al-Ḥannān al-Mannān al-Karīm — dari memecahkannya dan meletaknya dalam segitiga sehingga tiga sudutnya keluar sisi sama, nampak kepadamu bukti itu; dan ia dzikir bagi ahli rahsia, dan pemecahannya demikian: ال ال ال رم ك ون رف اى لم — dan ia dalam emas pada Jumaat jam pertama, dan digambar dalam bulatan 'Yang mencipta langit dan bumi dengan benar' hingga firman-Nya 'Yang Mengetahui'; dan sesuai padanya halus ini, dan ia bagi memohon keinginan dunia dan akhirat — dan ia sembilan nama, keamanan bagi yang takut dan keintiman bagi yang sunyi: al-Raḥmān, al-Raḥīm, al-Ra'ūf, al-'Afw, al-Mannān, al-Karīm, Pemilik kepanjangan, Pemilik keagungan dan kemuliaan — ia capai apa dikehendaki.
