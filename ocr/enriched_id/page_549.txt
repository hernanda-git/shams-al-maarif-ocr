English:
— 543 —

"…the Ascendant of the geomancy to the rank of the house which has for it relatings, by considering its course to it and its alighting in it — they become a cyclic, successive [order]; and from there is recorded its position in the form of a circle called 'the circle of settling [dā'irat al-tasīn]' — each house of it sought by its right from its left, and its proof and witness its third likewise, and its left its sixth, and its right its tenth; and each of its forms that appears in the first striking of the geomancy is counted Ascendant, and it relies upon its course from the right of the circle of settling. So wherever the rank of the number ends to — from the pole of the circle, and it is the inward [al-dākhilah], which is the first of the houses — it gives it that rank from its left; and it has for it the relatings of that house and its connotations and its rules in their specific and general [aspects]. And this is the manner of the circle:

[The settling-circle diagram: an outer ring of 4 houses (16, 2, 1) and an inner ring of 4 houses (10, 16, 2, 1) with their letter-labels read as h q ṭ ḥ / q ṭ kh h / ṭ ḥ h q / h q ṭ — the geomantic figure-rows arranged concentrically, with the example: 'for instance, there appeared in the Ascendant of the geomancy a striking [of] Ṭarīq — its numeric rank from the right of the circle is five, so its course is to the house Naqī al-Khad whose rank from the left of the circle is five — so the connotation and the rule of the relatings in that house [apply], and there remains for it the rule of the sought from the left of the circle which is the house of the inward threshold ['atabah al-dākhilah], and the rule of the house of the proof and the house of its left, and from the geomancy also.']

((The Thirteenth Intent: on the settling of the aspects)) The consideration of the look in the inference and the ruling by the relatings is [directed] to the Ascendant and what falls from it, and the aspects of sextile and square and trine and the look of opposition which is the sought itself, and what that has of the partakings and the manner of the occurrence of the quests and the clarification of their rules and their relatings — and that is by the way of the settling of this circle and its manner.

Indonesia:
— 543 —

"…Ascenden geomancy ke pangkat rumah yang mempunyainya kaitan, dengan menganggap perjalanannya kepadanya dan pendaratannya padanya — mereka jadi siklik, bersilih [turutan]; dan dari situ direkam kedudukannya berbentuk bulatan bernama 'bulatan penetapan [dā'irat al-tasīn]' — tiap rumah daripadanya dituntut kanan dari kirinya, dan bukti serta saksinya ketiganya begitu, dan kirinya keenamnya, dan kanannya kesepuluhnya; dan tiap bentuknya yang muncul dalam pukulan pertama geomancy dikira Ascenden, dan ia bersandar pada perjalanannya dari kanan bulatan penetapan. Maka di mana jua pangkat bilangan berakhir ke — dari kutub bulatan, iaitu yang dalam [al-dākhilah], yang pertama rumah — ia memberinya pangkat itu dari kirinya; dan ia mempunyainya kaitan rumah itu serta konotasi dan hukumnya dalam khusus dan umum. Dan ini cara bulatan:

[Rajah bulatan penetapan: lingkaran luar 4 rumah (16, 2, 1) dan lingkaran dalam 4 rumah (10, 16, 2, 1) dengan label huruf dibaca h q ṭ ḥ / q ṭ kh h / ṭ ḥ h q / h q ṭ — baris angka geomancy tersusun konsentrik, dengan contoh: 'misalnya, muncul dalam Ascenden geomancy suatu pukulan Ṭarīq — pangkat bilangannya dari kanan bulatan ialah lima, maka perjalanannya ke rumah Naqī al-Khad yang pangkatnya dari kiri bulatan lima — maka konotasi dan hukum kaitan dalam rumah itu [berlaku], dan tinggal baginya hukum yang dituntut dari kiri bulatan iaitu rumah ambang dalam ['atabah al-dākhilah], dan hukum rumah bukti serta rumah kirinya, dan dari geomancy juga.']

((Maqasad Ketiga Belas: tentang penetapan aspek)) Pertimbangan pandangan dalam istimbat dan hukum dengan kaitan ditujukan ke Ascenden dan apa jatuh daripadanya, serta aspek sextile, square, trine dan pandangan opposition yang ialah yang dituntut sendiri, dan apa padanya dari perkongsian dan cara kejadian tuntutan serta penjelasan hukum dan kaitannya — dan itu melalui cara penetapan bulatan ini dan bentuknya.
