English:
— 203 —

…and the Imam 'Uthmān ibn 'Affān, may Allah be pleased with him, asked the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, about { "In the name of Allah, the Merciful, the Compassionate" } over Ibrāhīm while he was in the catapult's palm, and Allah made the fire coolness and peace. And al-Awzā'ī, may Allah have mercy on him, related: "A phantom appeared to me at night and I was terrified of it, so I said { 'In the name of Allah, the Merciful, the Compassionate' } and it said to me: 'You have indeed sought refuge in the Great One,' and it withdrew from me." And indeed every letter of { "In the name of Allah, the Merciful, the Compassionate" } is a key to a name of the names of Allah, exalted — so the Bā' is the key to His name Baṣīr (Seeing), the Sīn is Samī' (Hearing), the Mīm is Mubdi' (Originator), the Alif is Allāh, the Lām is Latīf (Subtle), the Hā' is Hādī (Guide), the Rā' is Rāziq (Provider), the Ḥā' is Hannān (Compassionate), the Mīm is Māni' (Withholder) and Giver; and all of these are called upon at the opening of every thing, and the supplication by these names is answered inevitably. And were we to begin to expand the speech upon them and what they have of properties and wonders and subtleties, time would straiten for us and the pens would tire of that. And some of its properties advanced, and it has a great congruence by which a five-hollow circumference is encompassed — so whoever combines its lettered quarter in one square and carries it sees of the gentleness of Allah, exalted, what descriptions fall short of and what does not enter enumeration; and whoever knows its measure is enriched by it from other than it — for in it is the Greatest Name of Allah, which when called upon answers and when asked gives. And Allah is the One who grants success; and this is its form:

[figure of the square]

And Ibn 'Abbās said: 'Alī ibn Abī Ṭālib, may Allah honor his face, told me, and we went out to al-Baqī' at the beginning of the night, and he said to me: "Recite, O Ibn 'Abbās" — so I recited { "In the name of Allah, the Merciful, the Compassionate" } and he began to lean on me in the Bā' and its requirement until the rising of the dawn. So understand that, and Allah gives His dominion the verse. And he, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said: "I was given the Opening of the Book from beneath the Throne"; and he, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said: "Whoever comes to his dwelling and recites Sūrat al-Fātiḥa and al-Ikhlāṣ — poverty is removed from him and his good multiplies"; and he, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said: "The Opening of the Book is a healing from every disease." And know that among the greatest of the secret of { "Praise be to Allah, Lord of the worlds" } — the first of the Mighty Book — is the seven oft-repeated; so understand the secret of praise, and the praise of the Book connects to Muḥammad, may Allah bless him and grant him peace. And the Imam 'Alī, may Allah honor his face, said: "If I wished to load from the numbering of the Basmala and the Opening of the Book seventy camels, I would have done it." And some of the great ones said: "In this sūra are a thousand manifest properties and a thousand hidden properties." …dan Imam 'Uthmān bin 'Affān raḍiyallāhu 'anhu bertanya pada Nabi shallallahu 'alaihi wa sallam tentang { "Dengan nama Allah Yang Maha Pemurah lagi Maha Penyayang" } atas Ibrāhīm saat ia di pelukan manjanik (ketapel), maka Allah menjadikan api dingin dan damai. Dan al-Awzā'ī raḥimahullāh berkata: "Seekor hantu menampak padaku di malam hari dan aku ketakutan, lalu aku membaca { 'Dengan nama Allah Yang Maha Pemurah lagi Maha Penyayang' } dan ia berkata kepadaku: 'Engkau sungguh memohon perlindungan dengan Yang Agung,' lalu pergi dariku." Dan sesungguhnya setiap huruf { "Dengan nama Allah Yang Maha Pemurah lagi Maha Penyayang" } adalah kunci bagi sebuah nama dari nama-nama Allah Ta'ala — maka Bā' adalah kunci nama-Nya Baṣīr (Melihat), Sīn adalah Samī' (Mendengar), Mīm adalah Mubdi' (Mengadakan), Alif adalah Allāh, Lām adalah Latīf (Halus), Hā' adalah Hādī (Pemberi petunjuk), Rā' adalah Rāziq (Pemberi rezeki), Ḥā' adalah Hannān (Sangat Penyayang), Mīm adalah Māni' (Penghalang) dan Pemberi; dan semua ini dipanjatkan saat memulai setiap perkara, dan doa dengan nama-nama ini pasti dikabulkan. Dan kalau kita mulai melebarkan pembicaraan atas mereka dan apa yang mereka miliki dari sifat, keajaiban, dan kehalusan, waktu akan sempit bagi kita dan pena-pena letih dari itu. Dan sebagian sifatnya telah lalu, dan ia mempunyai keselarasan agung yang dengannya melingkupi lima rongga; maka barangsiapa menggabungkan seperempat hurufnya dalam satu bujur sangkar dan membawanya menyaksikan dari kelembutan Allah Ta'ala apa yang tiada terjangkau deskripsi dan tiada masuk hitungan; dan barangsiapa mengetahui kadarnya dicukupkan olehnya dari selainnya — karena padanya terdapat nama Allah Yang Paling Agung yang apabila dipanjatkan menjawab dan apabila diminta memberi.
