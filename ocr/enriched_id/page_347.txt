And Rūmah he opens it and Qusṭā * and divides its wealth by measure, fully reckoned.
His stay will be twenty years * and twentyfold the gain.
There the one-eyed Dajjāl will come * to the two Syrias in dominion and wealth.
With him a mighty mountain of tharīd * and his description — an event not asked.
His stay in the earth is certain * seven months, the number of perfection.
And the Messiah will kill him in the land of Ludd * and will show the creation the sign.
And his soldiers will be killed in every region * and no place remains for them therein.
And Gog and Magog will come * like a swarm from the utmost similitude.
So neither the Euphrates suffices them * nor Sayḥān and the heavy Tigris,
Nor the river of Syria and the Nile of Egypt * and a sea equal of pure water.
They graze the plants so no plant * returns, and they pull the leaves of the mountains.
And as for the sun, it rises from the west * the heavy rocks melt from its heat.
It remains three days complete * and its heat burns the trees of the sands.
The bottom of the sea appears without doubt * and the beasts and birds remain amazed.
The clouds are cut off so no cloud * and no number returns, no gain.
No piety returns, no zakāt * no excellence returns, no gain.
No child treats his parents kindly * no father relieves his dependents.
Signs of the hardest times in eternity * and the most wicked nation and foulest state.
And [war] is busied with slaughter in every land * as the burning appears in blazing.
And Mecca is ruined and the dwellings of Ṣan'ā' * by plague and heavy ailments.
And Ṭaybah is ruined and the dwellings of Wahb * and its houses remain desert, empty.
And Mosul is ruined and the dwellings of Bakr * and the cities of Sind by the north wind.

Dan Rūmah ia buka serta Qusṭā * dan membagi hartanya secara terukur, terhitung penuh.
Kedudukannya akan dua puluh tahun * dan duapuluh kali lipat keuntungan.
Di sana sang Dajjāl bermata satu akan datang * ke dua Syam dalam kekuasaan dan harta.
Bersamanya gunung tharīd yang dahsyat * dan gambarannya — suatu peristiwa yang tak ditanya.
Kedudukannya di bumi pasti * tujuh bulan, bilangan kesempurnaan.
Dan Al-Masih akan membunuhnya di negeri Ludd * dan menunjukkan kepada makhluk tanda itu.
