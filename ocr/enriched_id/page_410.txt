English:
- 404 -

"Answer, O Ṭūṭā'īl, YHWH, HDWH, YDWH, Wahhāb — answer and rely upon such-and-such. And if you recite this invocation after every prayer, Allah raises your rank among the high ones and good things pour upon you." (The letter Zāy) — it is a cold, moist letter; among its properties is the directing of all predatory beasts. And this letter appeared only in His name Zakkā, and the last of His names — the Exalted, the Mighty — on Thursday when the moon is opposite Jupiter, so its bearer attains glory and awe. And if you write it on a camel's shank with its number while the moon is in it, its bearer never thirsts; and if he sleeps in the wilderness no harmful animal approaches him. And if you will that clouds and rain come to a place, write the letter on black sheep-skin, place it on a ram's head, recite the invocation and the implicit concentration with presence of heart, beseeching Allah the Exalted for the rain to descend, and say "Come, O clouds and rain" — it comes by the power of Allah; some asked for it when people went out to seek rain and were given to drink. And among its properties: if placed in something, it is blessed, especially clarified butter and milk; and if you write it while the moon is in it on a silver dirham with the implicit concentration around it and cast it into the clarified butter, it is blessed; and if you write it with the implicit concentration and carry it, Allah provides for him. And whoever writes the circle of Zāy with musk and saffron and writes with it the name of whom he wills — here is its figure. It has a majestic retreat and recitation of the names after every prayer 21 times; the servant appears and speaks to you of what you will. The incense is saffron, small raisins, and a little olive oil. And if you will to employ it, recite the implicit concentration with the invocation and the oath; and if you write the letter in a ring, carry it, and recite the conjuration, the king appears, makes a pact with you, and fulfills your need. And this is the description of the invocation: "In the name of Allah, the Merciful, the Compassionate — increase in us, O Allah, longing for You and desire in You for what is dearest to Your remembrance, and treat me with Your hidden gentleness and clothe me in light and beauty, by which I seek help to unveil the secrets of the Point, from whose kind the mountains trembled and were crushed from the awe of the Lord of Might — glory be to your Lord, Lord of Might, above what they describe… O hastener, O servant of the letter Zāy, by Māh Ziyāh 2, Yadīz Yūh 2, Zūh 2, Bazūh 2 — Bāṣir, Allah, Lord of the worlds, glorious, beautiful, glory be to Him and exalted; is it not that by the remembrance of Allah hearts are reassured — Hayā, Buṭyāṭiyā, Ṭalā, Ṭalaban… upon him the Judge, Hayā Amān, 'Al Watr, Yātī, and unveil to me your affair, Hayā Yāzāy, by the might of Him who begets not nor is begotten and has no equal — one; answer and rely upon such-and-such by the Alif — there is no power nor strength save in Allah, the High, the Great." And if you recite this invocation in the desert, the game comes to you from every side. And its implicit concentration: "Answer, O master Ṭamashtā'īl, by the right of Sa'das, Haṭāṭam 2 — descend; answer by the right of His growth — al-waḥā, the hastening, the hour." (The letter Ḥā') — it is of the secrets of life, and its number is 8, for it is of the proportion of the Throne; and it is at the first degree of the sphere. Among its properties is the healing of ailments: that it be written with the patient's name and every name beginning with Ḥā', and given to the patient in a vessel with a little bee's honey for a week — and he recovers. And whoever mentions the names beginning with Ḥā' in the days of intense heat and travels in the heat and recites the name at sunrise and at sunset — he feels not the pain of heat nor thirst. And in it is a secret for the masters of states regarding entering the fire, for it is extinguished. And among its properties, to prolong desire: if written on a ring and carried with the name of the king and the implicit concentration, it benefits him. And know that the Ḥā', when it occurs in a Syriac and Arabic name, its ruling is thus; and if the shape of the letter Ḥā' appears in the middle of the name, it is stronger over the causes. And this letter has a majestic retreat with its condition, and recitation of the invocation after every prayer 18 times — and it appears to you.

Indonesia:
- 404 -

"Jawab, wahai Ṭūṭā'īl, YHWH, HDWH, YDWH, Wahhāb — jawab dan bertawakal pada anu. Dan jika engkau membaca doa ini selepas setiap solat, Allah meninggikan martabatmu di alam tinggi dan kebaikan melimpah kepadamu." (Huruf Zāy) — huruf sejuk lembap; di antara sifatnya ialah mengatur segala binatang buas. Dan huruf ini hanya muncul dalam nama-Nya Zakkā, dan nama-Nya yang terakhir — Yang Maha Tinggi, Maha Perkasa — pada hari Khamis ketika bulan berlawanan dengan Musytari, maka pembawanya memperoleh kemuliaan dan keagungan. Dan jika engkau menulisnya di betis unta dengan bilangannya ketika bulan berada padanya, pembawanya tidak pernah dahaga; dan jika ia tidur di padang, tiada binatang berbahaya mendekatinya. Dan jika engkau menghendaki awan dan hujan datang ke suatu tempat, tulis huruf pada kulit biri-biri hitam, letakkan di kepala biri-biri jantan, bacakan doa dan idmār dengan hadir hati, memohon kepada Allah agar hujan turun, dan katakan "Datanglah, wahai awan dan hujan" — ia datang dengan kekuasaan Allah; sebahagian mereka memintanya ketika orang keluar memohon hujan dan diberi minum. Di antara sifatnya: jika diletakkan pada sesuatu, ia diberkati, terutama mentega jernih dan susu; dan jika engkau menulisnya ketika bulan padanya pada dirham perak dengan idmār mengelilinginya dan membalingnya ke dalam mentega jernih, ia diberkati; dan jika engkau menulisnya dengan idmār dan membawanya, Allah memberinya rezeki. Sesiapa menulis bulatan Zāy dengan kasturi dan za'faran dan menulis dengannya nama yang dikehendaki — inilah rupanya. Ia mempunyai khalwat agung dan bacaan nama selepas setiap solat 21 kali; khadam muncul dan berkata kepadamu apa yang dikehendaki. Ubatan ialah za'faran, kismis kecil, dan sedikit minyak zaitun. Jika engkau mahu menggunakannya, bacalah idmār dengan doa dan sumpah; dan jika engkau menulis huruf dalam cincin, membawanya, dan membaca conjuration, raja muncul, berikat denganmu, dan memenuhi hajatmu. Dan inilah sifat doa: "Dengan nama Allah Yang Maha Pemurah lagi Maha Penyayang — tambahkanlah kami, wahai Allah, rindu kepada-Mu dan keinginan pada-Mu bagi apa yang paling disayangi dalam zikir-Mu, dan layanilah aku dengan lemah-lembut tersembunyi-Mu dan pakaikanlah aku cahaya dan keindahan, yang dengannya aku memohon pertolongan untuk membuka rahsia Titik, yang darinya gunung-gunung bergoncang dan hancur kerana kehebatan Tuhan Maha Perkasa — Maha Suci Tuhanmu, Tuhan Maha Perkasa, daripada apa yang mereka sifatkan… Wahai yang segera, wahai khadam huruf Zāy, demi Māh Ziyāh 2, Yadīz Yūh 2, Zūh 2, Bazūh 2 — Bāṣir, Allah, Tuhan sekalian alam, mulia, indah, Maha Suci Dia dan Maha Tinggi; bukankah dengan zikir Allah hati menjadi tenteram — Hayā, Buṭyāṭiyā, Ṭalā, Ṭalaban… atasnya Hakim, Hayā Amān, 'Al Watr, Yātī, dan bukakanlah kepadaku urusanmu, Hayā Yāzāy, demi keagungan Dia yang tidak beranak dan tidak diperanakkan serta tiada sekutu bagi-Nya — satu; jawab dan bertawakal pada anu demi Alif — tiada daya dan kekuatan melainkan pada Allah Yang Maha Tinggi Lagi Maha Agung." Dan jika engkau membaca doa ini di padang pasir, buruan datang kepadamu dari setiap penjuru. Dan idmārnya: "Jawab, wahai tuan Ṭamashtā'īl, demi hak Sa'das, Haṭāṭam 2 — turunlah; jawab demi hak pertumbuhan-Nya — al-waḥā, yang segera, saat itu." (Huruf Ḥā') — ia daripada rahsia kehidupan, dan bilangannya 8, kerana ia daripada nisbah 'Arasy; dan ia di darjat pertama falak. Di antara sifatnya menyembuhkan penyakit: ditulis dengan nama pesakit dan setiap nama bermula dengan Ḥā', dan diberi minum kepada pesakit dalam bekas dengan sedikit madu lebah selama seminggu — maka ia sembuh. Sesiapa menyebut nama bermula dengan Ḥā' di hari-hari panas terik dan bermusafir dalam panas serta membaca nama ketika matahari naik dan terbenam — ia tidak merasa sakit panas mahupun dahaga. Dan padanya rahsia bagi tuanku keadaan memasuki api, kerana api dipadamkan. Di antara sifatnya memanjangkan nafsu: jika ditulis pada cincin dan dibawa dengan nama raja dan idmār, ia memberi manfaat. Ketahuilah bahawa Ḥā', apabila terdapat dalam nama Suryani dan Arab, hukumnya demikian; dan jika bentuk huruf Ḥā' muncul di tengah nama, ia lebih kuat atas sebab-sebab. Huruf ini mempunyai khalwat agung dengan syaratnya, dan bacaan doa selepas setiap solat 18 kali — maka ia muncul kepadamu.
