English:
— 542 —

"…and the look of opposition [is] an opposing enemy. (The Ninth Intent: on the falling [sawāqiṭ] of the aspect) The four remaining houses from the twelve — they are the second, sixth, eighth, and twelfth — are non-aspecting; they fall from the Ascendant, and it does not look to them. And they — except the first of them, which is the third house — are intense enemies to the Ascendant. So the third of them, which is the eighth house, has an intense defilement, for it is the house of non-existence and perdition; and the second, which is the sixth house, is the house of diseases and defects and calamities; and the fourth, which is the twelfth house, is the house of afflictions and sorrows. And every form that alights in them is stripped of strength and benefit, and may portend harm. As for the first, it is fortunate, not blameworthy, and is known as 'al-Khayrah' (the best), for it ascends to the Ascendant. (The Tenth Intent: on the quests [muṭālabāt] of the houses) The sixteen inscribed houses — each of them seeks its seventh, and the seventh is sought for it; and likewise the sought seeks its seventh, and its seventh is sought for it, and so on until the quest ends at the quester, so he becomes at that moment sought by the ultimate of his quests. And these quests are confined to the two houses al-Jawdah and al-Ḥayān — for each of them seeks its seventh from the preceding settled [tasīn] whose position was set, and the seventh seeks its seventh in succession until the quest ends at them; upon them hinge the quests. And the basis of the work in the beginnings of the look into the matter of the Ascendant of the geomancy is: considering it sought by the first, i.e. from the settling [tasīn] not the struck geomancy; and considering the rule in the relatings of that quester and the sought; and this is the manner of the quests — each form sought by what is after it, and its quester what is before it. [The two quest-chains are listed:] (al-Jawdah) Ḥumrah, 'Atabah Dākhilah, Naṣrah Khārijah, Jamā'ah, Naqī, Ijtimā' — (al-Ḥayān) Inkīs, Qabḍ Khārij, Bayāḍ, 'Aqlah, Qabḍ Dākhil, 'Atabah, Naṣrah Dākhilah. (The Eleventh Intent: on the proofs [adillah] of the witnesses of the houses) Just as every house seeks its seventh, so it seeks a proof and a witness — so the proof of every house is from the settling [tasīn] and from the struck geomancy: the house whose third it is, he is its proof and witness, and its manner is as the quests. And this is its form: [al-Jawdah: Rāyah, Aṣah, Ḥumrah, Naṣrah Khārijah, Ijtimā', Ṭarīq, Jamā'ah; al-Ḥayān: Bayāḍ, 'Atabah, Inkīs, 'Aqlah, Sa'ādah, Qabḍ Khārij, Qabḍ Dākhil]. And likewise for every house there is a left [yasār] as the proof and witness — and it is the house whose sixth it is; and a right [yamīn] — and it is the house whose tenth it is; and the basis of that is upon this division — each form of it, what is after it is its left, and its first is the left of its last, as you see: [al-Jawdah: 'Atabah Khārijah, Ijtimā', Qabḍ Dākhil; Naqī: al-Ḥadd, 'Aqlah, Jamā'ah, Bayāḍ; Naṣrah Khārijah: Qabḍ Khārij, 'Atabah Dākhilah, Inkīs; Ṭarīq: al-Ḥayān, Ḥumrah, Naṣrah Dākhilah]. (The Twelfth Intent: on the settling [tasīn] of the circle of geomancy) And the houses of geomancy, with respect to the rules of the quests and the proofs and the witnesses and the left and the right as preceded, and the rules of the course of the Ascendant…

Indonesia:
— 542 —

"…dan pandangan opposition [ialah] musuh menentang. (Maqasad Kesembilan: tentang jatuhan [sawāqiṭ] aspek) Empat rumah selebihnya dari dua belas — mereka rumah kedua, keenam, kelapan, dan kedua belas — tidak beraspek; mereka jatuh dari Ascenden, dan ia tidak memandang mereka. Dan mereka — kecuali pertama dari mereka, iaitu rumah ketiga — musuh hebat pada Ascenden. Maka ketiga dari mereka, iaitu rumah kelapan, mempunyai najis hebat, kerana ia rumah ketiadaan dan kebinasaan; dan kedua, iaitu rumah keenam, rumah penyakit dan cacat serta bala; dan keempat, iaitu rumah kedua belas, rumah kesengsaraan dan duka. Dan tiap bentuk yang turun padanya terlucut kekuatan dan faedah, serta mungkin membawa mudarat. Adapun yang pertama, ia bertuah, tidak tercela, dan dikenali 'al-Khayrah' (yang terbaik), kerana ia naik ke Ascenden. (Maqasad Kesepuluh: tentang tuntutan [muṭālabāt] rumah) Enam belas rumah terukir — tiap daripadanya menuntut ketujuhnya, dan ketujuh dituntut baginya; dan begitu yang dituntut menuntut ketujuhnya, dan ketujuhnya dituntut baginya, hingga tuntutan berakhir pada penuntut, maka ia saat itu dituntut oleh hujung tuntutannya. Dan tuntutan ini terbatas pada dua rumah al-Jawdah dan al-Ḥayān — kerana tiap mereka menuntut ketujuhnya dari tasīn terdahulu yang kedudukannya ditetapkan, dan ketujuh menuntut ketujuhnya bersilih hingga tuntutan berakhir padanya; padanya bergantung tuntutan. Dan asas kerja dalam permulaan pandangan ke urusan Ascenden geomancy ialah: menganggap ia dituntut oleh yang pertama, iaitu dari tasīn bukan geomancy dipukul; dan menganggap hukum dalam kaitan penuntut dan yang dituntut itu; dan ini cara tuntutan — tiap bentuk dituntut oleh apa selepasnya, dan penuntutnya apa sebelumnya. [Dua rantaian tuntutan disenaraikan:] (al-Jawdah) Ḥumrah, 'Atabah Dākhilah, Naṣrah Khārijah, Jamā'ah, Naqī, Ijtimā' — (al-Ḥayān) Inkīs, Qabḍ Khārij, Bayāḍ, 'Aqlah, Qabḍ Dākhil, 'Atabah, Naṣrah Dākhilah. (Maqasad Kesebelas: tentang bukti [adillah] saksi rumah) Sebagaimana tiap rumah menuntut ketujuhnya, maka ia menuntut bukti dan saksi — maka bukti tiap rumah dari tasīn dan dari geomancy dipukul: rumah yang ketiganya, ia bukti dan saksinya, dan caranya seperti tuntutan. Dan ini bentuknya: [al-Jawdah: Rāyah, Aṣah, Ḥumrah, Naṣrah Khārijah, Ijtimā', Ṭarīq, Jamā'ah; al-Ḥayān: Bayāḍ, 'Atabah, Inkīs, 'Aqlah, Sa'ādah, Qabḍ Khārij, Qabḍ Dākhil]. Dan begitu tiap rumah ada kiri [yasār] sebagai bukti dan saksi — ia rumah yang keenamnya; dan kanan [yamīn] — ia rumah yang kesepuluhnya; dan asas itu atas pembahagian ini — tiap bentuknya, apa selepasnya kiri, dan pertamanya kiri akhirnya, sebagaimana engkau lihat: [al-Jawdah: 'Atabah Khārijah, Ijtimā', Qabḍ Dākhil; Naqī: al-Ḥadd, 'Aqlah, Jamā'ah, Bayāḍ; Naṣrah Khārijah: Qabḍ Khārij, 'Atabah Dākhilah, Inkīs; Ṭarīq: al-Ḥayān, Ḥumrah, Naṣrah Dākhilah]. (Maqasad Kedua Belas: tentang penetapan [tasīn] bulatan geomancy) Dan rumah geomancy, berkenaan hukum tuntutan dan bukti dan saksi serta kiri dan kanan seperti lalu, dan hukum perjalanan Ascenden…
