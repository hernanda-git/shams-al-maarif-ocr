"…[the Fourth Type continued:] Ḥumrah is good in the Ascendant in the question about disputes and the debate of opponents; and Inkīs is good in all the pegs [awtād], indicating good contrary to its relatings. And the outward threshold ['atabah khārijah] is good at the end of the hand in the question about the absent and the traveler and about the transfer of a resident of a town to another town. And Qabḍ Khārij is good in the locus of the connotation and in the question about the self. {The Fifth Type} al-Ḥayān — its alighting in the sixth and eighth and twelfth and sixteenth is bad; and al-Bayāḍ is bad in the eighth and ninth and twelfth, and in the fourth upon the settling [is] the seventh — for it is the shroud of the sick; and Naṣrah Khārijah in the fourth and eighth and twelfth is bad; and 'Atabah Dākhilah and Naqī al-Khad and Naṣrah Dākhilah and Qabḍ Dākhil — their alighting is bad in the sixth and eighth and twelfth houses. {The Sixth Type} the external figures in the question about what is outside the hand and in the rule of the external; and the internal figures are good in that. {The Twenty-Ninth Intent: on the settlings [tasākīn] of geomancy} The settling of Aḥwadh has the originality in geomancy and in the rules of the aspects and the partakings and the quests and the deduction of the concealed [matters] and other than that; and other settlings not renowned, and in the inner of the affair they do not oppose the established way. So among that is this settling, for the one taking the omen from the connotation of the question…

"…[Jenis Keempat disambung:] Ḥumrah baik dalam Ascenden dalam soalan tentang pertikaian dan perdebatan lawan; dan Inkīs baik dalam semua pasak [awtād], menunjuk baik bertentangan kaitannya. Dan ambang luar ['atabah khārijah] baik di hujung tangan dalam soalan tentang yang ghaib dan musafir serta tentang pemindahan penduduk pekan ke pekan lain. Dan Qabḍ Khārij baik dalam tempat konotasi dan dalam soalan tentang diri. {Jenis Kelima} al-Ḥayān — pendaratannya dalam keenam, kelapan, kedua belas, dan keenam belas buruk; dan al-Bayāḍ buruk dalam kelapan, kesembilan, kedua belas, dan dalam keempat atas penetapan [ialah] ketujuh — kerana ia kain kafan orang sakit; dan Naṣrah Khārijah dalam keempat, kelapan, kedua belas buruk; dan 'Atabah Dākhilah dan Naqī al-Khad dan Naṣrah Dākhilah dan Qabḍ Dākhil — pendaratan mereka buruk dalam rumah keenam, kelapan, kedua belas. {Jenis Keenam} angka luar dalam soalan tentang apa di luar tangan dan dalam hukum luar; dan angka dalam baik dalam itu. {Maqasad Dua Puluh Sembilan: tentang penetapan [tasākīn] geomancy} Penetapan Aḥwadh mempunyai keaslian dalam geomancy dan dalam hukum aspek, perkongsian, tuntutan, dan istimbat perkara tersembunyi serta selain itu; dan penetapan lain tidak termasyhur, dan dalam batin urusan mereka tidak menentang cara mapan. Maka antaranya penetapan ini, bagi yang mengambil alamat dari konotasi soalan…
