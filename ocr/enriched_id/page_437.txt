English:
— 231 —

"Know that Allah created the world — each of the two [worlds] most-intelligent according to its capacity; so He created the speaking animal and created in it the diverse signs for the reception of the lights and the unveiling of the secrets of the Dominion-things. So He created man, then the mountains, and branched from them the minerals. And the Mīm was its principle, for it encompasses the essence of the intellects, and He is beneficial by the very count of them — and therefore Allah lodged it in the most beloved of creation to Him, and addressed him and answered him in the first of the stages. And He created the spirit by the Spirit, so in it was the wisdom of awe, and in it is the detailing of the spirit — that is the received acceptance of the perfections and secrets. So that lordly bestower divided from the dominion, and Allah disposed for the spirit angels above it, by which it receives upon them the secrets of the unseen by the realities of the Dominion; and He made them the world of dominion. And this world contains 3 worlds for all the world of plant, animal, and mineral; and the best of the animals was the human essence, and this essence is confined to essences, soul, and heart. And since the knowledge of power is not bound by the plant-world, the plant-world is found in the deserts and wilds and is not confined to one place — so the thoughts of the heart are innumerable; and the saying for the heart is 7 regions, just as the earth is a division upon 7 regions of the soul, for the heart is the reality of the form, and He poured upon the secret and the spirit the two halves of faith, and poured upon the soul, intellect, and secret 8. And I make clear to you regions from the seven: the first is the region of the fu'ād, which is the place of dominion — for Allah said: 'My earth and My heaven encompassed Me not, but the heart of My believing servant encompassed Me.' The second is the region of the suwaydā', which is the place of the heart which is the rank of the vizier. The third is the region of the shaghāf, which is the place of the vizier. The fourth is the region of the jihah, which is the place between the shaghāf and the black [core]; the region of the damīr, which is the place of the secret. The sixth is the region of the ghilāf. The seventh is the region of the encompassing of the heart. And for every region is a gate: the first gate is the secret of life; the second gate the secret of knowledge; the third the secret of power; the fourth the secret of will; the fifth the secret of mercy; the sixth the secret of wisdom; the seventh the secret of action. And the regions have 40 veils, which are between the servant and his Lord — and for that I made the discipline 40 days, for each day unveils a veil; and thereby the seeker overlooks the seven regions and looks at their wonders and what Allah deposited in them of plant, animal, and mineral. And these are the curtains: first the curtain of dust, then the curtain of water, then the curtain of air, then the curtain of fire, then the curtain of dryness, then the curtain of moisture, then the curtain of heat, then the curtain of the yellow bile, then the curtain of phlegm, then the curtain of black bile, then the curtain of blood, then the curtain of ignorance, then the curtain of heedlessness, then the curtain of counting, then the curtain of density, then the curtain of opposition, then the curtain of sedimentation, then the curtain of desire, then the curtain of claim, then the curtain of fear, then the curtain of hope, then the curtain of honor, then the curtain of deeds, then the curtain of sayings, then the curtain of contraction, then the curtain of expansion, then the curtain of nourishment, then the curtain of worship, then the curtain of distress, then the curtain of sleep, then the curtain of day, then the curtain of night, then the curtain of the seal [death], then the curtain of the precedent — and these 40 curtains are the seven gates. And these curtains are lifted by four kinds, and for every 10 curtains is one light: the first ten — their lifting is the light of life; the second — the light of knowledge; the third — the light of power; the fourth — the light of will. And I disclose the disposition of that: the first in the rankers (al-ṣāffāt) a ranking; the second in the restrainers (al-zājirāt) a restraining; the third in the reciters (al-tāliyāt) a remembrance; the fourth in the scatterers (al-dhāriyāt) a scattering; the fifth in the bearers (al-ḥāmilāt) a load; the sixth in the runners (al-jāriyāt) an ease; the seventh in the dividers (al-muqassimāt) a command; the eighth in the Mount; the ninth in a written book; the tenth in the inhabited House; the eleventh in the raised roof; the twelfth in the emissaries (al-mursalāt) a custom; the thirteenth in…"

"Ketahuilah Allah cipta alam — dua alam paling akal mengikut daya; Dia cipta haiwan bercakap cipta padanya tanda berbeza untuk terima cahaya buka rahsia Malakut. Cipta manusia gunung cabang daripadanya galian. Mīm prinsipnya kerana meliputi inti akal, baik dengan bilangannya — maka Allah letak pada makhluk paling dicintai, bercakap menjawab di peringkat pertama. Cipta roh dengan Roh, ada hikmah gerun, terperinci roh — penerimaan sempurna rahsia. Pemberi rabbani bahagi dari domini, Allah atur malaikat atas roh terima rahsia ghaib hakikat Malakut; jadikan alam domini. Alam ini isi 3 alam tumbuhan haiwan galian; terbaik haiwan zat manusia, zat ini terhad pada zat jiwa hati. Ilmu kuasa tak terikat alam tumbuhan, tumbuhan di padang tak terhad satu tempat — fikiran hati tak terhitung; hati 7 wilayah seperti bumi 7 bahagian jiwa, hati hakikat bentuk, limpah pada sirr roh dua separuh iman, limpah pada jiwa akal sirr 8. Aku terangkan wilayah dari tujuh: pertama wilayah fu'ād tempat domini — firman 'Bumi langit-Ku tak muat, tapi hati hamba-Ku yang mukmin muat.' Kedua wilayah suwaydā' tempat hati darjat wazir. Ketiga wilayah shaghāf tempat wazir. Keempat wilayah jihah antara shaghāf hitam; wilayah damīr tempat sirr. Keenam wilayah ghilāf. Ketujuh wilayah meliputi hati. Tiap wilayah pintu: pertama rahsia hayat; kedua ilmu; ketiga kuasa; keempat kehendak; kelima rahmat; keenam hikmah; ketujuh amal. Wilayah ada 40 tabir antara hamba Tuhan — maka riyadhah 40 hari, tiap hari buka tabir; pencari lihat 7 wilayah keajaiban apa Allah taruh tumbuhan haiwan galian. Inilah tabir: debu, air, angin, api, kering, lembap, panas, hempedu kuning, kahak, hempedu hitam, darah, jahil, lalai, kiraan, ketumpatan, penentangan, mendakan, nafsu, dakwaan, takut, harap, kemuliaan, perbuatan, kata-kata, kecut, luas, makanan, ibadah, sempit, tidur, siang, malam, penutup (mati), mendahului — 40 tabir ialah 7 pintu. Tabir diangkat 4 jenis, tiap 10 tabir satu cahaya: 10 pertama cahaya hayat; kedua ilmu; ketiga kuasa; keempat kehendak. Aku beritahu susunan: pertama dalam al-ṣāffāt barisan; kedua al-zājirāt cegah; ketiga al-tāliyāt zikir; keempat al-dhāriyāt serak; kelima al-ḥāmilāt muatan; keenam al-jāriyāt mudah; ketujuh al-muqassimāt perintah; kelapan at-Tūr; kesembilan kitab tertulis; kesepuluh Baitul Ma'mur; kesebelas bumbung diangkat; kedua belas al-mursalāt adat; ketiga belas dalam…"
