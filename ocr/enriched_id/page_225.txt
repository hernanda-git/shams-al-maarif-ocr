English:
— 219 —

…fearful things and terrifying matters, so he complained to some shaykhs and laid it before him, and the shaykh said to him: "When you turn to your bed, seek refuge in Allah from the accursed Satan three times; then when you reach His saying, exalted: { 'and their guardianship does not burden Him, and He is the High, the Great' } repeat it three times and sleep — for you will be safe from what you find." He did so and after that found nothing he disliked. * And of its properties: when you want to enter upon a king or a tyrant and fear his evil, say as you enter upon him: "The faces bore witness" three times, and recite the Throne Verse three times, and say: "O Allah, cast upon me of Your adornment and Your love and Your honor and the might of Your lordship what subdues hearts by it, and souls are humbled before it, and eyes are dazzled by it, and thoughts are dulled by it, and every proud tyrant submits to it — O Exalted, O Forgiving, O One, O Single, preserve me in what You have given me possession of, of which You are more Owner than I; and supply me with a subtle emanation of the emanations of the preserving king, so I snatch by it the gazes of existents, and clothe me in a coat of mail of Your sufficiency and guardianship, and gird me with the sword of Your victory and honor and protection, and crown me with the crown of Your honor and might, and return me in a robe of You and Your well-being, and mount me on the mount of deliverance until death, and supply me with a subtle emanation of Your subjugating names, by which I repel from myself whoever intends me evil from all Your creation — as You subjected the sea to Mūsā son of 'Imrān, and soften for me their hearts as iron was softened for David, upon him peace; for they do not speak except by Your permission; their forelocks are in Your grasp, You turn them as You will — O Turner of hearts, by the marks of the unseen, You extinguished the anger of So-and-so son of So-and-so" — and if you wish you say: "You extinguished the anger of the people by 'there is no god but Allah' " — "and attracted their affection and love by the love of the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace: { 'So when they saw him, they magnified him and cut their hands and said: Glory be to Allah — this is no man; this is but a noble angel' } — and there is no power nor strength except by Allah, the High, the Great.' * And know that of the properties of the Throne Verse: if you are in a feared place, sit you and those with you on the ground, and command the people to put their backs to one another, and draw a circle around them with you inside it, and recite over the line the Throne Verse seven times, and say after it: { 'and their guardianship does not burden Him, and He is the High, the Great' }, { 'and We preserved it — that is the estimation of the Exalted, the Knowing' }, { 'and We preserved it from every rebellious satan' }, { 'and We preserved it from every accursed satan' }, { 'Indeed We sent down the Reminder and indeed We are its preservers' }, { 'For him are successive guardians before and behind him who preserve him by the command of Allah' }, { 'Nay, it is a glorious Qur'an in a preserved tablet' }, { 'And We saved him and his family from the great distress' }, { 'And We saved him from grief, and thus We save the believers' }, { 'and you are not over them a guardian' }, { 'indeed every soul has over it a guardian' }, { 'But if they turn away, say: Allah is sufficient for me; there is no god but He; upon Him I rely, and He is the Lord of the Mighty Throne' }, { 'Allah is a Guardian over them, and you are not over them a trustee' } — and you say: 'Preserver, preserver, O Preserver, O Trusted, preserve us, O Allah; guard us by Your eye that does not sleep, and shelter us by Your shelter that is not assailed, O Allah, O Lord of the worlds' — hold fast, you and those with you, and do not speak; for were Rabī'a and Muḍar to enter upon you, they would not harm you nor hurt you, and Allah preserves you from them. * And know that this noble verse descended from beneath the leg of the Throne, and when it descended upon the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, with it descended seventy thousand angels honoring it, and it is the savior from fear and fright and from jinn and men; and whoever recites it in fear is safe from every evil and guarded — for it is the verse of guarding; and it has 72 operations, which I have refrained from mentioning out of fear of the ignorant. * And of its properties: when a man goes out to travel and wants to leave his town, let him say: "A thousand thousand thousand: 'Say: He is Allah, One,' and the Throne Verse" — by it he protects wealth, child, and family — "a thousand thousand thousand: 'Say: He is Allah, One,' and the Throne Verse to my right and left; I have taken refuge by them from everyone — there is no veil but the encompassing God on high, and I have fortified myself by Allah."

…hal yang menakutkan dan mengerikan, maka ia mengeluh pada sebagian syaikh dan menyampaikannya, lalu syaikh berkata kepadanya: "Apabila engkau menuju tempat tidur, mohonlah perlindungan kepada Allah dari setan yang terkutuk tiga kali; maka apabila engkau sampai pada firman-Nya Ta'ala: { 'dan memelihara keduanya tidak memberatkan-Nya; dan Dia Mahatinggi lagi Mahaagung' } ucapkan tiga kali dan tidurlah — maka engkau aman dari apa yang engkau rasakan." Ia melakukannya dan setelah itu tiada mendapati sesuatu yang ia benci. * Dan dari sifatnya: apabila engkau hendak menemui raja atau tiran dan takut kejahatannya, bacalah saat masuk kepadanya: "Wajah-wajah menyaksikan" tiga kali, serta membaca Āyat al-Kursī tiga kali, dan membaca: "Ya Allah, tumpahkan padaku dari perhiasan-Mu, cinta-Mu, kemuliaan-Mu, dan keagungan ke-tuhan-an-Mu apa yang menundukkan hati, tunduk padanya jiwa, terpesona padanya pandangan, tumpul padanya pikiran, dan patuh padanya setiap yang sombong tiran — Wahai Mahaagung, Wahai Maha Pengampun, Wahai Esa, Wahai Tunggal, peliharalah aku dalam apa yang Engkau kuasakan padaku yang Engkau lebih berkuasa atasnya dariku; dan berilah aku sebersit dari sebersit malaikat Maha Memelihara, maka kurebut dengannya pandangan makhluk, dan memakaikan aku baju zirah dari kecukupan dan pemeliharaan-Mu, dan menyelimutiku dengan pedang kemenangan, kemuliaan, dan perlindungan-Mu, serta memahkotai aku dengan mahkota kemuliaan dan keagungan-Mu, dan mengembalikan aku dalam jubah dari-Mu dan kesejahteraan-Mu, dan menaikkan aku di kendaraan keselamatan hingga mati, serta memberiku sebersit dari nama-nama penunduk-Mu, yang dengannya aku tolak siapa yang hendak mencelakai aku dari seluruh makhluk-Mu — sebagaimana Engkau tundukkan laut bagi Mūsā bin 'Imrān, dan lunakkan hati mereka bagiku sebagaimana besi dilunakkan bagi Dāwūd 'alaihissalām; karena mereka tiada berkata melainkan dengan izin-Mu, ubun-ubun mereka di genggaman-Mu, Engkau balikkan sesuka-Mu — Wahai Pembolak-balik hati, dengan tanda gaib, Engkau padamkan amarah Si Fulan bin Fulanah" — dan apabila engkau kehendaki engkau membaca: "Engkau padamkan amarah manusia dengan 'tiada tuhan selain Allah' " — "dan tarik kasih serta cinta mereka dengan cinta Rasulullah shallallahu 'alaihi wa sallam: { 'Maka tatkala mereka melihatnya, mereka menghormatinya dan memotong tangan mereka serta berkata: Mahasuci Allah — ini bukan manusia; ini hanyalah malaikat mulia' } — dan tiada daya nora kekuatan kecuali dengan Allah Yang Mahatinggi lagi Mahaagung.' * Dan ketahuilah bahwa dari sifat Āyat al-Kursī: apabila engkau di tempat yang ditakuti, duduklah engkau dan yang bersamamu di tanah, dan perintahkan orang meletakkan punggung mereka berhadapan, serta gambarlah lingkaran di sekitar mereka dengan engkau di dalamnya, dan bacalah pada garis Āyat al-Kursī tujuh kali, dan setelahnya membaca: { 'dan memelihara keduanya tiada memberatkan-Nya; dan Dia Mahatinggi lagi Mahaagung' }, { 'dan Kami pelihara itu — itulah ketentuan Yang Mahatinggi lagi Maha Mengetahui' }, { 'dan Kami pelihara dari setiap setan durhaka' }, { 'dan Kami pelihara dari setiap setan terkutuk' }, { 'Sesungguhnya Kamilah yang menurunkan Peringatan dan Kamilah pemeliharanya' }, { 'Baginya para penjaga di depan dan belakang yang memeliharanya dengan perintah Allah' }, { 'Bahkan ia al-Qur'an yang mulia dalam loh terpelihara' }, { 'Dan Kami selamatkan dia dan keluarganya dari kesusahan besar' }, { 'Dan Kami selamatkan dia dari duka, dan demikian Kami selamatkan orang mukmin' }, { 'dan engkau bukan penjaga atas mereka' }, { 'sesungguhnya tiap jiwa mempunyai penjaga' }, { 'Maka jika mereka berpaling, katakanlah: Cukuplah Allah bagiku; jagalah kami dengan mata-Mu yang tiada tidur, dan lindungilah kami dengan lindungan-Mu yang tiada terjamah, ya Allah ya Tuhan semesta alam' — berpeganglah engkau dan yang bersamamu dan jangan berkata-kata; maka sekalipun Rabī'a dan Muḍar masuk kepadamu, mereka tiada membahayakan dan melukaimu, serta Allah memelihara engkau dari mereka. * Dan ketahuilah bahwa ayat mulia ini turun dari bawah kaki 'Arsy, dan ketika turun pada Nabi shallallahu 'alaihi wa sallam bersamanya turun tujuh puluh ribu malaikat menghormatinya, dan ia penyelamat dari takut dan kaget serta dari jin dan manusia; dan barangsiapa membacanya dalam ketakutan aman dari setiap kejahatan dan terjaga — karena ia ayat penjagaan; dan ia mempunyai 72 ragam, yang aku tinggalkan penyebutannya karena takut pada orang jahil. * Dan dari sifatnya: apabila seseorang keluar bepergian dan hendak meninggalkan negerinya, hendaklah ia membaca: "Seribu ribu ribu: 'Katakanlah: Dialah Allah Yang Esa,' dan Āyat al-Kursī" — dengannya ia menjaga harta, anak, dan keluarga — "seribu ribu ribu: 'Katakanlah: Dialah Allah Yang Esa' dan Āyat al-Kursī di kananku dan kiriku; aku berlindung dengannya dari setiap orang — tiada tabir selain Allah yang meliputi lagi Mahatinggi, dan aku bentengi diri dengan Allah."
