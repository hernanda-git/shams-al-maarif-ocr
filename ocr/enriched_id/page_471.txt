English:
— 465 —

"(Section on His name the Exalted, al-Shakūr) Know that al-Shakūr and al-Shākir are one in meaning from the side of the attribute and the thanking and the hyperbole — and He is the one who gives upon all the obediences many degrees, and gives by the little deed abundant bliss; and one recites it its number by His simplicity, so the angel attends and fulfills his need, and his name is ṬAWYĀYĀ'ĪL. And of its special properties, for blessing in the provision and the continuance of the blessing and the attainment of the goals — he writes its square on a tablet of gold or silver and carries it and recites the name, so Allah opens to him the provision. And this is its figure. And as for his mention — the basmala: 'O Allah, You are al-Shakūr, who inspired Your servant the praise and the thanking | ل | ش | كو | ر | and strengthened them upon the obediences and the mention, so You are al-Shakūr, the Good-doer by Your majesty, the blessings — by what | ١٩٨ | ٢٤ | ٣٢ | ٢٩٩ | You inspired with thanking and good-doing; sanctified are Your attributes by the courses of the magnification, | ٢٥ | ١ | ٣٤ | ٣٣ | the obediences by the abundant favoring and the raising of the highs from the degrees. I ask You that You make me of Your thanking servants and by Your favor of Your bounties, of Your praising rememberers, so You accept my little deed by Your abundant favor and illumine my heart by Your holy light, that I be of Your people and gather to me the assemblings of the good-deeds and the growths of the blessings in the life and the death. O Allah, O Shakūr, I ask You that You subordinate to me Your servant QURṬĀYĀ'ĪL — indeed You are over all things powerful.' (Section on His name the Exalted, al-'Alī) Know that al-'Alī is the one whose rank is not above it a rank; and the high is either a sensible highness, as the degrees, or a highness in the ranks of the sayables, as the difference between the cause and the caused, the complete and the deficient. So when you understood this gradation, you knew that the existents cannot be divided into mutually-different degrees in the intellectual degrees. And it has a majestic retreat that gives its possessor the highness of the rank; and QAYNYĀYĀ'ĪL is its servant, so you recite it until it mingles with his flesh and his blood in a retreat with discipline after every prayer — the servant comes to him and fulfills his need. And whoever writes it and carries it is provided awe and acceptance; and if to it is added al-Kabīr and he makes it tripled in the inward of the square and carries it — any ruler — Allah provides him awe and gives him the troops; and if it is written on silver and carried by one distressed in marriage, the suitor comes to her. And its mention with the basmala: 'O Allah, You are al-'Alī, the Most High, whose highness none like the highness of the creatures, and none equals Your light the light of the existents and the earth and the heavens — for Your noble Footstool which encompassed all the creatures, and Your great Throne above the highness of the high degrees, and every existent in it a dust of dusts. And as for the highness of Your matter — it is exalted above state and place, and sanctified above what is found in the appearances and the times, for it is the highness of greatness and glory and the loftiness of splendor and perfection. I ask You by the highness of Your mercy upon all the highs and the sublimity of Your divinity above the great majesties, and Your oneness and Your uniqueness above the nobility of the refinement of the perfections, that You raise my nearness to You by the beauties of the obediences, and make me sincere in it to Your noble face in all times to the deaths. O Allah, place me in Your fortress and prevent from me every opponent, and send down the subdual of Your highness upon who wills my harm from every envier and rebel. O Allah, take my heart to the highness of Your mercy of Your equality, and take my innermost to the manifestation of Your high holiness, and make me worthy of Your sainthood and the company of Your prophets, O Allah, O 'Alī — whoever persists in this… (30 — Sun of Knowledges — Fourth)"

Indonesia:

"(Fasal al-Shakūr) al-Shakūr al-Shākir satu makna dari sifat syukur hiperbola — Dia beri banyak darjat atas ketaatan, dengan amal sedikit nikmat banyak; baca bilangannya malaikat hadir tunaikan namanya ṬAWYĀYĀ'ĪL. Sifat: berkat rezeki kekal capai matlamat — tulis segi empat loh emas perak bawa baca Allah buka rezeki. Inilah rupanya. Zikir basmalah: 'Ya Allah Engkau al-Shakūr ilhamkan hamba puji syukur | ل | ش | كو | ر | kuatkan ketaatan zikir, ya Shakūr baik dengan kemuliaan nikmat | ١٩٨ | ٢٤ | ٣٢ | ٢٩٩ | ilham syukur ihsan, suci sifat tasbih | ٢٥ | ١ | ٣٤ | ٣٣ | hamba bersyukur kurnia puji, terima amal sedikit nikmat cahaya suci hati, jadi ahlimu kumpul kebaikan berkat hidup mati. Ya Allah ya Shakūr takluk QURṬĀYĀ'ĪL.' (Fasal al-'Alī) al-'Alī tiada darjat di atasnya; tinggi deria (darjat) atau tinggi maqam qawl (sebab musabab sempurna kurang). Faham gradasi tahu wujud tak boleh bahagi darjat berbeza akal. Khalwat agung beri darjat tinggi; khadam QAYNYĀYĀ'ĪL baca hingga bercampur daging darah khalwat riyadhah selepas solat — khadam datang tunaikan. Tulis bawa beri takut hormat; tambah al-Kabīr tiga kali dalam segi empat bawa — raja Allah beri takut tentera; tulis perak bawa yang susah kahwin datang pinangan. Zikir basmalah: 'Ya Allah Engkau al-'Alī Tertinggi tiada sama tinggi makhluk, tiada sama cahaya-Mu cahaya wujud bumi langit — Kursi mulia limpah makhluk, 'Arasy agung di atas darjat tinggi, tiap wujud debu debu. Tinggi urusan-Mu di atas keadaan tempat suci dari zahir masa, keagungan mulia sempurna. Kuminta tinggi rahmat atas semua, keesaan kemuliaan penyucian, angkat dekat aku dengan ketaatan ikhlas wajah-Mu tiap masa hingga mati. Ya Allah kubu cegah lawan turunkan kekuasaan siapa mahu celakaku dengki derhaka. Ya Allah angkat hati ke rahmat kesetaraan, jiwa ke zahir tinggi suci, jadikan ahli wali nabi ya Allah ya 'Alī — istiqamah… (30 — Shams al-Ma'arif — Keempat)"
