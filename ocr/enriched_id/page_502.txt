English:
— 496 —

"…the humans and the jinn, and it is the middle of the states, and the absorption and the annihilation are the end of the states — so whoever was at the first of the states, the attribute of majesty appeared upon him; and whoever was middle in the states, the expansion appeared upon him; and whoever was at the end of the states, the states of the establishment appeared upon him, outwardly and inwardly. And it is related from Ibn al-Jalāl that he said: 'I was riding on a camel, and the camel's foot sank in the sand, so I said "His majesty (jalāluhu)!" and the camel answered: "Exalted be Allah (jalla Allāh)!"' So the camel had the power of readiness in two aspects: the first, that the camel was intent upon Allah the Exalted — and the witness in that is His saying (may Allah bless him and grant him peace): "If you were in a mountain, you would live upon Allah." The second aspect: that the camel, when the principles of the states coming upon the majesty did not overcome it, the camel was not let loose so that it could bear the states coming upon it and the knowledge from the majesty of Allah, so Allah the Exalted made articulate from the reality of the state upon the tongue of the camel — even though it is an animal with a spirit that articulated from the reality of the state. And he who knows His generosity the Exalted — he surrenders to Him his heart and his essence, and relies upon His management of him by His generosity, so He delivers him from the manifest and hidden enemy. Do you not see that the mother of Mūsā surrendered her heart and Allah the Exalted held it, and saved her son from destruction after she cast him in the box, and his enemy took him and raised him — and before that, seventy thousand male newborn had been killed on the day Mūsā came, and the strength of those boys whom Pharaoh was commanded to slaughter came to Mūsā, and that was his specific property (peace be upon him); and there remains who said [it was] by revolution and chain from some opponents of the sects of Islam, and this state was specific to him. And in some reports: when the servant intends a good deed, Allah the Exalted says 'And return to your Lord and submit to Him'; and when he intends a calamity, Allah the Exalted says 'So do you take him and his progeny as allies apart from Me?' — the verse. And upon you is the entrusting of all the affairs to Allah the Exalted, for if you fear Him in your inward, He preserves for you the movements of your outward, and secures you where you fear the creatures. And look at Maryam (peace be upon her) — when she was sincere to Allah the Exalted in what was in her womb, she said 'My Lord, I have delivered her female, and Allah knows best what she delivered' — how Allah the Exalted gave her this special property, and 'Īsā her son came, the Seal of the prophets, at his descent at the end of time upon the eastern minaret of Damascus — he breaks the cross and kills the swine and judges by the law of Muḥammad (may Allah bless him and grant him peace) and kills the Antichrist and rules the world; and we are not about this — so let us return to the special properties of this name, as came in some reports that it is the Greatest Name of Allah, and the witness in that is that the Prophet (may Allah bless him and grant him peace) was passing on a road when he saw a bedouin saying: 'O Allah, I ask You by Your great, greatest name, the Most Gentle, the Most Generous, MĀLIK AL-MULK, DHŪ AL-JALĀL WA-L-IKRĀM' — so the Prophet (may Allah bless him and grant him peace) said: 'He called by the Greatest Name of Allah, which if called by it He answers, and if asked by it He gives.' And he who draws near to Allah the Exalted by this name is obligated the watchfulness for Allah the Exalted and the recitation of it its number — for the servant KARHABĀ'ĪL and ṬAFYĀ'ĪL and MARJĀ'ĪL (peace be upon them) come to the one remembering, under the hand of each of them seventy rows, and they are from the servants of the greatness, and they pour upon the wayfarer the bounty and the bounties, and unveil to him the secrets of the creatures, and he attains the power in the worlds. And for this name there is a magnificent square — 25 in its like — and it has great special properties for who reflects on them, among which is the binding of iron: you write this square and write around it Sūrat al-Ḥadīd at the dignity of the Sun and conjure it by the invocation of the comprehensive letters and Sūrat al-Mulk, so its bearer — no weapon works in him, nothing of iron, by the power of Allah the Exalted. And the binding of the tongues: you write this arrangement with the name of the object and conjure it by Sūrat Yā-Sīn and carry it for three matters: for rulers and the efficacy of the word. Indonesia:
— 496 —

"…manusia dan jin, dan ia pertengahan keadaan, dan tenggelam serta fana ialah hujung keadaan — maka barangsiapa di awal keadaan, sifat keagungan nampak padanya; dan barangsiapa pertengahan dalam keadaan, kelapangan nampak padanya; dan barangsiapa di hujung keadaan, keadaan penetapan nampak padanya, zahir dan batin. Dan diriwayatkan dari Ibn al-Jalāl bahwa ia berkata: 'Aku menunggang unta, dan kaki unta tenggelam dalam pasir, maka aku berkata "Keagungan-Nya (jalāluhu)!" dan unta menjawab: "Maha Suci Allah (jalla Allāh)!"' Maka unta mempunyai kuasa kesiapsiagaan pada dua aspek: pertama, bahwa unta itu menuju Allah Yang Maha Tinggi — dan saksi dalam itu firman-Nya s.a.w.: "Kalaulah kamu di gunung, nescaya kamu hidup di atas Allah." Aspek kedua: bahwa unta, bila prinsip keadaan yang datang atas keagungan tidak menguasainya, unta tidak dilepaskan supaya ia dapat menanggung keadaan yang datang atasnya serta ilmu dari keagungan Allah, maka Allah Yang Maha Tinggi menjadikan berbicara dari hakikat keadaan atas lidah unta — walaupun ia haiwan bersemangat yang bertutur dari hakikat keadaan. Dan yang mengetahui kemurahan-Nya Yang Maha Tinggi — ia serahkan kepada-Nya hati dan zatnya, serta bersandar pada urusan-Nya baginya dengan kemurahan-Nya, maka Ia selamatkan dia dari musuh zahir dan batin. Tidakkah engkau lihat bahwa ibu Mūsā menyerahkan hatinya dan Allah Yang Maha Tinggi memegangnya, serta menyelamatkan anaknya dari binasa setelah ia letakkan dalam peti, lalu musuhnya mengambil dan memeliharanya — dan sebelum itu, tujuh puluh ribu bayi lelaki dibunuh pada hari Mūsā datang, dan kekuatan budak lelaki yang Firaun diperintahkan menyembelih mereka datang kepada Mūsā, dan itu sifat khususnya a.s.; dan tinggal yang mengatakan [ia] dengan peredaran dan rantaian dari sebahagian penentang golongan Islam, dan keadaan ini khusus baginya. Dan dalam sebahagian khabar: bila hamba mahu berbuat baik, Allah Yang Maha Tinggi berfirman 'Dan kembalilah kepada Tuhanmu serta berserah padanya'; dan bila ia mahu musibah, Allah Yang Maha Tinggi berfirman 'Maka adakah kamu jadikan dia dan zuriatnya penolong selain-Ku?' — ayat. Dan atasmu menyerahkan segala urusan kepada Allah Yang Maha Tinggi, kerana bila engkau takut kepada-Nya dalam batinmu, Ia pelihara gerakan zahirmu, dan mengamankan engkau di mana engkau takut makhluk. Dan pandanglah Maryam a.s. — bila ia ikhlas kepada Allah Yang Maha Tinggi dalam kandungannya, ia berkata 'Wahai Tuhanku, aku melahirkannya perempuan, dan Allah lebih mengetahui apa yang dilahirkannya' — bagaimana Allah Yang Maha Tinggi beri dia sifat khusus ini, dan 'Īsā anaknya datang, Penutup nabi, pada turunnya di akhir zaman di menara timur Damsyik — ia memecahkan salib dan membunuh babi serta menghukum dengan syariat Muḥammad s.a.w. dan membunuh Dajjal serta memerintah dunia; dan kami tidak tentang ini — maka mari kembali pada sifat nama ini, sebagaimana datang dalam sebahagian riwayat bahwa ia Ism Allāh al-A'ẓam, dan saksi dalam itu bahwa Nabi s.a.w. sedang lalu di jalan bila melihat seorang Badwi berkata: 'Ya Allah, aku memohon dengan nama-Mu yang agung, teragung, Yang Amat Lembut, Yang Amat Pemurah, MĀLIK AL-MULK, DHŪ AL-JALĀL WA-L-IKRĀM' — maka Nabi s.a.w. berkata: 'Ia berdoa dengan Ism Allāh al-A'ẓam, yang bila dipanggil dengannya Ia menjawab, dan bila diminta dengannya Ia memberi.' Dan yang mendekat kepada Allah Yang Maha Tinggi dengan nama ini diwajibkan muraqabah kepada Allah Yang Maha Tinggi serta baca bilangannya — kerana khadam KARHABĀ'ĪL dan ṬAFYĀ'ĪL dan MARJĀ'ĪL a.s. datang kepada yang berdzikir, di bawah tangan masing-masing 70 baris, dan mereka dari khadam keagungan, serta mencurah atas musafir kurnia dan nikmat, dan membuka kepadanya rahsia makhluk, dan ia capai kuasa di alam. Dan bagi nama ini persegi agung — 25 dalam seumpamanya — dan ia mempunyai sifat besar bagi yang merenunginya, antaranya ikatan besi: engkau tulis persegi ini serta tulis di kelilingnya Surah al-Ḥadīd pada darjat Matahari dan dijampi dengan seruan huruf menyeluruh dan Surah al-Mulk, maka pembawanya — tiada senjata berkesan padanya, tiada sesuatu besi, dengan kuasa Allah Yang Maha Tinggi. Dan ikatan lidah: engkau tulis susunan ini dengan nama objek serta dijampi dengan Surah Yā-Sīn dan dipakai untuk tiga perkara: bagi pemerintah dan berkesannya kata. [halaman berakhir]
