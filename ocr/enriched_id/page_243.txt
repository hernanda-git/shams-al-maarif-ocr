English:
— 237 —

…the luminous letters have what corresponds to them of the dark letters. And as for the luminous letters, their gathering is your saying "Ṭaraqa sam'uka al-naṣīḥa", and also in this form from "Quṭi'aka ṣilatu siḥr". * Know, O seeker: when you take "basṭ" letters from the dark letters and mix them with the letters of the person's name at the lip of a plant and the moon in its waning, and bury it in a forgotten grave, then cares and sorrows seize upon his heart without cause. Exalted be Allah, exalted. Some of the excellent said: when you want a need fulfilled by anyone of the existent things, write the whole number of his name and his mother's name and the object's name; when the total gathers the number, draw it in an auspicious hour and keep it with you, and rise with it in seeking your need, and it is fulfilled by the permission of Allah, exalted. * Know, O seeker: when you want to bind a person, look at the letters of his name and his mother's name and the letters of his ascendant; remove the letters of the ascendant and the two names and cast them into his food or drink, and speak over it the words, and it is "basṭ", and that high nature seizes him and you dispose in it as you wish — and this is a profound secret from the secrets of Allah, exalted. * Said the shaykh, the excellent Imām, the complete scholar, the verifier, the scrutinizer, the unique Pole who gathers, the sign of his age and the solitary of his era, the Imām Ja'far al-Ṣādiq, may Allah be pleased with him: when you want a work you do, take the name of the seeker and the name of the object, and add to them from the numbers { RK } — and this is the number of the overcomer — and { A } and the name of gain, and the manner of entering into all works: calculate the name of the seeker by the great abjad and the name of the object, and look — if the overcomer is upon it after that, its example — you set the seeker's name Aḥmad and the object's name Muḥammad — you calculate by the ways of working thus: Aḥmad 53, Muḥammad 92, so add the numbers { RK } and the seeker's name became 273 and the object's 276; their speech mingled and became 249, then you drop from this 30, there remained of it 619, and we divided it into four parts so each part of it became 154, and there remained three broken, and the excess that increased from the calculation you descend by it — thus the goal is attained, if Allah, exalted, wills. * A benefit also: when three fractions meet, descend the house of five, one; and when one remained, descend the house of thirteen, one; the goal is attained for you, and Allah knows best. His saying, exalted: "And Allah brings out what you were concealing" — so we followed, strike with it some of them — "Thus Allah gives life to the dead and shows you His signs, that perhaps you understand" — by this verse the sleeper is made to speak, informing of what is in his breast; and this is the form of its placing as follows: so when you want that, write it on a jaw-bone, place it on the sleeper's chest and ask him what you want, and he informs you by the permission of Allah, exalted, and it is particular to the chapter of spiritual insights. His saying, glory be to Him, exalted: "And thus took your Lord when He took the towns while they were wrongdoing — indeed His seizure is painful, severe" — 169 — what you cast in the house of the other wrongdoer is written on the bone of an owl, properly slaughtered, hunted by weapon and raptors, and this is the form of its placing as follows: [the 4×4 magic square shown above].

Indonesia:
— 237 —

…huruf cahaya mempunyai yang membalasnya dari huruf gelap. Dan adapun huruf cahaya, pengumpulnya ucapanmu: "Ṭaraqa sam'uka al-naṣīḥa", dan juga dalam rupa ini dari "Quṭi'aka ṣilatu siḥr". * Ketahuilah wahai pencari: apabila engkau mengambil huruf "basṭ" dari huruf gelap dan mencampurnya dengan huruf nama orang pada bibir tanaman dan bulan pada waktu iktidal (mahāq), lalu menguburnya di kubur terlupakan, maka kecemasan dan duka menguasai hatinya tanpa sebab. Mahasuci Allah Ta'ala. Sebagian orang utama berkata: apabila engkau menghendaki hajat dipenuhi oleh siapa pun dari yang wujud, tulislah seluruh bilangan namanya, nama ibunya, dan nama yang diminta; apabila dari jumlah itu terkumpul bilangan, gambarlah pada jam baik dan simpan padamu, serta bangkitlah membawanya mencari hajatmu maka dikabulkan dengan izin Allah Ta'ala. * Ketahuilah wahai pencari: apabila engkau ingin mengikat seseorang, lihatlah huruf namanya, nama ibunya, dan huruf ṭāli'-nya; keluarkan huruf ṭāli' dan dua nama lalu lemparkan ke makanan atau minumannya, dan ucapkanlah atasnya kalimat yang merupakan "basṭ", maka sifat tinggi itu menangkapnya dan engkau bertasaruf di dalamnya sekehendakmu — dan ini rahasia mendalam dari rahasia Allah Ta'ala. * Berkata syaikh, imam utama, ulama sempurna, muḥaqqiq, muddaqqiq, Qutb al-Fard al-Jāmi', tokoh zamannya dan unik eranya, Imām Ja'far al-Ṣādiq raḍiyallāhu 'anhu: apabila engkau menghendaki suatu amal, ambillah nama pencari dan nama objek, dan tambahkan pada keduanya dari bilangan { RK } — ini bilangan yang mengalahkan — dan { A } serta nama perolehan, dan cara masuk ke segala amal: hitunglah nama pencari dengan abjad besar dan nama objek, lalu perhatikan apabila yang mengalahkan padanya setelah itu, contohnya — engkau tetapkan nama pencari Aḥmad dan nama objek Muḥammad — hitung dengan cara kerja demikian: Aḥmad 53, Muḥammad 92, maka tambahkan bilangan { RK } dan nama pencari menjadi 273 serta nama objek 276; bercampurlah ucapan keduanya menjadi 249, lalu engkau gugurkan darinya 30, tersisa 619, dan kami bagi empat bahagian maka tiap bahagian menjadi 154, serta tersisa tiga pecahan, dan kelebihan yang bertambah dari hitungan engkau turunkan dengannya — demikian tercapai maksud insya Allah Ta'ala. * Manfaat juga: apabila bertemu tiga pecahan, turunkan rumah lima satu; dan apabila tersisa satu, turunkan rumah tiga belas satu; tercapai maksudmu, dan Allah lebih tahu. Firman-Nya Ta'ala: "Dan Allah mengeluarkan apa yang kamu sembunyikan" — maka kami ikuti, pukullah sebagiannya — "Demikian Allah menghidupkan yang mati dan memperlihatkan kepadamu tanda-tanda-Nya agar kamu mengerti" — dengan ayat ini orang tidur dibuat berbicara, memberitahu apa yang di dalam dadanya; dan ini rupa peletakkannya sebagai berikut: maka apabila engkau menghendaki itu, tulislah pada rahang, letakkan di dada orang tidur dan tanyalah apa yang engkau kehendaki, maka ia memberitahumu dengan izin Allah Ta'ala, dan ia khusus pada bab bashā'ir. Firman-Nya Mahasuci lagi Mahatinggi: "Dan demikianlah Rabbmu mengambil ketika Dia mengambil negeri-negeri sedang mereka zalim — sesungguhnya azab-Nya itu pedih lagi keras" — 169 — apa yang engkau lemparkan di rumah orang zalim yang lain, ditulis pada tulang burung hantu yang disembelih semestinya, diburu dengan senjata dan burung pemangsa, dan ini rupa peletakkannya berikut: [kuadrat ajaib 4×4 di atas].
