"The answer before the decree descends… Q, and the Glorious Qur'an, Q — 'liyo with the little, without quluṭ, by the answer — answer and rely upon such-and-such,' by the command of the Subduer, the Able, the vanquished by subdual; and tremble, O Qāf — cease from stillness and dwell in standing until my need and my business are fulfilled. Ā. Qaf wa-saqwah, Harshq, Shafāq — hayā the kingdom and the Dominion, and by the breath of Israfil and the grasp of 'Azra'il and the cry of Gabriel and the seizing of the spirits for an abiding command, until my need is fulfilled by the might of Allah — that Allah decree a matter already done, glory be to Him by what He wills; and you are established by the light of Allah — but for what you said 'Qaf', a leaning, until you see from them power in strength — Allah, the Living, the Self-Subsisting, the Strong — answer. And peace be upon you and the mercy of Allah and His blessings. And its incense is the husk of mahlab; and its implicit concentration: 'Answer, by the right of 'Atalak, Mahfaṭ, Ṭahaj, Yāhīmūh, Qahr, Yūh — answer and do such-and-such.' (The letter Rā') — it is of the fifth degree, and among its properties, for directing headache: write the number of its degrees on the hide of a mule or a stallion with the name of whom you will and his mother, and place it under the pounding rod and recite the implicit concentration — and the one for whom it is made gets the headache, while you face the qibla and recite his name al-Raḥīm and carry it — and Allah eases provision. And if you write it with the moon in it on a piece of lead and carry it, you witness a great secret. And know that this letter is beneficial for the growth of trees and their turning at the moment after employment; and if you write it and place it in the stone into which water is poured for watering trees — they grow and beautify. And the manner of employment is that you enter the retreat with its conditions and recite the invocation — he attends, his breast split. And if you employ it and see the epileptic and point to him — he recovers, praising, for a little while — dispose him as you will. And this is its figure. And its invocation: the basmala — 'My Lord, I ask You for a spiritual support by which my partial and total powers are strengthened, until I subdue the soul of every tyrant in the universals and particulars, until my soul becomes soul-ish [~] Q. Ḍ — to it its subtleties, fallen rubble, by which my powers fall, until there remains in the universe no possessor of spirit save… and the fire I extinguished by the appearance of them like Your powers. O Mighty, subordinate to me the servant of the letter Rā' and His special trait, that I fulfill by it my business and my intent and my religion's affair. O Allah, O Strong, O Possessor of power and severe assault, O Guide, O Light, O Living, O Self-Subsisting — YHWH 2, Ahiyā, Sharāḥiyā, Adunay, Aṣbā'ūt, Āl Shaddai, YHWH 2, Bāh 2, Huwa, Hiya Huwa 2, my direction and my majesty, Shāshā, hayā bayh, yāy, yāh — the high God, glory be to Him, hayā yā Rā' by the answer — by the F, there is no power nor strength save in Allah, the High, the Great. And its implicit concentration: 'Answer, O master Hariyā'īl, by the right of Ṣaṭīq, Ḥamīd, Ḥayyūm, Qayyūm, Rawf 2, Halīkh, Yamūh 2, Yāh 2, Lūḥā — the hastening.' (The letter Shīn) — for reconciliation between discordant ones: write with the name of the sought and the implicit concentration in an auspicious hour, and carry it — he attains what he wills. And for judgment: write it reversed with the implicit concentration on lead and bury it in the place. And if you write it with the names beginning with Sh and a person carries it — Allah grants him awe and dignity. And it has a retreat and discipline of 28 and recitation of the invocation and implicit concentration until he attends, and his name is Ḥardiyā'īl — so dispose him. And this is its figure — the page to come. And its invocation…"

"Jawapan sebelum qada turun… Q, dan al-Qur'an yang Mulia, Q — 'liyo sedikit, tanpa quluṭ, dengan jawapan — jawab dan bertawakal anu,' demi perintah Yang Mengalahkan, Yang Berkuasa, yang dikalahkan dengan kekalahan; dan bergoncanglah wahai Qāf — berhentilah dari diam dan tetap dalam berdiri hingga hajatku dan urusanku selesai. Ā. Qaf wa-saqwah, Harshq, Shafāq — hayā kerajaan dan Dominion, dan demi nafas Israfil dan genggaman 'Azra'il dan teriak Jibril dan tawanan roh demi perintah kekal, hingga hajatku dipenuhi dengan keagungan Allah — Allah menetapkan qada yang telah dilakukan, Maha Suci Dia demi kehendak-Nya; dan engkau teguh dengan cahaya Allah — namun kerana engkau kata 'Qaf', condongan, hingga engkau lihat dari mereka kekuatan — Allah Yang Hidup Berdiri Sendiri Kuat — jawab. Salam atasmu dan rahmat serta berkat-Nya. Ubatan kulit mahlab; idmár: 'Jawab demi 'Atalak, Mahfaṭ, Ṭahaj, Yāhīmūh, Qahr, Yūh — jawab dan buat anu.' (Huruf Rā') — darjat kelima, sifat arah sakit kepala: tulis bilangan darjatnya pada kulit keldai atau kuda dengan nama yang dikehendaki dan ibunya, letak di bawah alu, baca idmár — orang yang dibuatnya dapat sakit kepala, sementara engkau menghadap kiblat baca nama al-Raḥīm dan bawa — Allah mudahkan rezeki. Jika tulis bulan padanya pada timah bawa — nampak rahsia besar. Huruf ini baik untuk tumbuh pokok; tulis di batu tempat air disiram — pokok membesar cantik. Cara: masuk khalwat syaratnya baca doa — ia hadir dada terbelah. Jika guna dan lihat sawan tunjuk padanya — sedar memuji. Inilah rupanya. Doanya basmalah — 'Tuhanku, kuminta bantuan rohani menguatkan kekuatanku sehingga aku tundukkan jiwa setiap zalim, hingga jiwaku jadi jiwa [~] Q. Ḍ — kepadanya halusnya runtuhan, dengannya kekuatanku gugur, hingga tiada berjiwa di alam melainkan… dan api kupadamkan dengan kemunculan mereka seperti kekuasaan-Mu. Wahai Kuat, taklukkan khadam Rā' dan sifatnya agar aku selesaikan urusan agamaku. Wahai Allah Yang Kuat yang Menguasai, wahai Pembimbing Cahaya Hidup Qayyum — YHWH 2, Ahiyā, Sharāḥiyā, Adunay, Aṣbā'ūt, Āl Shaddai, YHWH 2, Bāh 2, Huwa, Hiya Huwa 2, arah keagunganku Shāshā, hayā bayh, yāy, yāh — Tuhan tinggi, hayā wahai Rā' — demi F tiada daya melainkan Allah. Idmár: 'Jawab wahai tuan Hariyā'īl, demi Ṣaṭīq, Ḥamīd, Ḥayyūm, Qayyūm, Rawf 2, Halīkh, Yamūh 2, Yāh 2, Lūḥā — segera.' (Huruf Shīn) — perdamaian: tulis nama yang dikehendaki dan idmár di saat baik, bawa — tercapai. Untuk hukum: tulis songsang idmár pada timah tanam di tempat. Jika tulis nama bermula Sh dan bawa — Allah beri kekaguman. Khalwat riyadhah 28, baca doa idmár hingga hadir, namanya Ḥardiyā'īl. Inilah rupanya — halaman akan datang. Dan doanya…"
