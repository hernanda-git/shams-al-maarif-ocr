English:
— 490 —

"…and al-Bāṭin at times; and al-Ẓāhir without al-Bāṭin is what He enslaved you with by al-Ẓāhir and al-Bāṭin. He said exalted: 'And they were not commanded but to worship Allah, sincere to Him in religion.' And the worship upon the manifest of the raised is manifest from where the bodily act is, hidden from where the sincere heart is. And as for the worship of al-Bāṭin without al-Ẓāhir, He said exalted: 'And in yourselves — do you not then see?' and He said exalted: 'Do they not reflect in themselves — that Allah created not the heavens and the earth and what is between them but by the truth.' And as for the worship of Allah al-Ẓāhir without al-Bāṭin, He said exalted: 'Do they not look at the camels, how they were created?' And when Allah the Exalted created the people of al-Bāṭin by the devotions, He gathered for them the outward aspects in the nearings; and this vision is not the devotions — He gathered for them the secrets of the nearings by the inward aspects; and Allah the Exalted will not gather the secrets of the inward and the outward of the divinity for the people of deliverance — and that is His saying exalted: 'Alif-Lām-Mīm. That is the Book, no doubt in it…' to His saying '…the successful.' And know that this group whom Allah described in His Book — their faith in the unseen is in the people of sincerity, the first by the first thicket, and that unseen is the subtlest of the worlds of the Dominion, and from it the eschatological causes placed, its return upon the tongues of the messengers (peace be upon them) in their faith in the unseen — upon the famous, the secret of the subtleness from the unseen; for a thing is not perceived but by what is subtler than it, and is not perceived by what is beneath it — and if it is perceived by what is from it, it is not perceived but by the measure. And Allah the Exalted created the intellects and made them specializations of the subtletics of the realities of the worlds of His secrets, then returned them by that specific luminous secret, then after that addressed them, and they had two powers: the power of the first hearing and the eschatological power for the answering by the imitation of the justice and His preceding power and His subsequent bounty. And he who draws near to Allah the Exalted by these two names — that you populate the outward with piety and humility and the perseverance of silence and fasting in the retreat and the recitation of the two names and Sūrat al-Ikhlāṣ, and you observe the fancies in the higher and lower, and do not depart from his place from the thought, and recite the four names: al-Awwal, al-Ākhir, al-Ẓāhir, al-Bāṭin — until you see the lightnings of the world have descended; and the names of the servants: al-Awwal is 'UHTĪYĀ'ĪL (peace be upon him), and he unveils to you the unseen and their attachment in the worlds, and you see him as he addresses you in the best according to your preparedness. So when you recite these names in a retreat with the spiritual practice of this high rank, and if you want the unveiling of an obscure matter, write the two noble times and around them the names of the worlds and the standing mention, and recite the name its number, then seek the knowledge of that, and if… [page ends]

Indonesia:
— 490 —

"…dan al-Bāṭin pada satu masa; dan al-Ẓāhir tanpa al-Bāṭin ialah apa yang Ia perhambakan engkau dengannya melalui al-Ẓāhir dan al-Bāṭin. Firman-Nya: 'Dan mereka tidak diperintah melainkan untuk menyembah Allah, ikhlas kepada-Nya dalam agama.' Dan ibadah atas yang zahir dari yang diangkat ialah zahir dari mana perbuatan jasad, tersembunyi dari mana hati ikhlas. Dan adapun ibadah al-Bāṭin tanpa al-Ẓāhir, firman-Nya: 'Dan pada diri kamu — maka tidakkah kamu melihat?' serta firman-Nya: 'Tidakkah mereka memikirkan pada diri mereka — bahwa Allah tidak menciptakan langit dan bumi serta apa antara keduanya melainkan dengan kebenaran.' Dan adapun ibadah Allah al-Ẓāhir tanpa al-Bāṭin, firman-Nya: 'Tidakkah mereka memandang unta, bagaimana diciptakan?' Dan bila Allah Yang Maha Tinggi menciptakan ahli al-Bāṭin dengan ibadah, Ia himpunkan bagi mereka zahir dalam pendekatan; dan pandangan ini bukan ibadah — Ia himpunkan bagi mereka rahsia pendekatan dengan batin; dan Allah Yang Maha Tinggi tidak akan himpunkan rahsia batin dan zahir ketuhanan bagi ahli pelepasan — dan itu firman-Nya: 'Alif-Lām-Mīm. Itulah Kitab, tiada syak padanya…' hingga firman-Nya '…orang yang berjaya.' Dan ketahuilah bahwa golongan ini yang Allah sifatkan dalam Kitab-Nya — iman mereka pada ghaib ialah pada ahli ikhlas, yang pertama dengan semak pertama, dan ghaib itu halus alam Kerajaan, dan daripadanya sebab akhirat diletakkan, kembalinya atas lidah rasul (s.a.) dalam iman mereka pada ghaib — pada yang masyhur, rahsia halus dari ghaib; kerana sesuatu tidak difahami melainkan dengan apa yang lebih halus darinya, dan tidak difahami dengan apa yang di bawahnya — dan bila difahami dengan apa daripadanya, ia tidak difahami melainkan dengan ukuran. Dan Allah Yang Maha Tinggi menciptakan akal serta menjadikannya kekhususan halus hakikat alam rahsia-Nya, lalu mengembalikannya dengan rahsia cahaya khusus itu, lalu setelahnya berkalam dengannya, dan ia mempunyai dua kuasa: kuasa pendengaran pertama dan kuasa akhirat bagi jawapan dengan meniru keadilan serta kuasa terdahulu-Nya dan kurnia kemudian-Nya. Dan yang mendekat kepada Allah Yang Maha Tinggi dengan dua nama ini — engkau makmurkan zahir dengan takwa dan khusyuk serta kekal diam dan puasa dalam khalwat serta baca dua nama dan Surah al-Ikhlāṣ, dan engkau jaga khayalan di tinggi dan rendah, dan jangan berpindah dari tempatnya dari fikiran, serta bacalah empat nama: al-Awwal, al-Ākhir, al-Ẓāhir, al-Bāṭin — hingga engkau lihat kilat alam telah turun; dan nama khadam: al-Awwal ialah 'UHTĪYĀ'ĪL a.s., dan ia membuka kepadamu ghaib serta hubungannya di alam, dan engkau lihat ia sebagaimana ia berkalam kepadamu dengan terbaik menurut kesiapsiagaanmu. Maka bila engkau baca nama ini dalam khalwat dengan riadhah maqam tinggi ini, dan bila engkau mahu kasyaf perkara samar, tulis dua waktu mulia serta di kelilingnya nama alam dan dzikir berdiri, dan bacalah nama bilangannya, lalu minta ilmu tentang itu, dan bila… [halaman berakhir]
