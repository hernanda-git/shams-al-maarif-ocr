English:
— 433 —

"…a mention of Quddūs * and as for the people of nobility, their mention is * 'Subbūḥ Quddūs'; and as for the companions of the Tablet, their mention is 'Quddūs Subbūḥ, Lord of the angels and the spirit' — and the meaning of His name al-Quddūs is the exaltation in the subtleties of the highest Tremendousness, whose lights transcended the apprehensions. * And of the properties of this name: if its number agrees with a name, or one adds to it 'Subbūḥ' and persists in it — it unveils to him the upper worlds; and whoever says 'Subbūḥ Quddūs, Lord of the angels and the spirit' and persists in its mention — it unveils to him the worlds of the Dominion and the Tremendousness, and it is the mention of the bearers of the Throne after the 'hawqalah' * — it is recited once and the 'hawqalah' once, the mention of the Cherubim and the chiefs altogether. (Notice) Know that the Spirit of Holiness is at the Lote-tree of the Boundary, and it manifests to the faithful creations in the manifest hearts, and it is the inspiration-revelation, the utterance of the ones brought-near, and it is the discourse Allah casts upon the hearts by the mediation of the Spirit of Holiness, and it is upon 5 degrees — the degree of the secret, the intellect, the spirit, the soul, and the heart — and that is that the human world is sanctified in the original placing, transcendent above other-than-monotheism; so Allah the Exalted manifested His secret in the eye of nearness, and manifested His intellect by the lights of witnessing, and manifested His spirit by the lights of intermingling, and manifested His soul by the realities of Paradise, and manifested His heart by the light of faith — for the secrets are the subtleties of faith. And purity is three kinds: purity from colors by the clarity of the moment; the second, purity from reflection; the third, watching the abandoned from the secret to receive that according to the manifestation; and complete purity is the original sanctification, and he is submerged in the seas of greatness and the lights of the Eternal — and that is the rank of the truthful, the prophets, the saints, and the brought-near. And as for the sanctification of the intellects — it is upon three kinds: the first, the sanctification of the intellect from slips and looking to the eye of His wisdom; the second, steadfastness upon the first address by the continuance of witnessing and the demanding of the times in that, by Allah's success; the third, the ceasing from the first addressing in the witnessing of the first addressing in every passing thought, and the standing in the dissolution in the roots of pre-eternity — and it is the station of the righteous. And as for the sanctification of the spirits — it is upon three kinds: the first, steadfastness upon its witnessing in the world of the breath and how it was realized by the realities of the Tablet and the Pen, which are the principles of the highest spirits, free of defilements, and their acceptance until the intellect receives by the intellect. And as for the sanctification of the souls — it is upon three kinds: the first, their steadfastness upon the seven first [states] and their acceptance of the secret by what He measured for them, and that by the going of the determined desires and the cutting of the familiar habits by disciplines; the second, their witnessing the forms of the worlds which Allah deposited in the Preserved Tablet, for they are to the spirit of the human world by what Allah deposited in them of the secret of movements, and that by perusing the lordly sciences and the occupations upon the visitations of the symbols of the people of knowledge and realization and reflection in what the people of states disclose; the third, their transformation from the indication to the first realization, then to the blaming [soul], then to the tranquil — and it is the third — and that is that he cuts the progeny of the lower, problematic world from its essences, its deeds, and its attributes. And as for the sanctification of the hearts — it is upon three kinds: the sanctification of faith from the darkness of associationism, and the sanctification of deeds from ostentation; the second, the command and prohibition by sincerity — so the sanctification of faith is the watching of the breaths in the presence of the Truth, and that by the passing of support; then the sanctification of deeds, and he makes the Truth his qiblah and does not turn to any direction but looks to all the realities; the third, the standing in service in every soul and the absence of leadership. And every heart in which is the weight of a speck of love of leadership — Allah forbade him the drinking of the sweetness of faith, for he calls by what… (28 — Sun of Knowledges — Fourth)"

"…zikir Quddūs * ahli kemuliaan zikir mereka 'Subbūḥ Quddūs'; sahabat Loh zikir 'Quddūs Subbūḥ Tuhan malaikat roh' — makna al-Quddūs keagungan dalam lemah lembut jabarut tertinggi cahayanya lampaui tanggapan. * Sifat nama: bilangan sepakat nama atau tambah 'Subbūḥ' istiqamah — buka alam atas; sesiapa kata 'Subbūḥ Quddūs Tuhan malaikat roh' istiqamah — buka alam Malakut jabarut, ia zikir pemikul 'Arasy selepas hawqalah * — dibaca sekali hawqalah sekali, zikir Kerubim ketua. (Tunjuk) Roh Kudus di Sidratul Muntaha zahir pada makhluk beriman hati nyata, wahyu ilham kata orang rapat, firman Allah lempar ke hati dengan Roh Kudus, atas 5 darjat — sirr akal roh jiwa hati — alam manusia disucikan asal tersuci dari selain tauhid; Allah zahirkan sirr di mata dekat, akal cahaya syuhud, roh cahaya bercampur, jiwa hakikat syurga, hati cahaya iman — rahsia lemah lembut iman. Suci 3 jenis: dari warna kejernihan masa; kedua dari fikir; ketiga awasi yang ditinggalkan sirr terima mengikut zahir; suci sempurna penyucian asal tenggelam laut keagungan cahaya azali — darjat siddiqin nabi wali muqarrabin. Penyucian akal 3: pertama akal dari tersilap lihat hikmah; kedua teguh alamat pertama syuhud tuntut masa dengan taufiq; ketiga berhenti dari kata pertama dalam syuhud setiap khayal berdiri hancur akar qidam — maqam abrar. Penyucian roh 3: pertama teguh syuhud alam nafas bagaimana hakikat Loh Penanya prinsip roh tertinggi suci terima hingga akal terima akal. Penyucian jiwa 3: pertama teguh 7 pertama terima sirr dengan takwa hilang nafsu biasa riyadhah; kedua syuhud bentuk alam dalam Loh Mahfuz untuk roh alam manusia dengan rahsia gerakan, dengan baca sains rabbani lambang ahli ma'rifat; ketiga bertukar dari isyarat ke hakikat pertama ke lawwamah ke mutma'innah — ketiga, potong zuriat alam bawah dari zat perbuatan sifat. Penyucian hati 3: iman dari gelap syirik, amal dari riya; kedua suruh tegah ikhlas — suci iman awasi nafas di hadirat Haq bila sokongan pergi; suci amal jadikan Haq kiblat tak pandang arah lihat hakikat; ketiga berdiri khidmat tiap jiwa tiada kepimpinan. Tiap hati ada debu cinta kepimpinan — Allah haram minum manis iman, kerana dia seru dengan apa… (28 — Shams al-Ma'arif — Keempat)"
