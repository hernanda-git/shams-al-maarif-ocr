Then bring it down from the fire and set it on the ground until it cools, and open it while you turn your face away — for at the moment of opening there comes out of it a vapor like black smoke, and if it enter a human eye it blinds on the spot and has no cure. Turunkan dari api letak tanah hingga sejuk, buka sambil palingkan muka — bila dibuka keluar wap macam asap hitam, masuk mata buta serta-merta tiada ubat.
