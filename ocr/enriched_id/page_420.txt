[Conclusion of the Thā' invocation] "…Ṭā Bā — and the sun and the moon by calculation; that is the estimation of the Mighty, the Knowing." And the implicit concentration: "The implicit concentration, O servant Ḥamā'īl, by the right of Liyā Kīd, Yaldūs, Ṭa'at, Umma — His command al-ḥā; and enter the retreat and seek him — he fulfills your need." (The letter Ḥā') — it is watery, cold, dry; and if you write on a moist sherd with the implicit concentration reversed and cast it in running water and bury it in a place where people gather for sins — they separate. And if you write on a lead tablet and bury it in a place where selling has ceased. And if you write it on your fingers and recite the implicit concentration over it and say "So-and-so, fear, and open your palm" — he fears you. And this is its description. And its invocation: the basmala — "Allah delivered me from every lowly care of the world, and took my forelock to the good things — O Hidden, You are the Hidden, O Knower of the hidden affair, and He is knowing of it; I ask You by the good in the consciences, grant me happiness and guide me in my affair, O Aware — I ask You to clothe me in light by which I witness the secret of the Ḥā' until I fulfill my need, O Aware, hayā 3 — hasten, hasten, O Ḥā', by the seal, al-Ḥāmūnī, Khayūm — I ask You to supply me with the servant of the letter Ḥā' and protect me from Your creation, O He who knows the secret and the hidden — there is no god but He, His are the Beautiful Names, and by the F, there is no power nor strength save in Allah, the High, the Great. And the implicit concentration: 'Answer, by the right of Huwa Ṭiyāl, 'Iyūṭ, alā wa-Kas, wa-Kas, Khakhaj 2, Khafaj, Yāhīyūh — al-waḥā, the hastening, the hour' — and it has effect for what you will." (The letter Dhāl) — it is sweet to whom tastes it and sweet to whom you intend; so if you will agitation, write it on white silk rag with the name of the sought and his mother and the moon in the letter, then light it in a new lamp with the name of the sought and recite the implicit concentration — and he attends. And if you will to imagine a person's mind, write the letters and implicit concentration on the image of whom you will and cast it in his house — and it corrupts his mind. And for extinguishing anger and repelling thirst and lessening fatigue — it is written and carried. And if you will to employ it, enter the retreat and recite the invocation after every prayer 100 times — the servant attends; take the pact from him and dispose him as you will, and whatever you will is done by this letter. And this is its figure. And this invocation: "In the name of Allah, the Merciful, the Compassionate — Laḏḏan, O Allah, I ask You, O Lord — I have humbled myself before You, the humbling of the needy servant who is bound by needs to You, and I took delight in Your names, delighting in them now in my secret and open. O Allah, subordinate to me the servant of this letter by the right of these names — Huwa, Hiya, Ahiyāh, Yamūh — and there is no power nor strength save in Allah, the High, the Great." (The letter Ḍād) — it is a manifest, dry letter; whoever writes it on a silk rag with the implicit concentration and carries it becomes awe-inspiring and accepted in speech. And if you write its number with fat and execute and bury under the threshold of whom you will — lice and fleas and bugs and frogs gather upon him from every side; and if you will to burn a place, you command him afterward — and he does it. And if you recite this invocation over one afflicted — the helper is burned. And this is the description of the seal. (The letter Ẓā') — it disposes like the Ṭā'; and if you write on an oleander rod with fat … Qāf-Dhāl… and bury it in a place where harmful vermin gathered; and if you write and hang on children — they are safe from afflictions. And if you write on a lead tablet with the implicit concentration reversed and place it in a house — its people separate. And it has a retreat and recitation of the invocation 300 times — and when the servant attends, take from him…"

Indonesia:

[Pengakhiran doa Thā'] "…Ṭā Bā — matahari bulan mengikut kiraan; itulah takdir Yang Maha Perkasa Maha Mengetahui." Idmár: "Wahai khadam Ḥamā'īl, demi Liyā Kīd, Yaldūs, Ṭa'at, Umma — perintah-Nya al-ḥā; masuk khalwat mintanya — tunaikan hajatmu." (Huruf Ḥā') — berair sejuk kering; tulis sherd lembap idmár songsang campak air mengalir tanam di tempat berkumpul dosa — berpisah. Tulis loh timah tanam di tempat berniaga berhenti. Tulis di jari baca idmár kata "Si anu takut, buka tapakmu" — dia takut padamu. Inilah sifatnya. Doanya basmalah — "Allah selamatkan aku dari segala duka dunia, pegang ubun-ubunku ke kebaikan — wahai Tersembunyi, Engkau yang Tersembunyi, wahai Yang Tahu rahsia, aku minta kebahagiaan dan petunjuk, wahai Yang Mengetahui — pakaikan aku cahaya menyaksikan rahsia Ḥā' sehingga hajatku tercapai, hayā 3 — segera wahai Ḥā' demi cap al-Ḥāmūnī Khayūm — berilah khadam Ḥā' dan lindungiku, wahai Yang tahu rahsia tersembunyi — tiada tuhan melainkan Dia, bagi-Nya nama indah, demi F tiada daya melainkan Allah. Idmár: 'Jawab demi Huwa Ṭiyāl, 'Iyūṭ, alā wa-Kas, wa-Kas, Khakhaj 2, Khafaj, Yāhīyūh — al-waḥā segera saat itu' — berkesan." (Huruf Dhāl) — manis bagi yang merasa; untuk menggerakkan, tulis sutera putih dengan nama yang dikehendaki dan ibunya dan bulan dalam huruf, bakar di pelita baru dengan nama itu, baca idmár — hadir. Untuk rosakkan fikiran, tulis pada gambar orang baling ke rumahnya — fikirnya rosak. Padam marah dahaga letih — tulis bawa. Guna — masuk khalwat baca doa 100 kali — khadm hadir, ambil ikatan. Inilah rupanya. Doa: 'Bismillah — Laḏḏan ya Allah, aku tunduk, hamba fakir yang berhajat, aku nikmati nama-Mu — taklukkan khadam huruf ini demi nama ini — Huwa Hiya Ahiyāh Yamūh — tiada daya melainkan Allah.' (Huruf Ḍād) — huruf nyata kering; tulis sutera idmár bawa — disegani diterima. Tulis bilangannya dengan lemak tanam di ambang — kutu pinjal pijat katak berkumpul; suruh bakar tempat — ia buat. Baca doa pada yang sakit — pembantu terbakar. Inilah capnya. (Huruf Ẓā') — seperti Ṭā'; tulis rod oleander dengan lemak… Qāf-Dhāl… tanam di tempat serangga berbahaya; gantung pada anak — selamat. Tulis loh timah idmár songsang di rumah — berpecah. Khalwat baca doa 300 kali — bila khadam hadir ambil darinya…"
