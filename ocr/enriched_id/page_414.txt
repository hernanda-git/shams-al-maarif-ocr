"…the dominion and the knowledge of the Dominion. And if you write 40 and write with it 'Muḥammad, the Messenger of Allah' — the mentioned verse-count — and a person carries it, Allah opens to him the hidden matters and grants him success to unveil the world of dominion and the Dominion. And if you write with it every name beginning with Mīm — and that is 40 names — and carry it, he attains awe and acceptance with the higher and lower world. And whoever draws it on the wall of his retreat and looks at it every day 40 times, and reads His saying 'Say: O Allah, Owner of dominion…' the verse — when he [finishes] it grants him the execution of the word among the worlds. And if you write 40 and write the implicit concentration and the name of its King on a gold or silver ring while the Moon is in Pisces, and carry it — it holds the hearts of creatures by love. And if you bind it with the name of whom you will, and write the invocation and implicit concentration upon it, and make it into a wick — the sought is agitated. And it has a retreat: that you enter it and write the letter M on the wall and speak over it the invocation 40 [times], and likewise the implicit concentration — and the King attends and fulfills your need. And if you will to employ it, recite the invocation after every prayer 40 times, while you say 'Answer, O servant of the letter Mīm, and give me of your spirituality a spirit that serves me in my affairs.' And this is the invocation: 'In the name of Allah, the Merciful, the Compassionate — O Allah, a kingdom of Your kingdom I possess by it a complete kingdom. Yours is the dominion, O Possessor of majesty and honor, O Faithful, O Observer, O Giver, O Withholder, O Owner of the dominion — my servant of this letter, and mingle him with my spirituality, O Most Merciful of the merciful. Answer, O Mīm, and nullify the movements of the treasures and bring to me the provisions, and cast my love into the hearts of all creatures. O Allah, grant me a glance of Your glances, O Mīm — may Allah grant you favors, favor me with the complete favors — the day 'the heaven is stripped' — these, O Mīm, bliss, and it is a fullness, O Mīm, by the right of 'guide us the straight path' — Aymasa'r Yām, and Mariam, Wāh, Ḍār Yām, and La'ah, Sulaṭam al-wahm — answer, O Mīm, by the right of Gabriel and Michael and Israfil and by the power of the King Mihyā'īl — may Allah honor the letter Mīm so you are among the worlds of those brought near by it, and return to the generosity of Allah the Generous — descend and drive out these passers from such-and-such a place — al-waḥā, the hastening.' And if you will to enter any place with wealth, write the letter on the stone and cast it into the wealth and take your want while you recite the invocation — the sought is attained. And if you employ the letter and the King you need not these works; and likewise in repelling waters — look to the descent of the letters in the mansion, and draw it on a lead tablet or stone or a moist sherd, and write the implicit concentration around the letter, and read the invocation and cast the tablet in the water — it drains away. "…kerajaan dan ilmu Dominion. Jika engkau menulis 40 dan menulis dengannya 'Muḥammad Rasulullah' — bilangan ayat tersebut — dan seseorang membawanya, Allah membuka kepadanya perkara tersembunyi dan memberinya taufiq membuka alam kerajaan dan Dominion. Jika engkau menulis dengannya setiap nama bermula Mīm — iaitu 40 nama — dan membawanya, ia memperoleh kekaguman dan penerimaan di alam tinggi dan rendah. Sesiapa melukisnya di dinding khalwatnya dan memandangnya setiap hari 40 kali, serta membaca firman-Nya 'Katakanlah: Ya Allah, Pemilik kerajaan…' ayat itu — apabila [selesai] ia memberinya pelaksanaan kata di antara alam. Jika engkau menulis 40 dan menulis idmār serta nama Rajanya pada cincin emas atau perak ketika bulan di Pisces, dan membawanya — ia menahan hati makhluk dengan kasih. Jika engkau ikat dengan nama yang dikehendaki, dan menulis doa dan idmār padanya, lalu menjadikannya sumbu — yang dicari digerakkan. Dan ia ada khalwat: masuk dan tulis huruf M di dinding serta bercakap doa 40 kali, dan idmár — Raja hadir tunaikan hajat. Jika mahu menggunakannya, baca doa selepas setiap solat 40 kali sambil 'Jawab wahai khadam huruf Mīm, beri aku dari rohani-mu roh yang menyembahkanku dalam urusanku.' Dan inilah doanya: 'Dengan nama Allah Yang Maha Pemurah lagi Maha Penyayang — ya Allah, kerajaan dari kerajaan-Mu kumiliki dengannya kerajaan sempurna. Bagi-Mu kerajaan wahai Pemiliki kemuliaan dan penghormatan, wahai Yang Memelihara, wahai Yang Mengawasi, wahai Yang Memberi, wahai Yang Menahan, wahai Pemilik kerajaan — khadamku huruf ini, campurkanlah dia dengan rohaniku, wahai Yang Paling Penyayang. Jawab wahai Mīm, batalkan gerakan khazanah dan bawalah rezeki kepadaku, serta lemparkan kasihku ke hati sekalian makhluk. Ya Allah, anugerahkanlah aku pandangan dari pandangan-Mu, wahai Mīm — semoga Allah memberi kurnia, kurniakan aku kurnia sempurna — hari 'langit dikoyak' — ini, wahai Mīm, nikmat, ia penuhan, wahai Mīm, demi hak 'tunjuk jalan lurus' — Aymasa'r Yām, dan Mariam, Wāh, Ḍār Yām, La'ah, Sulaṭam al-wahm — jawab wahai Mīm, demi Jibril Mikail Israfil dan kuasa Raja Mihyā'īl — semoga Allah muliakan huruf Mīm agar engkau di alam yang didekatkan, kembalilah ke kemurahan Allah Yang Pemurah — turun dan halau yang lalu dari tempat anu — al-waḥā segera.' Masuk tempat ada harta, tulis huruf pada batu baling ke harta ambil hajat sambil baca doa — tercapai. Jika guna huruf dan Raja tak perlu kerja ini; begitu tolak air — lihat turun huruf di mansyon, lukis pada loh timah batu sherd lembap, tulis idmár keliling, baca doa baling loh ke air — kering. Jika tulis debu di leher ayam — bila jalan debu berselerak jatuh di tempat, keaiban lari. Jika mahu khazanah tak rosak, tulis huruf dan doa…"
