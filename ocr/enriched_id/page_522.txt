"…the awe in the hearts of the creation, and he calls by what relates to asking the awe and establishing the word and subduing the enemies and what suits this pattern and is akin to it of the names and the thoughts — the object is attained. And whoever reads this supplication and the mentioned verse in a dark house with his eyes closed, witnesses strange lights filling his heart; and if he perseveres in that, it takes form for him in the worlds of the unseen. And it is a mention suitable for the people of understanding and the masters of hearts; and its writer and bearer, there appears for him an increase of power in his self and the subduing of his enemy and opponent — for of the special properties of the Sun is the subduing of opponents and the tying of tongues and the hot illnesses such as the yellow bile; and it has in the endearing of hearts an effect that scarcely vanishes or changes. So whoever is able to cure by it the illnesses occurring in the head — especially from coldness — finds its effect on the spot. And we are not about exhaustive exposition of the clarification of everything, and the intelligent is sufficed by the indication over the explicit expression. And whoever writes His saying exalted 'Allah is the light of the heavens and the earth' in the mentioned hour and keeps it with him, his breast is expanded for what he wills and Allah the Exalted widens for him his provision and there appears upon him power and awe. And there is joined to it this supplication of the eighth hour of them — and it is this: you say 'O my God, You caused to rise upon my existence the sun of my witnessing of You in the existences and the colors, until I walk by what You showed me of the horizons of the dominical world, rejoicing and glad; and unveil in it the meaning of the word of the constitution, so it is influenced for me in every constituted [thing] and its acts by Your universal word, by Your permission who subjected to it what is in existence without a dark nature — indeed You are the Enlightener of all by Your dominion, and the Enlightener of the lights by Your light, which issued from Your name the Light, the Manifest, the Living, the Self-Subsistent — everything perishes but His face; to Him the judgment, and to Him you return.' Whoever calls by this supplication in this hour 49 times, Allah the Exalted clothes him with a light he finds in his self, and eases for him the apportioned provision, and his vigor flows in the causes a strange flowing; and that is upon ablution and purity and presence of heart. And it is a mention for the masters of unveiling, so it is established for them what they unveil by, and suits it from the mighty Qur'ān His saying exalted 'Have they not considered what Allah created, of a thing its shadows incline' until His saying 'abased.' * And from the Beautiful Names: al-'Alī, al-'Aẓīm, al-Kabīr — and measure by that; and more than this cannot be drawn out, and Allah is most knowing. * And as for His names the Exalted al-'Alī al-'Aẓīm al-Kabīr — so whoever breaks them and engraves them in a silver ring of the Sun and writes upon it the circle 'And it does not tire them to preserve them, and He is the Most High, the Great' — its bearer is trustworthy, and everyone who sees him loves him and seeks his company; and if an eye looks at him with evil, it returns to its owner. And as for His name the Exalted al-Ḥafīẓ — when it is engraved and its letters gathered and broken, its rememberer and bearer does not fear from a thing, nor does a thief transgress upon him, and he is preserved from the fears even if he falls into the places of fear and settles by it. And this is a sum of the names of Allah the Exalted for the gathering and the repelling of the whisperings and the affliction and the passion, and the repelling of the painful from the tremendous dreadful matters; and it is suitable for the kings and the masters of the state — if they persevere in their mention, Allah establishes their dominion and their state, and they possess their desires and their anger, and it rectifies for the people of the way. Indonesia:
— ٥١٦ —

"…kegentaran dalam hati makhluk, dan ia berdoa dengan apa yang berkait memohon kegentaran dan menegakkan kalimah serta menundukkan musuh dan apa yang sesuai corak ini dan seakannya dari nama dan fikiran — tercapai yang dimaksud. Dan barangsiapa membaca doa ini dan ayat disebut dalam rumah gelap dengan mata tertutup, menyaksikan cahaya aneh memenuhi hatinya; dan bila ia bertekun padanya, ia berbentuk baginya dalam alam ghaib. Dan ia dzikir sesuai bagi ahli faham dan tuan hati; dan penulis serta pemakainya, nampak padanya tambahan kuasa pada dirinya dan menundukkan musuh serta lawannya — kerana dari sifat khusus Matahari ialah menundukkan lawan dan mengikat lidah serta penyakit panas seperti hempedu kuning; dan ia pada menyenangkan hati berkesan yang jarang hilang atau berubah. Maka barangsiapa mampu menyembuh dengannya penyakit di kepala — khususnya dari sejuk — dapati kesannya serta-merta. Dan kami tidak bermaksud menghabiskan penjelasan segala perkara, dan orang bijak cukup dengan isyarat atas ungkapan jelas. Dan barangsiapa menulis firman-Nya 'Allah cahaya langit dan bumi' pada jam disebut serta menyimpannya, dadanya diluaskan bagi apa dikehendaki dan Allah Yang Maha Tinggi meluaskan rezekinya serta nampak padanya kuasa dan kegentaran. Dan disertai padanya doa ini dari jam kelapan mereka — iaitu: engkau ucapkan 'Wahai Tuhanku, Engkau jadikan terbit atas wujudku matahari penyaksianku pada-Mu dalam wujud dan warna, hingga aku berjalan dengan apa yang Engkau tunjukkan dari ufuk alam kerajaan, gembira dan riang; dan bukakan padanya makna kata kejadian, maka ia terpengaruh bagiku dalam tiap yang dijadikan dan perbuatannya dengan kata sejagat-Mu, dengan izin-Mu yang menundukkan padanya apa dalam wujud tanpa tabiat gelap — sesungguhnya Engkau Pencahaya segala dengan kerajaan-Mu, dan Pencahaya cahaya dengan cahaya-Mu yang keluar dari nama-Mu cahaya, Yang Menyatakan, Yang Hidup, Yang Berdiri Sendiri — tiap binasa kecuali wajah-Nya; bagi-Nya hukum, dan kepada-Nya kamu kembali.' Barangsiapa berdoa dengan doa ini pada jam ini 49 kali, Allah Yang Maha Tinggi memakaikan dia cahaya yang dirasai dalam dirinya, serta memudahkan bahagian rezeki, dan kegagahannya mengalir dalam sebab secara aneh; dan itu atas wuduk dan suci serta hadir hati. Dan adapun nama-Nya Yang Maha Tinggi al-Ḥafīẓ — bila diukir dan hurufnya dikumpul serta dipecahkan, yang menyebut dan memakainya tidak takut pada sesuatu, dan tiada pencuri melanggarinya, serta ia dipelihara dari ketakutan walaupun jatuh ke tempat takut dan menetap dengannya. Dan dalam ringkasan ini rahsia keagungan dan kegentaran serta kekayaan diri dan kesuciannya dari keji serta ketinggian azam, dan padanya pengambilan dzikir malaikat serta pembukaan rahsia kewalian bagi wali — dan mereka dibuat berdiri untuk ilmunya.
