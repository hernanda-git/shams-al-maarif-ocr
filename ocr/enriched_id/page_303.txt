English:

And the severe letter — he does not feel fatigue or toil. And whoever arranges their numbers in a silver ring and seals with it — Allah aids him in carrying heavy loads. As for His exalted Names Possessor of Severe Grasp, the Omnipotent — none wronged supplicates by them but Allah seizes his wrongdoer with the seizure of a Mighty, Omnipotent (One). And as for His exalted Name the One — in it is a letter of the Greatest Name; and its angel is 'Ḥaniyā'īl', and he is chief over four commanders, under each commander 13 ranks, each rank 13; and the invoker — the angel descends upon him. And his supplication: 'O One, You are the Single in Your oneness, and You are He who unified Your Self in the stations of the Names, and You are the Knower of what is beneath the dust and what is in the highest heavens — the Compassionate established upon the Throne. I ask You by the light of Your oneness and the radiance of Your unity, in the light of the gleam of Your lightning — that You make me one of the witnesses, singled out by knowledge and gnosis. Indeed You are Allah, the One, the Requiter.' And as for His exalted Name the Unique — it is a tremendous name; and its angel is 'Jahṭā'īl', and he is chief over four commanders, under each commander 284 ranks, each rank 284; and the invoker — the angel descends upon him as preceded. And his supplication: 'O Unique, You are He who was alone in His dominion in oneness, and You are the Eternal, the Subsisting by the Eternity. To You I have directed, and in You I have taken refuge, and upon Your bounty I have relied; You have no partner in Your dominion, nor minister, nor director, nor counselor, and over all things You are Able. I ask You that You cause to flow upon my hand and tongue the fulfillment of needs for creatures, and that You protect me from the destructive (sins) and stumbles. Indeed You are the Ally of good things and the Repeller of doubts.' And as for His exalted Name the Eternal — it is a tremendous name; and its angel is 'Nūriyā'īl', and he is chief over four commanders, under each commander 134 ranks, each rank 134; and the invoker — the angel descends upon him. And his supplication: 'O Eternal, You are He to whom they repair in needs, and the refuge to You in cares and distresses; and You are He who gives and withholds from Your bounty the constant customs. I ask You by Your independence of Your creatures and their need of You — that You make me a goal for the servants in dire matters, and that You cause to flow upon my tongue and hand the fulfillment of needs, and protect me from the destructive (sins). Indeed You are the Guide to good things.' And as for His exalted Name the Able (al-Qādir) — in it is a letter of the Greatest Name; and its angel is 'Haṭā'īl', and he is chief over four commanders, under each commander 305 ranks, each rank 305; and the spirit descends upon the invoker. And his supplication: 'O Able, You are He who executed His power in the constitution of essences, and You are He who manifested His will by changing evil deeds into good, and You are the Gatherer of the scattered. I ask You, O Allah, by the tremendous signs — that You make me able to repel slips. Indeed You are above spatial limitation and veil.' And as for His exalted Name the Omnipotent — it is a tremendous name; and its servant is 'Ḥafiyā'īl', and he is chief over four commanders, under each commander 44 ranks, each rank 44; and the spirit descends upon the invoker. And his supplication: 'O Omnipotent, You are He who gathered Your beloveds in the abode of good-pleasure, and You are He who made clear the mirror of him who turned to You for the appearance of the secret of security and safety. I ask You by Your tremendous power — that You grant me reaching to the hooves of steadfastness. Indeed You are the Lord of the worlds.' And as for His exalted Name the Advancing — it is a tremendous name containing two letters of the Greatest Name; and its angel is 'Qay'ā'īl', and he is chief over four commanders, under each commander 184 ranks, each rank 184; and the invoker — the angel descends upon him. And his supplication: 'O Advancing, You are He who advanced the people of friendship to the abode of eternity, and made them understand the secrets of the degrees of unveiling and witnessing, and illuminated their insights to see the traces of the manifestations of the worshipped King. I ask You by Your power by which You measured over all Your creation, and by Your mercy spread over the people of Your blessing and sea — that You make me advanced in good things, preceding to You upon the steed of gnosis and obediences, turning to You in the swiftest of times.'
