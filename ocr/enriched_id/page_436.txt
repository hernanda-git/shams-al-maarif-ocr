English:
— 430 —

"…after her death — and that is from the general mercy, for the coming-out of the plant in the desert and the barren wilds that the animal pastures upon is from the manifestation of His name al-Raḥīm. And the servant of this name is from the worlds of Gabriel (upon him peace); and whoever perseveres in reading it — Allah has mercy on him in the world and the Hereafter and attains the honor of the rank. And if it is written on a silver tablet and placed on the neck of the newborn who gets anger and crying — it leaves him. And if it is written in a ring and a man seals with it — Allah gives him mercy and compassion over His creation. And whoever reads it its simple number — Allah raises his rank. And it has a retreat of 40 days with the condition of perseverance in its mention. And if he sets a ring on a captive and writes the name upon it its number and recites it after every prayer — the servant attends, and his name is JARABĪL, and he is chief over 4 commanders, under each commander 200 rows, and he comes to the rememberer and fulfills his need. And this is its figure. And this is the invocation standing by it — the basmala: 'O Allah, You are the Merciful over the creatures and the Unveiler of the secret of the existents, and You are al-Raḥmān — I ask You to subordinate to me Your servant JARABĪL who fulfills my need and what I will. My God, I ask You the unveiling upon my existence and the attainment of my intent, and inform me of the existence of my sun — let it not be realized in every subtle [thing] and black witnessing that erases from a point other, and illuminate my heart by the light of Your name al-Raḥīm, that the spirits of the tyrants submit to me and the souls of the rebels yield to me, and unveil to me the reality of the world of dominion and Dominion, and might and Tremendousness; regard me with nearness to You, O Near, O Loving, O Merciful.' Whoever converses with his Lord by this invocation and recites this name — every difficulty is eased for him and the doors of provision are opened for him. [magic-square, columns ال ر ح ي م / 201 34 16 23 33 / 38 19 3 32 33] (Section on His name the Exalted, al-Malik) Know that the meaning of this name is that by which every thing is realized and to which every thing returns, and that is not but for Allah; and His dominion contains a world of dominion, Dominion, and Tremendousness — and that is that He made the letters of dominion three: M L K. So the Mīm is from the breaking of the One and the circles of the letters, and it is manifest for them, for when Allah brought out the Hā' — which is an encompassing letter — in the manifest of His formation and the inmost of His extension (for it has no reality upon which the letters are received), He created the Mīm and made it an encompassing form receiving its secret opposite it — the inmost of monotheism by the falling of worship — and the Mīm manifest for it; and Allah created its secret the Dominion, and created for its sake the Throne, for it is an encompassing in the form of the converse of the existents; and created from the heavens the secret of lordship and the secret of encompassing; and created from them the word of encompassing absolutely — lordship; and created from the heavens the secret of lordship and the secret of encompassing by the secret of the Dominion; and He singled out its lights, for their attachment to a pillar of the pillars of the Throne — served by upper sciences particular to His name al-Malik and the letters of the Mīm. And likewise this letter is repeated in the name of our Prophet in three indications: so if you face it with dominion, there face you the worlds of the Dominion; and if you face it with the Dominion, there face you the lights of the Dominion in the intellects — and it is the last letter. And as for the Lām — it is the letter by which Allah extended the world of Tremendousness; and since its load was heavy with the lights of the Dominion, it found none to receive from it — so thereupon Allah brought out the Kāf from the inmost of the Lām, which is known by 'Kun' — Allah created from it the world of dominion by the secrets of the Tremendousness and the secrets of the Dominion. (Subtlety) Know…"

Indonesia:
— ٤٣٠ —

"…selepas matinya — dari rahmat umum, tumbuh tumbuhan di padang belantara haiwan ragut dari zahir nama al-Raḥīm. Khadam nama ini dari alam Jibril; istiqamah baca — Allah rahmati dunia akhirat capai darjat mulia. Tulis keping perak di leher bayi yang marah menangis — hilang. Tulis cincin meterai — Allah bagi rahmat belas kasihan. Baca bilangan mudah — Allah angkat darjat. Khalwat 40 hari istiqamah zikir. Pasang cincin pada tawanan tulis nama bilangannya baca selepas solat — khadam hadir nama JARABĪL ketua 4 komander tiap 200 saf datang tunaikan hajat. Inilah rupanya. Zikir berdiri — basmalah: 'Ya Allah Engkau Yang Penyayang atas makhluk Pembuka rahsia wujud, Engkau al-Raḥmān — kuminta taklukkan khadam-Mu JARABĪL tunaikan hajatku; ya Tuhanku buka wujudku capai tujuan, beritahu wujud matahariku — jangan jadi pada tiap halus dan hitam menyaksikan hapus dari titik lain, terangi hatiku cahaya nama-Mu al-Raḥīm supaya roh tiran tunduk jiwa penderhaka patuh, buka hakikat alam domini Malakut 'izzah jabarut, pandang aku dekat-Mu wahai Dekat Pencinta Penyayang.' Sesiapa bermunajat zikir ini baca nama — segala susah mudah pintu rezeki terbuka. [segi empat, lajur ال ر ح ي م / 201 34 16 23 33 / 38 19 3 32 33] (Fasal nama al-Malik) Ketahui makna nama ini yang setiap benda terlaksana kembali padanya, tiada melainkan Allah; domini-Nya isi alam domini Malakut jabarut — Dia jadikan huruf domini tiga: M L K. Mīm dari pecah Yang Esa bulatan huruf zahirnya, bila Allah keluarkan Hā' huruf meliputi zahir batin sambungan (tiada hakikat huruf diterima), cipta Mīm jadikan bentuk meliputi terima rahsia berhadapan batin tauhid gugur ibadah — Mīm zahirnya; Allah cipta rahsia Malakut, cipta 'Arasy meliputi bentuk munajat makhluk; dari langit rahsia ketuhanan meliputi; cipta perkataan meliputi mutlak — ketuhanan; cipta dari langit rahsia ketuhanan meliputi rahsia Malakut; pilih cahayanya melekat tiang 'Arasy dikhidmat sains atas khusus nama al-Malik huruf Mīm. Huruf ini berulang dalam nama Nabi tiga isyarat: hadap dengan domini — alam Malakut; hadap Malakut — cahaya Malakut dalam akal, huruf terakhir. Lām — huruf Allah panjangkan alam jabarut; berat muatannya cahaya Malakut tiada terima, maka Allah keluarkan Kāf dari batin Lām yang dikenal 'Kun' — cipta alam domini rahsia jabarut Malakut. (Nuktah) Ketahui…"
