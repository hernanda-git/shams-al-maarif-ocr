and prosperity and obeys his command. And the Dajjāl goes forth, and his going forth is from Ṭabaristān in the east, and he comes to Iṣfahān
and seventy thousand tāliyāsān of its Jews follow him. He is an aged one-eyed man, written between his eyes "kāfir" (unbeliever) which everyone reads.
His stay in the earth is forty days: the first day like a year, the second like a month, the third like a Friday, and the rest of his days
like your days. And the Prophet (may Allah bless him and grant him peace) was asked about that; when it was said to him: "Does one prayer suffice him for a day?" he said: "No, rather
reckon for him his measure." And Allah sends Gog and Magog, and they all pass over the Lake of Tiberias and drink what is in it
of water and the water of all the seas of the world; then Allah sends upon them the worm and they die and become all dead at morning, and the people kindle

the fifth Mūsā, in the sixth 'Īsā, in the seventh Muḥammad (may Allah bless him and grant him peace). And it is related from him (may Allah bless him and grant him peace) that he said: "At the head of every hundred years Allah sends to this nation a man who renews for it the affair of its religion," and he left it
