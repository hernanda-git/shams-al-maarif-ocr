And know — may Allah guide me and you — that al-Raḥmān al-Raḥīm are noble invocations for the distressed and a security for the fearful; and no one engraves them on a ring on Friday at the end of the day and wears it but he is sought by it in all his movements and states.

( The Fourth Chapter: On His name, exalted, { al-Malik } ) It is fitting as a remembrance for kings and others; and it has a 3×3 square placed on a sheet of gold together with His saying, exalted: { Say: O Allah, Owner of the Dominion } — the verse; and the majestic commanders carry it, and its bearer becomes awesome before people; and it is fitting as a remembrance for one whose name is 'Abd al-Malik. And this is the form of its square. And if one engraves its like numerically on a sheet of gold in the dignity of the Sun, places upon it a red ruby, and sets it in a ring and enters with it upon a ruler or tyrant — he is humbled before him and cannot bear to look at him. And Plato placed it for Dhū al-Qarnayn, so the lions fled from him. And this is its form; and it has the number 90. [H…] and it is of the realities of the letters; and it is among the names strung according to the ranks of number in descent; and it is a rectangular odd-pair, whose parts are:

( The Fifth Chapter: On His name, exalted, { al-Quddūs } ) This name of majestic rank — whoever abounds in its remembrance is overcome by a state from it by which Allah removes from him every blameworthy desire; and it is placed in a numeric triangle surrounded by a letter-square in the dignity of Jupiter on Friday night, so that for its bearer Allah transforms him from every blameworthy trait to praiseworthy character, and he becomes beloved of creation and they praise him; and it is fitting as a remembrance for one whose name is 'Abd al-Quddūs and one whose name is Isḥāq. ( The Sixth Chapter: On His name, exalted, { al-Salām } ) This great name — no one ever carried it with him and saw anything hateful; and whoever abounds in its remembrance is safe from all afflictions, and his remembrance takes effect as a command for the people of beginnings and the people of ends; and whoever abounds in its remembrance while fearful — Allah, exalted, secures him; and it has the number 131, which is a primal number pointing to His name, exalted, Kāfil (The Guarantor).

Dan ketahuilah — semoga Allah memberi taufik kepadaku dan kepadamu — bahwa ar-Raḥmān ar-Raḥīm adalah dzikir-dzikir mulia bagi orang yang tertimpa kesusahan dan keamanan bagi yang takut; dan tak seorang pun mengukirnya pada cincin di hari Jumat akhir hari lalu memakainya melainkan ia dicari karenanya dalam segala gerak dan keadaannya.

( Bab Keempat: Tentang nama-Nya Ta'ala { al-Malik } ) Cocok sebagai dzikir bagi raja dan lainnya; dan ia mempunyai bujur sangkar 3×3 yang diletakkan pada lembaran emas bersama firman-Nya Ta'ala: { Katakanlah: Ya Allah, Pemilik kerajaan } — ayat tersebut; dan para panglima agung membawanya, serta pembawanya menjadi disegani di sisi manusia; dan cocok sebagai dzikir bagi yang bernama 'Abd al-Malik. Dan ini rupa bujur sangkarnya. Dan apabila seseorang mengukir yang semisalnya secara numerik pada lembaran emas dalam kemuliaan Matahari, meletakkan permata yakut merah di atasnya, dan menaruhnya dalam cincin lalu masuk dengan itu menemui penguasa atau tiran — ia tunduk kepadanya dan tak sanggup memandangnya. Dan Plato meletakannya untuk Dzū al-Qarnayn, maka singa-singa lari darinya. Dan ini rupanya; serta ia mempunyai bilangan 90. ( Bab Kelima: Tentang nama-Nya Ta'ala { al-Quddūs } ) Nama bermartabat agung ini — barangsiapa banyak berdzikir dengannya, ia dikuasai oleh suatu keadaan darinya yang dengannya Allah menghilangkan dari padanya setiap syahwat tercela; dan ia diletakkan dalam segitiga numerik yang dikelilingi bujur sangkar huruf dalam kemuliaan Yupiter pada malam Jumat, sehingga bagi pembawanya Allah mengubahnya dari setiap akhlak tercela menjadi akhlak terpuji, dan ia menjadi kekasih makhluk serta mereka memujinya; dan cocok sebagai dzikir bagi yang bernama 'Abd al-Quddūs dan yang bernama Isḥāq.
