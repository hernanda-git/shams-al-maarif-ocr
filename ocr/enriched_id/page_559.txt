English:

"…of the risings for the times * , and what they have in the desires of works of the specialities * — so I reflected upon seeking the subject carried upon this foundation * , and that it be the third of the two luminaries * , for the rising of the two full moons * — so I hastened to compose those benefits * and to versify these pearls * , and I named it (Fawātiḥ al-Raghā'ib * fī Khaṣūṣiyyāt Awqāt al-Kawākib [The Openings of the Desires in the Specialities of the Times of the Planets]) — may Allah the Exalted make it encompassed by acceptance * , and connected to the reaching of the asked and the hoped * ; and Allah suffices me and is enough * , and peace upon His servants whom He chose.

(The Planet: the Moon) Its exaltation is in the third degree of the sign of Taurus — by it the efficacy of the work is strengthened; its depression in the sign of Scorpio; and its weal in the sign of Capricorn. Its rising is in the form of a man of beautiful face riding upon a wild ass [a type of beast]; its ascendant in the forms of the faces of the zodiac: in the first face, white — it accords at its beginning for attracting the chiefs, and at its end for their affection; in the second face, red — it accords at its beginning for binding, and at its end for dissolution; in the third face, dusty — it accords at its beginning for trust, and at its end for repelling the beasts. The first hour of Monday and from the night of Friday is fit for works of love; and nothing is worked in it but that. The eighth hour of it — what is worked in it for reconciliation between the discordant. The fifth hour of it from Tuesday and from the night of Saturday — it is blameworthy, nothing is worked in it. The twelfth hour of it is blameworthy and accords for hatred and separation and evils and stoning. The second hour of Wednesday and from the night of Sunday is blameworthy — nothing is ever worked in it; the ninth hour of it [is] for that. The sixth hour of Thursday and from the night of Monday is fit for everything desired, and suits travel by land and sea. The third and fourth hours of Friday and from the night of Tuesday — they are two malefic [hours], worked in for separation, hatred, and removal. The seventh hour of Saturday and from the night of Wednesday accords for nosebleed and for bleeding for illness. The fourth hour of Sunday and from the night of Thursday is not fit for work; and selling and buying are disliked in it. The eleventh hour of it accords for works of talismans. Its ink is what is white like ceruse; its incense is white aloes, camphor, frankincense, white costus, and likewise white sandalwood, aloe, and dragon's-blood, and saffron — in partnership with the Sun in that. Its alighting in the lunar mansions [manāzil] is connected to them by the requiring of their fortunes and inauspiciousnesses and its connection to them — questions in which are intents in works and means: (Manzil al-Sharṭayn) fiery, inauspicious — it accords for the hernia and what relates to it, and is fit for travel and the use of medicines, and the detailing of needs. (Manzil al-Buṭayn) hot, moist — its fortune weak — it accords for extracting hidden treasures and for works of agitation, and prolongs in it the horizon and prison. (Manzil al-Thurayyā) fortunate — accords for works of love and for the work of alchemy — I mean the alchemy of medicine — and for the corruption of cattle and for sea travel. (Manzil al-Dabarān) earthy, dry — and if the moon is full in it, goodnesses multiply in the markets — it accords for absolute corruption except what attaches to the charm. (Manzil al-Haq'ah) dry, mixed — accords for absolute reform except what relates to partnership, and is specific to the commencement of sciences, and marriage and travel are praised in it. (Manzil al-Han'ah) fiery, fortunate, mixed with inauspiciousness — accords also for reform when the medicine is drunk, and is fit for travel and marriage and the cutting of cloth. (Manzil al-Dhirā') profitable, fortunate, mixed with inauspiciousness — accords for absolute corruption except what relates to the slave; accords for trade and the fulfillment of needs and the binding of wild beasts and the corruption of crafts.

"…dari terbit bagi waktu * , dan apa pada keinginan kerja kekhasan * — maka aku renung mencari subjek dipikul atas asas ini * , dan ia menjadi ketiga dari dua cahaya * , bagi terbit dua bulan penuh * — maka aku segera susun faedah itu * dan nazamkan mutiara ini * , dan kunanakan (Fawātiḥ al-Raghā'ib * fī Khaṣūṣiyyāt Awqāt al-Kawākib [Pembukaan Keinginan dalam Kekhasan Waktu Planet]) — semoga Allah Yang Maha Tinggi menjadikannya diliputi penerimaan * , dan bersambung ke capaian ditanya dan diharap * ; dan cukuplah Allah bagiku * , dan salam atas hamba-Nya yang dipilih.

(Planet: Bulan) Ketinggiannya di darjah ketiga buruj Taurus — dengannya keberkesanan kerja dikuatkan; kerendahannya di buruj Scorpio; dan kebaikannya di buruj Capricorn. Terbitnya berbentuk manusia wajah cantik menunggang keldai liar; ascendeninya dalam wajah buruj: wajah pertama, putih — sesuai di awal untuk menarik ketua, di hujung untuk kasih mereka; wajah kedua, merah — sesuai di awal untuk ikatan, di hujung untuk pembubaran; wajah ketiga, berdebu — sesuai di awal untuk kepercayaan, di hujung untuk menolak binatang. Jam pertama Isnin dan dari malam Jumaat sesuai kerja cinta; dan tiada dikerjakan selain itu. Jam kelapan daripadanya — apa dikerjakan untuk perdamaian antara yang berselisih. Jam kelima daripadanya dari Selasa dan malam Sabtu — tercela, tiada dikerjakan. Jam kedua belas daripadanya tercela dan sesuai untuk kebencian dan perpisahan serta kejahatan dan rejam. Jam kedua Rabu dan malam Ahad tercela — tiada pernah dikerjakan; jam kesembilan daripadanya [untuk] itu. Jam keenam Khamis dan malam Isnin sesuai segala yang dikehendaki, dan sesuai musafir darat dan laut. Jam ketiga dan keempat Jumaat dan malam Selasa — dua jam jahat, dikerjakan untuk perpisahan, kebencian, dan pemindahan. Jam ketujuh Sabtu dan malam Rabu sesuai pendarahan hidung dan biusan penyakit. Jam keempat Ahad dan malam Khamis tidak sesuai kerja; dan jual beli dibenci. Jam kesebelas daripadanya sesuai kerja talisman. Dakwatnya apa putih seperti ceruse; ubat asapnya kayu aloes putih, kafur, kemenyan, costus putih, dan demikian kayu cendana putih, gaharu, darah naga, dan za'faran — berkongsi dengan Matahari dalam itu. Pendaratannya dalam manzil [rumah bulan] dihubungkan pada mereka dengan keperluan tuah dan sial mereka dan hubungannya — soalan yang ada tujuan kerja dan wasilah: (Manzil al-Sharṭayn) berapi, sial — sesuai untuk hernia dan kaitannya, serta sesuai musafir dan guna ubat, dan perincian hajat. (Manzil al-Buṭayn) panas lembap — tuahnya lemah — sesuai keluarkan khazanah tersembunyi dan kerja kegelisahan, dan memanjangkan ufuk dan penjara. (Manzil al-Thurayyā) bertuah — sesuai kerja cinta dan kerja alkimia — maksud alkimia perubatan — dan kerosakan ternakan serta musafir laut. (Manzil al-Dabarān) tanah kering — dan jika bulan penuh padanya, kebaikan berlipat di pasar — sesuai kerosakan mutlak kecuali apa melekat pada azimat. (Manzil al-Haq'ah) kering bercampur — sesuai pembaharuan mutlak kecuali apa kait perkongsian, dan khusus permulaan ilmu, serta perkahwinan dan musafir dipuji. (Manzil al-Han'ah) berapi bertuah bercampur sial — sesuai juga pembaharuan bila ubat diminum, dan sesuai musafir dan kahwin serta potong kain. (Manzil al-Dhirā') untung bertuah bercampur sial — sesuai kerosakan mutlak kecuali apa kait hamba; sesuai perdagangan dan sempurna hajat serta ikat binatang liar dan kerosakan kerja tangan.
