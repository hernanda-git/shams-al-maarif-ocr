The Throne of al-Raḥmāniyya. He, exalted, said: { The Compassionate settled upon the Throne } — so this is the reality of the existence by which the heavens and the earth stand; it prevailed without a pillar, so the highs are level, held by elevation, and the secret of taking (it) is for invention. And to this Throne (is) its attention; so the hand (is) the heavens and the earth, and the reality of length and breadth, and the appearance of expansion and contraction, and the end of raising and lowering — its conduct is spiritual, its ascension spiritual, its witnessing intellectual, its elevation high, its contraction throne-like — no possessor of a body perceives it, nor one of form, nor one formed, by the concealment of the reality of the secret of the numbers without enumeration. So this is the reality of this Throne, and to it the end of the Trustworthy Spirit; with it stood the reality of Gabriel, upon him peace — and he is the principles of the highest assembly, and in it is heard the creaking of the pens as they record what does not accept change and is not imagined in the pages of formation. So blessed is he who understood these throne-indications and the holy subtleties. ( And the third Throne ) the Throne of al-Majīd; He, exalted, said: { Possessor of the Throne of Majesty }; and it is the end of loftiness, rather the spirits are empowered by it. So as for the first Throne — there is no veil or cover by it; Allah honored by it the prophets and the elect, and with it stood the reality of the intellects in the upper world, the abode of the spirits. And the Throne of al-Majīd — by it the spirits departed, and wandered in the temples of their phantoms, and disposed in the artifacts of their effect, and formed in the molds of the spirits by the witnessing of the difference of forms in the molds of composition. And to this Throne is the end of the worlds of spirits, and the drawing of the outpouring of lights in the molds of the letters in the circle of the isthmus, for the appearance of the seal upon the circle by the witnessing of beauty, and the emergence of the judgment, and the appearance of knowledge. So this is the reality of the veils — their outward is the outward of power, their inward is the inward of command; so whoever reached the two ends gathered the two upper matters, and whoever did not reach a presence that loosens his bond from absoluteness, and the departure of the talisman [~] by the fire of longings — he is of the saying in a degree (of loss), and of the betrayal in the existence of associationism, forsaken by the storms of beauty in the pit of the struggle. He, exalted, said: { If they had heard, they would not have answered you } (the verse); so whoever knew his aim did not don the garment of deception, and did not mix with the garments of Iblīs. And this after the pure ablution and the hidden restraint — it became incumbent upon him necessarily ways of ascent, and the forms of the human image, with standing upon His saying, exalted: { O regret for what I neglected in the side of Allah, and I was of the mockers }. So we seek refuge in Allah from betrayal and the alteration of the essences. ( And the fourth Throne ) the Throne of al-Karīm; and to it the end of the numbers, and the relation of the deeds in the great movement — and it is the secret of the support for accepting the courses of disposal, and by it is learned the reality of the Truth by which every thing stands — which in the right of the prophets is protection, and in the right of the saints is preservation, and in the right of the repenters is mercy, and in the right of the deniers is wrath. So whoever hangs in the balance of the Throne his balance of deed — the subtleties of his lights preponderate, and he extends by the fine of his traces. And know that the counterpart of the forms by the countering of the Throne — and every upper (has) a Throne for every lower; so appeared by the highs the secret of the lowers, so the secret of the two worlds. So the Throne comprises the manifestation of His insight — no insight for Him but by the conception of His deeds, and no forms and no manifestations until he cuts the relation of the forms upon them and their lowers, state and knowledge and witnessing and arrival. ( And the fifth Throne ) the Throne of al-'Aẓama; He, exalted, said: { Allah, there is no god but He, Lord of the Great Throne } — and it is the witnessing of the command by the realities of the light of sights as it was before its existence and after it revolved in the orbit of its limits, so he witnessed the wondrous making, and interceded for those whom the Truth called from a near place — and it is the Great Throne. And to it ended the bodily deeds and the intellectual swimnings; to it ascends the good word, and the remembrances, and the hidden lights in the secrets — without a lettered letter, and no marked sign, and no limit…

'Arsy al-Raḥmāniyyah. Berfirman Yang Maha Tinggi: { Yang Maha Pemurah bersemayam di atas 'Arsy } — maka ini hakikat keberadaan yang dengannya berdiri langit dan bumi; ia berkuasa tanpa tiang, maka yang tinggi setara, dipegang oleh ketinggian, dan rahasia pengambilan (nya) untuk pengadaan. Dan pada 'Arsy ini perhatiannya; maka tangan (ialah) langit dan bumi, dan hakikat panjang dan lebar, serta nampaknya kembang dan kuncup, dan akhir angkat dan turun — kelakuannya ruhaniah, naiknya ruhaniah, saksinya intelektual, ketinggiannya tinggi, kuncupnya seperti 'arsy — tiada pemilik tubuh yang memahaminya, dan tiada yang berbentuk, dan tiada yang dibentuk, oleh tersembunyinya hakikat rahasia bilangan tanpa penghitungan. Maka ini hakikat 'Arsy ini, dan kepadanya akhir Rūḥ al-Amīn; padanya berdiri hakikat Jibrīl 'alaihis-salām — dan ia prinsip majelis tertinggi, serta di dalamnya terdengar keriuhan pena saat mencatat apa yang tiada menerima perubahan dan tiada dibayangkan di halaman pembentukan. Maka berbahagialah yang memahami isyarat 'arsy ini dan kelembutan qudsi. ( Dan 'Arsy ketiga ) 'Arsy al-Majīd; berfirman Yang Maha Tinggi: { Pemilik 'Arsy yang mulia } — dan ia akhir ketinggian, bahkan ruh dikuatkan olehnya. Maka adapun 'Arsy pertama — tiada tabir dan tiada penutup padanya; Allah memuliakan dengannya para nabi dan pilihan, serta padanya berdiri hakikat akal di alam atas, tempat tinggal ruh. Dan 'Arsy al-Majīd — dengannya ruh pergi, dan mengembara di candi bayangan mereka, serta mengatur dalam buatan pengaruhnya, dan berbentuk di cetakan ruh dengan menyaksikan perbedaan rupa di cetakan susunan. Dan kepadanya akhir alam ruh, serta pengambilan limpahan cahaya di cetakan huruf di lingkaran barzakh, untuk nampaknya cap di atas lingkaran dengan menyaksikan keindahan, dan munculnya hukum, serta nampaknya ilmu. Maka ini hakikat tabir — zahirnya zahir kekuasaan, batinnya batin perintah; maka barangsiapa sampai ke dua ujung mengumpulkan dua perkara atas, dan barangsiapa tiada sampai ke hadirat yang melepaskan belenggunya dari kebebasan, serta perginya tilsyam [~] dengan api kerinduan — ia dari ucapan di derajat (kerugian), dan dari pengkhianatan dalam adanya kesyirikan, ditinggalkan oleh badai keindahan di lembah pertarungan. Berfirman Yang Maha Tinggi: { Sekiranya mereka mendengar, niscaya mereka tiada menjawabmu } ayat; maka barangsiapa mengetahui tujuannya tiada mengenakan jubah penipuan, dan tiada bercampur dengan pakaian Iblīs. Dan ini setelah mandi bersih dan penahanan tersembunyi — wajib atasnya pasti jalan naik, dan rupa insan, dengan berdiri di atas firman-Nya: { Wahai penyesalan atas apa yang aku lalaikan di sisi Allah, dan aku dahulu termasuk orang yang memperolok }. Maka kami berlindung kepada Allah dari pengkhianatan dan perubahan zat. ( Dan 'Arsy keempat ) 'Arsy al-Karīm; dan kepadanya akhir bilangan, serta nisbah amal dalam gerakan agung — dan ia rahasia pertolongan untuk menerima jalannya pengaturan, serta dengannya dipelajari hakikat al-Ḥaqq yang dengannya berdiri tiap sesuatu — yang dalam hak nabi adalah pemeliharaan, dan dalam hak wali adalah penjagaan, dan dalam hak ahli taubat adalah rahmat, dan dalam hak pendustanya adalah murka. Maka barangsiapa menggantung di timbangan 'Arsy timbangan amalnya — kelembutan cahayanya unggul, dan ia meluaskan dengan halus jejaknya. Dan ketahuilah bahwa yang sepadan dengan rupa dengan pembalasan 'Arsy — dan tiap atas (punya) 'Arsy untuk tiap bawah; maka nampak dengan yang tinggi rahasia yang rendah, maka rahasia dua alam. Maka 'Arsy memuat manifestasi bashirah-Nya — tiada bashirah bagi-Nya selain dengan konsepsi amal-Nya, dan tiada rupa dan tiada manifestasi hingga ia memutus nisbah rupa di atasnya dan yang rendah, keadaan dan ilmu dan saksi dan kedatangan. ( Dan 'Arsy kelima ) 'Arsy al-'Aẓama; berfirman Yang Maha Tinggi: { Allah, tiada tuhan selain Dia, Rabb 'Arsy yang agung } — dan ia penyaksian perintah dengan hakikat cahaya penglihatan sebagaimana sebelum keberadaannya dan setelah ia beredar di orbit batasnya, maka ia menyaksikan ciptaan yang menakjubkan, dan memberi syafaat bagi yang diseru al-Ḥaqq dari tempat dekat — dan ia 'Arsy yang agung. Dan kepadanya berakhir amalan jasmani, serta renungan intelektual; kepadanya naik kalimat baik, dan dzikir, serta cahaya tersembunyi di rahasia — tanpa huruf berhuruf, dan tiada tanda berlabel, dan tiada batas…"
