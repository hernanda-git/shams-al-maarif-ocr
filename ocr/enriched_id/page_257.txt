English:
— 251 —

...and a blue collyrium and the cane of dharr barh — and this is what is to be written: "Rely, O servants of these Names, upon casting love and affection between So-and-so son of So-and-so, by the right of these Names over you"; and you write this seal that comes [forth]: say "O Uniter of hearts, unite the heart of So-and-so son of So-and-so and So-and-so daughter of So-and-so, by the right of His saying to the heavens and the earth: 'Come willingly or unwillingly' — they said: 'We come willingly' (Q 41:11), and 'I cast upon you love from Me' (Q 20:39) and 'to be made before My eye.' Rely, O servants, upon casting love and affection between the hearts of those in enmity, upon the secret of the two facing [pairs]. Rely, O servants, this day and this hour, upon drawing the hearts of those in enmity to love, by the right of these Names — by the right of Ayyush, Wash, Badūh, Hubb, Wadūd, Hubb — O Badūh, gather love after hatred and union after separation. O Sharāhyā, Adonai, Asbā'ūt, Āl Shādāy — and it is a vow, if you but knew, great! Hayyā al-Wahhā, the calf, the hour ¤ I say: and whoever places this talisman in the wall of a wrongdoer and calls upon him with what follows, the wall is indeed demolished and the master of the house perishes and his gathering is scattered. So fear Allah, exalted, and do not do it except to one who wrongs and harms people by hand and tongue; and beware of doing it for other than its rightful recipient, for you will be called to account for it on the Day of Resurrection. So whoever pardons and makes peace, his reward is upon Allah. And this is what is to be written:

Indonesia:
— 251 —

...dan celak biru serta tebu dharr barh — dan ini yang harus ditulis: "Berserahlah, wahai pelayan nama-nama ini, untuk menebarkan cinta dan kasih sayang antara Si Anu bin Si Anu, demi hak nama-nama ini atas kalian"; dan engkau tulis cap ini yang muncul: ucapkan "Wahai Yang menyatukan hati, satukanlah hati Si Anu bin Si Anu dan Si Anu binti Si Anu, demi hak firman-Nya kepada langit dan bumi: 'Datanglah dengan suka hati atau terpaksa' — keduanya berkata: 'Kami datang dengan suka hati' (Q 41:11), dan 'Aku telah menaruh cinta dari-Ku kepadamu' (Q 20:39) serta 'untuk dibuat di hadapan mata-Ku.' Berserahlah, wahai pelayan, untuk menebarkan cinta dan kasih sayang antara hati orang-orang yang bermusuhan, demi rahasia dua pasang yang berhadapan. Berserahlah, wahai pelayan, hari dan jam ini, untuk menarik hati orang-orang yang bermusuhan pada cinta, demi hak nama-nama ini — demi hak Ayyush, Wash, Badūh, Hubb, Wadūd, Hubb — wahai Badūh, himpunlah cinta setelah kebencian dan persatuan setelah perpisahan. Wahai Sharāhyā, Adonai, Asbā'ūt, Āl Shādāy — dan itu adalah nazar, sekiranya kamu tahu, agung! Hayyā al-Wahhā, anak lembu, saat ¤ Aku berkata: dan barangsiapa menaruh jimat ini di dinding orang zalim dan mendoakannya dengan apa yang berikut, niscaya tembok itu rubuh dan pemilik rumah binasa serta kumpulannya berserak.
