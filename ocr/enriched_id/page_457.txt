English:
— 451 —

"…the provision of the people of the sky and the earth, for they are the people of nearness and the elite of the chosen — so their provision is from whence they are not aware, of what is in the worlds, their upper and lower; and their inward provision they realize its reality, for it is the falling-away of the intermediaries in the pursuits. He said exalted 'So seek the provision from Allah.' So whoever is his standing in the station of the names and the deeds — his provision is beloved from the world of composition; and whoever is his standing by the names of the attributes — his provision is Dominion-like; and whoever is his foot in his station by the names of the meanings of the Essence — his sustenance is from Allah the Exalted without intermediary to Him. The indication of Ibrāhīm (upon him peace) when he saw the wonders of the Dominion and passed the night, said 'He who created me, He guides me, and He who feeds me and gives me drink' — and he intended by that only the removal of the intermediaries in this place. And that Allah created the creatures and appointed their provisions before He created the heavens by a thousand years, and a clarification from His winds that those provisions flow in the existence — so some of it gathered in one place and some of it dispersed. And a man said to Wahb ibn Mālik: 'From where do you eat?' and he pointed to Him in it, and he said to him: 'Everyone knows that.' He said to him: 'That is He who created the winds their flour, and to Allah belong the treasuries of the heavens and the earth.' And the drawing near to Allah by this name is the occupation with the unity and the turning to Allah from the veil of probing by the inward of his state, and he knows that Allah apportioned his provision and measured it, and that he multiply the mention and the litanies for the names. And it has a retreat, and its recitation every day its number multiplied by itself, then he says 'O Allah, provide me provisions,' and that he be watchful in all his states in secret and open. And it has great properties, and its servant is JAHWARĪL, who arranges under his hand many commanders; and whoever persists in its recitation and adds to it His name al-Fattāḥ — Allah eases for him every locked door. And if it is written on a silver tablet and he persists in its recitation — whoever engages the causes, Allah eases for him what he wills; and if it is placed in a shop, his customers multiply and his standing in buying and selling; and if the name of a person agrees and he takes it as a mention, it is a Greatest Name in his right, and he disposes by it. And the discipline is not long — especially eating the lawful, avoiding its like. And this is its figure. And as for the standing mention by it — you say: 'In the name of Allah the Merciful, the Compassionate: O Allah, You are the Guarantor, the Provider, absolutely, the one reaching the provision to everyone of the creatures; glory be to You, Provider of the people of the heavens and the earth by the provisions, and You supplied them with all the spiritualities, and Provider of the people of the land and the sea, and Provider of the physical laws, and Provider of the fetus in its mother's womb from the subtle nourishment and the fine drinks. I ask You that You cause the provisions to flow from the horizons, and expand my breast, and extend to me by Your unveiling of it upon the subtleties of the provision, and that You make them for me a strength from Your generosity, O Generous; and grant me by the subtleties of the familiar, and place them in my provision and my aid, O Lord of the worlds, O Provider; and that You extend to me by them and revive my heart to the everlasting, O Allah, O Provider. There is no servant who recited this…' Allah suffices — He opens upon him. {Section on His name the Exalted, al-Fattāḥ} Know that the meaning of al-Fattāḥ is the One who opens the real doors and pours the opening upon all; and the opening is of two kinds: the opening of knowledge and the opening of everything obscure. And al-Fattāḥ is the One who opens the locks of the Dominion for the insights of His saints, and opens the doors of mercy for the believers, and opens the unseen. He said exalted to His Prophet (upon him peace): 'We have opened to you an open opening.' And the share of the servant…"

Indonesia:
— ٤٥١ —

"…rezeki ahli langit bumi, mereka ahli dekat elit terpilih — rezeki dari tempat tak disedari alam atas bawah; rezeki batin mereka capai hakikatnya, tiada perantara dalam cita. Firman 'Carilah rezeki dari Allah.' Sesiapa berdiri maqam nama perbuatan — rezeki disayangi alam susunan; nama sifat — rezeki Malakut; kaki maqam nama makna Zat — kurnia dari Allah tanpa perantara. Isyarat Ibrahim bila nampak keajaiban Malakut malam kata 'Yang cipta aku tunjuk, yang beri makan minum' — buang perantara di sini. Allah cipta makhluk tetapkan rezeki sebelum langit 1000 tahun, angin mengalir rezeki — sebahagian kumpul sebahagian berselerak. Lelaki tanya Wahb ibn Mālik 'Dari mana makan?' tunjuk pada-Nya 'Semua tahu.' 'Dia cipta angin tepung, Allah khazanah langit bumi.' Dekat Allah dengan nama ini dengan tauhid paling kepadanya dari tudung siasat batin, tahu Allah bahagi rezeki, perbanyak zikir wirid nama. Ada khalwat baca tiap hari darab diri kata 'Ya Allah beri rezeki,' awasi rahsia terbuka. Sifat besar khadam JAHWARĪL atur banyak komander; istiqamah tambah nama al-Fattāḥ Allah mudah setiap pintu tertutup. Tulis loh perak istiqamah sesiapa urus sebab Allah mudah dikehendaki; letak kedai pelanggan bertambah dalam jual beli; kalau nama sepakat jadi zikir Ism A'zam. Riyadhah tak lama — makan halal jauhi syubhat. Inilah rupanya. Zikir berdiri — 'Bismillah: ya Allah Engkau Penjamin Pemberi rezeki mutlak sampai rezeki tiap makhluk; Maha Suci Pemberi rezeki langit bumi, limpah rohani, Pemberi darat laut hukum jasad janin rahim dari makanan halus minuman halus. Kuminta alir rezeki dari ufuk lapangkan dada buka halus rezeki, jadikan kekuatan dari kemurahan ya Mulia; kurnia kebiasaan dalam rezeki bantuan ya Tuhan alam ya Razzāq; hidupkan hati abadi ya Allah ya Razzāq. Tiada hamba baca…' Allah cukup — buka padanya. {Fasal al-Fattāḥ} Makna al-Fattāḥ yang buka pintu hakiki limpah pembukaan pada semua; pembukaan dua: ilmu dan setiap kabur. Fattāḥ buka kunci Malakut untuk basirah wali, pintu rahmat mukmin, buka ghaib. Firman pada Nabi 'Kami buka padamu pembukaan nyata.' Bahagian hamba…"
