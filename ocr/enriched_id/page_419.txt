[Continuation — the Shīn invocation] "And its invocation: the basmala — 'Embrace me, O Allah, with Your gentle graces as You favored Your creation with boons and favors, and draw to me the servant of the letter, dispose him in what I will of the interests You favored — upon me, O Allah, by the disposal of success and deed and increase of intellect: Hiyāsh, Sahām, bi-sābahn, Shahr, I — by the right of Sāh, hasten to me the secret of the great King by the guarding of the wind and the Lord of Moses and Jesus and Dhū-l-Kifl and Job and Muḥammad the Chosen — upon him peace — shaf shafī shaf shu'shaf, answer, O Ishyan, Lord of the worlds; and its implicit concentration: its number Ṣ 2, Ṭalḥ Yās 2 — answer and do such-and-such.' (The letter Tā') — its nature is death, and it is the Alif — Taṣṭaḥ. And among its properties: if a person sees phantoms and dreams, write these letters its number with the implicit concentration and His saying 'Blessed is He in whose hand is the dominion' and carry it — he is safe. And if you write on a piece of lead with the name of whom you will to move from his place, and cast it in it — his departure from it is hastened. And 'adāris-him on tortoise shell and drink from it — the one with stomach trouble sees what pleases him. And it is written for binding tongues and the dumb, and buried under the threshold or given to drink — and whenever he would speak, it grips his heart. And its retreat 28 days — the servant attends, and his name is Wanūyā'īl, his light like the sun; and recite the implicit concentration and invocation, and the incense is Javanese and mastic; and this is its figure. And its invocation: the basmala — 'I have sought mediation with You, O Oft-Returning, O Master of the masters, O Reviver of bones and decay, O Raiser of the dead, O Spreader of the earths and Raiser of the heavens, O Unveiler of the pangs — by the rank of Muḥammad, upon him peace, the chosen, singled out for the greatest intercession — that You subordinate to me the servant of this letter to fulfill my need; You are over every thing powerful. Answer, O servant of this letter, may Allah bless you and upon you — Abwāb, hayā say'alūn 2, Maryamūt 2, glory be to You, there is no god but You, how great is Your affair, and Lahūb, glory be to You — whoever seeks refuge in You is sufficed, and whoever seeks help by You is saved. O Allah, fulfill my need by the F, there is no power nor strength save in Allah, the High, the Great. And its implicit concentration: 'Answer, O King Mar'iyā'īl, by the right of Suhayl, Ṣaqīl, Ṭūsam, Ṭāh, Yamūh, Bawwāb — the hastening, al-waḥā — hayā, may Allah bless you and upon you, amen.' (The letter Thā') — beneficial for fevers; if you write with the implicit concentration on a piece of silver and the one with fever carries it, or erases it and drinks it — he is cured, and it disposes like the Fā'. And if you write it in your palm and recite over it the implicit concentration and invocation and strike with it the chest of whom you will — he is agitated for you. And if you write with the name of whom you will and recite over it the implicit concentration — he inclines to you; and it is an inclination for kings and men of state. And if you employ it — he attends and fulfills your need. And this is its figure. And it has a majestic retreat and recitation of the invocation 41, and likewise the implicit concentration, until the servant attends; and the incense is the seed of nūm, beneficial in a retreat of 40 days, and he incenses with it at the time of recitation — the sought is attained. And its invocation says: 'In the name of Allah, the Merciful, the Compassionate — Your power is established, O Allah, Your existence in the foot of the foot, without how nor likeness; You created the drop and the clot and the lump, and clothed the bones with flesh, and brought out the nature in the soul, and made the sun submitted to what it was drawn to by the choosing of the command; and the implicit concentration — three thiyāl — for the inflaming of the fire of my innermost by the secret of the nature of going in the heart: answer, O Maryā, servant of the letter Thā', by the right of the Cleaver of the grain…'"

[Lanjutan doa Shīn] "Doanya basmalah — 'Peluklah aku wahai Allah dengan kurnia lembut-Mu sebagaimana Engkau kurniakan makhluk dengan nikmat, dan tarikkan kepadaku khadam huruf ini mentasarufkan apa yang kuingini — atasku wahai Allah, demi tasaruf taufiq dan amal serta tambah akal: Hiyāsh, Sahām, bi-sābahn, Shahr, I — demi Sāh, segerakan rahsia Raja Agung dengan penjagaan angin dan Tuhan Musa Isa Zulkifli Ayyub Muhammad yang terpilih — shaf shafī shaf shu'shaf, jawab wahai Ishyan Tuhan sekalian alam; idmár: bilangannya Ṣ 2, Ṭalḥ Yās 2 — jawab buat anu.' (Huruf Tā') — sifatnya mati, ia Alif — Taṣṭaḥ. Sifat: bila orang nampak khayalan bermimpi, tulis huruf bilangannya dengan idmár dan firman 'Maha Suci Yang di tangan-Nya kerajaan' bawa — selamat. Jika tulis timah dengan nama yang dipindah dan baling kepadanya — pergi segera. Tulis pada kulit kura-kura minum — sakit perut nampak yang disukai. Untuk ikat lidah bisu, tanam di ambang atau beri minum — bila hendak cakap pegang jantung. Khalwat 28 hari — khadam hadir nama Wanūyā'īl cahaya macam matahari; idmár doa, ubat Jawa damar. Inilah rupanya. Doanya basmalah — 'Kuminta syafaat-Mu wahai Yang Mengampun, wahai Tuan tuan-tuan, wahai Pembangkit tulang, wahai Pembangkit mati, wahai Yang menghamparkan bumi mengangkat langit, wahai Pembuka pedih — demi martabat Muhammad yang terpilih dengan syafaat agung — taklukkan khadam huruf ini tunaikan hajatku, Engkau Maha Kuasa. Jawab wahai khadam, semoga Allah berkati — Abwāb, hayā say'alūn 2, Maryamūt 2, Maha Suci Engkau tiada tuhan melainkan Engkau, betapa agungmu, dan Lahūb, Maha Suci Engkau — siapa berlindung pada-Mu cukup, siapa memohon pertolongan selamat. Ya Allah tunaikan hajatku demi F, tiada daya melainkan Allah. Idmár: 'Jawab wahai Raja Mar'iyā'īl, demi Suhayl, Ṣaqīl, Ṭūsam, Ṭāh, Yamūh, Bawwāb — segera al-waḥā — hayā semoga Allah berkati, amin.' (Huruf Thā') — baik untuk demam; tulis idmár perak bawa atau padam minum — sembuh, bertasaruf seperti Fā'. Tulis di tapak tangan baca idmár doa pukul dada yang dikehendaki — ia marah. Tulis nama idmár — condong padamu; untuk raja pembesar. Guna — hadir tunaikan. Inilah rupanya. Khalwat agung baca doa 41, idmár hingga hadir; ubat biji nūm untuk khalwat 40 hari. Doanya: 'Dengan nama Allah, kekuasaan-Mu teguh wahai Allah, wujud-Mu tanpa bagaimana, Engkau ciptakan nutfah 'alaq mudghah, balut tulang dengan daging, keluarkan fitrah jiwa, jadikan matahari tunduk — idmár tiga thiyāl — untuk membakar api jiwaku demi rahsia fitrah berjalan di hati: jawab wahai Maryā khadam Thā' demi Yang Membelah biji…'"
