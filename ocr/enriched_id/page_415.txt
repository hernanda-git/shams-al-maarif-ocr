"…and the invocation and the implicit concentration on the door. And if you write on a lead tablet the measure of a palm and place it in the flow of sand — it is praised for him; it has a majestic retreat. And when the servant attends you find his light like the sun and he makes a pact with you for what you will, and recitation of the invocation… and likewise the implicit concentration — he attends, and his name is Ṣafrayā'īl; and if he is slow upon you, conjure him from the letter Nūn — and he comes, disposing for you as you will. And this is his figure. And its invocation: the basmala — [N W R] 'O Allah, my heart and my poetry and my sight and my limbs and my body by the light of Your knowledge by which You illuminated the people of Your obedience — O Illuminator of sun and moon, O Light of all light, O Guide, O Light 2, O Light of everything and its guide — You who split the darkness by Your light. I ask You to illuminate me with lights, O He who answers the distressed when he calls Him and removes the evil. I ask You to send to me the letter Nūn that it come to me in this retreat of mine that I attain from it what my Lord loves — the glittering of the veils' lights and the light of the Creator. Hayā, Yābūn, by Him whose wage is not greater — answer the caller, honoring Nūn and the Pen and what they inscribe, and by the fire and the light and the shadow and the ḥūr and the sāh and the murūr, by the resting-place of the spirits, by Mawliyā Ghūliyān, bi-nūr Yān, Bishūr, Yān 2, 'Aliyūn 2, Ṭalūn, Qahr Yūn, glory be to the Manifest, the Judge of the Day of Reckoning — by the Alif, there is no power nor strength save in Allah, the High, the Great.' And the implicit concentration: 'Answer, O King Ṣafrayā'īl, by the right of Masalsalah, Shalasha', Shahqa', Sardīkh, Mardamakh, Mahlīsh, Fahjīm, Yāh, Yūh 2 — Nūr, no lights — answer and rely, the hastening, the hour — may Allah bless you and upon you.' The incense is narcissus, in repelling obstacles — its light. [The letter Sīn] — dry, in it is heat. And if you write it with the implicit concentration and carry it, one with migraine-headache and pains is cured. And if you write it with the names beginning with S on a silk card and write with it Yā-Sīn and the Wise Qur'an, and carry it — he attains love and acceptance and the binding of the tongue. And if you write on an egg and boil it — 10 — and the woman in childbirth eats it, Allah eases her delivery. And if you write in a vessel and erase with an ointment 5 and wash with it wounds and ulcers and boils — they dry. And if you write its number and carry it, one with sores — they dry. And its name — its servant — you enter the retreat and recite the sickness 90 — and its light descends like the sun and fulfills your need; and the figure is written in the retreat and its servant Ṭahfā'īl attends, disposing for you as you will. And this is its figure. And its invocation: the basmala — 'Peace, a word from a Lord Most Merciful, upon me and upon the righteous servants of Allah. O Allah, You are the Eternal, Allah the Self-Subsisting. O Diyān of the Day of Reckoning — I ask You, O Allah, by Your Names which are the greatest and noblest. I ask You, O Forbearing, O my Master, incline to me and be gentle with me in the hardships and remove them; and have mercy on me with the mercy of the lover, the beloved, by Arūf, O Merciful, by al-Ḥaṣ — manifest to me, O letter Sīn, that I behold You visibly, and fulfill my need in what benefits me of my affair. The hastening — Biṣīr, Ayru al-qalam in the Preserved Tablet, come out and appear, by the right of Ṣāṣ Ṣawṣ Ṣawn, by what they reach from the One, the Eternal — surely your Lord is the Watcher. O Allah, O One, O Eternal — answer, by the right of Solomon son of David, by the Alif, there is no power nor strength save in Allah, the High, the Great — the youth to whom You pointed by a matter of the matters, he did it.' "…dan doa serta idmār di pintu. Jika engkau menulis pada loh timah saiz tapak tangan dan meletakkannya di aliran pasir — ia dipuji; ia mempunyai khalwat agung. Apabila khadam hadir engkau dapati cahayanya seperti matahari dan ia berikat denganmu untuk apa yang dikehendaki, dan bacaan doa… begitu juga idmár — ia hadir, dan namanya Ṣafrayā'īl; dan jika ia lambat kepadamu, panggillah dia dari huruf Nūn — maka ia datang, mentasarufkan untukmu. Dan inilah rupanya. Doanya: basmalah — [N W R] 'Ya Allah, hatiku syairku penglihatanku anggotaku tubuhku dengan cahaya ilmu-Mu yang menerangi ahli ketaatan-Mu — wahai Penerang matahari bulan, wahai Cahaya segala cahaya, wahai Pemberi petunjuk, wahai Cahaya 2, wahai Cahaya segala sesuatu dan petunjuknya — Engkau yang membelah kegelapan dengan cahaya-Mu.
