English:
— 488 —

"…So when the reality of that is clear to you, know that He is the First and the Last, the Manifest and the Hidden; and the firstness is an essential attribute and the Oneness of His existence, and the lastness is an attribute standing for His creation and its subsistence after their perishing, just as He was before their existence by a ruling with which there is not with the first other than Him — for His governance does not entail the ordering of the station and the counting of the number, so that is not with Him; so there is ever other-than-Him, for it is a matter that the wayfarer ends at and to it ended the knowers of the gnostics — the first and the last from the command that He willed and the power that they measured. And the firstness is a report of His ancientness, and the lastness is a report of the impossibility of His non-existence; and that is what al-Shiblī (may Allah be pleased with him) said: 'The letters before the limits, and before the letters' — he pointed to the ancientness, may His glory be exalted; there is no limit to His Essence and no letters in His speech. And al-Junayd (may Allah be pleased with him) was asked about that, so he said: 'It is the singling out of the monotheist and the realization of His oneness and the perfection of His ahadiyyah — that He is the One who did not beget and was not begotten, denying the opposites and the peers of things; nay, He is not likened, nor doubted, nor parabolized, nor depicted — "There is nothing like unto Him, and He is the Hearing, the Seeing." So upon you, O brother, to be first in nearness to Him and last in the act of servitude before Him; for if you are first in rising to Him, He establishes your inward in witnessing the firstness in the Oneness; and if you are last in expending your servitude, He makes you the last end of those brought-near and witnesses to you the realities of the Hereafter over the course of the unveiling of its placing and its subsistence and its perpetuity.' And know that the subtletics of the Oneness are more abundant and subtler than to come out by the unveiling of the expression. And al-Junayd (may Allah be pleased with him) was asked about the Oneness, so he said: 'I heard a speaker saying these two verses:
"And he sang to me from richness * and I sang as he sang;
And we were where they were * and they were where we were."
The questioner said: "Is it the Qur'ān and the excellence?" He said: "No, but the monotheist is more reverent in the Oneness because of the address." So al-Awwal by the meaning of the preceding in His description — that He is the Ancient, who has no beginning and no end and no separation of His existence; and His being first entails that there not be with Him other ancient — [but] if He is last, it does not necessitate that there not be with Him other-than-Him, so He is the one who must have — no vanishing of His ancientness and no severing of His eternality. Exalted be Allah, the One, the Truth, the Most High, above the attributes of the angels and the resembling; exalted be His ahadiyyah above the intercessors; and He is Allah, the One in His ahadiyyah — none other than Him reaches it and none is unified by it but Him. And for that, al-Ṣiddīq (may Allah be pleased with him) said: "Allah did not make for His creation a way to the knowledge of Him but by the incapacity to encompass His knowledge"; and some of them said: "None knew Allah but Allah." And he who draws near to Allah the Exalted by this name becomes one who balances his fancies on the scale of the roots and the rules, outwardly and inwardly, secretly and openly; and look at the first of the world and its opposite, and it is the Hereafter, and look at the station and contemplate the saying of Allah the Exalted: "Those who repent, those who worship…" — the verse. And if you descend with the servitude until you are the lowest of the low in poverty and the servitude of faith, Allah the Exalted gathers for you between the firstness and the lastness. He said exalted in the description of the people of faith: "A troop of the first and a troop of the last." And these two names have no specific mention for the wayfaring of the station, but it is the rectification of the creed; and it behooves the seeker to mention these two names in his firstness, lest they veil you from witnessing the Oneness of the firstness or you see yourself in the Oneness — for if you see yourself in the Oneness, you are a monotheist in yourself, not the reality of the Oneness. And when you wayfare that, upon you is the purifying of the deeds for Allah the Exalted by the degrees of the Oneness and the subtletics of the singling-out, and make for Allah without recompense — for the looking for recompense is vile; we seek refuge in Allah from that and from the helpers of the soul and from the vile traits, and upon you is sincerity in your states, and do not act in a world from the worlds. Indonesia:
— 488 —

"…Maka bila hakikat itu jelas padamu, ketahuilah bahwa Ia Yang Awal dan Yang Akhir, Yang Zahir dan Yang Batin; dan keawalan ialah sifat zat dan tauhid wujud-Nya, dan keakhiran ialah sifat yang tegak bagi ciptaan-Nya serta kekekalannya setelah binasa mereka, sebagaimana Ia ada sebelum wujud mereka dengan suatu hukum yang bersamanya tiada pada yang awal selain Dia — kerana pemerintahan-Nya tidak memerlukan susunan maqam dan hitungan bilangan, maka itu tidak bersama-Nya; maka ada sentiasa selain Dia, kerana ia perkara yang musafir berakhir padanya dan kepadanya berakhir para arif; yang awal dan akhir dari perintah yang dikehendaki dan kuasa yang mereka ukur. Dan keawalan ialah khabar keazalian-Nya, dan keakhiran ialah khabar ketidakmungkinan tiadanya Dia; dan itu yang dikatakan al-Shiblī r.a.: 'Huruf sebelum had, dan sebelum huruf' — ia menunjuk ke azali, maha mulia keagungan-Nya; tiada had bagi Zat-Nya dan tiada huruf pada kalam-Nya. Dan al-Junayd r.a. ditanya tentang itu, maka ia berkata: 'Ia pemisahan ahli tauhid dan realizasi keesaan-Nya serta kesempurnaan ahadiyyah-Nya — bahwa Ia Yang Esa yang tidak beranak dan tidak diperanakkan, menafikan lawan dan sekutu perkara; bahkan Ia tidak diserupakan, tidak diragui, tidak dibanding, tidak digambarkan — "Tiada sesuatupun yang serupa dengan-Nya, dan Ia Yang Maha Mendengar lagi Melihat." Maka atasmu wahai saudara, menjadi awal dalam dekat kepada-Nya dan akhir dalam perbuatan hamba di hadapan-Nya; kerana jika engkau awal dalam bangkit kepada-Nya, Ia tegakkan batinmu dalam menyaksikan keawalan dalam tauhid; dan jika engkau akhir dalam membelanjakan hambamu, Ia jadikan engkau pengakhiran bagi yang didekatkan serta menyaksikan kepadamu hakikat akhirat sepanjang kasyaf penempatannya dan kekekalannya. Dan ketahuilah bahwa halus tauhid lebih banyak dan lebih halus dari terzahir dengan kasyaf ungkapan. Dan al-Junayd r.a. ditanya tentang tauhid, maka ia berkata: 'Aku dengar seorang pembicara mengucapkan dua bait ini:
"Dan ia menyanyi kepadaku dari kekayaan * dan aku menyanyi sebagaimana ia menyanyi;
Dan kami berada di mana mereka berada * dan mereka berada di mana kami berada."
Penanya berkata: "Adakah ia Qur'ān dan keihsanan?" Ia berkata: "Tidak, tetapi ahli tauhid lebih hormat dalam tauhid kerana khitbah." Maka al-Awwal dengan makna yang mendahului dalam sifat-Nya — bahwa Ia Yang Azali, yang tiada awal dan tiada akhir dan tiada pemisahan wujud-Nya; dan keawalan-Nya menuntut bahwa tiada bersama-Nya yang azali lain — [tetapi] bila Ia akhir, tidak menuntut bahwa tiada bersama-Nya selain Dia, maka Ia yang wajib — tiada lenyap keazalian-Nya dan tiada putus kekekalan-Nya. Maha Suci Allah, Yang Esa, Yang Hak, Yang Maha Tinggi, dari sifat malaikat dan penyamaan; maha suci ahadiyyah-Nya dari pemberi syafaat; dan Ia Allah, Yang Esa dalam ahadiyyah-Nya — tiada selain Dia mencapaiinya dan tiada yang ditauhidkan dengannya melainkan Dia. Dan kerana itu al-Ṣiddīq r.a. berkata: "Allah tidak menjadikan bagi makhluk-Nya jalan ke ilmu tentang-Nya melainkan dengan kelemahan mencapai ilmu-Nya"; dan sebahagian mereka berkata: "Tiada yang mengenal Allah melainkan Allah." Dan yang mendekat kepada Allah Yang Maha Tinggi dengan nama ini menjadi yang menimbang khayalannya pada neraca akar dan kaedah, zahir dan batin, rahsia dan terang; dan pandanglah awal alam dan lawannya, iaitu akhirat, serta pandang maqam dan renungkan firman Allah Yang Maha Tinggi: "Orang yang bertaubat, yang beribadah…" — ayat. Dan bila engkau turun dengan hamba sehingga engkau yang terendah dari terendah dalam kefaqiran dan hamba iman, Allah Yang Maha Tinggi himpunkan bagimu antara keawalan dan keakhiran. Firman-Nya pada sifat ahli iman: "Sekumpulan dari yang awal dan sekumpulan dari yang akhir." Dan dua nama ini tiada dzikir khusus bagi perjalanan maqam, tetapi ia pembetulan iktiqad; dan sepatutnya pencari menyebut dua nama ini dalam keawalannya, agar ia tidak menutupmu dari menyaksikan tauhid keawalan atau engkau nampak diri dalam tauhid — kerana bila engkau nampak diri dalam tauhid, engkau ahli tauhid pada dirimu, bukan hakikat tauhid. Dan bila engkau jalani itu, atasmu penyucian amal bagi Allah Yang Maha Tinggi dengan darjat tauhid dan halus pemisahan, serta jadikan untuk Allah tanpa balasan — kerana memandang balasan itu hina; kami berlindung kepada Allah dari itu dan dari penolong nafsu serta sifat hina, dan atasmu ikhlas dalam keadaanmu, dan jangan bertindak dalam alam dari alam.
