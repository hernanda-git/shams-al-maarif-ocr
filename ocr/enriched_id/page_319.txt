And others. And whoever places the names derived from it in a 4-by-4 square upon a silver ring, and seals with it from [Q Q Q Q Q] the Yemeni — he bears heavy loads, and attains its secret at once. ( The Letter Kāf ) It is a hot, moist, speaking letter [K K K K K], auspicious — whatever is written 4 times in a vessel and placed upon the spleen — it is burned and departs. And whoever writes it 21 in a red copper vessel, the Moon safe from malefics on Friday at the hour of Venus, and the Moon conjoined with Jupiter, and carries it with him — Allah settles his love in the hearts of His creatures. And if hung upon a shop — its customers multiply, and its owner is provided from whence he expects not. And it has names by which it is invoked: "O Allah, indeed I ask You — O Schemer, O Sufficient, O Generous, O Generous — by what You lodged in the letter Kāf of the stored secrets and the hidden lights — that You subject to me the servants of this letter in what You commanded them. Indeed You are over all things Able." ( The Letter Lām ) It is a cold, speaking, auspicious letter; and from the secret of its extension is the hidden subtle (grace), and its derivation is from the name 'Laṭīf' (Subtle); and its properties — whoever writes it 23 times upon a tin sheet on Thursday the 14th of the month, and if it is in Ramaḍān it is better, and carries it upon his head — Allah drives away from him every disliked thing, saves him from every distress, and secures him from every fear and trial. And there is written around the array: { And He is with His servants, Subtle } (the verse), and it is upon a gold ring-stone, and the engraver is fasting — so no one seals with it but Allah honors him with His hidden subtlety, secures him from every disliked thing, saves him from every distress, and secures him from deceit; and its supplication is subtle. ( The Letter Mīm ) It is a speaking letter, hot and dry, with some moisture; and it has properties in benefit and harm; and it has a form written Arabic and Indian — written 24 times in a square, each line thus. And if written upon a citron-wood tablet, and hung upon one who has colic — he is cured. And whoever places its numbers falling upon it in a square in pure paper on Monday at the Moon's hour, by the name of a dear one — the hand of love (acts), so he cannot bear patience from him for an hour. And it has names by which it is invoked: "O Allah, indeed I ask You — O King, O King, O Granter of security, O Granter of security, O Guardian, O Supreme — etc. the letters whose first is Mīm, and they are 40 names — I ask You by what You lodged in the letter Mīm of the stored secrets and the hidden lights — that You subject to me Your noble angels. Indeed You are over all things Able." ( The Letter Nūn ) It is a speaking, cold, dry letter, with some moisture; and it is a speaker in the element of air, and the eye in the element of water. And if written upon the brow of the afflicted — it burns, drives him away. And know — that among the letters of the alphabet are 3 of the secret of Allah's extension and His Greatest Name which is read forward and backward, as His saying, exalted: { And your Lord is Greater }, and His saying: { Each in a orbit swimming }; so if cut letters are written they are read forward and backward; and likewise the Mīm, Nūn, and Wāw — for they read them thus for the abundance of their secrets. Dan lainnya. Dan barangsiapa meletakkan nama yang diturunkan darinya dalam petak 4x4 di atas cincin perak, serta menegnya dari [Q Q Q Q Q] Yaman — ia memikul beban berat, dan mencapai rahasianya seketika. ( Huruf Kāf ) Ia huruf panas lembap berucap [K K K K K], membawa keberuntungan — apa yang ditulis 4 kali di dalam bejana dan diletakkan di limpa — ia terbakar dan pergi. Dan barangsiapa menulisnya 21 di bejana tembaga merah, Bulan aman dari bintang celaka hari Jumat saat Venus, dan Bulan bersatu dengan Musytari, lalu membawanya — Allah menaruh cintanya di hati makhluk-Nya. Dan apabila digantung di toko — pelanggannya bertambah, dan pemiliknya dirizki dari tempat tiada disangka. Dan ia memiliki nama yang dipohon dengannya: "Ya Allah, sesungguhnya aku memohon pada-Mu — wahai Kayd, wahai Kāfī, wahai Karīm, wahai Karīm — dengan apa yang Engkau letakkan pada huruf Kāf dari rahasia tersimpan dan cahaya terpendam — semoga Engkau tundukkan padaku pelayan huruf ini dalam apa yang Engkau perintahkan. Sesungguhnya Engkau Maha Kuasa atas segala sesuatu." ( Huruf Lām ) Ia huruf dingin berucap yang membawa keberuntungan; dan dari rahasia bentangannya adalah kelembutan tersembunyi, serta turunannya dari nama "Laṭīf"; dan sifatnya — barangsiapa menulisnya 23 kali di atas lembaran timah hari Kamis tanggal 14 bulan, dan apabila di bulan Ramaḍān lebih baik, lalu membawanya di kepalanya — Allah menjauhkannya dari setiap yang tidak disukai, menyelamatkannya dari setiap kesusahan, dan mengamankannya dari setiap takut dan fitnah. Dan di sekeliling susunan ditulis: { Dan Dia Maha Penyantun pada hamba-hamba-Nya } ayat, dan ia di atas permata cincin emas, serta pengukirnya berpuasa — maka tiada yang menegnya melainkan Allah memuliakannya dengan kelembutan tersembunyi-Nya, mengamankannya dari setiap yang tidak disukai, menyelamatkannya dari setiap kesusahan, dan mengamankannya dari tipu daya; dan doanya lembut. ( Huruf Mīm ) Ia huruf berucap, panas kering, dengan sedikit lembap; dan ia memiliki sifat dalam manfaat dan mudarat; serta bentuknya ditulis Arab dan India — ditulis 24 kali dalam petak, tiap baris begini. Dan apabila ditulis di atas lempeng kayu limau, dan digantung pada yang ada kolik — ia sembuh. Dan barangsiapa menurunkan bilangannya yang jatuh padanya dalam petak di atas kertas bersih hari Senin saat Bulan, dengan nama orang yang dicintai — tangan cinta (berlaku), maka ia tiada sanggup sabar darinya sejam. Dan ia memiliki nama yang dipohon dengannya: "Ya Allah, sesungguhnya aku memohon pada-Mu — wahai Malik, wahai Malik, wahai Mu'min, wahai Mu'min, wahai Muhaemin, wahai Mutakabbir — dst. huruf yang awalnya Mīm, dan itu 40 nama — aku memohon dengan apa yang Engkau letakkan pada huruf Mīm dari rahasia tersimpan dan cahaya terpendam — semoga Engkau tundukkan padaku malaikat-Mu yang mulia. Sesungguhnya Engkau Maha Kuasa atas segala sesuatu." ( Huruf Nūn ) Ia huruf berucap, dingin kering, dengan sedikit lembap; dan ia berbicara di unsur udara, serta mata di unsur air. Dan apabila ditulis di dahi orang yang terkena — ia terbakar, mengusirnya. Dan ketahuilah — bahwa di antara huruf hija' ada 3 dari rahasia limpahan Allah dan nama-Nya yang paling agung yang dibaca maju dan mundur, seperti firman-Nya: { Dan Rabbmu lebih besar }, serta firman-Nya: { Masing-masing di orbit berenang }; maka apabila huruf terpotong ditulis ia dibaca maju dan mundur; dan demikian pula Mīm, Nūn, dan Wāw — karena mereka membacanya demikian untuk banyaknya rahasia mereka.
