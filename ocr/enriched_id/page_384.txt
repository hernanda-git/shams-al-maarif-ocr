Then you take of silver and melt it, and cast upon it like a third of it of tin, and they are wrapped with three times their amount of qiyāq — so the moon is a part and tin is a part. And you melt the silver and cast upon it like it of tin; so when they mix, pour them upon the ra'wāq after it has been wiped on a shard with something of oil, and stir it with a rod upon the ra'wāq. Then you take it and sublimate it, and return the upper upon the lower, and sublimate it repeatedly until it calcines and is prepared and all of it sublimes. So when the form becomes one, a cloth — you prepared it with white quicksilver — then spirit and body have gathered. Then add to them the sublimated white pure soul, like half of the body and spirit combined, until it equals the body and spirit. Then grind all of them until they mix finely by grinding; then these mentioned mixtures are irrigated from the sharp water of white vitriol used in them before — for three days they are irrigated by it and roasted in the sun or on a gentle tepid fire; and repeat upon them the grinding and roasting as preceded, until you see it no longer accepts water. Then immerse it until the grinding is complete — and it is the second cooking that runs upon the heated plate. So when this inlay is complete upon this description, introduce it then into vinegar in a bottle closed at the head with thin ice; place that in hot dung used for that specifically — and if you renew the dung each week, three weeks or four, it is best, for it makes its white in this period or less or more according to the heat in the dung. And if the dung is absent, cast it into the bath of the sages known as the pot and water. So when this inlay dissolves and is sound, then you whiten what you will of copper plates; and if you wish figures written or unwritten, dip them in this broth, and they whiten like pure silver and do not change even if smelted a thousand times. And if you coagulate this broth and dissolve it a second time and use it once upon the former operation, it is confirmed; and if you coagulate it after dissolving and repeat upon it the dissolution and coagulation, it is a complete elixir that whitens a mass of 300 mithqāls of red copper, and coagulates quicksilver into pure moon, and turns tin into pure silver, and stops fever and flux. So this is the working of this broth, and it is a particular elixir in which there is no doubt among the scholars of the craft. And if in place of silver you enter gold or copper or purified lead, and you chew this management alike in the wiring and sublimation, and put in the dyed water in place of orpiment red sulfur and yellow marcasite, and in place of egg-white its yolk, and the spirit for all and the sublimates are one, and the natron and magnesia in the boiled water with which the bare earth is taken — and with the hair is added blood, and the salts are left as they are, and the alum in the mentioned urine — and he reckons it, and reaches by it the sounding and coagulation according to what you say; it is confirmed for deliverance and fever, and turns quicksilver into pure gold. So understand. ( And this is an inlay ) found in the saddlebag of a noble man, written in a script of glorification, and his father bestowed it from the people of merit, and it is sound. You take of white rahj and the collyrium oval, and red ra'wāq fed with something of qalamī rhubarb and zinjifar, one center a part; and they are ground thoroughly with oil, and incubated with mastic stones in the midst of a gharaqiyya fire; and a little is thrown from it upon lead until it reddens, and is added by a tenth. So this is easy. And as for the other craft and its knowledge — I shall awaken you and confirm you from the secrets of wisdom, and inform you of matters the sages concealed. So I say: ( A chapter on the knowledge of the noble stone and what it has of properties, secrets, symbols, and indications )

Indonesia:

Lalu engkau ambil perak dan lebur, serta tuang padanya sepertiga darinya timah, dan dibungkus tiga kali jumlahnya qiyāq — maka bulan adalah bagian dan timah bagian. Dan engkau lebur perak dan tuang padanya sepadannya timah; maka saat bercampur, tuang pada ra'wāq setelah dilap pada pecahan dengan sedikit minyak, dan aduk dengan batang di atas ra'wāq. Lalu ambil dan sublimasikan, serta kembalikan atas pada bawah, dan sublimasi berulang hingga terkalsin dan disiapkan dan seluruhnya naik. Maka saat bentuk jadi satu, kain — engkau siapkan dengan raksa putih — maka roh dan tubuh berkumpul. Lalu tambah pada mereka jiwa putih murni yang disublimasi, seperti separuh tubuh dan roh digabung, hingga menyamai tubuh dan roh. Lalu giling semuanya hingga bercampur halus; lalu campuran ini disiram air tajam vitriol putih yang dipakai sebelumnya — selama tiga hari disiram dan disangrai di matahari atau api lembut hangat; dan ulangi giling dan sangrai seperti lalu, hingga tak lagi menyerap air. Lalu rendam hingga giling tuntas — dan itu pemasakan kedua yang berjalan di pelat dipanaskan. Maka saat selai ini tuntas, masukkan ke dalam cuka dalam botol ditutup es tipis; letakkan di kotoran panas khusus; dan bila diperbarui tiap pekan, tiga atau empat pekan lebih baik, sebab memutihkan dalam masa itu atau kurang/lebih menurut panas. Dan bila tak ada kotoran, masukkan ke pemandian cerdik ("pot dan air"). Maka saat selai larut dan sehat, engkau putihkan pelat tembaga; dan bila mau gambar bertulis/tak bertulis, celupkan ke kuah ini, mereka putih seperti perak murni dan tak berubah meski dilebur seribu kali. Dan bila engkau gumpalkan kuah ini dan larutkan kedua kalinya serta pakai sekali pada amalan lalu, ia teguh; dan bila gumpal setelah larut dan ulangi larut-gumpal, ia eliksir sempurna yang memutihkan massa 300 mithqāl tembaga merah, dan menggumpal raksa jadi bulan murni, serta ubah timah jadi perak murni, dan hentikan demam dan flux. Maka ini kerja kuah ini, eliksir khusus tanpa keraguan di kalangan cerdik kerajinan. Dan bila ganti perak dengan emas, tembaga, atau timah hitam murni, serta kunyah tata ini sama pada pengawatan dan sublimasi, dan taruh di air dicelup ganti orpimen belerang merah dan markasit kuning, ganti putih telur kuningnya, dan roh untuk semua serta sublimat satu, dan natron dan magnesia di air rebus dengan bumi telanjang — dan dengan rambut ditambah darah, garam dibiarkan, tawas di urine disebut; dan ia hitung, serta capai bunyi dan gumpal menurut katamu; ia teguh untuk keselamatan dan demam, serta ubah raksa jadi emas murni. Maka pahamilah. ( Dan ini selai ) ditemukan di bawaan lelaki mulia, ditulis huruf tasbih, dan ayahnya anugerahkan dari ahli utama, dan ia sah. Ambil rahj putih, kolirium oval, ra'wāq merah diberi rhubarb qalamī dan zinjifar, satu pusat bagian; digiling halus dengan minyak, dan dieram dengan batu mastic di api gharaqiyya; sedikit dilemparkan pada timah hitam hingga merah, dan ditambah sepersepuluh. Maka ini mudah. Adapun kerajinan lain dan pengetahuannya — akan kubangunkan dan teguhkan dari rahasia hikmah, serta kabarkan hal yang cerdik sembunyikan. Maka kuberi: ( Bab pengetahuan batu mulia dan sifat, rahasia, simbol, serta isyaratnya )
