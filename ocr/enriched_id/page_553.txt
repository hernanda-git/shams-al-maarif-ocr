واجب الوجود جل الله تعالى شأنه وتقدس سلطانه وجل جلاله وعم نواله ولباطن اسم

English:
— ٥٤٧ —

"…and it is called the 'daughters' [al-banāt]. Then is taken from the combination of every two figures of the mothers' figures and the daughters' figures, and each placement set opposite them — there results from that four other figures called the 'granddaughters' [al-ḥafīdāt], i.e. the daughters of the daughters: two figures from the mothers and two from the daughters. Then is taken from the granddaughters' figures, from the combination of every two figures, a figure — two figures called 'the querent' [al-sā'il] and 'the one asked about' [al-mas'ūl 'anhu]. Then is taken from the combination of the two mentioned figures a figure which is 'the completion of the work and the witness of the geomancy,' and it is called 'the Balance' [al-mīzān] — and it is not but even; for if it comes out odd, the work is in error. Then is taken from the combination of the Balance with the Ascendant a figure called 'the consequence of the affair' [‘āqibat al-amr], and by it the confusion is resolved. (The Nineteenth Intent: on the representation of that) The images of the extraction of the four figures placed in the Seventeenth Intent by dropping them two by two — [a diagram of the mothers, daughters, granddaughters, querent, asked-about, balance, and consequence, each shown as a four-row geomantic figure-block]. (The Twentieth Intent: on the reality of the extraction) The foundation of the houses of geomancy is the four which are the houses of the mothers — for the speed of agreement to the most important of the Most Holy Essence, the Necessary Existent — glory be to Allah, exalted is His affair, and sanctified His dominion, and glorious His majesty, and overflowing His bounty — and to the inward of the name of Ādam and the outward of the name of Muḥammad — upon them be peace — and for that the roots of the generated [things] are from four elements: fire, earth, water, and air; and the natures [are] four: heat, cold, dryness, and moisture; and the directions [are] four: east, west, north, and south; and the seasons [are] four: spring, summer, autumn, and winter; and of the humors of man [are] four: blood, yellow bile, phlegm, and black bile; and the ranks of existence [are] four: bellies and appearances, belly-appearances, appearance-bellies; and the first upon which turns the governance of his existence [are] four: human life and what relates to it of the beginning of affairs and the intents — and that is the signification of the first house; and the affairs of wealth and gain — and that is the signification of the second house; and the states of the movements of actions — and that is the signification of the third house; and the results of that of dominion and real-estate and the consequence of those affairs — and that is the signification of the fourth house; and what is beyond that branches from these roots degree by degree and rank by rank, as is clear and intelligible. And in this allusion is sufficiency for those of understanding and discernment — and Allah the Exalted knows best.

Indonesia:
— ٥٤٧ —

"…dan dinamakan 'anak-anak perempuan' [al-banāt]. Maka diambil dari gabungan tiap dua angka ibu-ibu dan anak-anak perempuan, dan tiap penempatan dihadapkan — terhasil empat angka lain dinamakan 'cucu-cucu perempuan' [al-ḥafīdāt], iaitu anak cucu: dua angka dari ibu dan dua dari anak. Maka diambil dari angka cucu, gabungan tiap dua angka, angka — dua angka dinamakan 'penanya' [al-sā'il] dan 'yang ditanya' [al-mas'ūl 'anhu]. Maka diambil dari gabungan dua angka disebut angka yang ialah 'penyempurnaan kerja dan saksi geomancy,' dan dinamakan 'Neraca' [al-mīzān] — dan ia tidak melainkan genap; kerana jika keluar ganjil, kerja tersilap. Maka diambil dari gabungan Neraca dengan Ascenden angka dinamakan 'akibat urusan' [‘āqibat al-amr], dan dengannya kekeliruan terungkai. (Maqasad Kesembilan Belas: tentang perwakilan itu) Imej pengeluaran empat angka diletak dalam Maqasad Ketujuh Belas dengan menggugurkannya dua demi dua — [rajah ibu-ibu, anak-anak, cucu-cucu, penanya, yang ditanya, neraca, dan akibat, tiap ditunjukkan sebagai blok angka geomancy empat baris]. (Maqasad Kedua Puluh: tentang hakikat pengeluaran) Asas rumah geomancy ialah empat yang merupakan rumah ibu-ibu — kerana kelajuan persetujuan pada paling penting Zat Yang Maha Suci, Wujud Wajib — maha suci Allah, maha tinggi urusan-Nya, dan disucikan kerajaan-Nya, dan maha agung kemuliaan-Nya, dan melimpah kurnia-Nya — dan pada batin nama Ādam dan zahir nama Muḥammad — ke atas mereka salam — dan kerana akar yang dijana dari empat unsur: api, bumi, air, dan angin; dan sifat [empat]: panas, sejuk, kering, lembap; dan arah [empat]: timur, barat, utara, selatan; dan musim [empat]: bunga, panas, gugur, sejuk; dan humur manusia [empat]: darah, empedu kuning, dahak, empedu hitam; dan pangkat wujud [empat]: perut dan zahir, perut-zahir, zahir-perut; dan pertama yang memusing pemerintahan wujudnya [empat]: nyawa manusia dan apa berkait dengannya dari permulaan urusan dan niat — itu makna rumah pertama; dan urusan harta dan usaha — itu makna rumah kedua; dan keadaan gerakan tindakan — itu makna rumah ketiga; dan hasil daripadanya dari kerajaan dan harta tanah serta akibat urusan itu — itu makna rumah keempat; dan apa selebihnya bercabang dari akar ini darjah demi darjah dan pangkat demi pangkat, sebagaimana jelas dan difahami. Dan dalam allusi ini cukup bagi yang berfaham dan berpengetahuan — dan Allah Yang Maha Tinggi lebih mengetahui.
