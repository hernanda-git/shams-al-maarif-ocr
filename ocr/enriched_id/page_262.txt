English:
— 256 —

"My God, there is no god but You, and to You be praise. My God, no dominion but Yours. My God, no one is like You. My God, no creator but You. My God, no proof but You. My God, no compeller but You. My God, no subjugator but You. My God, no provider but You. My God, no able one but You. My God, no hearer but You. My God, no helper but You. My God, the Sufficient for guidance. My God, You are the best of those who open. My God, You are the Turner of hearts. My God, You are the God of the heavens and the earth. My God, You are the Remover of cares. My God, You are the Merciful, the Compassionate. My God, You are the best of creators. My God, You are the best of forgivers. My God, You are the best of helpers. My God, You are the best of providers. My God, You are the Sufficient, the Healer. My God, You are the Giver, the Originator. My God, You make the night enter the day and the day enter the night. My God, You are the Near, the Answerer. My God, You are the Oft-Returning, the Bestower. My God, You are the Lord of lords. My God, You are the Cause of causes. My God, You are the Master of masters. My God, You are the Most High in degrees. My God, You are the Cleaver of the heavens. My God, You are the Raiser, the Inheritor. My God, You are the Help of those who seek help. My God, You are the Creator, the Compeller. My God, You are the Right-Guided. My God, You are the Patient, the Eternal. My God, You are the Subduer, the Subjugator. My God, You are the One, the Eternal. My God, You are the Thankful, the Glorious. My God, You are the Finder. My God, You are the Light, the Guide. My God, You are the Judge, the Just. My God, You are the Overseer, the Mighty, the Compeller. My God, You are the Supremely Great. My God, You are the Creator, the Evolver" — to the end of the sūra. So when you resolve, rely upon Allah — for Allah loves those who rely.

"O Allah, incline toward me the hearts of Your servants, sons of Adam and daughters of Eve — male and female, free and slave, small and great — with lasting love, affection, tenderness, and mercy; and draw their hearts to me, and guard me from the evil they conceal and plot against me, and repel from me their guile and evil. O Allah, by the sanctity of what I have recited, I ask You to show me Your sanctuary by Your generosity and convey me to visiting Your intimate Muhammad, may Allah bless him and grant him peace — O Fulfiller of needs, O Responder of supplications. O Allah, O Lord of the worlds, O Merciful, O Compassionate, O Peace, O Granter of security, O Overseer, O Mighty, O Compeller, O Supremely Great, O Provider, O Opener, O Knowing, O Extender, O Exalter, O Honorer, O Humiliator, O Hearer, O Seer, O Subtle, O High, O Great, O Thankful, O Guardian, O Sustainer, O Reckoner, O Majestic, O Generous, O Watchful, O Answerer, O Vast, O Gatherer, O Rich, O Enricher, O Everlasting — O Light of everything and its guide, You who split the darknesses by Your light. O High, lofty above everything in loftiness. O Allah, I ask You that You dispose for me the servants of this noble sūra to be my aid in all I want and seek, by Your right over them and their obedience to You — 'Entrust and obey and answer; and by the right of the secrets in it, and whosoever of you lags, burn him with fire — hayā, the haste, the moment — and whoso answers not the caller of Allah is not powerless on earth' — { Answer and rely in what I command you, by the right of this noble sūra and its sanctity to you: hayā al-waḥā, the haste, the moment }." (The blessed supplication is complete) so know the worth of what has come to you, guard it, and protect it from other than its people — for it is of red sulphur. And it is finished.

Indonesia:
— 256 —

"Tuhanku, tiada tuhan selain Engkau, dan bagi-Mu segala puji. Tuhanku, tiada kerajaan selain milik-Mu. Tuhanku, tiada yang serupa dengan-Mu. Tuhanku, tiada pencipta selain Engkau. Tuhanku, tiada bukti selain pada-Mu. Tuhanku, tiada yang memaksa selain Engkau. Tuhanku, tiada yang menundukkan selain Engkau. Tuhanku, tiada yang memberi rizki selain Engkau. Tuhanku, tiada yang berkuasa selain Engkau. Tuhanku, tiada yang mendengar selain Engkau. Tuhanku, tiada penolong selain Engkau. Tuhanku, Yang Mencukupi bagi petunjuk. Tuhanku, Engkaulah sebaik-baik Yang Membuka. Tuhanku, Engkaulah Pembolak-balik hati. Tuhanku, Engkaulah Tuhan langit dan bumi. Tuhanku, Engkaulah Penyingkap kesusahan. Tuhanku, Engkaulah Ar-Raḥmān Ar-Raḥīm. Tuhanku, Engkaulah sebaik-baik pencipta. Tuhanku, Engkaulah sebaik-baik pengampun. Tuhanku, Engkaulah sebaik-baik penolong. Tuhanku, Engkaulah sebaik-baik pemberi rizki. Tuhanku, Engkaulah Yang Mencukupi lagi Menyembuhkan. Tuhanku, Engkaulah Pemberi lagi Permula. Tuhanku, Engkau memasukkan malam ke dalam siang dan siang ke dalam malam. Tuhanku, Engkaulah Yang Dekat lagi Memperkenankan. Tuhanku, Engkaulah Yang Menerima taubat lagi Pemberi karunia. Tuhanku, Engkaulah Rabbul arbab. Tuhanku, Engkaulah Sebab segala sebab. Tuhanku, Engkaulah Sayyidul sadah. Tuhanku, Engkaulah Yang Maha Tinggi derajatnya. Tuhanku, Engkaulah Yang Membelah langit. Tuhanku, Engkaulah Yang Membangkitkan lagi Mewarisi. Tuhanku, Engkaulah Penolong orang yang meminta tolong. Tuhanku, Engkaulah Pencipta lagi Yang Memaksa. Tuhanku, Engkaulah Yang Memberi petunjuk. Tuhanku, Engkaulah Yang Sabar lagi Qadim. Tuhanku, Engkaulah Yang Menundukkan lagi Maha Menundukkan. Tuhanku, Engkaulah Yang Esa lagi Tempat bergantung. Tuhanku, Engkaulah Yang Mensyukuri lagi Mulia. Tuhanku, Engkaulah Yang Maha Besar. Tuhanku, Engkaulah Pencipta lagi Yang Menjadikan" — hingga akhir surah. Maka apabila engkau menetapkan, bertawakallah pada Allah — sesungguhnya Allah mencintai orang yang bertawakal.

"Ya Allah, cintakanlah padaku hati hamba-hamba-Mu, anak Adam dan anak Hawa — laki dan perempuan, merdeka dan hamba, kecil dan besar — dengan cinta kekal, kasih sayang, belas kasihan, dan rahmat; serta tarik hati mereka padaku, dan peliharalah aku dari kejahatan yang mereka sembunyikan dan rencanakan bagiku, serta tolaklah tipu daya dan kejahatan mereka dariku. Ya Allah, dengan kemuliaan apa yang kubaca, aku memohon Engkau memperlihatkan haram-Mu dengan kemurahan-Mu dan menyampaikan aku menziarahi kekasih-Mu Muḥammad shallallāhu 'alaihi wa sallam — wahai Pemenuh keperluan, wahai Pembali doa. Ya Allah, ya Rabbul 'alamin, ya Ar-Raḥmān, ya Ar-Raḥīm, ya As-Salām, ya Al-Mu'min, ya Al-Muhaymin, ya Al-'Azīz, ya Al-Jabbār, ya Al-Mutakabbir, ya Ar-Razzāq, ya Al-Fattāḥ, ya Al-'Alīm, ya Al-Bāsiṭ, ya Ar-Rāfi', ya Al-Mu'izz, ya Al-Mudhill, ya As-Samī', ya Al-Baṣīr, ya Al-Laṭīf, ya Al-'Alī, ya Al-'Aẓīm, ya Asy-Syakūr, ya Al-Ḥafīẓ, ya Al-Muqīt, ya Al-Ḥasīb, ya Al-Jalīl, ya Al-Karīm, ya Ar-Raqīb, ya Al-Mujīb, ya Al-Wāsi', ya Al-Jāmi', ya Al-Ghanī, ya Al-Mughnī, ya Al-Bāqī — wahai Cahaya segala sesuatu dan petunjuknya, Engkau yang membelah kegelapan dengan cahaya-Mu. Wahai Yang Maha Tinggi lagi luhur di atas segala sesuatu. Ya Allah, aku memohon pada-Mu semoga Engkau tundukkan bagiku pelayan surah mulia ini menjadi penolongku dalam segala yang kuinginkan dan kucari, dengan hak-Mu atas mereka dan ketaatan mereka pada-Mu — 'Bertawakallah, taatlah, dan penuhilah; dan dengan hak rahasia di dalamnya, barangsiapa di antara kamu lalai, bakarlah ia dengan api — hayā, segera, saat ini — dan barangsiapa tiada membalas seruan Allah, ia tiada berdaya di bumi' — { Penuhilah dan bertawakallah dalam apa yang aku perintahkan padamu, dengan hak surah mulia ini dan kemuliaannya padamu: hayā al-waḥā, segera, saat ini }." (Doa diberkati telah sempurna) maka ketahuilah kadar apa yang sampai padamu, peliharalah, dan jagalah dari selain ahlinya — karena ia dari kibrīt ahmar (belerang merah). Dan selesai.
