«اللَّهُمَّ، اعْطِفْ عَلَيَّ قُلُوبَ عِبَادِكَ مِنْ أَوْلَادِ آدَمَ وَبَنَاتِ حَوَّاءَ — مِنْ كُلِّ ذَكَرٍ وَأُنْثَى وَحُرٍّ وَعَبْدٍ وَصَغِيرٍ وَكَبِيرٍ — بِالمَحَبَّةِ الدَّائِمَةِ وَالمَوَدَّةِ وَالرَّأْفَةِ وَالرَّحْمَةِ؛ وَاجْلُبْ لِي قُلُوبَهُمْ، وَاحْفَظْنِي مِنْ شَرِّ مَا يُضْمِرُونَ وَيُدَبِّرُونَ لِي، وَادْفَعْ عَنِّي مَكْرَهُمْ وَشَرَّهُمْ. اللَّهُمَّ، بِحُرْمَةِ مَا تَلَوْتُ، أَسْأَلُكَ أَنْ تُرِيَنِي حَرَمَكَ بِكَرَمِكَ، وَتُبَلِّغَنِي زِيَارَةَ قَرِينِكَ مُحَمَّدٍ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ — يَا قَاضِيَ الحَاجَاتِ، يَا مُجِيبَ الدَّعَوَاتِ.

يَا اللَّهُ، يَا رَبَّ العَالَمِينَ، يَا رَحْمَنُ، يَا رَحِيمُ، يَا سَلَامُ، يَا مُؤْمِنُ، يَا مُهَيْمِنُ، يَا عَزِيزُ، يَا جَبَّارُ، يَا مُتَكَبِّرُ، يَا رَزَّاقُ، يَا فَتَّاحُ، يَا عَلِيمُ، يَا بَاسِطُ، يَا رَافِعُ، يَا مُعِزُّ، يَا مُذِلُّ، يَا سَمِيعُ، يَا بَصِيرُ، يَا لَطِيفُ، يَا عَلِيُّ، يَا عَظِيمُ، يَا شَكُورُ، يَا حَفِيظُ، يَا مُقِيتُ، يَا حَسِيبُ، يَا جَلِيلُ، يَا كَرِيمُ، يَا رَقِيبُ، يَا مُجِيبُ، يَا وَاسِعُ، يَا جَامِعُ، يَا غَنِيُّ، يَا مُغْنِي، يَا بَاقِي — يَا نُورَ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ وَهُدَاهُ، أَنْتَ الَّذِي فَلَقْتَ الظُّلُمَاتِ بِنُورِكَ. يَا عَلِيُّ الشَّامِخِ فَوْقَ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ عُلُوًّا.

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ يَا اللَّهُ أَنْ تَسْخَرَ لِي خُدَّامَ هَذِهِ السُّورَةِ الشَّرِيفَةِ يَكُونُونَ لِي عَوْنًا فِي كُلِّ مَا أُرِيدُ وَأَطْلُبُ بِحَقِّكُمْ عَلَيْهِمْ وَطَاعَتِهِمْ لَدَيْكُمْ — «تَوَكَّلُوا وَأَطِيعُوا وَأَجِيبُوا؛ وَبِحَقِّ مَا فِيهَا مِنَ الأَسْرَارِ، وَمَنْ تَخَلَّفَ مِنْكُمْ أُحْرِقْ بِالنَّارِ — هَيَا العَجَلَ الوَحَا السَّاعَةَ — وَمَنْ لَا يُجِيبْ دَاعِيَ اللَّهِ فَلَيْسَ بِمُعْجِزٍ فِي الأَرْضِ» — { أَجِيبُوا وَتَوَكَّلُوا فِيمَا آمُرُكُمْ بِهِ بِحَقِّ هَذِهِ السُّورَةِ الشَّرِيفَةِ وَحُرْمَتِهَا لَدَيْكُمْ: هَيَا الوَحَا العَجَلَ السَّاعَةَ }».

( تُمَّتِ الدَّعْوَةُ المُبَارَكَةُ ) فَاعْرِفْ قَدْرَ مَا صَارَ إِلَيْكَ، وَاحْفَظْهَا، وَاصْنُهَا مِنْ غَيْرِ أَهْلِهَا — فَهِيَ مِنَ الكِبْرِيتِ الأَحْمَرِ. وَتُمَّتْ.

English:
— 256 —

"My God, there is no god but You, and to You be praise. My God, no dominion but Yours. My God, no one is like You. My God, no creator but You. My God, no proof but You. My God, no compeller but You. My God, no subjugator but You. My God, no provider but You. My God, no able one but You. My God, no hearer but You. My God, no helper but You. My God, the Sufficient for guidance. My God, You are the best of those who open. My God, You are the Turner of hearts. My God, You are the God of the heavens and the earth. My God, You are the Remover of cares. My God, You are the Merciful, the Compassionate. My God, You are the best of creators. My God, You are the best of forgivers. My God, You are the best of helpers. My God, You are the best of providers. My God, You are the Sufficient, the Healer. My God, You are the Giver, the Originator. My God, You make the night enter the day and the day enter the night. My God, You are the Near, the Answerer. My God, You are the Oft-Returning, the Bestower. My God, You are the Lord of lords. My God, You are the Cause of causes. My God, You are the Master of masters. My God, You are the Most High in degrees. My God, You are the Cleaver of the heavens. My God, You are the Raiser, the Inheritor. My God, You are the Help of those who seek help. My God, You are the Creator, the Compeller. My God, You are the Right-Guided. My God, You are the Patient, the Eternal. My God, You are the Subduer, the Subjugator. My God, You are the One, the Eternal. My God, You are the Thankful, the Glorious. My God, You are the Finder. My God, You are the Light, the Guide. My God, You are the Judge, the Just. My God, You are the Overseer, the Mighty, the Compeller. My God, You are the Supremely Great. My God, You are the Creator, the Evolver" — to the end of the sūra. So when you resolve, rely upon Allah — for Allah loves those who rely.

"O Allah, incline toward me the hearts of Your servants, sons of Adam and daughters of Eve — male and female, free and slave, small and great — with lasting love, affection, tenderness, and mercy; and draw their hearts to me, and guard me from the evil they conceal and plot against me, and repel from me their guile and evil. O Allah, by the sanctity of what I have recited, I ask You to show me Your sanctuary by Your generosity and convey me to visiting Your intimate Muhammad, may Allah bless him and grant him peace — O Fulfiller of needs, O Responder of supplications. O Allah, O Lord of the worlds, O Merciful, O Compassionate, O Peace, O Granter of security, O Overseer, O Mighty, O Compeller, O Supremely Great, O Provider, O Opener, O Knowing, O Extender, O Exalter, O Honorer, O Humiliator, O Hearer, O Seer, O Subtle, O High, O Great, O Thankful, O Guardian, O Sustainer, O Reckoner, O Majestic, O Generous, O Watchful, O Answerer, O Vast, O Gatherer, O Rich, O Enricher, O Everlasting — O Light of everything and its guide, You who split the darknesses by Your light. O High, lofty above everything in loftiness. O Allah, I ask You that You dispose for me the servants of this noble sūra to be my aid in all I want and seek, by Your right over them and their obedience to You — 'Entrust and obey and answer; and by the right of the secrets in it, and whosoever of you lags, burn him with fire — hayā, the haste, the moment — and whoso answers not the caller of Allah is not powerless on earth' — { Answer and rely in what I command you, by the right of this noble sūra and its sanctity to you: hayā al-waḥā, the haste, the moment }." (The blessed supplication is complete) so know the worth of what has come to you, guard it, and protect it from other than its people — for it is of red sulphur. And it is finished.

"Ya Allah, cintakanlah padaku hati hamba-hamba-Mu, anak Adam dan anak Hawa — laki dan perempuan, merdeka dan hamba, kecil dan besar — dengan cinta kekal, kasih sayang, belas kasihan, dan rahmat; serta tarik hati mereka padaku, dan peliharalah aku dari kejahatan yang mereka sembunyikan dan rencanakan bagiku, serta tolaklah tipu daya dan kejahatan mereka dariku. Ya Allah, dengan kemuliaan apa yang kubaca, aku memohon Engkau memperlihatkan haram-Mu dengan kemurahan-Mu dan menyampaikan aku menziarahi kekasih-Mu Muḥammad shallallāhu 'alaihi wa sallam — wahai Pemenuh keperluan, wahai Pembali doa.
