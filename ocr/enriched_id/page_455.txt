English:
— 449 —

"…and your measure is a place more manifest than the place of the letters, and He gave you disposal in the meaning by the difference of the lights, and gave you by the movement of the sensible phases in the human world that you fulfill what reached you of the meanings of speech. Then accrued to you by the knowledge of the Dominion — He made pouring His lights and the difference of His phases; and gave you a hearing that takes shape by the attributes of the letters in the compression of the air from the collision of the bodies, and your understanding of the meanings of the difference of the phases by composition; and provided you movement in the human knowledge that you fulfill what is reached of the meanings of sight. Then He gave you the knowledge of the Dominion, and you receive that by kinds of phases; then He gave you a secret hidden from the secrets — and it is by which the messengers believed and understood the divine address; and gave you the origination to the Abode of Settlement and the world of the Barrier, and you witness the absolute spirits in that abode; then gave you the return to the Abode of Gathering — and that is your deeds; then gave you the bliss in the gardens with the sight of Him. And as for the sight of Him and the causes — they are gradual, their number not counted but by Allah. And since these His gifts are uncountable and were manifest upon you and inward with you from the accidents, and there is no debating the motives. * And know that whoever worships Allah upon good is reassured by Him, and if a trial befalls him he turns upon his face. And in the report that Mūsā (upon him peace) said: 'My Lord, I find in the Torah a nation whose gospels are in their breasts, among them "O my Lord"'; He said: 'They are the nation of Muḥammad (upon him peace).' And he kept counting the special ones until he longed and bequeathed to Him, so He said exalted: 'O nation of Muḥammad, I have given you before you ask Me, and forgiven you before you seek forgiveness.' So look at what Allah gave you from eternity, and he who draws near to Allah by this name is conformed by the traces of the giving without motive and hoards nothing; and this is perfected in the lordly opening, for He opens upon him in a single hour with kinds of sciences and substitutes. And this name has a discipline of forty days and its recitation its number multiplied by itself with the striving of the soul, and its ring is ṬIYĀ'ĪL, and his glorification 'Glory be to al-Wahhāb, the Holy, there is no god but He, the Doer of what He wills.' * And it is related that a man of the people of rectitude entered the retreat and was dull, knowing nothing, and was commanded with the recitation of this name — Allah opened upon him wondrous things of the innate sciences, and the angel came to him between sleep and wakefulness and poured upon him of the divine gifts. And whoever persists in its recitation finds in his inward no need for a creature, and there open to him from the treasures of the unseen the giving. And I was one day sitting in the Holy House and saw a man stand and say: 'By Your might and majesty, if You do not feed me bread and gruel at this time, I will break the lamps of Your House.' So I said in myself: this one is mad. Then he returned to sleep and slept, and behold a man came with what he asked; he woke him and they ate until they were full, and the man left. I followed him and said: from where are you and how is your state? He said to me: 'I was a beggar at the House for bread and gruel, and behold a caller said to me: go to the mosque, for there is a man of My saints who only asks what is in your hand — feed him and eat with him. So I came with what you saw and ate with him.' He said to me: 'O this one, rejoice in forgiveness, for he (upon him peace) said: "Whoever eats with one forgiven, is forgiven."' Then I returned hurriedly to the sleeping man and did not find him. * And know that when the servant is truthful with Allah and conformed by this name, he sees all the worlds serving him by the gifts; and whoever multiplies his mention — Allah opens upon him with the gifts, and his recitation its number multiplied by itself; and if its design is written and a man carries it, Allah provides him of the gifts; and if it is written and erased and drunk by one dull of mind, Allah eases for him understanding and memorization. And this is the figure and its ordering in the coming sheet. (29 — Sun of Knowledges — Fourth)"

Indonesia:
— ٤٤٩ —

"…kadarmu tempat lebih zahir dari huruf, Dia beri kuasa makna bezanya cahaya, beri gerak fasa deria alam manusia tunaikan sampai makna ucapan. Dari ilmu Malakut limpah cahaya bezanya fasa; beri pendengaran berbentuk sifat huruf dari mampatan udara hentaman jisim, faham makna fasa dengan susunan; beri gerak ilmu manusia tunaikan makna penglihatan. Dia beri ilmu Malakut terima jenis fasa; beri rahsia tersembunyi dari rahsia — rasul iman faham khitab ilahi; beri kejadian alam Barzakh nampak roh mutlak; beri kembali alam kumpul — itulah amalmu; beri nikmat syurga dengan melihat-Nya. Melihat sebab beransur tak terhitung melainkan Allah. Kurnia tak terhitung zahir batin dari kebetulan tak dipertikaikan motif. * Sesiapa ibadah Allah baik tenang; kalau fitnah menimpa berpaling. Riwayat Musa 'Ya Tuhanku jumpa dalam Taurat umat injil di dada mereka "Ya Tuhanku"' — 'Mereka umat Muhammad.' Dia kira ahli khas hingga rindu wasiat, firman 'Wahai umat Muhammad Aku beri sebelum minta, ampun sebelum istighfar.' Lihat kurnia azali; dekat dengan nama ini conformed kesan pemberian tanpa motif tak simpan; sempurna pembukaan rabbani, dibuka sejam pelbagai ilmu badal. Nama riyadhah 40 hari baca darab diri dengan mujahadah, cincin ṬIYĀ'ĪL tasbih 'Maha Suci Wahhāb Suci tiada tuhan Dia buat kehendak.' * Kisah lelaki soleh bodoh masuk khalwat disuruh baca nama ini — Allah buka ilmu laduni, malaikat datang antara jaga tidur limpah kurnia. Istiqamah baca tiada hajat makhluk, dibuka khazanah ghaib pemberian. Aku duduk Baitul Maqdis nampak lelaki 'Demi kemuliaan-Mu kalau tak beri roti bubur aku pecah lampu Rumah-Mu' — aku kata gila. Dia tidur, lelaki datang bawa makanan kejut makan kenyang pergi. Aku ikut tanya 'Dari mana?' 'Aku peminta roti bubur, ada suara suruh ke masjid ada wali minta apa di tanganmu — beri makan dia.' Dia kata 'Bergembiralah ampun, sabda siapa makan dengan yang diampun diampun.' Aku balik lelaki tiada. * Hamba benar dengan Allah conformed nama ini nampak alam khidmat dengan kurnia; perbanyak zikir Allah buka kurnia baca darab diri; tulis bawa rezeki kurnia; tulis padam minum orang bodoh Allah mudah faham hafal. Inilah rupa helaian akan datang. (29 — Shams al-Ma'arif — Keempat)"
