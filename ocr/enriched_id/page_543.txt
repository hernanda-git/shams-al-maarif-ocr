English:
— ٥٣٧ —

"A collection of four treatises: (Treatise: The Balance of Justice in the Intents of the Rules of Geomancy [al-Raml]) — followed by — (Treatise: The Breezes of the Desires in the Specialities of the Times of the Planets) — followed by — (Treatise: The Blossom of the Meadows in the Indications of the Zodiacal Signs) — followed by — (Treatise: The Subtleties of the Allusion in the Special Properties of the Wandering Planets). By the erudite, the virtuous, the Sayyid 'Abd al-Qādir al-Ḥusaynī al-Adhamī — may Allah benefit the Muslims by his sciences, amen.
