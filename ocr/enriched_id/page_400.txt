ما عملت وذلك من أعظم النواميس السحرية وبه طلمسوا المياه داخل الكنوز ومثله إذا

( Works of ṭarī 51 ) When you will, take a sherd on which are 8 81, ṭaḥ lad ah mawqūfah, and zubd al-baḥr, and go out to the open and throw before you some of the ash, and throw to the two sides of your back and before you, and the earth at your right and left, and call out with al-khanṭariyāt 7 — and that place becomes a sea and waves, and it is not annulled until you lift what you worked. And that is among the greatest magical laws, and by it they sealed (ṭalamsū) the waters inside the treasures. And likewise, if you will to make the sea land — and its opposite — you throw the ash and al-bahsī 5mah in the sea, and the earth and zubd in the land, and it is only by the side of the river-bank, so it appears to the beholders that the sea became land. And if you will to annul it, call out with the words upon it 7, and there stands for you a column of smoke; so ask the annulling of that, and it is annulled. And this is what you write upon the sherd: 11, ṣaḥ m 11, 5818, aaaa 6 2 5, mah mah 44 4 — and the words upon it you say: "Iyāh 2, ahih ṣabā annanā adhniya adlihīyūn 1251, ahdinā huwāh naw kalū — by such-and-such, O servants of these names."

( The Fourth Treatise ) — and it is for the deliverance of the prisoner and everyone who falls into a grave matter: you picture the form ṭṭ 8 — the fully-equipped image — in the earth at the bottom of the prison, and ride in it and call out with al-khanṭariyāt 7 times, and throw between your hands some of the mentioned ash, and stretch your hand toward the direction of the servant — and he raises you and flies with you to whatever place you will. And if you have no access to that, take a basin or a wide vessel with water and call out with al-khanṭariyāt once, the burāmah in your hand, and throw some of the ash, then descend into it 5 tak — you are hidden from their eyes, and go where you will and are saved. And this is what you write: 44 5 6 7 8 9 7 — and the words upon it you say: "Aydamāh yārūḥ lawāh, ah 2, ah lahu lahūh, ajib ṭā'iman, waf'al kadhā wakadhā — and that happens."

( Amal ṭarī 51 ) Bila mahu, ambil pecahan bertanda 8 81, ṭaḥ lad ah mawqūfah, dan zubd al-baḥr, pergi ke padang, campak sedikit abu di hadapan, ke dua sisi belakang dan hadapan, tanah di kanan kiri, seru al-khanṭariyāt 7 — tempat itu jadi laut bergelombang, tak batal hingga kau angkat apa yang dikerja. Itu antara hukum sihir terbesar, dengannya mereka meterai (ṭalamsū) air dalam khazanah. Begitu juga bila mahu jadikan laut darat — dan sebaliknya — campak abu dan al-bahsī 5mah di laut, tanah dan zubd di darat, hanya di tepi tebing sungai, nampak pada pemerhati laut jadi darat. Bila mahu batalkan, seru kata padanya 7, berdiri tiang asap; minta pembatalan maka batal. Ini yang ditulis pada pecahan: 11, ṣaḥ m 11, 5818, aaaa 6 2 5, mah mah 44 4 — katanya: "Iyāh 2, ahih ṣabā annanā adhniya adlihīyūn 1251, ahdinā huwāh naw kalū — dengan sekian, wahai hamba nama ini."

( Maqalah 4 ) Untuk lepaskan tahanan dan sesiapa dalam urusan berat: lukis rupa ṭṭ 8 — rupa lengkap kelengkapan — dalam tanah dasar penjara, naik kepadanya seru al-khanṭariyāt 7 kali, campak sedikit abu di tangan, hulur tangan ke arah khadam — ia angkat dan terbang bawamu ke mana jua. Bila tiada akses, ambil b basin atau bekas luas berair, seru al-khanṭariyāt sekali, burāmah di tangan, campak abu, turun ke dalam 5 tak — tersembunyi dari mata mereka, pergi ke mana mahu selamat. Ini yang ditulis: 44 5 6 7 8 9 7 — katanya: "Aydamāh yārūḥ lawāh, ah 2, ah lahu lahūh, ajib ṭā'iman, buat sekian — jadi."

( Maqalah 5 ) Ambil lilin celup hijau tembaga, ambil kain — tulis nama ini pintal jadi sumbu, pakai dengan lilin hijau; ambil b tinah tembaga berair, tegakkan lilin nyalakan jauhkan, tabur abu, seru al-khanṭariyāt hingga nampak merak hijau datang dari segala tempat, main tepuk sayap jerit kuat, berterusan hingga sumbu habis.
