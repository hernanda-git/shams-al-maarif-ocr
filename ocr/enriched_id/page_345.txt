وَدَامَ لَكَ التَّمْكِينُ مَا دُمْتَ قَائِمًا ۞ بِحَقِّ مَا فِيهِ أَيْضًا لِلنَّعَائِمِ. ﵊ قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صَلَّىٰ اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ: «المُلْكُ فِي قُرَيْشٍ»، وَقَالَ عَلَيْهِ الصَّلَاةُ وَالسَّلَامُ: «لَا يَزَالُ الإِسْلَامُ عَزِيزًا إِلَىٰ اثْنَيْ عَشَرَ خَلِيفَةً مِنْ قُرَيْشٍ» — صَفَا لَهُمُ الزَّمَانُ ٥٣٩، وَهُمْ تِسْعَةٌ وَثَلَاثُونَ خَلِيفَةً. ﵊ وَقَدْ سُئِلَ الحَبْرُ الإِمَامُ مُعَلِّمُ السِّبْطَيْنِ يَحْيَى بْنُ عُقْبَ عَمَّا يَكُونُ فِي سَائِرِ البِلَادِ وَسَبَبِ خَرَابِهَا — فَأَجَابَ نَظْمًا حَيْثُ قَالَ: ﵊ رَأَيْتُ مِنَ الأَسْرَارِ عَجِيبَ حَالٍ ۞ وَأَسْبَابًا سَيَظْهَرُهَا المَقَالُ ﵊ بِمَا قَدْ أَنْزَلَ الرَّحْمَنُ حَقًّا ۞ يَكُونُ بِحُكْمِ رَبِّي ذِي الجَلَالِ ﵊ فَفِي بَغْدَادَ يَظْهَرُ عَنْ قَرِيبٍ ۞ مِنَ الخُلَفَاءِ مُلُوكٌ ذُو فِعَالِ ﵊ عَدَدُهُمْ تِسْعَةٌ وَثَلَاثُونَ شَخْصًا ۞ ثُمَّ يَنْقَرِضُونَ كُلًّا بِاحْتِمَالِ ﵊ يَكُونُ مَغْلِقًا عِشْرِينَ عَامًا ۞ وَأَرْبَعَةً عَلَىٰ سَيْرِ اللَّيَالِي ﵊ إِذَا مَا جَاءَهُمُ العَزْلُ حَقًّا ۞ تَهْلِكُ البِلَادُ بِلَا مُحَالِ ﵊ وَجَاءَتْ خَيْلُ بَرْبَرَ فَلَا تُحْصَىٰ ۞ لَهُمْ عَدَدًا كَثِيرًا كَالرِّمَالِ ﵊ فَكَمْ وَلَّتْ حَذَارًا لِلْمَنَايَا ۞ فَلَا حِصْنَ مَنِيعٌ وَلَا تُقَالُ ﵊ وَكَمْ تَلَاشَىٰ هُنَاكَ مِنْ دَارٍ ۞ تَقَلَّبَ نَوْتُ رَحَالٍ كَالمَقَالِ ﵊ وَكَمْ مِنْ نَخْرَةٍ هَبَّتْ بِحُزْنٍ ۞ وَقَدْ كَانَتْ مِنْ أَرْبَابِ المَجَالِ ﵊ وَدَقِياسٌ مُسْتَقْبَلٌ بَعْدَ هَذَا ۞ وَتَرْجِعُ الهَزِيمَةُ بِالشَّمَالِ ﵊ فَيَا أَسَفَىٰ عَلَىٰ حَلَبَ وَحُزْنٍ ۞ وَمَاذَا يَذُوقَانِ مِنَ القِتَالِ ﵊ وَفِي ضَرْبَاتِهِ شَيْءٌ عَجِيبٌ ۞ يَكُونُ عَلَيْهِمْ عَظِيمُ اغْتِيَالِ ﵊ فَلَيْسَ وَتُفْتَحُ رَمْلَةُ البَيْضَاءِ حَقًّا ۞ فَوَيْلٌ لِلسَّوَاحِلِ وَالرِّمَالِ ﵊ وَبَعْدَ القُدْسِ ذَا اليَوْمُ العَظِيمُ ۞ لَهُ تَبْكِي المَلَائِكُ ابْتِهَالًا ﵊ وَيَبْقَىٰ نَهْرُ كَنْعَانَ غَيْضًا ۞ إِذَا مَلَكَ البِلَادَ طُغَاةٌ رَجِسٌ ۞ قَلِيلُ الأَمَانَةِ وَالمَقَالِ ﵊ إِذَا حَفَوْا شَوَارِبَهُمْ وَقَصَّوْا ۞ لِحَاهُمْ صَارَتْ كَأَذْنَابِ البِغَالِ ﵊ وَصَنَّفُوا الثِّيَابَ وَوَسَّعُوهَا ۞ وَقَدَّمُوا الحَرَامَ مِنَ الحَلَالِ ﵊ إِذَا مَا جَاءَهُمُ العَرَبُ حَقًّا ۞ عَلَىٰ عَجَلٍ سَيَمْلِكُ لِلْأَحْمَالِ

English:
— 239 —

And the establishment endures for you while you stand ۞ by the right of what is in it also for the tender. The Messenger of Allah, upon him peace, said: "The dominion is in Quraysh", and he, upon him peace, said: "Islam will not cease honored until twelve caliphs from Quraysh" — the time cleared for them 539, and they are thirty-nine caliphs. And the Imam-scholar, teacher of the two grandsons, Yaḥyā ibn 'Uqba, was asked about what will be in all the lands and the cause of their ruin — so he answered in verse, saying: I saw of the secrets a wondrous state ۞ and causes the discourse will reveal * by what the Compassionate sent down truly ۞ it is by the judgment of my Lord, Possessor of Majesty * so in Baghdad there appears ere long ۞ of the caliphs kings of deed * their number thirty-nine persons ۞ then they perish each by endurance * there will be a closure of twenty years ۞ and four upon the course of the nights * when the removal comes to them truly ۞ the lands perish without fail * and came Berber horse not countable ۞ for them a numerous host like sands * so many turned away in caution of fates ۞ no impregnable fort and no telling * and how many a dwelling dissolved there ۞ its camp-fate turned as the saying * and how many a snorting rose in sorrow ۞ and it had been of the masters of the field * and a Dīqyās forthcoming after this ۞ and the defeat returns by the north * so woe upon Aleppo and grief ۞ and what they taste of the fighting * and in his blows is a wondrous thing ۞ there will be upon them a great assassination * so they are not gathered by a bond of youth ۞ and their clotted-blood naught but passing * and appears in the sky a great star ۞ having a tail like the high wind * so those are the signs of the Franks truly ۞ they will possess the coasts and the hills * and 'Akkā there will overcome it armies ۞ as the clouds rise over the mountains * and its houses are smeared with the blood of a folk ۞ who came to it fleeing from the fight * and Ramla the White is opened truly ۞ so woe to the coasts and the sands * and after Jerusalem this great day ۞ for it the angels weep in supplication * and remains the river of Canaan a mere trickle ۞ and cannot reach the sweet water * so woe to Ḥarrān and Ḥimṣ ۞ and what they meet of the injustice of the gifts * so woe then woe then woe ۞ to the people of Syria from a king of error * when the lands are ruled by filthy tyrants ۞ little of trust and of word * when they trim their mustaches and cut ۞ their beards become as the tails of mules * and they classify the clothes and widen them ۞ and advance the forbidden over the lawful * when the Arabs come to them truly ۞ in haste they will possess the loads

Dan pengukuhan kekal bagimu selama engkau berdiri ۞ demi hak apa yang padanya juga bagi yang lembut. Rasulullah shallallāhu 'alaihi wa-sallam bersabda: "Kekuasaan di Quraysh", dan beliau shallallāhu 'alaihi wa-sallam bersabda: "Islam tidak akan berhenti mulia hingga dua belas khalifah dari Quraysh" — masa terang bagi mereka 539, dan mereka tiga puluh sembilan khalifah.
