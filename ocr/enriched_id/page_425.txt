English:
— 419 —

"…these names, or a name of them — begin with fasting and discipline and the recitation of this invocation: 'My God, I ask You for a real bond that erases my slips and excuses my stumbles and rectifies my outward and gathers my wholeness and sanctifies my secret and eases my affair — grant me knowledge by which I excel over the children of my kind; indeed You are the Enlightener of the lights and the Unveiler of the secrets, and everything with You is by measure.' Whoever is constant in the recitation of this remembrance, Allah grants him awe in the hearts and stripping from the soul's stray thoughts, and Allah shows him the unveiling of the states of the names. And beware lest you turn your sight until the invocation is complete, for it is bound to the spiritual persons; and do not approach sleep nor onion, and sleep only a little with presence of heart, and eat only barley bread, and be abundant in seeking forgiveness at the dawn and the hours of night and day, and the remembrance by the names, and the reading of Yā-Sīn and Tabāraka; and spread the retreat with a fine carpet, and sleep only while sitting; and upon you is the recitation of the Qur'an and the noble name — and when you recite it you see from it secrets, and conceal the secret. And in this retreat no one approaches you, neither of mankind nor jinn, rather they flee from you. And be abundant in the remembrance of the remaining righteous: 'Glory be to Allah, and praise be to Allah, and there is no god but Allah, and Allah is greatest, and there is no power nor strength save in Allah, the High, the Great'; and be abundant in the recitation of 'There is no god but Allah, the King, the Real, the Manifest'; and be diligent in eating the lawful and avoid everything with a soul and what issues from it, and eat moist foods; and upon you are the prayers at their times and adherence to the congregation — and the spiritual comes to you in sleep and waking; and some of the spiritual come as light, and some like a flashing lightning, and some like the light of a mirror, and some take form like that light, and you see images like the green bird, and their faces like the faces of human beings. And this is the description of all the disciplines from the path of the people of retreat. And as for disposal by the names — it is according to the ranks of that name; and know that every name has a square, a triangle, and a pentagon, and each has properties that come. And if you will disposal by that name, you write it on a auspicious day and an auspicious ascendant upon a specific substance, and every name brings its square in its place; and if a need presents to a person, he brings the square of the name and his need comes, and he deputes the spiritual master of the name and attains the number — and it is so, and Allah is the Helper. (Section on the detail of the Name Allah) In the name of Allah, the Merciful, the Compassionate — know that this name is the Greatest Name by consensus, and the reality of extolling is the remembrance by His Beautiful Name. I say: whoever wills to declare His attributes transcendent that the sanctification of the attributes of His Master and Creator appear, strips from his heart the pleasure of reciprocations and the fondness of karāmāt and the unknowing in the degrees — by the reality of annihilation in monotheism — upon the secret He intended and the Wise Who measured it, and He clarified the book of essential purity from blameworthy attributes by the establishment of the locus at the Day of Measures and the dwelling of artifice at the first shock, and it remains scattered at the reality — and that is the neck of the neck in pre-eternity, and thrust among the first predecessors. He said exalted: 'Indeed for you by day is a long extolling' — i.e. reviving and passing away. And in the meaning of extolling, the nations return in every breath of the breaths. And in the meaning of His name Allah, the scholars differed whether it is derived or not; and among them some said it is derived from it — or because of the indications of non-derivation of this name: that the other names the Arabs derive from it, except this name which did not occur among the Arabs; and it is related that they used to write in their scrolls 'by Your name, O Allah,' and from it His saying exalted 'Do you know of any namesake for Him?' — and Allah is the Unique; what knew Allah the God and gave creation the names, and veiled them by them. He said exalted: 'So extol by the name of your Lord.' And I say — and by Allah the Great — what knew Allah the God in the suns and the two marginals and the two days; and the reality of this…"

Indonesia:
— ٤١٩ —

"…nama-nama ini, atau satu nama daripadanya — mula dengan puasa riyadhah dan bacaan doa: 'Tuhanku, kuminta ikatan nyata yang hapus salahku, maafkan tersandungku, betulkan zahirku, kumpulkan diriku, sucikan rahsia, mudahkan urusanku — berilah aku ilmu yang aku ungguli anak jenisku; Engkau Pencahaya cahaya Pembuka rahsia, segala pada-Mu berukuran.' Sesiapa tetap baca zikir ini, Allah beri kekaguman di hati, tanggal fikiran nafsu, zahirkan kashf keadaan nama. Jangan alih pandangan hingga doa sempurna, kerana ia terikat pada roh; jangan dekati tidur bawang, tidur sedikit dengan hadir hati, makan roti barli sahaja, banyak istighfar subuh malam siang, zikir nama, baca Yā-Sīn Tabārak; hampar permaidani halus, tidur duduk; baca Quran nama mulia — bila baca nampak rahsia, sembunyikan. Dalam khalwat ini tiada mendekati kamu, manusia jin lari. Banyak zikir baqiyaat: 'Subhanallah, walhamdulillah, wala ilaha illallah, wallahu akbar, wala hawla wala quwwata illa billahil 'aliyyil 'azhim'; banyak 'Laa ilaha illallah al-malikul haqqul mubin'; makan halal elak berjiwa dan keluarnya, makan lembap; solat waktu berjemaah — roh datang dalam tidur jaga; ada datang cahaya, ada kilat, ada cermin, ada berbentuk, nampak imej burung hijau wajah manusia. Inilah sifat riyadhah jalan ahli khalwat. Tasaruf nama menurut darjat; tiap nama ada segi empat segi tiga lima, masing-masing sifat. Bila mahu tasaruf, tulis hari baik ascenden baik atas bahan khusus, tiap nama bawa segi empat di tempatnya; bila hajat, bawa segi empat nama, datang, wakilkan roh tuan nama capai bilangan — demikian, Allah pembantu. (Fasal perincian Nama Allah) Bismillah — ketahuilah nama ini Ism A'zam sepakat, hakikat tasbih ialah zikir dengan nama-Nya yang indah. Sesiapa mahu mensucikan sifat-Nya agar zahir kesucian sifat Tuan Pencipta, tanggalkan dari hati nikmat timbal balas dan suka karamah dan jahil darjat — dengan hakikat fana dalam tauhid — atas rahsia yang dikehendaki, yang Maha Bijaksana takdirnya, dan nyatakan kitab kesucian zat dari sifat keji dengan tegaknya locus di Hari Ukuran dan tempat helah di kejutan pertama, kekal berselerak pada hakikat — itu tengkuk tengkuk azali, tercucuk pada awal pertama. Firman: 'Bagi kamu siang tasbih panjang' — menghidupkan lenyap. Makna tasbih, umat kembali tiap nafas. Pada nama Allah, ulama khilaf sama ada terbitan; ada kata terbit darinya — atau kerana tanda tak terbit: nama lain Arab terbit darinya, kecuali nama ini tiada pada Arab; diriwayat mereka tulis 'dengan nama-Mu ya Allah,' dan firman 'Tahukah engkau sekutu bagi-Nya?' — Allah unik; yang tahu Allah Tuhan beri makhluk nama, tabir mereka. Firman: 'Maka bertasbihlah dengan nama Tuhanmu.' Dan aku kata — demi Allah Agung — yang tahu Allah Tuhan dalam matahari dua tepi dua hari; hakikat ini…"
