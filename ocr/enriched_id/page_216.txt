And some of the knowers said: "Whoever recites the five verses while sitting on the earth, then circles them behind his back with the finger of his witness-hand until he closes them before him, then says: 'O servants of these verses and names, by their right upon you, hide me from the people and all creation' — then he is silent and does not speak, and he is hidden as long as he is silent. And whoever abounds in its remembrance and recites these verses, and is a possessor of a sincere state, witnesses of the making of Allah what descriptions fall short of. And know that the placing of these names — which are 14 names of the luminous letters in the openings of the sūras — and they are: Allāh, Laṭīf (Subtle), Malik (King), Kāfī (Sufficient), Hādī (Guide), 'Alīm (Knowing), Yassīr (Facilitator), Raḥmān (Merciful), Ḥayy (Living), Salām (Peace), Ṭayyib (Good), Qayyūm (Self-Subsistent), Nūr (Light), Hādī (Guide) — in a square on a gold leaf in the dignity of the Sun or by his hand, and carries it — his mention is raised, his rank elevated, his secret expanded, and his breast opened; and it is of the stored secrets; and this is its form as you see:

[the 14×14 cyclic square]

And whoever mentions these luminous secrets 56 times and sends blessings upon the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, 137 times — he does not ask Allah for a thing but He gives it to him; and in it is a wondrous secret for kings and the people of ranks, and many a king who mentioned it — his dominion expanded, his subjects multiplied, his word executed, and necks submitted to him; and in it is the Greatest Name of Allah and His greatest treasure — so reflect on it, for it is of the lordly secrets. And know that for every name of these names there is a special operation and its number, so gather the letters of each name and its number in a congruence and carry it with him and abound in its mention, and Allah uncovers for him by it the veil. Dan barangsiapa banyak berdzikir dan membaca ayat-ayat ini, serta mempunyai hal yang tulus, menyaksikan dari perbuatan Allah apa yang tiada terjangkau deskripsi. Dan ketahuilah bahwa peletakan nama-nama ini — yaitu 14 nama dari huruf bercahaya di awal sūra — dan mereka adalah: Allāh, Laṭīf (Halus), Malik (Raja), Kāfī (Cukup), Hādī (Pemberi petunjuk), 'Alīm (Mengetahui), Yassīr (Memudahkan), Raḥmān (Maha Pemurah), Ḥayy (Hidup), Salām (Sejahtera), Ṭayyib (Baik), Qayyūm (Berdiri sendiri), Nūr (Cahaya), Hādī (Pemberi petunjuk) — dalam bujur sangkar pada lembaran emas di derajat Matahari atau di tangannya, dan membawanya — sebutannya diangkat, kedudukannya tinggi, rahasianya terbentang, dan dadanya lapang; dan ia dari rahasia tersimpan; dan ini rupanya sebagaimana engkau lihat:
