English:
— 58 —

"…and He lowers His enemies by justice, and whoever raised his resolve above the sensible things and his resolve from what the beasts share with him — and He does not lower save Allah, and He raises none but Allah, and He is the one who raises the sky and lowers the earths; He placed that then the raising of the spheres and their measure. And it has a retreat that gives its possessor awe and dignity and acceptance. Of its special properties: that whoever recites it after the spiritual discipline between the hands of a ruler or a tyrant — he lowers his head to him; and whoever carries it in a dispute — he subdues his opponent; and whoever recites it its number after every prayer and requests his servant 'ABD KAYĀ'ĪL, he attends and fulfills his need. And as for His name the Exalted, al-Rāfi' — whoever recites it its number, Allah raises his rank among the creatures and unveils to him the descents of the raising and the lowering, and his servant is LĪMYĀ'ĪL, and his name is 'ARFA' in it — two letters from the Greatest Name; and it has many properties, among them: if a man is wronged by the times, he writes the square of his name al-Rāfi' and carries it with the recitation of the name — then Allah raises his rank and eases for him the provision, and he is feared near all the worlds. And if you enter the retreat and request the servant, he attends and disposes for what you will — and this is its figure. And as for its mention — the basmala: 'O Allah, You are for al-Khāfiḍ al-Rāfi' in all the existents, of the people of the earths and the heavens, and by what You choose of the obscure indications and the wills — glory be to You; You lower Your enemies from the station of nearness after Your sainthood, and raise Your beloveds to the attaining of Your bounties; so in them is the beauty of Your presence by the delightful address in the form of Your wall. I ask You by the secret affairs of the lowering of Your will in the eternity of the lowered-ones and the raising of the measures of Your secret affairs in the highness of the raised-ones, and the gather between the two commands in the hidden subtleties of the absent things. I ask You that You lower from me the corrupt wills and the capricious thoughts and the satanic remorses, and that You raise from my heart the veils of the dense darknesses and the heavenly luminous veils, until there shines in the secret affairs of my heart by Your exalted light in the courtyard of Your holiness, so my heart witnesses of the realization. O Allah, O Khāfiḍ, O Rāfi', I ask You, O Lord, that You subordinate to me the servants of these two noble names. O Allah, O Khāfiḍ, O Rāfi'.' ﴿ Section on His name the Exalted, al-Mu'izz al-Mudhill ﴾ Know that al-Mu'izz and al-Mudhill in reality are Allah; and He gives the dominion to whom He wills and seizes the dominion from whom He wills, and He honors whom He wills and abases whom He wills. And the dominion is in the deliverance from the abasement of need and the subdual of desire in the stain of ignorance. So whoever raises the veil from his heart witnesses the Presence and is provided contentment until he is independent of all creatures, and is conformed by His saying (upon him peace): 'Whoever knows himself has known his Lord,' and Allah gives him the dominion, and calls him 'O tranquil soul, return to your Lord, pleased, pleasing.' And whoever extends his hand to creatures when he needs them — the avarice dominates him until he is not satisfied with sufficiency, and there accrues to him the gradual lead astray until his soul changes and he remains in the darkness of ignorance — and that is the making of Allah as He willed. And the might is for the believers by contentment, and the abasement for the disbelievers by the remoteness from His door; and indeed Allah honored the knowers by the knowledges and the witnesses by the raising of the ranks, and abased the idolaters by the expulsion from His door and the remoteness from His beloveds. And of the special properties of these two names: that his name al-Mu'izz — whoever writes his square on silver on Friday and around it the name of the dominion and carries it with him and recites it near the tyrants and the wrongdoers, Allah raises his rank and they honor him and give him. And it has a retreat and its recitation its number, and its servant is RAṬYĀ'ĪL. * And as for His name the Exalted, al-Mudhill, his servant is SHARṬYĀ'ĪL; so if you have an enemy or a wrongdoer or a tyrant, enter the retreat and recite its number until the servant attends — he disposes for you; and he brings to him the four-square… [square of al-Mu'izz — columns ال ر ا ف; rows: 3 / 72 / 19 / 8; 32 / 20 / 73 / 8; 71 / 71 / 3 / 302]"
