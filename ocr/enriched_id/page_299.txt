( The twenty-seventh chapter, on the seventh pattern of the Names of Allah (exalted) and what hidden blessings they have )
And know that among the Names of Allah (exalted) are: al-Hakīm (the Wise), al-Ra'ūf (the Compassionate), al-Wadūd (the Loving), al-Ghafūr (the Forgiving), al-Hannān (the Affectionate), al-Latīf (the Subtle), al-Hafīz (the Preserver), al-Raqīb (the Watchful), al-Barr (the Good), al-Shāfī (the Healer). These ten names of Allah (exalted)—from the support of their secret is the conciliation of discordant hearts, the inclination of spirits, the secret of endearment, and the casting of tenderness and mercy in the remembrance, the relief of anguish, the vanishing of distresses, the protection from jinn and men, the adherence of shyness, the continuance of health in religion and body, and the continuous arrival of good supports upon the rememberer ۿ. As for the two names (exalted) al-Hakīm and al-Ra'ūf, they are two great names in the acceptance of repentance and the pardon of sin; no one mentions them who was ruined by sins except he repents and [receives] pardon for what he earned, and his repentance is accepted and he is protected ۿ. Whoever aligns His (exalted) name al-'Afw (the Pardoning) in a square and carries it, his evil deeds are with people as if they were good deeds ۿ. ( Bab kedua puluh tujuh, tentang pola ketujuh dari nama-nama Allah (Maha Tinggi) dan berkah tersembunyi yang ada padanya )
Ketahuilah bahwa di antara nama-nama Allah (Maha Tinggi): al-Hakīm, al-Ra'ūf, al-Wadūd, al-Ghafūr, al-Hannān, al-Latīf, al-Hafīz, al-Raqīb, al-Barr, al-Shāfī. Kesepuluh nama Allah (Maha Tinggi) ini—dari tiang rahasianya adalah pendamaian hati yang berselisih, kecenderungan ruh, rahasia kasih sayang, dan peletakan kelembutan serta rahmat dalam dzikir, hilangnya kesusahan, lenyapnya kesulitan, perlindungan dari jin dan manusia, pelekat rasa malu, keberlangsungan kesehatan dalam agama dan tubuh, serta sambungan bantuan kebaikan atas yang berdzikir ۿ. Adapun dua nama (Maha Tinggi) al-Hakīm dan al-Ra'ūf, keduanya nama agung dalam penerimaan taubat dan ampunan dosa; tak seorang pun menyebutnya yang telah binasa karena dosa melainkan ia bertaubat dan [mendapat] ampunan atas apa yang ia perbuat, serta taubatnya diterima dan ia dilindungi ۿ. Siapa pun yang menyelaraskan nama-Nya (Maha Tinggi) al-'Afw dalam sebuah persegi dan membawanya, kejahatannya di sisi manusia bagai kebaikan ۿ.
