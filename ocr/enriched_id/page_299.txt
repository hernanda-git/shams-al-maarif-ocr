«وَأَمَّا اسْمُهُ تَعَالَىٰ الوَكِيلُ — فَهُوَ اسْمٌ عَظِيمٌ؛ وَمَلِكُهُ «كَهْيَائِيلُ»، وَهُوَ رَئِيسٌ عَلَىٰ أَرْبَعَةِ قُوَّادٍ، تَحْتَ يَدِ كُلِّ قَائِدٍ ٦٦ صَفًّا، كُلُّ صَفٍّ ٦٦ أَلْفَ مَلَكٍ مِنَ المُوَكَّلِينَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ — وَهُمْ حُفَّاظُ الكُنُوزِ. وَذَاكِرُهُ يَنْزِلُ عَلَيْهِ المَلِكُ. وَدُعَاؤُهُ تَقُولُ: «يَا وَكِيلُ، أَنْتَ الَّذِي تَوَلَّيْتَ أُمُورَ الخَلَائِقِ؛ وَأَنْتَ الَّذِي كَمَّلْتَ الطُّرُقَ وَالحَقَائِقَ، وَأَنْتَ الَّذِي بَنَيْتَ الدَّقَائِقَ وَالرَّقَائِقَ — قُمْتَ بِكِفَايَةِ العَبِيدِ، وَتَجَلَّدْتَ فِي إِرَادَةِ المَزِيدِ وَالاقْتِدَارِ، وَلَكَ التَّمْكِينُ وَالاسْتِقْرَارُ. أَسْأَلُكَ يَا رَبَّ الأَرْبَابِ، وَمُسَبِّبَ الأَسْبَابِ — أَنْ تَرْزُقَنِي زِيَادَةً فِي القُوَّةِ، وَكَمَالًا فِي القُدْرَةِ، وَنُورًا فِي العِزَّةِ، وَمَتَانَةً فِي القُرَنِ، وَرُؤْيَةً أُدْرِكُ بِهَا التَّبْيِينَ، وَلِسَانًا أُدْرِكُ بِهِ البَّيَانَ. فَأَنْتَ الجَامِعُ المُتَفَرِّقَاتِ الأُمُورِ، وَأَنْتَ القَادِرُ عَلَىٰ بَعْثِ مَنْ فِي القُبُورِ».

وَأَمَّا اسْمُهُ تَعَالَىٰ القَوِيُّ — فَهُوَ اسْمٌ عَظِيمٌ؛ وَالرُّوحُ المَخْلُوقُ مِنْهُ «وَطَائِيلُ» عَلَيْهِ السَّلَامُ، وَهُوَ رَئِيسٌ عَلَىٰ أَرْبَعَةِ قُوَّادٍ، تَحْتَ يَدِ كُلِّ قَائِدٍ ١١٦ صَفًّا، كُلُّ صَفٍّ ١١٦ أَلْفَ مَلَكٍ. وَالذَّاكِرُ يَنْزِلُ عَلَيْهِ المَلِكُ وَيَقْضِي حَاجَتَهُ. وَدُعَاؤُهُ تَقُولُ: «يَا قَوِيُّ، أَنْتَ الَّذِي قَوَّيْتَ طُلَّابَ حَضْرَتِكَ عَلَىٰ الارْتِقَاءِ؛ وَأَنْتَ الَّذِي أَعْلَمْتَ أَهْلَ المَحَبَّةِ عَلَىٰ سُلُوكِ مَنَاهِجِ الكَشْفِ وَالإِجْلَاءِ. وَأَنْتَ الَّذِي نَوَّرْتَ قُلُوبَ أَحِبَّائِكَ بِالإِحَاطَةِ وَالاحْتِوَاءِ. أَسْأَلُكَ اللَّهُمَّ بِعَظِيمِ سُلْطَانِكَ، وَقَوِي شَأْنِكَ، وَنَافِذِ بُرْهَانِكَ — أَنْ تَرْزُقَنِي قُوَّةً مِنْكَ، وَقُدْرَةً أَتَمَكَّنُ بِهَا مِنْ قَطْعِ فِئَامِ مَا سِوَاكَ؛ وَأَيِّدْنِي بِلُطْفِكَ الشَّامِلِ حَتَّىٰ لَا أَجِدَ إِلَّا إِيَّاكَ. اللَّهُمَّ، يَا اللَّهُ، يَا اللَّهُ، يَا قَوِيُّ».

وَأَمَّا اسْمُهُ تَعَالَىٰ المُتِينُ — فَهُوَ اسْمٌ عَظِيمٌ فِيهِ حَرْفٌ مِنَ الاسْمِ الأَعْظَمِ؛ وَمَلِكُهُ «قَصْرُ يَائِيلُ»، رَئِيسٌ عَلَىٰ أَرْبَعَةِ قُوَّادٍ، تَحْتَ يَدِ كُلِّ قَائِدٍ ٠٠٠ صَفًّا، كُلُّ صَفٍّ ٠٠٠. وَالذَّاكِرُ يَنْزِلُ عَلَيْهِ المَلِكُ. وَدُعَاؤُهُ تَقُولُ: «يَا مُتِينُ، أَنْتَ الَّذِي رَسَّخْتَ فِي قُلُوبِ أَهْلِ التَّوْحِيدِ؛ وَأَنْتَ الَّذِي مَكَّنْتَ أَوْلِيَاءَكَ فِي طَلَبِكَ هَلْ مِنْ يَدٍ، وَأَنْتَ جَمَعْتَ العُلُومَ بِأَسْرِهَا فِي القُرْآنِ وَالقُرْآنِ المَجِيدِ. أَسْأَلُكَ الإِلَهِيَّةَ، وَبَسْطَ كِينُونَتِكَ اللَّدُنِّيَّةِ — أَنْ تَكْشِفَ عَنْ قَلْبِي سِرَّ أَسْرَارِ الكَائِنَاتِ، وَأَنْ تَجْذِبَنِي بِالمَيْلِ إِلَيْكَ إِلَىٰ أَعْلَىٰ الدَّرَجَاتِ، وَأَنْ تَرْفَعَنِي وَتُرَقِّينِي إِلَىٰ ذُرْوَةِ المُتَّقِينَ. أَسْأَلُكَ بِالقُوَّةِ وَالقُدْرَةِ التَّامَّةِ — أَنْ تُثَبِّتَنِي عَلَىٰ بَابِكَ بِالأَحْوَالِ السَّالِمَةِ. أَنْتَ أَنْتَ اللَّهُ العَلِيمُ بِالسَّرَائِرِ وَالخَفِيَّاتِ».

وَأَمَّا اسْمُهُ تَعَالَىٰ الوَلِيُّ — فَهُوَ اسْمٌ عَظِيمٌ؛ وَمَلِكُهُ «كَرِيَائِيلُ»، رَئِيسٌ عَلَىٰ أَرْبَعَةِ قُوَّادٍ، تَحْتَ يَدِ كُلِّ قَائِدٍ ٤٦ صَفًّا، كُلُّ صَفٍّ ٤٦. وَالذَّاكِرُ يَنْزِلُ عَلَيْهِ المَلِكُ، وَيَحْصُلُ لَهُ المَرْتَبَةُ العُلْيَا. وَدُعَاؤُهُ تَقُولُ: «يَا وَلِيُّ، أَنْتَ الَّذِي أَحْيَيْتَ ذَوِي العُقُولِ وَالبَصَائِرِ، وَأَظْهَرْتَ مَكْنُونَاتِ الضَّمَائِرِ؛ وَأَنْتَ الَّذِي رَفَعْتَ لِوَاءَ العِزِّ فِي أَوْدِيَةِ قُلُوبِ أَهْلِ السَّرَائِرِ. وَأَنْتَ المَوْلَىٰ، وَالظَّاهِرُ، وَالحَكَمُ، وَالنَّاظِرُ. أَسْأَلُكَ بِسِرٍّ مَنِ اجْتَبَيْتَهُ مِنَ الأَوْلِيَاءِ، وَسِرٍّ مَنْ أَحْبَبْتَهُ مِنَ الأَنْبِيَاءِ، وَبِنُورِ قُدْسِكَ المُثْبَتِ بِجَوَامِعِ الكَلِمِ — أَنْ تَنْصُرَنِي عَلَىٰ أَعْدَائِي، وَأَنْ تَكُونَ لِي فِي الشِّدَّةِ وَالرَّخَاءِ حِفْظًا».

وَأَمَّا اسْمُهُ تَعَالَىٰ الحَمِيدُ — فَهُوَ اسْمٌ عَظِيمٌ فِيهِ حَرْفٌ مِنْ حُرُوفِ الاسْمِ الأَعْظَمِ؛ وَمَلِكُهُ «بَطَايَئِيلُ»، رَئِيسٌ عَلَىٰ أَرْبَعَةِ قُوَّادٍ، تَحْتَ يَدِ كُلِّ قَائِدٍ ٦٢ صَفًّا، كُلُّ صَفٍّ ٦٢. وَالذَّاكِرُ يَنْزِلُ عَلَيْهِ المَلِكُ كَمَا تَقَدَّمَ. وَدُعَاؤُهُ تَقُولُ: «يَا حَمِيدُ، أَنْتَ الَّذِي حَمِدْتَ نَفْسَكَ بِمَا يَلِيقُ مِنْ جَلَالِكَ؛ وَأَنْتَ الَّذِي أَثْنَيْتَ عَلَىٰ لِسَانِ نَبِيِّكَ وَأَوْلِيَائِكَ. وَأَنْتَ المَحْمُودُ الَّذِي عَلَّمْتَ بِمَحْمَدَةِ نَفْسِكَ أَزَلًا وَأَبَدًا؛ وَأَنْتَ المَعْرُوفُ لِمَنِ التَجَأَ إِلَيْكَ دَائِمًا مَرْدُودًا. أَسْأَلُكَ بِسِرِّ حَمْدِكَ النَّازِلِ فِي قُلُوبِ أَهْلِ وُدِّكَ — أَنْ تَرْزُقَنِي قُرَّةً تَامَّةً، وَزُلْفَةً عَامَّةً، وَأَجْمَلَ أَعْمَالِي وَأَخْلَاقِي حَمِيدَةً، وَعَقِيدَتِي صَحِيحَةً، وَنَفْسِي بِكَ شَدِيدَةً؛ وَازْدَدْنِي بِنُورِكَ الذَّاتِيِّ حَتَّىٰ أَكُونَ مَائِلًا إِلَيْكَ. فَإِنَّكَ أَنْتَ الحَقُّ الدَّائِمُ، وَالمُلْكُ القَيُّومُ».

( الفَصْلُ السَّابِعُ وَالعِشْرُونَ — فِي النَّمَطِ السَّابِعِ مِنْ أَسْمَاءِ اللَّهِ تَعَالَىٰ، وَمَا لَهَا مِنَ البَرَكَاتِ الخَفِيَّاتِ )

وَاعْلَمْ أَنَّ مِنْ أَسْمَاءِ اللَّهِ تَعَالَىٰ: الحَكِيمُ، الرَّؤُوفُ، الوَدُودُ، الغَفُورُ، الحَنَّانُ، اللَّطِيفُ، الحَفِيظُ، الرَّقِيبُ، البَرُّ، الشَّافِي. هَذِهِ الأَسْمَاءُ العَشَرَةُ مِنْ أَسْمَاءِ اللَّهِ تَعَالَىٰ — مِنْ مَدَدِ سِرِّهَا ائْتِلَافُ القُلُوبِ المُتَنَافِرَةِ، وَانْعِطَافُ الأَرْوَاحِ، وَسِرُّ التَّوَدُّدِ، وَإِلْقَاءُ الرَّأْفَةِ وَالرَّحْمَةِ فِي الذِّكْرِ، وَتَفْرِيجُ الكُرُوبِ وَإِضْمِحَالُ الشَّدَائِدِ، وَالعِصْمَةُ مِنَ الجِنِّ وَالإِنْسِ، وَمُلَازَمَةُ الحِيَاءِ، وَدَوَامُ الصِّحَّةِ فِي الدِّينِ وَالبَدَنِ، وَتَوَاصُلُ إِمْدَادَاتِ الخَيْرِ عَلَىٰ الذَّاكِرِ.

English:
— 299 —

"As for His exalted Name the Trustee — it is a tremendous name; and its angel is 'Kahyā'īl', and he is chief over four commanders, under each commander 66 ranks, each rank 66 thousand angels of those appointed over everything — and they are the guardians of the treasures. And its invoker — the angel descends upon him. And his supplication says: 'O Trustee, You are He who undertook the affairs of creatures; and You are He who perfected the paths and realities, and You are He who built the subtleties and refinements — standing by sufficing the servants, and being girded in the will of increase and empowerment; and to You is the establishment and stability. I ask You, O Lord of lords, and Causer of causes — that You grant me increase in power, and completeness in ability, and light in glory, and firmness in the ages, and a vision by which I comprehend the clarification, and a tongue by which I attain eloquence. For You are the Gatherer of the scattered affairs, and You are Able over raising those in the graves.' As for His exalted Name the Strong — it is a tremendous name; and the spirit created from it is 'Waṭā'īl', upon him peace, and he is chief over four commanders, under each commander 116 ranks, each rank 116 thousand angels. And the invoker — the angel descends upon him and fulfills his need. And his supplication says: 'O Strong, You are He who strengthened the seekers of Your presence toward ascent; and You are He who taught the people of love to journey the paths of unveiling and manifestation. And You are He who illuminated the hearts of Your beloveds by encompassing and containing. I ask You, O Allah, by Your tremendous dominion, and Your strong station, and Your executing proof — that You grant me a power from You, and an ability by which I am established to cut the masses of what is other than You; and support me by Your all-encompassing gentleness until I find none but You. O Allah, O Allah, O Strong.' As for His exalted Name the Firm — it is a tremendous name containing a letter of the Greatest Name; and its angel is 'Qaṣr Yā'īl', chief over four commanders, under each commander 000 ranks, each rank 000. And the invoker — the angel descends upon him. And his supplication says: 'O Firm, You are He who rooted in the hearts of the people of unification; and You are He who empowered Your friends in seeking You — is there any from a hand? — and You gathered all the sciences in their totality in the Recitation and the glorious Recitation. I ask You the divinity, and the spreading of Your intimate existence — that You unveil from my heart the secret of the secrets of beings, and draw me by the inclination toward You to the highest degrees, and raise me and elevate me to the pinnacle of the pious. I ask You by the power and complete ability — that You establish me at Your door in safe states. You are You, Allah, the Knower of consciences and hidden things.' As for His exalted Name the Ally — it is a tremendous name; and its angel is 'Kariyā'īl', chief over four commanders, under each commander 46 ranks, each rank 46. And the invoker — the angel descends upon him, and he attains the highest station. And his supplication says: 'O Ally, You are He who gave life to the possessors of intellects and insights, and manifested the hidden things of consciences; and You are He who raised the banner of glory in the valleys of the hearts of the people of secrets. And You are the Patron, the Manifest, the Judge, the Watcher. I ask You by a secret of him whom You chose of the friends, and a secret of him whom You loved of the prophets, and by Your holy light established in the comprehensive words — that You help me against my enemies, and be for me in hardship and ease a preservation.' As for His exalted Name the Praised — it is a tremendous name containing a letter of the letters of the Greatest Name; and its angel is 'Baṭāyā'īl', chief over four commanders, under each commander 62 ranks, each rank 62. And the invoker — the angel descends upon him as preceded. And his supplication says: 'O Praised, You are He who praised His Self by what befits of Your majesty; and You are He who extolled upon the tongue of Your prophet and friends. And You are the Praised whom You taught by the praise of Your Self eternally and perpetually; and You are the Known to him who seeks refuge in You, ever returning. I ask You by the secret of Your praise descending in the hearts of the people of Your love — that You grant me complete delight, and universal nearness, and beautify my deeds and my praiseworthy character, and my creed correct, and my soul strong by You; and increase me by Your personal light until I am inclining toward You. For indeed You are the perpetual Truth, and the Self-Subsisting Kingdom.' ( Chapter Twenty-Seven — on the seventh pattern of the Names of Allah, exalted, and what belongs to it of the hidden blessings ) And know that among the Names of Allah, exalted, are: the Wise, the Compassionate, the Loving, the All-Forgiving, the Tender, the Subtle, the Preserver, the Watchful, the Kind, the Healer. These ten Names of Allah, exalted — from the support of Their secret is the reconciliation of divergent hearts, and the inclining of spirits, and the secret of endearment, and the casting of tenderness and mercy in the remembrance, and the relief of cares and dissipation of distresses, and protection from jinn and mankind, and the constant companionship of modesty, and the continuance of health in religion and body, and the continual connection of the outpourings of good upon the invoker."

Aku memohon pada-Mu keilahian, dan terhamparnya eksistensi intim-Mu — semoga Engkau singkap dari hatiku rahasia segala rahasia makhluk, serta tarik aku dengan kecenderungan pada-Mu ke derajat tertinggi, dan angkatlah serta tinggikanlah aku ke puncak orang bertakwa. Aku memohon pada-Mu dengan kekuatan dan kemampuan sempurna — semoga Engkau teguhkan aku di pintu-Mu dalam keadaan selamat. Engkaulah Engkau, Allah Yang Mengetahui hati nurani dan yang tersembunyi.' Adapun nama-Nya yang mulia al-Walī — ia nama agung; dan malaikatnya 'Kariyā'īl', pemimpin atas empat panglima, di bawah tiap panglima 46 shaf, tiap shaf 46. Dan orang yang berdzikirnya — malaikat turun padanya, dan ia meraih maqam tertinggi. Dan doanya membaca: 'Wahai al-Walī, Engkaulah Yang Menghidupkan pemilik akal dan bashīrah, serta menampakkan yang tersembunyi dalam hati nurani; dan Engkaulah Yang Meninggikan panji kemuliaan di lembah hati ahli rahasia. Dan Engkaulah Pelindung, Yang Nyata, Hakim, Yang Mengawasi. Aku memohon pada-Mu dengan rahasia orang yang Engkau pilih dari kekasih, dan rahasia orang yang Engkau cintai dari nabi, serta dengan cahaya qudsi-Mu yang diteguhkan dalam kalimah menyeluruh — semoga Engkau tolong aku atas musuhku, dan jadilah bagiiku dalam susah dan senang suatu pemeliharaan.' Adapun nama-Nya yang mulia al-Ḥamīd — ia nama agung yang mengandung huruf dari huruf nama yang paling agung; dan malaikatnya 'Baṭāyā'īl', pemimpin atas empat panglima, di bawah tiap panglima 62 shaf, tiap shaf 62. Dan orang yang berdzikirnya — malaikat turun padanya sebagaimana lalu. Dan doanya membaca: 'Wahai al-Ḥamīd, Engkaulah Yang Memuji Dzat-Mu dengan yang layak dari keagungan-Mu; dan Engkaulah Yang Menyanjung di lisan nabi dan kekasih-Mu. Dan Engkaulah Yang Terpuji yang Engkau ajari dengan pujian Dzat-Mu secara qadīm dan abadi; dan Engkaulah Yang Dikenal oleh siapa yang berlindung pada-Mu yang selalu kembali. Aku memohon pada-Mu dengan rahasia pujian-Mu yang turun dalam hati ahli cinta-Mu — semoga Engkau anugerahkan aku kelezatan sempurna, dan kedekatan universal, serta memperelok amalku dan akhlakku yang terpuji, dan aqidahku yang benar, serta jiwaku yang kuat karena-Mu; dan tambahkanlah aku dengan cahaya dzat-Mu hingga aku cenderung pada-Mu. Sesungguhnya Engkaulah al-Ḥaqq yang kekal, dan kerajaan yang qayyūm.' ( Pasal Dua Puluh Tujuh — tentang pola ketujuh dari nama Allah yang mulia, dan apa yang padanya dari berkah tersembunyi ) Dan ketahuilah bahwa di antara nama Allah yang mulia ialah: al-Ḥakīm, ar-Ra'ūf, al-Wadūd, al-Ghafūr, al-Ḥannān, al-Laṭīf, al-Ḥafīẓ, ar-Raqīb, al-Barr, ash-Shāfī. Kesepuluh nama Allah yang mulia ini — dari sandaran rahasianya ialah perdamaian hati yang berselisih, dan kecenderungan ruh, serta rahasia kasih sayang, dan pelemparan kelembutan dan rahmat dalam dzikir, serta kelapangan duka dan hilangnya kesusahan, dan perlindungan dari jin dan manusia, serta penyertaan malu yang kekal, dan keberlangsungan sihat dalam agama dan jasmani, serta sambungan berkesinambungan limpahan kebaikan atas orang yang berdzikir."
