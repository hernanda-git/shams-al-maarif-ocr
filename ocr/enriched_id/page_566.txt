{ The Third Sign — Gemini [al-Jawzā' ] }
Masculine, diurnal, embodied, western, ascending, crooked, long, inward, having a voice, obscure, shining, airy, hot, moist, sanguine, fortunate, praiseworthy — eighth of the places, choice and ranked in strength, the place of the Moon's joy. Its planet Mercury — in its third degree is the exaltation of the head; and in it the woe of Jupiter and the depression of the tail; and in the sixteenth degree is the hiding of the Ash'ari of the Manichaeans; and in the seventeenth the first appearance of al-Thurayyā; and in the nineteenth the mixing of the spring-summer separation. One looks to it in its faces: Jupiter, Mars, and the Sun. Its triangle [is with] Libra and Aquarius. Its ascendant: in the first face, in the form of a beautiful woman knowing the mixture, with her two calves and two horses standing — accords for talismans of controlling knowledge and books, especially the faces of judges; in the second face, in the form of a man with an iron egg and a red crown and a leaden breastplate, in his hand a bow and arrow he intends to shoot — accords for talismans of anger and the shedding of blood and blameworthy haste; in the third face, in the form of a man with a bow and a quiver [~] — accords for talismans of the affairs of rest and the works of idleness. It has, of letters: the jīm, the sīn disregarded, and the ẓā' with dot; and of the relation of man, from the orifices the two nostrils; and from the organs, the subtlety of the brain and the power of thought and the tongue; from the outer, the two forearms and the shoulder and the body; and it has the blood-illnesses; and of tastes, everything of acidity; and of colors, what is compounded of white and yellow and what tends to bitterness; and of places, the mountains and the deserts and likewise the sky and the horizons; and of metals, its kinds (sharing with the signs); and of animals, man and likewise the monkey and the game and the singing birds; and it shares with what is other than it in plants. It is specific to the affairs of movement and bestowal and brothers and sisters and brethren and friendship and near travels; and it indicates the deserts and the horizons and the winds and everything that is in the sky, and the high places like the cliff and the mountains and the trees and the knolls, and the difficult resources, and the changes and the pursuit and the transformation and the transfer, and the state of people, and the governorship and the sultanate, and the auspicious forelocks and the affairs of glory and joy, and what has passed of affairs and gone, and the renewing of things, and the affair which is not one, and every affair that has a second return, and the necessity of strength and irradiation — by its being a masculine, diurnal sign.

{ Tanda Ketiga — Gemini [al-Jawzā' ] }
Jantina lelaki, siang, berjasad, barat, naik, bengkok, panjang, dalam, berbunyi, kabur, bersinar, berangin, panas, lembap, sanguin, bertuah, terpuji — kelapan tempat, pilihan dan bertaraf kuat, tempat kegembiraan Bulan. Planetnya Utarid — di darjah ketiganya ketinggian kepala; dan padanya kecelakaan Musytari serta kerendahan ekor; dan di darjah keenam belas tersembunyi Ash'ari Manichaean; dan ketujuh belas kemunculan pertama al-Thurayyā; dan kesembilan belas campuran pemisahan bunga-musim panas. Dipandang padanya dalam wajah: Musytari, Marikh, dan Matahari. Segi tiganya [dengan] Libra dan Aquarius. Ascendeninya: wajah pertama, wanita cantik tahu campuran, dengan dua betis dan dua kuda berdiri — sesuai talisman kawal ilmu dan buku, khusus wajah hakim; wajah kedua, lelaki berkelelawar besi dan mahkota merah serta dada timah, di tangan busur dan anak panah hendak memanah — sesuai talisman marah dan tumpah darah serta tergesa tercela; wajah ketiga, lelaki berbusur dan quiver [~] — sesuai talisman urusan rehat dan kerja kemalasan.
