كل يشاء إيجاداً وإعداماً وبقاءً وفناءً والملك يعني المالك والمالك القادر التام القدرة والموجودات

صفة فعله لأنه مقتضٍ على خلقه عليها وأما ذو الجلال والإكرام فمختص بكرامة العالم
الآدمي قال تعالى ولقد كرمنا بني آدم إلى الطيبات وقد سبق لك في معنى اسمه الكريم ولسنا بصدد
الإطالة والإكرام منه خاصة وهو الإنعام وهو للطائع والعاصي والمؤمن والكافر بإسباغ النعم

على لسان نبيه صلى الله عليه وسلم وأيضاً بالسر الذي اختصه به أن جعله من أهل اليمين وكرمه ونعمه
في الدنيا في تعلق القلب بالجزاء عليها ونعمه في الآخرة مستوفية عنده من أعمال الجزاء وأما جلاله
فهو الذي عم جميع الأكوان على رؤيته في الدنيا بهيبة الجلال ورهبة العظمة وذلك إلى يوم القيامة

English:
— 495 —

"…the unveiling and the tenderness, and the recitation of the name its number, and its servant is RAWĀ'ĪL, and he is from the worlds of Mīkā'īl (peace be upon him), and he comes to the one remembering according to his striving. And this is its figure.

And as for the standing mention by it, you say (In the name of Allah the Merciful, the Compassionate): 'O Allah, You are al-Ra'ūf al-Raḥīm, the Existent, the Living, the Self-Subsistent, the Possessor of the vast mercy — You multiplied the good deeds and raised the degrees. I ask You the vast mercy, O Allah, O Raḥmān, O Raḥīm — I ask You that You give me my aim and do not disappoint my hope, and let me enjoy the witnessing of Your Essence and adorn me with the beauties of Your attributes always, as long as my life remains. O Allah, deliver me from what I fear and am cautious of, from all that is manifest and hidden. O Possessor of majesty and honor, O Lord of the worlds.' (Section on His two names the Exalted, MĀLIK AL-MULK, DHŪ AL-JALĀL WA-L-IKRĀM) (In the name of Allah the Merciful, the Compassionate) Know that the meaning of MĀLIK AL-MULK is He who executes His will in His creation as He wills — in bringing-forth and annulling, subsistence and perishing; and al-MULK means the possessor, and the possessor is the one of complete power, and all existents are His dominion and He is their possessor — that is, their powerful one. And the existents are all one kingdom because they are linked to one another — for even though they are many and divided, they have a unity from one aspect, and the example is the human body, for it is a kingdom for the reality of the human, and it is many different members but they cooperate for the realization of the purpose, the manager being one; and the parts of the world are many and they cooperate for one object — which is the completion of the goal according to what the divine existence decreed, for the ordering of them by the arrangement of what preceded — their linking by one bond made it a kingdom, and Allah the Exalted is its possessor; and the kingdom of every servant is his body specifically, so when His will is executed in the attributes of his heart and his limbs, he is MĀLIK AL-MULK to the measure he attained of the absolute divine power. And al-KARĪM — the two are absolute, and the attribute is an attribute of His Essence, and al-KARAM is an attribute of His act, for He necessitates upon His creation by it. And as for DHŪ AL-JALĀL WA-L-IKRĀM, it is specific to the honor of the human world — He said exalted 'And We have certainly honored the Children of Adam' — and the meaning of His name al-KARĪM preceded for you, and we are not about length; and His honoring specifically is the favor, and it is for the obedient and the disobedient, the believer and the disbeliever, by the completion of the bounties and the succession of the favors and the vast grace — and that is His saying exalted 'And We have certainly honored the Children of Adam.' And this is a sum from the aspect of the bringing-forth and the subjugation for the human world by the honoring. And as for His honoring of His believing servants specifically, by an attribute that expresses it: that He honors him in that He established him upon His service and taught him the causes of His power and made him witness the realities of their degrees in his life — so He promised them upon the tongue of His prophet (may Allah bless him and grant him peace), and also by the secret He specificized him with: that He made him of the people of the right and honored him and favored him in the world in the attachment of the heart to the reward for it, and His favor in the Hereafter fulfilled with Him from the deeds of the reward. And as for His majesty — it is that which pervaded all the universes in the seeing of it in the world by the awe of the majesty and the dread of the greatness, and that until the Day of Resurrection, so the lights of the look return upon them as a radiance renewed for it a power of perception in the second look — so their existence is delayed. And as Allah the Exalted said in the firmly-revealed: 'And it is said that the bearers of the Throne are angels whose faces are like the forms of the calf, they placed their hands upon their faces out of shyness from Allah the Exalted, when Mūsā came and they had worshiped the calf, and Mūsā wanted to kill al-Sāmirī, so Allah the Exalted blamed him for that and said "Do not kill him, for he was noble."' And know that the majesty and the greatness are the principles of the states.

Indonesia:
— 495 —

"…kasyaf dan kasih lembut, serta bacaan nama bilangannya, dan khadamnya RAWĀ'ĪL, dan ia dari alam Mīkā'īl a.s., serta datang kepada yang berdzikir menurut usahanya. Dan ini gambarannya.

Adapun dzikir berdirinya, engkau ucapkan (Bismillāh ar-Raḥmān ar-Raḥīm): 'Ya Allah, Engkau al-Ra'ūf al-Raḥīm, Yang Wujud, Yang Hidup, Yang Berdiri Sendiri, Pemilik rahmat luas — Engkau lipatgandakan amal baik dan meninggikan darjat. Aku memohon rahmat luas, ya Allah, ya Raḥmān, ya Raḥīm — aku memohon agar Engkau beri tujuanku dan jangan hampa harapanku, serta nikmatkan aku menyaksikan Zat-Mu dan hiasi aku dengan keindahan sifat-Mu selalu, selagi hidupku ada. Ya Allah, selamatkan aku dari apa yang kutakuti dan kubimbangi, dari semua yang zahir dan tersembunyi. Wahai Pemilik keagungan dan kemuliaan, wahai Tuhan semesta alam.' (Fasal dua nama-Nya Yang Maha Tinggi, MĀLIK AL-MULK, DHŪ AL-JALĀL WA-L-IKRĀM) (Bismillāh ar-Raḥmān ar-Raḥīm) Ketahuilah bahwa makna MĀLIK AL-MULK ialah Yang melaksanakan kehendak-Nya pada ciptaan-Nya sebagaimana dikehendaki — dalam mengadakan dan membinasakan, kekal dan fana; dan al-MULK bermaksud pemilik, dan pemilik ialah yang berkuasa sempurna, dan semua makhluk ialah kerajaan-Nya serta Ia pemiliknya — iaitu yang berkuasa atasnya. Dan makhluk semuanya satu kerajaan kerana dihubungkan sesama mereka — kerana walaupun ramai dan terbahagi, mereka mempunyai kesatuan dari satu aspek, dan contohnya badan manusia, kerana ia kerajaan bagi hakikat manusia, dan ia anggota ramai berbeza tetapi bekerjasama mencapai tujuan, pengurusnya satu; dan bahagian alam ramai serta bekerjasama bagi satu maksud — iaitu kesempurnaan matlamat menurut apa yang ditakdirkan wujud ketuhanan, bagi menyusun mereka dengan aturan yang lalu — hubungan mereka dengan satu ikatan menjadikannya kerajaan, dan Allah Yang Maha Tinggi pemiliknya; dan kerajaan tiap hamba ialah badannya khusus, maka bila kehendak-Nya dilaksanakan pada sifat hati dan anggotanya, ia MĀLIK AL-MULK menurut kadar yang dicapai dari kuasa ketuhanan mutlak. Dan al-KARĪM — kedua-duanya mutlak, dan sifat itu sifat Zat-Nya, dan al-KARAM sifat perbuatan-Nya, kerana Ia mewajibkan atas ciptaan-Nya dengannya. Dan adapun DHŪ AL-JALĀL WA-L-IKRĀM, ia khusus bagi kemuliaan alam manusia — firman-Nya 'Dan demi sesungguhnya Kami muliakan Bani Adam' — dan makna nama-Nya al-KARĪM telah lalu bagimu, dan kami tidak mahu panjang; dan kemuliaan-Nya khusus ialah kurnia, dan ia bagi yang taat dan maksiat, mukmin dan kafir, dengan sempurnanya nikmat dan berturutnya kurnia serta rahmat luas — dan itu firman-Nya 'Dan demi sesungguhnya Kami muliakan Bani Adam.' Dan ini ringkasan dari aspek pengadaan dan penundukan bagi alam manusia dengan kemuliaan. Dan adapun kemuliaan-Nya pada hamba mukmin khusus, dengan sifat yang menyatakannya: bahwa Ia muliakan dia kerana Ia tegakkan dia atas ibadah-Nya serta ajarkan sebab kuasa-Nya dan jadikan dia menyaksikan hakikat darjat mereka dalam hidupnya — maka Ia janjikan mereka di atas lidah nabi-Nya s.a.w., dan juga dengan rahsia yang dikhususkan baginya: bahwa Ia jadikan dia dari ahli kanan serta muliakan dan kurniakan dia di dunia dalam ikatan hati pada balasan baginya, dan kurnia-Nya di akhirat sempurna pada-Nya dari amal balasan. Dan adapun keagungan-Nya — ia yang meliputi segala alam semesta dalam pemandangan padanya di dunia dengan gentar keagungan dan takut kebesaran, dan itu hingga Hari Kiamat, maka cahaya pandangan kembali atas mereka sebagai sinar diperbaharui baginya kuasa pencerapan dalam pandangan kedua — maka wujud mereka tertangguh. Dan firman Allah Yang Maha Tinggi dalam yang teguh diturunkan: 'Dan dikatakan bahwa pembawa 'Arasy ialah malaikat yang wajahnya seperti bentuk anak lembu, mereka letakkan tangan pada wajah kerana malu dari Allah Yang Maha Tinggi, bila Mūsā datang dan mereka menyembah anak lembu, dan Mūsā mahu bunuh al-Sāmirī, maka Allah Yang Maha Tinggi keji dia kerananya serta berfirman "Jangan bunuh dia, kerana ia mulia."' Dan ketahuilah bahwa keagungan dan kebesaran ialah asas keadaan.
