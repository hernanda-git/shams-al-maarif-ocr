وطال

English:
— ٥٧٠ -
"[Mars continued] …and his coming-in is in the pegs and what follows them.
( Planet: the Sun )
Of his names: al-Siraj, al-Ghazala, Yawh, Dhaka', al-Jariya, al-Jawna, al-'Ayn, al-Duha, al-Nayyir al-A'zam; and the non-Arabic languages: Mihr, Bizah, Aftab. He has the fourth sphere, and he has the sign Leo. He is masculine, diurnal, fiery, hot, dry, moderate; his body is thirty degrees — fifteen from before him and the like from behind; he remains in a sign thirty days. His exaltation is in the nineteenth degree of Aries; his depression in Libra; and he has in Aquarius a fortunate, praiseworthy, fortunate [of fortunes] — the master of the reins of the planets; they are exalted by him and made fortunate when he prevails over them; and the assembly of the sphere is made inauspicious [when not]. He is the king of the sphere and the sovereign of the midst of existence. His joy is from the sphere in the ninth, from his descent in his house in the fifth, and from his rising being in the eastern horizon. And he has, of the lower world, the life and the nourishing power and the natural sciences like philosophy and soothsaying and heresy and magic and physiognomy and discernment and divination and augury; and he has the intellectual and pious crafts and the Frankish languages; and from the letters the dal and the dhal and the sad disregarded and the kaf; and of the days Sunday — and it is the first day Allah created, and it is a day of planting and building — and the night of Thursday. And he has, of places and locations, the councils of kings; and he has the citadels and the fortresses (sharing with Mars); and of animals, every noble [one] like man and the horse and the hunting birds; and of plants, every [one] that is great and long-lived like the date-palm and the olive (sharing with Saturn); and of tastes, every delicious and every sweet (sharing with Venus); and of colors, every red (sharing with Mars) and what is yellow tinged with red; and of metals, every shining delicate [one] like the ruby-stones and like gold; and of woven [things], the shining carpets and what is red mixed with yellowness. And he has, of his inner organs, the heart — it is the source of the secrets and the place of the will and the concealment and the sovereignty of his existence and the source of the sense and the good and corruption of it; and from it spring the powers of the inner senses from the places of the heart — the discernment and the speech and the surmise in the thing and the imagination and the thought, and the powers of the outer senses from the places of the face — hearing and sight and smell and taste and touch, and other than that. And he has, of his outer organs, the vertebrae, the right side of the nose, and the right eye by day and the left by night; and from the orifices, the navel; and of the natures, the heat and the dryness and the yellow-bile illnesses. He is specific to the affairs of kings and the people of truth and the philosopher-sages and the great rabbis; and he indicates the subtleties of cognitions and the delicacies of knowings and the brilliant flashes of truths and the radiant documents, and the affairs of joys and the benefits of reform, and the garments and the gifts; and his coming-in is in the pegs and what follows them, and his going-back in the falling from them.

Indonesia:
— ٥٧٠ -
"[Marikh disambung] …dan kedatangannya di pasak dan ikutannya.
( Planet: Matahari )
Namanya: al-Siraj, al-Ghazala, Yawh, Dhaka', al-Jariya, al-Jawna, al-'Ayn, al-Duha, al-Nayyir al-A'zam; dan bahasa bukan Arab: Mihr, Bizah, Aftab. Sfera keempat, tanda Leo. Jantina lelaki siang berapi panas kering sederhana; badan tiga puluh darjah; lima belas depan lima belas belakang; kekal tiga puluh hari. Ketinggian darjah kesembilan belas Aries; kerendahan Libra; dan di Aquarius bertuah terpuji — tuan tali kendali planet; mereka ditinggikan olehnya dan bertuah bila ia menguasai; dan himpunan sfera menjadi sial [bila tidak]. Ia raja sfera dan sultan tengah wujud. Kegembiraan dari sfera kesembilan, turun di rumah kelima, terbit ufuk timur. Alam bawah nyawa kuasa pemakanan dan ilmu tabii seperti falsafah nujum zindik sihir 'irfan firasah 'iyafah; kerja intelek dan bahasa Frank; huruf dal dhal sad diabaikan kaf; hari Ahad — hari pertama Allah cipta, hari tanam bina — malam Khamis. Tempat majlis raja; kubu benteng (kongsi Marikh); haiwan mulia seperti manusia kuda burung buruan; tumbuhan besar panjang umur seperti kurma zaitun (kongsi Zuhal); rasa sedap manis (kongsi Zuhrah); warna merah (kongsi Marikh) dan kuning kemerahan; logam bersinar halus seperti delima emas; tenunan permaidani bercahaya merah kekuningan. Organ dalam jantung — punca rahsia tempat kehendak kerajaan wujudnya dan punca deria dan baik rosaknya; darinya kuasa dalam dari hati — pembeza tutur sangka khayal fikir, dan kuasa luar dari muka — dengar lihat bau rasa sentuh. Organ luar tulang belakang sisi kanan hidung mata kanan siang kiri malam; liang pusat; sifat panas kering penyakit hempedu kuning. Khusus urusan raja ahli kebenaran hukama rabbi; menunjuk halus ma'rifat lembut ilmu kilauan kebenaran dokumen, urusan gembira faedah pembaharuan pakaian hadiah; datang di pasak ikutannya, undur di jatuh.
