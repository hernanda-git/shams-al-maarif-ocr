in the water of the receiver, and it has descended into it — so cut the fire from the eggs and leave it until that water cools, and preserve it from its vapor exiting, for its vapors are the spirit, so they corrupt it. And when you know it has cooled — after a long while — you take that water, place it in a bottle, cover it, and guard it from wind and sun and dust and other than that which dries it. Then you take of the first calcination an ounce or more according to what you will, and let that calcination be from the quarter; place it in a bottle and pour upon it of the distilled water three-quarters like it — I mean the calcination — and leave them seven days until they are infused. And when that is complete, you take a new thin linen rag and pour into it what is in the bottle thus, and nothing of the clay of the weight exits with it. Then you take an ounce of the first calcination and put upon it half an ounce of that water — and if it bears more, add. And let it be in a bottle whose width is a span minus a third, and its length a span and a third, and the height of its neck a span minus a third; and let it have a glass cover that is joined in the mouth of the bottle in the form of a closed-jar cover. Then you take the clay of wisdom — and it is hair cut with shears, and powdered charcoal, and iron dung, I mean its scoria like kohl — and you add all that to the clay and the hair, and beat with a pestle or a mallet until they mix some with some and return a clinging clay after you sprinkle it with the water it needs. Then make of it the form of a rim and circle it with the mouth of the cover a firm circling, and glue it; then pour it in the mouth of the bottle — I mean the cover — and spread upon it the mentioned clay, and add upon it from outside a white clay; then perfect its gluing lest vapor exit from it and nullify your work. And do not cease observing that gluing; and whenever you see vapor exiting, blot it with clay until it coagulates and its vapor is compressed, and you see it ascend to the head of the bottle and circle and return to its ground. And do not cease gluing with your saliva, for it is a vapor that wets the fingers and hand; and the secret is only in the vapor, so preserve it from anything of it returning. Then you take this bottle and place it in a pot or a vessel closed from its mouth — I mean the mouth of the pot — and the pot upon the tripod upon iron or stone like the brazier; and let there be water in the pot, and place the closed bottle in that water so that a span of it is submerged in the water, and the third of the length with the neck appears above and outside the water. And you place beneath the pot the brooding fire of dung that you have kindled with fire, and preserve the strength of the fire lest it corrupt and dry it. And do not cease watching outside the closed bottle in that water, and you see the vapor circle. So when what is in the bottle dries and blackens, peel the white [part] and remove the fire from beneath, and leave it until the water in the pot cools; and open the bottle and pour upon it of the water the measure of a third of the calcination, and repeat the work upon it until you see it return like the bow of the rain, coloring; and repeat the work upon it until it colors with different colors; and you increase upon it each time of that water the measure of a third. So when your work is complete, take the weight of a dirham and cast it upon whatever silver you will, if you want silver, or upon gold if you want gold — so it calcines. Take of the calcination what you will and cast it upon whatever mineral you will, white or red; and the minerals like lead, iron, copper, tin — there is attained what you want.

Description

Indonesia:
— ٣٨٦ —

di air penerima, telah turun — matikan api telur, biarkan air sejuk, jaga dari wap keluar, sebab wap adalah roh yang merosakkannya. Bila tahu sejuk (lama), ambil air itu taruh botol, tutup, jaga angin matahari debu pengering. Ambil kalsin pertama satu auns atau lebih, dari suku; taruh botol, tuang air suling tiga suku daripadanya, biar tujuh hari meresap. Selesai, ambil kain linen nipis baru, tuang isi botol, tiada tanah timbangan keluar. Ambil satu auns kalsin pertama, letak separuh auns air itu (bila tahan lebih, tambah). Dalam botol lebar sehasta kurang sepertiga, panjang sehasta sepertiga, leher tinggi sehasta kurang sepertiga; ada penutup kaca di mulut bentuk penutup balang. Ambil tanah hikmah — rambut dipotong gunting, arang tumbuk, tahi besi (skoria macam kohl) — campur tanah dan rambut, pukul lesung hingga bercampur jadi tanah melekat selepas siram air perlu. Buat bentuk rim, bulat mulut penutup ketat, lekat; tuang di mulut botol (penutup), sapu tanah disebut, luar tanah putih; sempurna lekatan agar wap tak keluar membatalkan kerja. Jangan berhenti awasi; bila nampak wap, tampal tanah hingga membeku mampat, naik ke atas botol berputar kembali ke tanah. Terus lekat dengan air liur, sebab wap basahkan jari; rahsia pada wap, jaga jangan kembali. Ambil botol taruh periuk tertutup mulut, periuk di kaki besi/batu macam dapur; ada air, botol tenggelam sehasta, sepertiga panjang leher di luar air. Api pengeraman tahi di bawah, jaga kuat api jangan rosak kering. Awasi luar botol, nampak wap berputar. Bila kering hitam, kelupas putih, buang api, sejuk air; buka botol tuang air sepertiga kalsin, ulang hingga nampak macam busur hujan berwarna; ulang hingga warna berbeza, tambah setiap kali sepertiga air. Selesai, ambil seberat dirham tuang pada perak (mahu perak) atau emas (mahu emas) — ia kalsin. Ambil kalsin apa jua tuang pada mineral putih/merah; plumbum, besi, tembaga, timah — tercapai kehendak.
( Cara kerja lain, bukan pertama, tanpa batu, dinamai "pinggiran" ) — yang cerdik buat untuk raja, tak betul kecuali bagi mereka demi mudah; ia neraca Matahari, seperti di helaian akan datang.
