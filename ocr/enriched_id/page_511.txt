English:
— 505 —

"…from where the judgment, and the light of the secret is from the light of the Qur'ān — so who manifested his secret from the observing of the essences by the mediation of the colors and the passing-through from the creation, which is the indicator of the universes by the reality that Allah the Exalted brought forth in the Qur'ān, receives from the lights of the realization and the realities of the knowledges and the lights of the manifestations — this light that swims in the depths of the Qur'ān and extracts from it the pearl and the coral, and swims in the seas of the bewilderment and brings out from it the jewel and the precious stone. And the light of the Qur'ān is the light of Allah the Exalted, and it is the highest unveiling. He said exalted 'And We sent down to you a clear light.' And the drawing near to Allah the Exalted by this [name] shines the mirror of his heart by the lights of the invocations and the recitation of the Qur'ān, and that he recite His saying exalted 'Allah is the light of the heavens and the earth — the verse,' and persevere in the fasting, and eat from the permitted lawful, and leave the familiar [things], and persevere in the essential purity — which is the ablution — and observe the times and the spiritual practice of fifty days. So when he does that, he sees the light going out from his mouth at the recitation of the Qur'ān, and his gaze transfers to the Throne and the Footstool, and he witnesses the manifest lights and is unveiled to him the rest of the worlds and the states in the higher. And of the light of the unveiling is what is in the story of 'Umar ibn al-Khaṭṭāb (may Allah be pleased with him) — how he was unveiled from al-Madīnah to Nihāwand, where he said 'O Sāriyah, the mountain!' And the Prophet (may Allah bless him and grant him peace) when he described the Garden and the Fire — and that is in a wall among the merchants, and the earth that His nation's dominion reaches. * And this name has a magnificent retreat of great measure — so when the wayfarer recites it with His saying exalted 'Allah is the light of the heavens and the earth — the verse,' his servant RAHĀ'ĪL (peace be upon him) descends upon him and he sees him in sleep or wakefulness according to his striving. * And of the special properties of this name is the enlightening of the hearts — so if it is written on a seal of gold or silver and the name is recited over it its number and he carries it, it is the Greatest Name in his regard, and he does by it what he wills, and sees of the awe and the dignity and the efficacy of the word what is beyond count. And this is its figure. And as for the standing mention by it, you say (In the name of Allah the Merciful, the Compassionate): 'O Allah, You are al-Nūr — You illuminated the heavens and the earth by the light of Your guidance by the unseen upon Your Oneness and Your knowledge, so You are the clear light, the Guide, the Strong, the Firm, and Your light has no like in the two worlds. Your Essence, the realized existence, has no quality of the similar. O Allah, illumine me by the light of Your attributes, the luminous, and Your holy Essence by the purification and the exaltation, and Your encompassing knowledge of the subtleties and the existents — that You appear in my heart what removes the eye of the stationary darknesses, and a light that removes from me the human veils, and takes from me the human wills, so my existence is annihilated in the existence of Your Essence by the luminous guidance. Indeed You are Allah, the Light — illumine me, O Light. O Allah, by Your light, make for me a light in my heart and a light in my flesh and a light in my blood and a light in my bone and a light in my hair and a light in my skin and a light from my right and a light from my left and a light from above me and a light from below me and a light surrounding me — the Enlightener of the light. (Allah is the light of the heavens and the earth — the verse.) There is no servant who perseveres upon this luminous mention but Allah the Exalted illumines his outward and his inward, and eases for him his provision, and opens for him the good, outwardly and inwardly. And Allah is over every thing Powerful.' (Section on His name the Exalted, al-Hādī) (In the name of Allah the Merciful, the Compassionate) Know that al-Hādī is He who measured the guidance and guided to the knowledge of His Essence until you loved the call and witnessed the division — and that is what He mentioned in His noble Book, that He attributed the guidance to Him by His saying exalted 'Indeed the guidance…' [page ends]

Indonesia:
— 505 —

"…dari mana hukum, dan cahaya rahsia dari cahaya al-Qur'ān — maka yang menzahirkan rahsianya dari memerhati zat dengan perantara warna dan lintas dari ciptaan, yang menjadi petunjuk alam dengan hakikat yang Allah Yang Maha Tinggi keluarkan dalam al-Qur'ān, menerima dari cahaya realisasi dan hakikat ilmu serta cahaya manifestasi — cahaya yang berenang dalam kedalaman al-Qur'ān dan mengeluarkan daripadanya mutiara dan marjan, serta berenang dalam laut kebingungan dan mengeluarkan daripadanya permata dan intan. Dan cahaya al-Qur'ān ialah cahaya Allah Yang Maha Tinggi, dan ia kasyaf tertinggi. Firman-Nya 'Dan Kami turunkan kepadamu cahaya yang nyata.' Dan mendekat kepada Allah Yang Maha Tinggi dengan [nama] ini menyinar cermin hatinya dengan cahaya dzikir dan bacaan al-Qur'ān, serta ia membaca firman-Nya 'Allah cahaya langit dan bumi — ayat,' dan bertekun puasa, serta makan dari yang halal, dan tinggalkan yang biasa, dan bertekun dalam kesucian zat — iaitu wuduk — serta jaga waktu dan riadhah lima puluh hari. Maka bila ia lakukan itu, ia nampak cahaya keluar dari mulutnya saat baca al-Qur'ān, dan pandangannya berpindah ke 'Arasy dan Kursi, serta menyaksikan cahaya nyata dan dibuka kepadanya baki alam dan keadaan di tinggi. Dan dari cahaya kasyaf ialah apa dalam kisah 'Umar ibn al-Khaṭṭāb r.a. — bagaimana ia dibuka dari al-Madīnah ke Nihāwand, di mana ia berkata 'Wahai Sāriyah, gunung!' Dan Nabi s.a.w. bila menceritakan Syurga dan Neraka — dan itu pada dinding di kalangan saudagar, serta bumi yang sampai kekuasaan umat-Nya. * Dan nama ini mempunyai khalwat agung lagi mulia — maka bila musafir membacanya dengan firman-Nya 'Allah cahaya langit dan bumi — ayat,' khadamnya RAHĀ'ĪL a.s. turun atasnya serta ia nampak dalam tidur atau jaga menurut usahanya. * Dan di antara sifat khusus nama ini ialah penerangan hati — maka bila ditulis pada meterai emas atau perak serta nama dibaca atasnya bilangannya dan ia memakainya, ia Nama Teragung pada pandangannya, dan ia lakukan dengannya apa yang dikehendaki, serta nampak dari kegentaran dan kewibawaan serta pengaruh kata apa yang tidak terhitung. Dan ini gambarannya. Adapun dzikir berdirinya, engkau ucapkan (Bismillāh ar-Raḥmān ar-Raḥīm): 'Ya Allah, Engkau al-Nūr — Engkau bercahayakan langit dan bumi dengan cahaya petunjuk-Mu yang ghaib atas tauhid dan ilmu-Mu, maka Engkau cahaya nyata, Pembimbing, Yang Kuat, Yang Teguh, dan cahaya-Mu tiada banding dalam dua alam. Zat-Mu, wujud yang terealisasi, tiada sifat yang serupa. Ya Allah, terangilah aku dengan cahaya sifat-Mu yang bercahaya, dan Zat-Mu yang suci dengan pensucian dan pengagungan, serta ilmu-Mu yang meliputi halus dan makhluk — agar Engkau nampak dalam hatiku apa yang menghilangkan mata kegelapan yang tetap, dan cahaya yang menghapus dariku tirai manusia, dan mengambil dariku kehendak manusia, maka wujudku fana dalam wujud Zat-Mu dengan petunjuk bercahaya. Sesungguhnya Engkau Allah, cahaya — terangilah aku, wahai Cahaya. Ya Allah, dengan cahaya-Mu, jadikanlah untukku cahaya dalam hatiku dan cahaya dalam daginku dan cahaya dalam darahku dan cahaya dalam tulangku dan cahaya dalam rambutku dan cahaya dalam kulitku dan cahaya dari kananku dan cahaya dari kiriku dan cahaya dari atasku dan cahaya dari bawahku dan cahaya yang mengelilingiku — Penerang cahaya. (Allah cahaya langit dan bumi — ayat.) Tiada hamba yang bertekun dengan dzikir bercahaya ini melainkan Allah Yang Maha Tinggi menerangi zahir dan batinnya, serta memudahkan rezekinya, dan membukakan baginya kebaikan, zahir dan batin. Dan Allah atas tiap sesuatu Berkuasa.' (Fasal nama-Nya Yang Maha Tinggi, al-Hādī) (Bismillāh ar-Raḥmān ar-Raḥīm) Ketahuilah bahwa al-Hādī ialah Yang mengukur petunjuk dan memberi petunjuk kepada ilmu Zat-Nya hingga engkau cintai seruan dan saksikan pembahagian — dan itu yang disebut dalam Kitab Mulia-Nya, bahwa Ia nisbahkan petunjuk kepada-Nya dengan firman-Nya 'Sesungguhnya petunjuk…' [halaman berakhir]
