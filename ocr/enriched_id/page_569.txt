{ البرج الثامن العقرب }
مؤنث ليلي ثابت حبوبي هابط مستقيم عريض صامت مظلم مائي بارد رطب بلغمي نحس مذموم سعد ممتزج مائل وذو ملقرب كوكبه المريخ فيه هبوط القمر ووبال الزهرة ساخط لا ينظر إلى الطالع ذو منحسة شديدة محل ضرر في حلول الكواكب فيه ينظر إليه في وجوهه كوكبه المريخ فالشمس فالزهرة طالعه الأول على هيئة رجل في يمينه رمح وفي يساره رأس بواق لسفك الدماء والغضب والهموم وفي الوجه الثاني على هيئة رجل على حمل في يده عقرب يوافق لشؤون الشهرة والظهور وفي الوجه الثالث على هيئة فرس وصورة حية يوافق للفسق وله من الحروف الحاء والراء المهملتين وله من نسبة الإنسان من المخارج العنان ومن الأعضاء الباطنة المعدة وكذلك الجاذبية ومن الظاهرة الأنف الأيمن والصدر والعورات وله الأمراض البلغمية وله ما تركب من الطعوم وما تركب من الألوان وله من المعادن جواهر الماء وله من البقاع والأمكنة مواضع الحرب والقفار وبيوت النار ومجالس الدولة وله من الحيوان الحشرات وله من النبات كل مر لونه إلى الحمرة اختصاصه بشؤون الموت والعدم والهلاك والشرور العميمة والاغتمام والمواريث والمطالبات والحقوق وأعوان المطلوب ويدل على جماعة الناس وعلى الأراضي الدارسة وعلى الأماكن التي فيها الرطوبة وعلى الماء وكل ما فيه وعلى كل عمل يكون من الرطوبة وعلى ما قد يكون من الأمور وعلى الثبات فيها ولازم الضعف والإظلام بكونه برجاً مؤنثاً ثابتاً ليلياً.
{ البرج التاسع القوس }
مذكر نهاري متجسد شرقي هابط مستقيم عريض ذو صوت عقيم مشرق ناري حار يابس صفراوي سعيد محمود ممتزج ساقط لا ينظر إلى الطالع سادس الأمكنة في القوة والحد والحيرة كوكبه المشتري في الدرجة الثالثة منه شرف الذنب وفيه هبوط الرأس ووبال عطارد وفي الدرجة التاسعة عشر امتزاج الفصلين الخريف والشتاء ينظر إليه في وجوهه عطارد والقمر وزحل طالعه

English:
- ٥٦٣ -

"…[Libra:] Masculine, diurnal, fixed, western, descending, straight, wide, having a voice, barren, mixed, airy, cold, dry, melancholic, inauspicious, mixed, praiseworthy — and it is southern, the beginning of the autumn season, the peg of the earth, third of the places of the sphere in strength; and it is an enemy of the ascendant, openly hostile with enmity and contention, and every planet that is in it hates the ascendant as a contender. Its planet Venus — in its twenty-first degree is the exaltation of Saturn; and in it the woe of Mars and the depression of the Sun. One looks to it in its faces: the Moon, Saturn, and Jupiter. Its ascendant: in the first face, upon it a man in his right hand a spear and in his left a bird inverted — and one seeks in it for justice and equity; in the second face, upon it the form of a black man whose make is like the horse — accords for adornment and reform; and in the third, in the form of a man upon a donkey — accords for the affairs of play and merriment. It has, of letters: the zāy and the qāf; and of the relation of man, from the orifices the two ears; from the inner organs the lustful [faculties] and the passages of nourishment and the semen (sharing with Taurus); from the outer the flanks and from the navel to the privates, and the left eye and the left nostril; and it has the blood-and-black-bile illnesses; and of tastes, what is compounded of sweetness and astringency; and of colors, what is compounded of white and green; and of places, the pastures and the places of trees; and of metals, the women's ornaments like gold and copper; and of animals, the delicate like women and like the gazelle and the sheep; and of plants, the pastures and the places of trees. It is specific to the affairs of women and spouses and the bed and the goods and everything preserved and the partners and the opponents and the transactions and the claims and the contentions and the wars; and it indicates the state of people, and the winds and everything that is in the sky, and the deserts and the wastelands and what is elevated of the earth, and the enemies and the striving and the contention and every transaction between two, and every seeker and sought like the runaway and the thief and the missing; and what is attached under the earth, and what has attended of affairs and what is in it, and strength in everything, and the plant in affairs and the changing in the reports, and the necessity of strength and irradiation — by its being a masculine, fixed, diurnal sign.

{ The Eighth Sign — Scorpio [al-'Aqrab] }
Feminine, nocturnal, fixed, western, descending, straight, wide, silent, dark, watery, cold, moist, phlegmatic, inauspicious, blameworthy, fortunate, mixed, inclining — its planet Mars; in it is the depression of the Moon and the woe of Venus, resentful, it does not look to the ascendant, possessed of severe inauspiciousness, a place of harm in the alighting of planets in it. One looks to it in its faces: its planet Mars, then the Sun, then Venus. Its first ascendant, in the form of a man in his right hand a spear and in his left the head of a sentinel for the shedding of blood and anger and cares; in the second face, in the form of a man upon a load, in his hand a scorpion — accords for the affairs of fame and appearance; in the third face, in the form of a horse and the form of a snake — accords for lewdness. It has, of letters: the ḥā' and the rā' disregarded; and of the relation of man, from the orifices the two reins; from the inner organs the stomach and likewise the gravitation; from the outer the right nostril and the chest and the privates; and it has the phlegmatic illnesses; and of tastes and colors what is compounded; and of metals, the watery jewels; and of places and locations, the places of war and the wastelands and the houses of fire and the councils of the state; and of animals, the insects; and of plants, everything bitter of color tending to red. It is specific to the affairs of death and non-existence and perdition and the intense evils and the griefs and the inheritances and the claims and the rights and the helpers of the sought; and it indicates the groups of people, and the ruined lands, and the places in which is moisture, and the water and everything in it, and every work that is from moisture, and what may be of affairs, and steadfastness in them, and the necessity of weakness and darkening — by its being a feminine, fixed, nocturnal sign.

"…[Libra:] Jantina lelaki, siang, tetap, barat, turun, lurus, lebar, berbunyi, mandul, bercampur, berangin, sejuk, kering, melankoli, sial, bercampur, terpuji — dan ia selatan, permulaan musim gugur, pasak bumi, ketiga tempat sfera dalam kuat; dan ia musuh ascenden, terbuka bermusuh dengan permusuhan serta pertikaian, dan tiap planet padanya benci ascenden sebagai penentang. Planetnya Zuhrah — di darjah kedua puluh satunya ketinggian Zuhal; dan padanya kecelakaan Marikh serta kerendahan Matahari. Dipandang padanya dalam wajah: Bulan, Zuhal, dan Musytari. Ascendeninya: wajah pertama, lelaki di tangan kanan lembing dan kiri burung terbalik — dan dicari padanya keadilan dan keadilan; wajah kedua, bentuk lelaki hitam rupa seperti kuda — sesuai hias dan pembaharuan; dan ketiga, lelaki atas keldai — sesuai urusan main dan riang. { Tanda Kelapan — Scorpio [al-'Aqrab] }
Betina, malam, tetap, barat, turun, lurus, lebar, senyap, gelap, berair, sejuk, lembap, berlendir, sial, tercela, bertuah, bercampur, cenderung — planetnya Marikh; padanya kerendahan Bulan dan kecelakaan Zuhrah, pendendam, tidak pandang ascenden, punya sial teruk, tempat mudarat bila planet mendarat. Dipandang padanya dalam wajah: planetnya Marikh, lalu Matahari, lalu Zuhrah. Ascenden pertama, lelaki di tangan kanan lembing dan kiri kepala penjaga untuk tumpah darah serta marah dan duka; wajah kedua, lelaki atas beban, di tangan kala — sesuai urusan kemasyhuran kemunculan; wajah ketiga, kuda dan ular — sesuai keji. Ia ada huruf: ḥā' dan rā' diabaikan; dan hubungan manusia, liang dua ginjal; dari organ dalam perut serta gravitasi; dari luar lubang hidung kanan dan dada serta kemaluan; dan penyakit berlendir; dan rasa warna campuran; dan logam permata berair; dan tempat peperangan gurun serta rumah api dan majlis negara; dan haiwan serangga; dan tumbuhan pahit warna cenderung merah. Ia khusus urusan mati tiada wujud binasa serta kejahatan hebat dan duka serta pusaka dan tuntutan dan hak serta penolong yang dicari; dan menunjuk kumpulan manusia, tanah rosak, tempat ada lembapan, air dan isinya, tiap kerja dari lembapan, apa mungkin urusan, keteguhan padanya, serta keperluan lemah gelap — kerana ia tanda betina tetap malam.
