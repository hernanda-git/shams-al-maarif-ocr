And we have mentioned the doctrine of the ancients concerning the form of the spheres and what is connected to them. As for the doctrine of the legal scholars, and they are heavens with them, and there were narrated in the hadith reports from Ibn ‘Abbas, and it was said [from] al-‘Abbas ibn ‘Abd al-Muttalib: he said, We were with the Prophet, may God bless him and grant him peace, at al-Batha’, and a cloud passed by us, and the Prophet, may God bless him and grant him peace, said, Do you know what this is? We said, the cloud. He said, and al-muzn? We said, al-muzn. He said, and al-‘anan? We said, al-‘anan, and we were silent. Then the Prophet, may God bless him and grant him peace, said, Do you know how much is between the heaven and the earth? We said, God and His Messenger know best. He said, Between them is the journey of five hundred years, and the thickness of each heaven, that is, its thickness, is five hundred, and above the seventh heaven is a sea, between its top and its bottom as between the heaven and the earth, then above them is a world, and He has called them, like the son of the heaven and the earth, and God (exalted is He) is above that, and nothing of the deeds of the children of Adam is hidden from Him, and the proof for it is His saying (exalted is He): God is He who created seven heavens and of the earth the like of them, so the distance of the whole becomes the journey of these children of Adam, and as for the angel, he traverses the whole in one hour, and likewise the devil reaches the earth before the angel in the heaven. And it was narrated that Abu Razin al-‘Uqayli, may God (exalted is He) be pleased with him, was asked how much is between the heaven and the earth, and he said, An answered supplication. And it was said to him, how much is between the east and the west? He said, The journey of a day. And al-Tha‘labi mentioned from Ibn ‘Abbas: he said, The sun rises every year upon three hundred and sixty windows, and the uppermost window does not rise that day until the coming year. I say: and in the sun are benefits and indications, and among the indications is that it is one and its light shines upon all the horizons and all the world; likewise the Creator, glory and exaltation be to Him, is one and He manages the world. Then the second: that the benefits of the sun are distant, its reaching is near, and God (exalted is He) is distant from creation in essence, near in response. The third: that its light is not withheld from anyone, and for that the provision of God is not withheld from any of creation. And the fourth: that its eclipse is a proof of the existence of the Resurrection and other than it of what indicates its darkness. The fifth: the cloud covers it, and for that al-ghashiyah covered the knowledge. And as for its benefits, they are very many. The first: that it is the lamp of the world. The Exalted said: And We made the sun a lamp. The second: that it is the ripening for their foods and ripening for their fruits, and without burden. The third: that it travels from the east to the west for their welfare. The fourth: that it does not stop in one place lest it harm creation. The fifth: that it is in winter in the lowest of the signs and in summer in their highest for the benefits of the world. The sixth: that it does not gather with the moon in its authority lest each one of them nullify the light of the other. If you say: it is in the fourth sphere, and the heavens did not veil it, yet the clouds veil it, I say: the heavens are subtle, transparent substances, and the cloud is dense because it rises from the earth, and God knows best.

(Chapter on the moon and its similitudes and what is in it)

It was narrated from Abu Hurayrah that he said: The Prophet, may God bless him and grant him peace, said, Do you see the full moon and there is no cloud beneath it? They said, Yes. He said, Then do you dispute concerning the sun, there is no cloud beneath it? They said, Yes. He, may God bless him and grant him peace, said, Then indeed you see it likewise. If you say, Why was the example not struck with the sun, since it is brighter and more complete in light, and the moon, its light is from it? I say: for two matters. The first: that the light of the sun overpowers the eyes, so one is not able to look at it. And the second: that it was broken for the sake of the Truth, so God (exalted is He) mended it. And that was when Gabriel struck the light of the moon with his wing, its heart was broken because it was resembling the light of the sun, so God (exalted is He) mended it with two things: one of them is that He made the eyes look at it in the world every month, and the second is that He commanded His Prophet Muhammad, may God bless him and grant him peace, to strike by it the example in the greatest things and the highest of them. If you say, God (exalted is He) said: The eyes do not perceive Him, while He perceives the eyes, I say: we do not claim that the eyes perceive Him in the sense of encompassing Him, but rather the perceived is a ray
