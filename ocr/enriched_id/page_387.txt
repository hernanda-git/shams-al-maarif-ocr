English:
— 381 —

"In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful." Know that this stone is one substance, and it only divides into two parts and two different forms — one of them spiritual, the second corporeal. So the first part: in it are dissolved the Moon, Mercury, and Venus; and the second: in it are coagulated the Sun, Mars, and Saturn. And for that the scholars named this stone "the small world," for in it is what is in the great world of spheres and what is in them of stars. And here I describe to you the management with a description that suffices for seeing. And it is that you go to what comes out of mashādir of beautiful faces; so place it in a cucurbit and an alembic, and let it be wide, and heat its sides with a gentle fire until the water rises; then tighten the fire until the oil rises and the drop ceases and the smoke scatters and goes out. And raise each one in its vessel, guarded from dust. And replace the head with a blind head — and let there be in its bottom a hole, and another at its side; and close the side hole and open the head hole, so long as the vapor comes out of it, and attend to it with calming until you know. Then close the hole and cast upon it the moist hand, and open the other side hole and insert into it a small column, while you tighten the fire — every time the black vapor comes out of it, wipe it until the blackness ceases from it. And remove the cucurbit and cool it a day and night. Then take the mashādir that rose in the alembic at its top, and extract the earth that remained in the cucurbit; and place the natron in a vessel, guarded from dust. Then you take the magnet, and it is the earth; make it in an earthenware jug enduring of fire, and clay upon it the clay of wisdom, and introduce it into the glass furnace or into itself, and place it in severe fire 7 days — so it calcines red like saffron, or mixed vessels, guarded from dust. Then you take the white water — and it is the spirit — place it in a cucurbit upon a quarter of it, and upon it an alembic with a spout; and place it in a copper pot full of water, and sublimate it 7 times, and every time you sublime it you return it to the cucurbit. And take what is in the cucurbit of the lees — and that is the marcasite. So place it in a vessel closed at the head, and dry it on a gentle fire; then cast upon it the white water in a cucurbit, and sublime it 7 times, and every time you sublime it extract it and dry it in a vessel, then return it to the cucurbit and cast the water upon it; then grind it on hardness and smooth it, and coat it in a vessel closed at the head until it is like camphor. And place it with the body as a part; and if then, place these bodies on smooth hardness, and cast the mashādir upon them and grind them, and place them in a cucurbit upon it a blind head, and close the head, and invert the cucurbit into a cucurbit or into the inside of a copper pot full of water, and kindle beneath it a gentle fire. So when the earth dries, give it of pure quicksilver — pour upon it by irrigation until there comes to it a shining white; so cast from it upon any body you will, and it becomes moon. Then you take what is in the earth and irrigate it with the red, and take you thus the soundest — the drying — until it becomes yellow; and this is the copper, for it dissolves in water. So cool that and open the cucurbit and take the water and raise it in a glass vessel, guarded from dust. Then you take the saffron body, and know its measure from the chosen spirit: 12, its like in weight; and introduce it into the inside of a copper pot full of water, and sublimate the water upon the body 7 times — each time you sublime it, when the earth comes out and you grind it on hardness, then return it to the cucurbit and cast the water upon it, and likewise the water of life. Then raise each one in a vessel 7 times by seven sublimations. Then take the oil and add to it the water of life, a measure of 3 times it; and make it in a cucurbit and fit upon it a blind head, and place it in a copper pot full of water, and kindle beneath it with a gentle fire like the fire of glass, for half a day. Then cool the cucurbit and open it — and you will find the water red like fire. So pour it into a bottle, and add of the water like the weight of the earth; do that to it 3 [times].

Indonesia:
— ٣٨١ —

"Dengan nama Allah Yang Maha Pengasih lagi Maha Penyayang." Ketahuilah batu ini satu zat, dan hanya terbagi dua bagian dan dua bentuk berbeda — satu ruhani, kedua jasmani. Maka bagian pertama: di dalamnya larut Bulan, Mercury, dan Venus; dan kedua: di dalamnya menggumpal Matahari, Mars, dan Saturnus. Maka cerdik menamai batu ini "dunia kecil," sebab di dalamnya apa di dunia besar berupa cakrawala dan bintang di dalamnya. Dan ini kuberi tata dengan deskripsi cukup menggantikan melihat. Yaitu engkau ambil apa yang keluar dari mashādir wajah indah; taruh di cucurbit dan alembik, lebar, dan panaskan sisinya api lembut hingga air naik; lalu perkuat api hingga minyak naik dan tetesan berhenti serta asap tersebar keluar. Angkat tiap di wadah, jaga dari debu. Ganti kepala dengan kepala buta — berlubang di dasar dan samping; tutup lubang samping, buka lubang kepala, selama uap keluar, tenangkan hingga tahu. Lalu tutup lubang, letakkan tangan basah, buka lubang samping lain, masukkan tiang kecil sambil perkuat api — tiap uap hitam keluar, usap hingga hitam lenyap. Angkat cucurbit dan dinginkan sehari semalam. Lalu ambil mashādir yang naik di alembik atasnya, dan keluarkan tanah tersisa di cucurbit; taruh natron di wadah, jaga debu. Lalu ambil magnet, ia tanah; masukkan ke krug tahan api, dan tanah liat "tanah kebijaksanaan", dan masuk ke tungku kaca atau sendiri, api kuat 7 hari — maka terkalsin merah seperti safron. Lalu ambil air putih — ia roh — taruh di cucurbit seperempatnya, alembik berparit; di panci tembaga penuh air, sublimasi 7 kali, setiap kali kembalikan ke cucurbit. Ambil ampas di cucurbit — itu markasit. Taruh di wadah tutup kepala, keringkan api lembut; lalu tuang air putih di cucurbit, sublimasi 7 kali, setiap kali keluarkan dan keringkan, kembalikan, tuang air; lalu giling halus, dan lapisi di wadah tutup hingga seperti kapur barus. Taruh dengan tubuh sebagai bagian; lalu tubuh di permukaan halus, tuang mashādir dan giling, di cucurbit berkepala buta, tutup, balik ke cucurbit atau panci tembaga penuh air, api lembut. Maka saat tanah kering, beri raksa murni — tuang dengan siram hingga putih bersinar; maka tuang pada tubuh apa pun, jadi bulan. Lalu ambil tanah dan siram merah, dan keringkan hingga kuning; ini tembaga, sebab larut di air. Dinginkan, buka cucurbit, ambil air di wadah kaca, jaga debu. Lalu ambil tubuh safron, tahu ukurannya dari roh terpilih: 12, seberatnya; masuk ke panci tembaga penuh air, sublimasi air pada tubuh 7 kali — tiap kali tanah keluar, giling halus, kembalikan, tuang air, dan air kehidupan. Angkat tiap 7 kali dengan tujuh sublimasi. Lalu ambil minyak dan tambah air kehidupan, 3 kali; di cucurbit berkepala buta, panci tembaga penuh air, api lembut seperti api kaca setengah hari. Dinginkan, buka — engkau dapati air merah seperti api. Tuang ke botol, tambah air seberat tanah; lakukan 3 [kali].
