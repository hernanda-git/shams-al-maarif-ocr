[Continuation — the Ẓā' covenant] "…the pact and covenant from him, and dispose him in what you will; and he disposes in destruction — in swallowing [the earth], killing, and ruin, and other than that. And this is its figure. And its invocation: 'In the name of Allah, the Merciful, the Compassionate — Your power appeared, O Allah, in the horizons, and attained; from him who appeared with gentleness, and he who appeared with opposites and peers went astray. I ask You, O Allah, by what You deposited in Your prophets and allies of the subtle pure great words, that You show me the unveiling of the secret of the Ẓā' — that I strike him who displays himself over Your creation with harm and indecencies, for the secret of the purposes and the divergent indication contrary to the command. Hayā, O Ẓā', manifest to me that I see You and address You — answer, by the right of Him who said "I am Allah, there is no god but I," and I ask You, O Lord, by the Beautiful Names — hayā, O Ẓā', by the right of Yāẓ and Ẓa'yā'īl and Ẓūr Yā'īl — appear by the luminous secrets and the lordly signs, the hastening, al-waḥā — fulfill my need by the right of Alū, Aḥad, al-Qahhār, and by the F, there is no power nor strength save in Allah, the High, the Great.' And the implicit concentration: 'Answer, O Yahmiyā'īl, by the right of Hamīṭūsh, Samdīl, Saṭūl, Nawmah, Ẓāẓ, Ẓ 9, Ẓ, Yāh, Yamūh — the hastening, al-waḥā, the hour.' (The letter Ghayn) — Bārdiyās; if you write it with every name beginning with Gh, and write the letter its number on a card and carry it on the head — its bearer attains love and his provision multiplies. And if you write with the name of whom you will and the moon in it and recite the implicit concentration over it and place under a heavy stone — the one worked for is overtaken by remorse, i.e. deficiency. And if you write with the sun in it — its bearer attains love. And it has a retreat; and when the servant enters, light no lamp nor other — and his name is Suqyā'īl; dispose him as you will; and recitation of the invocation 120, and likewise the implicit concentration. And this is its figure. And its invocation: the basmala — 'O Allah, enrich me and suffice me from the evil of calamity and the bad decree, and lower my gaze and immerse me in Your good, O Allah. O Allah, illumine me with Your light by which You illumined Your allies, and aid me with the acceptance of deed and the forgiveness of slips. O Allah, O Rescuer of those who seek rescue, O Allah — hayā, servant of the letter Ghayn, answer and do such-and-such by the right of these names and by the right of His name al-Ghafūr, the Merciful, the High, the Great — Ghulma Gh, Ghṣawgh, rescue me and immerse me in all I want from You, O Forgiving, O Allah, O Merciful — answer with the answer without languor, in what occurs by night and day without languor; the unseen lights shone 2 — answer, by the Alif, there is no power nor strength save in Allah, the High, the Great.' And the implicit concentration: 'Answer, O majestic King, Salasā'īl — Maṭṭ, Shahqī', Kalk, Hubūṭ, Ghanā, Mughnā, Ḥayy Qayyūm, al-waḥā.' (The letter Lā = Lām-Alif) — it has no equal in disposal; there disposes in it what the letters dispose in. And this is its figure. And know that this invocation and this letter are fitting for all that was written for it. And its invocation: the basmala — 'There is no god but Allah, Muḥammad is the Messenger of Allah, there is no god but You, O Allah, by the glory of Your presence — for nothing in the earth or the heaven is hidden from You; He who forms you in the wombs as He wills — there is no god but He, the Mighty, the Wise. I ask You, O Most Merciful, O Most Compassionate, by Your ancient speech and by Muḥammad — upon him peace — master of the messengers, that You show me the servant of the letter Lām-Alif, that I be illumined by Your light to unveil the veil between me and You. Answer, by the right of the Possessor of boons and favors — there is no god but You — rescue me and ease my affair for me, O Allah, 11 times — You are my object and to You my need; O Allah, O Lord, I called You, for You are Allah, there is no god but You; necks submitted to You and You are over every thing powerful. Hayā, delay not the twinkling of an eye, and unveil to me from You — Lā Lā Lā Lā Lā.'"

[Lanjutan ikatan Ẓā'] "…ikatan dan perjanjian daripadanya, tasarufkan apa yang dikehendaki; ia bertasaruf dalam kebinasaan — menelan bumi, membunuh, memusnah, dan lainnya. Inilah rupanya. Doanya: 'Bismillah — kekuasaan-Mu nyata wahai Allah di ufuk, tercapai; siapa zahir dengan lemah-lembut, siapa zahir dengan lawan sesat. Kuminta wahai Allah demi kata halus suci agung yang Engkau letak pada nabi wali-Mu, zahirkan kepadaku rahsia Ẓā' — agar aku pukul yang zahir menzalimi makhluk dengan kejahatan, demi rahsia tujuan dan petunjuk menyimpang. Hayā wahai Ẓā', zahirkan dirimu agar kulihat dan berkata kepadamu — jawab demi Yang berkata "Akulah Allah tiada tuhan melainkan Aku," dan kuminta wahai Tuhan demi nama indah — hayā wahai Ẓā' demi Yāẓ dan Ẓa'yā'īl dan Ẓūr Yā'īl — zahir dengan rahsia cahaya tanda rabbani, segera al-waḥā — tunaikan hajatku demi Alū Aḥad al-Qahhār, demi F tiada daya melainkan Allah.' Idmár: 'Jawab wahai Yahmiyā'īl, demi Hamīṭūsh, Samdīl, Saṭūl, Nawmah, Ẓāẓ, Ẓ 9, Ẓ, Yāh, Yamūh — segera al-waḥā saat itu.' (Huruf Ghayn) — Bārdiyās; tulis dengan nama bermula Gh, bilangannya kad, bawa di kepala — dapat kasih rezeki bertambah. Tulis nama bulan padanya idmár di bawah batu berat — orang yang dikerjakan menyesal (kekurangan). Tulis matahari padanya — dapat kasih. Khalwat — bila khadam masuk jangan pasang pelita; namanya Suqyā'īl; baca doa 120 kali idmár. Inilah rupanya. Doanya basmalah — 'Ya Allah kayakan aku dan cukupkan dari bala dan qada buruk, tundukkan pandanganku, limpahi kebaikan-Mu, wahai Allah. Cahayakan aku dengan cahaya-Mu yang menerangi wali-Mu, tolong terima amal ampun silap. Wahai Yang menyelamatkan peminta tolong, wahai Allah — hayā khadam Ghayn, jawab demi nama ini dan nama-Nya al-Ghafūr Rahim Agung — Ghulma Gh, Ghṣawgh, selamatkan dan limpahi aku, wahai Yang Mengampun, jawab tanpa lemah siang malam; cahaya ghaib bersinar 2 — jawab demi Alif tiada daya melainkan Allah.' Idmár: 'Jawab wahai Raja agung Salasā'īl — Maṭṭ, Shahqī', Kalk, Hubūṭ, Ghanā, Mughnā, Ḥayy Qayyūm, al-waḥā.' (Huruf Lā = Lām-Alif) — tiada banding dalam tasaruf; padanya bertasaruf apa yang huruf tasarufkan. Inilah rupanya. Doanya basmalah — 'Tiada tuhan melainkan Allah, Muhammad rasulullah, tiada tuhan melainkan Engkau, wahai Allah demi kemuliaan-Mu — tiada tersembunyi dari-Mu di bumi langit; Yang membentuk dalam rahim, tiada tuhan melainkan Dia Maha Perkasa Bijaksana. Kuminta wahai Yang Pemurah lagi Penyayang demi kalām qadim dan Muhammad penghulu rasul, zahirkan khadam Lām-Alif agar aku bercahaya membuka tabir antara aku dan-Mu. Jawab demi Yang Punya kurnia — tiada tuhan melainkan Engkau — selamatkan dan mudahkan urusanku, wahai Allah 11 kali — Engkau tujuanku, kepada-Mu hajatku; Engkau Allah tiada tuhan melainkan Engkau; tengkuk tunduk, Engkau Maha Kuasa. Hayā jangan tangguh sekelip mata, bukakan kepadaku — Lā Lā Lā Lā Lā.'"
