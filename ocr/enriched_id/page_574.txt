"[the fixed and of it the changing] and everything in the world is moving in the cycles of the celestial circle by increase and decrease, like heat and cold and summer and winter. So for the seven higher [bodies] there are relationships in the lower [things] — they are indicative of them by their natures and by the jewels of their signs. For Allah the Exalted divided the Atlas sphere, which is the sphere of the signs — and that is His saying: 'and the heaven possessing the signs' — into twelve divisions, and He revealed in these signs their affair; so for every sign in them is an affair by which it is distinguished from other than it of the signs. And Allah the Exalted made for these signs an effect from His revealed affair in what is below this heaven, of the world of composition — so its effect appears in it by the wisdom of Him who gave everything its creation, then guided. Is not His the creation and the command — blessed is Allah, Lord of the worlds? And this is the time of commencement in the intended, by the help of the worshipped King — glorified is His majesty and abundant is His bounty; and there is no good but His good, and no god other than Him.
( Planet: Saturn [Zuhal] )
His name in Persian is Kaywan; in Roman and Greek, Firunis; in Indian, Sanichar. He has the seventh sphere, and he has two signs: Capricorn and Aquarius. He is masculine, diurnal, earthy, cold, dry, excessive in his injustice — eighteen degrees; nine from before him and nine from behind; he remains in a sign two and a half years. His exaltation is in the twenty-first degree of Libra; his depression in Aries; and his woe in Cancer and Leo. His ascendant from light to darkness — dark, absolutely inauspicious, and purely evil; his rectification is by Jupiter, who turns his nature to good; and the least of what he binds of evil is in his inauspiciousnesses in the diurnal births and in the questions whose hours are by day and in the first of the month and in the masculine signs — [these are] the least harmful; and the opposite of that is the opposite. And his evil is magnified by his alighting in the front, and what is in him of evil turns to good in his house or his exaltation. His joy is from the Atlas sphere in the twelfth, and from his descent in his houses in Aquarius, for he is mentioned from his delights, being in the evening from the western horizon, and from the quarters, being in the mentioned quarter, from the peg of the sky to the peg of the east, and from the peg of the earth to the peg of the west — i.e. from the tenth to the first, and from the fourth to the seventh. And he has, of the lower world, the natural power and the subtle sciences from the sciences of letters and the wise crafts like the works of agriculture and flaying, and from the languages the Hebrew and the Coptic, and from the letters the alif and the ha' and the sin disregarded and the ta' dotted above, and of the days Saturday — and it is a day of guile and deception — and the night of Wednesday. And he has, of places and locations, every terrible [place] like the valleys and the narrow passes; and of animals, every ugly black [one] like the pigs; and of insects, like scorpions and spiders; and of plants, every thorny [one] of the trees whose life is long like the date-palm and the olive; and of tastes, the salty and the sour and the noble [karama] and every hideous [thing] like rue and myrobalan and onion and garlic; and of colors, every black and every blackness with greenness; and of metals, every black like lead and kohl and magnetite; and of clothes and woven [things], every coarse [one] and what is of wool. And he has, of man, of his inner organs the spleen; and of his outer [parts], the hair and the nails and the right and left ears (sharing); and of the natures, the coldnesses and drynesses and the melancholic illnesses. He is specific to the needs of the great ones — of sultans and kings and princes and the eminent — and asceticism and the like of peasants and slaves and thieves; and he has the extraction of treasures and hidden things and the observations; and he indicates the governorship and the oneness and the singularity and the contracts and the truth of promises and the fulfillment of covenants and the truth of affection, and the reform and the ruin, and the intellect and the guile and the experience in tricks, and the toil and the weariness and the stripping from merriment and joy and the isolation with care and anguish, and his joy is in the mouth and sorrow and singing...

"[yang tetap dan daripadanya berubah] dan segala di dunia bergerak dalam kitaran bulatan samawi dengan tambah susut, seperti panas sejuk panas musim sejuk. Maka tujuh benda atas ada hubungan dalam benda bawah — menunjuk dengan sifat dan permata tanda mereka. Allah Yang Maha Tinggi bahagikan sfera Atlas, ia sfera tanda — firman-Nya: 'dan langit yang mempunyai tanda-tanda' — kepada dua belas bahagian, dan mewahyukan dalam tanda urusannya; maka tiap tanda ada urusan membezakannya dari tanda lain. Dan Allah menjadikan bagi tanda kesan dari urusan wahyu-Nya dalam apa di bawah langit ini, alam susunan — kesannya zahir dengan hikmah Yang Memberi setiap ciptaan lalu membimbing. Bukankah Dia punya ciptaan dan perintah — bermahalah Allah Tuhan semesta? Ini masa memulakan yang dimaksud dengan bantuan Raja disembah — maha suci keagungan-Nya — tiada baik melainkan baik-Nya dan tiada tuhan selain-Nya.
( Planet: Zuhal )
Namanya Persia Kaywan; Romawi Yunani Firunis; India Sanichar. Ia ada sfera ketujuh, dua tanda: Capricorn dan Aquarius. Jantina lelaki siang tanah sejuk kering melampau zalim — lapan belas darjah; sembilan depan sembilan belakang; kekal dalam tanda dua setengah tahun. Ketinggian darjah kedua puluh satu Libra; kerendahan Aries; kecelakaan Cancer Leo. Ascenden dari cahaya ke gelap — gelap sial mutlak jahat tulen; pembetulan oleh Musytari yang ubah sifatnya ke baik; paling sedikit dia ikat kejahatan dalam sial kelahiran siang dan soalan jam siang dan awal bulan dan tanda jantina lelaki — paling kurang mudarat; sebaliknya sebaliknya. Kejahatannya membesar bila mendarat di depan, dan kejahatan padanya berubah ke baik di rumah atau ketinggiannya. Kegembiraannya dari sfera Atlas di kedua belas, dan turun di rumah Aquarius, kerana disebut dari kesenangannya di petang dari ufuk barat, dan dari penjuru dari pasak langit ke pasak timur dan pasak bumi ke barat — iaitu dari kesepuluh ke pertama dan keempat ke ketujuh. Ia ada dari alam bawah kuasa tabii dan ilmu halus dari ilmu huruf dan kerja bijak seperti pertanian dan menyembelih, bahasa Ibrani Qibti, huruf alif ha' sin diabaikan dan ta' bertitik atas, hari Sabtu — hari tipu daya — dan malam Rabu.
