"(The Third Intent: on the houses of geomancy) The questions and indications of geomancy are sixteen — they are its houses, which are the subject of the discussions of its cases; and each of its houses has a name specific to it. And they are: al-Jawdah, al-Ḥayān, al-'Atabah al-Dākhilah, al-Bayāḍ, Naqī al-Khad, al-'Atabah al-Khārijah, al-Ḥumrah, al-Inkīs, al-Naṣrah al-Khārijah, al-'Aqlah, al-Ijtimā', al-Naṣrah al-Dākhilah, al-Ṭarīq, al-Qabḍ al-Khārij, al-Jamā'ah, al-Qabḍ al-Dākhil. And the first may be nicknamed 'al-Kūsaj,' the second 'al-Ḍāḥik,' the third 'al-Thābit,' the fourth and fifth 'al-Ashqar,' the eighth 'al-Mankūs,' the tenth 'al-Thiqāf,' the twelfth 'al-Sa'ādah' — and they are gathered, in the mentioned order, by these two verses: 'Jawdah, Ḥayān — a banner of its whiteness * Naqī upon the thresholds, Ḥumrah, Inkīs * Naṣrah, 'Aql, Ijtimā' for Naṣrah * Ṭarīq for Qabḍ, and Jamā'ah in the kīs.' (The Fourth Intent: on the forms of the houses) The forms resulting from geomancy are the indications of the cases which are its houses; each of its forms is a state in a house from those houses, specific to it and indicating it, in the preceding order in their names as you see. [The display: a table of the 16 houses with their geomantic figure-rows, composed of dot/colon patterns (::: , ::. , .::. etc.) arranged in four rows per house-block.] (The Fifth Intent: on the significations of the houses) Each of the mentioned houses has a specific signification indicating by itself: (the first) indicates the self and life and the beginning of affairs and the intents; (the second) indicates wealth and gain and helpers and what is recommended; (the third) indicates brotherhood and bestowal and movement and what is also recommended; (the fourth) indicates the fathers and mothers and the elders and the great and the consequence; (the fifth) indicates the children and servants and gifts and the joys of affairs; (the sixth) indicates illnesses and cares and calamities and the slave-girls and slaves; (the seventh) indicates women and partners and opponents and the states of people and every transaction between two; (the eighth) indicates death and non-existence and evils and inheritances; (the ninth) indicates travels and messengers and absence and knowledge and religion; (the tenth) indicates judgment and authority and law and trade and glory; (the eleventh) indicates hopes and expectation and friends and happinesses; (the twelfth) indicates despair and severance and gloom and sorrows and enmities; (the thirteenth) indicates the secret and the question and the image of the querent's self; (the fourteenth) indicates the one asked about and the image of the sought; (the fifteenth) is the balance of the questions of geomancy; (the sixteenth) the consequence of the affair — and these significations are gathered, in the mentioned order, by the allusions of these two verses.

"(Maqasad Ketiga: tentang rumah geomancy) Soalan dan petunjuk geomancy adalah enam belas — mereka rumahnya, yang menjadi subjek perbincangan kesnya; dan tiap rumah mempunyai nama khusus. Dan mereka: al-Jawdah, al-Ḥayān, al-'Atabah al-Dākhilah, al-Bayāḍ, Naqī al-Khad, al-'Atabah al-Khārijah, al-Ḥumrah, al-Inkīs, al-Naṣrah al-Khārijah, al-'Aqlah, al-Ijtimā', al-Naṣrah al-Dākhilah, al-Ṭarīq, al-Qabḍ al-Khārij, al-Jamā'ah, al-Qabḍ al-Dākhil. Dan yang pertama mungkin dipanggil 'al-Kūsaj,' kedua 'al-Ḍāḥik,' ketiga 'al-Thābit,' keempat dan kelima 'al-Ashqar,' kelapan 'al-Mankūs,' kesepuluh 'al-Thiqāf,' kedua belas 'al-Sa'ādah' — dan mereka dikumpul, dalam susunan disebut, oleh dua bait ini: 'Jawdah, Ḥayān — panji putihnya * Naqī atas ambang, Ḥumrah, Inkīs * Naṣrah, 'Aql, Ijtimā' bagi Naṣrah * Ṭarīq bagi Qabḍ, dan Jamā'ah dalam kīs.' (Maqasad Keempat: tentang bentuk rumah) Bentuk terhasil dari geomancy ialah petunjuk kes yang merupakan rumahnya; tiap bentuk ialah keadaan dalam rumah dari rumah-rumah itu, khusus padanya dan menunjuknya, dalam susunan nama terdahulu sebagaimana engkau lihat. [Paparan: jadual 16 rumah dengan baris angka geomancy, tersusun dari corak titik/kolon (::: , ::. , .::. dll.) dalam empat baris setiap blok rumah.] (Maqasad Kelima: tentang makna rumah) Tiap rumah disebut mempunyai makna khusus menunjuk dengan sendirinya: (pertama) menunjuk diri dan nyawa serta permulaan urusan dan niat; (kedua) menunjuk harta dan usaha serta penolong dan apa disunatkan; (ketiga) menunjuk persaudaraan dan anugerah serta gerakan dan apa juga disunatkan; (keempat) menunjuk bapa dan ibu serta orang tua dan besar serta akibat; (kelima) menunjuk anak dan khadam serta hadiah dan kegembiraan urusan; (keenam) menunjuk penyakit dan keresahan serta bala dan hamba perempuan dan hamba lelaki; (ketujuh) menunjuk wanita dan rakan kongsi serta lawan dan keadaan manusia serta tiap urusan antara dua; (kelapan) menunjuk mati dan ketiadaan serta kejahatan dan pusaka; (kesembilan) menunjuk perjalanan dan utusan serta keghaiban dan ilmu dan agama; (kesepuluh) menunjuk hukum dan kuasa serta undang-undang dan perdagangan dan kemuliaan; (kesebelas) menunjuk harapan dan sangkaan baik serta kawan dan kebahagiaan; (kedua belas) menunjuk putus asa dan pemutusan serta kelam dan duka serta permusuhan; (ketiga belas) menunjuk rahsia dan soalan serta imej diri penanya; (keempat belas) menunjuk yang ditanya dan imej yang dicari; (kelima belas) neraca soalan geomancy; (keenam belas) akibat urusan — dan makna ini dikumpul, dalam susunan disebut, oleh allusi dua bait ini.
