And the best of these climes [are those of] the choicest and most excellent and noble — the clime of Mercury is not the heart of these climes but their center, sheltered by three southern [climes]: the clime of Jupiter and the clime of Saturn and the clime of Venus; and three northern [climes]: the clime of the Moon and the clime of Mars and the clime of the Sun — balanced in the first form, and the second form is similar to it. And 'the best of affairs are their middles,' as in the report from the Master of Mankind — may Allah bless him and grant him peace. And likewise from it appeared the prophets and messengers — blessings and peace of Allah upon them — and from it arose the scholars and the sages and the perfect of the servants, who have in the knowings and the gnoses and the excellences and the virtues the utmost preparedness. So just as it is the last of the rest of the climes, Allah — exalted is He — chose from it His best creation and the lamps of His creatures. In each of these seven climes are diverse-colored nations, and other than that of natures and characters and opinions and religions and schools and creeds and deeds and crafts and customs and worships — they do not resemble one another. And likewise the animals and the minerals and the plants differ in color and form and taste and smell according to the difference of the climes of the countries and the soil of the regions of the earth and the fallings of the ray of the Sun upon the places, as is determined in their stations by the wisdom and the management of the Wise, the Knowing, the Expert — to whom is the creation and the fine origination of what He wills, the One who made excellent the heavens and the earth, the Possessor of the Glorious Throne, the Originator, the Restorer, the Doer of what He wills — exalted is His power and great is His wisdom, and sublime is His majesty and great is His dominion. There is no good but other than He. * And this is the last of what was made easy by His facilitation — its collection and what He inspired of the beginning of the way of its placing from the scattered particles of these benefits and the fruits of these customs, in the fortress of Ta'if, in the shelter of the graces, under the shades of the beautiful gnoses of assistance upon the singularity of the smooth-flowing knowings in their kinds, in the folds of their witnessing, above the pulpits of their branches, with the glad-tidings of the allusions and the brilliances of the expressions.
'He chose him in trial by His decree * distant from understandings is its comprehension;
He enforced for him a destiny upon a destiny that sufficed * [so that] he bore upon the others in it the perils.'
And praise belongs to Allah, by whose favor the righteous deeds are completed and He makes them by His bounty among the abiding righteous deeds. And glorified is He who is, every day, in a state, and to Him is the greatness and the dominion and His blessings and His peace and His greeting and His honoring upon His chosen Messenger and his family and his companions, the masters of truthfulness and fidelity; and peace upon the messengers; and praise belongs to Allah, Lord of the worlds.

SELATAN
Dan terbaik iklim ini dari yang terpilih mulia — iklim 'Utarid bukan jantung tetapi pusat, dilindungi tiga iklim selatan: Musytari Zuhal Zuhrah; dan tiga utara: Bulan Marikh Matahari — seimbang dalam bentuk pertama, bentuk kedua serupa. 'Sebaik urusan ialah pertengahannya' seperti hadis Penghulu Manusia. Demikian darinya zahir nabi dan rasul — dari sana ulama hukama hamba sempurna yang ada persediaan sempurna dalam ma'rifat. Maka seperti ia akhir iklim, Allah pilih dari sana ciptaan terbaik-Nya pelita makhluk. Tiap tujuh iklim ada bangsa berbeza warna, sifat pendapat agama mazhab amalan kerja adat ibadat — tidak sama. Demikian haiwan galian tumbuhan berbeza warna rupa rasa bau mengikut iklim negara tanah jatuh sinar matahari, ditetapkan dengan hikmah Yang Bijaksana Maha Tahu. Bagi-Nya ciptaan dan kejadian halus — Yang Memperbaiki langit bumi, Tuhan Arasy, Yang Memulakan Mengembalikan Mengadakan kehendak-Nya. Tiada baik melainkan selain-Nya. * Dan ini akhir yang dimudahkan dengan pertolongan-Nya — kumpulan dan ilham permulaan cara susunan dari serpihan faedah ini di kubu Ta'if, naungan kurnia, di bawah gnosis indah. 'Dipilih dalam ujian dengan hukum-Nya * jauh dari faham pemahamannya; dikuatkan takdir atas takdir yang cukup * dipikul atas lain bahaya.' Segala puji bagi Allah yang dengannya amal soleh sempurna; maha suci yang setiap hari dalam urusan, bagi-Nya kebesaran kerajaan selawat kehormatan atas Rasul terpilih keluarga sahabat penaung kebenaran; salam atas rasul; puji Allah Tuhan semesta.
