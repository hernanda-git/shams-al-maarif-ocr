"…the attributes and the descriptions — I ask You by the permanence of Your life and the eternality of the existence of Your Essence and the everlastingness of Your attributes, that You make me travel the paths of the special ones of the servants and the truthful of the saints, and that You make me with the eminent, the chosen; and revive for me, O Living, before every living one, and O Sustainer, the One standing by the management of the existents from the worlds and the creatures from every world. I ask You that You provide me what You apportioned for me in Your knowledge, without hardship and the movement of the moving ones and the stillness of the still ones, and You made everything in a rank of the oppositions and the equivalences from every silent and speaking. I ask You by the secret of the Sustenance in the existents and the power of the bringing-forth in the hidden knowns and the encompassing of the efficacy of the power in the Dominion and the Kingdom, that You establish me by Your obedience in everything that turns from me the darkness of the materialists, and unveil to me the secret of the Sustenance, and raise me to the heart-connections. O Allah, O Living, O Sustainer.

[Section on His name the Exalted, al-Qayyūm] Know that al-Qayyūm is an exaggeration of al-Qiyām and al-Qā'im, and al-Qayyūm is He by whom every existent stands, so the things are not conceived except by His eternal existence; and al-Qayyūm — for His subsistence is by His Essence, and the subsistence of everything is by His worlds. And know that the manifestation of this name — His divine manifestation — does not appear but in the Hereafter, for His outward is a circle that appeared in existence, and He established the worlds of the Dominion of the heavens and the earth upon the world of the Dominion by His Sustenance and the management of the states by His Sustenance — and that is a specificity; and He established the intellects and established the world of the Dominion and established the natural disposition and took the covenant and established the bodies and the spirits and the Garden and the Fire. And the likeness of that is what Allah established of the Essence of the station and the witnessed station and the witnessing — they stood by the gathering, and the gathering stood by the days and the days by the hours and the hours by the degrees and the degrees by the minutes and they are the seconds, and so on. And al-Qayyūm is of the subtletics of the worlds in the Essence of the soul of the soul, so the law stood by it; so the clinging stood, the drop stood, the drop stood the clinging, the bones by the muscles and the muscles by the ligaments and the ligaments by the membranes and the membranes by the web and the web by the veins and the veins by the flesh and the flesh by the blood and the flesh by His Sustenance — and it is an inventive attribute; and by the food the body stood, and the body for the water and the water by the mercy and the mercy is an attribute, for He is the Generous; and the gathered of that standing is the human — so the human stands by his worlds, and therefore the deeds stood by the knowledge and the knowledge stood by the search and the highest search stood by the realization of the circles of the world over its states and the rulings of its acts by the circles of his station — the secret of His Sustenance, so the name al-Qayyūm appears in the abode of the Hereafter over the secret that He deposited in the Footstool of the secret of the Sustenance, bearing the heavens and the earth and who is in them by the secret of the Sustenance that Allah deposited in it. And know that the knowledge of the names of Allah the Great is of the noblest of the sciences, and it is like the hidden pearl; and the difference of the scholars in the meaning of the Greatest Name is upon three views: the first — that to the Greatest Name belongs every name that is answered at the constraint; the second — that the most important of what Allah mentioned in it are sayings, so among them who said it is al-Jalālah and it is the most correct, and among them who said it is Dhū al-Jalāl wa-al-Ikrām, and among them who said it is al-Laṭīf, and among them who said it is Salām, a saying of a Merciful Lord, and among them who said it is al-Ḥannān al-Mannān Dhū al-Jalāl wa-al-Ikrām, and among them who said it is the first of iron [al-ḥadīd], and among them who said it is the last of the gathering [al-ḥashr], and among them who said it is al-Wadūd, and among them who said it is in Sūrat al-Ḥajj in His saying exalted 'And those who emigrated in the path of Allah then were killed or died — He will surely provide for them a good provision', and among them who said it is the first of the luminous letters of the chapters, and among them who said it is al-Māni', and among them who said it is the word of al-Jalālah when you repeat it, and among them who said His name is al-'Alīm, and among them who said it is al-'Alī al-'Aẓīm, and among them who said it is the testimony that there is no god but Allah; and reports multiplied by authentic narrations, and the hadith narrated from him (peace be upon him): 'Al-ẒABĀYĀ, Dhū al-Jalāl wa-al-Ikrām' — and it is a decisive proof. "…sifat dan keterangan — Aku memohon dengan kekalnya hidup-Mu dan keazalian wujud Zat-Mu serta keabadian sifat-Mu, agar Engkau jalankan aku melalui jalan orang khusus dari hamba dan siddiq dari wali, serta jadikan aku bersama tuan yang terpilih; dan hidupkanlah untukku, wahai Yang Hidup, sebelum tiap yang hidup, dan wahai Yang Berdiri Sendiri yang mengurus makhluk dari alam dan makhluk tiap alam. Aku memohon dengan rahsia Qayyūmiyyah dalam makhluk dan kuasa pengadaan dalam rahsia yang diketahui serta meliputi kesan kuasa dalam kerajaan dan alam ghaib, agar Engkau tegakkan aku dengan ketaatan-Mu dalam segala yang memalingkan dari aku kegelapan materialis, serta bukakan kepadaku rahsia Qayyūmiyyah dan angkat aku ke pertalian hati. Ya Allah, wahai Yang Hidup, wahai Yang Berdiri Sendiri.

[Fasal al-Qayyūm] Ketahuilah bahwa al-Qayyūm ialah pelebihan dari al-Qiyām dan al-Qā'im, dan al-Qayyūm ialah Yang dengannya tiap makhluk berdiri, maka perkara tidak dibayangkan melainkan dengan wujud kekal-Nya; dan al-Qayyūm — kerana berdirinya dengan Zat-Nya, dan berdirinya tiap perkara dengan alam-Nya. Dan ketahuilah bahwa tajalli nama ini — tajalli ilahi-Nya — tidak nampak melainkan di akhirat, kerana zahirnya ialah bulatan yang muncul dalam wujud, dan Ia menegakkan alam Kerajaan langit dan bumi atas alam Kerajaan dengan Qayyūmiyyah-Nya dan pengurusan keadaan dengan Qayyūmiyyah-Nya — dan itu kekhususan; dan Ia menegakkan akal serta menegakkan alam Kerajaan dan menegakkan fitrah serta mengambil perjanjian dan menegakkan jasad, roh, Syurga, dan Neraka. Dan misalnya ialah apa yang Allah tegakkan dari Zat maqam dan maqam yang disaksikan serta penyaksian — mereka berdiri dengan himpunan, dan himpunan berdiri dengan hari dan hari dengan jam dan jam dengan darjat dan darjat dengan minit dan ia saat, dan demikian. Dan al-Qayyūm ialah dari halus alam dalam Zat jiwa dari jiwa, maka hukum berdiri dengannya; maka 'alaqah berdiri, nutfah berdiri, nutfah berdiri 'alaqah, tulang dengan otot dan otot dengan urat sambung dan urat sambung dengan selaput dan selaput dengan jala dan jala dengan urat dan urat dengan daging dan daging dengan darah dan daging dengan Qayyūmiyyah-Nya — dan ia sifat penciptaan; dan dengan makanan jasad berdiri, dan jasad untuk air dan air dengan rahmat dan rahmat itu sifat, kerana Ia Yang Pemurah; dan himpunan yang berdiri itu ialah manusia — maka manusia berdiri dengan alamnya, dan kerana itu amal berdiri dengan ilmu dan ilmu berdiri dengan pencarian dan pencarian tinggi berdiri dengan realizasi bulatan alam atas keadaannya dan hukum perbuatan dengan bulatan maqamnya — rahsia Qayyūmiyyahnya, maka nama al-Qayyūm nampak di negeri akhirat atas rahsia yang Ia letak dalam Kursi dari rahsia Qayyūmiyyah yang memikul langit, bumi, dan yang di dalamnya dengan rahsia Qayyūmiyyah yang Allah letak padanya. Dan ketahuilah bahwa ilmu nama Allah Yang Agung ialah dari ilmu ter mulia, dan ia seperti mutiara tersembunyi; dan perselisihan ulama dalam makna nama Agung atas tiga pandangan: pertama — bahawa bagi nama Agung setiap nama yang dijawab pada kesempitan; kedua — bahawa yang paling penting dari apa yang Allah sebut padanya ialah ucapan, maka di antaranya yang berkata ia al-Jalālah dan ia paling sahih, dan di antaranya yang berkata Dhū al-Jalāl wa-al-Ikrām, dan di antaranya yang berkata al-Laṭīf, dan di antaranya yang berkata Salām (ucapan Tuhan Maha Pemurah), dan di antaranya yang berkata al-Ḥannān al-Mannān Dhū al-Jalāl wa-al-Ikrām, dan di antaranya yang berkata permulaan besi [al-ḥadīd], dan di antaranya yang berkata akhir pengumpulan [al-ḥashr], dan di antaranya yang berkata al-Wadūd, dan di antaranya yang berkata dalam Surah al-Ḥajj pada firman-Nya 'Dan orang yang berhijrah di jalan Allah lalu dibunuh atau mati — nescaya Dia memberi mereka rezeki yang baik', dan di antaranya yang berkata permulaan huruf bercahaya surah, dan di antaranya yang berkata al-Māni', dan di antaranya yang berkata kalimah al-Jalālah bila diulang, dan di antaranya yang berkata nama-Nya al-'Alīm, dan di antaranya yang berkata al-'Alī al-'Aẓīm, dan di antaranya yang berkata persaksian tiada tuhan melainkan Allah; dan riwayat berlipat ganda dengan khabar sahih, dan hadis diriwayatkan daripadanya s.a.w.: 'Al-ẒABĀYĀ, Dhū al-Jalāl wa-al-Ikrām' — dan ia dalil qat'ie. Dan nama ini disebut dalam bahasa Suryani dengan khabar sahih.
