English:
— 523 —

"…the angels had been given this form, so he said to him: 'O Muḥammad, if you had seen Isrāfīl with his seventy wings, each wing the measure of all the wings together' — and the Prophet (may Allah bless him and grant him peace) saw him in the most complete description on the night of the Ascension, and that he diminishes until he becomes the measure of a sparrow when he mentions the greatness of Allah, and grows until he fills all the existences by the power of Allah. And likewise my master 'Abd al-Qādir — when he mentions the names, his heart flies in their meanings and magnifies [~] for their greatness, and rises at times for their honor and the loftiness of their structures; and he in both aspects is ascending and rising — and by Allah is the success. (Section) On the management of the high [names] in the bodies and the stimulators * . Know — may Allah set right me and you — that the names have a management; and what is chosen for the soul in it, of the minerals and the stones, is gold mixed — a fifth of gold and four-fifths of silver; and of the stones, the crystal and the agate — for there appears to it a tremendous effect, on condition of the preservation and the perseverance of the purity and the magnifying of the sanctities of Allah the Exalted. And as for the seven stimulators, they have a glorification suited to them, and it is their mention by which they glorify Allah; and he who disposes by it is averse — let him engrave each planet in its stone and its mine, and Allah subdues the acts of that planet in the essence of the speaker * . And the outcome: if you want to engrave these talismans, take any name you will of the names, or two names, in the meaning you want and the need — so spread it and break it and place it in the numbers that complete the breaking, and that the first of it appears as its last, so the letters mingle and agree, and you find the secret of that never broken. And the description of the breaking and the spreading is as I describe to you — its example in al-Ḥayy al-Qayyūm is thus:

[The square of al-Ḥayy al-Qayyūm — 6 columns ḥ ي q ى w م: row 1 = 40 / 6 / 10 / 100 / 10 / 8; row 2 = 8 / 40 / 6 / 10 / 100 / 10; row 3 = 10 / 8 / 40 / 6 / 10 / 100; row 4 = 100 / 10 / 8 / 40 / 6 / 10; row 5 = 10 / 100 / 10 / 8 / 40 / 6; row 6 = 6 / 10 / 100 / 10 / 8 / 40]

[The letter-circle of al-Ḥayy al-Qayyūm — 6 columns ḥ ي q ى w م: row 1 = ḥ / y / q / ى / w / m; row 2 = y / q / ى / w / m / ḥ; row 3 = q / ى / w / m / ḥ / y; row 4 = ى / w / m / ḥ / y / q; row 5 = w / m / ḥ / y / q / ى; row 6 = m / ḥ / y / q / ى / w]

And the description of the breaking: the spreading is in the square, and drop the repeated, there remain six lines, so there gather in it the special properties of the letters and they enter some into some, and the special properties of the numbers in their inner [aspects] which Allah deposited in them — and it is its specific act from the noble mention indicating the life in everything. And that the numerical talismans have special properties and benefits upon which most of the scholars agreed — and it is the mingling of the benefit and the harmonious benefit of the letter and the name. So if you multiply 30 × 35, it was the name al-Ḥayy * in the utterance, though it is six in the writing, for the doubled letter is by two letters, and the doubled yā' in the two names * — if multiplied by 7, the result is 35. And this talisman is of the compounds, and it has a strong effect in what is wanted of the obtaining and the gathering of things. So the outcome of the breaking is 42 letters — for we said 'alif lām ḥā' yā' *' and it is the name al-Ḥayy spread: 'alif lām ḥā' yā'' was 10, there resulted from it 6 non-repeated letters after the interpenetration: a l ḥ y, and so on… [page ends, truncated]

Indonesia:
— 523 —

"…malaikat diberi rupa ini, maka ia berkata kepadanya: 'Wahai Muḥammad, sekiranya engkau melihat Isrāfīl dengan tujuh puluh sayapnya, tiap sayap ukurannya sama dengan semua sayap' — dan Nabi s.a.w. melihatnya dalam sifat paling sempurna pada malam Isra', dan bahwa ia mengecil hingga jadi ukuran burung pipit bila menyebut keagungan Allah, dan membesar hingga memenuhi seluruh wujud dengan kuasa Allah. Dan begitu penghulu 'Abd al-Qādir — bila menyebut nama, hatinya terbang dalam maknanya dan membesarkan [~] untuk keagungannya, serta naik kadang-kadang untuk kemuliaan dan ketinggian binaannya; dan ia dalam kedua aspek naik dan meningkat — dan dengan Allah taufik. (Fasal) Tentang pengurusan nama tinggi dalam badan dan perangsang * . Ketahuilah — semoga Allah membaiki aku dan kamu — bahwa nama mempunyai pengurusan; dan apa yang dipilih untuk jiwa padanya, dari mineral dan batu, ialah emas bercampur — seperlima emas dan empat perlima perak; dan dari batu, kristal dan akik — kerana nampak padanya kesan dahsyat, dengan syarat pemeliharaan dan tekun suci serta membesarkan kesucian Allah Yang Maha Tinggi. Dan adapun tujuh perangsang, mereka mempunyai tasbih sesuai, iaitu dzikir mereka yang dengannya mereka bertasbih pada Allah; dan yang mengurus dengannya benci — hendaklah ia mengukir tiap planet dalam batu dan logamnya, dan Allah menundukkan perbuatan planet itu dalam zat pembicara * . Dan hasilnya: bila engkau mahu mengukir talisman ini, ambil mana nama yang dikehendaki dari nama, atau dua nama, dalam makna dikehendaki dan hajat — maka bentangkan dan pecahkan serta letak dalam bilangan yang menyempurnakan pemecahan, dan awalnya nampak sebagai akhirnya, maka huruf bercampur dan sepakat, dan engkau dapati rahsia itu tidak pernah putus. Dan sifat pemecahan dan pembentangan seperti kuterangkan: contohnya pada al-Ḥayy al-Qayyūm demikian:

[Segi empat al-Ḥayy al-Qayyūm — 6 lajur ḥ ي q ى w م: baris 1 = 40 / 6 / 10 / 100 / 10 / 8; baris 2 = 8 / 40 / 6 / 10 / 100 / 10; baris 3 = 10 / 8 / 40 / 6 / 10 / 100; baris 4 = 100 / 10 / 8 / 40 / 6 / 10; baris 5 = 10 / 100 / 10 / 8 / 40 / 6; baris 6 = 6 / 10 / 100 / 10 / 8 / 40]

[Bulatan huruf al-Ḥayy al-Qayyūm — 6 lajur ḥ ي q ى w م: baris 1 = ḥ / y / q / ى / w / m; baris 2 = y / q / ى / w / m / ḥ; baris 3 = q / ى / w / m / ḥ / y; baris 4 = ى / w / m / ḥ / y / q; baris 5 = w / m / ḥ / y / q / ى; baris 6 = m / ḥ / y / q / ى / w]

Dan sifat pemecahan: pembentangan dalam segi empat, dan gugurkan yang berulang, tinggal enam baris, maka terkumpul padanya sifat khusus huruf dan mereka masuk sebahagian ke sebahagian, serta sifat bilangan dalam batin yang Allah taruh padanya — dan ia perbuatan khusus dari dzikir mulia yang menunjukkan kehidupan dalam tiap perkara. Dan segi empat bilangan mempunyai sifat khusus dan faedah yang kebanyakan ulama sepakati — dan ia percampuran faedah dan faedah selaras huruf dan nama. Maka bila darab 30 × 35, ia nama al-Ḥayy * dalam sebutan, walaupun enam dalam tulisan, kerana huruf bertasydid dengan dua huruf, dan yā' bertasydid dalam dua nama * — bila darab 7, hasil 35. Dan talisman ini dari campuran, dan berkesan kuat dalam apa yang dikehendaki dari pencapaian dan pengumpulan perkara. Maka hasil pemecahan 42 huruf — kerana kami katakan 'alif lam ha ya' * dan ia nama al-Ḥayy terbentang: 'alif lam ha ya' jadi 10, terhasil daripadanya 6 huruf tidak berulang selepas jalinan: a l h y, dan begitu… [halaman berakhir, terpotong]
