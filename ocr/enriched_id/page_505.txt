English:
— 499 —

"…the Exalted returned to your brother his wrong. He said: 'O Lord, there remains not of my good deeds anything.' So He said to the wronged: 'He bears from his burdens to their measure.' Then the eyes of the Messenger of Allah (may Allah bless him and grant him peace) overflowed, and he said: 'That is for a tremendous day in which the people need who bears from them their burdens.' So Allah the Exalted says to the wronged: 'Raise your head' — and he raises it and looks at the Gardens and what is in them, and says 'O Lord, for which prophet or saint is this?' So Allah the Exalted says: 'This is for who gives the price.' He says: 'O Lord, and who owns the price of this?' So Allah says to him: 'You own it.' He says: 'By what?' He says: 'By your pardon of your brother.' He says: 'O Lord, I call You to witness that I have pardoned him.' So Allah the Exalted says: 'Take the hand of your brother and enter the Garden.' Then he (may Allah bless him and grant him peace) said: 'Fear Allah, fear Allah, and rectify between yourselves — for Allah the Exalted judges between the believers on the Day of Resurrection.' And he was asked about equity, so he said: 'None is capable of it but the Lord of lords.' And the special properties of this name are for extinguishing the anger of an angry one, if his name al-'Afw is added to it; and it is recited at the quarrel, and you say: 'O Allah, I ask You by Your name al-'Afw al-Muqsiṭ, that You extinguish from me the anger of so-and-so' — and it is so. And if it is written with the standing mention and a human carries it, he extinguishes by it everyone who had anger at him, and improves his anger by the permission of Allah the Exalted; and it is written also for a newborn who cries much, and it departs by the permission of Allah the Exalted. And this is its figure as you see, so understand. And as for the standing mention by it, you say (In the name of Allah the Merciful, the Compassionate): 'O Allah, You are al-Muqsiṭ the Just, You give justice to the wronged from the wrong-doer surrounding in the subtleties of what was and what will be in the worlds, the Aware of what the souls hide in the breasts and what the acts and the sayings reveal in all the affairs. You sought the justice and forbade the wrong. I ask You, O He who brought forth the justice in the bodily and spiritual world, and preferred the establishment of justice in the world of the human dominion by Your upheld, estimated dignity in the world of the expansion and the luminous ones, and You equalize the weights of the existents in the earth and the heavens — that there be equality in the essence of the bodily power and in the body of the spiritual power, that You pour into my heart from Your lordly lights for the witnessing of that Oneness. O Muqsiṭ, O Raḥmān, O Raḥīm.' (Section on His name the Exalted, al-Jāmi') (In the name of Allah the Merciful, the Compassionate) Know that al-Jāmi' is He who composes between the likes and the opposites. As for the gathering of Allah between the likes — He gathers the many creation of the human upon the face of the earth and musters them in one plain. As for the differing — from the heavens and the planets and the air and the earth and the sea and the animals and the plants and the mineral, and it is of different genera, and all of it differing in forms and colors and tastes and attributes, and He gathered them in the earth and gathered the all in the world — and that is a gathering of the flesh and the nerve and the veins and the brain and the blood and the rest of the humors in the animals. And as for the opposites — He gathers between the heat and the cold and the moisture and the dryness in the temperaments of the animals, and they are discordant opposites; and the obligation of the gathering and the detail of His gathering reached, except for who knows the detail of His gatherings in the world and the Hereafter — and this is a long speech. And know that al-Jāmi' from the human is he who gathers between the sight and the insight; and when the human is characterized by this name, there is attained for him the unveiling and he knows the way of the gathering in the Oneness, and Allah the Exalted opens upon his heart until he looks at the opposites and what resembles them. And this name has a magnificent retreat that gives its possessor the unveiling upon the realities of the names.

Indonesia:
— ٤٩٩ —

"…Yang Maha Tinggi mengembalikan kepada saudaramu kezalimannya. Ia berkata: 'Wahai Tuhanku, tidak tinggal dari amal baikku sesuatu.' Maka Ia berkata kepada yang dizalimi: 'Ia memikul dari bebannya menurut ukurannya.' Lalu mata Rasulullah s.a.w. berlinangan, dan ia bersabda: 'Itu bagi hari yang agung di mana manusia perlu yang memikul dari mereka beban mereka.' Maka Allah Yang Maha Tinggi berfirman kepada yang dizalimi: 'Angkat kepalamu' — maka ia angkat dan memandang Syurga serta apa yang di dalamnya, lalu berkata 'Wahai Tuhanku, untuk nabi atau wali mana ini?' Maka Allah Yang Maha Tinggi berfirman: 'Ini bagi si yang memberi harga.' Ia berkata: 'Wahai Tuhanku, dan siapa yang memiliki harga ini?' Maka Allah berfirman kepadanya: 'Engkau memilikinya.' Ia berkata: 'Dengan apa?' Ia berfirman: 'Dengan pemaafan engkau pada saudaramu.' Ia berkata: 'Wahai Tuhanku, aku jadikan Engkau saksi bahwa aku telah memaafkannya.' Maka Allah Yang Maha Tinggi berfirman: 'Pegang tangan saudaramu dan masuklah Syurga.' Lalu ia s.a.w. bersabda: 'Bertakwalah kepada Allah, bertakwalah, dan perbaikilah antara kamu — kerana Allah Yang Maha Tinggi menghukum antara mukmin pada Hari Kiamat.' Dan ia ditanya tentang kepastian, maka ia bersabda: 'Tiada yang mampu melainkan Tuhan semesta tuan.' Dan sifat khusus nama ini ialah memadam kemarahan yang marah, bila namanya al-'Afw ditambah kepadanya; dan ia dibaca saat pertengkaran, serta engkau ucapkan: 'Ya Allah, aku memohon dengan nama-Mu al-'Afw al-Muqsiṭ, agar Engkau padamkan dariku kemarahan si fulan' — dan ia menjadi demikian. Dan bila ditulis dengan dzikir berdiri serta manusia memakainya, ia padamkan dengannya setiap yang ada marah padanya, dan memperbaiki marahnya dengan izin Allah Yang Maha Tinggi; dan ia ditulis juga bagi bayi yang banyak menangis, dan ia hilang dengan izin Allah Yang Maha Tinggi. Dan ini gambarannya sebagaimana engkau lihat, maka fahamilah. Adapun dzikir berdirinya, engkau ucapkan (Bismillāh ar-Raḥmān ar-Raḥīm): 'Ya Allah, Engkau al-Muqsiṭ yang Adil, Engkau beri keadilan kepada yang dizalimi dari yang zalim meliputi halus apa yang telah dan akan ada dalam alam, yang Mengetahui apa yang disembunyikan jiwa dalam dada dan apa yang dizahirkan perbuatan serta kata dalam segala urusan. Engkau kehendaki keadilan dan melarang kezaliman. Aku memohon, wahai Yang mengadakan keadilan dalam alam jasad dan rohani, serta mengutamakan tegaknya keadilan dalam alam kerajaan manusia dengan kemuliaan-Mu yang dijaga dan ditakdirkan dalam alam kelapangan dan cahaya, serta Engkau samakan timbangan makhluk di bumi dan langit — agar ada keseimbangan dalam zat kuasa jasad dan dalam jasad kuasa rohani, agar Engkau curah ke dalam hatiku dari cahaya ketuhanan-Mu bagi menyaksikan keesaan itu. Wahai Muqsiṭ, wahai Raḥmān, wahai Raḥīm.' (Fasal nama-Nya Yang Maha Tinggi, al-Jāmi') (Bismillāh ar-Raḥmān ar-Raḥīm) Ketahuilah bahwa al-Jāmi' ialah Yang menghimpunkan antara yang sejenis dan yang berlawanan. Adapun himpunan Allah antara yang sejenis — Ia himpunkan makhluk manusia yang banyak di permukaan bumi serta kumpulkan mereka di satu padang. Adapun yang berbeza — dari langit, planet, udara, bumi, laut, haiwan, tumbuhan, dan mineral, dan ia dari genus berbeza, serta semuanya berbeza bentuk, warna, rasa, dan sifat, dan Ia himpunkan mereka di bumi serta himpunkan semuanya di alam — dan itu himpunan daging, saraf, urat, otak, darah, dan baki humur pada haiwan. Dan adapun yang berlawanan — Ia himpunkan antara panas dan sejuk dan lembap dan kering dalam tabiat haiwan, dan mereka bertentangan; dan kewajipan himpunan serta tafsil himpunan-Nya tercapai, kecuali bagi yang tahu tafsil himpunan-Nya di dunia dan akhirat — dan ini perbicaraan panjang. Dan ketahuilah bahwa al-Jāmi' dari manusia ialah yang menghimpunkan antara penglihatan dan keinsafan; dan bila manusia berakhlak dengan nama ini, tercapai baginya kasyaf serta ia tahu jalan himpunan dalam tauhid, dan Allah Yang Maha Tinggi bukakan atas hatinya hingga ia memandang yang berlawanan dan yang menyerupainya. Dan nama ini mempunyai khalwat agung yang memberi pemiliknya kasyaf atas hakikat nama.
