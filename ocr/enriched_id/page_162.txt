English:
— 156 —

The house of sainthood — its pillars were divided, / our masters in it are among the substitutes (abdāl): / between silence and constant withdrawal, / and hunger and lofty, pure sleeplessness.
* And he, upon him peace, said: "Silence begets knowledge of Allah; seclusion begets knowledge of the world; hunger begets knowledge of Satan; sleeplessness begets knowledge of the self." * And know that the righteous predecessors, may Allah be pleased with them, agreed that the divine opening (al-fatḥ al-rabbānī) and the ṣamadānī unveiling are not fitting for one whose stomach contains a mustard-seed's weight of food — and that is the limit of bodily ṣamadāniyya. There differed on it: it is said [the period] is two weeks, and the months with them are forty — which is what Allah stipulated for His interlocutor Mūsā, upon him peace, in the forty [days], that his stomach be purified of dense nourishments, so the spirituality of his spirit is strengthened, his intellect is clarified, his heart is fortified, and his self is sweetened. This is the ṣamadāniyya of spirits; the righteous predecessors set its limit at sixty days, and in it are attained the wonders of the Dominion, the subtleties of the Jabarūt, and the secrets of the dominion. * As for the ṣamadāniyya of intellects by the whole of the human essence — it is seventy days, and it is the end of the term for the ailing; and from it arises another specific genesis — specific lights no one among the masters of states nor the ranks of deeds has known — so the secrets are unveiled to him and the veils are lifted from his secrets; and he is the one who died by passing-away then was revived by permanence. And this is the last rank of ṣamadāniyya in humanity — the collective of its knowledge and kinds of its manifestations. * And know that the material of natural desires is not cut off except after general hunger — the ancient custom has run thus in the secrets of the essence. * As for the ṣamadāniyya of the obedient one, its limit is twenty-eight days or less; for the wayfarer at the beginnings of the secrets of ṣamadāniyya, forty-one days. * As for one whose custom was broken and whose self overcame him by its inclination to what it craves — they bound his evil and expelled him from the retreats by the spears of its invalidity from the divine sources and the faith-gifts of the divine. And some of them would decrease his eating each day by the measure of a date-stone; and among them is one who does not work at reducing the provision but works at delaying it gradually until his grading reaches seven days and ten days up to the full forty; and among them is one who measures his eating by a fresh stick and decreases his eating each day by the measure of the stick's drying. And Sahl, may Allah be pleased with him, said: "Whoever passes forty days, there appear to him the traces of power in the Dominion." We have unveiled the secrets of the journey and clarified the path — so understand.

{ And this is the Prayer of Sufficiency (ṣalāt al-kifāya) }
You pray six rak'ahs at any time; then you sit after completing the prayer and say: "Glory be to Allah, praise be to Allah, there is no god but Allah, and Allah is Greatest, and there is no power nor strength except by Allah the High, the Great. O Allah, send blessings upon our master Muḥammad and his family, his companions, and his wives the mothers of the believers, as You sent blessings upon Ibrāhīm and the family of Ibrāhīm in the worlds — indeed You are Praiseworthy, Glorious." Then after that you magnify and prostrate, and in your prostration recite the Opening of the Book seven times, and Āyat al-Kursī likewise, and the saying "There is no god but Allah alone, no partner has He; His is the dominion and His is the praise; He gives life and causes death, and He is over all things powerful" ten times. "O Allah, I ask You by the knots of might from Your Throne, and by the utmost of mercy from Your Book, and by the right of Your greatest, most tremendous Name, and Your highest eminence, and Your perfect words" — then he asks his need and raises his head; and that he slaughter a ram free of defects in an isolated place, a lawful slaughter facing the qiblah, and says at the slaughter: "O Allah, this is from You and to You, so make it my ransom and accept it from me"; and he digs a pit for it and buries it in the earth, then takes it out and cuts it into sixty portions and distributes it to the poor and needy, or feeds sixty [poor].

Indonesia:
— 156 —

Rumah kewalian — tiang-tiangnya terbagi, / para penolong kita di dalamnya dari kalangan abdāl: / antara diam dan mengasingkan diri terus-menerus, / serta lapar dan terjaga yang luhur dan suci.
* Dan beliau shallallahu 'alaihi wa sallam bersabda: "Diam memberikan ilmu mengenal Allah; uzlah memberikan ilmu tentang dunia; lapar memberikan ilmu tentang setan; terjaga memberikan ilmu tentang diri." * Dan ketahuilah bahwa salaf shalih radhiyallahu 'anhum telah sepakat bahwa pembukaan rabbānī dan pembukaan ṣamadānī tidak layak bagi orang yang perutnya masih mengandung makanan seberat biji sawi — dan itulah batas ṣamadāniyya jasmani. Terdapat perbedaan pendapat: ada yang mengatakan [tempohnya] dua minggu, dan bulan-bulan bagi mereka adalah empat puluh — yang merupakan apa yang Allah syaratkan bagi kekasih-Nya Mūsā 'alaihissalam dalam empat puluh [hari] agar perutnya disucikan dari makanan yang kasar, sehingga ruhani ruhnya menguat, akalnya jernih, hatinya kuat, dan jiwanya harum. Inilah ṣamadāniyya arwah; salaf shalih telah menetapkan batasnya enam puluh hari, dan di dalamnya tercapai keajaiban malakūt, nuansa Jabarūt, dan rahasia alam raya. * Adapun ṣamadāniyya 'uqūl dengan keseluruhan dzat insan adalah tujuh puluh hari, dan itulah akhir tempoh bagi orang yang sakit; dan darinya muncul genesis lain yang khusus — cahaya-cahaya khusus yang tidak dikenal oleh seorang pun dari ahli hal maupun tingkatan amalan; maka rahasia dibuka baginya dan tabir diangkat dari rahasia-rahasianya; dan ia adalah orang yang mati dengan fana kemudian dihidupkan dengan baqa. Inilah maqam terakhir ṣamadāniyya dalam kemanusiaan — kumpulan ilmunya dan jenis-jenis manifestasinya. * Dan ketahuilah bahwa materi syahwat alami tidak terpotong kecuali setelah lapar secara menyeluruh — kebiasaan purba berjalan demikian dalam rahasia dzat. * Adapun ṣamadāniyya orang yang taat, batasnya dua puluh delapan hari atau kurang; bagi salik di permulaan rahasia ṣamadāniyya, empat puluh satu hari. * Adapun orang yang kebiasaannya rusak dan nafsunya menguasainya karena condong pada apa yang diingininya — mereka mengikat kejahatannya dan mengeluarkannya dari khalwa dengan tombak kebatilannya dari sumber-sumber rabbānī dan anugerah iman ilahi. Sebagian mereka mengurangi makannya setiap hari seukuran biji kurma; dan di antara mereka ada yang tidak mengurangi makanan tetapi menundanya secara berangsur hingga tingkatannya mencapai tujuh hari dan sepuluh hari sampai genap empat puluh; dan di antaranya ada yang mengukur makannya dengan sebatang ranting segar dan mengurangi makannya setiap hari seukuran pengeringan ranting itu. Dan Sahl radhiyallahu 'anhu berkata: "Barangsiapa menyepi empat puluh hari, nampak padanya jejak kuasa dalam malakūt." Kami telah membuka rahasia perjalanan dan menjelaskan jalan — maka pahamilah.

{ Dan ini adalah Shalat al-Kifāya (Shalat Kecukupan) }
Engkau shalat enam rakaat pada waktu kapan pun; kemudian engkau duduk setelah selesai shalat dan membaca: "Subḥānallāh, al-ḥamdulillāh, lā ilāha illallāh, wallāhu akbar, wa lā ḥawla wa lā quwwata illā billāhil 'aliyyil 'aẓīm. Ya Allah, limpahkanlah shalawat atas penghulu kita Muḥammad, keluarga, sahabat, dan istri-istrinya para ibu orang mukmin, sebagaimana Engkau limpahkan shalawat atas Ibrāhīm dan keluarga Ibrāhīm di seluruh alam — sesungguhnya Engkau Maha Terpuji lagi Mulia." Kemudian setelah itu engkau bertakbir dan sujud, serta membaca dalam sujudmu Pembukaan Kitab tujuh kali, dan Āyat al-Kursī demikian, serta ucapan "Tiada tuhan selain Allah sendiri, tiada sekutu bagi-Nya; milik-Nya kerajaan dan milik-Nya segala puji; Yang menghidupkan dan mematikan, dan Dia Mahakuasa atas segala sesuatu" sepuluh kali. "Ya Allah, aku memohon kepada-Mu dengan ikatan keperkasaan dari 'Arsy-Mu, dan puncak rahmat dari Kitab-Mu, dan hak nama-Mu yang agung lagi maha agung, dan kemuliaan-Mu yang tertinggi, dan kalimat-kalimat-Mu yang sempurna" — kemudian ia meminta hajatnya dan mengangkat kepalanya; serta menyembelih seekor domba jantan yang bebas cacat di tempat yang sepi, sembelihan yang syar'i menghadap kiblat, dan membaca saat menyembelih: "Ya Allah, ini dari-Mu dan kepada-Mu, maka jadikanlah ia tebusanku dan terimalah dariku"; lalu ia menggali lubang untuknya dan menguburkannya di tanah, kemudian mengeluarkannya dan memotongnya menjadi enam puluh bahagian serta membagikannya kepada fakir dan miskin, atau memberi makan enam puluh [orang miskin].
