Treatise: Zahr al-Murūj fī Dalā'il al-Burūj [The Blossom of the Meadows in the Indications of the Zodiacal Signs]
* In the name of Allah, the Most Merciful, the Compassionate *
Praise belongs to Allah — glorious and sublime — the Raiser of the high heavens, who placed in the sky the zodiacal signs and adorned them for the beholders, and placed the stars in the darknesses of land and sea as guidance for the travelers. I praise Him — glorified and exalted is He — and thank Him with abundant thanks; and I testify that there is no god but He, the Creator of the heavens and the Governor of the affairs of beings in expansion and contraction; and I testify that our master Muḥammad is His servant and His messenger, the possessor of the loftiest station, who drew near and descended until he was two bows' length or nearer — may Allah the Exalted, by the majesty of His greatness, bless and grant peace upon his noble self and his exalted station, and upon his family, the masters, the illustrious, and his companions, the leaders, the guides, and upon those who follow their guidance among mankind as long as a star has risen in the sky of the zodiac and traveled * . (Now then) — says the one hoping from his Lord, the manifest grace, 'Abd al-Qādir al-Ḥusaynī al-Adhamī, son of 'Abd al-Qādir, the servant of the function of the noble chamber in the fragrant, elevated Muḥammadan room — may Allah be for him by His pure guardianship and the watching of His care and His protection, amen, O Allah, amen. This is a concise treatise of the pearls of gems, precious in the jewels of the twelve zodiacal signs, from each of which is drawn a judgment — I named it (Zahr al-Murūj fī Dalā'il al-Burūj). I gathered the scattered gems of its benefits and versified the scattered benefits thereof, as completion of my treatise (Lata'if al-Ishārah) on the special properties of the wandering planets; and I ask Allah the Exalted — He is the pearls of it — that He benefit by it the specific and the general. Upon Him I rely and to Him I turn; and He — glorified is He — is over every soul for what it earned, a watcher; and the best of hopes and the answerer and the most generous bestower and the recompenser for the best of the best; and to Him returns the whole affair; and Allah suffices me and is enough; and peace upon His servants whom He chose.

Risalah: Zahr al-Murūj fī Dalā'il al-Burūj [Mekar Padang dalam Petunjuk Buruj]
* Dengan nama Allah Yang Maha Pemurah Lagi Mengasihani *
Segala puji bagi Allah — maha agung dan maha tinggi — Yang Mengangkat langit tinggi, yang menjadikan di langit tanda zodiak dan menghiasinya bagi yang memerhati, serta meletak bintang dalam kegelapan darat dan laut sebagai petunjuk musafir. Aku memuji-Nya — maha suci dan maha tinggi Dia — dan bersyukur dengan syukur melimpah; dan aku bersaksi tiada tuhan melainkan Dia, Pencipta langit dan Urusan makhluk dalam luas dan sempit; dan aku bersaksi penaung kami Muḥammad ialah hamba dan rasul-Nya, pemilik maqam tertinggi, yang mendekat dan turun hingga dua ufuk busur atau lebih dekat — semoga Allah Yang Maha Tinggi dengan keagungan kebesaran-Nya selawat dan salam atas zat mulianya dan maqam agungnya, serta keluarganya tuan-tuan mulia, dan sahabatnya pemimpin pembimbing, dan atas pengikut petunjuk mereka selama bintang terbit di langit zodiak dan berjalan * . (Adapun kemudian) — berkata yang mengharap dari Tuhannya limpah kurnia, 'Abd al-Qādir al-Ḥusaynī al-Adhamī, anak 'Abd al-Qādir, khadam fungsi bilik mulia dalam ruang Muḥammad wangi lagi tinggi — semoga Allah bersamanya dengan pemeliharaan suci-Nya dan pengawasan jagaan-Nya serta perlindungan-Nya, amin ya Allah amin. Ini risalah ringkas mutiara permata, berharga dalam permata dua belas buruj, dari tiap satunya diambil hukum — kunanakan (Zahr al-Murūj fī Dalā'il al-Burūj). Aku kumpulkan mutiara bertaburan faedahnya dan nazamkan faedah berseleraknya, sebagai penyempurnaan risalahku (Lata'if al-Ishārah) tentang sifat khusus planet wandaran; dan aku memohon Allah Yang Maha Tinggi — Dia permata itu — agar Dia memberi manfaat dengannya pada khas dan am. Pada-Nya aku berserah dan kepada-Nya aku kembali; dan Ia — maha suci Dia — atas tiap jiwa bagi apa diusahakannya, pengawas; dan sebaik harapan serta memperkenankan dan penganugerah mulia serta pembalas bagi terbaik dari terbaik; dan kepada-Nya kembali segala urusan; dan cukuplah Allah bagiku; dan salam atas hamba-Nya yang dipilih.
