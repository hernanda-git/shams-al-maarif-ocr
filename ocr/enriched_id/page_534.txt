- ٥٢٨ -

"…and the measure of the peers, and the gathering of the opposites, and His ruling over all the creatures by the exhaustion — so glorified is He, from a producer who produced the creatures and perfected the made things without attempts and without instruments; only His command — when He wills a thing, He says to it 'Be,' and it is. The Protector — screening with the light of His splendor the eclipses, and revolving by His support and His industries the spheres, and the necks of the tyrants and the kings submitted to the glory of His dominion. I ask You by all that Your knowledge encompassed and Your forbearance embraced, and by Your beautiful names and Your lofty attributes, and Your boons that are not counted, and by Your knowledge in which the absent and the present are equal, and by Your perfect words which no pious nor immoral oversteps, and by the light of Your noble face — and I ask You, O Allah, until there is not beyond it a target, nor after it an end, nor above it a named [being], that You bless our master Muhammad, Your trustworthy servant and Your clear truthful messenger and the seal of Your prophets and Your sent ones, and upon his family and his companions and his wives and his most noble offspring, and upon all the prophets and the sent ones, and upon all the people of Your obedience together. And protect us, O Allah, from the evil of what You created and originated and brought into being, and the evil of what enters the earth and what comes out of it, and the evil of what descends from the sky and what ascends in it, and from the evil of every creature You take by the forelock — indeed my Lord is on a straight path. O Allah, grant us of knowledge the most beneficial, and of deed the loftiest, and of provision the widest, and of speech the most truthful, and of certainty the most apt, and of good the most complete, and of patience the most beautiful, and of judgment the most just, and of piety the most enduring, and of guidance the greatest, and of life the most pleasant, and of sight the most prudent, and of mercy the noblest, and of blessing the most comprehensive, and of well-being the most exalted, and of worship the best. O Allah, protect us from the evil of the resting-place and convey us to the good of the return, and secure us at the greatest fright, and steady us at the terror of the unveiling, and do not disgrace us at the heads of the witnesses in that gathering. O Allah, the sins that preceded us to You, and what we advanced and what we delayed in the written Tablet — it awaits us, and we await the mercy that encompassed everything and overflowed upon every living. O Allah, realize our hope by what we look at of Your mercy, and secure us from what we fear, and do not take us to account for what we advanced, and forgive us what we committed. O Allah, give us of the good certainty what eases for us the awaiting of death, and grant us of our beautiful opinion what we are certain attains the wish, and protect us from the wrong of the wrongdoers and the malice of the misguided haters. O Allah, give us the reward of those who return and recompense us the recompense of the good-doers, and gather us with the God-fearing and admit us by Your mercy among Your righteous servants — let us not go astray in any of our states, and employ us in what pleases You from us, and make [it] a price from You; and O our hope, from You a helper. O Allah, preserve for us our knowledge and our deed. O Allah, grant us the good turning to You and the listening to You and the understanding from You and the insight in Your affair, and the penetrating in Your obedience and the persistence upon Your will and the hastening to Your service and the good courtesy in Your treatment, and the submission to You and the contentment with Your bounty. My God, how shall we converse with You in the prayers — who disobeys You in the privacies — but for Your forbearance? Or how shall he call You in the needs — who forgets You at the desires — but for Your bounty? Or how do the eyes sleep while every night they say 'Is there a repentant? Is there a seeker of forgiveness? Is there an asker?' so He gives him his ask. Or how is he cut off from You — who was not cut off from these messages? Or how is the remaining sold for the vanishing, and our days are few days. O most caring of every stranger and O intimate of every sorrowful — any cut-off to You who was not sufficed, or any seeker who was not satisfied by Your mercy, or who emigrated any emigration in Your truth so it did not reach him, or who was alone with Your mention so You did not console him, or any caller who called You so You did not answer him. And it is related from You that You said: 'And I was not angry at anyone like My anger at one who committed a sin and magnified it in the side of Allah's forgiveness — who prevents whom He is angry at, [upon] whom…' [page ends; the sentence continues on the next page]
