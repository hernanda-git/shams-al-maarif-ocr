الروحانيات والعزلة فيه محمودة ومن ولد فيه كان رديئاً مشؤوماً كذاباً نماماً فاجراً بخوره قشر رمان

English:
— 22 —

A happy one, in which there descends into the world, by the permission of God Most High, a mixed Ruhaniyyah that does evil in it and its opposite, and it is suitable for the place in it and the knots, and what was intermediate among the works. And the making of the new is disliked in it, and no talisman is worked in it, and the Ruhaniyyah are not treated in it. And solitude in it is praiseworthy. And whoever is born in it was base, ill-omened, a liar, a tale-bearer, a libertine. Its incense: pomegranate peel and mastic. (The saying concerning the station of al-Nu‘aim, and this is its description) . . . . . . And it has the letter ra’ if the moon descends in it, and it is a fiery, happy planet, unmixed, in which there descends into the world, blessed, happy, and it calls to affection and fortune and happiness, and it is praiseworthy in outcome in all conditions, and the management of honored industries is suitable in it, and judgment and admonitions and jurisprudential sciences and the making of talismans begin in it, and build a building, and plant the trees, and wear the new, for whoever wears it will remain in joy and gladness until it wears out. And whoever is born in it was blessed, happy, successful in all his movements. Its incense: frankincense. And God knows best. (The saying concerning the station of al-Balda, and this is its description) . . . . . . And it has the letter shin if the moon descends in it, and it is an inauspicious fiery planet; a Ruhaniyyah descends in it that works in it for enmity, hatred, severance, the honored stone, and the exalted jewel. And the Ruhaniyyah are not treated in it, and no planting is planted in it, and no travel is undertaken in it, and no mixing with kings, and no marriage, and do not buy slaves, and do not wear the new, and do not do any work from the works. And whoever is born in it will be unfortunate, a trickster. Its incense: spikenard and oud. And God knows best. (The saying concerning the station of Sa‘d al-Dhabih, and this is its description) . . . . . . And it has the letter tha’ if the moon descends in it, and it is an earthy, inauspicious, mixed planet; a Ruhaniyyah descends in it that works in it for hatred, enmity, and severance, and the outcomes of matters and works are not praised in it, and the kings are moved in it with anger and wrath, and buying and selling are completed in it, and digging, excavation, and agriculture are suitable in it, and hidden things and treasures come out in it, and secrets are concealed. And whoever is born in it was handsome, blessed, eager for the world, a trickster. Its incense: safflower. And God knows best. (The saying concerning the station of Sa‘d Bula‘, and this is its description) . . . . . . And it has the letter ta’ if the moon descends in it, and it is a mixed planet; there descends into the world in it, by the permission of God Most High, a Ruhaniyyah that does evil and its opposite, and it is the good and the bad. And it is suitable in it to buy slaves and mamluks, and it is suitable for buying beasts of burden and speaking with the shaykhs and the important matters of agriculture, and splitting rivers and digging wells and what is like that of hard works and fires and making foods. And whoever is born in it will be blessed, righteous. Its incense: chamomile. And God knows best. (The saying concerning the station of Sa‘d al-Su‘ud, and this is its description) . . . . . . And it has the letter ha’ if the moon descends in it, and it is a mixed planet from earth and air; a Ruhaniyyah descends in it that erases the last of what was before it, and all works are suitable in it. So begin in it with the making of love and affection and what is like that of mending hearts, and treat the Ruhaniyyah in it, and meet kings and chiefs and holders of offices and others, and do in it whatever you wish of acts of affection; your work will succeed. And whoever is born in it loves the righteous. Its incense: oud and mastic. And God knows best. (The saying concerning the station of Sa‘d al-Akhbiyah, and this is its description) . . . . . . And it has the letter dhal if the moon descends in it, and it is a windy planet; a Ruhaniyyah descends into the world in it that works in it for severance, seditions, hatred, division, and wars, and the works are not completed in it, and if they are completed they are not praiseworthy, and the sick are not treated in it, nor the Ruhaniyyah, and no talisman is worked in it, and no description of al-kimya and al-alsimya is managed in it. And whoever is born in it will be wicked, unbelieving. Its incense: frankincense, anzarut, and pepper. And God knows best. (The saying concerning the station of al-Fargh al-Muqaddam, and this is its description) . . . . . . And it has the letter dad.
