English:
— ٥٤٨ —

(The Twenty-First Intent: on the questions of the practice of geomancy) The correct practice of geomancy is not free of one of two matters: either it is diagonal [qaṭrī] or it is side [ḍil'ī] — by looking to the Balance of the work, which is the fifteenth house: if [the two figures] are two odds, it is diagonal; otherwise it is side. And in both states it is not but even; for if it comes out odd, there is an error in the work, as was established — so those are the questions of the practice. (The Twenty-Second Intent: on the rules of the connotation [ḍamīr]) It is recorded in the face of the Ascendant's figure the connotation of the struck geomancy, and in its rank from the last of the mothers' houses to the last of the daughters' houses — so it is confined to the five inscribed houses without figures, according to the ranks of the generality of the figures. There appears in the particulars of that the type of the implied affair — one or more — according to the expansion of the particulars and the encompassing of the relatings. So if what is manifest in the Ascendant is the figure of Ṭarīq, the appearance of the connotation is in the fourth of the houses — and it may be in the fourth of the figures subsisting in it, which is the last of the mothers; and if what is above it is the rank of the figure of al-Jumla in the daughters' figures — and movement is not cut except in the seventh and tenth houses; so if it comes out from two hidden [figures], it is light, and if from two heavy [figures], it is heavy. And it has been said to restrict it to [the connotation] — and Allah the Exalted knows best. ((The Twenty-Third Intent: on the rule of the sought [al-maṭlūb] in geomancy)) The sought in the struck geomancy is the sought of the manifest Ascendant-figure in the geomancy as it is in the settling of the circle — so if it is sought manifest in the geomancy, one looks to its house in the circle of settling and counts from it to the house in which it appeared in the settling of the geomancy, whichever house it was. So if its appearance is by considering its alighting in fortunate houses, it indicates the happiness of the sought; and if its course is to inauspicious houses, it indicates the requirement of their relatings — e.g., [if] the Ascendant was in the second house, the inward threshold ['atabah al-dākhilah], sought by it in the settling of the circle [is] the outward victory [naṣrah khārijah] — it arose in the settling of the geomancy in the twelfth house; its alighting in the fourth is fortunate, indicating its happiness; or it arose in the fourteenth, recorded [as] its course to the sixth house, inauspicious, indicating what its relatings indicate in it. ((The Twenty-Fourth Intent: on the rules of the sought)) The figure of the sought in the geomancy is the subject of the rule of the look to the connection of the sought and its absence — by combining the figure of the sought with the master of the house in which it is sought. So if the resulting figure is fortunate, the sought is obtained in the easiest manner; and if inauspicious, it is obtained after toil and difficulty; and if the figure generated from them is external, nothing is obtained — if it is inauspicious, the prevention is without his choice; and if fortunate, the prevention is by his choice. And if the figure generated from them is inverted, the sought is inverted from face to face; so if the inverted figure is fortunate, the sought is obtained, and if inauspicious, nothing is obtained. And if the resulting figure is fixed, it is delayed a time — so if fortunate, it is obtained after that period, and if inauspicious, nothing is obtained — and Allah the Exalted knows best. ((The Twenty-Fifth Intent: on the concealment of the figure of the sought)) If the figure of the Ascendant's sought in the geomancy from the settling of the circle did not arise manifest in the struck geomancy, then either: it is taken in the Ascendant — the figure of the sought is taken and combined with the sixteen figures that are in the struck geomancy in succession; so if it is present in it, [one] counts from its house to the house in which it appeared — so if its appearance is in fortunate houses, it indicates the happiness of the sought; otherwise [it indicates] according to the rule mentioned; or [the sought] is absent — the figure that alighted in the house of the sought is taken and combined with the figure of the sought, and from them arises its rule as described, but it indicates slowness of attainment. ((The Twenty-Sixth Intent: on the rule of the inference [istidlāl] in geomancy))

Indonesia:
— ٥٤٨ —

(Maqasad Dua Puluh Satu: tentang soalan amalan geomancy) Amalan geomancy yang betul tidak lepas dari satu dari dua keadaan: sama ada pepenjuru [qaṭrī] atau sisi [ḍil'ī] — dengan melihat Neraca kerja, iaitu rumah kelima belas: jika [dua angka] ganjil dua, ia pepenjuru; jika tidak ia sisi. Dan dalam kedua keadaan ia tidak melainkan genap; kerana jika keluar ganjil, ada silap dalam kerja, sebagaimana ditetapkan — maka itu soalan amalan. (Maqasad Dua Puluh Dua: tentang hukum konotasi [ḍamīr]) Direkam pada wajah angka Ascenden konotasi geomancy dipukul, dan dalam pangkatnya dari akhir rumah ibu-ibu ke akhir rumah anak-anak — maka terbatas pada lima rumah terukir tanpa angka, menurut pangkat keumuman angka. Terzahir dalam perincian itu jenis urusan tersirat — satu atau lebih — menurut keluasan perincian dan liputan kaitan. Maka jika yang nyata dalam Ascenden ialah angka Ṭarīq, kemunculan konotasi dalam rumah keempat — dan mungkin dalam keempat angka yang ada padanya, iaitu akhir ibu-ibu; dan jika di atasnya pangkat angka al-Jumla dalam angka anak-anak — dan gerakan tidak dipotong melainkan rumah ketujuh dan kesepuluh; maka jika keluar dari dua tersembunyi, ia ringan, dan jika dari dua berat, ia berat. Dan dikatakan membataskannya — dan Allah Yang Maha Tinggi lebih mengetahui. ((Maqasad Dua Puluh Tiga: tentang hukum yang dicari [al-maṭlūb] dalam geomancy)) Yang dicari dalam geomancy dipukul ialah yang dicari angka Ascenden nyata dalam geomancy sebagaimana dalam penetapan bulatan — maka jika dicari nyata dalam geomancy, pandang rumahnya dalam bulatan penetapan dan kira daripadanya ke rumah munculnya dalam penetapan geomancy, rumah mana jua. Maka jika kemunculannya dengan pertimbangan pendaratannya dalam rumah bertuah, ia menunjuk kebahagiaan yang dicari; dan jika perjalanannya ke rumah sial, ia menunjuk keperluan kaitannya — misalnya, [jika] Ascenden dalam rumah kedua, ambang dalam ['atabah al-dākhilah], dicari olehnya dalam penetapan bulatan [ialah] kemenangan luar [naṣrah khārijah] — ia bangkit dalam penetapan geomancy di rumah kedua belas; pendaratannya dalam keempat bertuah, menunjuk kebahagiaannya; atau ia bangkit dalam keempat belas, direkam perjalanannya ke rumah keenam, sial, menunjuk apa kaitannya tunjuk padanya. ((Maqasad Dua Puluh Empat: tentang hukum yang dicari)) Angka yang dicari dalam geomancy ialah subjek hukum pandangan ke hubungan yang dicari dan ketiadaannya — dengan menggabung angka yang dicari dengan tuan rumah di mana ia dicari. Maka jika angka terhasil bertuah, yang dicari diperoleh dengan paling mudah; dan jika sial, diperoleh setelah lelah dan susah; dan jika angka dijana daripadanya luar, tiada diperoleh — jika sial, pencegahan tanpa pilihannya; dan jika bertuah, pencegahan dengan pilihannya. Dan jika angka dijana daripadanya terbalik, yang dicari terbalik dari muka ke muka; maka jika angka terbalik bertuah, yang dicari diperoleh, dan jika sial, tiada diperoleh. Dan jika angka terhasil tetap, ia ditangguh suatu masa — maka jika bertuah, diperoleh selepas tempoh itu, dan jika sial, tiada diperoleh — dan Allah Yang Maha Tinggi lebih mengetahui. ((Maqasad Dua Puluh Lima: tentang tersembunyinya angka yang dicari)) Jika angka yang dicari Ascenden dalam geomancy dari penetapan bulatan tidak bangkit nyata dalam geomancy dipukul, maka sama ada: diambil dalam Ascenden — angka yang dicari diambil dan digabung dengan enam belas angka dalam geomancy dipukul bersilih; maka jika ada padanya, [dikira] dari rumahnya ke rumah munculnya — maka jika kemunculannya dalam rumah bertuah, ia menunjuk kebahagiaan yang dicari; jika tidak [menunjuk] menurut hukum disebut; atau [yang dicari] tiada — angka yang mendarat dalam rumah yang dicari diambil dan digabung dengan angka yang dicari, dan daripadanya bangkit hukumnya sebagaimana dijelaskan, tetapi ia menunjuk kelambatan pencapaian. ((Maqasad Dua Puluh Enam: tentang hukum istidlāl [inferens] dalam geomancy))
