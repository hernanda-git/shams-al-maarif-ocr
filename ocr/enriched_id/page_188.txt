( The Fifty-Seventh Chapter: On His name, exalted, { al-Ḥamīd } ) This faithful pearl and majestic secret — whoever abounds in its remembrance is one praised in all traits, thanked in deeds, magnified before all people; and whoever writes it in a glass cup and gives it to drink to any sick person — Allah, exalted, cures him; and it is fitting as a remembrance for one whose name is Maḥmūd; and whoever realises this name is one of praised character; and whose unveiling is complete is the most praised of you.
