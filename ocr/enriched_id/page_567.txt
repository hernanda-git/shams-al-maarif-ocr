والدور والأرضين والعقار وما في ذلك من مطابخ الأفكار ملامح الأنظار ويدل على جماعات
الناس وعلى الأماكن التي فيها الرطوبة وعلى كل عمل يكون من الرطوبة وعلى المواضع الكثيرة
الحيات والعقارب من الماء وعلى كل ما فيه وعلى كل شيء تحت الأرض مدفون وعلى الأبيض

يابس صفراوي سعيد محمود ما يلي وتد الأرض سابع الأمكنة في القوة والحمد والخبرة كوكبه الشمس

الوجه الثاني على هيئة رجل على رأسه إكليل من ريحان أبيض وبيده قوس طلاسم شؤون استطالة
السفلة ونحو ذلك وفي الوجه الثالث على هيئة شيخ زنجي قبيح المنظر فيه فاكهة ويفيده إبريق يوافق
لطلاسم المودة والمحبة له من الحروف الهاء والفاء وله من نسبة الإنسان من المخرج السرة ومن
الأعضاء الباطنة القلب ومن الظاهرة الفقرات وجهة الأنف الأيمن والعين اليمنى نهاراً واليسرى ليلاً
وله الأمراض الصفراوية وله من الطعوم كل عذب وكل حلو اشتراكاً وله من الألوان كل أحمر وله
من المعادن كل براق لطيف كالياقوت وكالذهب وله من الأمكنة والبقاع مجالس الملوك والحصون
والقلاع وله من الحيوان كل لطيف كالإنسان كالفرس وطيور الصيد وله من النبات كل ما طال

"…[Cancer:] Feminine, nocturnal, cardinal, northern, descending, straight, wide, silent, dark, watery, cold, moist, phlegmatic, fortunate, praiseworthy — the peg of the earth [watad al-arḍ], and it is southern, fourth of the places in strength and choice. Its planet the Moon — in its fifteenth degree is the exaltation of Jupiter; and in it the woe of Saturn and the depression of Mars. One looks to it in its faces: its planet the Moon, then Venus, then Mercury. Its triangle [is with] Scorpio and Pisces, and it is the first of the summer season. Its ascendant: in the first face, in the form of a man of crooked face and fingers, white of feet like the leaves of trees — accords for talismans of play and adornment; in the second face, in the form of a beautiful woman upon her head a crown of green basil, and in her hand a rod of lotus — accords for talismans of grace and joy; in the third face, in the form of a man whose legs are like the turtle, upon him ornaments of gold, and in his hand a snake — accords for talismans of the reaching of affairs and the obtaining of needs and the execution of speech by dominance and subjugation. It has, of letters: the dāl disregarded, the 'ayn, and the ghayn; and of the relation of man, from the orifice the mouth; from the inner organs the lung and the nature and what the ribs contain; and from the outer, the speech (sharing with Aries), the left side of the nose (sharing with Libra); and it has the phlegmatic illnesses; and of tastes, the salty; and of colors, white and every dusty color; and of places, the groves and the shores; and of metals, every watery jewel; and of animals and plants, every watery and the aerial birds. It is specific to the affairs of the consequences of deaths and fathers and mothers and elders and the great and the majestic appearances and the houses and the lands and the real-estate and what is in that of the kitchens of thoughts and the features of sights; and it indicates the groups of people, and the places in which is moisture, and every work that is from moisture, and the places abundant in snakes and scorpions of water, and everything in it, and everything buried under the earth, and the white of everything, and the metals and everything that comes out of them — and it attaches like alchemy; and the documents and the books, and everything in which is the color white and dust; and what has attended of affairs and what is in it, and strength in everything, and the swift turning of the affair which is in it to other than it — by the necessity of weakness and darkening, by its being a sign loving, feminine.

( The Fifth Sign — Leo [al-Asad] )
Masculine, diurnal, fixed, eastern, descending, straight, wide, having a voice, barren, dispersed, standing in its movement, fiery, hot, dry, yellow-bilious, fortunate, praiseworthy — what follows: the peg of the earth, seventh of the places in strength and praise and choice. Its planet the Sun — there is in it no exaltation for any of the planets except in the seventh degree is the rising of al-Shi'rā al-Yamāniyyah [Sirius], and in it the woe of Saturn. One looks to it in its faces: Saturn, Jupiter, and Mars. Its ascendant: in the first face, in the form of a man of filthy clothes, with another like the face of the wolf or the dog, looking to the north — accords for talismans of strength and activity and dominance; in the second face, in the form of a man upon his head a crown of white basil, and in his hand a bow — talismans of the affairs of the elongation of the base ones and the like; in the third face, in the form of an ugly black elder, in him fruit and he is given a ewer — accords for talismans of affection and love. It has, of letters: the hā' and the fā'; and of the relation of man, from the orifice the navel; from the inner organs the heart; from the outer the vertebrae, the right side of the nose and the right eye by day and the left by night; and it has the yellow-bile illnesses; and of tastes, every pleasant and every sweet (sharing); and of colors, every red; and of metals, every shining delicate like the ruby and like gold; and of places and locations, the councils of kings and the fortresses and the citadels; and of animals, every delicate like man like the horse and the hunting birds; and of plants, everything of long fruit like the date-palm and like the olive. It is specific to the affairs relating to children, sons and daughters, and what is hoped in that…

"…[Cancer:] Betina, malam, kardinal, utara, turun, lurus, lebar, senyap, gelap, berair, sejuk, lembap, berlendir, bertuah, terpuji — pasak bumi [watad al-arḍ], dan ia selatan, keempat tempat dalam kuat dan pilihan. Planetnya Bulan — di darjah kelimanya ketinggian Musytari; dan padanya kecelakaan Zuhal serta kerendahan Marikh. Dipandang padanya dalam wajah: planetnya Bulan, lalu Zuhrah, lalu Utarid. Segi tiganya [dengan] Scorpio dan Pisces, dan ia permulaan musim panas. Ascendeninya: wajah pertama, lelaki muka bengkok dan jari, kaki putih seperti daun pokok — sesuai talisman main dan hias; wajah kedua, wanita cantik di kepala mahkota selasih hijau, di tangan ranting teratai — sesuai talisman rahmat dan kegembiraan; wajah ketiga, lelaki kaki seperti kura-kura, pada badan perhiasan emas, di tangan ular — sesuai talisman capai urusan dan peroleh hajat serta laksana kata dengan dominan dan tunduk. Ia ada huruf: dāl diabaikan, 'ayn, dan ghayn; dan hubungan manusia, liang mulut; dari organ dalam paru dan fitrah serta apa rusuk isi; dari luar tutur kata (kongsi Aries), sisi kiri hidung (kongsi Libra); dan penyakit berlendir; dan rasa masin; dan warna putih dan setiap berdebu; dan tempat rimba dan pantai; dan logam setiap permata berair; dan haiwan tumbuhan setiap berair dan burung udara. Ia khusus urusan akibat kematian serta bapa ibu serta tua dan besar dan zahir agung serta rumah dan tanah dan harta serta apa padanya dari dapur fikiran dan ciri pemandangan; dan menunjuk kumpulan manusia, tempat ada lembapan, dan tiap kerja dari lembapan, tempat banyak ular serta kala air, dan segala padanya, dan segala tertanam bawah tanah, dan putih segala, dan logam serta segala keluar darinya — dan melekat seperti alkimia; dan dokumen buku, dan segala warna putih debu; dan apa hadir urusan dan apa padanya, serta kuat dalam segala, dan pantas berubah urusan padanya ke lain — kerana keperluan lemah dan gelap, ia tanda penyayang betina.

( Tanda Kelima — Leo [al-Asad] )
Jantina lelaki, siang, tetap, timur, turun, lurus, lebar, berbunyi, mandul, berselerak, tegak dalam gerakannya, berapi, panas, kering, hempedu kuning, bertuah, terpuji — apa menyusul: pasak bumi, ketujuh tempat dalam kuat dan puji serta pilihan. Planetnya Matahari — tiada padanya ketinggian mana planet kecuali di darjah ketujuh terbit al-Shi'rā al-Yamāniyyah [Sirius], dan padanya kecelakaan Zuhal. Dipandang padanya dalam wajah: Zuhal, Musytari, dan Marikh. Ascendeninya: wajah pertama, lelaki pakaian kotor, bersama lain seperti muka serigala atau anjing, pandang utara — sesuai talisman kuat aktif dominan; wajah kedua, lelaki di kepala mahkota selasih putih, di tangan busur — talisman urusan memanjangkan yang hina dan seumpama; wajah ketiga, tua hitam hodoh, padanya buah dan diberi cerek — sesuai talisman kasih sayang cinta. Ia ada huruf: hā' dan fā'; dan hubungan manusia, liang pusat; dari organ dalam jantung; dari luar tulang belakang, sisi kanan hidung dan mata kanan siang serta kiri malam; dan penyakit hempedu kuning; dan rasa setiap sedap dan manis (kongsi); dan warna setiap merah; dan logam setiap bersinar halus seperti delima dan emas; dan tempat majlis raja serta kubu benteng; dan haiwan halus seperti kuda dan burung buruan; dan tumbuhan berbuah panjang seperti kurma dan zaitun. Ia khusus urusan berkait anak lelaki perempuan serta apa diharap dalam itu…
