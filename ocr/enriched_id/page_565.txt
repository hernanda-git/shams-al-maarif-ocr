"…[Aries continued:] opposition and contention and open enmity; and the square is a middle look — it does not openly oppose with enmity; and the trine is complete affection; and the sextile is half affection; and fortune in the aspects is stronger than inauspiciousness in them. One looks to it in its faces: its planet Mars, then Jupiter, then Saturn. Its triangle [is with] Leo and Sagittarius, and it is the first of the spring season. Its ascendant: in the first face, in the form of a black man, red-eyed, angry, huge, in his middle a white cloak, and in his hand an ax he intends to cut with; in the second face, in the form of a red-blond man, in his hand a sword, and in the other hand a wooden rod, like one hastening after good and forbidden from it; in the third face, in the form of a woman with one leg, upon her head a crown, upon which appears ecstasy.
