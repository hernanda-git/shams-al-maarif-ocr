There is no servant who perseveres upon this luminous mention but Allah illuminates his heart and gives him all he longs for, and unveils to him what he wills. (Section on His name the Exalted, al-Wālī al-Muta'āl) (In the name of Allah the Merciful, the Compassionate) Know that His name al-Wālī did not occur in the great Qur'ān, and its meaning is the master of things and the one dominating them and administering in them by His will, and His command executes in them and His affair flows over them — for He, exalted and lofty, is alone in managing them, and He is the executor of the management in realization, and the one standing over them by the perpetuation and the preservation. And that preceded. (And as for His name the Exalted, al-Muta'āl) it occurred in the great Qur'ān in His saying exalted 'the Great, the Exalted' — and it is by the meaning of the elevation with a kind of exaggeration, and its meaning preceded. And Allah is the One granting success. (Section on His name the Exalted, al-Barr) (In the name of Allah the Merciful, the Compassionate) Know that the meaning of al-Barr is the truth, and the absolute goodness is that from which is every delight and beneficence; and the servant is only made goodly to the measure he engages of the goodness, especially toward his parents and his masters. And it is related that Mūsā (peace be upon him), when his Lord spoke to him, saw a man standing at the foot of the Throne, and wondered at his place, so he said 'O Lord, how did this servant reach this station?' He said: 'He does not envy any of My creation, and he was dutiful to his parents.' So that is al-Barr with Allah. And as for the detail of the goodness of Allah the Exalted and His subtleness toward His believing servant — that He made him of the people of the right and inspired him the answer in the abode upon contentment with him, so He derived for him, then provided him the answer for what he asked, for His sight shows you in the world of beauty, and the natural nearings accumulated upon him and the habits of closeness — the custom of the Truth the Exalted over the place of his faith from Him, and His goodness the Exalted sends the messengers (peace be upon them) and the Books revealed to them, and Allah the Exalted provided them the acceptance, and it is the greatest. Then He inspired him to establish what He commanded him of His laws; then He promised him the establishment of the deed; then He took from him the trusts of the desires and His goodness by His spirit, so he attains from the goodness and the generosity in the abode of the Hereafter — for it is a passage of the Barzakh and the abode of His drawing, in the craws of green birds from the Garden grazing in the meadows of the Garden until the Day of the Resurrection; then His goodness by that He gave him life after his death — the greatest goodness and the complete mercy; then He established him upon the straight path lest he descend from this outlook into the Fire after the attainment of faith, by His showing peace from his right and the Qur'ān before him and the Sunna his bearer; then His goodness by that He gave him drink from the basin of life — a draught after which he is not thirsty; then His goodness by that He entered him into the Garden and favored him with the looking upon His noble Face; then His goodness by that He made him in this bliss of the eternals to the eternity of eternities and the everlasting ever; then His goodness by that He made subservient to him His speech as He made subservient to him in the abode of this world the universes — He said exalted 'And He made subservient to you what is in the heavens and what is in the earth, all of it.' So this is the sum of the disposal of Allah the Exalted by His subtleness and His goodness in His creation and His believing servants. (Fasal nama-Nya Yang Maha Tinggi, al-Wālī al-Muta'āl) (Bismillāh ar-Raḥmān ar-Raḥīm) Ketahuilah bahwa nama-Nya al-Wālī tidak terdapat dalam Qur'ān agung, dan maknanya ialah pemilik perkara serta yang menguasai dan mengurus dengannya dengan kehendak-Nya, dan perintah-Nya dilaksanakan padanya serta urusan-Nya mengalir atasnya — kerana Ia Yang Maha Tinggi lagi Agung, Esa dalam mengurusnya, dan Ia pelaksana urusan dalam realizasi, serta Yang Berdiri atasnya dengan kekekalan dan pemeliharaan. Dan itu telah lalu. (Adapun nama-Nya Yang Maha Tinggi, al-Muta'āl) ia terdapat dalam Qur'ān agung pada firman-Nya 'Yang Agung, Yang Tinggi' — dan ia dengan makna ketinggian dengan suatu pelebihan, dan maknanya telah lalu. Dan Allah Yang Memberi Taufik. (Fasal nama-Nya Yang Maha Tinggi, al-Barr) (Bismillāh ar-Raḥmān ar-Raḥīm) Ketahuilah bahwa makna al-Barr ialah kebenaran, dan kebaikan mutlak ialah yang daripadanya tiap kelazatan dan kebajikan; dan hamba hanya menjadi baik menurut kadar ia menceburi kebaikan, terutama pada ibu bapanya dan syeikhnya. Dan diriwayatkan bahwa Mūsā a.s., bila Tuhannya berkalam kepadanya, melihat seorang lelaki berdiri di kaki 'Arasy, dan hairan pada tempatnya, lalu ia berkata 'Wahai Tuhanku, bagaimana hamba ini sampai maqam ini?' Ia berfirman: 'Ia tidak hasad sesiapa dari makhluk-Ku, dan ia berbakti kepada ibu bapanya.' Maka itu al-Barr di sisi Allah. Dan adapun tafsil kebaikan Allah Yang Maha Tinggi dan halus-Nya pada hamba mukmin-Nya — bahwa Ia jadikan ia dari ahli kanan dan ilhamkan jawapan dalam negeri atas keredhaan kepadanya, maka Ia ambil baginya, lalu rezekikan jawapan bagi apa yang dimintanya, kerana pandangan-Nya tunjukkan engkau dalam alam keindahan, dan pendekatan tabii berkumpul atasnya serta kebiasaan kedekatan — adat al-Ḥaqq Yang Maha Tinggi atas tempat imannya dari-Nya, dan kebaikan-Nya Yang Maha Tinggi mengutus rasul (s.a.) serta Kitab yang diturunkan kepada mereka, dan Allah Yang Maha Tinggi rezekikan mereka penerimaan, dan ia yang terbesar. Lalu Ia ilhamkan dia menegakkan apa yang diperintahkan-Nya dari syariat-Nya; lalu Ia janjikan dia tegaknya amal; lalu Ia ambil daripadanya amanah nafsu dan kebaikan-Nya dengan roh-Nya, maka ia capai dari kebaikan dan kemurahan dalam negeri akhirat — kerana ia lorong Barzakh dan negeri tarikan-Nya, dalam tembolok burung hijau dari Syurga yang meragut di padang Syurga hingga Hari Kiamat; lalu kebaikan-Nya dengan bahwa Ia menghidupkannya setelah matinya — kebaikan terbesar dan rahmat sempurna; lalu Ia tegakkan dia di jalan lurus agar tidak turun dari pandangan ini ke Neraka setelah capaian iman, dengan menunjukkan salam dari kanannya dan Qur'ān di hadapannya serta Sunah pembawanya; lalu kebaikan-Nya dengan bahwa Ia beri minum dia dari telaga kehidupan — tegukan yang selepasnya tidak dahaga; lalu kebaikan-Nya dengan bahwa Ia masukkan dia ke Syurga dan kurniakan dia pandangan wajah Mulia-Nya; lalu kebaikan-Nya dengan bahwa Ia jadikan dia dalam nikmat ini dari yang kekal hingga kekal abadi; lalu kebaikan-Nya dengan bahwa Ia tundukkan bagi dia kalam-Nya sebagaimana Ia tundukkan baginya dalam negeri dunia alam semesta — firman-Nya 'Dan Dia tundukkan bagi kamu apa yang di langit dan di bumi, semuanya.'
