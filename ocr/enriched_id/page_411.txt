"…to you a white light and speaks to you and makes a pact with you. So if you will a matter, say: 'Answer, O servant of the letter Ḥā', the folk of such-and-such.' And if you will to employ the king Ṭaffā'īl, write the letter and enter the retreat and recite the names — this invocation: the basmala; 'Glory to the Forbearing from him who disobeyed Him. O Allah, O Forbearing, my state is ill and You are knowing of it. I ask You by the rank of Muḥammad — upon him peace — and Moses the Speaker, take my hand and grant me victory over him who wronged me, and direct me in the fulfillment of needs, and make me one guided by Your command, and aid me with speech and disposal in this secret until I dispose it as I will. Yā Ḥāḥlamat 'Āj, Ḥāḥ, Ḥaṭūḥ, Ḥayth ilā, Ḥaḥaj, Ḥaj, Ḥawā, Aḥrā, Ḥawā, Jat, Ḥawāy — in the instant my need is fulfilled by the right of Khidmūhā, she is the hour, and hasten with the answer, and dispose in it your disposal — al-waḥā, the hastening, by the Alif — there is no power nor strength save in Allah, the High, the Great.' And the incense is fenugreek. And its implicit concentration says: 'Dahlij, Ya'shalā — how great is His affair and mighty His dominion; answer, O king Ṭaffā'īl, and do such-and-such in this hour — the hastening, al-waḥā.' (The letter Ṭā') — it is the gathering of the two letters, and it has a secret and disposal in the higher worlds, and it is a flyer in the worlds. And if it is written on a tablet with the moon in it, Ṭahā, Āt, and Hā', and the implicit concentration and the name of the king — its bearer is acknowledged by all the worlds. And if written on one who complains of head-pain, he is cured. And whoever writes 9 Ṭā' and Hā' with the moon in the mansion and hangs it on a newborn — no harmful animal has power over him. And the number of Ṭā' is 18; if drawn in a magic-square of 9 in 9 on gazelle parchment for 14 days of the month and carried by one who would walk in travel — he does not weary. * And if you write the letter with the implicit concentration around it and hang it in a place or shop, his customers multiply, and its bearer — Allah provides him hidden causes. And if placed under a person's head, he is safe from evil dreams. (A universal rule) — know that every name whose number is odd disposes in the worlds of contraction, and every even number disposes in the worlds of expansion; and this is a secret of Allah for His allies. * And among the properties of the magic-square consonant with the secret of numbers: if you write with the letter Ṭā' in your palm and speak over it with the implicit concentration and hold fire or place it, it does not harm you; and whoever carries this square, his understanding increases and his movement increases, and it is fitting for one long afflicted with fever — he writes and carries it. And if written on a piece of sulfur and cast under the hearth-fire, the people of that place are burned. And if the dullard recites it 81 times, his dullness departs. And whoever takes dust from beneath the foot of whom he wills, forms him completely, and writes the letter on it 81 times, then recites the conjuration over the circle of the letter Ṭā' and speaks over it with the implicit concentration and the invocation, and casts it into the house of whom you will — it has a great effect. And it has a retreat and discipline of 14 days, and the implicit concentration after every prayer for 9 days — and the servant appears to you, his light red, and speaks to you of what you will. * And know that what preceded of disposal is fulfilled by writing the letter and reciting the implicit concentration after every prayer its number — the servant appears, and his name is 'Aṭā'īl, dispose it. And this is the invocation: 'In the name of Allah, the Merciful, the Compassionate — I have wronged by seeking Allah's help against my mount of color until He spreads to me the Ṭā' with the expulsion of him who wronged me. Answer, O Ṭāyiṭāwūl, greatness of the Possessor of extent, the severe, Ṭiyāṭiyū, O Abā Allāh, O Lord of the worlds, Ṭalṭīyāṭ 2, Yāh Bāṭ, Ṭīṭū, Ṭalā, Ṭalā, Ṭahīfaṭ, Ṭaṭūṭ — al-waḥā, Tatṭaṭaw, expel from him who fought me by the right of these expelling names — I attained from the Possessor of extent my request. Hasten, O servant of the Ṭā', or I complain to K [the Knower] of the unseen. And there is no power nor strength save in Allah, the High, the Great.' And the incense is tahlīb. And if you recite it at the door of a treasure, the inhabitants flee; and if you incense with it the offered goods, its presenter is burned — your command repels the enemies. And its implicit concentration: 'Answer…'"

"…kepadamu cahaya putih dan berkata kepadamu serta berikat denganmu. Maka jika engkau menghendaki suatu urusan, katakan: 'Jawab, wahai khadam huruf Ḥā', ahli anu.' Dan jika engkau mahu menggunakan raja Ṭaffā'īl, tulislah huruf dan masuklah khalwat serta bacalah nama-nama — doa ini: basmalah; 'Maha Suci Yang Penyantun daripada orang yang menderhakai-Nya. Ya Allah, wahai Yang Penyantun, keadaanku sakit dan Engkau mengetahuinya. Aku memohon kepada-Mu demi martabat Muḥammad — ke atasnya selawat — dan Musa yang Berkata, peganglah tanganku dan berilah aku kemenangan atas orang yang menganiayaku, dan aturkanlah daku dalam menyempurnakan hajat, dan jadikanlah aku yang dibimbing perintah-Mu, dan tolonglah aku dengan kata dan tasaruf dalam rahsia ini sehingga aku mentasarufkannya sebagaimana kuingini. Yā Ḥāḥlamat 'Āj, Ḥāḥ, Ḥaṭūḥ, Ḥayth ilā, Ḥaḥaj, Ḥaj, Ḥawā, Aḥrā, Ḥawā, Jat, Ḥawāy — pada saat itu hajatku dipenuhi demi hak Khidmūhā, ia saat itu, dan segerakanlah jawapan, dan tasarufkanlah di dalamnya tasarufmu — al-waḥā, yang segera, demi Alif — tiada daya dan kekuatan melainkan pada Allah Yang Maha Tinggi Lagi Maha Agung.' Dan ubatannya ialah halba. Dan idmārnya berkata: 'Dahlij, Ya'shalā — betapa agung urusan-Nya dan perkasa kuasa-Nya; jawab, wahai raja Ṭaffā'īl, dan lakukan anu pada saat ini — yang segera, al-waḥā.' (Huruf Ṭā') — ia kumpulan dua huruf, dan mempunyair rahsia serta tasaruf di alam tinggi, dan ia terbang di alam. Jika ditulis pada loh dengan bulan padanya, Ṭahā, Āt, dan Hā', serta idmār dan nama raja — pembawanya diterima oleh seluruh alam. Dan jika ditulis pada orang yang mengadu sakit kepala, ia sembuh. Sesiapa menulis 9 Ṭā' dan Hā' dengan bulan di manzilah dan menggantungnya pada bayi — tiada binatang berbahaya berkuasa atasnya. Bilangan Ṭā' ialah 18; jika dilukis dalam segi empat ajaib 9 dalam 9 pada kulit gazel selama 14 hari bulan dan dibawa oleh orang yang mahu berjalan musafir — ia tidak letih. * Dan jika engkau menulis huruf dengan idmār mengelilinginya dan menggantungnya di suatu tempat atau kedai, pelanggannya bertambah, dan pembawanya — Allah memberinya sebab tersembunyi. Dan jika diletakkan di bawah kepala seseorang, ia selamat daripada mimpi buruk. (Kaedah menyeluruh) — ketahuilah bahawa setiap nama yang bilangannya ganjil bertasaruf di alam kekangan, dan setiap bilangan genap bertasaruf di alam keluasan; dan ini rahsia Allah bagi wali-wali-Nya. * Di antara sifat segi empat ajaib yang sepadan dengan rahsia bilangan: jika engkau menulis dengan huruf Ṭā' di tapak tanganmu dan bercakap atasnya dengan idmār serta memegang api atau memasukkannya, ia tidak memudaratkanmu; dan sesiapa membawa segi empat ini, fahamannya bertambah dan gerakannya bertambah, dan sesuai bagi orang yang lama ditimpa demam — ia menulis dan membawanya. Dan jika ditulis pada kepingan belerang dan dibaling ke bawah tungku api, penduduk tempat itu terbakar. Dan jika si bodoh membacanya 81 kali, kebodohannya hilang. Sesiapa mengambil debu dari bawah kaki orang yang dikehendaki, membentuknya lengkap, dan menulis huruf padanya 81 kali, kemudian membaca conjuration atas bulatan huruf Ṭā' dan bercakap atasnya dengan idmār dan doa, lalu membalingnya ke rumah orang yang dikehendaki — ia berkesan besar. Ia mempunyai khalwat dan riyadhah 14 hari, dan idmār selepas setiap solat selama 9 hari — maka khadam muncul kepadamu, cahayanya merah, dan berkata kepadamu apa yang dikehendaki. * Ketahuilah bahawa apa yang terdahulu dari tasaruf terpenuhi dengan menulis huruf dan membaca idmār selepas setiap solat bilangannya — khadam muncul, dan namanya 'Aṭā'īl, tasarufkanlah ia. Dan inilah doanya: 'Dengan nama Allah Yang Maha Pemurah lagi Maha Penyayang — aku telah berdosa dengan meminta pertolongan Allah atas tungganganku yang berwarna sehingga Ia menghulurkan kepadaku Ṭā' dengan pengusiran orang yang menganiayaku. Jawab, wahai Ṭāyiṭāwūl, keagungan Pemilik keluasan, Yang Keras, Ṭiyāṭiyū, wahai Abā Allāh, wahai Tuhan sekalian alam, Ṭalṭīyāṭ 2, Yāh Bāṭ, Ṭīṭū, Ṭalā, Ṭalā, Ṭahīfaṭ, Ṭaṭūṭ — al-waḥā, Tatṭaṭaw, usirlah dari orang yang memerangiku demi hak nama-nama pengusir ini — aku memperoleh dari Pemilik keluasan permintaanku. Segerakan, wahai khadam Ṭā', atau aku mengadu kepada K [Yang Mengetahui] perkara ghaib. Dan tiada daya dan kekuatan melainkan pada Allah Yang Maha Tinggi Lagi Maha Agung.' Dan ubatannya ialah tahlīb. Jika engkau membacanya di pintu khazanah, penghuninya lari; dan jika engkau mengasapi barang yang dipersembahkan, penyembahnya terbakar — perintahmu menghalau musuh. Dan idmárnya: 'Jawab…'"
