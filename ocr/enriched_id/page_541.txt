Arabic:
— ٥٣٥ —
تم بحمد الله وعونه وحسن توفيقه الجزء الرابع من كتاب شمس المعارف الكبرى
للإمام العالم العلامة والحبر الفهامة أحمد بن علي البوني المتوفى
سنة ٦٢٢ اثنتين وعشرين وستمائة وبتتمة هذا الجزء
تمام الكتاب نفع الله به المسلمين ورحم الله مؤلفه
وقدس الله سره آمين بجاه سيد
المرسلين سيدنا محمد صلى الله
عليه وسلم وشرف ومجد
وعظم وكرم

English:
— 535 —

"It is completed, by the praise of Allah and His help and His good success — the fourth part of the book 'Shams al-Ma'arif al-Kubrā' by the Imam the erudite, the fount of understanding, Aḥmad ibn 'Alī al-Būnī, who died in the year 622 [AH] (six hundred and twenty-two). And with the completion of this part, the completion of the book — may Allah benefit by it the Muslims, and may He have mercy on its author and sanctify his secret, amen — by the rank of the Master of the messengers, our master Muḥammad (may Allah bless him and grant him peace), and [by his] honor and glory and greatness and nobility.

Indonesia:
— 535 —

"Tamat, dengan puji Allah dan pertolongan-Nya serta kejayaan baik-Nya — bahagian keempat kitab 'Shams al-Ma'arif al-Kubrā' oleh Imam al-'Allamah, mata air kefahaman, Aḥmad ibn 'Alī al-Būnī, yang wafat tahun 622 H. Dan dengan sempurnanya bahagian ini, sempurnalah kitab — semoga Allah memberi manfaat dengannya pada Muslim, dan merahmati pengarangnya serta menyucikan rahsianya, amin — dengan pangkat Penghulu rasul, penghulu kami Muḥammad s.a.w., dan kemuliaan serta keagungan dan kebesaran dan keutamaannya.
