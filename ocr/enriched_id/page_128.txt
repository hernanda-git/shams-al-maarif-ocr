( Chapter of the famous discipline of Qul Uhiya )
Know, O brother, that if you desire that, fast 3 days, the first of them Tuesday, then Wednesday and Thursday, and it is your fasting from that which has no soul, and you fumigate with frankincense and jawi incense night and day, and you recite the noble surah during the span of 3 days a thousand times; in that mentioned period you recite every day 333 times and more, and the intent is to complete it to a thousand in that mentioned period. And strive that your ختم of its recitation be on the night of Friday, the middle third of the night, for its servant will appear to you, and he is a man, short, with long arms; he will sit before you and say to you, Peace be upon you. So steady your heart, for upon him is a tremendous awe, and he is from the kings of the believing jinn who submitted at the hand of the Prophet, peace be upon him. Then you will see three men behind him. If you steady yourself, your need will be fulfilled; and if you imagine or stammer, then they will depart from you and your work will fail. So you must embolden yourself and not stop, for his name is Abu Yusuf. So say: O Abu Yusuf, my right has become due upon you, and you see what I am in of poverty and distress, and I desire from you this hour the permissible, lawful thing, to use as assistance for my time and as provision for the pilgrimage to the Sacred House of Allah, and your reward is upon Allah. And know, my brother, that if you strengthen your heart and speak the speech that we mentioned, then he turns to one of the men who are behind him and then commands them with something, and he brings it by lightning, and it is what Allah has apportioned for you from pre-eternity.
