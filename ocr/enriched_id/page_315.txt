English:
— 309 —

Able. ( The Letter Dhāl ) It is a speaking, silent letter, for it is in the last of the element of heat and dryness, and its extension is like the Dāl; and it is a fiery letter, and its works are in the cold and the moist interchangeable — so if you contemplate that, the model opens to you. And this letter — whoever writes it 7 times in a china vessel, and wipes it with defoamed honey, and drinks it for a period of seven days — Allah removes from him the phlegm. And if it is written 81 times with an Indian pen inside a circle, upon a gold tablet or china vessel, and wiped with defoamed honey, and given to the sought-one for 7 consecutive days on an empty stomach — it benefits him. And whoever breaks its second extension, which is [Dhāl Alif Lām], in an open (space) on Monday at the hour of Mars, upon an iron tablet, and these four Names are written in its four sides outside the open (space): "Able, Omnipotent, Strong, Standing" — and the tablet is bound upon his right upper-arm — Allah gives him mighty strength. And it has names by which it is invoked: "O Allah, indeed I ask You — O Possessor of tremendous bounty, O Possessor of favor and generosity and nobility, O Possessor of good treatment and grace, O Possessor of majesty and honor, O Possessor of severe grasp, O Forgiving, O Possessor of pardon — I ask You by what You lodged in the letter Dhāl of the hidden secrets and the stored lights — that You subject to me Your noble angels. Indeed You are over all things Able." ( The Letter Rā' ) It is a silent, watery letter, cold and moist, in the rank of the third (element); its moisture exceeds its coldness — thus. And this letter has properties — so whoever writes it upon a tin tablet in the exaltation of Jupiter, and the engraving is fine and the tablet delicate, and it is placed under the tongue in intense heat on a journey — he obtains severe cold, and the saliva descends into his inward, and he feels not the pain of thirst. And if this tablet is placed in water, and one drinks from it three draughts on an empty stomach — the thirst departs. And whoever writes this letter and outside it 10 Indian Rā's upon tanned bat-skin, and a man carries it — he does not sleep as long as it is with him. And whoever writes 10 Indian Rā's with henna upon the prison door by the name of whom he wills of the prisoners — he is freed quickly. And it has names by which it is invoked: "O Allah, indeed I ask You — O Compassionate, O Merciful, O Provider, O Exalter, O Watchful, O Severe, O Compassionate, O Lord — by what You lodged in the letter Rā' of the stored secrets and the hidden lights — that You subject to me the servants of this noble letter, (whom) You commanded. Indeed You are over all things Able." ( The Letter Zāy ) It is a speaking letter, not muffled at its end; and it is among the silent letters hot and moist. Its properties in works of good — 11 Indian Zāy are written upon a special silver tablet on Monday, and the Moon is conjoined with Jupiter in a conjunction of affection, and it is carried upon the upper-arm — Allah suffices him against the tongues and hands of creatures, and he sees from them nothing but good. And whoever breaks its valor (lion) with the number of its letters upon a wooden tablet on Monday — and if it is in the strength of the crescent it is better, or in the sacred months — then the owner of the spleen carries it — it departs from him in the nearest period. And whoever places its numbers in a 4-by-4 square on Thursday first hour, and carries it at the front of his head — he is beloved near all people, and none sees him but loves him. And whoever places the Indian Zāy in the hour of Jupiter 99 times in paper on the first (day) of Thursday, and puts it in a wall — it demolishes quickly.

Indonesia:

Maha Kuasa. ( Huruf Dhāl ) Ia huruf berucap senyap, karena ia di akhir unsur panas dan kering, dan bentangannya seperti Dāl; dan ia huruf berapi, serta amalannya pada yang dingin dan lembap yang saling bertukar — maka apabila engkau renungkan itu terbuka bagimu contohnya. Dan huruf ini — barangsiapa menulisnya 7 kali di bejana porselin, dan menghapusnya dengan madu yang dibuang buihnya, serta meminumnya selama tujuh hari — Allah menghilangkan darinya dahak. Dan apabila ditulis 81 kali dengan pena India di dalam lingkaran, di atas lempeng emas atau bejana porselin, dan dihapus dengan madu yang dibuang buihnya, lalu diberi minum pada yang diminta 7 hari berturut-turut dalam keadaan kosong perut — ia bermanfaat. Dan barangsiapa memecahkan bentangan keduanya, yaitu [Dhāl Alif Lām], di ruang terbuka hari Senin saat Marikh, di atas lempeng besi, dan keempat nama ini ditulis di empat sisinya di luar ruang: "Qādir, Muqtadir, Qawī, Qā'im" — serta lempeng diikat di lengan kanan atasnya — Allah memberinya kekuatan dahsyat. Dan ia memiliki nama yang dipohon dengannya: "Ya Allah, sesungguhnya aku memohon pada-Mu — wahai Pemilik anugerah agung, wahai Pemilik karunia dan kemurahan serta kemuliaan, wahai Pemilik kebaikan dan kenikmatan, wahai Pemilik keagungan dan kemuliaan, wahai Pemilik cengkeraman kuat, wahai 'Afūw, wahai Pemilik pemaaf — aku memohon dengan apa yang Engkau letakkan pada huruf Dhāl dari rahasia tersembunyi dan cahaya tersimpan — semoga Engkau tundukkan padaku malaikat-Mu yang mulia. Sesungguhnya Engkau Maha Kuasa atas segala sesuatu." ( Huruf Rā' ) Ia huruf senyap, berair, dingin lembap, di tingkat ketiga; lembapnya melebihi dinginnya — demikian. Dan huruf ini memiliki sifat — barangsiapa menulisnya di atas lempeng timah di saat Musytari mulia, dan ukirannya halus serta lempengnya tipis, lalu diletakkan di bawah lidah saat panas terik dalam perjalanan — ia mendapat dingin yang sangat, dan ludah turun ke batinnya, serta tiada merasa sakit dahaga. Dan apabila lempeng ini diletakkan di air, dan diminum darinya tiga teguk dalam keadaan kosong perut — dahaga hilang. Dan barangsiapa menulis huruf ini dan di luarnya 10 Rā' India di atas kulit kelawar yang disamak, serta dibawa manusia — ia tiada tidur selama bersamanya. Dan barangsiapa menulis 10 Rā' India dengan inai di pintu penjara dengan nama siapa yang dikehendakinya dari para tahanan — ia segera bebas. Dan ia memiliki nama yang dipohon dengannya: "Ya Allah, sesungguhnya aku memohon pada-Mu — wahai Raḥmān, wahai Raḥīm, wahai Razzaq, wahai Rāfi', wahai Raqīb, wahai Syadīd, wahai Ra'ūf, wahai Rabb — dengan apa yang Engkau letakkan pada huruf Rā' dari rahasia tersimpan dan cahaya terpendam — semoga Engkau tundukkan padaku pelayan huruf mulia ini, yang Engkau perintahkan. Sesungguhnya Engkau Maha Kuasa atas segala sesuatu." ( Huruf Zāy ) Ia huruf berucap, tiada sengau di akhirnya; dan ia dari huruf senyap panas lembap. Sifatnya dalam amalan kebaikan — 11 Zāy India ditulis di atas lempeng perak khusus hari Senin, dan Bulan bersatu dengan Musytari dalam persatuan kasih, lalu dibawa di lengan atas — Allah mencukupinya dari lidah dan tangan makhluk, serta tiada ia melihat dari mereka selain kebaikan. Dan barangsiapa memecahkan "singanya" bersama bilangan hurufnya di atas lempeng kayu hari Senin — dan apabila di saat kekuatan bulan sabit maka lebih baik, atau di bulan-bulan suci — lalu pemilik limpa membawanya — ia pergi dalam waktu terdekat. Dan barangsiapa menurunkan bilangannya dalam petak 4x4 hari Kamis jam pertama, dan membawanya di depan kepalanya — ia dicintai di sisi semua manusia, dan tiada yang melihatnya melainkan mencintainya. Dan barangsiapa meletakkan Zāy India di saat Musytari 99 kali di atas kertas pada hari Kamis pertama, dan meletakkannya di dinding — ia meruntuhkan dengan cepat."
