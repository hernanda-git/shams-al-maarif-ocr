"…that every limb of it is opposite a door of the doors of Gehenna, until the seven doors open to you in Paradise. Therefore he (upon him peace) said: 'Whoever makes ablution, then perfects the ablution, then says "There is no god but Allah alone, He has no partner; to Him the dominion and to Him the praise, and He is over all things powerful" — the doors of Paradise are opened for him, he enters from whichever he wills.' And the prayer is the key of the doors of Paradise, and it is the connection to the divine realities; so your inward lights are the reality of the world of command, the world of the unseen, the world of dominion, the world of the Dominion, the world of unveiling, the world of the unsewing, the world of the sewing, the world of invention, the world of origination, the world of the secret, the world of the retreat, the world of the oath, the world of the fetuses, the world of the response, the world of the prime matter, the world of the births, the world of the composition, the world of the manifestation, the world of the intellect, the world of the soul, the world of the heart, the world of the Throne, the world of the Footstool, the world of the Tablet, the world of the Pen, the world of Saturn, the world of Jupiter, the world of Mars, the world of the Sun, the world of Venus, the world of Mercury, the world of the Moon, the world of Fire, the world of Air, the world of Water, the world of Earth, the world of the Animal, the world of the Perfect Man — and he is compounded of three worlds, of the world of deeds and the world of sayings, and there gathered in him six worlds: so the first is the world of the secret, and it is the first world of the world of existence, and it is the secret of the particularization by the standing in the worlds of unity according to the eternal estimation; then the intellect by the secret, then the intellect and the spirit and the intellect, then the spirit and the intellect — they understood, then the spirit, then the intellect the spirit of the spirit; then by the soul and by the spirit they understood the spirit, then the spirit the spirit of the soul, and the heart by the soul, and the mother of the heart is the body of the soul, and the soul is the spirit of the heart; then the body, and the heart its spirit — so these are six worlds that flowed as six worlds, and they are your straight path. So the results — the day of their requital is upon their path in a day whose measure is fifty thousand years; and the masters of souls, their day is as a day; and the masters of secrets, their day is as a degree of a sphere; and the people of subtleties, their day is as a minute, a second, a third, a fourth, etc. So as for the layer of the path of bodies, it is upon the elementary subterranean layer, so whoever falls is in the lowest subterranean of the fire, next to the second perceptible; and as for the seventh subterranean, it is for the fitting — and this is their path upon it, so [kun] be — every path and its description is in the world of its origination and the phase of its relation, and he saw what he saw of the witnessing and what separated from him; else its layer is known and the days of his sojourn understood, until he is from the first day whose measure is fifty thousand years, and his rank was the doubling in every world, so from it came out the rank of the numbers. (Notice) He said exalted: 'Allah who created you from weakness…' to His saying exalted 'He creates what He wills' — and this is a phased origination and the realities of the cyclical names. And that is that Allah the Exalted taught Adam all the names according to the difference of their kinds and the succession of their accidents, so He placed in the human Friday some of them, and in the human innate disposition their secret, and made their denoted things the place of the judgment, submitting to the delineation of knowledge. And Allah commanded you to traverse the high names for your nobility over the lordly realities — so the first made thing is the unsewing; so if you complete from His names His name al-Khāliq — He said exalted 'And We made from water every living thing,' so all that is in it for life is the ascent of the world of worlds, so it is inside the sphere of water, and that is from what was acquired from the outpouring of the eternal, everlasting Throne, whose water is His cushion — He said exalted 'And His Throne was upon the water.' (Notice) Know that of His name the Exalted al-Khāliq, the number of its spiritual factors in the creative phase is seven hundred and thirty-one, so you find it in the sphere of its circumscription; and this point the governor turns for forty days, and for each day of the spirituality related to the force of the specified name is the expanse of its secret; so when the forty phased are completed and His name the Exalted al-Bārī revolves upon them with its governances…"

"…setiap anggota berhadapan pintu neraka hingga 7 pintu dibuka syurga. Sabda 'Sesiapa berwuduk sempurna lalu kata 'Tiada tuhan melainkan Allah Esa tiada sekutu, bagi-Nya kerajaan pujian, Dia atas segala berkuasa' — pintu syurga dibuka masuk mana dikehendaki.' Solat kunci pintu syurga hubungan hakikat ketuhanan; cahaya batinmu hakikat alam perintah ghaib domini Malakut kasyaf buka jahit alam penciptaan ibda' rahsia khalwat sumpah alam janin jawab 'materi' kelahiran susunan zahir akal jiwa hati 'Arasy Kursi Loh Pen Zuhal Musytari Marikh Matahari Zuhrah Utarid Bulan Api Udara Air Tanah Haiwan Insan Sempurna — ia campuran 3 alam perbuatan kata, kumpul 6 alam: pertama alam sirr alam pertama wujud rahsia khas berdiri alam tauhid ikut takdir azali; akal dengan sirr, akal roh akal, roh akal faham, roh roh sirr, jiwa roh faham roh roh jiwa, hati jiwa ibu hati jasad jiwa, jiwa roh hati; jasad hati rohnya — 6 alam jadi sirat lurusmu. Hasil — hari pembalasan atas sirat hari 50 ribu tahun; ahli jiwa hari seperti hari; ahli rahsia seperti darjat sfera; ahli lemah lembut seperti minit saat. Lapisan sirat jasad atas lapisan unsur bawah, jatuh di bawah api kedua; ketujuh untuk layak — sirat mereka [kun] jadi setiap sirat sifat di alam kejadian fasa nisbah, nampak syuhud yang terpisah; lapisan diketahui hari diketahui hingga hari pertama 50 ribu tahun darjat ganda tiap alam keluar darjat bilangan. (Tunjuk) Firman 'Allah yang cipta dari lemah…' 'Dia cipta apa dikehendaki' — kejadian berfasa nama berkitar. Allah ajar Adam semua nama jenis berturut, dalam Jumaat insan sebahagian, fitrah insan rahsianya, yang dinamakan tempat hukum tunduk garis ilmu. Allah suruh lalu nama tinggi kemuliaan hakikat rabbani — pertama dibuat buka; lengkap nama al-Khāliq — 'Kami jadikan dari air setiap benda hidup,' semua hidup naik alam alam dalam sfera air, dari limpah 'Arasy kekal air bantal-Nya 'Arasy di atas air.' (Tunjuk) Nama al-Khāliq faktor rohani fasa cipta 731 dalam sfera lingkungan; titik ini diurus 40 hari, tiap hari rohani kuasa nama luas rahsia; bila 40 fasa lengkap nama al-Bārī putar dengan tadbirnya…"
