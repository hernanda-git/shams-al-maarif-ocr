"The intimate converse (munājāt) — whoever works by it and takes it as a mention, Allah the Exalted opens to him and subdues for him the knowledge and the excellence and its people, and he attains by it the unveiling, and his speech is refined, and he is correct in utterance and wisdom, and the one who mentions it sees in sleep what he asks about and wills — the clarification of the things he wants to do: whether they are good or evil. So when you want to unveil a secret from the secrets of the Real from the various unveiling sciences and their kinds, Allah eases that for you by the perseverance of the one mentioning it, and he carries it upon the face that we mentioned — of engraving or writing with the perseverance of the mention — for all the invocations are like the presence and the repetition until you mention with it the worlds of that mention, and it did not appear in one or two [times] — all is by perseverance; and if there must be an effect, the repetition is the principle relied upon. And from that is this noble group in the spreading of the glorious sciences, and it is the root of things. And whoever works by it and takes it as a mention, Allah the Exalted opens to him and blesses him and subdues for him the people of knowledge and excellence, and he attains the unveiling of its secrets. And it is six names: al-'Alīm, al-Ḥakīm, al-Khabīr, al-Mubīn, al-Hādī, 'Allām al-Ghuyūb. And it suits that magic which descends in it the command of our Lord to the lowest heaven, saying 'Is there a caller, so I answer him? Is there one seeking forgiveness, so I forgive him? Is there an asker, so I give him his ask?' — and the first third of the last [portion], the ninth hour, and it is attributed to the Moon. And this is a supplication specific to the rectitude of the hearts and the understanding of the sciences — whoever calls by it in the last third of the night and persists upon it until the dawn, after the prayer and the seeking of forgiveness and the mention of 'Allah is Greatest,' Allah the Exalted inspires him all the causes of good altogether. And whoever writes it and carries it, there appear upon him the attributes of beauty and the good state such as he did not know of himself before that. And whoever asks Allah the Exalted by it what befits the rectitude of his state and the rectitude of the spirits and the souls and the understanding of the sciences and what is eased for him of the nearness (walāyah) and the devotion to the mighty religion — he has that, and there appears upon him of the beautiful attributes what gladdens him; and Allah judges by the truth. "Munājāt (bicara mesra) — barangsiapa mengamalkannya dan menjadikannya dzikir, Allah Yang Maha Tinggi membukakan kepadanya serta menundukkan baginya ilmu dan keutamaan serta ahlinya, dan ia capai dengannya kasyaf, serta perkataannya halus, dan ia tepat dalam tutur dan hikmah, dan yang menyebutnya nampak dalam tidur apa yang ditanyakan dan dikehendaki — penjelasan perkara yang hendak dilakukannya: sama ada baik atau jahat. Maka bila engkau mahu membuka rahsia dari rahsia al-Ḥaqq dari pelbagai ilmu kasyaf dan jenisnya, Allah memudahkannya bagimu dengan tekun yang menyebutnya, dan ia memikulnya atas cara yang kami sebut — ukiran atau tulisan dengan tekun dzikir — kerana semua dzikir seperti kehadiran dan pengulangan hingga engkau sebut dengannya alam dzikir itu, dan ia tidak nampak dalam satu atau dua [kali] — semua dengan tekun. Dan bila mesti ada kesan, pengulangan ialah asas yang diharapkan. Dan dari itu ialah kumpulan mulia ini dalam penyebaran ilmu agung, dan ia akar perkara. Dan barangsiapa mengamalkannya dan menjadikannya dzikir, Allah Yang Maha Tinggi membukakan kepadanya serta memberkatinya dan menundukkan baginya ahli ilmu dan keutamaan, dan ia capai kasyaf rahsianya. Dan ia enam nama: al-'Alīm, al-Ḥakīm, al-Khabīr, al-Mubīn, al-Hādī, 'Allām al-Ghuyūb.
