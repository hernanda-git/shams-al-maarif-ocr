English:

"In the name of Allah, the Most Merciful, the Most Compassionate. Praise belongs to Allah — to Him is the creation and the command, as [He is] the originating and the bringing into being [§] — He knows what is before them and what is behind them, and they do not encompass a thing of His knowledge but as He wills. I praise Him, glorified and exalted is He, and He is the Master of praise and the Lord of the bounties [§] — the locus of the Nūn and the Pen, who taught man what he knew not by the Pen — a praise befitting the majesty of His greatness and the immensity of His boons, and He is the Patron, the Praised [§] . And He merits from the rarities of His boons and the graces of His favors the honor of the increase, and I thank Him — glorious is His glory — and His beneficence overflowed upon the universal subtleties of His preservation [§] and the support of the emotions of His bounty by the happiness of His aid and support [§] — a thanksgiving befitting the glory of His lordship and the greatness of His divinity, and He is the High, the Grateful [§] . And He merits the permanence of His care and the comprehensiveness of His guardianship in every affair and matter [§] . And I testify that there is no god but He — the Knowing, the Aware, the Knower of the unseen and the witnessed [§] — than whom there is nothing like, and He is the Hearing, the Seeing; to Him is the judgment and the decree and the will [§] . And I testify that our master and our support Muḥammad is His servant and His messenger — the point of the circle of existence [§] , the watering-place of every gnosis and the source of every knower and the resort of every bounty and bestowal [§] — He who guided by him His servants to the most straight path [§] and honored us by his mission over all the communities [§] ; his likeness is the verse of the bringing-into-being and the system of the messengers, and the chain of the prophets is certain [§] ; and He apprised him — and he is the trustworthy, the trusted [§] — of the hidden of the secret of His guarded [knowledge] [§] — of the subtleties of what was and what will be [§] — from the realities of the subtle [things] of His stored knowledge; may Allah the Exalted bless and grant peace and honor and exalt upon his perfected self and his sanctified station [§] , and upon his family of noble hereditary honor and his companions of majestic rank and most precious guidance [§] , and upon those who follow their straight way and who followed them in excellence [§] — over the passage of time by the succession of mankind in every epoch and era [§] — a prayer and peace perpetual and inseparable as long as the night darkened and the dawn breathed [§] — in every glance and breath, [as many as] the knowledge of Allah the ancient, the most holy, encompassed [§] . (Now then) — says the incapable, the lowly, who affiliates to every weakness [§] — 'Abd al-Qādir ibn 'Alī al-Ḥusaynī al-Adhamī [§] , the resident of the Abode of Emigration, the precincts of the Protector of the Neighbor, al-Madīnah al-Munawwarah [§] , the servant of the noble chamber in the elevated purified Prophetic room [§] — may Allah the Exalted be for him wherever he was by the aid of His care [§] and the eye of His guardianship [§] , and lodge him in the approaches of the courtyard of His protection [§] by the permanence of His safeguard [§] — amen, O Allah, amen [§] . This is a brief and concise extract — I plucked the pearls of its benefits [§] , and a precious treatise in which I recorded the customs of its intents [§] — in the composing of important roots and the establishing of the results of branches from the science of geomancy [al-raml] [§] — by the brides of expressions and the rare treasures of allusions, for the one whose beauty is near in its attainment to the knower of it [§] , and a reminder for the one who points to it [§] — the realization of the hope of the seekers [§] and the nearing of the work of the desirers [§] — by the aid of the support of the cosmos by the knowledges of the preserves, glorious is His glory [§] , and His beneficence overflows.

[Poetry:] 'When Allah wills the honoring of His preserve [§] He bestowed the subtleties of the aid of His support; and if the aid of Allah to the man is connected [§] the Reckoner made his knowledge of the cosmos serve.'

And I made it upon thirty intents [maqāṣid] [§] — it became tender and radiant as a source and resort and its recourse [§] ; I made easy the plucking of the realities of its dense subtleties [§] , and I detailed by a concise exposition the summation of the important sentence, a detailing; and I named it 'The Balance of Justice [§] in the Intents of the Rules of Geomancy [al-Raml].'

Indonesia:

"Dengan nama Allah, Yang Maha Pemurah, Yang Maha Mengasihani. Segala puji bagi Allah — bagi-Nya ciptaan dan perintah, sebagaimana [Dia] yang mengawali dan mengadakan [§] — Dia mengetahui apa di hadapan mereka dan apa di belakang mereka, dan mereka tidak meliputi sesuatu dari ilmu-Nya melainkan sebagaimana Dia kehendaki. Aku memuji-Nya, maha suci dan maha tinggi Dia, dan Dia Pemilik pujian serta Tuhan nikmat [§] — tempat Nūn dan Pena, yang mengajarkan manusia apa yang tidak diketahuinya dengan Pena — puji yang sesuai kemuliaan keagungan-Nya dan besarnya kurnia-Nya, dan Ia Penjaga, Yang Terpuji [§] . Dan Ia berhak dari keajaiban kurnia-Nya dan rahmat nikmat-Nya akan kemuliaan tambahan, dan aku bersyukur kepada-Nya — maha agung keagungan-Nya — dan kurnia-Nya melimpah atas halus pemeliharaan-Nya yang menyeluruh [§] serta sokongan perasaan kurnia-Nya dengan kebahagiaan pertolongan-Nya [§] — syukur yang sesuai kemuliaan ketuhanan-Nya dan keagungan ketuhanan-Nya, dan Ia Yang Tinggi, Yang Bersyukur [§] . Dan Ia berhak kekalan pemeliharaan-Nya dan menyeluruh penjagaan-Nya dalam tiap urusan dan perkara [§] . Dan aku bersaksi tiada tuhan melainkan Dia — Yang Mengetahui, Yang Waspada, Yang Mengetahui ghaib dan nyata [§] — yang tiada serupa dengan-Nya sesuatu, dan Ia Yang Mendengar, Yang Melihat; bagi-Nya hukum dan ketetapan serta kehendak [§] . Dan aku bersaksi bahwa penghulu dan sandaran kami Muhammad ialah hamba dan rasul-Nya — titik bulatan wujud [§] , telaga tiap ma'rifat dan sumber tiap 'arif serta tempat tiap kurnia dan anugerah [§] — Dia yang memberi petunjuk dengannya hamba-Nya ke jalan paling lurus [§] dan memuliakan kami dengan kerasulan-Nya atas segala umat [§] ; misalnya ayat pengadaan dan sistem rasul, dan rantaian nabi pasti [§] ; dan Dia memberitahuinya — dan ia terpercaya, yang dipercayai [§] — rahsia tersembunyi ilmu-Nya yang terjaga [§] — dari halus apa yang telah dan akan ada [§] — dari hakikat halus ilmu-Nya yang tersimpan; semoga Allah Yang Maha Tinggi selawat, salam, kemuliaan dan pengagungan pada zatnya yang sempurna dan maqam sucinya [§] , serta keluarga kemuliaan warisan mulia dan sahabat pangkat agung serta hidayah paling berharga [§] , dan atas pengikut jalan lurus mereka serta yang mengikuti mereka dengan kebaikan [§] — sepanjang masa dengan silih ganti manusia di tiap zaman [§] — selawat dan salam kekal tidak terpisah selama malam gelap dan subuh bernafas [§] — tiap pandangan dan nafas, [sebanyak] ilmu Allah yang qadim lagi suci meliputi [§] . (Adapun kemudian) — berkata yang lemah, hina, yang menyandang tiap kelemahan [§] — 'Abd al-Qādir ibn 'Alī al-Ḥusaynī al-Adhamī [§] , penghuni Dar al-Hijrah, lingkungan Pelindung Jiran, al-Madīnah al-Munawwarah [§] , khadam bilik mulia dalam ruang suci Nabawi yang tinggi [§] — semoga Allah Yang Maha Tinggi bersamanya di mana jua dengan bantuan pemeliharaan-Nya [§] dan mata penjagaan-Nya [§] , serta menempatkannya di halaman pelindungan-Nya [§] dengan kekalnya penjagaan-Nya [§] — amin, ya Allah, amin [§] . Ini ringkasan singkat — aku petik mutiara faedahnya [§] , dan risalah berharga yang kurakam adat tujuan-tujuannya [§] — dalam menyusun akar penting dan menetapkan hasil cabang dari ilmu raml (geomancy) [§] — dengan pengantin ungkapan dan khazanah jarang allusi, bagi yang keindahannya dekat dicapai oleh 'arifnya [§] , dan peringatan bagi yang menunjuk kepadanya [§] — mewujudkan harapan pencari [§] dan mendekatkan amal orang berkeinginan [§] — dengan bantuan sokongan alam melalui ilmu peliharaan, maha agung keagungan-Nya [§] , dan kurnia-Nya melimpah.

[Sajak:] 'Bila Allah kehendaki memuliakan peliharaan-Nya [§] Dia anugerahkan halus pertolongan-Nya; dan bila pertolongan Allah pada manusia bersambung [§] Yang Menghitung jadikan ilmu alamnya berkhidmat.'

Dan aku jadikannya atas tiga puluh tujuan [maqāṣid] [§] — menjadi lembut dan bercahaya sebagai sumber dan tempat kembali [§] ; aku mudahkan petikan hakikat halusnya yang padat [§] , dan kuterangkan dengan huraian ringkas ringkasan ayat penting, suatu penghuraian; dan kunamakan 'Mizan al-'Adl [§] fi Maqāṣid Aḥkām al-Raml (Neraca Keadilan dalam Tujuan Hukum Geomancy).'
