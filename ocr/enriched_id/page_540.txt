English:
— 534 —

"…for it is the best companion and the best tender friend and the best loyal beloved for the people of the path and the reality, and the best weapon for the striving and the best lances for the witnessing — until I did not speak from caprice but from the flame of a fire I kindled from the most auspicious valley of happiness, ignited at the valley of Ṭūr of light upon the branches of the tree of presence, when I traveled the valley of realization with the arrival of the companion of success — the iron limit and the new earnestness and the auspicious fortune and the firm resolve. Indeed in that is a reminder for who has a heart or lends the ear while he is witness. And some of the sages said: 'Who is not moved by the lute and its strings, and the spring and its flowers — his temperament is corrupted and he may need treatment.' [Poetry:] 'The sun of the forenoon — its rising — does not harm * that it is not seen by who has no sight.' So who understood its allusions and untied the talismans of its treasures attained the hidden knowledge and the guarded secret and the Greatest Name and the most glorious mention. So if you are called in the garden of the sundus meadow and the narcissus garden and the most noble tree and the alluded degree and the meaningful breath and the kingly withdrawals and the Paradise gardens and the holy pages and the luminous dictation and the Self-Sufficient secrets and the merciful supplications and the Sufi subtleties and the spiritual descents and the Qur'ānic knower[s] and the Throne indications and the divine allusions and the unveiling managements and the Sufi skills and the Davidic psalms and the Ladunnian sciences and the Mosaic managements and the Solomonic seals and the Lokmanian exhortations and the Meccan openings and the temporal breaths and the beauty realities and the foundational forms and the Atlantean circles and the most glorious benefits — then upon you is the unveiling of the veils from your insight, to peruse your tablet which is the firm Book of Allah and His straight secret and His ancient treasure. The Exalted said: 'And in yourselves — do you not see?' So who does not know His book which is He, then he is not he. [Poetry:] 'And read the forms of temples that are lined * they inform you of the secret of the obscure address. So read my book — it suffices as your witness * guiding you from it by a knowledge you did not know.' And perhaps the veil is an unveiling, and the manifestation is a concealment * . And know that the false does not come to my book from before it nor from behind it, as He said: 'For it has successors from before it and from behind it, guarding it by the command of Allah' * . What you found in it — know that the matter in it is as you found it; and by Allah I swear I do not cast it to you but manifest, and I do not leave you in it pondering * . If you remember it and cast it, then a house of a Lord protecting it — and be clever for its reception. So who has intellect, Allah was his witness; and who has a self, the body was its witness. So O my regret over who was excessive in the day of his heedlessness, and from the company of the people of knowledge was parted — indeed his loss appeared at the profits of the two worlds, and his name was erased from the Tablet of the near ones. May Allah protect us and you from the abasement of the servant and the hate of rejection — indeed He is bountiful, generous, adorned, merciful, Compassionate, liberal, Bestower, Recompenser by the beneficence. * And Allah I ask that He inspire the understanding of what we aimed at and the unveiling of what we concealed — a brother, a friend, an agreeing, a true [companion]. And in this measure is sufficiency for who Allah granted success; and there is no power nor strength but by Allah the High, the Great; and may Allah bless our master Muhammad, the seal of the prophets and the Imam of the messengers, and his family and his companions altogether, and the followers until the Day of Religion — as the rememberers mentioned, and the heedless neglected to mention; and praise belongs to Allah, Lord of the worlds.

Indonesia:
— 534 —

"…kerana ia sahabat terbaik dan kawan penyayang terbaik dan kekasih setia terbaik bagi ahli tariqat dan hakikat, serta senjata terbaik bagi mujahadah dan lembing terbaik bagi mushahadah — hingga aku tidak berkata dari hawa nafsu melainkan dari nyala api yang kunyah dari lembah kebahagiaan paling baik, dinyalakan di lembah Ṭūr cahaya pada dahan pohon kehadiran, bila aku jelajah lembah realisasi dengan ketibaan sahabat kejayaan — batas besi dan kesungguhan baru dan nasib baik dan azam teguh. Sesungguhnya pada itu peringatan bagi yang berhati atau memberikan telinga sedang ia saksi. Dan sebahagian hikmat berkata: 'Siapa tidak tergerak dengan lut dan talinya, dan musim bunga serta bunganya — tabiatnya rosak dan mungkin perlu rawatan.' [Sajak:] 'Matahari waktu dhuha — terbitnya — tidak memudaratkan * bahawa ia tidak dilihat oleh yang tiada penglihatan.' Maka siapa faham allusinya dan buka talisman khazannya mencapai ilmu tersembunyi dan rahsia terpelihara dan Nama Teragung dan dzikir paling mulia. Maka bila engkau diseru dalam taman padang sundus dan kebun narsis dan pohon paling mulia dan darjah terallusi dan nafas bermakna dan penarikan raja dan taman Syurga dan helaian suci dan dikte bercahaya dan rahsia al-Ṣamad dan doa rahmani dan halus sufi dan turunan rohani dan 'arif Qur'ān dan petunjuk 'Arasy dan allusi ketuhanan dan pengurusan kasyaf dan kemahiran sufi dan zabur Daud dan ilmu Ladunnī dan pengurusan Musa dan mohor Sulaiman dan nasihat Lokman dan pembukaan Mekah dan nafas masa dan hakikat keindahan dan bentuk asas dan bulatan Atlan dan faedah paling mulia — maka atasmu membuka tabir dari bashirahmu, untuk membaca lohmu yang ialah Kitab Allah yang teguh dan rahsia lurus-Nya serta khazanah purba-Nya. Yang Maha Tinggi berfirman: 'Dan pada dirimu — tidakkah kamu melihat?' Maka siapa tidak kenal kitab-Nya yang ialah Dia, maka ia bukan dia. [Sajak:] 'Dan bacalah bentuk bait yang tersusun * mereka khabarkan engkau rahsia alamat keliru. Maka bacalah kitabku — ia cukup sebagai saksimu * membimbingmu daripadanya dengan ilmu yang tidak engkau tahu.' Dan mungkin tabir itu pembukaan, dan zahir itu penyembunyian * . Dan ketahuilah bahwa batil tidak datang ke kitabku dari hadapannya mahupun belakangnya, sebagaimana firman-Nya: 'Kerana ia mempunyai pengganti dari hadapannya dan belakangnya, memeliharanya dengan perintah Allah' * . Apa yang engkau dapati padanya — ketahuilah urusan padanya sebagaimana engkau dapati; dan demi Allah aku bersumpah tidak aku lemparkan padamu melainkan nyata, dan tidak aku biarkan engkau memikirnya * . Bila engkau ingat dan lemparkannya, maka rumah Tuhan memeliharanya — dan jadilah cerdik untuk menerimanya. Maka siapa berakal, Allah saksi baginya; dan siapa berjiwa, jasad saksi baginya. Maka wahai sesalku atas yang berlebihan dalam hari lalainya, dan dari teman ahli ilmu terpisah — sesungguhnya kerugiannya nampak pada keuntungan dua alam, dan namanya dipadam dari Loh orang dekat. Semoga Allah pelihara kami dan kamu dari kehinaan hamba dan kebencian penolakan — sesungguhnya Ia murah, pemurah, berhias, penyayang, Pengasih, liberal, Pemberi, Pembalas dengan kebaikan. * Dan Allah aku mohon agar Dia ilhamkan pemahaman apa yang kami tuju dan pembukaan apa yang kami rahsiakan — saudara, kawan, yang sepakat, yang benar. Dan pada ukuran ini cukup bagi yang Allah berikan kejayaan; dan tiada daya mahupun kuasa melainkan dengan Allah Yang Tinggi, Yang Agung; dan semoga Allah selawatkan penghulu kami Muhammad, penutup nabi dan Imam rasul, serta keluarga dan sahabatnya seluruhnya, dan pengikut hingga Hari Agama — sebagaimana peringat sebut, dan yang lalai abaikan menyebut; dan segala puji bagi Allah, Tuhan semesta alam.
