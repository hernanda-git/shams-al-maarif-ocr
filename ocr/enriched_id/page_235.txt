...a surrounding enclosure behind them; nay, it is a Glorious Qur'ān in a Preserved Tablet. And His power encompassed the dominion of the heavens, so unto Him all return. Our Lord, His mercy and knowledge encompass all things. The spirits departed and the faces became ugly, and all their spectres wandered in "hāhā," and were scattered in the artifacts of His preference, and took form in the molds of the spiritual beings for witnessing the difference of forms in the molds of composition, in the circular Barzakh, by the appearance of judgment over the indication, and the appearance of knowledge—its outward is the outward of power, and its inward is the inward of command, and He is the hand-secret for the acceptance of the courses of judgment and acting by it. His Throne encompasses the heavens and the earth, and their preservation does not weary Him, and He is the Exalted, the Great. Expand for us in knowledge from Your Ever-Livingness, and in what You dispose by it in the beings. There is no power nor strength for me except by You. I have raised my poverty to You and grasped with You the hand of Your two hands, so do not disappoint my hope in You, and You are the All-Embracing, the Great Lord. I ask You by the diversity of the life of the spiritual spirits, and by the kinds of the secrets of the Greatest, the Most Great King—through whose manifestation the thirst of the hearts of the people of clear love and evident proof were benefited, so they fell in the valleys of the purity of their secret and the lights of their essences—and they called out: O He whose Throne encompasses the heavens and the earth, and their preservation does not weary Him, and He is the Exalted, the Great! O Generous, O Merciful, O Compassionate, O Forbearing! He is only Allah; there is no god but He, the Living, the Self-Subsisting; slumber does not seize Him nor sleep. To Him belongs what is in the heavens and what is in the earth. Who is it that intercedes with Him except by His permission? He knows what is before them and what is behind them, and they do not encompass a thing of His knowledge except as He wills. His Throne encompasses the heavens and the earth, and their preservation does not weary Him, and He is the Exalted, the Great. I ask You, O Allah, by the right of these mighty verses and noble names, to illuminate our hearts, widen our provisions, and refine our character. O Companion of hearts, O Concealer of faults, O Remover of griefs, O Forgiver of sins, O Knower of the unseen! Accept what was of my asking and my apology in my seclusion, and my being excused from my slip, and my turning away from my sin. And You, O Allah, know my concern, the Observer of my intention, the Knower of my inner state. Owner of the dominion, my Lord, and Seizer of my forelock, my Goal in my requests and my hope at my hardship, my Comforter in my loneliness, my Mercy on my weeping, my Excuser from my stumbling, my Answerer of my call. So if I fell short of what You commanded me and committed what You forbade me from, my zeal came to You and by Your veil You concealed me. O Most Generous of the generous, O Responder of the seekers, O Owner of the Day of Religion! You know what I hid in the breast, and the Disposer of the affairs of the small and the great. So if You have decreed my need—Your bounty—I ask You that it intercede for my soul, and that You have mercy on me with Your mercy which encompasses all things, O Most Merciful of the merciful. And I ask You, O Allah, by the right of this noble verse and these great names, to bless Muhammad and his family and his companions and grant him peace, and to give me what I asked of You, O Lord of the worlds. * And among the special properties of the noble Āyat al-Kursī: when the servant is full of sins and faults and wants to repent with his need and has desisted from what he did, let him stand in the white nights of any month—the thirteenth, fourteenth, and fifteenth—let him purify his clothes and place, stand in the deep of night, perform ablution, and pray four rak'ahs, reciting in the first al-Fātiḥah once and Āyat al-Kursī seven times; he does likewise in each rak'ah. When he gives the salām he sits and seeks great forgiveness of Allah seventy times, and sends blessings on the Prophet (may Allah bless him and grant him peace) seventy times. The description of the blessing: you say "O Allah, bless our master Muhammad with a blessing that delivers us from all terrors and afflictions, fulfills for us all needs, purifies us from all evils, raises us by it to the highest degrees, and conveys us by it to the utmost ends among all good things in life and after death—a blessing he stored for the Day of the Greatest Terror and its fear—and upon his family and companions, and grant him peace." Then you begin reading this supplication, saying: "My God, You are the Oft-Returning to whoever repented, the One who brings near whoever turned, and the Remover of his darkness."

Dan aku memohon kepada-Mu, ya Allah, demi hak ayat mulia dan nama agung ini, untuk bershalawat atas Muhammad, keluarga, dan sahabatnya serta memberi salam, dan memberikan kepadaku apa yang kuminta dari-Mu, wahai Tuhan semesta alam. * Dan di antara khususiyat Āyat al-Kursī yang mulia: apabila seorang hamba banyak dosa dan kesalahan dan hendak bertaubat dengan kebutuhannya serta telah menjauhi apa yang diperbuatnya, hendaklah ia berdiri pada malam-malam putih dari bulan apa pun—tanggal tiga belas, empat belas, dan lima belas—hendaklah ia bersihkan pakaian dan tempatnya, berdiri di tengah malam, berwudhu, dan shalat empat rakaat, membaca pada rakaat pertama al-Fātiḥah sekali dan Āyat al-Kursī tujuh kali; ia lakukan serupa pada tiap rakaat. Apabila ia salam, ia duduk dan memohon ampun kepada Allah Yang Mahaagung tujuh puluh kali, dan bershalawat atas Nabi (saw) tujuh puluh kali. Rupa shalawat: engkau ucapkan "Ya Allah, limpahkan shalawat atas penghulu kami Muhammad dengan shalawat yang menyelamatkan kami dari segala ketakutan dan bencana, memenuhi bagi kami segala kebutuhan, menyucikan kami dari segala kejelekan, mengangkat kami ke derajat tertinggi, dan menyampaikan kami ke tujuan paling jauh di antara segala kebaikan dalam hidup dan setelah mati—shalawat yang ia simpan untuk Hari Ketakutan Terbesar dan rasa takutnya—dan atas keluarga serta sahabatnya, serta memberi salam." Lalu engkau mulai membaca doa ini, seraya berkata: "Tuhanku, Engkaulah Yang Maha Penerima taubat bagi siapa pun yang bertaubat, Yang Mendekatkan siapa pun yang kembali, dan Yang Menyingkirkan kegelapannya."
