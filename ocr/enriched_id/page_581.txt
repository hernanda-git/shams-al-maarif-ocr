English:
- ٥٧٥ -
"[Sixth Clime continued:] on the island of Cyprus and Rhodes, and passes through the lands of Tangier, ending at the ocean to the west. In this clime are twenty-five great mountains, and twenty-five long rivers, and twelve known cities; and the colors of its people are between swarthiness and whiteness mostly.
( The Seventh Clime )
[It is] for the orb of Aquarius, and specific to it is the planet Moon. It begins from the east to the lands of Gog and Magog, and passes through the lands of Khurasan — and in it Khwarazm and Adharbayjan and Sijistan and Bardha'a and Arran and Khilat — and passes over the lands of Rum to Greater Rome and al-Andalus, then ends at the ocean in the Maghreb. In this clime are thirty great mountains, and fifteen long rivers of the known cities; and its people are mostly white of color.
( Supplement [Tatmim] )
The beginning of these seven climes is from the equator-line between east and west from the direction of the South Pole, and their end is at the North Pole, midway in that half of the terrestrial globe; and the eastern part of it and the western part are ruined, and the inhabited [part] is between them, and the other half of the globe from the equator to the South Pole is ruined, with no habitation in it — so it is the inhabited quarter of the globe. And the authorities differed in the manner of its placement, according to the habitation they discovered and that appeared to them in the northern half of the terrestrial globe in this form [a diagram]:

Indonesia:
- ٥٧٥ -
"[Iklim Keenam disambung:] pulau Cyprus Rhodes, melalui Tangier ke lautan barat. Dua puluh lima gunung, dua puluh lima sungai, dua belas kota; penduduk sawo putih.
( Iklim Ketujuh )
Orb Aquarius, khusus Bulan. Dari timur tanah Yajuj Majuj, melalui Khurasan (Khwarazm Adharbayjan Sijistan Bardha'a Arran Khilat), ke tanah Rum Rom Besar Andalus, berakhir lautan Maghreb. Tiga puluh gunung, lima belas sungai; penduduk kebanyakan putih.
( Tatmim )
Permulaan tujuh iklim dari garis khatulistiwa antara timur barat dari Kutub Selatan, berakhir Kutub Utara pertengahan separuh bumi; bahagian timur barat runtuh, yang didiami di antaranya, separuh lagi dari khatulistiwa ke Kutub Selatan runtuh tiada penempatan — ia suku didiami bumi. Ahli berbeza cara letak mengikut penempatan di separuh utara bumi begini [gambarajah]: UTARA [gambarajah] khatulistiwa SELATAN. Dan yang lain berpendapat ia di separuh utara bumi susunan membulat — dua ke Kutub Utara (ketujuh enam), tiga melintang timur barat (kelima dari barat, keempat, ketiga), dua di khatulistiwa (kedua dari barat, pertama ke timur). Tiga di timur — ketujuh Marikh, kelima Matahari, kedua Zuhal; tiga di barat — keenam Bulan, ketiga Zuhrah, pertama Musytari; keempat 'Utarid di antaranya; disusun demikian.
