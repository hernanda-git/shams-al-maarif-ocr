And there remains the counting of the letters; so if you add these names or secure them upon the numerical talisman as the people of the talismans adjust, with the intention of a matter agreeing with the name al-Ḥayy al-Qayyūm — and what is added to the talisman, there appears its effect in what is willed by it of the acts. And measure by that the special properties and the multiplication of the breaking and the mingling of the natures of the letters some into some and their interpenetration, and the special properties of the numbers which Allah deposited in them and in their natures. And know that who mentions the name of Allah al-Ḥayy and the names beginning with the ḥā', and they are: al-Ḥayy, al-Ḥakīm, al-Majīd, al-Ḥalīm, al-Ḥannān, al-Ḥasīb, al-Ḥafīẓ, al-Ḥaqq — at the rising of the sun in the hot days, he never feels the pain of the heat ever; and consider in the ranks of the numbers of these names, for you find after the letter ḥā' a letter from the first ranks of the tens — so in al-Ḥayy how the yā' emerged, and in al-Ḥakīm the kāf after it, and it is 20, and in al-Ḥalīm the lām 30, and so on.
