Arabic:
— ٣٠٠ —

«وَأَسْرَارُهُمْ أَجْرًا غَيْرَ مَكْنُونٍ المَكْنُوتُ المَظْهَرُ أَنْوَاعَ الكَائِنَاتِ الكَافِ وَالنُّونِ * وَأَمَّا اسْمُهُ تَعَالَىٰ الوَالِدُ — فَهُوَ اسْمٌ عَظِيمٌ، وَالمَلِكُ المَخْلُوقُ مِنْهُ «مَعْيَائِيلُ»، وَهُوَ رَئِيسٌ عَلَىٰ أَرْبَعَةِ قُوَّادٍ، تَحْتَ يَدِ كُلِّ قَائِدٍ ٥٤١ صَفًّا، كُلُّ صَفٍّ ٥٤١؛ وَالرُّوحُ يَنْزِلُ عَلَىٰ الذَّاكِرِ. وَدُعَاؤُهُ تَقُولُ: «يَا وَالِدُ، أَنْتَ الَّذِي تَوَلَّيْتَ أَمْرَ البَرِيَّةِ، وَكَلَّمْتَ ذَوَاتِهِمْ بِرَفْعِ البَيْنِيَّةِ، وَأَوْصَلْتَ كُلَّ مَخْلُوقٍ لِمَا خَلَقْتَهُ لَهُ مِنَ المَوَاهِبِ السَّنِيَّةِ. أَسْأَلُكَ اللَّهُمَّ — الوِلَادَةَ الكُبْرَىٰ، وَالحِكْمَةَ العُلْيَا، وَالنُّورَ الأَبْهَىٰ، وَالوُصُولَ إِلَىٰ المَسْجِدِ الأَقْصَىٰ؛ وَارْزُقْنِي رُؤْيَةَ حَقَائِقِ الأَشْيَاءِ بِكَشْفِ مَنَازِلِ الأَنْبِيَاءِ. إِنَّكَ جَزِيلُ الخَيْرِ وَالنَّعْمَاءِ».

وَأَمَّا اسْمُهُ تَعَالَىٰ المُهَيْمِنُ — فَهُوَ اسْمٌ عَظِيمٌ، وَالمَلِكُ المَخْلُوقُ مِنْ عَدَدِهِ «جَهَايِيلُ»، وَهُوَ رَئِيسٌ عَلَىٰ أَرْبَعَةِ قُوَّادٍ، تَحْتَ يَدِ كُلِّ قَائِدٍ ٢٠٢ صَفًّا، كُلُّ صَفٍّ ٢٠٢؛ وَالرُّوحُ يَنْزِلُ عَلَىٰ الذَّاكِرِ. وَدُعَاؤُهُ تَقُولُ: «اللَّهُمَّ، أَنْتَ الَّذِي فَتَحْتَ طُرُقَ الهِدَايَةِ، وَعَرَّفْتَ أَوْلِيَاءَكَ أَسْرَارَ الكَشْفِ وَالفَتْحِ وَالدَّرَايَةِ، وَنَوَّرْتَ بَصَائِرَ أَهْلِ العِرْفَانِ، وَخَلَّصْتَهُمْ مِنَ الضَّلَالَةِ وَالغَوَايَةِ. أَسْأَلُكَ بِعُلُوِّ شَأْنِكَ، وَقُوَّةِ سُلْطَانِكَ، وَاسْتِيلَاءِ أَمْرِكَ، وَبُرْهَانِكَ — أَنْ تَرْفَعَنِي مِنْ حَضِيضِ الإِنْسَافِ إِلَىٰ فَتْقِ الجَمْعِ وَالكَمَالِ، وَأَيِّدْنِي بِأَحْسَنِ النَّوَالِ، وَحَقِّقْ مَنَاهِجَ بَوَاطِنِ الوِصَالِ. إِنَّكَ أَنْتَ اللَّهُ المُحْسِنُ الفَعَّالُ».

وَأَمَّا اسْمُهُ تَعَالَىٰ البَرُّ — فَفِيهِ حَرْفٌ مِنَ الاسْمِ الأَعْظَمِ؛ وَمَلِكُهُ «فِيَائِيلُ»، وَهُوَ رَئِيسٌ عَلَىٰ أَرْبَعَةِ قُوَّادٍ، تَحْتَ يَدِ كُلِّ قَائِدٍ ٢٠٢ صَفًّا، كُلُّ صَفٍّ ٢٠٢؛ وَالرُّوحُ يَنْزِلُ عَلَىٰ الذَّاكِرِ. وَدُعَاؤُهُ تَقُولُ: «يَا بَرُّ، أَنْتَ الَّذِي أَحْسَنْتَ لِكُلِّ مَخْلُوقٍ بِقُدْرَتِكَ؛ وَأَنْتَ الَّذِي أَخْفَيْتَ كُلَّ نَاقِصٍ، وَأَخْفَيْتَ أَمْرَهُ فِي أَمْرِكَ. وَأَنْتَ المُحْسِنُ المُتَفَضِّلُ عَلَىٰ مَنْ أَقْبَلَ عَلَيْكَ بِجُلُوسِ الإِيمَانِ، رَاجِعًا إِلَيْكَ بِقَلْبٍ وَلِسَانٍ. وَأَنْتَ الَّذِي تَقْصِمُ الطَّغَاةَ، وَتُشَدِّدُ العِقَابَ عَلَىٰ الطَّغَاةِ، وَتَعْفُو عَنِ المُذْنِبِينَ، وَتُبْدِلُ سَيِّئَاتِهِمْ حَسَنَاتٍ — ذُو الرَّأْفَةِ فِي حَقِّ الرَّاضِينَ، وَالرَّحْمَةِ فِي حَقِّ الصَّالِحِينَ، وَالعِزَّةِ وَالكِبْرِيَاءِ فِي حَقِّ الآئِبِينَ إِلَيْكَ وَالرَّاحِمِينَ إِلَىٰ يَوْمِ الدِّينِ».

English:
— 300 —

"And their secrets are a reward, not hidden — the concealed made manifest of the kinds of beings, the Kāf and the Nūn. * As for His exalted Name the Begetter — it is a tremendous name, and the angel created from it is 'Ma'yā'īl', and he is chief over four commanders, under each commander 541 ranks, each rank 541; and the spirit descends upon the invoker. And his supplication says: 'O Begetter, You are He who undertook the affair of creation, and spoke to their essences by raising the separation, and conveyed every creature to what You created for it of the splendid gifts. I ask You, O Allah — the greatest begetting, and the highest wisdom, and the most radiant light, and reaching the Farthest Mosque; and grant me vision of the realities of things by unveiling the stations of the prophets. Indeed You are the abundant in good and blessings.' As for His exalted Name the Guardian — it is a tremendous name, and the angel created from its number is 'Jahā'īl', and he is chief over four commanders, under each commander 202 ranks, each rank 202; and the spirit descends upon the invoker. And his supplication says: 'O Allah, You are He who opened the paths of guidance, and made Your friends know the secrets of unveiling and opening and discernment, and illuminated the insights of the people of gnosis, and purified them from error and misguidance. I ask You by the height of Your station, and the power of Your dominion, and the seizure of Your affair, and Your proof — that You raise me from the lowliness of human-ness to the bursting of gathering and perfection, and support me by the best of gifts, and realize the methods of the inwardness of union. Indeed You are Allah, the Good-Doer.' As for His exalted Name the Kind — in it is a letter of the Greatest Name; and its angel is 'Fiyā'īl', and he is chief over four commanders, under each commander 202 ranks, each rank 202; and the spirit descends upon the invoker. And his supplication says: 'O Kind, You are He who did good to every creature by Your power; and You are He who concealed every deficient thing, and hid its affair in Your affair. And You are the Good-Doer, the Bestower upon him who turns to You by sitting in faith, returning to You with heart and tongue. And You are He who crushes the tyrants, and tightens the punishment upon the tyrants, and pardons the sinners, and substitutes their evil deeds with good — Possessor of tenderness toward the content, mercy toward the righteous, and might and majesty toward those who return to You and the merciful, until the Day of Recompense.'"

Indonesia:

"Dan rahasia mereka adalah pahala, tiada tersembunyi — yang tersembunyi dijadikan nyata dari jenis makhluk, Kāf dan Nūn. * Adapun nama-Nya yang mulia al-Wālid — ia nama agung, dan malaikat yang diciptakan darinya ialah 'Ma'yā'īl', dan ia pemimpin atas empat panglima, di bawah tiap panglima 541 shaf, tiap shaf 541; dan ruh turun atas orang yang berdzikir. Dan doanya membaca: 'Wahai al-Wālid, Engkaulah Yang Mengurus urusan ciptaan, dan berfirman pada dzat mereka dengan mengangkat perpisahan, serta menyampaikan setiap makhluk pada apa yang Engkau ciptakan baginya dari anugerah yang indah. Aku memohon pada-Mu ya Allah — kelahiran terbesar, dan hikmah tertinggi, serta cahaya paling cemerlang, dan sampainya ke Masjid al-Aqsa; serta rizkikanlah aku melihat hakikat segala sesuatu dengan penyingkapan maqam para nabi. Sesungguhnya Engkaulah Yang Maha Berlimpah kebaikan dan nikmat.' Adapun nama-Nya yang mulia al-Muhaymin — ia nama agung, dan malaikat yang diciptakan dari bilangannya ialah 'Jahā'īl', dan ia pemimpin atas empat panglima, di bawah tiap panglima 202 shaf, tiap shaf 202; dan ruh turun atas orang yang berdzikir. Dan doanya membaca: 'Ya Allah, Engkaulah Yang Membuka jalan petunjuk, dan memperkenalkan kekasih-Mu rahasia penyingkapan dan pembukaan serta pemahaman, dan menerangi bashīrah ahli ma'rifat, serta menyucikan mereka dari kesesatan dan penyesatan. Aku memohon pada-Mu dengan ketinggian maqam-Mu, dan kekuatan kerajaan-Mu, serta pengambil-alihan urusan-Mu, dan hujjah-Mu — semoga Engkau angkat aku dari kerendahan kemanusiaan ke terbukanya pengumpulan dan kesempurnaan, serta tolonglah aku dengan anugerah terbaik, dan nyatakanlah cara-cara batin persatuan.
