English:
— 539 —

"…[the science of geomancy] — and I ask Allah the Generous, from His ample bounty, * that He make by it the general benefit * across the duration of time, for the specific and the general * , and make the striving by it thanked * , and the deed righteous, accepted, and approved * , and suffice us the evil of every envious reprobate * , and the guile of every hateful sinful enemy * , and Allah the Exalted is the best answerer * , and the most generous refuge and hope and recompenser * — upon Him I rely and to Him I turn * , and He, exalted is His affair, is the nearest near one * , and Allah suffices me and is enough * , and peace upon His servant whom He chose. (The First Intent: on the matter of this science) It is in reality a great secret * , from the secrets of the Mighty, the Wise * — it descended from the sky, and the dust received it and what is in it * , so its rules bring forth the conscience in everything and make it manifest * ; and it is, in the eyes of the seeing * , observed by the eyes of consideration * . It is related that the first to descend with it by the command of the King, the All-Knowing * , was Jibrīl the Trustworthy upon Idrīs, and after him upon Nūḥ — upon them be peace * . And there cared for it, of the early and the late * , a great multitude, and the banners of the scholars, and the imams of the knowers * . And it came — upon what was said of the allusion to it * — in the hadith of our Prophet the Chosen (may Allah bless him and grant him peace) * : 'There was a prophet among the prophets who would draw lines; so whoever matched his lines, then that [is it] * — ' so he is, of magnificent affair and great proof, a noble science of comprehension * ; and according to the extent of the encompassing of it is the knowledge * , and according to the extent of the adornment is the manifestation with the perfection of the attribute * — for its expressions are deep of depth * , and its allusions are successive of cycle * , and its rules hidden of perception * , and its results far of paths * ; it needs the immersion of the sound heart in the intent of the straight way of its essence * . That is the bounty of Allah — He gives it to whom He wills, and Allah is the possessor of the great bounty. (And its subject) Geomancy [al-raml] — and it is the point; and that is that the investigation of it is from two aspects: the even [zawj] and the odd [fard], and they are intrinsic accidents of it, and its locus is the houses [buyūt] and the forms [ashkāl] subsisting in them. (And its rule) That it is conjectural in establishment, conjectural in indication; its induction and encompassing by it [are possible] in a balanced manner — and Allah is most knowing.

Indonesia:
— 539 —

"…[ilmu geomancy] — dan aku memohon Allah Yang Mulia, dari kurnia luas-Nya, * agar Dia jadikan dengannya faedah umum * sepanjang masa, bagi khas dan am  * , dan jadikan usaha dengannya diberi syukur * , dan amal soleh, diterima, diredhai * , serta cukupkan kami kejahatan tiap yang hasad lagi celaka * , dan tipu daya tiap musuh benci lagi berdosa * , dan Allah Yang Maha Tinggi sebaik pemakbul * , dan tempat berlindung, harapan dan pembalas paling mulia * — pada-Nya aku berserah dan kepada-Nya aku kembali * , dan Ia, maha tinggi urusan-Nya, Yang Dekat paling dekat * , dan cukuplah Allah bagiku * , dan salam atas hamba-Nya yang dipilih. (Maqasad Pertama: tentang perkara ilmu ini) Ia hakikatnya rahsia agung * , dari rahsia Yang Perkasa, Yang Bijaksana * — turun dari langit, lalu tanah menerimanya dan apa padanya * , maka hukumnya mengeluarkan hati nurani dalam tiap perkara dan menzahirkannya * ; dan ia, di sisi yang berpandangan * , diperhatikan dengan mata pertimbangan * . Diriwayatkan bahwa pertama turun dengannya dengan perintah Raja Yang Maha Mengetahui * ialah Jibrīl al-Amīn atas Idrīs, dan setelahnya atas Nūḥ — ke atas mereka salam * . Dan mengurusnya, dari awal dan akhir * , ramai yang banyak, dan panji ulama serta imam orang 'arif * . Dan ia datang — pada apa disebut allusi kepadanya * — dalam hadis Nabi kita yang Terpilih s.a.w. * : 'Ada nabi dari nabi-nabi yang menulis garisan; maka barangsiapa sepadan garisannya, maka itu [dia] * —' maka ia, urusan agung dan hujah besar, ilmu mulia kefahaman * ; dan menurut kadar liputan terhadapnya ialah ilmu * , dan menurut kadar perhiasan ialah tajalli dengan kesempurnaan sifat * — kerana ungkapannya dalam sedalam-dalamnya * , dan allusinya bersilih gilir * , dan hukumnya tersembunyi tanggapan * , dan hasilnya jauh laluan * ; ia perlukan tenggelamnya hati sihat dalam tujuan jalan lurus zatnya * . Itu kurnia Allah — Dia berinya pada siapa dikehendaki, dan Allah Pemilik kurnia agung. (Dan subjeknya) Geomancy [al-raml] — dan ia titik; dan ia bahwa penyelidikannya dari dua aspek: genap [zawj] dan ganjil [fard], dan mereka aksiden intrinsiknya, serta tempatnya rumah [buyūt] dan bentuk [ashkāl] yang ada padanya. (Dan hukumnya) Bahwa ia sangkaan dalam penetapan, sangkaan dalam petunjuk; induksinya dan liputannya dengannya [mungkin] secara seimbang — dan Allah lebih mengetahui.
