English:
— 423 —

"…from all the knowledge — 'There is no god but Allah,' for the knowledge of the firsts and the lasts is folded in the saying 'There is no god but Allah,' and that all the prophets altogether attained the manifestation of the word 'There is no god but Allah.' And the Exalted said to His Prophet: 'So know that there is no god but Allah, and seek forgiveness for your sin.' And he (upon him peace) said: 'The best of remembrance is "There is no god but Allah," and the best of supplication is "Praise be to Allah."' And that all the deeds the angels ascend with them except 'There is no god but Allah' — for it ascends by itself. And some of the exegetes said in the meaning of His saying 'When the sun is folded up and when the stars fall' — i.e. on the Day of Resurrection the word 'There is no god but Allah' manifests upon whom its last word was; and the key of Paradise is 'There is no god but Allah.' And know that all the deeds and acts of obedience on the Day of Resurrection vanish except extolling and the testimony that there is no god but Allah — for it ascends with its bearer to the pure light until the lights shine upon him in the Gathering. And the worships at the end of time become habits and are not accepted except by the saying 'There is no god but Allah'; and Jonah (upon him peace) used to mention it in the belly of the whale. And know that every obedience of the servant — the angels come to it except the word 'There is no god but Allah,' for it comes out of the person's soul as if it were a standing light and ascends by itself, and it has a chanting with extolling. And were we to begin in its merit and the circumstances of its remembrance, the discourse would lengthen upon us and go beyond the bound of prolixity. I say: whoever has a need with Allah the Exalted, let him sit in an empty place, then begin with the recitation of the remembrance — and it is the saying 'There is no god but Allah' seventy thousand times — for he does not rise from his station except that his need is fulfilled; so know that. (Section) And the Lām of the Name of Glory divides to a final — and it is when you bring the name of the Essence and soften it, for it utters the name of divinity; the example of that — if you remove the Lām and gather it, it utters the name Ilāh; and if you remove the Alif and the Lām, it utters the name Āh; and if you drop the Lām and the Hā', it utters the great name MARYĀN, and it is the Alif; and if you make the Alif and the two Lāms articulate and a letter utters its name 'Huwa' — and it is Amam, articulating from the name of the Essence and gathering to all the names related to it; and all the names, if you dissolve them, do not utter this meaning — the A-Hū-Wā, if you dissolve it, it utters when we pair it. And the reason for its naming 'the Gatherer' is that it gathers the secrets; from that: when you say 'O Raḥmān, O Raḥīm, O Allah, help me and have mercy on me, O Allah'; and when you say 'O Ghaffār, O Allah, help me and forgive me, O Allah'; and when you are in distress, say 'Relief from me, O Allah' — and for that is its relation in all the names: whatever name a human utters of the names, he is not but related to the name of the Essence in all the names, and their relation from it is by this meaning — so understand. (Section) And among the special properties of this noble name for the healing of ailments and illnesses is that one writes this name its number — and it is 66 times — and erases it and drinks it for the cure of Allah the Exalted; and this Greatest Name is also written for all the afflicted, erased and drunk. And if you will to imprison a jinn, write its letters on his fingers — and he is confined. And if you will to burn a jinn, write the Name of Glory in letters on a blue rag and burn its edge and let him smell it — and if you will to burn him and kill him or have him speak, do it. And if the ailing one writes this name in a ring of gold on Sunday with the Ascendant in Aries and persists in its mention its number — Allah the Exalted raises his rank among all creatures; and if he writes it on Monday on white silver and persists in it — Allah the Exalted raises his rank and exalts his mention. And the Messenger of Allah (upon him peace) said: 'When the believer says "Allah," Allah the Exalted says "Here I am, My servant — I am Allah, so your need."' And Allah knows best that none knows the reality of the greatness of Allah the Exalted — the A-Hū-Wā — and He is the Lord of all, and He is of everything knowing in reality; for His standing foot is established without beginning and His remaining without end, and Oneness not from a number, and His attributes are outside the attributes of the creatures — it is necessary that the describers not reach the reality of His description, and were it so a limit and likeness would appear, and that is impossible. And know that the Imam al-Khwārizmī said: 'My heart has called out for the knowledge of the name of Allah, so journey with me in its seeking.'"

Indonesia:
— ٤٢٣ —

"…dari segala ilmu — 'Laa ilaha illallah,' kerana ilmu awal akhir terlipat dalam 'Laa ilaha illallah,' dan semua nabi capai zahir kalimah 'Laa ilaha illallah.' Firman pada Nabi: 'Ketahuilah tiada tuhan melainkan Allah, minta ampun dosamu.' Sabda: 'Sebaik zikir Laa ilaha illallah, sebaik doa Alhamdulillah.' Semua amal malaikat naik dengannya kecuali 'Laa ilaha illallah' — ia naik sendiri. Ahli tafsir kata makna 'Bila matahari dilipat bintang gugur' — hari kiamat kalimah 'Laa ilaha illallah' zahir atas siapa akhir katanya; kunci syurga 'Laa ilaha illallah.' Hari kiamat semua amal taat hilang kecuali tasbih dan syahadah 'Laa ilaha illallah' — naik dengan pembawanya ke cahaya suci hingga cahaya bersinar di Padang. Ibadah akhir zaman jadi adat tak diterima melainkan 'Laa ilaha illallah'; Yunus zikir dalam perut ikan. Tiap taat malaikat datang kecuali 'Laa ilaha illallah' — keluar dari jiwa seperti cahaya berdiri naik sendiri, ada rentak tasbih. Kalaulah kami sebut fadilatnya memanjang. Sesiapa ada hajat pada Allah, duduk tempat sunyi, baca zikir 'Laa ilaha illallah' tujuh puluh ribu kali — tak bangun melainkan hajat tertunai. (Fasal) Lām Jalalah bahagi akhir — bila bawa nama Zat dan lembutkan, ia sebut nama Uluhiyyah; contoh: buang Lām kumpul sebut Ilāh; buang Alif Lām sebut Āh; buang Lām Hā' sebut MARYĀN iaitu Alif; bila Alif dua Lām disebut huruf sebut 'Huwa' — Amam dari nama Zat kumpul semua nama; semua nama bila pecah tak sebut makna ini — A-Hū-Wā bila pecah sebut bila dipasang. Dinamakan 'Pengumpul' kerana kumpul rahsia; bila kata 'Wahai Raḥmān Raḥīm Allah tolong rahmati,' 'Wahai Ghaffār Allah tolong ampun,' 'Wahai Allah lega' — nisbahnya semua nama: apa manusia sebut nama, ia berhubung nama Zat dengan makna ini. (Fasal) Sifat nama mulia sembuh penyakit: tulis nama bilangannya 66, padam minum untuk sihat; nama Agung tulis untuk semua yang tertimpa, padam minum. Nak kurung jin, tulis huruf di jarinya — terkurung. Nak bakar jin, tulis Nama Jalalah di kain biru bakar hujung hidu — nak bakar bunuh atau bercakap, buat. Orang sakit tulis nama dalam cincin emas Ahad ascenden Aries istiqamah sebut bilangannya — Allah angkat darjat; tulis Isnin perak putih istiqamah — Allah angkat darjat sebutannya. Sabda: 'Bila mukmin kata Allah, Allah kata Labbaik hambaku Aku Allah tunaikan hajatmu.' Allah lebih tahu tiada tahu hakikat keagungan Allah — A-Hū-Wā — Tuhan segala, Maha Mengetahui; zat-Nya tegak tanpa awal kekal tanpa tamat, Esa bukan bilangan, sifat-Nya luar sifat makhluk — mustahil pencirinya capai hakikat sifat-Nya, kalau begitu ada had contoh mustahil. Imam Khwarizmi kata: 'Hatiku seru ilmu nama Allah, marilah dalam mencarinya.'"
