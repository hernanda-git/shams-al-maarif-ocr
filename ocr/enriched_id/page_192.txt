* And sometimes al-Ḥayy and al-Qayyūm are combined in one square, and this is its form
* And know that al-Ḥayy al-Qayyūm are two great names and they are a remembrance for the people of the presence, and they are of the invocations of Isrāfīl, upon him peace, and his angels — blessings of Allah all together; and whoever engraves these two names in the first [hour] of Friday while facing the qibla and keeps it with him — Allah, exalted, answers his heart and his remembrance if he was obscure, and flows his provision; and if he mounts his beast and one hundred and seventy-four and perfects it and carries it, he witnesses wonders — and this is its form and description.
And al-Kinānī, may Allah, exalted, have mercy on him, said: "I saw the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, in a dream, and I said to him: 'O Messenger of Allah, pray for me that Allah not cause my heart to die on the day the hearts die.' So the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said to me: 'Say every day: "O Living, O Self-Subsistent, by You I seek help — there is no god but You." '" * And know that whoever places His name, exalted, al-Ḥāfiẓ (Preserver) in a square and deposits it in the interior of the aforementioned figure in the dignity of the Sun and carries it with him — Allah, exalted, revives his heart, widens his provision, and preserves him in his family, self, and wealth; and whoever writes it on anything is preserved; and whoever knows its secret is enriched by it from other than Him, for it is of perfection to the utmost and expression does not reach it, and it is the Greatest Name of Allah.

( The Sixty-Fifth Chapter: On His name, exalted, { al-Wājid } ) This name of majestic rank — whoever abounds in its remembrance does not lose anything he wants to exist; and by it the wayfarers know their selves; and whoever persists in its remembrance until a state overcomes him from it finds in his inward a state he had not known before; and it is [known to] fit as a remembrance for one whose name is 'Abd al-Wāḥid. And it has the number 14, which is a pair-odd rectangle, for in it is nobility from its being compounded of the multiplication of the first pair in the first complete number, so it is counted by the seven twice — and it is the number of the luminous letters and the nights of increase of light, for they are nights of existence, and the nights of decrease are nights of loss; and it is a deficient number whose parts 10 point to the letter Yā' which is the name of the high revelation in His saying: { "By Me is heard and by Me is seen" } — therefore the like of its letters pointed to His saying "He is a deliverer"; and this is its form as you see:

* Dan terkadang al-Ḥayy dan al-Qayyūm digabung dalam satu bujur sangkar, dan ini rupanya
* Dan ketahuilah bahwa al-Ḥayy al-Qayyūm adalah dua nama agung dan keduanya adalah dzikir bagi ahli hadirat, serta termasuk dzikir Isrāfīl 'alaihissalām dan para malaikatnya — shalawat Allah semuanya; dan barangsiapa mengukir kedua nama ini pada jam pertama hari Jumat sambil menghadap kiblat dan menyimpannya — Allah Ta'ala mengabulkan hatinya dan dzikirnya apabila ia tersembunyi, serta mengalirkan rezekinya; dan apabila ia menaiki tunggangannya serta seratus tujuh puluh empat dan menyempurnakannya serta membawanya, ia menyaksikan keajaiban — dan ini rupanya dan sifatnya.
Dan al-Kinānī rahimahullah Ta'ala berkata: "Aku melihat Rasulullah shallallahu 'alaihi wa sallam dalam mimpi, lalu aku berkata: 'Wahai Rasulullah, doakanlah aku agar Allah tidak mematikan hatiku pada hari hati-hati mati.' Maka Rasulullah shallallahu 'alaihi wa sallam bersabda: 'Ucapkanlah setiap hari: Ya Ḥayyu ya Qayyūm, dengan Engkau aku memohon pertolongan, tiada tuhan selain Engkau.' " * Dan ketahuilah bahwa barangsiapa meletakkan nama-Nya Ta'ala al-Ḥāfiẓ (Yang Memelihara) dalam bujur sangkar dan menaruhnya di dalam rupa yang disebutkan tadi dalam kemuliaan Matahari serta membawanya — Allah Ta'ala menghidupkan hatinya, meluaskan rezekinya, dan memeliharanya pada keluarga, diri, dan hartanya; dan barangsiapa menulisnya pada apa pun menjadi terpelihara; dan barangsiapa mengetahui rahasianya, ia cukup dengannya dari selain-Nya, karena ia mencapai puncak kesempurnaan dan ungkapan tak menjangkaunya, dan ia adalah nama Allah Yang Paling Agung.

( Bab Keenam Puluh Lima: Tentang nama-Nya Ta'ala { al-Wājid } ) Nama bermartabat agung ini — barangsiapa banyak berdzikir dengannya tidak kehilangan apa pun yang ingin ada; dan dengannya para salik mengenal diri mereka; dan barangsiapa istiqamah dalam dzikirnya hingga keadaan menguasainya darinya mendapati dalam batinnya keadaan yang belum pernah ia kenal; dan ia diketahui cocok sebagai dzikir bagi yang bernama 'Abd al-Wāḥid.
