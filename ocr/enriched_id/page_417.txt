English:
— 411 —

[Continuation of the Fā' invocation — cipher names] Shalhūf bi-nuwār, yalhūf, yalhūfk, rafīq al-fawz bi-l-jināt, balhūf, falīfūf, shafshafūf 2, shafsh 2, shafsh 2 — shaghnaف 2, shaghf 2, ḍayf, shī'thawā 2, ṣayṣṣī'sī'sa's 'aṣūf — yaḍ'uف jins ḥasaf bi-amrika fāhānā; "so you are Sūh and Mūrawfā, and walk, and there is no harm in anger nor languor — bi-l-fūr, the standing alif, between me and such-and-such; and do for me such-and-such — by the F, there is no power nor strength save in Allah, the High, the Great." And the incense is pepper and fal, and the implicit concentration says: "Answer, O Samasṭasṭā'īl, by the right of Saṭf, Saṭīṭāl, Kayṭam, Laṭam, Laṭash 22, hanka — answer and unveil the veil between me and you, the hastening, al-waḥā, the hour." ﴿The letter Ṣād﴾ — it is of the letters of the Greatest Name, and among its properties: if you write on a piece of silk, then write on it the name of the higher king with the implicit concentration, and place it under the stone of a ring — its bearer attains good and blessing and is preserved from harms, and it disposes like the Jīm; and this is its figure.

[image of talisman]

And as for the retreat and employment: enter the retreat and recite the invocation and implicit concentration after every prayer 700 times, while you say "Answer, O servant of the letter Ṣād, and do for me such-and-such" — and he comes. And whoever persists in reciting the invocation, Allah grants him strength for obedience. And it is the basmala — "I ask You, O He who placed humility on the necks of His servants, so they are fearful of His authority; O He who is alone in might and eternal greatness and majesty — all His creation from His fear are in awe and submit under His command; O He whose allies on the Day of the greatest terror are secure — I ask You, O Generous, by the power by which You looked to the heaven and it rose, and to the earth and it spread, and to the mountains and they flattened, and to the springs and they gushed, and to the rivers and they flowed, and to the hearts and they feared and trembled, and to the dumb humans and they spoke, saying 'You are Allah, the One, the Subduer' — that You clothe me in light by which I am illumined for unveiling, and that You subordinate to me the servant of the letter Ṣād, the king Samā'īl, and the great heavenly name — dispose him in my business by Your command; conceal me with a servant and a tongue that drinks the beloved, increased his passion and departed with haste in my intent; salūh la-khabarā salk, so he does not blame me — he is the master of things, fa-'aw yāh 2 — and of his secret these words and the empowering: Laṭm, wa-hayā — by the F, there is no power nor strength save in Allah, the High, the Great — and answer, O Ṭa'īl, by the right of Ḥaṭī', Sa', Sa', 'Alṭaḥ, Sa' layj, yamūh yāh, huwa 2 — 'Peace, a word from a Lord Most Merciful' — answer and do such-and-such." ﴿The letter Qāf﴾ — if you write it with every name beginning with Q on a silk rag and place it under the stone of a ring or under a jacinth or Yemeni agate stone, and a person carries it — he attains acceptance. And among its properties, for subduing enemies and silencing tongues: you write it a hundred times on a paper with the name of whom you will, and hang it in the wind, and recite the invocation and implicit concentration after every prayer 100 [times], and draw the figure of the letter and place it on your head and seek him — and he attends, and his light is like the sun and the wayfarer sees it in his retreat, and his sitting is toward the qibla; and this is his figure. And its invocation: the basmala — "Your power, O Allah, overpowering to Your enemies, and Your strength and awe stand toward Your allies. I ask You, O Allah, to receive me on the shore of Your nearness and the closeness to You, O Allah. O Qarīb, gladden my heart's anxiety until it meets what illumines its joy, and it settles by the Qāf of Your power, and supply me with Your strength. O Qawī, strengthen by Your power and strong strength until I draw near to Him whom the earth does not draw near nor Your elevation, O Maqṣūd — so I drew near to You, the Qāf, and the Qāf trembled until it does not settle by him. Answer, Iqāf, and hasten to me."

Indonesia:
— ٤١١ —

[Lanjutan doa Fā' — nama-nama cipher] Shalhūf bi-nuwār, yalhūf, yalhūfk, rafīq al-fawz bi-l-jināt, balhūf, falīfūf, shafshafūf 2, shafsh 2, shafsh 2 — shaghnaف 2, shaghf 2, ḍayf, shī'thawā 2, ṣayṣṣī'sī'sa's 'aṣūf — yaḍ'uف jins ḥasaf bi-amrika fāhānā; "maka engkau Sūh dan Mūrawfā, dan berjalanlah, tiada bahaya dalam murka mahupun lemah — bi-l-fūr, alif berdiri, antara aku dan anu; dan buatlah untukku anu — demi F, tiada daya dan kekuatan melainkan pada Allah Yang Maha Tinggi Lagi Maha Agung." Dan ubatannya lada dan fal, dan idmárnya: "Jawab wahai Samasṭasṭā'īl, demi hak Saṭf, Saṭīṭāl, Kayṭam, Laṭam, Laṭash 22, hanka — jawab dan bukakan tabir antara aku danmu, yang segera, al-waḥā, saat itu." ﴿Huruf Ṣād﴾ — ia daripada huruf Ism teragung, dan sifatnya: jika engkau menulis pada sepotong sutera, lalu menulis padanya nama raja tinggi dengan idmár, dan meletakkannya di bawah batu cincin — pembawanya memperoleh kebaikan dan berkat serta terpelihara dari mudarat, dan ia bertasaruf seperti Jīm; inilah rupanya.

[imej tilasm]

Adapun khalwat dan penggunaan: masuk khalwat dan baca doa serta idmār selepas setiap solat 700 kali, sambil engkau berkata "Jawab wahai khadam huruf Ṣād, dan buatlah untukku anu" — maka ia datang. Sesiapa bertekun membaca doa, Allah memberinya kekuatan untuk ketaatan. Dan ia basmalah — "Kuminta wahai Yang meletakkan kehinaan di leher hamba-Nya, maka mereka takut akan kuasa-Nya; wahai Yang Esa dalam keagungan dan kekal mulia dan kebesaran — semua makhluk-Nya gentar dan tunduk di bawah perintah-Nya; wahai Yang wali-Nya pada Hari Ketakutan Terbesar aman — kuminta wahai Yang Mulia, demi kuasa yang dengannya Engkau pandang langit lalu naik, bumi lalu terhampar, gunung lalu rata, mata air lalu memancur, sungai lalu mengalir, hati lalu takut gentar, manusia bisu lalu berkata 'Engkau Allah Yang Esa lagi Maha Memaksa' — agar Engkau memakaikan aku cahaya yang kuterangi untuk membuka, dan menaklukkan kepadaku khadam huruf Ṣād, raja Samā'īl, dan nama besar samawi — tasarufkan dia dalam urusanku demi perintah-Mu; sembunyikanlah aku dengan khadam dan lidah yang meminum kekasih, bertambah cintanya dan pergi dengan segera dalam kehendakku; salūh la-khabarā salk, maka ia tidak mencelaku — dia tuan segala sesuatu, fa-'aw yāh 2 — dan daripada rahsianya kata-kata dan kuasa ini: Laṭm, wa-hayā — demi F, tiada daya melainkan Allah — jawab wahai Ṭa'īl, demi hak Ḥaṭī', Sa', Sa', 'Alṭaḥ, Sa' layj, yamūh yāh, huwa 2 — 'Salam, kata dari Tuhan Penyayang' — jawab dan buat anu." ﴿Huruf Qāf﴾ — jika engkau menulisnya dengan setiap nama bermula Q pada kain sutera dan meletakkannya di bawah batu cincin atau batu yāqūt atau akik Yaman, dan seseorang membawanya — ia memperoleh penerimaan. Di antara sifatnya menunduk musuh dan membisu lidah: tulis seratus kali pada kertas dengan nama yang dikehendaki, gantung di angin, baca doa dan idmár selepas setiap solat 100 kali, lukis rupa huruf di kepala dan mintanya — ia hadir, cahayanya seperti matahari dan musafir melihatnya di khalwatnya, duduknya menghadap kiblat; inilah rupanya. Doanya basmalah — "Kekuasaan-Mu wahai Allah yang mengalahkan musuh-Mu, kekuatan dan kehebatan-Mu tegak bagi wali-wali-Mu. Kuminta wahai Allah terimalah aku di pantai kedekatan-Mu dan mendekat kepada-Mu, wahai Allah. Wahai Qarīb, gembirakanlah keresahan hatiku hingga bertemu yang menerangi kegembiraannya, dan tetap dengan Qāf kekuasaan-Mu, berilah aku kekuatan-Mu. Wahai Qawī, kuatkanlah dengan kekuasaan dan kekuatan-Mu yang kuat hingga aku dekat kepada Yang bumi tidak mendekatinya mahupun ketinggian-Mu, wahai Maqṣūd — maka aku dekat kepada-Mu, Qāf pun bergoncang hingga tidak tetap padanya. Jawab wahai Iqāf, dan segerakanlah kepadaku."
