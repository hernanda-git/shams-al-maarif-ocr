— 247 —

If you draw [it] on pure parchment and carry it with you for the name of a person, he cannot part from you as long as the parchment is with you, and this is its description:

( Chapter on returning the runaway, even if he is in chains and shackles ) write this wafq whose exposition follows, then take a dung-beetle — if [the subject] is male, [take] a male one; if female, a female — and bind it in the middle of the circle with a fine thread, and drive the nail into the pole of the circle and tie the beetle to it. As the beetle turns seeking release, so the runaway turns and returns to the place where this talisman is; even if he is in chains, Allah causes his return by the blessing of this blessed talisman beneath the figure, which is... * And if you wish to put to sleep whom you will of the sick, or one in painful pain and the like, write these Names and place them under his turban or under his pillow, and he sleeps from that moment, not awakening until you remove the Names from beneath his head; and these Names benefit children who cry much. They are these: "And they remained in their cave three hundred years, and nine more..." (Q 18:25) — you would think them awake while they were asleep, and We turn them to the right and to the left. Do you perceive of them any or hear from them a sound?
