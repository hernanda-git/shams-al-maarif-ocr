English:
— 221 —

…the chest of money was preserved, and the money was not emptied from the chest as long as it was in it. * And of the properties of this verse: he recites His saying: "Alif-Lām-Mīm, Allah — there is no god but He, the Living, the Self-Subsistent" — this verse has the Greatest Name of Allah, and it has from the number 483; whoever writes it by the secret of interpenetration on the first [hour] of Friday on a noble substance and carries it sees of the wonders of Allah what descriptions fall short of — of rank, might, and awe in the eyes of people; and in it is a wondrous secret for entering upon rulers, commanders, great ones, and ministers, and seeking needs and accomplishing affairs. And this is its form: [the 4×4 "Alif-Lām-Mīm Allāh" magic square]. And from al-Ḥasan ibn 'Alī, may Allah be pleased with them both, that he said: "I guarantee for whoever recites this noble verse up to twenty verses that Allah protects him from every insolent tyrant, and a rebellious satan, and a wrongdoing sultan, and a treacherous thief, and a harmful beast" — the Throne Verse, and three verses from al-A'rāf: "Indeed your Lord is Allah who created the heavens and the earth in six days"; and ten verses from the beginning of al-Ṣāffāt to "firm"; and three verses from al-Raḥmān: "O company of jinn and men" to His saying "except by authority"; and the closing verses of Sūrat al-Ḥashr; and the end of Sūrat al-Takāthur. And of the properties of the Throne Verse: when one of you intends a matter, let him perform ablution in the darkness of night and pray two units, each unit with al-Fātiḥa and the Throne Verse three times; then when he gives salām he recites the verse seven times and says after it this supplication: "O Allah, You hear my speech and see my place and know my secret and my open; nothing of my affair is hidden from You. I call You with the call of the wretched, poor, one seeking help, confessing his sin and shortcoming; and I ask You the asking of the needy, and I beseech You with the beseeching of the weak, sinful, lowly servant — the beseeching of one whose neck submitted to You and whose tears flowed to You and whose cheek was humbled and whose nose was rubbed in the dust — that You give life to our hearts, open our breasts, make our efforts sincere for Your noble countenance, and the cause of success to bliss; and direct us to what is purepleasing to You, and conclude for us from You with good; and make us tomorrow with those You favored of the prophets, the truthful, the martyrs, and the righteous — and excellent are those as companions; and suffice us in what concerns us of the affairs of this world and the next; and let not enemies nor the envious rejoice over us; and make not our affliction in our religion; and make not the world our greatest concern nor the limit of our knowledge; and subject not over us by our sins one who does not fear You nor have mercy on us; and let us enjoy our hearing and sight, and give us a good life, and open for us the doors of good, and provide for us — and You are the best of providers. Our Lord, forgive us and our brothers who preceded us in faith, and place not in our hearts resentment toward those who believed — our Lord, indeed You are Kind, Merciful. Our Lord, forgive us our sins and our excess in our affair, and make firm our feet, and help us against the disbelieving people. Our Lord, give us good in this world and good in the next, and protect us from the punishment of the Fire, by Your mercy, O most Merciful of the merciful." And of the properties of this verse: if it is written on the shroud of the dead in three places — at his head, at his middle, and at his shins — that dead one is not punished in his grave, and the two angels are gentle with him at the questioning; for it is the greatest verse in the Qur'an, and it begins with His noble name.

Indonesia:
— 221 —

…peti uang terpelihara dan uang tidak habis dari peti selama ia di dalamnya. * Dan dari sifat ayat ini: ia membaca firman-Nya: "Alif-Lām-Mīm, Allah — tiada tuhan selain Dia, Yang Hidup, Yang Berdiri Sendiri" — ayat ini mempunyai nama Allah Yang Paling Agung, dan bilangannya 483; barangsiapa menulisnya dengan rahasia saling-memasukkan pada jam pertama Jumat pada benda mulia dan membawanya, melihat keajaiban Allah yang tiada terjangkau deskripsi — dari kedudukan, keagungan, dan kewibawaan di mata manusia; dan padanya rahasia menakjubkan untuk menemui penguasa, panglima, tokoh, dan menteri, serta memohon hajat dan menyelesaikan urusan. Dan ini bentuknya: [kuadrat 4×4 "Alif-Lām-Mīm Allāh"]. Dan dari al-Ḥasan bin 'Alī raḍiyallāhu 'anhumā bahwa ia berkata: "Aku jamin bagi yang membaca ayat mulia ini hingga dua puluh ayat, Allah memeliharanya dari setiap tiran yang membangkang, setan durhaka, sultan zalim, pencuri khianat, dan binatang buas yang membahayakan" — Āyat al-Kursī; tiga ayat dari al-A'rāf: "Sesungguhnya Tuhanmu ialah Allah yang menciptakan langit dan bumi dalam enam hari"; sepuluh ayat dari awal al-Ṣāffāt hingga "tetap"; tiga ayat dari al-Raḥmān: "Wahai golongan jin dan manusia" hingga firman-Nya "kecuali dengan kekuasaan"; penutup Sūrat al-Ḥashr; dan akhir Sūrat al-Takāthur. Dan dari sifat Āyat al-Kursī: apabila salah seorang dari kalian membulatkan suatu urusan, hendaklah ia berwudu di gelapnya malam dan shalat dua rakaat, tiap rakaat dengan al-Fātiḥah dan Āyat al-Kursī tiga kali; maka apabila salam, ia membaca ayat tujuh kali dan membaca setelahnya doa ini: "Ya Allah, Engkau mendengar ucapanku, melihat tempatku, mengetahui rahasia dan terbukaku; tiada sesuatu dari urusanku tersembunyi dari-Mu. Aku menyeru-Mu dengan seruan orang celaka, fakir, meminta tolong, yang mengakui dosa dan kekurangannya; dan aku memohon permohonan orang papa, serta memohon dengan permohonan hamba lemah, berdosa, hina — permohonan yang lehernya tunduk pada-Mu, air matanya mengalir pada-Mu, pipinya hina, dan hidungnya terinum di debu — agar Engkau hidupkan hati kami, lapangkan dada kami, jadikan usaha kami ikhlas demi wajah-Mu yang mulia dan sebab meraih kenikmatan; tunjukilah kami pada yang murni ridha-Mu, dan tutuplah kami dari-Mu dengan kebaikan; jadikanlah kami esok bersama yang Engkau anugerahkan dari nabi, shiddiq, syuhada, dan shalih — dan mereka sebaik-baik kawan; cukupilah kami dalam urusan dunia dan akhirat; jangan biarkan musuh dan orang dengki bergembira atas kami; jangan jadikan cobaan kami pada agama kami; jangan jadikan dunia kecemasan terbesar kami atau batas ilmu kami; dan jangan tundukkan atas kami dengan dosa kami siapa yang tiada takut pada-Mu dan tiada mengasihi kami; dan karuniakan kami kenikmatan pendengaran dan penglihatan, hidupkan kami dengan kehidupan baik, bukakan pintu kebaikan, dan rizkilah kami — dan Engkau sebaik-baik pemberi rizki. Ya Tuhan kami, ampunilah kami dan saudara kami yang mendahului kami dalam iman, dan janganlah Engkau jadikan dalam hati kami dengki pada yang beriman — ya Tuhan kami, sesungguhnya Engkau Maha Lembut lagi Maha Penyayang. Ya Tuhan kami, ampunilah dosa kami dan kelewatan kami, teguhkanlah tapak kami, dan tolonglah kami atas kaum kafir. Ya Tuhan kami, berikan kami kebaikan di dunia dan kebaikan di akhirat, serta peliharalah kami dari azab neraka dengan rahmat-Mu, wahai Maha Penyayang di antara para penyayang." Dan dari sifat ayat ini: apabila ditulis pada kain kafan mayit di tiga tempat — di kepalanya, di tengahnya, dan di betisnya — mayit itu tiada disiksa dalam kuburnya, dan dua malaikat lemah-lembut padanya saat pertanyaan; karena ia ayat teragung dalam al-Qur'an, dan ia dimulai dengan nama-Nya yang mulia.
