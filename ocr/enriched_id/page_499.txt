"And as for the standing mention by it, you say (In the name of Allah the Merciful, the Compassionate): 'O Allah, You are al-Barr al-Raḥīm, the Possessor of the blessings known by the generosity and the honor in the earth and the heavens — You favored with the beneficence and the grace upon all the existents, and brought forth the subtletics of Your blessing upon their essences by the spirit of life, according to the essence of everything, to the end by the non-existence and the death. I ask You by Your encompassing knowledge and the power of Your capability over the creatures by the rulings of the detail and the division, that You make perpetual over me Your blessing to the completion of the life, and favor upon me with the continuous successive bounties, and enable my joy by the looking upon You in the world and the Hereafter, O Most Merciful of the merciful.' (Section on His name the Exalted, al-Tawwāb) (In the name of Allah the Merciful, the Compassionate) Know that al-Tawwāb is He who eases the causes of the repentance for His servants again and again by what appears to them of His signs and drives to them of His awakenings, and makes them reach His intimidations and His warnings, until when they reach the perils of the sins they feel the fear and are fearful in it, so they return to Him, and He returns to them — and the favor of Allah is vast. He said exalted 'And He is who accepts the repentance from His servants and pardons the evils'; and the repentance is accepted as long as [the soul] does not gurgle [at death], and it is the desisting from the sins, great and small, and the resolve not to return to them, and the returning of the wrongs to their people. And this name is of the invocations of the people of sainthood; so among its special properties: if it is written with the standing mention and he who is persistent in the disobedience drinks it, he leaves them and Allah the Exalted turns to him; and whoever is characterized by this name and takes it as a mention and looks at the disobedience — Allah the Exalted turns to him. And this name has a magnificent retreat and a glorious servant; and this name is recited with the seeking of forgiveness and is aided by for the fulfillment of the needs; and its servant is from the servants of the bearers of the Throne, and under his hand seventy rows of angels who seek forgiveness for him. And whoever is straitened in his livelihood, let him increase the seeking of forgiveness; and whoever recites this name — Allah the Exalted opens for him the doors of the provision and attains what he wills. And if it is written in a square and the name and the standing mention are recited over it and he carries it, the doors of good are opened for him and the doors of the provision are eased for him. And we related by multiple routes that whoever is straitened in his livelihood, let him increase the seeking of forgiveness, for Allah the Exalted expands for him his provision. And this is its figure.

"And the signs, and He spread for them from Your presence the good deeds, and you see them the places of the intimidation coming — so there gather for them the causes of the nearings. I ask You, O Allah, O Determiner of the success by the wills, and Cause of causes by Your lordship, O Lord of lords, I ask You that You accept my repentance and make me with You of the specific of the beloveds, until there remains not between me and You a veil, and that You forgive my faults and my slips and multiply my wage and my good deeds, and make me in the enclosures of Your highest sanctity. "Adapun dzikir berdirinya, engkau ucapkan (Bismillāh ar-Raḥmān ar-Raḥīm): 'Ya Allah, Engkau al-Barr al-Raḥīm, Pemilik kurnia yang dikenali dengan kemurahan dan kemuliaan di bumi dan langit — Engkau kurniakan kebajikan dan anugerah atas segala makhluk, serta mengeluarkan halus berkat-Mu atas zat mereka dengan roh kehidupan, menurut zat tiap perkara, hingga ke akhir dengan tiada wujud dan mati. Aku memohon dengan ilmu-Mu yang meliputi dan kuasa kemampuan-Mu atas makhluk dengan hukum tafsil dan pembahagian, agar Engkau kekalkan atasku berkat-Mu hingga sempurna hidup, dan kurniakan atasku nikmat berturut-turut, serta mampukan kegembiraanku dengan memandang kepada-Mu di dunia dan akhirat, wahai Yang Paling Mengasihani.' (Fasal al-Tawwāb) (Bismillāh ar-Raḥmān ar-Raḥīm) Ketahuilah bahwa al-Tawwāb ialah Yang memudahkan sebab taubat bagi hamba-Nya berulang-kali dengan apa yang nampak pada mereka dari tanda-Nya dan penggera-Nya, serta sampaikan kepada mereka ancaman dan peringatan-Nya, hingga bila mereka sampai bahaya dosa mereka rasa takut dan gentar padanya, maka mereka kembali kepada-Nya, dan Ia kembali kepada mereka — dan kurnia Allah luas. Firman-Nya 'Dan Dia yang menerima taubat dari hamba-Nya serta mengampuni kejahatan'; dan taubat diterima selagi [nyawa] belum gargar [di hujung nyawa], dan ia meninggalkan dosa besar dan kecil serta berazam tidak kembali padanya, serta mengembalikan kezaliman pada ahlinya. Dan nama ini dari dzikir ahli wilayah; maka di antara sifatnya: bila ditulis dengan dzikir berdiri serta yang berterusan maksiat meminumnya, ia tinggalkan mereka dan Allah Yang Maha Tinggi menerima taubatnya; dan barangsiapa berakhlak dengan nama ini serta menjadikannya dzikir dan memandang maksiat — Allah Yang Maha Tinggi menerima taubatnya. Dan nama ini mempunyai khalwat agung dan khadam mulia; dan nama ini dibaca dengan istighfar serta dibantu baginya menunaikan hajat; dan khadamnya dari khadam pembawa 'Arasy, dan di bawah tangannya 70 baris malaikat yang memohon ampun untuknya. Dan barangsiapa sempit rezekinya, hendaklah ia perbanyak istighfar; dan barangsiapa membaca nama ini — Allah Yang Maha Tinggi bukakan padanya pintu rezeki serta capai apa yang dikehendaki. Dan bila ditulis dalam persegi serta nama dan dzikir berdiri dibaca atasnya dan ia memakainya, pintu kebaikan dibuka baginya dan pintu rezeki dimudahkan. Dan kami riwayatkan dengan pelbagai jalan bahwa barangsiapa sempit rezekinya, hendaklah memperbanyak istighfar, kerana Allah Yang Maha Tinggi meluaskan rezekinya. Dan ini gambarannya.

"Dan ayat, serta Ia sebarkan bagi mereka dari sisi-Mu amal soleh, dan engkau lihat mereka tempat ancaman datang — maka terkumpul bagi mereka sebab pendekatan. Ya Allah, ya Tawwāb.'
