English:
— 232 —

…and Allah confirms with His help whom He wills, and Allah is the Possessor of tremendous bounty. * A blessed benefit for protection from enemies and fear and terror from highway-robbers and others: some righteous ones, may Allah have mercy on him, said: we descended in one of our journeys upon a flowing river, and a people came to us saying that one of the rivers had deposited his goods in this place, so my companions departed from fear and terror, and I remained behind for a saying I heard from Ibn 'Umar, may Allah be pleased with him, from the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace: "Whoever recites thirty-three verses of the Book of Allah, exalted, that night, seven harmful ones and no thieving intruder shall harm him, and he is preserved in his person, family, and wealth until morning." So when evening came I did not sleep until I saw a group who came with swords drawn and their hands toward me, but they did not reach me. When morning came I went and an old man on a horse met me and said: "O this one, are you of man or of jinn?" I said: "Rather of man, of the children of Adam." He said to me: "How is it that we came to you this night more than seventy times, and a wall of iron was placed between us and you?" I said to him: "Ibn 'Umar, may Allah be pleased with him, told me from the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace: whoever recites thirty-three verses of the Book of Allah, exalted, in a night, seven harmful ones and no thieving intruder shall harm him, and he is in the security of Allah, exalted, until morning." When the old man heard that he dismounted from his horse and kissed my head, and gave Allah a covenant never to return to what he had been. And the blessed verses of rank: you recite four verses of Sūrat al-Baqara to His saying, exalted, 'the successful'; and the noble Throne Verse; and two verses after it to His saying 'abiding'; and three verses from the end of al-Baqara, 'To Allah belongs what is in the heavens and what is in the earth' to the end of the sūra; and three verses of al-A'rāf, His saying, exalted, 'Indeed your Lord is Allah, who created the heavens and the earth' to His saying 'the good-doers'; and ten verses from the beginning of al-Ṣāffāt to His saying, exalted, 'firm'; and the end of al-Isrā', 'Say: call upon Allah or call upon the Compassionate, whichever you call' to the end of the sūra; and two verses of al-Raḥmān, 'O company of jinn and men' to His saying 'you will be helped'; and the ends of al-Ḥashr, 'If We had sent down this Qur'an upon a mountain' to the end of the sūra; and two verses of al-Jinn, 'and that He, exalted, is the Lord of our Lord — He took not a consort nor a child' to His saying 'excess.' And know that these verses are called 'the guardian verses,' indicating that in them is healing from a hundred diseases like leprosy and vitiligo and others. And it is related from Muḥammad ibn 'Alī, may Allah be pleased with him, that he said: I recited them over a shaykh who had fallen ill, and Allah, exalted, removed that from him by His permission and the blessing of His ancient speech. So understand that. And whoever engraves this noble congruence on a silver seal or a plate of it in the first hour of Friday — which is from sunrise until the shadow becomes twenty-eight feet — whoever does that sees wonders in himself, and love, acceptance, and awe, and his provision is expanded, and this is its description in the following sheet: [292 — the great 9×9 guardian magic square, shown in the table below]. And know that these verses are called the guardian verses, and in them is healing from a hundred diseases like leprosy and vitiligo and others.

Indonesia:
— 232 —

…dan Allah meneguhkan dengan pertolongan-Nya siapa yang dikehendaki, dan Allah Pemilik anugerah agung. * Manfaat berkah untuk penjagaan dari musuh, rasa takut, dan teror dari perampok jalanan dan lainnya: sebagian orang shalih raḥimahullāh berkata: kami turun dalam suatu perjalanan di sungai yang mengalir, lalu datang suatu kaum berkata bahwa salah seorang dari sungai telah menurunkan barangnya di tempat ini, maka sahabatku pergi karena takut dan teror, dan aku tertinggal karena suatu hadis yang kudengar dari Ibn 'Umar raḍiyallāhu 'anhu dari Nabi shallallāhu 'alaihi wa sallam: "Barangsiapa membaca tiga puluh tiga ayat dari Kitabullah Ta'ala pada malam itu, tujuh yang membahayakan dan tiada pencuri yang masuk akan menyakitinya, dan ia terpelihara pada diri, keluarga, dan hartanya hingga pagi." Maka ketika petang aku tiada tidur hingga kulihat sekumpulan orang datang dengan pedang terhunus dan tangan mencapai aku, namun tiada sampai padaku. Ketika pagi aku pergi dan seorang tua di atas kuda menemuiku seraya berkata: "Wahai orang, kamu manusia atau jin?" Aku berkata: "Bahkan manusia, dari anak Adam." Ia berkata: "Mengapa kami datang padamu malam ini lebih dari tujuh puluh kali, dan dinding besi dijadikan antara kami dan kamu?" Aku berkata padanya: "Ibn 'Umar raḍiyallāhu 'anhu menceritakan kepadaku dari Nabi shallallāhu 'alaihi wa sallam: barangsiapa membaca tiga puluh tiga ayat dari Kitabullah Ta'ala dalam satu malam, tujuh yang membahayakan dan tiada pencuri yang masuk akan menyakitinya, dan ia dalamjaminan Allah Ta'ala hingga pagi." Ketika orang tua itu mendengar, ia turun dari kudanya dan mencium kepalaku, serta berikrar pada Allah tidak kembali pada yang dahulu selamanya. Dan ayat-ayat berkah lagi mulia: engkau baca empat ayat Sūrat al-Baqarah hingga firman-Nya Ta'ala 'orang yang beruntung'; dan Āyat al-Kursī yang mulia; dan dua ayat sesudahnya hingga firman-Nya 'kekal'; dan tiga ayat akhir al-Baqarah, 'Milik Allah apa yang di langit dan di bumi' hingga akhir surah; dan tiga ayat al-A'rāf, firman-Nya Ta'ala 'Sesungguhnya Tuhanmu ialah Allah yang menciptakan langit dan bumi' hingga firman-Nya 'orang yang berbuat baik'; dan sepuluh ayat awal al-Ṣāffāt hingga firman-Nya Ta'ala 'teguh'; dan akhir al-Isrā', 'Katakanlah: serulah Allah atau serulah Ar-Raḥmān, dengan nama apa pun kamu seru' hingga akhir surah; dan dua ayat al-Raḥmān, 'Wahai golongan jin dan manusia' hingga firman-Nya 'kamu diberi pertolongan'; dan akhir al-Ḥashr, 'Sekiranya Kami turunkan al-Qur'an ini pada gunung' hingga akhir surah; dan dua ayat al-Jinn, 'dan sesungguhnya Dia Yang Mahatinggi adalah Tuhan kami — tiada mengambil istri dan tiada anak' hingga firman-Nya 'sangat berlebih-lebihan'. Dan ketahuilah bahwa ayat-ayat ini dinamakan 'ayat penjagaan', menunjukkan padanya kesembuhan dari seratus penyakit seperti kusta dan belang serta lainnya. Dan diriwayatkan dari Muḥammad bin 'Alī raḍiyallāhu 'anhu bahwa ia berkata: aku membacanya pada seorang syaikh yang sakit, lalu Allah Ta'ala menghilangkan itu darinya dengan izin-Nya dan berkah firman-Nya yang dahulu. Maka pahamilah. Dan barangsiapa mengukir keselarasan mulia ini pada cincin perak atau lempengnya di jam pertama hari Jumat — yaitu dari terbit matahari hingga bayangan menjadi dua puluh delapan kaki — barangsiapa melakukannya melihat keajaiban pada dirinya, cinta, penerimaan, dan kewibawaan, serta rizkinya dilapangkan, dan ini sifatnya di lembaran berikut: [292 — kuadrat penjagaan besar 9×9, tampak di tabel di bawah]. Dan ketahuilah bahwa ayat-ayat ini dinamakan ayat penjagaan, dan padanya kesembuhan dari seratus penyakit seperti kusta dan belang serta lainnya.
