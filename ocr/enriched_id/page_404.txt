English:
And to Allah belong the Beautiful Names, so call upon Him by them.

In the name of Allah, the Merciful, the Compassionate.

( Chapter Thirty-Eighth, on the employment of the letters and their spiritual retreats, and what secrets they bear )
۞ Know — may Allah grant you success, and you alone — that the letter Alif is the first created thing created of the letters, and it is the One in number; for from it are the secrets of sayings, just as the letters are from the secrets of deeds; and the letters have no time that encloses them — rather they act by the special property for whomsoever Allah wills that. And this letter is of the quintessence of the ascent to the degrees of the watchers (al-wāṣilīn), the inheritors; and whoever realizes what is in its manifest and hidden worlds employs, by the grace of Allah, all that is in existence — and this resembles the bliss of Paradise. ۞ And know that the Alif is the cream of the world and the furthest goal; nay, it is the return of every world — the secret of origination. And the standing of the Alif is from the secrets of His Name al-Qayyūm; and it is the first of the Greatest Name of Allah, and the first of the Fātiḥah, and the firsts of the sūrahs; and it is a light-giving letter, standing by itself, a nation among nations, and it has many works without retreat and employment, and works with both, and special works. Among them, by the hand of nature: when you write the Alif once on a silk rag and hang it on the breast of the dullard, his intellect opens and he remembers all he heard. And when you write the letter Alif its original count — which is 111 — and bind with it your name and the name of whom you will, and carry it with you, Allah turns him toward you and eases for you the difficult matters. And when you write the Alif with the name of the seeker and the sought, and bind the two names with the letter on Sunday at the hour of the Sun, and carry it, he sees from it what he wills of affinity, love, and acceptance. And when you write the letter Alif on a gold ring while the Moon is in Pisces, and join it with the implicit concentration of the coming letters, and invoke it, and write the name of the master of the letter, it is acceptance for everyone who carries it among all the great ones. And this is its figure on the second face.

( Bab 38 — Penggunaan huruf dan khalwatnya, serta rahsia yang ada padanya )
۞ Ketahuilah — semoga Allah memberi taufiq kepadamu — bahawa huruf Alif adalah makhluk pertama dicipta dari huruf, dan ia Satu dalam bilangan; darinya rahsia kata-kata sebagaimana huruf dari rahsia perbuatan; huruf tiada masa yang membatasinya — ia berkesan dengan sifat khas bagi sesiapa Allah kehendaki. Huruf ini inti pendakian ke darjat para wasil pewaris; sesiapa haqqiki apa dalam alam zahir batinnya, menguasai dengan kurnia Allah segala di alam — menyerupai nikmat syurga. ۞ Alif adalah inti dunia dan matlamat tertinggi; bahkan kembali setiap alam — rahsia kejadian. Tegaknya Alif dari rahsia nama al-Qayyūm; ia permulaan Ism Allah teragung, permulaan al-Fātiḥah, permulaan surah; huruf bercahaya berdiri sendiri, satu umat, banyak amalan tanpa khalwat, dengan khalwat, dan amalan khusus. Antaranya: bila tulis Alif sekali pada kain sutera gantung di dada si bodoh, terbuka fikirnya ingat segala didengar. Tulis Alif bilangan asalnya 111 ikat nama sendiri dan nama dikehendaki, bawa bersama — Allah condongkan dia padamu mudahkan urusan sukar. Tulis Alif dengan nama pencari dan dicari, ikat dua nama dengan huruf hari Ahad jam Matahari, bawa — nampak apa dikehendaki dari mesra kasih penerimaan. Tulis Alif pada cincin emas bulan di Pisces, gabung dengan idmar huruf akan datang, doa, tulis nama pemilik huruf — penerimaan bagi semua yang membawa dari kalangan besar. Inilah rupanya pada muka kedua.
