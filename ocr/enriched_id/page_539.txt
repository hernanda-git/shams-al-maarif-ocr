English:
- ٥٣٣ -

"…he succeeds; and the first that issues from the glances of his lofty spiritual aspiration, and he witnessed the blessings of the incoming [states] at the beginning of his love that conveys to the eternal happiness — a manifestation of the natural letters before the existence of their cosmic-ness, and an understanding of the relation of their supportive structure without the witnessing of their own eye; and praise belongs to Allah, Lord of the worlds, a praise that matches His favors and recompenses His increasing generosity — glorified is He, I do not count praise of You, You as You praised Yourself. Indeed the poor servant, the weak one, for the emulation of a guiding virtuous shaykh and an erudite knower — who is rare in this abode — so glad is he who saw him, and who saw who saw him, for he attained a great attainment. And the shaykh the Imam Abū 'Abd Allāh al-Sulamī — may Allah sanctify his spirit — did well in his discourse, after he narrated from the Prophet (may Allah bless him and grant him peace) that he said: 'Blessed is he who saw me, and blessed is he who saw who saw me' — that is, blessed is he upon whom there impacted the blessings of my glance and my witnessing, and blessed is he upon whom impacted the glance and witnessing of my companions; and thus the state continues until it reached the sages of the nation and the awliyā' of Allah the Exalted in His earth — so everyone upon whom impacted the glance of a sage or the witnessing of a walī, that impact is only from the glance of the Prophet (may Allah bless him and grant him peace) upon his companions according to the difference of their states, so each impacted according to his state. And for this the impact ran in the shaykhs and the seekers and runs until the end of time — for the chain of states is as the chain of rulings, and that gentleness is subtler. * And know, O he who has reached my book, that I have made clear to you in its chapters what Allah the Exalted inspired me and returned to me His beneficence and His bestowal, and ran upon my tongue of the subtle Sunnic [matters] and the unveiling knowledges and the garden of sundus and the shining ruby and the flashing pearl and the luminous gem and the shining pearl and the luminous glimmer and the merciful leaf and the cast image and the Josephine form and the Lokmanian wisdom and the Solomonic proof and the Jonahian call and the Mosaic staff and the Adamic vestment and the Sethian pages and the Noahic ark and the Tablet lines and the Qadarī night and the magical breeze and the splendid jewels and the Sethian cream and the interceding olive and the non-strange and the Muḥammadan mantle and the Aḥmadī rose and the musk fragrance and the kingly breath and the meaningful allusions and the Throne lights and the Coptic marks and the Idrīsian lines and the Jesusian sciences and the opening understandings and the Indian numbers and the Greek observations and the geometric forms and the Qur'ānic secrets and the spiritual traces and the Self-Sufficient special properties and the Lordly names and the numeric indications and the letter expressions and the holy words and the high supplications and the spelled circles and the spousal subtleties and the single knowledges and the chrysolite mines and the pure talismans — in them the greatest aliment and the red sulfur and the radiant ruby and the green emerald and the preserved jewel and the hidden pearl and the supreme name and the luminous mention and the intense musk and the hoary ambergris; they make you understand the secrets of the beginnings and show you the landmarks of the ends — so blessed is he who circles at His threshold and stands at 'Arafāt of His knowledge. [Poetry:] 'Its meanings are beneath the letters as if they * shine as full moons with the lights of the realities.' So I alluded more subtly than they alluded, and made clear some of what they concealed; and were it not for the fear of publishing the secrets, I would have lifted the veils, in compliance with the saying of him (may Allah bless him and grant him peace): 'Divulging the secret of the Lordship is unbelief,' and the saying of 'Alī (may Allah honor his face): 'Speak to the people according to their intellects,' and the Exalted says: 'And there is not a thing but with Us are its stores, and We send it not down but by a known measure.' And had I willed, I would have expanded from the tongue of explicitness and unveiled the allusion. [Poetry:] 'Whoever they deposited upon a secret, so he was completed by it * they did not disclose to him the secrets as long as…' And who wants to ascend the lowly self to the highest of the Abode of Return — upon him is the perusal of my book this once after another.

Sesungguhnya hamba faqir yang lemah, untuk mencontohi shaykh pembimbing soleh dan 'alim 'arif — yang jarang di tempat ini — maka beruntunglah siapa melihatnya, dan siapa melihat yang melihatnya, kerana ia capai pencapaian agung.
