English:
— 470 —

"…Know that Allah the Exalted brought forth the existents as He willed, and brought forth for every existent a name from His names, and spread over it that name that it accept the Oneness of the natural disposition and the bringing-forth by it; then He spread the meaning of His name over the existents. And he who is characterized by this name witnesses the made-things of Allah, and everything the Book spoke of is truth, and he witnesses every movement and every action and every deed — it is from the truth. And upon you is much litany and spiritual practice and sincerity and recitation of the name its number — so the angel descends upon you, RUMYĀ'ĪL, and fulfills your need; and he who is characterized by it distinguishes between the truth and the falsehood. And of its special properties: it benefits for the fulfillment of needs — whoever has its square and directs himself to a need, it is answered; and if he matches his number and recites it with the standing mention, he witnesses wonders of the making of Allah the Exalted; and if it is written on silver and carried by the possessor of cold phlegm, it benefits him; and if it is written and placed in a place of a just ruler and the truth, and he who increases much sees very strange things — and this is its figure.

[The square of al-Ḥaqq — columns ā ل ḥ ق: row 1 = 29 / 3 / 99 / 99; row 2 = 32 / 32 / 6 / 98; row 3 = 7 / 97 / 4 / 30]

And as for his mention, you say (In the name of Allah the Merciful, the Compassionate): 'O Allah, You are the absolute Truth existing in the reality of Your Essence, described by the realities of the highest attributes in Your sanctities. I ask You by the secret of the lights of Your names, the highest, that You realize for me every truth in existence and nullify for me every falsehood perished and absent. I ask You by the secret of Your existence by which You realized the realities of Your attributes, that You raise my heart by the truth of the Truth to the witnessing of the realities of Your Essence, so I be with You and with the existence of every existent eternally, constantly, O Ḥaqq, O Mubīn.' (Section on His name the Exalted, al-Wakīl) Know that al-Wakīl is He to whom all affairs are entrusted — He manages them as He wills; and He is of two divisions: a division to whom some affairs are entrusted — so that is deficient; and a division to whom all affairs are entrusted — so that is complete, and He is Allah the Exalted. And the meaning of the agency is the suretyship. And whoever looks to the rectitude of his inward and realizes his intention, Allah sends down upon him the light of satiety and the tranquility of self-sufficiency; and that is five divisions. The first: entrusting with the preserving of the hearts by the faith, then He aided it by a spirit from Him, then He ordered it, then He sent down the tranquility for the increase of the faith — the deeds for the ordering, for the first is the faith of the natural disposition, and it is the self-sufficing by the reality with the realities of the faith arriving upon it in every breath of its time; so when he saw that, he knew that the trust was established for him, and that is not for the heart but by the continuance of the mention and the adherence to sincerity; then he follows it by the faith — i.e. the faith of the deeds which the knowledge fell upon from the deeds, for Allah made upon it a sign by which it is known, as He said exalted 'He has made beloved to you the faith and adorned it in your hearts and made hateful to you disbelief and defiance and disobedience' — so this is a sign by which the existence of the faith is known, and it is by the meaning of the first natural disposition which is the knowledge of the gnostic from where the specificity of the Truth; and what was specific to the hand of His care from the bearer of the trust which was magnified; and from it the saying of the Prophet (peace be upon him): 'The Holy Spirit breathed into my conscience that no soul reaches its term until it completes its provision.' And he who is characterized by this name perseveres in the reverential piety and the cutting-off to Allah the Exalted; and the scholars said in the chapter of trust many things, and it is of the invocations of the saints and the realized masters. And it has a retreat and administers in everything the majestic administers — for its number is as its number, and its recitation after every prayer its number, and there accrue to him many knowings. And as for the standing mention by it, you say (In the name of Allah the Merciful, the Compassionate): 'O Allah, You are al-Wakīl, the Preserver of what You brought into existence in the details of the Jabarūt and in the details of the Dominion and the treasuries of the Kingdom, administering the world of the Throne and the Footstool and the secrets of the high worlds. I ask You that You witness me the completeness of the trust and witness me that in my states from the world of the Throne and the Footstool and the secrets of the high worlds to the worlds of the Divinity; and that You realize my trust in You and my reliance upon You, that I be by my trust in You screened by Your complete screening, watched over by Your highest names and Your attributes that are by Allah. O Wakīl.' [page ends — concluded on 481]

Indonesia:
— 474 —

"…Ketahuilah bahwa Allah Yang Maha Tinggi mengeluarkan makhluk sekehendak-Nya, dan mengeluarkan bagi tiap makhluk satu nama dari nama-nama-Nya, serta meliputi padanya nama itu agar ia menerima tauhid fitrah dan pengadaan dengannya; lalu Dia meliputi makna nama-Nya atas makhluk. Dan yang berakhlak dengan nama ini menyaksikan buatan Allah, dan tiap yang dibicarakan Kitab adalah benar, dan ia menyaksikan tiap gerak dan tiap perbuatan dan tiap amal — ia dari kebenaran. Dan atasmu banyak wirid dan riadhah serta ikhlas dan bacaan nama bilangannya — maka malaikat turun atasmu, RUMYĀ'ĪL, dan menunaikan hajatmu; dan yang berakhlak dengannya membezakan antara benar dan batil. Dan di antara sifatnya: bermanfaat bagi menunaikan hajat — barangsiapa mempunyai perseginya dan menghadap suatu hajat, ia dijawab; dan jika ia padankan bilangannya dan membacanya dengan dzikir berdiri, ia menyaksikan keajaiban ciptaan Allah Yang Maha Tinggi; dan jika ditulis di perak dan dipakai si empedu sejuk, ia bermanfaat; dan jika ditulis dan diletak di tempat penguasa adil dan benar, dan yang memperbanyak melihat perkara amat ganjil — dan inilah gambarannya.

[Segi empat al-Ḥaqq — lajur ā ل ḥ ق: baris 1 = 29 / 3 / 99 / 99; baris 2 = 32 / 32 / 6 / 98; baris 3 = 7 / 97 / 4 / 30]

Adapun dzikirnya, engkau ucapkan (Bismillāh ar-Raḥmān ar-Raḥīm): 'Ya Allah, Engkau Kebenaran mutlak wujud dalam hakikat Zat-Mu, yang disifatkan dengan hakikat sifat tertinggi dalam kesucian-Mu. Aku memohon dengan rahsia cahaya nama-Mu yang tertinggi, agar Engkau realizasikan bagiku tiap kebenaran dalam wujud dan batalkan bagiku tiap kebatilan yang binasa lagi tiada. Aku memohon dengan rahsia wujud-Mu yang Engkau realizasikan hakikat sifat-Mu, agar Engkau angkat hatiku dengan kebenaran al-Ḥaqq kepada penyaksian hakikat Zat-Mu, agar aku bersama-Mu dan bersama wujud tiap makhluk kekal lagi sentiasa, ya Ḥaqq ya Mubīn.' (Fasal al-Wakīl) Ketahuilah bahwa al-Wakīl adalah Yang diamanatkan segala urusan — Dia uruskan sekehendak-Nya; dan Ia terbahagi dua: bahagian yang diamanatkan kepadanya sebahagian urusan — maka itu kurang; dan bahagian yang diamanatkan kepadanya segala urusan — maka itu sempurna, dan Ia Allah Yang Maha Tinggi. Dan makna perwakilan itu ialah penanggungan. Dan barangsiapa memandang kebaikan batinnya dan merealisasikan tujuannya, Allah turunkan atasnya cahaya kenyang dan ketenangan cukup — dan itu lima bahagian. Pertama: mengamanatkan dengan pemeliharaan hati dengan iman, lalu Dia tolong dengan roh dari-Nya, lalu mengaturnya, lalu menurunkan ketenangan bagi tambahan iman — amal bagi susunan, kerana yang pertama ialah iman fitrah, dan ia cukup dengan hakikat bersama hakikat iman yang datang padanya dalam tiap nafas waktunya; maka bila ia melihat itu, ia tahu bahawa amanah tegak baginya, dan itu tidak bagi hati melainkan dengan kekal dzikir dan berpegang ikhlas; lalu ia ikuti dengan iman — iaitu iman amal yang jatuh ma'rifat atasnya dari amal, kerana Allah jadikan padanya tanda yang dikenalinya, sebagaimana firman-Nya 'Dijadikan-Nya mencintai kamu akan iman dan menghiasinya di hatimu serta membenci kekufuran dan derhaka dan maksiat' — maka inilah tanda dikenali wujud iman, dan ia dengan makna fitrah pertama yang iaitu ma'rifat arif dari kekhususan al-Ḥaqq; dan apa yang dikhususkan tangan pemeliharaan-Nya dari pembawa amanah yang diagungkan; dan daripadanya sabda Nabi s.a.w.: 'Roh al-Qudus meniup ke dalam hatiku bahawa tiada jiwa sampai ajalnya hingga sempurna rezekinya.' Dan yang berakhlak dengan nama ini istiqamah dalam takwa dan memutuskan diri kepada Allah Yang Maha Tinggi; dan ulama berkata dalam bab amanah banyak perkara, dan ia daripada dzikir wali dan tokoh muhaqqiq. Dan ia mempunyai khalwat serta mengurus tiap yang diurus keagungan — kerana bilangannya seperti bilangannya, dan bacaannya selepas tiap solat bilangannya, dan diperolehnya banyak ma'rifat. Adapun dzikir berdirinya, engkau ucapkan (Bismillāh ar-Raḥmān ar-Raḥīm): 'Ya Allah, Engkau al-Wakīl Pemelihara apa yang Engkau adakan dalam tafsil Jabarut dan dalam tafsil Kerajaan dan perbendaharaan Kerajaan, mengurus alam Arasy dan Kursi serta rahsia alam tinggi. Aku memohon agar Engkau saksikan aku kesempurnaan amanah dan saksikan aku bahwa dalam keadaanku dari alam Arasy dan Kursi serta rahsia alam tinggi hingga alam Ketuhanan; dan realizasikan amanahku kepada-Mu dan tawakkalku kepada-Mu, agar aku dengan tawakkal kepada-Mu terlindung dengan penutupan sempurna-Mu, terjaga dengan nama mulia-Mu dan sifat-Mu yang oleh Allah. Ya Wakīl.' [halaman berakhir — disambung 481]
