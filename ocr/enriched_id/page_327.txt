For you is goodness, and love stood for you in the hearts of the creatures — even the creeping thing and the creeping thing. And as for the difference of rains and their withholding, and the difference of winds and their withholding — they have specific balances; if you know them, you have possessed the whole world, then the hereafter if you are of the masters of intellects. And be most careful — when Allah teaches you — not to stop it upon a creature, for it is sealed in it, unlike gold and silver and rubies and jewels. And this is the balance of repulsion — so if you know it, you remove it at whatever time; and its letters: A R B ' W N T S ' M H A T N — their number 16 letters, and their numbers 1279. And there is added to it the balance of the "detainer" (al-ḥābb) preceding. And as for the four winds — this is their balance: M A T Ā N ' Sh R H A Ḥ D Th M A N Ā H — their number 19, and there is added to it the balance of "compulsion" (al-jabb). ( And as for bringing the wild beasts ) — you add to it the balance of repulsion; and the balance of the creeping things: envision the balance, and take the number of its letters, work with it as preceded — thus: Kh M S H S T A Ḥ D A R B ' W N — their sum 16, and their numbers 1016; and bringing them as preceded. ( And as for the balance of the sea-beasts ) — it is: A R B ' H S T 10 Ḥ D Th L A Th W N Th M A N Ā H M A T Ā N — its number 42; and the first of the letter-numbers is understood. And the balance of the birds — you add to it what you will of good or evil, and it comes out from the numbers we mentioned its extraction; its example the extraction of ten from ten, and of nine from nine, and so on. And if you will to know the extraction of the fiery element — which is of 1125 — so if you will to work with it for any matter, you take its extractions, and add to the balance of whatever work; and thus all the numbers in all the works. And as for the four elements — each element of them has four degrees, and each degree of them has a balance. So as for the element of water — it is of five degrees; and these are its balances: * The first — the air of fire: (the first degree) a kindled fire — expand its letters 28 letters, and the numbers of the letters 3969. (The second degree) a fire that eats and drinks — its letters 66, and its numbers 535. * ( And as for the balances of air ) — it is kinds of air that blow from what benefits people on land and sea; its expansion is known, and its letters 138.
