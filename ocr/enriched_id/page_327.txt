English:
— 321 —

For you is goodness, and love stood for you in the hearts of the creatures — even the creeping thing and the creeping thing. And as for the difference of rains and their withholding, and the difference of winds and their withholding — they have specific balances; if you know them, you have possessed the whole world, then the hereafter if you are of the masters of intellects. And be most careful — when Allah teaches you — not to stop it upon a creature, for it is sealed in it, unlike gold and silver and rubies and jewels. And this is the balance of repulsion — so if you know it, you remove it at whatever time; and its letters: A R B ' W N T S ' M H A T N — their number 16 letters, and their numbers 1279. And there is added to it the balance of the "detainer" (al-ḥābb) preceding. And as for the four winds — this is their balance: M A T Ā N ' Sh R H A Ḥ D Th M A N Ā H — their number 19, and there is added to it the balance of "compulsion" (al-jabb). ( And as for bringing the wild beasts ) — you add to it the balance of repulsion; and the balance of the creeping things: envision the balance, and take the number of its letters, work with it as preceded — thus: Kh M S H S T A Ḥ D A R B ' W N — their sum 16, and their numbers 1016; and bringing them as preceded. ( And as for the balance of the sea-beasts ) — it is: A R B ' H S T 10 Ḥ D Th L A Th W N Th M A N Ā H M A T Ā N — its number 42; and the first of the letter-numbers is understood. And the balance of the birds — you add to it what you will of good or evil, and it comes out from the numbers we mentioned its extraction; its example the extraction of ten from ten, and of nine from nine, and so on. And if you will to know the extraction of the fiery element — which is of 1125 — so if you will to work with it for any matter, you take its extractions, and add to the balance of whatever work; and thus all the numbers in all the works. And as for the four elements — each element of them has four degrees, and each degree of them has a balance. So as for the element of water — it is of five degrees; and these are its balances: * The first — the air of fire: (the first degree) a kindled fire — expand its letters 28 letters, and the numbers of the letters 3969. (The second degree) a fire that eats and drinks — its letters 66, and its numbers 535. * ( And as for the balances of air ) — it is kinds of air that blow from what benefits people on land and sea; its expansion is known, and its letters 138. (The third degree) the air of passion and love — its expansion known, and its letters 66. (The fourth degree) it is the gathering of birds — its number-expansion known, and its letters 436. (The fifth degree) a cold corrupting air — its numbers known to whoever extracts the numbers. And I only left its numbers so the seeker may attain extracting its number and unlocking its symbols. ( The balances of water ) and they are five degrees: (The first degree) the sweet fresh water — its number is expanded, and the number of letters 94409. (The second degree) the bitter thick water — its number expanded, and its letters 73. (The third degree) the bitter-salt water — its number expanded, and its letters 41972. (The fourth degree) the tasteless drizzle-water — expanded in number, and its letters 94. (The fifth degree) the water heavy upon man — its expansion 93, and its numbers understood. And as for the balances of earth — they are these: (The first degree) the soil of grain and sowing — its number expanded 29, and its numbers 3124. (The second degree) the soil of metals — its expansion 45, numbers 314. (The third degree) the used soil — its expansion 118. (The fourth degree) the soil of wandering in which no plant sprouts — its expansion 19141. So these are important balances. So if you will to dispose in all existences entirely — of good or evil, or repulsion, or setting a beast upon, or restraining rains, or other — expand the letters of that type, and see what dominated it of the natures; so add to it the nature of that element. Then if the time in which you began was night or day — add to it the balance of that, then the balance of the hour from whatever balance of these and from the planets. So if these balances gathered with you — ( 21 — Shams al-Ma'ārif — 5 )

Indonesia:
— 321 —

Bagimu kebaikan, dan cinta tegak bagimu di hati makhluk — hingga hewan melata dan hewan melata. Dan adapun perbedaan hujan dan penahanannya, serta perbedaan angin dan penahanannya — mereka punya neraca khusus; apabila engkau mengetahuinya, engkau memilikimu dunia seluruhnya, lalu akhirat jika engkau dari ahli akal. Dan berhati-hatilah — apabila Allah mengajarkanmu — jangan hentikan pada makhluk, karena ia termeterai di dalamnya tidak seperti emas, perak, ruby, dan permata. Dan ini neraca tolak — maka apabila engkau mengetahuinya, engkau menghilangkannya kapan saja; dan hurufnya: A R B ' W N T S ' M H A T N — bilangannya 16 huruf, dan bilangannya 1279. Dan ditambahkan padanya neraca "penahan" (al-ḥābb) yang lalu. Dan adapun empat angin — ini neraca mereka: M A T Ā N ' Sh R H A Ḥ D Th M A N Ā H — bilangannya 19, dan ditambahkan neraca "paksa" (al-jabb). ( Dan adapun mendatangkan binatang liar ) — engkau tambahkan padanya neraca tolak; dan neraca hewan melata: bayangkan neraca, dan ambil bilangan hurufnya, amalkan sebagaimana lalu — demikian: Kh M S H S T A Ḥ D A R B ' W N — jumlah 16, dan bilangannya 1016; dan mendatangkannya sebagaimana lalu. ( Dan adapun neraca binatang laut ) — ia: A R B ' H S T 10 Ḥ D Th L A Th W N Th M A N Ā H M A T Ā N — bilangannya 42; dan bilangan huruf pertama dipahami. Dan neraca burung — engkau tambahkan padanya apa yang engkau kehendaki dari baik atau buruk, dan keluar dari bilangan yang kami sebutkan ekstraksinya; contohnya ekstraksi sepuluh dari sepuluh, dan sembilan dari sembilan, dan demikian. Dan apabila engkau hendak mengetahui ekstraksi unsur berapi — yang dari 1125 — maka apabila engkau hendak mengamalkannya untuk urusan apa pun, ambil ekstraksinya, dan tambahkan pada neraca amalan apa pun; dan demikian semua bilangan dalam semua amalan. Dan adapun empat unsur — setiap unsur punya empat derajat, dan setiap derajat punya neraca. Maka adapun unsur air — ia dari lima derajat; dan ini neraca mereka: * Yang pertama — udara api: (derajat pertama) api menyala — kembangkan hurufnya 28 huruf, dan bilangan huruf 3969. (Derajat kedua) api yang makan dan minum — hurufnya 66, dan bilangannya 535. * ( Dan adapun neraca udara ) — ia jenis udara yang bertiup dari apa yang bermanfaat bagi manusia di darat dan laut; pengembangannya diketahui, dan hurufnya 138. (Derajat ketiga) udara asmara dan cinta — pengembangannya diketahui, dan hurufnya 66. (Derajat keempat) ia pengumpulan burung — ekstraksi bilangan diketahui, dan hurufnya 436. (Derajat kelima) udara dingin perusak — bilangannya diketahui oleh yang mengekstrak bilangan. Dan aku hanya meninggalkan bilangannya agar pencari dapat mengekstrak bilangannya dan membuka simbolnya. ( Neraca air ) dan mereka lima derajat: (Derajat pertama) air tawar segar — bilangannya dikembangkan, dan bilangan huruf 94409. (Derajat kedua) air pahit kental — bilangannya dikembangkan, dan hurufnya 73. (Derajat ketiga) air asin pahit — bilangannya dikembangkan, dan hurufnya 41972. (Derajat keempat) air rintik yang tiada rasa — dikembangkan dalam bilangan, dan hurufnya 94. (Derajat kelima) air berat bagi manusia — pengembangannya 93, dan bilangannya dipahami. Dan adapun neraca tanah — mereka ini: (Derajat pertama) tanah gandum dan benih — pengembangan bilangannya 29, dan bilangannya 3124. (Derajat kedua) tanah logam — pengembangannya 45, bilangan 314. (Derajat ketiga) tanah terpakai — pengembangannya 118. (Derajat keempat) tanah pengembaraan yang tiada tumbuh tanaman — pengembangannya 19141. Maka ini neraca penting. Maka apabila engkau hendak mengatur seluruh eksistensi — dari baik atau buruk, atau tolak, atau menurunkan binatang, atau menahan hujan, atau lainnya — kembangkan huruf jenis itu, dan lihat apa yang menguasainya dari sifat alam; maka tambahkan padanya sifat alam unsur itu. Lalu apabila waktu engkau mulai malam atau siang — tambahkan padanya neraca itu, lalu neraca jam dari neraca mana pun dari ini dan dari planet. Maka apabila neraca ini berkumpul padamu — ( 21 — Shams al-Ma'ārif — 5 )"
