أو مصاب أو ضيف عافاه الله، وإذا علقت على أصحاب الرياح السوداء يبرأ [بها]، وإذا كتب في جام
زجاج بماء ورد وزعفران ومحاها وشربها صاحب الأسقام عوفي، وتكتب يوم السبت وتحمل للمحبة
والقبول وإبراء الأسقام والبركة وجلب الزبون، وحجاب للمصاب تكتب في رق غزال في ساعة سعيدة،
وكان عيسى عليه السلام يحيي بها الموتى، وهذه الدائرة خلوة عظيمة، وهو أن يدخلها ويكتب الدائرة
ويضعها في صدر المصلى، ثم ابتدئ بالذكر القائم بها حتى يغلب عليك الحال وأنت تتلو الدعوة، فإنه
يدخل عليك ٧ أشخاص يسلمون عليك وهم خدام الملوك العلوية، ويقولون لك: أيها الرجل الصالح
نحن ممتثلون أمرك في كل ما تريد، فتقسم على صاحب اليوم ووكله بالعمل، وهذه الأسماء التي تتلوها
عند الخلوة: بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم، اللهم إني أسألك بما سألك به جبريل عند عرشك العظيم أن تسخر لي
ملائكتك الكرام خدام هذه الأسماء، اللهم سخر لي كسفيائيل ودردائيل وشمخائيل

أيتها الملوك والرؤساء وأعينوني على قضاء حوائجي بحق ما تعلمون من عظيم سر الله، وبحق هذا الاسم
العظيم الأعظم الله الله الله بعلمك وقدرتك على الخلائق، وباسمك العظيم الكبير المتعال الله الله الله
الاسم الذي فضلته على سائر الأسماء، أسألك أن تسخر لي هذه الأرواح وأن يأتوني في نومي أو يقظتي،
إنك على كل شيء قدير يا الله ٣، وتذكر الاسم الجامع بعد اسم كل ملك ٣ مرات، وأما التقرب
إلى الله بهذا الاسم مفرداً ٦٦ مرة دبر كل صلاة من غير خلوة، وفي الخلوة العدد المذكور مضروباً

English:
— 425 —

"…no one looks upon him except he reveres and respects him; and whoever carries it in complete purity — Allah casts his love into the hearts of His creation. And if it is written on gazelle parchment with rose-water and saffron and a woman carries it while giving birth — the delivery is eased for her. And if one with epilepsy or affliction or guest unbinds it — Allah cures him. And if it is hung on the sufferers of black winds — they are healed [by it]. And if it is written in a glass bowl with rose-water and saffron and erased and drunk by the one with ailments — he is cured. And it is written on Saturday and carried for love and acceptance and healing of ailments and blessing and bringing customers; and a talisman for the afflicted, written on gazelle parchment in an auspicious hour. And Jesus (upon him peace) used to revive the dead by it. And this circle is a great retreat: that you enter it and write the circle and place it on the breast of the one praying, then begin with the remembrance standing by it until a state overcomes you while you recite the invocation — and there enter upon you 7 persons who greet you, and they are the servants of the upper kings, and they say to you: 'O righteous man, we are obedient to your command in all you will' — so you charge the master of the day and deputize him with the work. And these are the names you recite at the retreat: 'In the name of Allah, the Merciful, the Compassionate — O Allah, I ask You by what Gabriel asked You by at Your great Throne, that You subordinate to me Your noble angels, the servants of these names; O Allah, subordinate to me Kasfiyā'īl and Dardā'īl and Shamkhā'īl and Ṭūṭiyā'īl and Rūfiyā'īl and Sam'iyā'īl and Ṭaghāyā'īl and Jibrā'īl and Mīkā'īl and Samasā'īl and Ṣarfiyā'īl — answer, O kings and chiefs, and aid me in the fulfillment of my needs by the right of what you know of the great secret of Allah, and by the right of this Greatest Name — Allah Allah Allah — by Your knowledge and power over creatures, and by Your great, tremendous, exalted name — Allah Allah Allah — the name You preferred over all names; I ask You to subordinate to me these spirits and that they come to me in my sleep or waking; indeed You are over every thing powerful, O Allah' 3 — and mention the Gathering Name after the name of each king 3 times. And as for drawing near to Allah by this name singly — 66 times after every prayer without retreat; and in the retreat, the mentioned number multiplied by itself gives the sum 4356 — and when that is completed, he comes to you trembling, and his name is KAHYYĀL, and fulfills your need. And this is his seal. And the description of the retreat: you enter it and recite the name after every prayer 66 [days], and the duration of stay in the retreat 66, and it is called the Ṣamadiyya retreat, and its completion 70 days — and the angel descends upon you and rules over 66 rows of angels obedient to his command. And among its properties: it is written in a gold ring on Sunday with the servant's name around it, and you enter the retreat — the recitation after every prayer the number coming from its multiplication by itself; and the angel KAHYYĀL places the crown on his head and falls prostrate to Allah; and in the midst of his prostration '251 Ayl 2, O Allah' 3 — 'You know' — and Allah the Exalted says: 'Fulfill the need of My servant' — and the servant comes, and thereupon Allah unveils the sight of the reciter and he sees the lights coming out of his mouth at the recitation, and is able to dispose; and if he looks at a wrongdoer with his majesty — he perishes at once; then he dispatches the servant and says to him: 'Allah answered your supplication' — and he goes and what was sought remains present, and you attain the rank of the Abdāl. And if you will acceptance — write this triangle on a silver ring on Monday and incense it with good incense, then place it in your hand, and write around it the name: [magic-square table 3×4:] 9 22 19 / 20 15 21 / 14 17 24 / 22 12 18."

Indonesia:
— ٤٢٥ —

"…tiada yang pandang melainkan hormat takut, sesiapa bawa dengan suci — Allah letak kasih di hati makhluk. Tulis pada kulit gazel dengan air mawar za'faran, wanita bawa masa bersalin — mudah bersalin. Epilepsi/tertimpa/tetamu buka — Allah sembuh. Gantung pada penghidap angin hitam — sembuh [dengannya]. Tulis mangkuk kaca air mawar za'faran padam minum pesakit — sembuh. Tulis Sabtu bawa untuk kasih penerimaan sembuh berkat tarikan pelanggan; azimat tertimpa tulis kulit gazel saat baik. Isa menghidupkan mati dengannya. Bulatan ini khalwat agung: masuk tulis bulatan letak di dada yang solat, baca zikir berdiri hingga keadaan kuasai sambil baca doa — masuk 7 orang salam, khadam raja atas, kata 'Wahai soleh, kami patuh perintahmu'; maka suruh tuan hari wakilkan amal. Nama dibaca khalwat: 'Bismillah — ya Allah kuminta dengan apa Jibril minta di 'Arasy agung, taklukkan malaikat mulia khadam nama ini; ya Allah taklukkan Kasfiyā'īl Dardā'īl Shamkhā'īl Ṭūṭiyā'īl Rūfiyā'īl Sam'iyā'īl Ṭaghāyā'īl Jibrā'īl Mīkā'īl Samasā'īl Ṣarfiyā'īl — jawab wahai raja ketua tolong tunaikan hajatku demi rahsia agung Allah, demi Ism A'zam Allah Allah Allah dengan ilmu kuasa-Mu, nama agung besar tinggi Allah Allah Allah yang Engkau lebihkan — taklukkan roh ini datang dalam tidur jaga; Engkau Maha Kuasa ya Allah' 3 — sebut Nama Jamii selepas tiap raja 3 kali. Dekat pada Allah dengan nama ini sendiri 66 kali selepas solat tanpa khalwat; dalam khalwat bilangan darab diri jadi 4356 — bila sempurna dia datang gemetar nama KAHYYĀL tunaikan hajat. Inilah capnya. Sifat khalwat: masuk baca nama selepas solat 66 hari, tempoh 66, dipanggil khalwat Ṣamadiyya, lengkap 70 hari — malaikat turun urus 66 saf malaikat patuh. Sifat: tulis cincin emas Ahad nama khadam keliling, masuk khalwat — bacaan selepas solat bilangan darab diri; malaikat KAHYYĀL letak mahkota sujud; tengah sujud '251 Ayl 2, ya Allah' 3 — 'Engkau tahu' — Allah kata 'Tunaikan hamba-Ku' — khadam datang, Allah buka penglihatan pembaca nampak cahaya dari mulut, boleh tasaruf; pandang zalim dengan kemuliaan binasa; suruh khadam 'Allah jawab doamu' — pergi, yang diminta kekal, capai darjat Abdāl. Nak penerimaan — tulis segi tiga cincin perak Isnin ubat membakar, letak di tangan, tulis nama keliling: [jadual segi empat 3×4:] 9 22 19 / 20 15 21 / 14 17 24 / 22 12 18."
