"۞ And know that Allah has deposited His knowledges in His creation and disclosed them to the most special, and taught Adam all the names; then after the total outpouring He sent down upon him the letters, and he composed from them the names — so beneath every letter of the letters were nine thousand and eight hundred and twenty-nine sciences, and beneath every science twenty-eight sciences; so Allah disclosed them to Adam, then to the vicegerents after him who are the ones of resolve. Then He created the people of the inward who are the people of the sainthood from the singulars — so the lowest degree of the saint is that there is unveiled to him from the Throne to the carpet [the indication that] the Throne is the Deity, overseeing Paradise and the Fire and the Preserved Tablet in things, and he knows its quiddity. (Notice) And know that Allah made the vicegerents seven and placed them in the seven earths, so the drawing of all the lowers was from these, and they draw from the foldings, each pouring upon the other. And as for al-Ghawth, he is from the relation of the absolute Throne, so his outpouring is upper, and he is the one of the northern signation; therefore the drawing of the world was from him. And al-Ghawth supplies the four, and the four supply the seven, and the seven supply the forty, and the forty supply the seventy, and the seventy supply the three hundred and sixty. (Notice) Know that the four are your natures, and the seven your heart, and the forty is your hardest phase, and the seventy your age, and the three hundred and sixty your limbs. * And know that the phases of the creature [extend] from the first creation to its last, the composition; and it is the barzakhi origination which the perfect human, its Lord, will unveil — its fifth the proportioning, its sixth the blowing, its seventh the address. So from it, every rank of these ranks [has] six lights of the outpouring of the Real the Exalted, poured from the address with the lights of the speech in it — so he understood the address of the high judgment, and poured upon the blowing the lights of life, and that is the first address, so it was the first rank of life, and the total outpouring was from His name; and He poured upon His servant the outpouring of the will — therefore He particularized man with the kind of the charges, and unveiled the counted knowledges, and understood the difference of the phases of the two worlds, and the passing of the separation and the gathering upon what is in the two abodes, and the passing of the muster in the two barriers. And when He poured upon the human creature — expressed by the heart — so it was the place of unveiling and the passing of the acceptance, the witnessing of the knower by the lights of the first speech in it, by it he reflected, and by it he took lesson, and by it he drew near, and by it he was 'or nearer than that,' and by it the falling of the intermediaries from…"
