أو ما يريد ويمده بالمواهب * واعلم أن هذا الاسم يحكم على الدور الأول ويحكم على عوالم زحل ومن

English:
— 462 —

"…if he recites it — the traveler — in his retreat with discipline, he sees the psychic attachments being a passing thought behind his appearance; and its recitation its number, and it is 1641 times — forty times — so there descends to him the angel, and his name is QAṬYĀ'ĪL, and he says: 'Your servant called me and requests the fulfillment of his need,' and he brings it down to him in sleep or wakefulness according to his preparedness, and unveils to him about himself [whether] of death or life or what he wills, and supplies him with the gifts. * And know that this name rules over the first cycles and rules over the worlds of Saturn; and of its special properties is the reciting of it for everything you will of good or evil or benefit or harm or attraction or repulsion — so if one recites it, of whom the affairs were straitened, Allah relieves him. And it befell al-Sāmi' (the hearer), the father of al-'Azīz Muḥammad ibn al-Mundhir — that when his father died and he sought the path and the entering into the names, he gave me the lordly unveiling, and I saw written upon his forehead that he was to be stripped; so when he came to me and I looked upon his face in this state, my soul shrank from casting at him the mention and the names. So I sought the omen of Allah that I cast to him the name, and commanded him with its recitation every day and night 9000 times; so when he completed them, he saw in sleep that the ruler came to him and they killed him and he died, and they washed him and buried him; so he awoke from his sleep terrified and fearful, and came to me, and I looked upon his face — and what I had found was gone, and his face was most luminous; and he mentioned to me his vision, and I praised Allah and taught him the mention and the names until he became of the masters of the sainthoods. {And of its special properties} is the easing of the provision and the attainment of the goals, even if the human were careworn or a seeker of a need and recites it — fulfilled. * And whoever writes his particular figure and adds to it the name of the Essence upon gold or silver at an auspicious time and carries it — Allah opens to him and he is tenderly cared for in all his states; and if you will to rebuke a spirit, he attends immediately — and this is its figure. And its supplication with the basmala: 'O Allah, I ask You, O Laṭīf, by Your servants, O Hā, O Compassionate, O Trustworthy, O Laṭīf, O Possessor of Majesty and Honor, O Laṭīf, O Rabbāh, by whom glory be to You — there is no god but You and no god other than You and no worshipped save You; O Laṭīf, O Allah, You are the truth, the real, O Laṭīf, O Trustworthy, who took not a consort nor a child and has none equal to Him; O Laṭīf, O Originator of the heavens and the earth, O Laṭīf, O Responder, O Hā — answer; may Allah bless you, and do for me such-and-such as I will, and manifest to me in my retreat, O Shaykh, the high one, from every distance; O Laṭīf, the gentleness — You are the present, not absent; O Laṭīf, O Rabbāh — You, none judges over You, a judge; O Laṭīf, O Hā — You are the Sultan, the Strong, none strong overcame You; O Laṭīf, O Trustworthy, who is every day in a matter. Subordinate to me the servant of this name, that he do for me such-and-such by a thousand — there is no power and no strength but in Allah the High, the Great; there is no god but You, the King, the Holy, KHY'Ṣ, ḤM'SQ. I ask You by Your Greatest Name by which He particulized the most special of Your creatures, that You fulfill my need, O Lord of the worlds.' And this name disposes in the reeds of the paper — gold and silver and water — and his name is RŪMĀN, the king; recite the name 7000 times with the condition of the discipline and the recitation of the supplication 21 on the night of Friday after the 'ishā', you pray two units with Sūrat al-Kahf and Yāsīn; then after that you recite the name and say: 'Answer, O King RŪMĀN,' and he attends and gives you a black stone and gives you something of the lower world; then he informs you of what you will. You fumigate it with 'ūd and lubān gum, and the mention, and you say: 'Aid by the right of what you came with of the obedience' — and it goes; and whenever you will his presence, fumigate and bring the stone near the fire. And as for the mention by it — the basmala: 'O Allah, You are al-Laṭīf, the hidden from the gaze of the eyes, the exalted above the comprehension of the intellects and the thoughts, the Knower of the encompassing of the existents, the manifested by the secrets of the hearts…' [square of al-Laṭīf — columns ل ط ى ف; rows: 38 / 32 / 79 / 19; 32 / 41 / 8 / 78; 9 / 77 / 34 / 40]"

Indonesia:
— ٤٦٢ —

"…kalau dia baca dalam khalwat riyadhah nampak ikatan jiwa fikiran di belakang rupa; bilangan 1641 — 40 kali — turun malaikat QAṬYĀ'ĪL kata 'Hamba-Mu panggil aku minta tunaikan hajat,' turun dalam tidur jaga ikut kesediaan buka mati hidup kurnia. * Nama ini kuasa siklus pertama dunia Zuhal; sifat: baca untuk baik jahat untung rugi tarik tolak — kalau terhimpit Allah lepaskan. Terjadi pada al-Sāmi' bapa al-'Azīz Muhammad ibn al-Mundhir — bila bapanya mati cari tariqat masuk nama, beri kasyaf aku nampak di dahi 'akan dirampas'; bila datang aku lihat muka malu nak beri zikir nama. Istikharah Allah suruh baca tiap hari malam 9000; bila tamat nampak tidur raja datang bunuh mati mandi kebumi, jaga takut takut datang — apa kujumpai hilang mukanya bercahaya; cerita vision aku puji Allah ajar zikir nama jadi ahli wali. {Sifat} mudah rezeki capai matlamat walau gundah minta baca — tertunaikan. * Tulis rupa khusus tambah nama Zat emas perak waktu baik bawa — Allah buka dijaga; kalau tegur roh hadir — inilah rupanya. Doa basmalah: 'Ya Allah kuminta ya Laṭīf dengan hamba-Mu ya Hā ya Penyayang ya Amanah ya Laṭīf ya Pemilik Mulia ya Laṭīf ya Rabbāh Maha Suci tiada tuhan Engkau tiada sembahan ya Laṭīf ya Allah Engkau benar ya Laṭīf ya Amanah tiada isteri anak sekutu; ya Laṭīf ya Pencipta langit bumi ya Laṭīf ya Pemakbul ya Hā jawab; berkatilah beri demikian zahir khalwat ya Shaykh tinggi jauh; ya Laṭīf lemah Engkau hadir tiada ghaib; ya Laṭīf ya Rabbāh tiada hakim menghukum; ya Laṭīf ya Hā Sultan Kuat tiada terkalah; ya Laṭīf ya Amanah setiap hari urusan takluk khadam nama ini 1000 — tiada daya melainkan Allah Tinggi Agung tiada tuhan Engkau Raja Suci KHY'Ṣ ḤM'SQ. Kuminta Ism A'zam khusus ciptaan tunaikan hajat ya Tuhan alam.' Nama urus bulu pena emas perak air raja RŪMĀN; baca 7000 dengan riyadhah doa 21 malam Jumaat selepas 'isyak 2 rakaat Kahf Yāsīn; baca kata 'Jawab ya RŪMĀN' hadir beri batu hitam dunia; lugukan 'ūd luban, 'Bantu dengan ketaatan' — pergi; bila mahu hadir luguk dekatkan batu api.
