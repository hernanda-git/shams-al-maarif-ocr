[Conclusion of the Lām-Alif invocation — fully voweled text] "…Ṭal'a ba'īlan wa-samīlan Ṭalāfan — so do not hasten with such-and-such by the Alif, there is no power nor strength save in Allah, the High, the Great. And the employment: you enter the retreat and seek him — he attends; and the duration of the retreat is 28 days. (Section) And know that all the actions of these letters have no time that prevents them and no beauty that veils them; so if you will to enter a retreat, take the number of the letters and subtract 3030 — what remains is the days of the retreat. And if you will a need among needs, look to the need and do what was mentioned by way of example: if you will the expulsion of a person, ask it of the letter Ṭā'; and if you will love, of the Mīm. And upon you is piety and leaving vile foods, and wear each day garments of the colors of the planets. And if you will the retreat, aim for a pure house and begin with recitation of the invocation until the light appears to you, and be abundant in recitation in the times of night; and command the qiṭṭah (the spiritual cat-form) to attend you — and when you complete the days of the retreat, recite it — it comes to you like the world and grows until it remains like the moon, and is formed and addresses you and fulfills your need. * And this invocation: the basmala — 'What Allah willed — there is no power save in Allah, the High, the Great. By Allah, and by Allah we return. Allah is my Lord, Allah — I associate nothing with Him. Allah is the ally of the believers — He brings them out of the darknesses into the light. Allah suffices me — there is no god but He; upon Him I rely, and He is Lord of the mighty Throne. In the name of Allah — O Banā, blessed be the walls — Yā-Sīn, our ceiling — we sought help in Allah for the passing of the supply of the qiṭṭah; He, yā Huwa, ease it for me, my place, O qiṭṭah of existence, and I ask You, O Allah, O Eternal of bounty, O Soother of ailments, O Eternal of the eternal, O He who coils the night over the day and coils the day over the night — I ask You to subordinate to me this qiṭṭah and spread it for me. Hayā, O Gatherer of the root of existence — Hā 7, yā Miyāh 7, hayā Hayyūt 7, hayā 7, Ahiyyah 7, Ahāb 7, Lāhāhī 7, Hāhā 7, yā Hahā 7 — answer me, O embodied form, by the might of Badūḥ 7, Ḥūdūb 7, Nūḥ 7, Ḥū Laḥū Yad 7, Bariḥ 7, Waḥyūh 7, Wadaḥb 7, bi-Jūd Bāh 7 — Ajhazaṭ al-waḥā by what is in the Tablet of the names, and by the right of the four lines and what is in them, and by the dotted letters — answer, O spiritual spirits, by the right of the basmala and what is in it, and by the dotted letters — the great Berahah, Owner of the dominion, Possessor of majesty and honor — Qafq Saqfāṭīs, Fa-saqīnā bi-'izzat Ṣāliyā — glory be to your Lord, Lord of might — the Kh…' (Section) On the manner of employing these letters in a way other than the first. * Know, O brother, that every speech and every knowledge is from the letters; so if you will to employ these letters for love and acceptance and obedience and binding the tongue and attraction and squaring and nullifying magic and stoning and opening treasures and nullifying their obstacles, and all the states and matters that occur to your mind — aim for a pure place and make in it 3 protecting circles from the obstinate spirits and the 'ummār (the indwellers), then after that write the names: 'Reveal to me according to my capacity — may Allah bless you' — and you see the light every night growing, and hear the glorification of the spiritual spirits; and in 21 days there come to you 4 companies who greet you, and in the hand of each a Qur'an — and you say 'And peace be upon you and the mercy of Allah; I want from you, O masters, obedience to Allah, and I do not ask Him' — and after that there appear to you 4 up to 28 angels; and at that you read the names of the covenant and say 'I want from you service and the fulfillment of my interests in accordance with Book and Sunna' — and they say to you 'Hearing and obedience, as long as you are upon obedience.' And if you will to nullify the obstacles of a treasure — kill the oath and the command of the master Sharīṭāyīl and Ṭūṭāyīl, and they attend; and if you will to open the door — write the aḍrār (harms) on 4 eggs and speak with the great names and strike the place with one after one. And if you enter the retreat for the sake of aid…"
