نطفة في قرار مكين ثم خلقنا النطفة علقة الآية فهذه سبعة أطوار مشكلات فأنت في ست أطوار غير
مشكلات وذلك أن الله يأمر ملائكة العرش الموكلين بمعرفة النطف المخلقة وغير المخلقة فيأخذون النطفة في
مقابلة من يريد الله إبرازه ولا يزالون يتقدمون حتى تقع النطفة في الرحم فتلقاها أيدي الملائكة
ويضعونها في الرحم مهلاً يطوفون في الرحم ويسمون الله عليها فلا يقربها شيطان ويدومون على
ذلك أربعين يوماً ولذلك أمرنا النبي عليه السلام إذا أتينا أهلنا أن نكون على وضوء وطهارة
وصلاة وركوع ونسمي الله ونقول اللهم جنبنا الشيطان وجنب الشيطان ما رزقتنا اللهم ارزقنا
ولداً صالحاً والحكمة في اختصاص ملائكة العرش بذلك لأن العرش عليه اسم الرحمن لأن الرحم
مشتق من الرحيم ولذلك قال عليه السلام يقول الله تعالى هي الرحم وأنا الرحمن اشتققت لها اسماً من
أسمائي فمن وصلها وصلني ومن قطعها قطعني ولا يزال ملائكة النطف طائفين بالنطفة أربعين

يكون سريع النزول وقالت الأطباء أن المولود لسبعة يعيش وإذا ولد لثمان لا يعيش ووقع بحث بين
الحكماء والمنجمين فقال الحكماء أن الولد عند كمال السبعة أشهر يتحرك للخروج فإن خرج عاش
وإن لم يتهيأ يشرع في البطن عقب الحركة ولا يتحرك في الشهر الثامن وهذا ثقل حركته وهذا قبل
البحران لأن الطبيعة في أيام البحران تشتغل بدفع البحران للمولود في المدة يوماً وليلة ويسكن للتشريح

إلى السابع فإذا انتهى إلى الثامن استراح وهو ذو زحل لأنه بارد يابس طبعه الموت ولا يعيش الولد
والأول أصح * واعلم أن المولود إذا بلغ الأربعين الأولى فإن ملائكة العلم يتسلمونه ويتدبرون
أمره فإذا أراد الله به أمراً مثل موته أو سقطه أنساه أمره وإذا أراد الله تمام خلقه فإنه يتناوله

English:
— 442 —

"…and the darkness of His mercy — so He made the phases of your bodily origination upon a sphere; for the first He said exalted 'from a despised water,' then 'We made him a drop in a firm resting-place,' then 'We created the drop a clot…' — and these are seven configured phases, and you are in six unconfigured phases. And that is that Allah commands the angels of the Throne charged with the knowledge of the fertilized and unfertilized drops, and they take the drop in response to whom Allah wills to bring forth, and they keep advancing until the drop falls in the womb, and the hands of the angels receive it and place it in the womb gently, circling in the womb and naming Allah over it, so no satan approaches it, and they persist in that for forty days. Therefore the Prophet (upon him peace) commanded us that when we go to our families we be in ablution and purity and prayer and bowing, and name Allah and say: 'O Allah, keep the satan away from us and keep the satan away from what You have provided us; O Allah, provide us a righteous child.' And the wisdom in the particularization of the Throne-angels for that is that upon the Throne is the name al-Raḥmān, for the womb is derived from al-Raḥīm; therefore he (upon him peace) said: 'Allah the Exalted says: She is the womb, and I am al-Raḥmān; I derived for her a name from My names — so whoever connects her connects Me, and whoever cuts her cuts Me.' And the drop-angels keep circling the drop for forty — and that is the extent of its hardest, the extent in their other knowledge. (Notice) Know that when the fetus reaches four months and moves, it is quick to descend; and the physicians said the child born at seven lives, and if born at eight does not live. And a debate occurred between the sages and the astrologers: the sages said the child at the completion of seven months moves to exit, so if he exits he lives; and if he is not ready he begins in the belly after the movement and does not move in the eighth month — and this is the heaviness of his movement, and this is before the crisis, for nature in the days of the crisis is busy repelling the crisis for the newborn in the period a day and a night, and is still for the dissection. And the very moving in the eighth stands in its place like the two heats, so the child weakens to the utmost and does not live. And the astrologers said the child when he is in the womb is reared by the rearing of the first sphere Saturn and the second Jupiter up to the seventh; so when he reaches the eighth he rests, and it is of Saturn, for it is cold dry — its nature is death, and the child does not live; and the first is sounder. * And know that when the newborn reaches the first forty, the angels of knowledge receive him and govern his affair; so if Allah wills for him an affair like his death or his fall, He makes him forget his affair; and if Allah wills the completion of his creation, He takes him with the bones of the people of the heavens by a divine wisdom, and it is not considered by the saying, and the completion of the origination by the nūn of the jewel is vaster. And when his origination is completed, the angels of unity receive him, and likewise the angels of the trust — if he is of the people of the right, Allah gathered for him between the trust and the wisdom and the lights of relation; so then appears in his birth the light of Allah filling what is between the heaven and the earth, and the angels raise their voices with the declaration of oneness and magnification. And this is particular to the prophets, the truthful, the martyrs, and the righteous. And if Allah blinds the light of his innate disposition and the lights of His wisdom, He fills the heavens and the earth with darkness, so the satans and the spirits of the profligate feed, and the fire is kindled for a preceding disobedience — not for an appeared opposition, but for the appearance of the compelling wisdom and the completer of will. And the centers of the lowers are 4 — fire, air, earth, water; the center of heat is the sphere of the sun, the center of cold the sphere of the moon, the center of moisture the sphere of Jupiter, the center of dryness the sphere of Saturn; and its parts of natures intermingled in relation to each sphere of the seven spheres — and these are the natural elements which are the center of the lowers. (Notice) Know that the realities of the letters are the names, and the names are the trust; so you are the bearer of the trust, and it is the names, and their condition is that you complete them with righteous deeds — and they are the prayer by day and night, and its key is the ablution and its establishment by…"

Indonesia:

"…dan gelap rahmat-Nya — Dia jadikan fasa kejadian jasadmu atas sfera; pertama firman 'dari air hina,' 'Kami jadikan setitik di tempat tetap,' 'Kami cipta setitik darah beku…' — 7 fasa tersusun, engkau dalam 6 fasa tak tersusun. Allah suruh malaikat 'Arasy urus pengetahuan nutfah subur tak subur, ambil nutfah bila Allah mahu zahirkan, maju hingga jatuh rahim, tangan malaikat terima letak lembut berputar di rahim sebut Allah, syaitan tak hampir, istiqamah 40 hari. Maka Nabi suruh bila ke keluarga berwuduk suci solat rukuk sebut nama 'Ya Allah jauhkan syaitan dari kami dan dari rezeki, beri anak soleh.' Hikmah khusus malaikat 'Arasy: atas 'Arasy nama al-Raḥmān, rahim dari al-Raḥīm; sabda 'Dialah rahim, Aku al-Raḥmān, Aku ambil nama dari nama-Ku — penyambung sambung Aku, pemotong potong Aku.' Malaikat nutfah berputar 40 — itu had paling susah. (Tunjuk) Janin 4 bulan gerak cepat turun; tabib kata lahir 7 hidup, 8 tak hidup. Debat ahli hikmah nujum: hikmah kata 7 bulan sempurna gerak keluar, keluar hidup; tak sedia mula perut selepas gerak tak gerak bulan 8 — berat gerak sebelum krisis, tabiat sibuk tolak krisis bayi sehari semalam diam bedah. Gerak bulan 8 ganti 2 haba, bayi lemah tak hidup. Nujum kata janin dibesarkan sfera 1 Zuhal 2 Musytari hingga 7; sampai 8 rehat Zuhal sejuk kering mati tak hidup; pertama lebih betul. * Bayi sampai 40 pertama malaikat ilmu terima urus; Allah mahu mati gugur dilupakan; mahu sempurna ciptaan ambil tulang penghuni langit hikmah ilahi, lengkap dengan nūn permata lebih luas. Selesai kejadian malaikat tauhid terima, juga malaikat amanah — ahli kanan dikumpul amanah hikmah cahaya nisbah, zahir cahaya Allah penuh langit bumi, malaikat angkat suara takbir. Khas nabi siddiq syuhada soleh. Kalau Allah butakan cahaya fitrah hikmah, penuh gelap syaitan roh fasik api menyala untuk maksiat lalu bukan pembangkang zahir tapi hikmah paksa penyempurna kehendak. Pusat bawah 4 — api udara tanah air; panas matahari, sejuk bulan, lembap Musytari, kering Zuhal; bahagian sifat bercampur tiap sfera 7 — ini unsur tabii pusat bawah. (Tunjuk) Hakikat huruf nama, nama amanah; engkau pembawa amanah nama, syarat sempurnakan amal soleh — solat siang malam kunci wuduk tegak dengan…"
