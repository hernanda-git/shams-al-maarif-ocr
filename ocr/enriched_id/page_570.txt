"…[Sagittarius:] Masculine, diurnal, embodied, eastern, descending, straight, wide, having a voice, barren, shining, fiery, hot, dry, yellow-bilious, fortunate, praiseworthy, mixed, falling — it does not look to the ascendant; sixth of the places in strength and bound and confusion. Its planet Jupiter — in its third degree is the exaltation of the tail; and in it the depression of the head and the woe of Mercury; and in the nineteenth degree is the mixing of the two seasons autumn and winter. One looks to it in its faces: Mercury, the Moon, and Saturn. Its ascendant: in the first face, in the form of a yellow body and another white and another red — accords for works of help and strength; in the second face, in the form of a man driving cattle and a wolf — accords for works of fear and hair and constriction; in the third face, in the form of a man upon his head a golden cap, killing another — accords for the terror. It has, of letters: the ṭā' disregarded and the shīn with dot; and of the relation of man, from the orifices the two hands; from the inner organs the liver and the gall-bladder; from the outer the two thighs and the vertebrae of the back; and it has the chest and the privates (sharing with Scorpio), and likewise the head and what is in it, and the gravitation (sharing with Aries); and it has the yellow-bile illnesses; and of tastes, every bitter and what is compounded of bitterness; and of colors, every tending to red and yellow; and of metals, the hoped-for jewels; and of places, the wastelands and the places of thieves and the places of fire and what is made with it (sharing with Aries); and of animals, the insects (sharing with Scorpio), the four-legged cloven-hooved (sharing with Aries); and of plants, what is eaten of taste like the almond. It is specific to the affairs of travels and removal and change and transfer and messengers and correspondences and the unseen and the attachments of these causes; and it indicates knowledge and religion and piety and asceticism and worship and the knowing of Allah the Exalted, and the righteous dreams and the wisdom and the unseen and the affairs of the afterlife, and the man who has turned away from his knowledge or changed from his way and was raised by it and his state, and the sultans and kings and princes and the great, and the mountains and the difficult places, and glory and standing and the elevation of place, and the fire and everything that enters it of jewels and other than them, and what may be of the future of affairs, and the renewing of things, and the affair which is not of invocation — upon every affair that has a second return, and the necessity of strength and illumination — by its being a masculine, embodied, diurnal sign.

( The Tenth Sign — Capricorn [al-Jady] )
Feminine, nocturnal, cardinal, northern, ascending [?] — long, in half a voice, toilsome, dark, earthy, cold, dry, moist, mixed, phlegmatic, fortunate, praiseworthy — the peg of the sky [watad al-samā'], fixed of the places in strength and choice, the beginning of the season [?]; its planet Saturn — in its complete twentieth degree, the exaltation of Mars; and in it the depression of Jupiter and the woe of the Moon. One looks to it in its faces: Jupiter, Mars, and the Sun. Its ascendant: in the first face, in the form of a man in his right hand a reed and in his left a hoopoe — accords for talismans of prosperity and decline in the helpless; in the second face, in the form of a man before him a single one — accords for seeking what is not attained; in the third face, in the form of a man with him an open Qur'an and before him a wolf-fish — accords for talismans of desire and greed. It has, of letters: the yā' dotted below and the tā' dotted above; and of the relation of man, from the orifices the flowing; from the inner organs the spleen; from the outer the two knees and from the two legs; and it has the phlegmatic and melancholic illnesses; and of tastes, every sour and what is compounded of bitterness and what is compounded of… of colors (sharing with Scorpio); and of places and locations, the cisterns and the places of slaves and the dwellings of the bedouin; and of metals, the watery jewels; and of animals, the privates and the insects and every tall like the camel; and of plants, every thorny [?]. It is specific to the affairs of dominion and authority and law and glory and rank and the governors and the judges and the workers and the works and the trades and the industries; and it indicates the easy places and the inhabited places and the lands of tillage and what is in it, and the plants and everything that comes out of the earth, and what has attended of affairs and what is in it…

"…[Sagittarius:] Jantina lelaki, siang, berjasad, timur, turun, lurus, lebar, berbunyi, mandul, bersinar, berapi, panas, kering, hempedu kuning, bertuah, terpuji, bercampur, jatuh — tidak pandang ascenden; keenam tempat dalam kuat dan sempadan serta kekeliruan. Planetnya Musytari — di darjah ketiganya ketinggian ekor; dan padanya kerendahan kepala serta kecelakaan Utarid; dan di darjah kesembilan belas campuran dua musim gugur dan sejuk. Dipandang padanya dalam wajah: Utarid, Bulan, dan Zuhal. Ascendeninya: wajah pertama, badan kuning dan satu putih dan satu merah — sesuai kerja bantuan dan kuat; wajah kedua, lelaki memandu lembu dan serigala — sesuai kerja takut dan rambut serta sempit; wajah ketiga, lelaki di kepala topi emas membunuh lain — sesuai teror. Ia ada huruf: ṭā' diabaikan dan shīn berdot; dan hubungan manusia, liang dua tangan; dari organ dalam hati dan pundi hempedu; dari luar dua peha dan tulang belakang; dan dadanya serta kemaluannya (kongsi Scorpio), dan demikian kepala serta isinya, dan gravitasi (kongsi Aries); dan penyakit hempedu kuning; dan rasa setiap pahit dan campuran pahit; dan warna setiap cenderung merah kuning; dan logam permata diharap; dan tempat gurun serta pencuri dan api dan apa dibuat (kongsi Aries); dan haiwan serangga (kongsi Scorpio), berkaki empat berkuku (kongsi Aries); dan tumbuhan apa dimakan rasanya seperti badam. Ia khusus urusan musafir dan pindah serta ubah pindah dan utusan dan surat-menyurat serta ghaib dan ikatan sebab ini; dan menunjuk ilmu agama taqwa zuhud ibadah ma'rifat Allah Yang Maha Tinggi, mimpi soleh dan hikmah dan ghaib serta urusan akhirat, dan lelaki yang berpaling dari ilmunya atau berubah dari jalannya dan dibesarkan olehnya serta keadaannya, sultan raja putera besar, gunung tempat susah, kemuliaan dan kedudukan tinggi, api serta permata masuknya, apa mungkin masa depan urusan, pembaharuan benda, urusan bukan doa — tiap urusan ada kembali kedua, serta keperluan kuat dan cahaya — kerana ia tanda jantina lelaki berjasad siang.

( Tanda Kesepuluh — Capricorn [al-Jady] )
Betina, malam, kardinal, utara, naik [?] — panjang, separuh suara, penat, gelap, tanah, sejuk, kering, lembap, bercampur, berlendir, bertuah, terpuji — pasak langit [watad al-samā'], tetap tempat dalam kuat dan pilihan, permulaan musim [?]; planetnya Zuhal — di darjah penuh kedua puluhnya, ketinggian Marikh; dan padanya kerendahan Musytari serta kecelakaan Bulan. Dipandang padanya dalam wajah: Musytari, Marikh, dan Matahari. Ascendeninya: wajah pertama, lelaki di tangan kanan buluh dan kiri burung hudhud — sesuai talisman makmur dan susut dalam yang lemah; wajah kedua, lelaki di hadapan seorang — sesuai cari yang tidak tercapai; wajah ketiga, lelaki bersamanya al-Qur'an terbuka dan di hadapan ikan serigala — sesuai talisman keinginan dan tamak. Ia khusus urusan pemerintahan kuasa undang kemuliaan pangkat gabenor hakim pekerja kerja niaga industri; dan menunjuk tempat senang dan tempat makmur serta tanah tanaman dan isinya, tumbuhan segala keluar bumi, dan apa hadir urusan dan apa padanya…
