English:
— 572 —
"[Venus continued:] and joy and delight and merriment and play and song and jesting and beauty and comeliness and delicacy and perfection and confirmed love and domination and the seeking of marriage-contracts and the goodliness of desire and the purity of state and the brightness of cleanliness, and [she has] ornaments and adornment and gold and silver and the joyous states and the luminous affairs and the couches and the goods.
( Planet: Mercury ['Utārid] )
His name in Persian is Hirmis; he has the second sphere, and he has two signs: Gemini and Virgo. He is mixed in nature according to what he mixes with of the planets — when he is alone in his nature, femaleness and coldness and dryness predominate over him; the body of his light is fourteen degrees — seven from before him and seven from behind; he remains in a sign seventeen days. His exaltation is in the fifteenth degree of Virgo; his depression in Pisces; and his woe is in him and in Sagittarius, praiseworthy, fortunate, added to what he mixes; he is subtle, light, his nature and delicacy are not known, and there is no limit to his description — with the fortunate he is fortunate, and with the inauspicious he is inauspicious; and with the masculine of the planets he is masculine, and with the feminine feminine; and with the luminous luminous, and with the dark dark; he is a mixing, conforming [planet], swift in motions — the scribe of the Sun, the wonder of the substances of wisdom; his fortunes are magnified in his house or his exaltation or his triangle or his bound, and he turns from the accompanying of evil to good; his state is in the eighth and his corruption in his falling. His joy is from the sphere in the ascendant, and from his descent in his houses in Virgo, and from the quarters in the mentioned quarters — from the middle of the sky to the ascendant, and from the peg of the earth to the seventh, and the feminine from the peg of the west to the peg of the sky, and from the peg of the east to the peg of the earth; so if he is with the masculine planets his joy is in the mentioned quarter, and if with the feminine in the feminine quarter. And he has, of the lower world, the power of thought and the mathematical sciences like arithmetic and geomancy and divination and philosophy and heresy and magic and soothsaying and physiognomy and tracking and augury; and he has the intellectual crafts like calligraphy and drawing and engraving and imaging and embroidery; and of the languages the foreign language; and from the letters the waw and the mim and the ra' and the dha' with dot; and of the days Wednesday — and it is a day of continuous inauspiciousness — and the night of Sunday. And he has, of places and locations, the temperate [ones] and the plants and the nourishing and the lustful; and of tastes, every sour; and of colors, every colored; and of woven [things], every delicate; and of metals, quicksilver and the colored stones. And he has, of man, of his inner organs the brain and the imagination and the thought and the fancy and the discernment and the veins and the nerves; and of the outer, the mouth and the tongue and the shoulder-blade and the back and the two arms and the body and the beard; and in the natures mixed coldness and dryness and heat and moisture; and he has the black-bile illnesses and the blood. His specificity is to the affairs of writing and arithmetic and the stars and geometry and imaging and crafting, and the states of merchants and enumeration; and he indicates wisdom and the subtleties of the important sciences, and knowledge and excellence and intellect and majesty of rank and truth and justice and assistance in the knowings and the arts; and he indicates cunning and guile and vileness and evil report and soothsaying and self-display — as he indicates joy and good appearance.
( Planet: the Moon )...

Indonesia:
— 572 —
"[Zuhrah disambung:] dan kegembiraan keseronokan main nyanyian jenaka kecantikan kelembutan kesempurnaan kasih disahkan kuasa cari akad nikah keindahan nafsu kebersihan keadaan kecerahan perhiasan emas perak keadaan gembira urusan bercahaya hamparan harta.
( Planet: 'Utarid )
Namanya Persia Hirmis; sfera kedua, dua tanda Gemini Virgo. Bercampur sifat mengikut campuran planet — bila sendirian betina sejuk kering lebih; badan cahaya empat belas darjah; tujuh depan tujuh belakang; kekal tujuh belas hari. Ketinggian darjah kelima belas Virgo; kerendahan Pisces; kecelakaan padanya dan Sagittarius, terpuji bertuah campur; halus ringan sifat halus tidak diketahui tiada had pada sifat — dengan bertuah bertuah, dengan sial sial; dengan jantina jantan jantan, betina betina; dengan bercahaya bercahaya, gelap gelap; bercampur serasi pantas gerak — penulis Matahari keajaiban zat hikmah; tuah membesar di rumah ketinggian segi tiga ikatannya, ubah dari kejahatan ke baik; keadaan di kelapan kerosakan di jatuh. Kegembiraan dari sfera di ascenden, turun di rumah Virgo, penjuru dari pertengahan langit ke ascenden dan pasak bumi ke ketujuh, feminin dari pasak barat ke langit dan pasak timur ke bumi; bila dengan jantan gembira di penjuru disebut, bila betina di penjuru betina. Alam bawah kuasa fikir dan ilmu hisab seperti hitung geomancy nujum falsafah zindik sihir firasah qafah 'iyafah; kerja intelek seperti tulisan lukisan ukiran imej sulaman; bahasa asing; huruf waw mim ra' dha' berdot; hari Rabu — hari sial berterusan — malam Ahad. Tempat sederhana tumbuhan pemakanan nafsu; rasa masam; warna berwarna; tenunan halus; logam raksa batu berwarna. Manusia: organ dalam otak khayal fikir sangka deria urat saraf; luar mulut lidah belikat belakang dua lengan badan janggut; sifat campur sejuk kering panas lembap; penyakit hitam darah. Khusus urusan tulisan hisab bintang geometri imej kerja niaga kiraan; menunjuk hikmah halus ilmu penting, ilmu kecemerlangan akal maruah kebenaran keadilan bantuan ma'rifat seni; menunjuk helah keji buruk lapor nujum riak — juga kegembiraan rupa baik.
