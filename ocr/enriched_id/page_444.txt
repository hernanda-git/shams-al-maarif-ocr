[The square of al-Muhaymin — columns الم / هي / من; rows: 16 / 89 / 32; 88 / 13 / 42; 41 / 34 / 14]
"And as for the mention — the basmala: 'Glory be to You, how great is Your affair and how mighty Your dominion; there is no god but You, Lord of lords and Owner of necks. You are al-Muhaymin, the Bestower; I ask You, O Allah, by the secret of the exposition of Your wisdom in the hearts and the secrets, and the light of Your manifestation upon the righteous, the good, that You clothe me with awe and acceptance among the sons of my kind, and that You unveil to me the secrets of the Over-seer — O Muhaymin, You are the Knower of what comes to be, You turned away the understandings and tongues from describing Your perfection, and You are too majestic and great for Your Essence to be attained; I ask You that You extend to me a subtlety from the subtleties of Your name al-Muhaymin, and that You extend to me the servant of this name ṬALĪ'ĪL, that I may know the lofty ranks from the innate sciences — O Allah, O Muhaymin. Whoever persists in this mention, Allah subordinates the hearts to him and he attains every sought thing.' (Section on His name the Exalted, al-'Azīz) Know that the meaning of al-'Azīz is the one of grave worth who has no equal, and to Him the needs are tied, and His end is the Overcomer, the Subduer. And know that might is the root of continuance, for the Real was fortified by His continuance, and He granted might and continuance in Paradise to the believers, and the might of His messenger (upon him peace) is the afterlife life, and the essence by the light of prophethood is His particularization by the messengership, and His speech remains by His continuance. Therefore He does not bring it down — no, from the secret that perishes by His continuance in the Abode of the Hereafter, so the remaining hears by the remaining; therefore the inheriting scholars have the prophetic might and the life of the folk, and their reality in faith is the life of hearts and the service of Allah, and the life of Allah is by the love of Allah, and the life of bodies is by the standing in Allah's commands — when the servant completes these stations he is called 'Azīz. And whoever wills the reality of realization by this name — let him be patient in the lordship by the secret of servitude and submission. The Messenger of Allah (upon him peace) said: 'Whoever humbles himself before a rich man for his riches has lost two-thirds of his religion,' for a man is by three things — his heart, his tongue, and his hand; so if he humbles himself by his tongue he has lost a third of his religion, and if he humbles himself by his heart he has lost his religion. And he who is conformed by this name does not recite with it anything, and is empty of people, leaving the desires, and is rich by Allah the Exalted. And this name is of the invocations of the reliant, for he who relies on this name — Allah provides him from whence he does not reckon. * And whoever writes its square in a ring of silver or gold and carries it with persistence in recitation — Allah provides him might; and if the number of a person's name agrees and he takes it as a mention, Allah opens to him the doors of might and he is awed among the lower and upper worlds. And as for the standing mention — 'In the name of Allah the Merciful, the Compassionate: O Allah, You are al-'Azīz the Overcomer whose force is not overcome; I ask You that You strengthen me for Your obedience, and that You subordinate to me Your servant RAḌĪ'ĪL, servant of this name, who extends to me awe and dignity and fulfills my needs, and that You revive my heart and spirit by a gleam from the luminous lightnings — none is fortified by might but the mighty; have mercy on me and raise me to the rank of the saints and the righteous, O Lord of the worlds, and make me firm as You made firm Your brought-near saints and all the people of Your obedience.' (Section on His name the Exalted, al-Jabbār) Know that the meaning of al-Jabbār is the One whose judgment proceeds by the way of compulsion in everyone, and no wariness wards Him off — and He is Allah; and the absolute Jabbār is the One who compels everyone. And the consideration of that is upon countless kinds in its detail, for the greatest witness in that is the world of dominion, which is expressed as the world of witness, for it is the nearest consideration for the considerate — except that it is a place of illusion, so the line of governance is to Allah, for 'We sent down from the sky one water by Our mercy for a known measure, the cloud took it…' and it is…"

[Segi empat al-Muhaymin — lajur الم / هي / من; baris: 16 / 89 / 32; 88 / 13 / 42; 41 / 34 / 14]
"Zikir — basmalah: 'Maha Suci Engkau, betapa agung urusan-Mu dan betapa perkasa kerajaan-Mu; tiada tuhan melainkan Engkau, Tuhan segala tuhan dan Pemilik tengkuk. Engkau al-Muhaymin Pemberi; kuminta ya Allah dengan rahsia hikmah-Mu dalam hati rahsia, cahaya zahir-Mu pada soleh baik, kenakan aku kekaguman penerimaan di kalangan anak jenisku, buka rahsia Pengawas — wahai Muhaymin, Engkau Yang Tahu apa terjadi, Engkau palingkan faham lidah dari gambarkan kesempurnaan-Mu, Engkau terlalu agung untuk dicapai zat-Mu; kuminta limpahkan halus dari nama-Mu al-Muhaymin, dan khadam nama ini ṬALĪ'ĪL agar kukenal darjat tinggi ilmu laduni — ya Allah ya Muhaymin. Sesiapa istiqamah zikir ini, Allah takluk hati padanya capai setiap diminta.' (Fasal nama al-'Azīz) Ketahui makna al-'Azīz yang berharga tiada banding, kepadanya diikat hajat, kesudahannya Perkasa Pembuka. Kekuatan akar kekal, Yang Haq teguh dengan kekal-Nya, kurnia kekuatan kekal syurga pada mukmin, kekuatan rasul hidup akhirat, zat cahaya nubuwah khas rasul, kata kekal dengan kekal-Nya. Maka tak turunkan — dari rahsia yang binasa dengan kekal-Nya di akhirat, yang kekal dengar yang kekal; ulama waris ada kekuatan nabi hidup kaum, hakikat iman hidup hati khidmat Allah, hidup Allah dengan kasih Allah, hidup jasad dengan tunaikan perintah — bila hamba sempurna maqam dipanggil 'Azīz. Sesiapa mahu hakikat tamkin dengan nama ini — sabar dalam ketuhanan rahsia hamba tunduk. Sabda 'Sesiapa rendah diri pada kaya kerana kaya hilang dua pertiga agama,' kerana manusia dengan tiga: hati lidah tangan; rendah diri lidah hilang sepertiga, rendah diri hati hilang agama. Ahli conformed nama ini tak baca bersamanya, kosong dari manusia tinggal nafsu, kaya dengan Allah. Nama ini zikir tawakal, sesiapa bertawakal Allah rezeki dari tempat tak disangka. * Tulis segi empat cincin perak/emas bawa istiqamah — Allah bagi kekuatan; kalau bilangan nama sepakat jadikan zikir, Allah buka pintu kekuatan dikagumi alam bawah atas. Zikir berdiri — 'Bismillah: ya Allah Engkau al-'Azīz Perkasa tak terkalahkan; kuatkan aku taat, takluk hamba RAḌĪ'ĪL khadam nama ini beri kekaguman maruah tunaikan hajat, hidupkan hati roh dengan sinar cahaya; tiada teguh melainkan yang perkasa; rahmat angkat aku ke maqam wali soleh ya Tuhan dunia, teguhkan seperti wali muqarrab ahli taat.' (Fasal al-Jabbār) Makna al-Jabbār yang hukum-Nya jalan paksa pada setiap, tiada kewaspadaan halang — Dia Allah; Jabbār mutlak yang paksa semua. Pertimbangan berjenis tak terhitung, saksi terbesar alam domini disebut alam saksi, terdekat pada ahli pandang — kecuali tempat khayal, maka tadbir pada Allah, 'Kami turun dari langit air satu dengan rahmat takaran diketahui awan ambil…' dan ia…"
