And the one who, by his call, made the sphere of tawhid revolve ❊ and the suns of his wisdom shone forth ❊ and the stars of misguidance sank away by his sight ❊ and the dawn of tawhid broke forth by his felicity ❊ may the best of prayers be upon him and upon his family ❊ and may Allah be pleased with the verifying, truthful Companions ❊ may Allah be pleased with them all together ❊ a prayer that reaches them the highest ranks and the loftiest degrees ❊ (Now then) ❊ then the truth has banners and for the reality there is a system and for the spirits, by the divine knowledges, an acquaintance, and the means is sought and the ability to its divisions is bestowed, and felicity is coupled with the suns of perfection, and eternal experience by the use of the rites of the Shari‘ah is bestowed, and the highest degrees in ‘Illiyyin are the degree of the doers, and the highest of them is the station of the guiding, verifying ones, and there is no station for a scholar in the religion of Allah who does not benefit, just as there is no existence of life for the reality of a soul that does not benefit, and the farthest of people from felicity is he who makes light of the rulings of the millah and breaches the conditions of the verifying ones from among the people of the qiblah (And I), when I saw the speech of the eminent ones, those whose word has become lofty and whose wisdom has spread in the horizons and whose blessing has encompassed the creatures, they had authored in the conjugation of names and attributes and the secrets of letters and remembrances and supplications, and there had inclined to me from one attached to me his affection in clarifying what they had authored and the treasure of what they had stored up, so I answered him, with acknowledgment of incapacity to understand the perceptions of the past forefathers and the guiding verifying imams, and I hoped from Allah, by the exertion of acknowledgment and acquisition, that He would extend to me from the spirits of the spirits of them the subtle assistances, so that the utterance would be in agreement with the verification and detailed by the tongue of confirmation (So I say) ❊ with Allah is the recourse, and upon Him is the reliance: the intended purpose of the chapters of this book is the knowledge of the nobility of the Names of Allah (exalted is He), and what Allah (exalted is He) has deposited in their sea of the kinds of the wisdom-jewels and the divine subtleties, and how to act by the names and the invocations and what follows them of the letters of the surahs and the verses. And I made this book into chapters, so that each chapter would indicate what it has chosen and enumerated of the precise sciences by which one reaches the Divine Presence without toil and without perceiving hardship, and what one reaches by them of the desires of the world and what is coveted in it. And I named this book, the chosen, unparalleled, lofty knowledge (Shams al-Ma‘arif wa Lata’if al-‘Awarif), for what is contained within it of the subtleties of disposition and the recognitions of effects. So it is forbidden for whoever this book of mine falls into his hand to reveal it to other than its people or to divulge it in other than its place, for if he does that, Allah (exalted is He) denies him its benefits and withholds from him its advantages and its blessing. And do not touch it except while you are pure, and do not read by it except when you are mindful, so that you attain from it what you want, and do not direct it except to what pleases Allah (exalted is He), for it is the book of the awliya’ and the righteous and the obedient and the seekers and the workers, the aspirants, so be sparing with it and leave not of it little nor much, and let your certainty be truthful and your faith in its realities trusting. Verily the deeds are by the intentions, and verily to every man is what he intended. And when there stands for you an intention in a work from the works, then have faith in it and confirm, for the saying of him, upon him be peace, “None of you should supplicate except while he is certain of the answer,” and be decisive upon your work with soundness, for his saying, upon him be peace, “When one of you asks, let him resolve the asking, for he is not disliked,” and the certainty of the answer, and be decisive upon your work with soundness, for the saying of him, upon him be peace and blessing, “One of you is answered so long as he does not hasten, saying: I supplicated and I was not answered.” ❊ And beware that you deem the answer slow, and cease not to be in a state of purity constantly, awaiting it. ❊ And this upright law and straight path comprises forty chapters; every chapter comprises meanings and indications and hidden and manifest symbols, so reflect upon it with your intellect and contemplate it with your thought. ❊ And these chapters: (the first chapter) is in the letters.

dengan kebahagiaannya ❊ semoga shalawat terbaik tercurah atasnya dan atas keluarganya ❊ dan semoga Allah ridha atas para Sahabat yang memverifikasi lagi jujur ❊ semoga Allah ridha atas mereka Dan aku menjadikan kitab ini berpasal-pasal, agar setiap pasal menunjukkan apa yang dipilih dan dihitungnya dari ilmu-ilmu yang teliti yang dengannya sampai ke hadirat rabbani tanpa lelah dan tanpa merasakan kesukaran, dan apa yang sampai dengannya dari keinginan duniawi dan apa yang dicita-citakan di dalamnya. Dan aku menamakan kitab ini, yang terpilih, tiada bandingnya, ilmu yang luhur (Syams al-Ma‘arif wa Latha’if al-‘Awarif), karena apa yang terkandung di dalamnya dari latha’if pengaturan dan ma‘rifat pengaruh. Maka haram bagi siapa saja yang kitabku ini jatuh ke tangannya untuk menampakkannya kepada selain ahlinya atau membocorkannya bukan pada tempatnya, karena jika ia berbuat demikian, Allah (subhanahu wa ta’ala) menghalanginya dari manfaat-manfaatnya dan mencegah darinya keuntungan-keuntungannya serta berkahnya. Sesungguhnya amal-amal itu dengan niat, dan sesungguhnya bagi setiap manusia apa yang ia niatkan. Dan apabila berdiri pada dirimu suatu niat dalam suatu amal dari amal-amal, maka berimanlah kepadanya dan benarkan, karena sabda beliau shallallahu ‘alaihi wa sallam: “Tidaklah salah seorang dari kalian berdoa kecuali dalam keadaan yakin akan dikabulkan,” dan tegaslah atas amalmu dengan kesehatan, karena sabda beliau shallallahu ‘alaihi wa sallam: “Apabila salah seorang dari kalian meminta, hendaklah ia meneguhkan permintaannya, karena ia tidak dibenci,” dan keyakinan akan dikabulkan, serta tegaslah atas amalmu dengan kesehatan, karena sabda beliau shallallahu ‘alaihi wa sallam: “Salah seorang dari kalian dijawab selama ia tidak tergesa-gesa, lalu berkata: ‘Aku berdoa namun tidak dijawab bagiku.’” ❊ Dan janganlah engkau menganggap jawaban itu lambat, dan janganlah berhenti dalam keadaan suci senantiasa sambil menantinya. ❊ Dan hukum yang lurus serta jalan yang benar ini mencakup empat puluh pasal; setiap pasal mencakup makna-makna, isyarat-isyarat, dan simbol-simbol yang tersembunyi maupun yang nyata, maka renungkanlah ia dengan akalmu dan tadabburilah ia dengan fikirmu. ❊ Dan pasal-pasal ini: (pasal pertama) adalah tentang huruf-huruf.
