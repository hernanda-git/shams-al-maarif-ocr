قُضيت في الوقت، خصوصاً إذا دعا على ظالم يحصل المطلوب. وإذا أردت تصريف هذه الأسماء فضعه

English:
— 54 —

And this is the description of the circle as you see, so understand and you will be guided.

And from Abū Huḍayl, may Allah be pleased with him, who said: "When 'Isā, peace be upon him, wanted to revive the dead, he would pray two rak'ahs; and when he finished, he would prostrate and supplicate with these names, and they are: 'O Eternal, O Everlasting, O One, O One, O Self-Sufficient.'" Muqātil ibn Sulaymān, may Allah be pleased with him, said: "I sought the names by which 'Isā, peace be upon him, used to revive the dead for a period of forty years until I found them with a man of knowledge—and they are the aforementioned names."

And he said: "Whoever calls upon Allah with them in the dawn prayer a hundred times and seeks any need he wishes, it is fulfilled; and whoever desires the destruction of a wrongdoer, let him pray the dawn prayer and say, while sitting before speaking to anyone: 'In the name of Allah the Most Merciful the Most Compassionate, and there is no power nor strength except with Allah the Exalted the Great: O Eternal, O Everlasting, O Unique, O Odd, O One, O Self-Sufficient, O Living, O Self-Subsisting, O Generous, O Merciful, O Support of him who has no support, O unto Whom is the recourse, O Who begets not nor was begotten, and there is none comparable to Him, O Possessor of Majesty and Generosity,' a hundred times, and asks Allah, exalted, for any need—it is fulfilled at once, especially if he supplicates against a wrongdoer, the sought is attained. And if you desire to deploy these names, place them in a circle like the circle of the weather, and write the names and hang it and carry it, and you will find the sought, and Allah knows best."

And this is the supplication of the circle; you say: "O Allah, I ask You by Jibrā'īl, peace be upon him, when he ascended Your Throne, and by the right of Your name 'Allah, Allah, Allah,' that You subject to me Your angels—the king Kasfā'īl, Dardā'īl, Samkhā'īl, Dūbā'īl, Samkā'īl, Ṭahir Yā'īl, and Karmā'īl—answer, O noble angels and pure spirits brought near to Allah by His Oneness, by the right of Allah the Greatest, the Mighty, the Holy, who preferred (Himself) over all names altogether, its mighty, glorious, and great (aspects), that You subject to me these noble angels by fulfilling my need, which is such-and-such of what is pleasing to Allah therein. And beware of envy, for Bil'ām ibn Bā'ūrā' {We gave him Our signs, but he stripped himself of them}—the verse—so preserve this name and guard it, for many a creation occupied themselves with it and became among the learned and the righteous and attained their aim and object; and Allah guides whom He wills to a straight path.

And know—may Allah grant me and you success to His obedience—that whoever writes this wafq and carries it while pure, Allah establishes his affair outwardly and inwardly, aids him in obedience, and provides him victory over enemies; and no tyrant looks at him but reveres him. And whoever writes it and carries it on his head, every tyrant is humbled before him, and Allah secures his heart, outward and inward, and strengthens his heart for outward and inward deeds. And no one carried it and disputed with his enemy but subdued him, overcame him, and was victorious over him by permission of Allah, exalted. And whoever entered battle with it, Allah granted him victory over his enemies and no harm befalls him. And if a king carries it, the soldiers, commanders, and notables obey him, and he is aided and victorious.
