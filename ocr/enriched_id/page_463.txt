هذا الاسم بحق اسمك القابض وبحق الملائكة المقربين وأن تنورني وألبسني نوراً من أنوار
هذا الاسم يا الله يا قابض ما من عبد تلا هذا الذكر إلا فتح الله عليه ورزقه القوة وكان ملطوفاً به
( فصل في اسمه تعالى الباسط )
اعلم أن الباسط هو الذي يبسط الأرواح في الأشباح يوم الرجعة وليس ذلك إلا لله تعالى وأما شهود
ذلك في العموم فإن الله تعالى يقبض بالسكون ويبسط بالحركة فهذا قبض عموم في الإيجاد الأول يوم
القبضتين قبض الله بواطن أهل الشمال بين حقائق الإيمان وبسط قلوب أهل القبضة اليمنى لأنوار
الإيمان وشرح صدورهم لقبول الإسلام وقبض الله الجماد بجمود ليوم النمو والازدياد وقبض الليل
بقدم الحركات وبسط النهار بظهور الحركات وقبض الباطن في عالم الأمر وهيبته وبسط الخلق في
عالم رحمته تحقيق في القبض والبسط والتقرب إلى الله بهذين الاسمين أن تقبض نفسك عن
الشهوات المخالفات وجسمك عن الحرام ولسانك عن الكلام ونظرك عن المحرمات وأذنك عن
الغيبة ويدك عن المعاصي وعقلك عن الهوى وروحك عن الالتفات إلى
الكرامات وسرك عن كشف أسرار الله فإذا تخلقت باسمه الباسط فتح الله عليك من الأنوار باباً
فتكون حواسك الخمس سامعة ظاهرة واللسان ناطقاً بالذكر ويشرق قلبك بنور الفراسة وصحة
الإخلاص ويطلعك الله على حقائق الملكوت وإذا بسط الله لك بأنواره أشهدك حقائق العلويات
والسفليات والتصرف وله خلوة وتلاوته دبر كل صلاة ٨٢٤ والذكر القائم به ٢١ فإنه يحضر الخادم
واسمه بسطائيل موكل ببسط النفوس ويراه الذاكر يقظة ومناماً ويفيض عليه من الكرامات
والخيرات وله مربع يصلح لمن غلبت عليه السوء القهرية يكتب ويسقى له ٧ أيام على الريق ثم
يكتب المربع والذكر القائم به في لوح فضة ويحمله يعافى وإذا وافق عدده اسم شخص وكتب المربع
على الخاتم وأهم الملك حوله ولازم على تلاوته كان مهاباً مقبولاً ولا ينقبض قلبه وإذا أضيف له الودود
رزقه الله البسط والمودة وإذا حصل للإنسان قبض وتلاوته فتح الله عليه أبواب البسط وسهل له الأمور

English:
— 457 —

"…and bind me by Your favor. O Allah, I ask You by the secret of the two fists and that You subordinate to me Your servant FIḌĀ'ĪL, servant of this name, by the right of Your name al-Qābiḍ and by the right of the near angels, and that You illumine me and clothe me with a light from the lights of this name. O Allah, O Seizer, there is no servant who recited this mention but Allah opened to him and provided him the power and he was one tenderly cared for. (Section on His name the Exalted, al-Bāsit) Know that al-Bāsit is the one who spreads the spirits in the specters on the Day of Return, and that is only for Allah the Exalted. And as for the witnessing of that in the generality — for Allah the Exalted seizes by stillness and spreads by movement; so this is the general seizing in the first bringing-forth, the day of the two fists: Allah seized the inwards of the people of the left between the realities of faith, and spread the hearts of the people of the right fist by the lights of faith, and expanded their breasts for the acceptance of Islam; and Allah seized the inanimate by its inertness for the day of growth and increase; and seized the night by the precedence of the movements, and spread the day by the appearance of the movements; and seized the inward in the world of the command and its awe, and spread the creation in His world of mercy — a realization in the seizing and the spreading. And the drawing near to Allah by these two names is that you seize your soul from the contrary desires and your body from the forbidden and your tongue from speech and your look from the forbidden and your ear from backbiting and your hand from disobediences and your intellect from caprice and your spirit from turning to the marvels, and your secret from unveiling the secrets of Allah. So when you are adorned by His name al-Bāsit, Allah opens to you from the lights a door, and your five senses become hearing and manifest, and the tongue speaking with the mention, and your heart shines with the light of discernment and the soundness of sincerity, and Allah discloses to you the realities of the Dominion. And when Allah spreads to you by His lights, He makes you witness the realities of the highs and the lowers and the disposal. And it has a retreat and its recitation after every prayer 245, and the standing mention by it 11 — so the servant attends; and its name is BASTĀ'ĪL, charged with spreading the souls, and the one remembering sees him in wakefulness and sleep, and there pours upon him of the honors and the goods. And it has a square that rectifies for one overcome by the compulsive evil — he writes it and is given it to drink for 7 days on an empty stomach; then he writes the square and the standing mention on a silver tablet and carries it and is cured. And if the number of a person agrees and he writes the square on the seal and the king around it and persists in its recitation, he is awesome and accepted, and his heart is not contracted. And if to it is added al-Wadūd, Allah provides him the spreading and the love. And when there accrues to the human the seizing and its recitation, Allah opens to him the doors of the spread and eases for him the affairs — and this is its figure. And its mention with the basmala: 'O Allah, O Bāsit, You are the Spreader of the earths and the heavens, You measured the things and spread them by Your wisdom, the fixing of the command and the preservation of the heart and its spreading, and the unveiling of the unseen matters, and the establishing upon the unveiling of the hidden subtleties and the manifest affairs, and extend to me a subtlety from Your subtleties that Your creation may address me — every atom of the atoms of existence — by the spreading. O Bāsit, O Wadūd, whoever persists in it, ease for him the causes of the spreading and clarify from him the seizing.' (Section on His name the Exalted, al-Khāfiḍ al-Rāfi') Know that al-Khāfiḍ is the one who lowers the disbelievers by vengeance and raises the believers by felicitation and raises His saints by the nearness… [square of al-Khāfiḍ — columns ال با s ط; rows: 2 / 3 / 2 / 8 / 6; 3 / 2 / 5 / 8 / 7; 5 / 9 / 6 / 2 / 4]"

Indonesia:
— 457 —

"…ikat aku dengan kurnia-Mu. Ya Allah kuminta rahsia dua tapak tangan takluk hamba FIḌĀ'ĪL khadam nama ini dengan hak nama al-Qābiḍ malaikat hampir, terangilah aku pakaikan cahaya nama ini. Ya Allah ya Qābiḍ tiada hamba baca melainkan Allah buka beri kuasa dijaga. (Fasal al-Bāsit) al-Bāsit hampar roh dalam bayangan hari kembali, hanya Allah. Syuhud umum: Allah cabut dengan diam hampar dengan gerak; ini cabut umum kejadian pertama hari dua tapak: Allah cabut batin ahli kiri antara hakikat iman, hampar hati ahli kanan cahaya iman luaskan dada terima Islam; cabut benda mati untuk tumbuh; cabut malam hampar siang; cabut batin alam perintah takut hampar makhluk rahmat — realizasi cabut hampar. Dekat Allah dua nama: cabut jiwa nafsu badan lidah pandang telinga tangan akal roh kasyaf rahsia. Bila berakhlak al-Bāsit Allah buka pintu cahaya, pancaindera mendengar nyata lidah zikir hati cahaya firasat ikhlas, zahir Malakut. Bila hampar cahaya nyata alam tinggi rendah pentadbiran. Khalwat baca 245, zikir 11 — khadam hadir; namanya BASTĀ'ĪL hampar jiwa, nampak jaga tidur limpah kemuliaan kebaikan. Segi empat untuk yang dikuasai jahat: tulis minum 7 hari perut kosong; tulis loh perak bawa sembuh. Kalau bilangan sepakat tulis cincin raja keliling istiqamah — hebat diterima hati tak ciut. Tambah al-Wadūd Allah beri hampar kasih. Bila manusia cabut baca Allah buka pintu hampar mudah urusan — inilah rupanya. Zikir basmalah: 'Ya Allah ya Bāsit Penghampar bumi langit ukur benda hampar hikmah tetap perintah pelihara hati hampar buka ghaib teguh halus tersembunyi nyata, limpahkan halus makhluk berdialog tiap atom wujud dengan hampar. Ya Bāsit ya Wadūd istiqamah mudah sebab hampar buang cabut.' (Fasal al-Khāfiḍ al-Rāfi') al-Khāfiḍ rendahkan kafir balas dendam angkat mukmin bahagia wali hampir… [segi empat al-Khāfiḍ — lajur ال با s ط; baris 2 / 3 / 2 / 8 / 6; 3 / 2 / 5 / 8 / 7; 5 / 9 / 6 / 2 / 4]"
